### Study
"Identification of cardiac ion channel protein networks and their signature in the human electrocardiogram" (PRIDE PXD028021; publication doi:10.1038/s44161-023-00294-y, Nature Cardiovascular Research 2023; full text not retrieved). Odense/SDU-affiliated record (MS at Copenhagen, Q Exactive HF-X). Antibody pull-downs of 13 cardiac ion channels from the membrane fraction of adult male C57BL/6JRj mouse hearts (4 pools of 5 hearts), with mouse and rabbit IgG control IPs, gel-separated into 4 slices; plus a 12-fraction deep proteome of the membrane lysate pools.

### Scope (deposited vs annotated)
`files/all`: 277 files: 276 RAW + `proteinGroups_Pride.txt`. All 276 RAW annotated: 15 IP series (13 baits + 2 IgG controls) × 4 pools × 4 gel slices = 240, and 36 proteome runs (pools REP1, REP2, REP4 × 12 fractions; no REP3 proteome was deposited). The generated name list matched PRIDE by SHA-256 (including the two `Kv11-1_062_D4/D8_IgG` names without the zero padding).

### Sample / file mapping evidence
IP files `…_MOUSE_<bait>_<letter><nn>_<slice>.raw`: nn 01–16, four consecutive numbers per replicate in the slice order 125–400 kDa, 50–125 kDa, 0–50 kDa, IgG band (the protocol: "each of the lanes were excised into four parts, combining the IgG bands in one part"). So pool = ceil(nn/4), fraction = slice 1–4. The protocol says lysates from 5 mice were pooled to give 4 replicates, so biological replicate = pool (1–4), and the same pool number is shared across baits. The proteome files `REP<k>_Frac01–12` = membrane-fraction pools k digested in solution (Lys-C + trypsin) and fractionated into 12 concatenated fractions.

### Judgement calls for reviewers
The pool = biological replicate reading across baits; the high-pH assumption; whether the Inf2 reverse pull-downs described in the protocol belong to this deposit (no Inf2 files are present).
