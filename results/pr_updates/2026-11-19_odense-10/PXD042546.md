### Study
"Spectral library search for improved TMTpro labelled peptide assignment in human plasma proteomics" (Palstrøm NB, Campbell AJ, Lindegaard CA, Cakar S, Matthiesen R, Beck HC; Proteomics 2023, doi:10.1002/pmic.202300236, PMID 37706597). Odense University Hospital (Beck lab). Europe PMC: not open access, no PMC full text; only the abstract plus the PRIDE protocols were available. A TMTpro-specific spectral library was built from highly fractionated TMTpro-labelled human plasma (un-depleted, MARS14-depleted, ProteoMiner-enriched), abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA) wall and intraluminal thrombus, and the Pierce HeLa protein digest standard; ten TMTpro plasma runs (`Sample_1..10`) were used to evaluate the library.

### Scope (deposited vs annotated)
`files/all`: 162 files, all carrying `projectAccessions` = PXD042546. 79 RAW, 79 mzXML (PEAK), 11 `.msf`/`pep.xml` search files, 3 library files (`.msp`, `.sptxt`, `.zip`).
- **Annotated: 69 library RAW files × 16 TMTpro channels = 1104 rows.**
- **Not annotated: `Sample_1.raw` … `Sample_10.raw` (10 RAW).** These are TMTpro-16 plasma runs (Orbitrap Eclipse, method `HCD_OTOT_120k30kTurbo_FAIMS_200minTMT`) with one Proteome Discoverer `.msf` each. The paper is closed, the PRIDE protocol does not describe them, and no channel→sample map was found (PRIDE files, protocols, Europe PMC abstract). Annotating 160 channels without donor identities, disease state or reference/pool channels would mean inventing sample identity, so they are left out. The per-file `.msf` (SQLite) is the place to look if a later pass wants to try to recover PD's study-factor table.
