### Study
"Integrated Single-Myofibre Multi-Omics Correlates Inflammatory State to Myosin Energetics in ICU-Acquired Weakness". J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle 2025, doi:10.1002/jcsm.70407, PMID 42896625, PMC13680048 (full Methods read via NCBI efetch; the titles matched). Proteomics at SDU Odense. Single human quadriceps (rectus femoris) myofibres from 8 ICU-acquired weakness (ICU-AW) patients and 8 orthopaedic controls (total hip replacement for hip arthritis) were profiled sequentially for Mant-ATP chase, RNA-seq, and proteomics on the same fibre. Label-free diaPASEF on a timsTOF SCP with an Evosep One (60 SPD).

### Sample / file mapping evidence
`metadata.csv` (178 rows) maps `Analytical Sample ID` S1–S178 to `Subject ID` and plate position; each RAW name carries `_E0000048x_S<n>_S2-<well>_`, and every file's S-number and well matched the metadata (0 misses). Subject IDs read `<donor>f<fibre>`: donors `c14,c15,c17,c18,c19,c22,c24,c25` (8 controls) and `s5_2,s8_2,s14_2,s15_2,s20,s21,s23,s26` (8 ICU-AW), matching the paper's n = 8 + 8. `characteristics[individual]` = donor, `biological replicate` = fibre number within the donor, `source name` = the full subject ID (one fibre = one source).
