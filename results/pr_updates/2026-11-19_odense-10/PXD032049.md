Proteomes 2022;10(2):15, doi:10.3390/proteomes10020015, PMID 35645373, PMC9149909 (Methods read). MS at SDU Odense (Larsen group). UPEC strain UTI89 grown in human urine or MOPS (T = 0–4 h), in MOPS ± glucose to exponential (OD600 0.5) / stationary (OD600 2.0) phase, and recovered from infected J82 bladder cells (trypsin- or scrape-detached pellets).
