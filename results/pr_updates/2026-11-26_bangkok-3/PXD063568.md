### Judgement calls for reviewers
- Split into two files because the cell-lines template forbids reserved words in `cell line`, and the primary fibroblasts are not a cell line. Together the files cover all 30 runs exactly once.
- The DDA and SWATH runs of one sample share a source name, with technical replicate 1 (DDA) and 2 (SWATH). This is based on the submitter's statement that the SWATH samples came "from the same subjects".
- The dia-acquisition template is not declared, so that DDA and SWATH runs of one sample stay in the same file. SWATH rows carry `SWATH MS` (PRIDE:0000447); the window scheme is in the campaign report only.
- Search settings come from the paper's MaxQuant Methods. The deposited Mascot `.dat` files were not read.
- The VSMUi002 lines are not in Cellosaurus, so `cellosaurus accession` = not available.
