## Add sandbox SDRF draft for PXD078037

**Dataset**: [PXD078037](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD078037)

Draft, not ready for `datasets/`. 163 of 508 RAW (inside the 5 figure zips): the breast-cancer FFPE LMD tissue series (area titration, tumor vs stroma, single nuclei, 4 patients).

### Why it is a draft
Left out: 345 method-optimisation runs (buffers, DDM, de-crosslinking, digestion time, input series, HeLa buffer tests, cell-count tests, blanks) whose sample source is not stated per series. Patient clinical data are not deposited.

### To promote
A map of the excluded method-optimisation series to a biological source (or a decision to leave them out), and patient clinical fields.
