## Add sandbox SDRF draft for PXD050784

**Dataset**: [PXD050784](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD050784)

Draft, not ready for `datasets/`. Two files: `PXD050784-human.sdrf.tsv` (288 HeLa/HepG2 runs) and `PXD050784-rat.sdrf.tsv` (978 rat organ runs). 1266 of 1311 RAW inside the 5 zips.

### Why it is a draft
Left out: 45 runs in Pilot_experiments.zip (TMT-10 TPP vs PISA, U2OS LFQ, Lumos filter-vs-centrifuge) — TMT channel layout and sample source not deposited. HepG2-folder files are named 'HELA' (folder + HepG2 FileList used). Several rat drug concentrations are codes only.

### To promote
A decision on the pilot series, confirmation that the HepG2-folder 'HELA' runs are HepG2, and numeric doses for the rat cancer-drug / staurosporine codes.
