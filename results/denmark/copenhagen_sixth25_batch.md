# Copenhagen sixth batch: 25 PXDs

Selected on 2026-11-16 from confirmed Copenhagen candidates, excluding the five earlier Copenhagen cohorts, local annotation or block records, the saved community-corpus list, and two previously identified processed-only RAW mislabels. The [tracker](copenhagen_sixth25_batch.tsv) orders 25 accessions by the sum of accession-scoped PRIDE file sizes for entries categorised as RAW. Every cached file-list entry was checked against its accession. `scripts/batches/select_copenhagen_sixth25.py` reproduces the selection and preserves tracker statuses on rerun.

The cohort runs from PXD017475 (21.81 GB RAW-labelled files) through PXD066033 (34.01 GB). RAW-labelled ZIP files may contain multiple vendor acquisitions; archive interiors and sample assignments must be verified before SDRF construction.

## Initial evidence review

### PXD017475

The [paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-22746-4), [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD017475), and [supplementary Data 2](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC8080841/supplementaryFiles) separate this accession into nine secretome runs and ten MIR31HG/luciferase RNA pull-down runs. The secretome [9-row SDRF](../../annotations/PXD017475-secretome.sdrf.tsv) records three proliferating controls, three senescent controls, and three MIR31HG-depleted senescent samples. Its A/B/C run map was checked against 479 shared proteins in the published MaxQuant table (matching-condition centered LFQ correlations 0.94–0.98). The pull-down [10-row SDRF](../../annotations/PXD017475-pulldown.sdrf.tsv) records five replicates per oligo target, senescent BJ ER:BRAF cells, and UV crosslinking. Both files cover all 19 accession-matched RAW files. They keep their distinct Lys-C/trypsin versus trypsin-only digests and search settings. Official validation, structure, offline term checks, and fresh independent reviews pass; current hash-bound approval receipts are recorded. The validator only warns that engineered BJ ER:BRAF is absent from its offline cell-line ontology cache. Pull-down search tolerances and fragmentation method remain unspecified because the accession record does not establish them. [Secretome evidence](../../annotations/PXD017475-secretome.evidence.json) and [pull-down evidence](../../annotations/PXD017475-pulldown.evidence.json) document the mappings and limits.
