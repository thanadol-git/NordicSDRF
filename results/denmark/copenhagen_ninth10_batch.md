# Copenhagen ninth batch: 10 PXDs

Started 2026-11-18 on `codex/copenhagen-ninth10`, based on the latest NordicSDRF `origin/main` (`b82e0dd`). The [tracker](copenhagen_ninth10_batch.tsv) lists the ten smallest remaining confirmed Copenhagen deposits by accession-scoped PRIDE RAW-file size after prior batches and local annotations. PXD006579 was skipped because its PRIDE human organism conflicts with the mouse search database; it requires identity resolution before annotation. Current BigBio `upstream/main` (`97ab7fdd8`) and open PRs were checked for all ten accessions; none was present. PRIDE project and file records were refreshed on 2026-11-18, with accession ownership asserted for each file list. A PRIDE RAW-category ZIP may contain multiple acquisitions and will be inspected before annotation.

Before starting each PXD, rerun `python scripts/batches/check_pxd_absent.py PXDnnnnnn --bigbio-repo /path/to/sdrf-annotated-datasets --refresh`. The guard checks local annotation files and Git refs, BigBio `main`, and the titles, bodies, branch names, and changed files of every open BigBio PR. It passed for all ten on 2026-11-18. Each PXD then requires an evidence manifest, deterministic validation, and independent adversarial review before its own commit. The tracker is an inventory; `queued` does not imply an approved SDRF.

PXD070086: 36 acquisitions in 12 condition/capture groups, all matched to the deposited design and ZIP archive. Official `parse_sdrf` validation, structural checks, and independent review passed. The first review found a control-label error; the corrected SDRF records non-antibody-conjugated control beads as stated in the paper and passed a fresh independent review.

PXD018217: 32 RAW acquisitions in four CHROMASS groups, four biological preparations and two technical injections each. Lys-C and Trypsin are both recorded as repeated cleavage columns. Official validation and independent review passed; unspecified search tolerances and modifications were left out.

PXD031548: 96 RAW acquisitions from eight ES-R1 biological preparations, six fractions, and two technical acquisition methods. Donor stage and hybrid strain trace to Cellosaurus CVCL_2167. Official validation, structural checks, and independent adversarial review passed; the sole ontology warnings concern the exact ES-R1 line and hybrid strain absent from the validator cache.

PXD074572 phosphoproteome: 32 acquisitions from 16 biological mouse hearts, each split into pY and TiO2 enriched peptide fractions. Official validation, structural and offline ontology checks, exact RAW mapping, byte-for-byte regeneration, and independent review passed.

PXD024280: 24 CHROMASS acquisitions (no-DNA, mock undamaged chromatin, UV-C chromatin; four biological preparations with duplicate technical injections) and 12 SCAI-IP acquisitions (IgG, SCAI-N, SCAI-C antibodies; four replicates each). Both SDRFs passed official validation, structural and offline checks, exact RAW mapping, byte-for-byte rebuild, and separate independent reviews. The C=no-DNA filename interpretation is documented as an inference from the paper comparison design.

PXD074572 EGFR-IP: 8 Bruker .d acquisitions, each matched to a deposited .d.zip stem and to the depositor run map. Published supplementary Methods explicitly confirm on-bead trypsin digestion. Official validation, structural and offline ontology checks, exact run mapping, byte-for-byte regeneration, and fresh independent review passed. With the earlier phosphoproteome commit, this accession is complete.

PXD056094: 28 ancient Egyptian artifact paint/adhesive RAW acquisitions. The publication distinguishes three AE1045/AE1060 Q Exactive HF runs from 25 Q Exactive HF-X runs; each instrument method has its own SDRF. Both record sequential Lys-C and Trypsin digestion and source-backed search settings. Mixed or analytically identified material taxa are not misrepresented as a single source organism. Official validation, structural checks, exact RAW mapping, byte-for-byte regeneration, and separate independent adversarial reviews passed.

PXD025523 human biopsy subset: 46 high-pH fractions from eight TMT reporter channels: three control donors and five ARVC donors, yielding 368 SDRF rows. The explicit channel-to-donor map is in the PRIDE protocol and its disease groups agree with the primary paper and processed workbook, resolving an apparent copied HCM sentence in the deposit. Both digestive enzymes are preserved as repeated columns. Official three-template validation, structural/offline checks, exact run mapping, byte-for-byte regeneration, and independent adversarial review passed. Twelve separate mouse RAW files remain unmapped to the six cKO and six control subjects, so the accession is partial.

PXD072522: depositor `metadata.txt` maps all 36 RAW acquisitions exactly to Naxian or Baxian specimens, tissue, taxonomic identification, and extraction buffer. The SDRF records distinct Baxian preparation groups and S1/S2 injections, per-run Exploris 480 or Fusion Lumos instrument, and unspecific enamel or Trypsin-digested dentine workflows. The exact Stegodon identification is retained in a custom characteristic while the controlled organism field uses broad Mammalia because Stegodon is absent from the validator taxonomy cache. Official validation, structural and offline checks, deterministic rebuild, exact RAW reconciliation, and independent review passed.

PXD014342: all 27 RAW files map to the Atapuerca Homo antecessor and Dmanisi Homo erectus fossil extracts or extraction blanks. Welker et al. Extended Data Table 1 links stage-tip numbers to specimen, tissue, extraction method, and Q Exactive HF or Fusion Lumos platform. The paper defines methods 1 and 3 as undigested and method 2 as Lys-C plus Trypsin. Blank method assignments follow paired consecutive stage-tip numbers and are marked as an inference in the evidence manifest. Official validation, structural/offline checks, exact RAW reconciliation, byte-for-byte rebuild, and independent review passed.
