# Copenhagen fifth 20 PXD batch

Selected on 2026-11-13 from confirmed entries in `copenhagen_candidates.tsv`, excluding the first three Copenhagen cohorts, existing local SDRFs and reports, accessions in the available community-corpus index or local corpus checkout, and the previously identified processed-only RAW mislabels PXD066727 and PXD066932. The cohort is ordered by the sum of accession-scoped PRIDE `fileSizeBytes` for entries classified as RAW. The cached PRIDE file list for every candidate was checked to contain its own accession. The reproducible selector is `scripts/select_copenhagen_fifth20.py`.

The [batch tracker](copenhagen_fifth20_batch.tsv) records the 20 selected PXDs and their annotation states. The cohort starts with PXD030529 (9,646,260,050 RAW-labelled bytes) and ends with PXD013725 (21,570,525,854 bytes). ZIP entries may contain multiple acquisitions; a RAW-labelled entry's internal file and sample mapping must be verified before writing an SDRF.

### PXD074049 evidence review

PRIDE lists 16 RAW files, fractions 1–16 of a single 32-channel TMTpro experiment. The deposited `experimental_design_Global.tsv` (https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2026/04/PXD074049/experimental_design_Global.tsv) gives all 32 channel-to-group assignments, including deuterated labels such as `127D`, `128CD`, and `135ND`. This accession is blocked on format/tool support, not missing biological mapping.

### PXD061135 evidence review

The deposited `Annotation-extended.xlsx` maps 72 eight-minute DDA runs and 49 five-minute DIA runs, including one zero-load control. PRIDE classifies 120 files as RAW and misclassifies one `.raw` acquisition as search output, so the true run count is 121. The workbook repeats the `_02` filename for two replicate rows of the 50 ng DIA preaccumulation condition; the row labelled replicate 1 maps to the deposited `_01` file. `scripts/build_pxd061135_inputs.py` records these two explicit corrections and generates separate builder input tables for DDA, DIA and the zero-load control.

The matching [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12322952/) (Harking et al., DOI `10.1021/acs.jproteome.5c00186`) identifies the instrument as Orbitrap Exploris 480. The PRIDE structured instrument and linked publication instead refer to an Astral Zoom study and conflict with this accession's title, file names and matching paper. Although the preparation paragraph describes PAC digestion without naming an enzyme, the paper's Results explicitly call the acquired DDA material a **HeLa tryptic digest** and its Methods call the DIA injections **HeLa tryptic peptides**. That evidence supports Trypsin for the [72-row DDA SDRF](../../annotations/PXD061135-dda.sdrf.tsv) and [48-row DIA SDRF](../../annotations/PXD061135-dia.sdrf.tsv); the [one-row zero-load control](../../annotations/PXD061135-dia-blank.sdrf.tsv) has `not applicable` cleavage. All three pass `parse_sdrf`, structure validation and independent review, with hash-bound approvals for DDA (`ffd49826...a011`), DIA (`f2b59b2f...2489`) and blank (`35ff7305...78f86`). The automated PRIDE reconciler still flags trypsin because it examines the deposit protocol rather than the matching paper. Offline ontology lookup warns for HeLa S3 and cervix, while Cellosaurus confirms HeLa S3 as CVCL_0058 of cervical origin.

### PXD025344 evidence review

PRIDE lists 21 RAW files from O-GlcNAc-enriched *Drosophila melanogaster* material. Its sample protocol says wild-type peptides were labeled with light dimethyl reagent and `ogadel.1` knockout peptides with medium deuterated dimethyl reagent, then mixed 1:1 before WGA enrichment and fractionation. The RAW names show twelve IEF fractions, eight high-pH fractions and one LWAC run, covering female and male sample preparations. The deposited acquisition therefore has **two biological channels per RAW**. A builder probe with `dimethyl light` failed ontology validation; using the generic valid `NT=Dimethyl Labeling;AC=PRIDE:0000513` for both channels caused `sdrf-tools build` to refuse the second source claiming the same RAW. Encoding each RAW as one sample would discard the WT/knockout distinction. This accession is blocked on a channel representation and builder limitation, not a missing channel-to-genotype assignment.
