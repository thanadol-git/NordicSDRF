Immunoprecipitation of EGFR and MS/MS analysis

Immunoprecipitation of EGFR

1mg of homogenized cardiac tissue was pre-cleared for 1h at 4◦C with
100µL (3mg) of Protein A Dynabeads ® (Invitrogen, 100.02D). Protein A
beads were pre-blocked with 1% BSA in PBS pH7.4 for 30 min at 4◦C prior
use. Precleared lysate was incubated with 0.8µg anti-EGFR mouse
preferred antibody (Cell Signaling Technology, CST 71655) overnight at
4◦C. Next, the antibody-antigen complex was immunoprecipitated with 60µL
of beads for 3h at 20 r.p.m. 4◦C. Beads were washed 4 times with 1mL of
Tris Buffered Solution (TBS) at pH 7.4 and stored at -20◦C until mass
spectrometry analysis.

On beads digestion protocol

Washed beads were incubated for 30 min with elution buffer 1 (2 M Urea,
50 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 2 mM DTT, 20 µg/ml trypsin) followed by a second
elution for 5 min with elution buffer 2 (2 M Urea, 50 mM Tris-HCl pH
7.5, 10 mM Chloroacetamide). Eluates were combined and further digested
at room temperature overnight. Tryptic peptide mixtures were acidified
to 1% TFA and loaded on Evotips (Evosep).

LC-MS/MS

Peptides were separated on 15 cm, 150 μM ID columns packed with C18
beads (1.9 μm) (Pepsep) on an Evosep ONE HPLC applying the ‘30 samples
per day’ method and injected via a CaptiveSpray source and 10 μm emitter
into a timsTOF pro mass spectrometer (Bruker) operated in PASEF mode
[7].

Data search and analysis

Raw MS data files were converted to mzML format using ProteoWizard
MSConverter tool. Files search was performed with FragPipe (v. 22.0)
using the “LFQ-Phospho” workflow for label-free quantification using
IonQuant [8] (v. 1.10.27) and site localization with PTM-Prophet.
