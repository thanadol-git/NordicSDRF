# Copenhagen next 20 PXD batch

Selected from `copenhagen_candidates.tsv` entries marked `confirmed`, excluding accessions already annotated here or present in the local community corpus. Size is the sum of PRIDE `fileSizeBytes` for files labelled `RAW` in the archive file list, as checked on 2026-11-10. The ordered cohort and per-accession outcome are in [copenhagen_next20_batch.tsv](copenhagen_next20_batch.tsv).

Two smaller PRIDE deposits were set aside before choosing the 20: PXD066727 labels a DIA-NN `.mzML.quant` processed quantification file as RAW, and PXD066932 labels five direct-infusion ASCII spectral exports as RAW without vendor or mzML acquisition files. Their declared RAW byte totals would misrepresent available acquisition data. The selected cohort begins at PXD007693 and ends at PXD042583.

**13 of 20 annotations are approved.** They passed the official SDRF parser and independent, hash-bound review. PXD062200 and PXD042583 encode sequential Lys-C and trypsin digestion as repeated cleavage-agent columns using the local builder extension at [build_repeated_cleavage.py](../../scripts/build_repeated_cleavage.py). PXD063178 has source-backed run-specific instrument assignments, and PXD058040 records the run-specific treatment as `not available` because the public files do not map it. The repository-wide review gate still fails because of unrelated pre-existing pending annotations and three drafts in this batch; the 13 approved files each have a current hash-bound approval receipt.

**7 of 20 remain blocked by public evidence or independent review.** PXD007963 and PXD013676 have draft SDRFs that failed review because of unsupported acquisition or label claims. PXD050370 has a draft that failed review because deposited metadata, MaxQuant and PEAKS disagree on which bleach RAW is the tooth and which is the extraction blank. PXD028982 and PXD024851 combine intact and peptide acquisition workflows without a complete per-run design. PXD071573 lacks a RAW-to-bait and wound-state map; the S01 archive's SampleInfo.xml confirms its instrument and method but not its biological condition. PXD083842 has one deposited RAW without a link to its liver/plasma source or cohort group, while the processed tables contain many samples. Each accession has a report under `annotations/<PXD>.report.md` with the exact gap. These seven should not be represented as completed SDRFs until the missing metadata is supplied or a source-backed resolution is found.
