# Copenhagen fourth 20 PXD batch

Selected on 2026-11-13 from confirmed Copenhagen candidates, excluding prior Copenhagen batches, existing local SDRFs and reports, and accessions in the live `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets` corpus. The cohort is ordered by the sum of accession-scoped PRIDE `fileSizeBytes` for files categorised as RAW. The two previously identified processed-only RAW mislabels, PXD066727 and PXD066932, remain excluded. The selected cohort starts with PXD004341 (9,830,389,512 bytes) and ends with PXD052074 (17,368,244,462 bytes).

The [batch tracker](copenhagen_fourth20_batch.tsv) records each accession's annotation and review state. A RAW-labelled ZIP or 7z file may contain several acquisitions; its contents and sample map must be verified before annotation.

Initial evidence review began with PXD035661. Its 28-file draft passed parser validation, but independent review identified unsupported acquisition and replicate assumptions. See [PXD035661 evidence report](../../annotations/PXD035661.report.md). It is recorded as blocked; no unapproved SDRF is included in the committed batch.

## Current annotation progress

- PXD018050: four HF-X archaeological runs passed independent review. The four HF reference runs remain blocked because the paper lists Trypsin, Elastase, and Chymotrypsin digestion without mapping each enzyme to a run. See [PXD018050 report](../../annotations/PXD018050.report.md).
- PXD063196: eight Drosophila Rab2 runs passed independent review. The deposited design maps four S20N and four Q65L samples, and the MaxQuant settings confirm the Drosophila database despite a copied human-database sentence in PRIDE.
- PXD068380: six SCAF1 interactome runs passed independent review. The deposited design maps three `rS1 KO4+` and three `rS1 KO4-` samples; those labels are retained verbatim because their precise molecular meaning is not defined in the design file.
- PXD047218: 24-run draft passed parser validation but failed independent review because the sample digestion used a Trypsin/Lys-C mix and the active builder emitted only Trypsin. The draft is in ignored scratchpad and is not ready for contribution. See [PXD047218 report](../../annotations/PXD047218.report.md).
- PXD045721: six LAD2 pulldown runs were built and validated, but independent review found that repeated `BR1` labels cannot be safely classified as technical replicates. The draft was moved to ignored scratchpad; see [PXD045721 report](../../annotations/PXD045721.report.md).
- PXD034106: 11 ancient beeswax runs and four polymer-contamination blank controls were built as separate SDRFs and passed independent review. The two queen-cell runs use sequential Lys-C and Trypsin digestion and remain unresolved with the current single-cleavage-agent builder.
- PXD052074: 12 left-atrium runs were mapped from the deposited overview, matching search outputs, and the raw archive index. The overview repeats one filename prefix in error; unique run IDs resolve all 12 mappings. A first independent review found omitted settings from the paper. The corrected SDRF passed validation and a fresh independent review.
- PXD053300: 16 targeted histone PRM runs were mapped to two clone codes, DMSO/dTAG comparison, and four biological replicates per group. The SDRF passed independent review. Validation reports an ontology warning for submitter-supplied `129/Ola` strain nomenclature. The deposit does not map clone codes to the two genotype descriptions.
- PXD009914: the deposited MaxQuant `SEARCH.zip` identifies eight accession-matched Chymotrypsin runs (four AdOx and four untreated) and their search modifications. The corrected eight-run SDRF passed independent review. The remaining 14 RAW files are not covered by that search archive and lack a run-level acquisition map; see [PXD009914 report](../../annotations/PXD009914.report.md).
- PXD049201: the deposited identified-proteins workbook yielded a complete 40-channel TMT map across four pools. The current builder drops one of the two sequential cleavage enzymes, and the three fraction suffixes lack an explicit ordering. The accession remains blocked; see [PXD049201 report](../../annotations/PXD049201.report.md).
- PXD019345: the 16 RAW files lack a deposited condition-to-run map, and the documented sequential LysC/Trypsin digest cannot be represented faithfully by the current builder. See [PXD019345 report](../../annotations/PXD019345.report.md).
- PXD025407: seven unlabeled HEK293 EThcD-only acquisitions at 10–40 NCE passed independent review after the Cellosaurus accession was moved into the required standard column. The approved SDRF covers 7 of 25 RAW files; 18 other RAW runs and one processed mzML remain for separate mapping. The offline validator warns that the EThcD PSI-MS term is absent from its local cache, but the independent reviewer verified MS:1002631 against the CV. The reviewer noted two minor omissions: published search mass tolerances and a factor-name mismatch with the collision-energy comment column. Approval receipt SHA-256: `6805f69da8904d9a4c730c4764391d6e933d33c66bba78e1b9f90f7a4f3ef4d8`.
- PXD068367: the deposited FragPipe peptide tables map all six RAW basenames and confirm dinitrotyrosine search chemistry. The experimental meaning of `aSN_1_4`, per-run TNM exposure, and optional dimethyl labeling remain undefined. See [PXD068367 report](../../annotations/PXD068367.report.md).
- PXD044505 and PXD044728: each accession has 16 numbered TMTpro fractions, but the publication and project records do not assign individual animal samples and hibernation states to reporter channels. The Trypsin/LysC mixture also needs a faithful builder representation. See the [bear report](../../annotations/PXD044505.report.md) and [squirrel report](../../annotations/PXD044728.report.md).
- PXD052232: three naming groups span label-free and TMT arms; neither run-level construct mapping nor TMT channel assignment is available in the inspected project record. See [PXD052232 report](../../annotations/PXD052232.report.md).
- PXD004341: 42 heterogeneous CHO runs need a verified fraction, instrument and acquisition map. See [PXD004341 report](../../annotations/PXD004341.report.md).
- PXD072200: the record documents DDA and DIA Bruker methods but does not identify which of the 45 deposited raw acquisitions used each mode or which tissue area and donor each code denotes. See [PXD072200 report](../../annotations/PXD072200.report.md).
- PXD022368: two GASP dental-calculus runs have an exact match between deposited filenames and the publication's sample table. Independent review found that the proposed DDA value was unsupported. The template rejects `not available` for acquisition mode, so the draft is in ignored scratchpad and the accession is blocked pending run-method evidence. See [PXD022368 report](../../annotations/PXD022368.report.md).
