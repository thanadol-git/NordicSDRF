## Findings

### F1 — important — design/spec: the row-uniqueness coordinate collides on 54 pairs of rows

- **Location:** all 108 rows. Columns `source name`, `characteristics[biological replicate]`, `comment[technical replicate]`, `comment[fraction identifier]`.
- **Evidence:**
  - Each DDA run and its SWATH counterpart share the same coordinate tuple. For example, `METH-01-rep1-SA-NT.d.rar` and `SWATH-METH-01-rep1-SA.d.rar` are both (`PFC_SA_METH_mouse1`, 1, 1, 1).
  - 54 of these tuples occur twice.
  - The spec key (source + assay + label) is unique, which is why parse_sdrf passes.
  - Repository invariant 10 says the `sdrf-annotated-datasets` review gate rejects such collisions.
- **Correction criterion:** zero duplicate coordinate tuples. Either:
  - split the file into a DDA SDRF and a SWATH SDRF (this also lets the SWATH file declare `dia-acquisition`); or
  - number SWATH technical replicates 4–6, if that is justified and documented.

### F2 — important — source evidence / status: the sample-to-file map is inferred, not stated

- **Location:** `source name`, `characteristics[biological replicate]` and `comment[technical replicate]` on all rows.
- **Evidence:**
  - The paper never states how many mice were profiled, which of the 5 per group were used, or that `rep1–3` are repeat injections of one digest. Process replicates are also possible.
  - The paper also never states that DDA `-0n-` and SWATH `-0n-` are the same animal. "Same samples" is stated only at the cohort level.
  - The producer itself labels these points INFERRED.
  - The interpretation is plausible: n ≤ 3 is below the group N of 5. But it rests only on filename convention.
- **Correction criterion:** cite stated evidence for the mapping, such as an author statement, PRIDE sample metadata, MaxQuant experimentalDesign/summary output, or `.d` headers. Otherwise the dataset cannot carry `annotated` under the campaign rule.

### F3 — important — source evidence: search parameters come from the paper's MaxQuant search, but the deposited outputs are `.dat` files (conventionally Mascot)

- **Location:** DDA rows. Columns `comment[modification parameters]` x3, `comment[fragment mass tolerance]` = 0.5 Da, `comment[precursor mass tolerance]`.
- **Evidence:**
  - PROXI lists 54 `.dat` "Search engine output file URI" files, one per DDA run.
  - The paper describes only MaxQuant/Andromeda. No MaxQuant output (txt/ or mqpar) is deposited.
  - The `.dat` headers (MODS, IT_MODS, TOL, ITOL) were not read by the producer. I could not read them either: the FTP is robots-blocked to WebFetch, and the shell has no internet.
  - PROXI's modification list (acetyl, carbamidomethyl) does not mention oxidation, which neither confirms nor refutes the SDRF.
  - The producer's report says paper prose was used "against the brief's precedence rule".
- **Correction criterion:** read one `.dat` header and either confirm the values or replace them with the deposited search's values. Otherwise leave the conflict documented and keep the status at sandbox.

### F4 — minor — inferred specificity: `PP=Protein N-term` on Acetyl

- The paper says only "N-terminal acetylation"; the protein N-term position is the MaxQuant default, not stated.
- Acceptable if F3 resolves it. Otherwise note it in the evidence record as inferred.

### F5 — minor — inferred value: `comment[dissociation method]` = CID is inferred from the instrument

- The compact Q-TOF fragments only by collision cell CID, so this is defensible.
- The label "CID" is an exact synonym of the OBO name "collision-induced dissociation".

### F6 — minor — the SWATH rows could use the more specific `NT=SWATH MS;AC=PRIDE:0000447`

- The paper calls the acquisition "SWATH mode", and PRIDE:0000447 is the more specific DIA descendant.

### F7 — minor — `factor value[treatment]` labels do not reuse the `characteristics[treatment]` strings

- The factor uses short group codes such as "METH-WD"; the characteristic uses long prose.
- This is consistent one-to-one (6 to 6) and not invalid, but the factor value would be clearer if it matched the characteristic or if the mapping were documented.

### F8 — minor — label case: "dithiothreitol (DTT)" and "iodoacetamide (IAA)"

- The OBO names are capitalised: "Dithiothreitol (DTT)" and "Iodoacetamide (IAA)".
- This matches the campaign convention and is harmless.

### No finding: values that checked out against the paper or PROXI

- organism, organism part, strain ICR, sex, developmental stage adult, disease normal, material type tissue;
- instrument; cleavage agent (Trypsin);
- reduction and alkylation reagents;
- ms1 scan range 150–2200 on DDA rows;
- the choice to leave the SWATH scan range and precursor tolerance "not available";
- the 6 treatment groups and their phase split;
- the choice not to declare `dia-acquisition` on a mixed file.
