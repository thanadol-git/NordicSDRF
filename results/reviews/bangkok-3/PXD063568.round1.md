## Findings

### F1 (important): the two piNSC lines cannot be told apart, and the factor merges them
- **Location:** all 24 rows whose source name starts `VSMUi002-B_*` or `VSMUi002-E_*`. Columns: `characteristics[*]`, `factor value[cell type]`, and the missing cell-line column.
- **Evidence:**
  - The paper (PMC12499565) analyses the lines separately. The ANOVA compares PTFs with each piNSC line (VSMUi002-B or VSMUi002-E). The SWATH analysis uses four groups: VSMUi002-B, VSMUi002-E, VSMUi002-B-NGs and VSMUi002-E-NGs.
  - In the SDRF, `VSMUi002-B_piNSC_R1` and `VSMUi002-E_piNSC_R1` are identical in every characteristics and factor column. Both also carry biological replicate 1.
  - The only factor, `factor value[cell type]`, therefore pools 6 runs into "neural stem cell" and 6 into "neural cell". A downstream tool would merge the two lines, which the paper treats as separate groups.
  - The rows assert `material type` = cell line but never name the line.
  - The cell-lines template is usable for these rows:
    - `characteristics[cell line]` has only a warning-level ontology check.
    - `characteristics[cellosaurus accession]` allows `not available`. Cellosaurus returns 0 hits for "VSMUi002".
  - TEMPLATES.adoc says the cell-lines template applies "when samples are cultured cell lines".
- **Correction criterion:**
  - Record the line identity (VSMUi002-B / VSMUi002-E) in a licensed characteristic. Preferred: `characteristics[cell line]` under cell-lines, declared on the piNSC and piNSC-NG rows, with cellosaurus accession `not available`. Any other licensed column is acceptable if the line is recorded.
  - Make the factor structure distinguish line as well as cell state, for example by adding `factor value[cell line]`, so that the five groups in the paper can be recovered from the factor values.
  - Re-run parse_sdrf once for each declared template, on the rows that declare it.
  - Optionally add `characteristics[passage number]` (3/20/21) once cell-lines licenses it.

### F2 (important): the de-duplication coordinate collides on all 15 sources
- **Location:** rows 2-16 versus rows 17-31. For example, PTF_R1 appears with assay PTF_R1 (DDA) and with assay PTF_R1_SW (SWATH).
- **Evidence:**
  - (`source name`, `biological replicate`, `technical replicate`, `fraction identifier`) is identical for the DDA row and the SWATH row of every source: tech rep = 1 and fraction = 1 everywhere. That gives 15 duplicate coordinates.
  - The repo's checklist (skills/sdrf-validate/SKILL.md:257, sdrf-knowledge:64) and the CLAUDE.md invariant 10 require this coordinate to be unique. CLAUDE.md states that the dataset review gate rejects collisions.
  - The spec defines technical replicate as "repeated measurements of the same sample (e.g., multiple injections)" (README.adoc:331).
- **Correction criterion:**
  - Make the coordinate unique per row in a way the spec supports. For example, number the two injections of each source as technical replicate 1 (DDA) and 2 (SWATH), with a stated rationale that they are separate injections of the same sample.
  - Alternatively, document an explicit waiver that the contribution gate accepts.
  - Do not invent fractions.

### F3 (minor): the DIA rows use a generic term where the paper names SWATH
- **Location:** 15 `*_SW` rows, `comment[proteomics data acquisition method]`.
- **Evidence:** the paper says DIA was performed in SWATH mode. PRIDE:0000447 "SWATH MS" is_a PRIDE:0000450.
- **Correction criterion:** either use `NT=SWATH MS;AC=PRIDE:0000447` or keep the generic DIA term deliberately.
- **Related note:** SWATH window parameters (150, 2200, 25) cannot be recorded without dia-acquisition, and that template rejects the DDA rows. If templates are declared per row, dia-acquisition could be declared on the SW rows only. This is optional.

### F4 (minor): organism part is `not applicable` on derived cells
- **Location:** 24 piNSC and piNSC-NG rows.
- **Evidence:** the lines were reprogrammed from tail fibroblasts of the same piglet. The paper states that PTFs "served as the parental somatic cell source".
- **Correction criterion:** keep the value, or record the origin, for example tail via `sampling site` under cell-lines. This is a consistency choice, not an error.

### F5 (minor): treatment is free text
- **Location:** 6 NG rows, `characteristics[treatment]`.
- **Evidence:** TERMS.tsv lists NCIT and EFO as the preferred values for this column. The current value is free text and is accurate to the paper.
- **Correction criterion:** optionally map it to an ontology term. Acceptable as is.

### F6 (minor): provenance of the DDA tolerances
- **Location:** 15 DDA rows, mass tolerance columns.
- **Evidence:**
  - The values 0.25 Da and 0.5 Da match the MaxQuant parameters in the paper.
  - The archive holds Mascot `.dat` search results, which the paper does not mention.
- **Correction criterion:** none required. Optionally note the discrepancy in the report.
