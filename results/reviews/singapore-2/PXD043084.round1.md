## Findings

No blocker or important findings.

1. **minor** (template semantics). Location: `comment[sdrf template]` NT=clinical-metadata, together with `characteristics[genotype]` / `factor value[genotype]`.
   - Evidence: clinical-metadata is described as being "for clinical study samples", and its genotype column is the "genotype of the subject". Here it is declared only so the column can hold an engineered transgene construct in a cell line. It validates and follows the PXD054300 precedent, but it is a semantic stretch.
   - Correction criterion: optional. Keep it if campaign policy accepts the precedent. Otherwise, consider an alternative encoding the spec sanctions for engineered constructs, such as `characteristics[genetic modification]` if one exists in a future template.
2. **minor** (free text in ontology-validated columns; warning level). Location: `characteristics[treatment]`, `characteristics[enrichment process]` and `characteristics[culture medium]` on all rows.
   - Evidence: sample-metadata/cell-lines YAML attach `ontology` validators with `error_level: warning`. Enrichment expects a child of EFO:0009090; no proximity-labelling/streptavidin child exists in pride_cv.obo. Full OLS validation would emit warnings.
   - Correction criterion: optional. Shorten to ontology-resolvable heads where possible (for example, culture medium `Dulbecco's Modified Eagle Medium` per NCIT). Free text is acceptable where no term exists.
3. **minor** (verbosity). Location: `factor value[genotype]`.
   - Evidence: the factor levels embed vector and selection details ("dox-inducible lentiviral pTRIPZ, stably transduced, puromycin-selected"), which are identical across the two levels.
   - Correction criterion: optional. Shorter factor levels (for example, `MYC-BirA*-ZNF524 wild type` vs `MYC-BirA*-NLS control`) would help downstream grouping. The characteristics column can keep the full detail.
4. **minor** (unverified from deposited output). Location: `comment[modification parameters]`.
   - Evidence: the modifications are taken from the paper Methods. The deposited MaxQuant zip (202 MB) was not opened, by the producer or by this review.
   - Correction criterion: none required. The Methods statement is explicit and dataset-specific ("for BioID ZNF524 WT vs NLS: version 2.0.1.0" shares the same search settings paragraph).

## Notes

- The treatment dose (300 vs 40 ng/mL) is perfectly confounded with the bait construct. This is inherent to the published design (doses titrated to equalise expression) and is correctly recorded as a per-arm characteristic, not as a separate factor.
