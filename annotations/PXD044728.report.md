# PXD044728: TMTpro sample-to-channel map needed

The accession deposits 16 RAW files, with exact `Fr1` through `Fr16` suffixes, for one fractionated TMTpro plex of thirteen-lined ground-squirrel skeletal-muscle fibre samples. The [publication](https://elifesciences.org/articles/94616.pdf) describes summer active, interbout arousal and torpor groups and a Trypsin/LysC digest, and the [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD044728) names the same workflow. Neither source assigns individual animals or physiological states to TMTpro reporter channels in the deposited plex. `PD_output.zip` is present in the accession, but a channel-to-sample design table has not been recovered from it.

An SDRF row per channel and raw fraction would require that map. Inferring it from the group counts would risk assigning the wrong animal or state to a reporter channel. The active builder also has only one cleavage-agent field and does not faithfully represent the documented Trypsin/LysC mixture. No SDRF was added.

Required evidence: the PD reporter-channel design or depositor sample sheet, plus a reviewed representation of the dual-enzyme digestion. The 16 fraction filenames themselves are unambiguous and are preserved in `scratchpad/PXD044728/files.json`.
