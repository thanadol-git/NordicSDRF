# PXD015071 annotation blocked

PXD015071 is "In vivo hyperglycemia exposure elicits distinct period-dependent effects on human pancreatic progenitor differentiation, conveyed by oxidative stress" from Simona Chera's lab, Department of Clinical Science, University of Bergen, Norway (submitter Thomas Aga Legøy). The paper is Legøy TA et al., Acta Physiol (Oxf) 2020;228(4):e13433 (PMID 31872528, PMC7078042, NIHMS1065751, doi:10.1111/apha.13433). It is a TMT 10/11-plex, multi-notch SPS-MS3 study. hiPSC-derived pancreatic progenitors (stage 5) were alginate-encapsulated and xenotransplanted intraperitoneally into normoglycaemic or diphtheria-toxin-diabetic male NSG RIP-DTR mice. They were retrieved after 1 week (1w-postTX) or 4 weeks (4w-postTX), and human islets from deceased donors served as a reference. It is not yet annotated upstream: `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets` `datasets/PXD015071` and `sandbox/PXD015071` both return 404.

## Blocker

The record has no TMT channel-to-sample map. An SDRF for this deposit needs one row per reporter channel per RAW file, each with a sample identity: diabetic host, normoglycaemic host or human islet donor, plus the mouse or donor number. Neither the channel order nor the exact plex composition is stated anywhere I could reach. Assigning TMT126…TMT131(C) to samples without evidence would be invention, so no SDRF is emitted.

What the record does say only bounds the composition:
- **Results:** encapsulated cells from diabetic (D) and normoglycaemic (N) hosts were compared at 1 and 4 weeks "using tandem mass tag (TMT) 10-plex proteomics". The 1-week D/N transplants were also compared with human islets "by TMT 10-plex-based quantitative proteomics".
- **Methods:** "Tandem Mass Tag (TMT) 11-plex labeling". The PRIDE data-processing text says "normalized assuming equal protein loading of all 10 samples". The RAW method file is named `..._TMT10_...`. So the plex is either 10 or 11 channels, and the record contradicts itself.
- **Methods:** male RIP-DTR NSG mice (8–12 weeks) "were separated into two groups, six mice per group". Islets came "from 8 donors, men and women between 41–63 years old (Supp. Table 1) … one of the human islet samples was excluded due to technical issues."
- **Figures:** Figure 3A and 4A draw one TMT 10-plex per time point (1 WEEK postTX and 4 WEEKS postTX; diabetic vs normal host). The Figure 3C PCA shows human islet (I), TX-in-diabetic (D) and TX-in-normal (N) points with no per-sample labels. Nothing states whether the 4-week plex also holds islets, how the 7 retained islet donors split between plexes, or the channel order.

## What was established

- **Files:** 28 Thermo RAW files and 28 mzIdentML files (Sequest + in-house LDA). The PRIDE v2 `/files` and v3 `files/all` lists agree, and every entry's `projectAccessions` contains PXD015071. The mzIdentML files hold only software, sequences and PSMs, with no sample or channel section.
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Fusion Lumos with EASY-nLC 1200, confirmed from the RAW headers. This matches PRIDE `MS:1002732`.
- **Plex and fraction structure (range-read RAW headers):** each header carries a sample ID `tal_<plex>_<fraction>[b]` and the generic sample name `Sample048`. This gives two plexes, A and C, each with 7 fractions, each acquired twice. That matches the paper's "analyzed in duplicate, one with advanced peak determination (APD) activated and a second run with this option off":

  | Plex | APD on (`Iso07_100u_120min_TMT10_60_NoLM_cosmo.meth`) | APD off (`..._NoLM_NoAPD_cosmo.meth`) |
  |---|---|---|
  | A | a12995_20190317181753 (A_01), a12996–a13000 (A_02–A_06), a13015 (A_07) | a13019–a13024 (A_01b–A_06b), a13040 (A_07b) |
  | C | a13009–a13013 (C_01–C_05), **a13013_20190319140120 (C_06)**, a13017 (C_07) | a13033–a13038 (C_01b–C_06b), a13042 (C_07b) |

  - The deposited `a13013_20190319140120.raw` has original name `a13013` in its header, but its sample ID is `tal_C_06` (autosampler row 185, whereas `a13013.raw` is row 184). It is fraction 6 of plex C, not a re-injection of fraction 5.
  - The paper reports 6 combined high-pH fractions, but each series has 7 runs. Whether the 7th run is a further fraction or a re-injection is not documented.
  - Which plex is the 1-week and which the 4-week experiment is not stated. The labels "A"/"C" and the figures suggest one plex per time point, but this is not evidenced.
  - The autosampler rows (A: 166–171, 187; C: 180–185, 189) interleave with the rows reported for the sibling Bergen deposit PXD015955 (173–178, 188). The plexes were therefore run in one batch, but this adds nothing to the channel map.
- **Search settings (PRIDE data-processing protocol):** TMT (+229.163 Da) on K and peptide N-term, fixed; carbamidomethyl C, fixed; oxidation M, variable; 50 ppm precursor tolerance; 0.9 Da fragment tolerance; human UniProt; MS2 CID in the ion trap, MS3 HCD in the Orbitrap. The cleavage agent is not stated in the PRIDE text or the paper's Methods, which cite earlier work.
- **Sample-level facts:** organism Homo sapiens (transplanted hiPSC-derived cells; islet donors). The host mice were male NOD.Cg-Prkdc^scid Il2rg^tm1Wjl Tg(Ins2-HBEGF)6832Ugfm/Sz (NSG RIP-DTR), consistent with the MeSH terms "Male", "Mice, Transgenic". The islet donors were 41–63 years, of both sexes, with per-donor values only in the unreachable Supp. Table 1.

## Channel-map ladder checked

1. **Paper and supplementary:** full text read via the NCBI BioC API (PMC7078042 author manuscript) and the PMC article page. Figures 3 and 4 were inspected; they have no channel table. Supplementary items are Supp_FigS1-3.pdf, Supp_TableS1.pdf (islet donors), Supp_TableS2.xlsx and Supp_TableS3.xlsx (DEP tables). Every download route was blocked:
   - `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/instance/7078042/bin/…` and `www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/…/bin/…`: reCAPTCHA challenge page.
   - `europepmc.org/articles/PMC7078042/bin/…`: HTTP 403.
   - Wiley `onlinelibrary.wiley.com` article and `downloadSupplement`: Cloudflare 403.
   - The PMC open-data S3 bucket holds only the article `.xml`/`.txt`/`.json`.
   - Unread, so it is unknown whether Table S2/S3 carry per-channel column headers.
2. **Deposited PRIDE files** (v2 and v3, 56 files, `.raw` and `.mzIdentML` only): no sample sheet, design file or quantification table. The RAW sample ID gives only plex and fraction, and the sample name is generic.
3. **Europe PMC `supplementaryFiles`** for PMC7078042: "Article with id PMC7078042 is not open access one".
4. **Data Availability / external repositories:** the paper cites only PXD015071, GEO GSE141309 (RNA-seq of S5 cells and islets, not TMT) and PXD012081 (the in vitro Wnt5a samples, a separate deposit). There is no Zenodo, figshare, GitHub or other repository. A Europe PMC full-text search for "PXD015071" returns only this paper, and there is no preprint.
5. **Cross-validation:** not possible, because nothing could be reconstructed.

## What would unblock it

- **A per-channel table for plex A and plex C** (TMT126…TMT131/131C → sample), from the submitters (Thomas Aga Legøy, tle088@uib.no; Simona Chera, simona.chera@uib.no) or from Joao A. Paulo (Harvard Medical School), who ran the samples. For each channel it should give the time point (1w/4w-postTX), host glycaemia (diabetic/normoglycaemic), mouse number or islet donor ID, and any empty or excluded channels.
- **Supplementary Tables S1–S3** fetched by a human through a browser. They may carry per-sample column headers and islet donor sex and age, but they will only unblock the annotation if they tie samples to reporter channels.
- **A note on the 7th run in each series**: a further fraction or a re-injection.

With the map, the deposit becomes one SDRF of 28 runs × 10 (or 11) channels, 280–308 rows. Templates would be `ms-proteomics` + `human` (+ `cell-lines` for the hiPSC-derived grafts). APD on/off would be recorded per run, with factor values for host glycaemia and time post-transplant.
