# PXD034803 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Heimbürger SMN, …, Gorshkov V, Kjeldsen F, Færgeman NJ, …, Knop FK. "GIP Affects Hepatic Fat and Brown Adipose Tissue Thermogenesis but Not White Adipose Tissue Transcriptome in Type 1 Diabetes". J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2022;107(12):3261-3274, doi:10.1210/clinem/dgac542, PMID 36111559 (not in PMC; PRIDE protocols and the submitter's `giphot.sdrf.tsv` used). Proteomics at SDU Odense (Kjeldsen). Randomised, placebo-controlled, double-blind crossover: 20 men with type 1 diabetes received 6-day s.c. GIP (6 pmol/kg/min) and placebo (saline) infusions with a washout; plasma on days 0, 1 and 6 of each period. Label-free DIA (Exploris 480, Spectronaut 14) of individual plasma samples, plus a DDA spectral library from pooled plasma (depleted and undepleted, high-pH fractionated).

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + human v1.1.0 + clinical-metadata v1.1.0 (`individual`).

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 172 files: 162 RAW + mzTab/Spectronaut/FASTA/lipidomics/`giphot.sdrf.tsv`. All 162 RAW annotated: 120 DIA runs (`Giphot_<patient>_<day><A|B>`, 20 × 3 days × 2 periods) and 42 DDA library runs (`LibDepleted_01-20, _FT` and `LibRaw_01-20, _FT`). The submitter SDRF covers only the 120 individual runs. List matched PRIDE by SHA-256.

## Sample map
Patient, day and treatment per run from the submitter's `giphot.sdrf.tsv` (`factor value[treatment|day|individual]`). A/B is the crossover period; its treatment varies by patient (randomised order), and the submitter file gives it per run. **For patients 2 and 4 the submitter file gives the same treatment to both periods** (patient 2: placebo/placebo; patient 4: GIP/GIP), which contradicts the crossover design; these 12 runs are annotated with `treatment = not available` (period kept in `comment[study period]`) rather than guessing which period was which. Library runs: one pooled sample per preparation (pool of 24 randomly chosen individual samples), fractions 1–20 + flow-through (21).

## Key decisions
- Instrument Orbitrap Exploris 480 (RAW headers: `…_DIA_60x10mz_2sLoop.meth` for individuals, `…_30K.meth` for the library).
- Individual runs DIA (PRIDE:0000450); library DDA with high-pH RP (PRIDE:0000564), 20 concatenated fractions + flow-through.
- Lys-C + trypsin. Modifications from the PD search: Carbamidomethyl C fixed, Oxidation M variable (PD: 5 ppm / 0.02 Da, semi-tryptic). No reduction/alkylation reagent is described in the protocol, so both are `not available`.
- Disease type 1 diabetes mellitus (MONDO:0005147); male; adult; organism part blood plasma. Depleted library: `enrichment process = abundant-protein depletion` (depletion kit not named).
- Factors: treatment and time (study day).
