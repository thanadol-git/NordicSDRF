## Design

Label-free DDA of wild bilberry (*Vaccinium myrtillus*) fruit at four ripening stages encoded in the RAW names (`GreenS2`, `GreenS3`, `PurpleS4`, `BlueS5`). Three biological × three technical replicates per stage = 36 files. 12 unique sources.

| Stage token | Characteristic / factor label | Bio × tech | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| GreenS2 | small green fruit S2 | 3 × 3 | 9 |
| GreenS3 | large green fruit S3 | 3 × 3 | 9 |
| PurpleS4 | purple ripening fruit S4 | 3 × 3 | 9 |
| BlueS5 | ripe blue fruit S5 | 3 × 3 | 9 |

The PRIDE description also mentions a white-colored mutant at the ripe stage. **No corresponding RAW files are in the deposit.** Those samples were not invented.

Filename map: `211202_##_Bil_<Stage>_<N>Bio_<T>.raw` → stage, biological replicate N, technical replicate T.

## Conservative choices

- **Developmental stage:** OLS/PO has no clean 1:1 term for the four bilberry colour stages (tomato/BBCH synonyms would over-map). Used the paper/PRIDE stage names as characteristics and the same as the factor. Validator warns they are not EFO labels.
- **Organism part:** `fruit` (BTO:0000486). PRIDE also lists `plant cell` (CL:0000610); ignored as too generic for a whole-berry extract.
- **Cell type / disease:** `not available` (whole-fruit homogenate; no disease recorded).
- **Strain or breed:** `wild`. **Growth condition:** `natural forest, Oulu, Finland` (sample-processing protocol).
- **White mutant:** omitted (not in RAW list).
- **Alkylation reagent:** `not available`. Search used carbamidomethyl as static, but the wet-lab protocol does not name iodoacetamide. Reduction is DTT (in the extraction buffer).
- **N-terminal acetylation:** `NT=Acetyl;AC=UNIMOD:1;PP=Protein N-term;MT=Variable` — PD-default reading of “acetylation for N-terminal modification”; peptide N-term is possible but not stated.
- **Deamidation:** two columns, `TA=N` and `TA=Q` (UNIMOD:7).
- **Dissociation:** HCD → `NT=beam-type collision-induced dissociation;AC=MS:1000422` (not MS:1002481). Collision energy `30% NCE`.
- **MS1 range:** `375m/z-1500m/z` from the full-scan window in the data-processing protocol.
