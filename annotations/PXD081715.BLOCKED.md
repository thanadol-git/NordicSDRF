# PXD081715 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

No deposited file or paper text maps the TMTpro channels (127N-134N, plus 126 pool) to the three conditions (DMEM control, ODM, ODM + Go6976) or to biological replicates. The file-to-fraction/experiment mapping is also unresolved. A channel-to-sample map and a run-to-fraction map cannot be written without inventing them.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE v2 project record and the complete file list (pages 0-1; 29 entries: 28 RAW + 1 `ID24_0002_Morsczeck_Merged.pdResult`; page 1 empty). No SDRF-like, mqpar, sample-table or manifest files are deposited.
- Paper (PMC13509162, Biomedicines 2026, 14(8):1769): states 3 conditions, n = 3 biological replicates, "tags 127N through 134N" plus a 126 pool, seven high-pH fractions. It gives no channel-to-condition table. 3 x 3 = 9 samples does not equal the 8 channels named (127N-134N is 15 TMTpro channels, not 8), so the stated range cannot be used to infer the layout.
- Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21128464 (`ID24_0002_Morsczeck_EX3_Peptide_merge.xlsx`): processed table with columns "Abundance Ratio: (127N)...(132C) / (126)" only, no sample names.
- `.pdResult` SQLite StudyDefinition (first 4 MB read): Factors empty; each of the 10 `ID24_0002_Morsczeck_EX3_A2..A11` file sets has FractionNumber=1 (they are not seven fractions of one injection); study variables are only "Quan Channel" and "Sample Type"; no condition/replicate names.
- RAW headers (first 3 MB) show two distinct acquisitions: `ID24_0002_Morsczeck_EX3_A*` on Orbitrap Exploris 480 (HCD_OTOT_60k_50k_TMT_A8_90min.meth, 90 min), and `CM_JAN_A3..A11` plus their `_2` re-runs on Orbitrap Astral (60 min DDA, HCD NCE 35, method dated 2025). The paper describes only the Astral method and Proteome Discoverer 3.0.1.27/UniProt 2024, whereas PRIDE protocol text describes PD 2.4.1/UniProt 2019 and the PRIDE record lists Exploris 480. 28 RAW files (10 Exploris + 9 Astral + 9 Astral `_2`) do not fit "seven fractions" and cannot be assigned to experiments or fractions from any deposited source.

No deposited community SDRF was consulted.

## Evidence needed to unblock

A submitter-provided table giving, for each RAW file, the TMTpro channel to sample/condition/replicate map (including which file series corresponds to which experiment and the fraction number of each run), or a PD study definition with named conditions.
