Checked: 2026-09-26

## Evidence

- ProteomeXchange record: https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/ui?pxid=PXD063099
  - Title: *DEqMS - a method for differential protein expression analysis of quantitative mass spectrometry data*.
  - Human, DIA, label-free; two instrument platforms (timsTOF series and Orbitrap Exploris 480).
  - 149 files total, including 144 raw files. The indexed page shows only a subset, with names such as `230627_DeqMS_DIA_72sample-mix_90min_500ngsample2B.raw` and `OLENA_DeqMS_DIA_44min_sample8F_23-08-24_7952.d.zip`.
- Publication: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41596-026-01349-7
  - Identifies PXD063099 as the interferon experiment dataset, while also discussing other benchmark datasets.

## Blocker

The public project page does not expose all 144 raw names or a complete crosswalk from each encoded sample position to interferon/control condition, biological sample, instrument arm, and replicate. The paper identifies the dataset at project level but does not provide a run-level map in the web-visible text. A complete, non-inferred file mapping therefore cannot be established.

No SDRF was written or validated.
