# PXD006271 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Proteome Analysis of Human Embryonic Stem Cells Organelles. Shekari F et al., J Proteomics 2017;162:108-118, PMID 28435121, DOI 10.1016/j.jprot.2017.04.017 (Royan Institute, Tehran). The PX record links this paper and the same paper's abstract describes exactly the 6 fractions deposited. Companion Data in Brief article by the same authors: Shekari F et al., Data Brief 2017;13:371-376, DOI 10.1016/j.dib.2017.05.036, PMC5480819. It states that its data relate to the J Proteomics organelle paper. It does not cite a PXD.

## Scope, evidence and decisions
**Status: sandbox.** 18 rows x 40 columns. Templates: ms-proteomics, human, cell-lines. Label-free.

- **Deposited vs annotated:** PROXI v4 (fetched 2026-11-27) lists 54 files: 18 `.mzml` (as "Associated raw file URI"), 18 `.mgf` and 18 Mascot `.xml`. The OmicsDI mirror shows the same 54. There are no vendor `.wiff` files, and the submission type is PARTIAL ("Unsupported dataset by repository"). The 18 mzML = 6 fractions (Nucleus, Mitochondria, Crudemembrane, Cytoplasm, Heavymicrosome, Lightmicrosome) x rep1-3. All 18 are annotated: 18/18. `comment[data file]` = mzML. The mgf and xml files are derived files and are not listed. The PRIDE FTP listing and the PRIDE v3 API were unreachable (upstream timeout), so the file list was checked against PROXI and OmicsDI only.
- **Sample:** H9 hESC. Stated in the PRIDE sample protocol ("freshly harvested H9 hESCs") and in the Data in Brief article (WiCell H9). Cellosaurus CVCL_9773 (ID WA09; SX Female, checked live). Cell type: embryonic stem cell. Disease: normal.
- **Subcellular fraction:** encoded in source name and assay name, and in `characteristics[organism part]` as GO cellular-component labels: nucleus, mitochondrion, membrane (= crude membrane), cytoplasm, microsome (= both heavy and light microsome). The template validates organism part against UBERON/BTO at warning level only, which follows the precedent of PXD020870 "cell nucleus". `factor value[organism part]` repeats it. Heavy and light microsome therefore share the same factor value. They are distinguished only by source name.
- **Replicates:** rep1-3 = biological replicates. INFERRED from the Data in Brief statement "three biological replicates of the hESC H9 cell line", which that article makes for its whole-cell/mitochondrial data.
- **Instrument:** TripleTOF 5600 (MS:1000932), from the PX record, the protocol and the abstract. No raw files were read (none deposited).
- **Search (PRIDE data protocol, stated):** Mascot 2.3.02, IPI human 3.87. Variable modifications: Deamidated (NQ, UNIMOD:7), Methylthio (C, UNIMOD:39), Oxidation (M, UNIMOD:35). Two missed cleavages, peptide tolerance 10 ppm. Methylthio is Variable as searched, even though MMTS alkylation is used. The PX "No PTMs" field is overridden by the data protocol.
- **Reagents:** TCEP (PRIDE:0000609) and MMTS (PRIDE:0000605), trypsin. These come from the companion Data in Brief Methods, not from the J Proteomics Methods. They agree with the Methylthio search modification.
- **Culture medium:** DMEM/F-12 + 20% KSR + 4 ng/ml bFGF on MEF feeders, from the Data in Brief article (same lab, same line). Entered as free text.
- **Fractionation:** the protocol says "Gel-assisted tryptic digested". GAD is the Chen YJ lab method (Chen and Han are co-authors): proteins are embedded in a polyacrylamide gel without electrophoretic separation. There is one file per sample and no fraction files. Set to `not applicable`, with fraction identifier 1. If the J Proteomics Methods instead show SDS-PAGE lanes sliced and merged per sample, change this to `NT=SDS PAGE;AC=PRIDE:0000568`.

## Stated vs INFERRED
- **Stated:** H9; the 6 fractions; TripleTOF 5600; trypsin; Mascot modifications; 10 ppm.
- **INFERRED:**
  - Data-dependent acquisition (IDA on TripleTOF 5600; acquisition mode not stated).
  - beam-type CID (TripleTOF CID).
  - rep = biological replicate.
  - TCEP/MMTS and culture medium, taken from the companion article.
  - No electrophoretic fractionation.
