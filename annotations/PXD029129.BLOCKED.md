# PXD029129 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Proteome of neutrophils upon migration towards inflamed synovial fluid
- **PRIDE record:** PXD029129
- **Publication:** Arve-Butler S. et al., Front Immunol 2022, DOI [10.3389/fimmu.2021.795260](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2021.795260), PMCID [PMC8792960](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8792960/)

## Evidence recovered

- 20 RAW files `20210706_LFQ_Sabine_090…133.raw` (Orbitrap Fusion, HCD, verified in RAW headers) plus 21 mzML and one mzIdentML.
- Design (PRIDE and paper): healthy-donor neutrophils migrated through a synovial-membrane transwell model towards 10 % synovial fluid from three JIA patients, or incubated in the same fluid without migration; triplicates → 18 samples.
- RAW headers carry only the instrument sequence name (AM_2107_090…) with empty sample-information fields.
- Supplementary Table 1 (leukocyte counts) and Table 2 (fold changes) do not name runs.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Nothing links a run number to migrated vs non-migrated neutrophils or to the synovial-fluid donor, and 20 runs for 18 described samples leaves two runs unexplained (possibly normal-serum controls). The condition factor cannot be assigned per row.
