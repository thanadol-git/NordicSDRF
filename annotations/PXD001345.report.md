# PXD001345 annotation report

Secretome (and a small intracellular set) of *Enterococcus faecalis* V583 after 1.5 h in 2xYT medium or pooled sterile human urine. NMBU, Ås. Paper: PLoS One 2015, PMC4411035 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 24 rows, one per raw file.
- **Fractions:** each SDS-PAGE lane was cut into 10 pieces and pooled 3–4 at a time into 3 LC-MS samples. File tags `_1_3`, `_4_6` and `_7_10` are fractions 1–3, matching the deposited MaxQuant `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt`.
- **Secretome samples** `A`–`F`: three biological replicates per medium, run on three different days.
  - The A–C = 2xYT and D–F = urine assignment is inferred from the paper's Fig. 2 legend ("three 2xYT replicates (A–C) and the three urine replicates (D–F)").
  - The MaxQuant experiment names use the same letters.
- **Intracellular samples** `IC_E` and `IC_F` (MaxQuant experiments E and F) are not described in the paper. Following the letter scheme they are annotated as cell pellets from urine replicates E and F (biological replicates 2 and 3). **Please check.**
