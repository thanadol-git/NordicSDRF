# PXD006458 annotation report

Super-SILAC proteome profiling of CD138+ plasma cells from multiple myeloma (MM) and MGUS patients, and of myeloma/lymphoma cell lines. Slupphaug lab, NTNU. Paper: J Transl Med 2024, PMC11162064 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 414 rows: 207 raw files × 2 channels.
- **Channels:** the deposited MaxQuant names are `<date>_SS+<sample>` / `<sample>+SS`.
  - Every run is a light sample spiked with the heavy (Lys8/Arg10) super-SILAC standard.
  - The standard is made of IH-1, INA-6, KJON, OH-2, VOLIN, RPMI-8226-LR5, Karpas-422 and Ramos.
- **Light channel:**
  - **Patient samples:** patient ID and diagnosis (MM/MGUS) come from the file name; `run1–3` are technical replicates (paper: "at least three technical replicates").
  - **Repeat runs:** patients re-run in 2017 (`…_20170516`/`…_20170620`, `20288MM2015`, `35610MM2013`) share the patient's source name and continue the technical-replicate numbering.
  - **Cell-line runs:** ANBL-6, IH-1, INA-6, U-266, KJON, OH-2, VOLIN, LR5, Karpas-422 and Ramos.
  - **Cell-line replicates:** the `1`/`2` suffixes (e.g. `KJON1`, `OH2_2`, `INA6_2`) and later single re-runs are encoded as separate biological replicates.
- **Heavy channel:** a `super-SILAC_standard` source, with the component lines in `pooled sample`.

## Values
- **Patients:** bone marrow, `plasma cell`. Disease is `plasma cell myeloma` or `monoclonal gammopathy of uncertain significance`. Sex and age are `not available`; per-patient data are only in the paper's Supplementary Table 1 PDF.
- **Cell lines:** Cellosaurus accessions, age, sex and disease.
- **Factor:** `disease`.
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Elite, from the raw header; CID.
- **Search** (deposited MaxQuant 1.6.0.1, multiplicity 2, Arg10;Lys8):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidation NQ variable.
  - ITMS MS/MS 0.5 Da.
- **Overlap with PXD003138:** all 159 raw files of PXD003138 are re-deposited here. Both SDRFs are provided because each PXD is annotated on its own.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags trypsin as not named in the PRIDE record. The deposited MaxQuant search uses Trypsin/P, and the companion PXD003138 protocol specifies trypsin; this is a false positive.
