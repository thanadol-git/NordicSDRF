RAW files `EN_T1-3` and `RG_T1-3`: **`EN` = ENRICH-iST group (stated: "enrichment ... (n = 3)")**; `RG` is not explained, and is read as the conventional-extraction group (the only other group, n = 3). Factor: `factor value[enrichment process]`. The analysed proteins are of human origin (serum) although the carrier membrane is porcine; organism is written as Homo sapiens (the search database).
