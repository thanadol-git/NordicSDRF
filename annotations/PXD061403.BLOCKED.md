# PXD061403 annotation blocked

The deposit describes two HDX-MS experiments and supplies condition tables, but all acquisitions are hidden inside two large ZIP archives (`RAW_RUN1_PARP15.zip` and `RAW_Run2_PARP15.zip`). The PRIDE file API does not enumerate their internal RAW filenames, and the condition tables do not map archive members to construct, ligand, exchange time, replicate and blank status.

Treating either ZIP as a single acquisition would collapse a multi-condition HDX design and violate SDRF run coverage.

To unblock: provide archive member inventories plus a mapping from every RAW file to experiment, PARP15 construct, EB-47 condition, exchange time, biological/technical replicate and blank/control status.

Independent review: not applicable; no SDRF draft was produced.
