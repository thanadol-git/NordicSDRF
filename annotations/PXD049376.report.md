- Precursor tolerance not available; fragment 20 ppm from parameters.txt. Collision energy 28 given as NCE (inferred unit).
- Organism part, developmental stage, strain and growth condition are not available (not stated for the source potato material; PRIDE lists only "plant cell").
- Reduction (DTT) / alkylation (iodoacetamide) reagents are described only by reference to earlier papers; not recorded.
- Search archives (zips) not downloaded. Paper not read; PRIDE protocol used.
