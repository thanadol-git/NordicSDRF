# PXD065743 annotation report

## Scope

The SDRF represents every channel of the deposited nine-channel TMT 10-plex across all eight RAW fraction files (72 assay rows).

## Evidence and decisions

- The deposited `experiment_annotation.tsv` maps channels to DMSO, S1, and S3 samples, each in triplicate. S1 and S3 are retained verbatim because the table does not define those treatment codes.
- PRIDE metadata and methods identify U-2 OS cells, a PISA experiment, TMT 10-plex labeling, trypsin/Lys-C digestion, fractionation, and DDA Orbitrap Fusion acquisition.
- The raw-file suffix identifies fraction 1–8. Each TMT channel is represented as a separate assay row sharing its respective fraction file.

## Validation

Validated with `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf --template ms-proteomics --skip-ontology`.
