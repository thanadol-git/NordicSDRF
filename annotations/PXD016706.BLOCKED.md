# PXD016706 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Oats comparison under *Fusarium graminearum* infection
- **PRIDE record:** PXD016706
- **Publication:** Willforss *et al.*, *Journal of Proteomics* (2020), DOI [10.1016/j.jprot.2020.103688](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jprot.2020.103688)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits 18 Thermo RAW files for the *Avena sativa* infection comparison.
- The submitted `quant_batch1.zip` contains `design_batch1.tsv`, an explicit experimental-design table for cultivar (Argamak/Belinda), infection status, time (4 h/1 d/4 d), and biological replicate.
- That design table maps only 9 of the 18 deposited RAW basenames.  The remaining RAW files have no matching row in the design table or other recovered public source.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The available design sheet is sufficient for a subset but not for complete raw-file coverage.  An SDRF must map every deposited raw file; annotating the nine mapped files alone or inferring the other nine from acquisition order would be incomplete or fabricated.

The full sample manifest covering all deposited RAW files, or confirmation that the unmatched RAW files are intentionally out of scope/deprecated and should be omitted from the accession-level SDRF.
