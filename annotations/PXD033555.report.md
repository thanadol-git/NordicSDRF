# PXD033555 annotation report

Citrullination of matrisome proteins in spheroid cultures of fibroblasts and cancer cells. University of Turku. Paper: Front Oncol 2022 (PMC9753687).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 25 rows. The key is the deposited `Sample_mapping.xlsx` (it writes `+PAD4` where the file names have `PAD4`).
- **SCC set:** gingival fibroblasts ± UT-SCC-2 and skin fibroblasts (`JUSA`) ± UT-SCC-7, each treated with PAD4 or buffer only. Each was injected at two loadings (`140ngul`, `undil`), annotated as technical replicates 1 and 2.
- **CAF set:** cancer-associated fibroblasts S23 alone or with one of eight cancer lines. No treatment.

## Values
- **Cell lines:** each cancer line has its Cellosaurus accession and disease. Fibroblast-only spheroids have cell line `not applicable`. The disease of CAF S23 is `not available`.
- **Enzyme:** Trypsin and Lys-C (Trypsin/Lys-C mix, S-Trap).
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF, per all 25 raw headers. HCD.
- **Search:** Proteome Discoverer 2.4 / MS Amanda 2.0, 5 ppm / 0.02 Da. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation K, M and P and Deamidated N, Q and R variable (citrullination is UNIMOD:7 on R).

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: two findings, both false positives:
  - `run_names_name_a_cell_line`: the HepG2 co-culture rows are annotated as cell line HepG2, with organism part `liver` as its tissue of origin.
  - `cleavage_agent_incomplete`: Lys-C is declared in a second cleavage column, which the tool does not read.
