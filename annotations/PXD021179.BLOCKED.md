# PXD021179 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: Proteomic analysis of the immunological response to implanted decellularized aortic scaffolds

Publication: Kallenbach K et al., Tissue Engineering Part A 2022, DOI 10.1089/ten.tea.2021.0146

## Evidence collected

- 40 Q Exactive HF RAW files in two 20-fraction TMT11 sets
- Deposited quantitative workbook headers map reporter channels to scaffold-group codes such as DLN, DBNN, LNSC, and BNDCS
- The publication explains donor strain, recipient strain, scaffold state, implantation site, and syngeneic/allogeneic group meanings
- The PRIDE protocol reports six scaffold samples per group, but the workbook repeats some group codes without identifying individual animals or whether a reporter contains an individual or pooled sample
- PRIDE describes an iodoacetamide-derivatized cysteine residue, while the available workflow interpretation suggested methylthio; the deposited workbook does not resolve the search modification

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

The multiplex channel map establishes experimental groups but not biological source identity or replicate structure. Assigning repeated channels to invented `rep1`/`rep2` animals would be unsupported, and broadcasting donor age to implanted recipient-derived samples would misstate the evidence. The unresolved fixed-cysteine modification also prevents a defensible technical annotation.

## What would unblock it

The original TMT sample sheet mapping every channel to animal or pool identity and biological replicate, plus the search-parameter export identifying the fixed cysteine modification and a per-sample age/sex table.
