# PXD076195 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"Proteomic analysis of acid adapted cancer cells" (PRIDE PXD076195; no publication linked in PRIDE, and a Europe PMC search on the accession finds nothing). Odense (SDU). Label-free DIA secretome of MDA-MB-231 wild-type cells vs an acid-adapted (AA) derivative, adapted to pH 6.7 for one month; conditioned serum-free medium was collected after 48 h. Sources: PRIDE protocols, the submitter's deposited `metadata_sdrf.tsv` (lesSDRF v0.1.0) and the RAW headers.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + human v1.1.0 + cell-lines v1.1.0. No `dia-acquisition`: the DIA window scheme isn't in the available text, and the Astral method (`OTAS_44min_evo_DIA.meth`) wasn't decoded.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 21 files (all with `projectAccessions` = PXD076195): 18 RAW, 1 FASTA, 1 Spectronaut `.sne`, 1 `metadata_sdrf.tsv`. All 18 RAW annotated, one row per RAW (18 rows × 39 columns).

## Sample map
From the submitter's `metadata_sdrf.tsv`, which matches the RAW order 1:1 and the protocol (3 biological × 3 technical replicates per condition): wells C1–C9 = WT1–WT3 (runs 1–3 each), C10–C12 = AA1, D1–D3 = AA2, D4–D6 = AA3. Source names `WT1..3` / `AA1..3` are kept from the submitter file.

## Key decisions
- Instrument: Orbitrap Astral (MS:1003378), from the RAW headers of all 18 files and the protocol; MS2 analyzer = asymmetric track lossless time-of-flight analyzer (MS:1003379).
- DIA (PRIDE:0000450); HCD; Evosep One, 30 SPD.
- Trypsin (MS:1001251; Spectronaut "Trypsin/P"); DTT (PRIDE:0000608) and IAA (PRIDE:0000599) as stated.
- Modifications from the Spectronaut settings in the protocol: Carbamidomethyl C fixed, Oxidation M variable, Acetyl protein N-term variable. The submitter SDRF dropped the N-terminal acetylation; restored here from the data-processing protocol.
- Cell line MDA-MB-231, Cellosaurus CVCL_0062 (EFO:0001209). Donor attributes from Cellosaurus: female, 51Y, adult. Disease: breast adenocarcinoma (MONDO:0004988; PRIDE lists "breast adenocarcinoma"; the submitter wrote "breast cancer"). Cell type epithelial cell (CL:0000066), organism part breast (UBERON:0000310).
- Material type: `cell`. The analyte is conditioned medium (secretome), but the template's allowed material types don't include a supernatant term, so the secretome nature is recorded here and in the treatment rationale.
- Factor: treatment (`control` vs `acid adaptation, pH 6.7 for 1 month`).

## Not available / not applicable
ancestry category (Cellosaurus population not checked live); precursor/fragment mass tolerance (Spectronaut directDIA defaults are dynamic; the submitter's 20 ppm / 0.6 Da look like template defaults rather than search settings, so they aren't copied); fractionation method `not applicable` (no fractionation).

## Conflicts / overrides
Submitter SDRF: organism part `not applicable`, disease `breast cancer`, acquisition method as free text, modifications without protein N-term acetyl. Corrected as described above.
