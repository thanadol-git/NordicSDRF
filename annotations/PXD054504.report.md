# PXD054504 annotation report

Status: parser validated; independent hash-bound review approved.

Fifteen skeletal-muscle RAW files map to eight WT and seven NM samples through the deposited `experimental_design.csv`, `mqpar.xml` and the ZIP central directory. The deposit supplies exact S1–S15 filenames, and the SDRF uses those inner RAW files rather than the outer `raw.zip` container. PRIDE specifies an Orbitrap Exploris 480 with DDA/HCD and physical trypsin digestion. The MaxQuant search config uses Trypsin/P specificity; the SDRF cleavage field records the physical reagent. Individual TNNT1 variant, age, sex and detailed diagnosis are not mapped by the design table and are not inferred.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD054504), [deposited design](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2025/01/PXD054504/experimental_design.csv), [MaxQuant config](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2025/01/PXD054504/mqpar.xml), and [raw archive](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2025/01/PXD054504/raw.zip). Official parser, structure, reconciliation and offline term checks passed.
