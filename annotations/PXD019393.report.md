## Caveats
- Template: `human` template not used (it would force age/sex/ancestry on bacterial samples); ms-proteomics only. Organism is `Chlamydia trachomatis` for pathogen samples although the search database is human (the human serum proteins deposited on the bacteria are the analytes). Serum rows: Homo sapiens, blood serum, disease `normal` (healthy volunteers).
- Cleavage: single protease (trypsin). The acetylation step is chemical blocking of amines before digestion, not a second protease.
- Modifications taken from MaxQuant summary.txt, which differs from the PRIDE prose: prose lists deamidation (NQ) and "carbamidomethyl (M)"; the parameters show Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed and no deamidation. Serum search used Acetyl (Protein N-term), Ctr search Acetyl (N-term) (recorded as Any N-term) and Acetyl (K). Reduction/alkylation used TCEP/chloroacetamide, but carbamidomethyl is the correct mod.
- Acquisition method DDA is inferred (not stated). Precursor tolerance and dissociation method not in the deposit (`not available`). Fragment tolerance 20 ppm from parameters.txt.
- Two MaxQuant versions (1.6.3.4 Ctr, 1.6.10.43 serum) and different FASTA files; Sampleoverview lists "Serum_063" capitalized while the RAW files are lowercase.
- Age/sex omitted (donors unspecified). Paper full text not retrieved.
