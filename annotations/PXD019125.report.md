# PXD019125 annotation report

Skin-mucus proteome of pre-smolt Atlantic salmon fed black soldier fly larvae (BSFL) meal or paste for seven weeks. NMBU, Ås. Paper: Front Immunol 2021, PMC7946862 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `vertebrates` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 21 rows: 7 diets × 3 fish. The deposited `FoN29.txt` design maps runs 01–21 to diet groups.
- `Control1–3` = Control-1.
- `625/1250/2500InsectMeal` = 6.25IM, 12.5IM and 25IM.
- `0875Formicacid` = Control-2 (0.88% formic acid).
- `625/1250Insectpulp` = the BSFL-paste diets.

**Please check:** the paper names the paste diets 3.7IP and 6.7IP (3.7% and 6.7% protein replacement). Mapping the design's nominal `625`/`1250` labels onto 3.7IP and 6.7IP is my reading.

## Values
- **Sample:** organism part `skin mucus`, pre-smolt.
- **Factor:** `diet`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD. LC-MS was done at the UiO proteomics core.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.4.1.2 per the paper. No `parameters.txt` was deposited, and the in-solution digest had no reduction/alkylation step. Modifications are therefore encoded as MaxQuant's defaults (Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable), and tolerances as `not available` / 20 ppm FTMS default.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: every annotated value agrees with the deposit record.
