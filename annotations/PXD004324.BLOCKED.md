# PXD004324 — annotation blocked

"Identification of BER/SSBR proteins … in replisomes, Batch 3": iPOND (isolation of proteins on nascent DNA) in HEK293 cells, run on an Orbitrap Elite. Otterlei/Slupphaug lab, NTNU. The linked paper (NAR 2017, PMC5737410) cites PXD004288 and PXD004494, not this batch.

## What is known
- **Conditions** (77 raw files, decoded from file names and the PRIDE protocol):
  - `NEG` = DMSO negative control.
  - `5P`, `10P`, `15P` = EdU pulse for 5, 10 or 15 min.
  - `PC` = pulse followed by a 30 min thymidine chase.
- **Formaldehyde cross-linking:** the protocol lists 0.13, 0.25 and 1%. The `130802_…_025_…` runs are 0.25%.
- **Late series:** the `131107_…` series adds `pulse_mms` and `pulse_chase_mms` arms (MMS treatment) next to `negativ`, `pulse` and `pulse_chase`, each `_1`–`_5`.
- **Search** (deposited MaxQuant 1.5.3.30): Carbamidomethyl C fixed; FTMS 20 ppm / ITMS 0.5 Da.

## Why it is blocked
- **Cross-linking %:** the formaldehyde concentration of the untagged `130731_…` batch is not stated (0.13% or 1%).
- **Meaning of 1–5:** whether the `_2`…`_5` (and `_1`…`_5`) indices are biological replicates, technical injections or gel pieces is not stated anywhere.
- **MMS arms:** the MMS dose and timing for the `131107` series are unpublished.
- **Problem files:**
  - `130802_karine_025_10P_CORRUPTED.raw` is flagged by the submitter.
  - `130802_karine_025_10P` has 0 MS/MS in the deposited `summary.txt`.
  - The `130814_…_reinj` files are re-injections whose summary statistics are identical to the originals.

## What would unblock it
A sample sheet giving, per raw file:
- the formaldehyde %;
- the replicate meaning of the 1–5 index;
- the MMS treatment details.
