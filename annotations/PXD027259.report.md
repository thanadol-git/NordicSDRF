## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- characteristics[age]: Table S1 'Age' is age at LADC diagnosis in half-years (README sheet). Because the rounding convention is not stated, every row gives the whole-year range covering [age − 0.25, age + 0.5) years; metastasis rows first add the lung–brain interval (age at brain-metastasis diagnosis). Primary rows use age at diagnosis as age at resection.
- Disease staging: primary rows carry the pathological stage at diagnosis (Table S1); metastasis rows are stage IV, since a brain metastasis is distant (M1) disease.
- Run batch is confounded with tissue: primary tumours were run in the 20191128/20191201 batches, metastases in the 20191206 'LUAD_B' batch.
- FFPE preservation is stated in the report only (no licensed SDRF column).
- mLADC-19 (outlier, 3.6 % tumour) is kept; the paper excluded it from group comparisons.
- reconcile flags Trypsin/Lys-C as not named in the PRIDE record; both are in the supplementary methods.
