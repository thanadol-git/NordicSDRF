# PXD039337 annotation report

HIC1 interactome in human cord-blood iTreg cells at 24 h and 72 h. University of Turku. Paper: Immunol Lett 2023 (not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 38 rows: HIC1 IP vs IgG control × 24 h/72 h × 3 biological replicates (`R1–R3`) × 3 injections. The two `HIC1_Interactome_Pool` runs are one pooled sample with technical replicates 1–2. The groups match the deposited MaxQuant summary.

## Values
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF, per the raw header. PRIDE lists Q Exactive. HCD.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.6.0.16. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.
