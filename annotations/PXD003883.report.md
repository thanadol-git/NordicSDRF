# PXD003883 - SDRF annotation report

**Study:** Protein profiling of Chlamydia trachomatis growth forms (Ostergaard et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 2016, PMID 27784728, PMC5141270). GeLC-MS/MS, label-free, LTQ Orbitrap XL. RB, EB and IFN-gamma-induced ARB forms of C. trachomatis D/UW-3/CX purified from infected HeLa 229 cells.

**Outcome:** ANNOTATED, 280 rows x 32 columns, templates `ms-proteomics v1.1.0` only. `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -t ms-proteomics` exit 0 with one warning (see below). `sdrf-tools reconcile`: no disagreements.

## Template choice
`ms-proteomics` only. No organism template fits a bacterium (human/vertebrates/invertebrates/plants/metaproteomics are all wrong); `cell-lines` was rejected because it would force a `characteristics[cell line]` value onto bacterial rows and the host line is not the analysed sample. `clinical-metadata` not applicable (in vitro culture).

## Scope (deposited vs annotated)
All 280 deposited RAW files annotated (280 = 28 experiments x 10 gel bands). Non-RAW files (3 FASTA, tables.pdf, Readme.txt, MaxQuantOutput.tar.gz) are not runs. Mapping source: `experimentalDesign.txt` inside MaxQuantOutput.tar.gz (streamed, not stored); its 280 names equal the PRIDE RAW list exactly. Experiments: 6 RB, 6 ARB, 6 EB, 6 EB-HeLa (3 biological x 2 technical each), and 4 HeLa (1 biological x 4 technical).

## Key decisions
- Experiment name `<group>_<form>_<bio>_<tech>` gives biological and technical replicate; fractions = band order from the design file.
- Growth form is stored in `characteristics[cell type]` (no better licensed column) and repeated as `factor value[cell type]`. ARB is not in CL/BTO/CLO, hence the single validator warning. Reviewer should attack this choice.
- `characteristics[treatment]` = interferon gamma on ARB rows, `not applicable` elsewhere. ARB is confounded with IFN-gamma by design.
- Readme: "EB-HeLa" in the design file = "EB" in the manuscript; "EB" in the design file = "EB-pre" in the manuscript. Paper: "EB before spiking (EB pre) and EB spiked with HeLa (EB)". Both are `elementary body`; the HeLa spike-in is recorded as `characteristics[spiked compound]` = `CT=protein;CN=HeLa protein lysate` on EB-HeLa_n rows (manuscript EB) and `not applicable` on EB_n rows (EB pre). The design-name-to-manuscript mapping comes from the Readme.
- HeLa_1 rows (5_HeLa): organism Homo sapiens INFERRED from the experiment label and the human FASTA; paper does not describe the sample; all other sample fields `not available`.
- Modifications read from deposited MaxQuant parameters.txt/summary.txt: Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed; Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term, PP=) variable. The wet-lab alkylation reagent is unstated, so `comment[alkylation reagent]` and `reduction reagent` are `not available` (DTT 5 mM appears only in lysis buffer, not stated as the in-gel reduction).
- Cleavage: Trypsin (MaxQuant used Trypsin/P). Fragment tolerance 0.5 Da (ITMS). Precursor tolerance 6 ppm (paper; not in parameters.txt). The paper states 1 missed cleavage (parameters.txt: 2); no SDRF column carries it.
- Instrument LTQ Orbitrap XL from paper + PRIDE (agree); raw headers were not inspected.
- `characteristics[disease]` = not applicable for bacterial cultures; PRIDE's "bacterial infectious disease" is a project-level dropdown and was not broadcast. `strain or breed` = D/UW-3/CX on Chlamydia rows.
- Host HeLa 229 is described only in this report; no column carries it.

## Not available / not applicable
sample type, reduction and alkylation reagents (not stated); organism part/disease/material type not applicable on bacterial rows; most HeLa_1 fields not available.
