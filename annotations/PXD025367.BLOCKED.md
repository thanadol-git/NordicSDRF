# PXD025367 — annotation blocked

*Vibrio anguillarum* secretome and cell-pellet proteomes under conditions mimicking vibriosis (oxidative stress, iron deprivation, salmon serum) and across growth phases in LB. NMBU, Ås. Paper: PMID 34737109, 2022 (not open access).

## What is known
- **Runs:** 60 raw files `V1–V60`; the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` names the experiments `1–60`.
- **Conditions** (PRIDE protocol): LB at OD 0.3–0.4, 0.6–0.7 and >1.0, and M9va with pooled salmon serum, each split into secretome and pellet. The title adds oxidative stress and iron deprivation.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.6.17.0. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term, Deamidation NQ and Gln→pyro-Glu variable.

## Why it is blocked
No key maps `V1–V60` to condition, fraction (secretome/pellet) or replicate, and the paper is closed access.
