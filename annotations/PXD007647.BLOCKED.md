# PXD007647 annotation blocked

PXD007647 is an *Escherichia coli* MG1655 TMT-10plex study of laboratory-derived mecillinam-resistant mutants and the parental strain. The archive exposes 34 RAW files from Q Exactive and Orbitrap Fusion fractionated sets, and two search files, but no sample sheet or reporter-channel annotation.

The study and public re-analysis establish that ten samples were multiplexed and that the work compares mecillinam-resistant mutants with the parental strain. They do not assign the TMT reporter channels to individual strains, genetic backgrounds, or growth/treatment conditions. An SDRF for a TMT experiment must preserve that channel-to-sample map; using a presumed channel order would be unsupported. No SDRF is emitted.

Evidence checked: PRIDE / ProteomeXchange PXD007647 and its 36-file archive record; Thulin and Andersson, *Antimicrobial Agents and Chemotherapy* (2019), PMID 31332059 / PMCID PMC6761508; the article's public supplementary-material record; and the public PXD007647 re-analysis by Vorontsov. No inspected source supplied the original reporter-channel assignment.

Unblock with the original TMT channel/sample worksheet, a Proteome Discoverer export that names reporter channels, or confirmation from the authors.
