# PXD075285 - annotation blocked

Status: blocked; no SDRF emitted. Nothing here has been independently reviewed.

## Exact blocker

The TMTpro channel-to-sample map is not recoverable from the deposit. PXD075285 is a rat (Rattus norvegicus) urinary extracellular vesicle study: control, 2-week lithium and 4-week lithium groups, 8 rats each (24 rats), split across 2 TMT sets. Each set has 13 reporter channels (126 to 132C), but the deposit never says which channel holds which rat or group.

- The PRIDE protocol says only "Eight rats each from control, 2-week Li, and 4-week Li were distributed into 2 TMT sets." It gives no per-set composition.
- Counting channels, 2 x 13 = 26, against 24 rats. So there are probably 2 extra channels, for example a pool or reference, in 126 or elsewhere. The deposit does not say.
- Each .msf (TMT_set1.msf, TMT_set2.msf) holds only generic sample names: `TMT_setN_C_fraction - [126]` through `[132C]`. Channel 126 is flagged `SampleType="Control"` and the other 12 are `Sample`. That is the Proteome Discoverer reference-channel flag, not a biological control. No factor values are defined.
- PRIDE lists no publication (references empty, no DOI). A Europe PMC search on the accession returns 0 hits.
- The only deposited files are 48 RAW files and the 2 .msf files. There is no supplement or manifest.
- Inferring groups from protein abundance patterns or from convention would be invention, so no rows were built.

## Facts established, for use once the map is supplied

- Organism: Rattus norvegicus (NEWT:10116). Material: urinary extracellular vesicles, isolated by ultracentrifugation.
- Digestion: FASP, then TMTpro labelling. Pooled, then 8 high-pH fractions.
- Instrument: Orbitrap Fusion, from the PRIDE field only. It was not verified from the RAW headers.
- Search: Proteome Discoverer 2.4 with SEQUEST and Mascot. Precursor tolerance 10 ppm, fragment tolerance 0.02 Da, up to 2 missed cleavages. Fixed modifications: carbamidomethyl (C), TMTpro at the peptide N-terminus and on K. Variable modifications: N-terminal acetylation and Met oxidation.
- Run layout: 48 RAW files, 24 per set. The .msf files treat them as fractions 1 to 24 per set. Each of the 8 nominal fractions has 3 RAW files (`..._fractionN_01.raw`, a timestamped `..._01_<date>.raw`, and for set1 fraction 8 a `_03.raw`).
- Open question: the file-name triplets could be repeat injections or sub-fractions. The .msf numbering treats them as separate fractions.

A submitter-provided channel map or sample sheet giving, for each TMT set, the rat or group assigned to each of the 13 channels and which channel is a pool or reference. Contact: Qi Wu, Department of Biomedicine, Aarhus University (qi.wu@biomed.au.dk). A linked publication would also do.
