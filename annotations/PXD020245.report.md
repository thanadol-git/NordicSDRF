# PXD020245 SDRF annotation report

## Scope

- Organisms: *Mus musculus* intestinal tissue and *Homo sapiens* Caco-2 cells
- Acquisition: Q Exactive DDA, label-free MED-FASP
- Digestion: paired Lys-C and trypsin runs
- RAW files annotated: 132

## Evidence and mapping

The deposited `metadata_Mouse.txt` maps all mouse AA identifiers to sample ID, treatment, and tissue type. `metadata_Caco2.txt` maps all Caco-2 S identifiers to treatment. Every deposited RAW filename carries one of these identifiers plus its Lys-C/trypsin digest designation, allowing a complete run-level mapping.
