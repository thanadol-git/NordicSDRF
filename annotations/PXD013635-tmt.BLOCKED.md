# PXD013635 TMT-TAILS arm

Status: blocked locally; the label-free secretome arm is annotated separately as `PXD013635-lfq.sdrf.tsv`.

The two TMT10plex TAILS runs (`20190130_EV_LB_SW_PO3.raw`, `20190130_EV_LB_SW_NPO3.raw`) have no recoverable reporter-channel to sample map. The paper (bioRxiv 10.1101/620534; PMID 31917255) says only that two conditions (LoO2LoT+, HiO2HiT+) with n=5 pooled-fascicle samples each were run as a 10plex, without assigning conditions or biological replicates to TMT126-131 channels. PRIDE metadata and file names do not carry it. The deposited .msf/.pdResult (0.6 GB) were not downloaded. It is also unclear which of PO3/NPO3 is preTAILS or TAILS (PD treated them as fractions).

Required to unblock: the channel-to-sample table (supplement or authors), and the identity of PO3 vs NPO3.
