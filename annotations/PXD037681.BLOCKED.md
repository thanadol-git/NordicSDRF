# PXD037681 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE identifies a human TMT experiment comparing dasatinib and staurosporine across four concentrations, and deposits 24 RAW fraction files named `200602_CATCH_Staurosporine_f01.raw` through `_f24.raw` with PTM and protein result tables. The protocol specifies a multiplexed TMT workflow but contains no reporter-channel assignment.
- **Why blocked:** The RAW filenames identify only one drug and fraction number; they do not map multiplex channels to concentration, control, sample, or replicate. The deposited result tables likewise do not provide a sample manifest. Assigning the multiple conditions described by the project would be unsupported.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A deposited TMT design/run sheet mapping each reporter channel to its treatment, concentration, control status, and biological replicate.
