# PAD000045 annotation report

## Result

60-row affinity-proteomics SDRF: 60 vitiligo-patient EDTA plasma samples (30 donors x baseline / 3 months) in the Novartis ATI013 SomaScan v4.0 ADAT. Partial by file: the second ADAT (2015, SomaScan 1.3k V3.2, 20 Covance Young/Old plasma samples) is not annotated. Templates: affinity-proteomics v1.0.0 + human v1.1.0. Factor: clinical time point.

## Evidence

PRIDE PAD000045 (Ouwerkerk et al., J Invest Dermatol 2026, PMID 40848929); deposited Sampledata.xlsx (Sample ID -> donor, sample type, visit, description); ADAT row data.

## Sample map

All 60 study SampleIds in the Novartis ADAT match Sampledata.xlsx; all are EDTA plasma (Visit 1 / Visit 2 for 30 donors). Sampledata also lists blister fluid and serum samples, which are not in any deposited ADAT. The 1.3k ADAT holds 20 'Young'/'Old' Covance plasma samples with sex in SampleGroup.

## Conservative choices

Calibrator, buffer and QC wells omitted. Visit 1 = baseline, Visit 2 = 3 months on standard-of-care treatment (protocol). One sample with RowCheck FLAG kept (comment[row check]). The 1.3k ADAT is left out: it is a different assay version (one platform per SDRF), and its link to the vitiligo study is not described in the PRIDE record or paper text.

## Conflicts

PRIDE describes blister fluid proteomics, but no blister-fluid samples are in the deposited ADATs.
