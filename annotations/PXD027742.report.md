# PXD027742 annotation report

## Study
"A deeper look at carrier proteome effects for single-cell proteomics" (Ye, Batth, Ruther, Olsen; Commun Biol 2022, PMC8863851). Mixed-species benchmark: HeLa, E. coli and yeast tryptic digests labelled with TMTpro 16-plex; channel 126 = carrier proteome (H, EY or HEY; absent in "no carrier" runs), 127C empty, 14 single-cell channels (127N-134N) at defined species ratios. Orbitrap Exploris 480 + Evosep One, DDA, HCD.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 only. Not cell-lines (yeast/E. coli are not cell lines), not human, and not single-cell (no cells were isolated; the single-cell template would force required cell identifier / isolation protocol columns that do not apply). Carrier and design are carried by factor values. Reason for not declaring single-cell: its required single cell isolation protocol and cell identifier do not apply to diluted bulk peptide mixtures; comment[carrier channel] is optional and carrier status is expressed by the 126 rows and carrier columns.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
Deposited: 1201 files over 13 PRIDE pages (240 .raw, 960 .pdResult, 1 .txt). Annotated: all 240 RAW (matched one-to-one to the 240 rows of Supplementary Data 2 RawFiles), 15 channel rows per run (126 carrier + 127N-134N... i.e. 14 single-cell channels plus the carrier; 14 rows for no-carrier runs; 127C empty never a row) = 3580 rows x 45 columns. .pdResult and .txt files are not annotated as data rows.

## Key decisions
- Channel map: 126 carrier, 127C empty (no row), 127N-134N single-cell channels, from the paper text; per-channel E. coli/Human/yeast relative amounts read from Fig. 1a bar labels (image) into characteristics[species composition]. No-carrier runs: channel 126 treated as empty (paper: samples without any CPC).
- Per-run conditions (carrier, level, amount, AGC, MS2 resolution, NCE) from Supplementary Data 2 RawFiles; all 240 deposited RAW names matched one-to-one. Factors: carrier proteome, carrier level, amount per single-cell channel, AGC target, MS2 resolution; NCE in comment[collision energy].
- Technical values from the deposited Proteome Discoverer workflow (.pdResult, first 8 MB of one file): Carbamidomethyl C fixed, TMTpro K/N-term fixed, Oxidation M variable, 10 ppm, 0.02 Da, Trypsin (Full), 1 missed cleavage, HCD (written NT=HCD;AC=MS:1000422).
- Organism: three repeated characteristics[organism] columns (Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Escherichia coli). Single-cell channels and HEY carrier carry all three; EY carrier = S. cerevisiae + E. coli (3rd cell empty); H carrier = Homo sapiens (2nd and 3rd empty). Species composition kept as a supplement.
- Cleavage agent: two repeated columns, Lys-C (MS:1001309) then Trypsin (MS:1001251) (Lys-C 1:200 2 h, then trypsin 1:50 overnight; search enzyme Trypsin).
- Source names carry the acquisition date and the run condition (AGC for 20200820; resolution + NCE for 20200827), technical replicate 1 for every row: runs differing in AGC/resolution/NCE are not asserted to be reinjections of one mixture. 20200820 biological replicate = the _1/_2 suffix (Supplementary Data 2 "Replicates"); the 48 20200827 files have a blank Replicates cell, so their biological replicate is the placeholder 1 (the validator requires an integer) and means "not stated", never merged with 20200820 sources.
- Matching characteristics for each factor (carrier proteome, carrier level, amount per single-cell channel, AGC target, MS2 resolution; NCE in comment[collision energy] as `32 NCE`) plus factor value[collision energy] (same values as comment[collision energy]). The carrier channel (126) has amount `not applicable`.
- Build limitation: sdrf-tools build cannot emit per-row repeated organism or a second cleavage column, so these were produced by a deterministic post-processing step (scratch post.py: rename helper columns organism_2/organism_3 and insert the Lys-C column before Trypsin) applied to the build output; all values come from samples.tsv/technical.tsv.
