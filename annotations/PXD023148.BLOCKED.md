# PXD023148 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE deposits 94 fractionated TMT RAW files for the plasma-depletion study.  The linked paper (PMID 34320313 / PMCID PMC8419864) and its supplementary PDF were checked.  The supplement gives HiRIEF gradient lengths and identifies plasma samples 13–26, but not reporter-channel assignments or a raw-file/sample map.
- **Why blocked:** The deposited filenames encode method and fraction, but cannot connect the TMT channels to human plasma sample, depletion method, or replicate without unsupported inference.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A submitted sample/TMT channel map tied to the corresponding RAW fraction files.
