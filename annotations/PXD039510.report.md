# PXD039510 annotation report

## Scope

The annotation covers 104 of 166 RAW files for which deposited workbooks provide an explicit run-to-condition map: 48 global-medium files (`Marc_P2011_*`) and 56 extracellular-vesicle files (`Marc_P2104_*`). The 62 `Marc_H1804_*` files are excluded because their `MS14`–`MS40` sample IDs have no public treatment crosswalk.

The combined SDRF preserves the complete mapped evidence. Acquisition-specific files contain 48 DDA rows and 56 DIA rows and are the validation-ready forms.

## Validation

- `PXD039510-dda.sdrf.tsv` and `PXD039510-dia.sdrf.tsv`: `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -t ms-proteomics -t human --skip-ontology` PASS.
- Both splits pass `sdrf-tools structure`, offline ontology checking, and reconciliation; quality score: 95/100 each.
- The combined file intentionally reports the mixed-acquisition structural finding; it is retained as a complete 104-row accession-level map.
