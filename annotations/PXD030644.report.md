## Study

Nat Commun 2022;13:2736, doi:10.1038/s41467-022-30376-7, PMID 35585066, PMC9117253 (Methods read; the title matched). DIA arm of the UbiSite study (SDU Odense, Blagoev lab): U2OS cells treated 3 h with DMSO, 10 µM MG132 (proteasome inhibitor) or 20 µM PR-619 (pan-DUB inhibitor); Lys-C digest, UbiSite immuno-enrichment of ubiquitin-remnant peptides, trypsin, DIA on an Orbitrap Exploris 480.
