## Study

"Tryps-IN: A streamlined palaeoproteomics workflow enables ZooMS analysis of 10,000-year-old petrous bones from Jordan rift-valley", Jensen et al., J Archaeol Sci Rep 52:104238 (2023), doi:10.1016/j.jasrep.2023.104238. Twenty-eight petrous bones from Shkarat Msaied (Jordan) were screened by ZooMS (MALDI); eight were sequenced by LC-MS/MS and searched with MaxQuant against a COL1 database. Eight raw files are in PRIDE; seven were in the deposited MaxQuant run.

## Templates

`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `vertebrates` v1.1.0. The tool is MS, DDA, label-free. The animals are bovids, so `vertebrates` is the nearest organism layer. No cell-line, clinical or oncology layer applies (archaeological bone, no donor).

## Key decisions and evidence

- **Organism (per sample, stated in paper Results)**: SM4, SM5, SM10, SM23, SM26 = `Capra` (NCBITaxon:9922; genus level only, ibex vs bezoar not resolved). SM18 = `Bovidae` (NCBITaxon:9895; "either Capra sp. or Antilopinae"). SM12 = `Antilopinae` (NCBITaxon:9948; paper: tentatively Nanger, more probably a Gazella). PRIDE's `Capra sp.` for all samples is wrong for SM12 and over-specific for SM18, so it was not broadcast.
- **Organism part**: `petrous part of temporal bone` (UBERON:0001694), stated.
- **Instrument (raw headers beat prose and PRIDE field)**: EXPL2 files (SM4/5/10/18/26) read `Orbitrap Exploris 480`; JORD12/JORD23 read `Q Exactive HF-X - Orbitrap MS`. PRIDE's structured field says only `Q Exactive`. The paper text says ABC extractions ran on HF-X and Tryps-IN extractions on Exploris 480, and treats all eight sequenced samples as Tryps-IN; the raw files put two of them on HF-X. The raw headers were followed; this is the `instrument_contradicted` MAJOR still printed by `reconcile` (the prose names both instruments).
- **Modifications** read from the deposited search (`summary.txt`, `mqpar.xml`): all variable, none fixed: Oxidation (M), Hydroxyproline, Gln->pyro-Glu, Glu->pyro-Glu, Deamidated (NQ), Acetyl (protein N-term). `sdrf-tools search-params` could not map MaxQuant's "Hydroxyproline"; it was written as Oxidation UNIMOD:35 with TA=P (MaxQuant's Hydroxyproline is oxidation of P). **Inferred mapping**, flagged for review.
- **Cleavage agent**: `Trypsin` (MS:1001251) for the wet-lab digest (Promega trypsin). The first draft used MaxQuant's `Trypsin/P`, but reconcile flagged it; Trypsin/P semispecific is a search setting, so it is described here, not in the column.
- **Tolerances**: precursor 4.5 ppm (main search), fragment 20 ppm (FTMS), from `mqpar.xml`.
- **Acquisition**: DDA (PRIDE:0000627), from the file names and PRIDE experiment type.

## Values left `not available` / `not applicable`

- `not available`: developmental stage, strain or breed, sex (individual animals, ancient, no data in the paper); reduction reagent, alkylation reagent (not described for the bone samples, and the search has no fixed Carbamidomethyl; silence is not evidence of omission); dissociation method (not stated, not read from scan headers).
- `not applicable`: disease, cell type (archaeological tissue, not a cell-type study).
- `characteristics[age]` is not licensed by these templates, so the ~10,000-year archaeological age is not recorded as a column.
- No factor value: there is no experimental comparison.

## Conflicts and how resolved

PRIDE instrument vs raw vs paper (above); PRIDE organism `Capra sp.` vs paper per-sample taxa (paper wins); PRIDE says `submissionType PARTIAL`, and the deposit is a MaxQuant output set with raw files for 7 of 8 sequenced samples plus one unexplained file.

Stated: organisms, tissue, enzyme, search modifications, tolerances, instruments (raw headers). Inferred: Hydroxyproline = UNIMOD:35 (TA=P); `material type` = tissue; DDA from file names; biological replicate = 1 per source (one bone per source).
