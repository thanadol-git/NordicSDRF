# PXD026802 SDRF annotation report

## Study
CSF proteomes (exosome, microvesicle and soluble fractions) of multiple system atrophy, Parkinson disease, dementia with Lewy bodies, progressive supranuclear palsy and control subjects, DIA on Q Exactive HF-X (Cell Mol Life Sci 2022, PMID 35657417).

## Templates
ms-proteomics + human v1.1.0

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
432/432 RAW: 144 subjects (plate positions A-L x 01-12) x 3 ultracentrifugation fractions (a few fractions missing / re-injected).

## Sample map
Deposited Spectronaut ConditionSetup_*.tsv files give the condition (DLB, IPD, MSA, PSP, HCPD) for every run; the condition of a plate position is identical across the three fractions (checked). Group sizes 20/40/28/39/17 match the record.

## Key decisions
HCPD read as healthy control -> disease 'normal'; IPD = idiopathic Parkinson disease. Plate position (CSF-A-01 ...) used as subject id. Fraction from the E / M / Sv suffix and the protocol (20k x g pellet = microvesicles, 120k x g pellet = exosomes, supernatant = soluble). Re-injections (_N or digit suffix, e.g. -Sv3, -M1) -> technical replicates of the same subject and fraction. TCEP/CAA, Lys-C + trypsin; Spectronaut mods from the record.
