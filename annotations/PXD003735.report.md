# PXD003735 annotation report

## Scope

All six deposited MGF files are represented.

## Evidence and decisions

- PRIDE specifies *Populus trichocarpa* cell-suspension material and acyl-biotin exchange (ABE) purification for S-acylated proteins.
- The MGF names provide three replicates in hydroxylamine and without-hydroxylamine ABE conditions. The method describes gel digestion but does not state the enzyme, so unspecific cleavage is retained rather than assuming a protease.
