# PXD014234 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Anti-tumor effects of PIM/PI3K/mTOR triple kinase inhibitor IBL-302 in neuroblastoma
- **PRIDE record:** PXD014234
- **Publication:** Mohlin *et al.*, *EMBO Molecular Medicine* (2019), DOI [10.15252/emmm.201810058](https://doi.org/10.15252/emmm.201810058), PMID [31310053](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31310053/), PMCID [PMC6685085](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6685085/)

## Evidence recovered

- The methods state that LU-NB-3 cells were treated with 50 nM IBL-302 or vehicle for 48 h, with four control and three treated biological samples used for mass spectrometry.
- PRIDE contains 14 RAW files: seven `G_*` and seven `P_*`, each with the expected four control and three IBL-302 sample numbers.
- The publication reports global proteome and phosphoproteome analyses but the available sources do not provide a per-file mapping that defines the `G` and `P` workflow branches or confirms whether same-numbered files are aliquots of the same biological sample.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Mapping `G` or `P` to global versus phosphoproteome purely from their letters would be an inference.  An SDRF must explicitly identify the processing/enrichment branch for each raw file, and must not collapse potentially paired aliquots into sources without evidence.

## Source artifacts

- `scratchpad/PXD014234/evidence/pride.json`
- `scratchpad/PXD014234/evidence/files.json`

## Unblocking requirement

An author-provided mass-spectrometry sample sheet or workflow table defining every `G_*` and `P_*` raw file, its enrichment/analysis branch, and whether matched numbers share a biological source.
