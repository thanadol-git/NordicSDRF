# PXD013134 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** ProTargetMiner: A proteome library of anticancer molecules for functional discovery
- **PRIDE record:** PXD013134
- **Publication:** Lapek *et al.*, *Molecular Systems Biology* (2019), PMID [31844049](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31844049/)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits 96 RAW files with source-level names for MCF7 or RKO cells, biological replicate, and fraction.
- The protocol specifies TMT10 labelling after vehicle or anticancer-compound treatment and describes concatenated fractions.
- No submitted channel design maps any TMT10 reporter channel to vehicle or a specific compound treatment, and no file-to-plex crosswalk is deposited.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Each fractionated RAW file is a multiplexed TMT measurement.  Cell line and fraction naming alone cannot assign the individual reporter channels to treatment conditions.  Inferring those assignments would fabricate the central experimental metadata.
