# PXD060117 annotation report

C2C12 myotubes grown with 0, 4 or 400 µM arginine for 24 or 48 h. Bjørkøy/Pettersen lab, NTNU. Paper: Cancer Metab 2025, PMC12142917 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `vertebrates` and `cell-lines`, all v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 36 rows: 6 plates × 6 wells, one `.d.tar` per sample.
- **Well key:** the deposited `combined/txt/label.txt` (inside `txt.tar`) decodes the wells:
  - A = 400 µM (control), B = 4 µM, C = 0 µM, all at 24 h.
  - D = 400 µM, E = 4 µM, F = 0 µM, all at 48 h.
- **Replicates:** the plate number (P1–P6) is the replicate (`rep` in `label.txt`) and is used as the biological replicate.

## Values
- **Cell line:** C2C12 (CVCL_0188); female, 2M, C3H, per Cellosaurus. Cell type `myotube`.
- **Factors:** `treatment` (arginine concentration) and `time`.
- **Instrument:** timsTOF Pro 2, from `analysis.tdf`. PRIDE lists timsTOF Pro.
- **Sample prep:** DTT and iodoacetamide; trypsin.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 2.0.3.0):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidation NQ variable.
  - 20 ppm precursor and fragment tolerance, from the PRIDE protocol.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags the instrument: the PRIDE protocol says timsTOF Pro. `analysis.tdf` in the raw data reports timsTOF Pro 2, so the raw header is followed.
