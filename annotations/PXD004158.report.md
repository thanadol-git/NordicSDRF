## Honesty choices

1. **Organism** = `Bos taurus` (nasal cartilage explants). Explants were incubated with normal human serum before urea extraction; the search database is human+bovine, but the physical sample is bovine cartilage. Human serum is not modeled as a second organism row.
2. **Organism part** = `nasal cartilage` (protocol: adult bovine nasal cartilage septa).
3. **Paper** is not PMC open access; annotation does **not** rely on paywalled full text. Condition labels come only from `Sample_description.xlsx` + PRIDE protocols.
4. **Instrument** = Q Exactive Plus from the PRIDE sample-processing protocol (PRIDE instrument CV lists Q Exactive).
5. **Hydroxyproline** recorded as variable `Oxidation` on P (`UNIMOD:35;TA=P`), alongside Met oxidation and fixed carbamidomethyl, per the deposited TPP search description.
