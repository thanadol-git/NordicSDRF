# PXD058691 annotation report

Colorectal cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) treated with the MEK inhibitor trametinib or DMSO: CAF proteome and decellularized ECM. University of Turku. Paper: Cancer Res 2026 (PMC13266349).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human`, `dia-acquisition` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 12 rows: CAF or ECM × DMSO or trametinib × 3 replicates. The key is the deposited `Raw_file_mapping.xlsx`.

## Inferences to check
- **Culture format:** the deposited mapping labels `CAF_*` runs as 2D and `ECM_*` runs as 3D. The paper describes CAF spheroids (3D) and ECM decellularized from plates. The deposited labels are kept.
- **Trametinib dose:** not in the deposit. The paper uses 10 nM for ECM production, and does not give the spheroid dose. The compound column says `trametinib` without a dose.

## Values
- **Cell type:** `fibroblast`; the cancer-associated origin is given by the disease (colorectal cancer) and the source name, because CL has no CAF term.
- **Enzyme:** Trypsin and Lys-C (S-Trap).
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Fusion Lumos with FAIMS, DIA, 395–1000 m/z. HCD.
- **Search:** Spectronaut 16.2 directDIA+. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: two findings, both false positives:
  - `disease_on_control_runs`: DMSO is the vehicle arm, and the CAFs still come from colorectal cancer.
  - `cleavage_agent_incomplete`: Lys-C is declared in a second cleavage column, which the tool does not read.
