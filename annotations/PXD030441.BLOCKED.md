# PXD030441: BLOCKED (no TMT channel-to-sample map)

Study: "Cycloastragenol inhibit experimental abdominal aortic aneurysm progression" (Melin et al., Biomedicines 2022, doi:10.3390/biomedicines10020359, PMC8962318). Male Sprague-Dawley rats, elastase-induced AAA, cycloastragenol (CAG, n=10) vs vehicle (n=10) in the proteomics comparison.

## What is deposited
24 RAW files (Orbitrap Eclipse, per the RAW-derived file table in the deposited .pdResult) plus one 15 GB Proteome Discoverer `.pdResult`. The RAW files are 6 TMT plexes (A to F) x 4 high-pH fractions (files 6 to 9), fraction order taken from the PD study definition (FractionNumber 1 to 4).

## Why it cannot be annotated
Each RAW file mixes all channels of one plex, so each SDRF row must be (animal, channel). The mapping from TMT channel to animal and to CAG vs vehicle group is not recoverable:
- Paper: only says 20 animals (10/10) entered the proteomics, "randomly labelled" with 10-plex TMT, channel 126 a pool of all AAA samples. No channel table, no plex layout. Supplementary Table S1 (xlsx) holds protein-level fold changes and n only; the supplementary PDF holds figures S1/S2 and Table S2 only.
- PRIDE record: no sample table, no SDRF-like file; sample attributes are project-level only.
- Deposited search result (`Lasse_StubbeF_sesarch.pdResult`, read by HTTP range requests; AnalysisDefinition table): 66 samples named only "Lasse_StubbeX - [channel]" for X = A..F and channels 126..131C (method TMT 11plex), `Factors` empty, no FactorValues. No animal IDs or group labels.
- Inconsistencies that prevent inferring a layout: 6 plexes x 11 channels (66 channel slots) versus 20 animals plus a pool; paper and PRIDE say 10-plex, PD method says 11plex. Which channels are animals, pool or unused, and which animal is CAG or vehicle, is not stated anywhere.

Inventing a channel identity is not acceptable, so no SDRF was written.

## Facts established (for a future annotation if the authors supply the map)
- Organism Rattus norvegicus (strain Sprague-Dawley, male, 7 to 10 weeks), tissue: infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm wall, day 28 after elastase infusion; treatment CAG 125 mg/kg/day by oral gavage vs vehicle.
- Instrument: Orbitrap Eclipse (raw-derived file table in .pdResult; paper says "Eclipse raw data files"); PRIDE record says Q Exactive HF (conflict, raw and paper win).
- PD 2.4 / Sequest; trypsin, 1 missed cleavage; fixed carbamidomethyl C, TMT at K and N-term; variable Met oxidation, deamidation; precursor 8 ppm, fragment 0.05 Da (PRIDE data-processing protocol; not read from the .pdResult workflow).
- Needed from the authors: per-plex channel layout (which animal or pool in each of 126..131C for plexes A..F) and group assignment.
