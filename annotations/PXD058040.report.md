# PXD058040 annotation report

Status: approved after parser validation and independent hash-bound review.

Four RAWs are annotated as separate SH-SY5Y WT culture-medium sources. The PRIDE protocol supports the human cell line, Orbitrap Fusion DDA, trypsin and HCD settings; [Cellosaurus](https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_0019) supplies cell-line provenance. The generic RAW names and paired Mascot spreadsheets do not map each run to its chondroitinase or heparinase treatment, so no treatment factor is asserted. The required biological-replicate coordinate is 1 for each separate RAW-derived source; this does not claim that the four runs are matched biological replicates.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD058040), [file list](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD058040/files/all), and Cellosaurus. The culture-medium field uses `not available` because the source says only “serum-free RPMI” without a formulation that resolves to the template ontology. The PRIDE processing protocol also names three variable Ser hexasaccharide glycopeptide modifications at 993.2809, 1073.2377 and 1153.1945 Da. Their exact structural terms have no verified Unimod or PSI-MOD accession, so the SDRF records only the supported carbamidomethylation and oxidation terms. The official parser, structure, reconciliation, and offline term checks passed.
