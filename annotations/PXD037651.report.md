# PXD037651 annotation report

Proteomes of three colorectal cancer lines (HCT 116, HT-29, SW620): whole cells, their small extracellular vesicles (sEVs), and a phosphopeptide-enriched whole-cell fraction. Heck/Slupphaug, NTNU. Paper: Proteomes 2023, PMC9844407 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 27 rows, one per `.raw`, in three sets of 9 (3 lines × 3 biological replicates).

| Run names | Material | How it was decoded |
|---|---|---|
| `181006_*_totx_R1–3` | whole-cell lysate | The PD input-file folder is "Total totx". |
| `181108_*_totPHOS_R1–3` | whole-cell lysate, TiIMAC phosphopeptide enrichment | `enrichment of phosphorylated protein` (EFO:0010806) |
| `190118_KATHLENN_*_R5–7` | sEV lysate | **Decoded from the data**; there is no explicit label. |

How the sEV set was decoded: in the deposited PD `Proteins.txt`, ALIX/PDCD6IP, TSG101, CD63 and CD9 rank 35, 197, 189 and 357 in the R5 run. In the totx run they rank about 3600, 3400, absent and about 2000, while calnexin drops from rank 249 to about 2900. That is the sEV marker pattern the paper reports by western blot.

The sEV rows carry `cellular component` = `extracellular exosome` and `enrichment process` = `extracellular vesicle enrichment` (ultracentrifugation; free text). Replicate numbers are kept as in the run names. `SW_R5` exists only as a timestamped copy and is annotated as R5.

## Values
- **Cell lines** (Cellosaurus); the paper confirms SW620 (the run names only say "SW"):
  - HCT 116 (CVCL_0291): male, 48Y, colon, colon carcinoma.
  - HT-29 (CVCL_0320): female, 44Y, colon, colon adenocarcinoma.
  - SW620 (CVCL_0547): male, 51Y, lymph-node metastasis, colon adenocarcinoma.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF, from the raw header; HCD at 29 NCE.
- **Search** (paper and PD 2.3):
  - 10 ppm / 0.02 Da.
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Phospho STY, Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M and Deamidation NQ variable, for all searches.
- **Reagents:** digestion was on HILIC beads with unspecified reduction/alkylation reagents, so those columns are left out.
- **Not deposited:** the PD input lists also show targeted (PRM) runs and a housekeeping-peptide run. They are not in the deposit.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags trypsin as unsupported. That is a false positive: the paper states trypsin.
