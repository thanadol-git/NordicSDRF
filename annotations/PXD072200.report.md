# PXD072200: acquisition-mode and tissue-area map needed

The accession deposits 45 `.d.zip` Bruker acquisitions in several numbered spatial-proteomics groups, including `211-218`, `256-262`, `327-334`, `343-350`, `354-360` and `361-368`. The PRIDE protocol explicitly says data were acquired in both DDA-PASEF and DIA-PASEF modes, but the deposited raw basenames do not identify which mode applies to each run. The same record describes human atherosclerotic plaque spatial sampling and a separate initial murine-myocardium method study. The filename codes alone do not establish the corresponding tissue area, donor, or biological replicate for each acquisition.

No SDRF was added. The accession includes FragPipe and DIA-NN search outputs, but their available file names do not supply a complete 45-run mode and tissue-area map. A small FragPipe manifest and DIANN search archives are listed in the repository; their content needs to be retrieved and reconciled with all raw basenames before rows are built. The two acquisition modes require different SDRF values, so a project-level assignment would be incorrect.

Required evidence: accession-scoped design/sample sheet or search manifests tying each raw basename to acquisition mode, tissue region and source. Sources: [PRIDE project record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD072200), `scratchpad/PXD072200/pride.json`, and accession-scoped `scratchpad/PXD072200/files.json`.
