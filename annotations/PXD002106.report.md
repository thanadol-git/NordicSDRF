# PXD002106
Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

## Dataset

"Proteomic Analysis of Minimally Damaged Renal Tubular Tissue from Two-Kidney-One-Clip Hypertensive Rats Demonstrates Extensive Changes Compared to Tissue from Controls". Renal Research Group, Department of Clinical Medicine, University of Bergen, Norway (submitter/PI Kenneth Finne). Paper: Finne K et al., Nephron 2016;132(1):70-80, PMID 26745798, DOI 10.1159/000442825. The paper is not open access; I used the abstract and the PubMed MeSH headings.

Design: laser-microdissected tubulointerstitial tissue from FFPE kidney sections. The tissue comes from the non-clipped kidney of 4 two-kidney-one-clip (2K1C) hypertensive rats and from 6 sham-operated rats. Label-free DDA, one injection per rat, no fractionation. Not previously annotated: `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets` returned 404 for both `datasets/PXD002106` and `sandbox/PXD002106`.

## Revision after independent review (round 1: FAIL)

The first version had SHA-256 `a592665379c7dec927bb0c97ca9901b356bd8dd1429831257b08f14b98958c22`.

- **Important finding: sex and strain were `not available`. Fixed.** I fetched the PubMed record for PMID 26745798 with `efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=26745798&retmode=xml`; its ArticleTitle matches PXD002106. Its MeSH headings include `Male` (D008297) and `Rats, Wistar` (D017208). I set `characteristics[sex]` = `male` and `characteristics[strain or breed]` = `Wistar` (EFO:0001342 in live OLS) on all 10 rows. I did not upgrade the strain to "Wistar Hannover", and I added no age or stage, because the full text is unavailable. `characteristics[developmental stage]` stays `not available`.
- **Minor finding 1: N-terminal C modification. Fixed.** The mzIdentML gives -17.026549 Da on N-terminal C, with Carbamidomethyl C fixed, so the delta is relative to carbamidomethyl-Cys. Unimod defines UNIMOD:26 Pyro-carbamidomethyl relative to *unmodified* Cys. Its own description says: "For a delta relative to alkylated Cys, see Ammonia-loss". UNIMOD:385 Ammonia-loss has the specificity "C, Any N-term, Pyro-carbamidomethyl as a delta from Carbamidomethyl-Cys". The modification is now `NT=Ammonia-loss;AC=UNIMOD:385;TA=C;PP=Any N-term;MT=Variable`.
- **Minor finding 2: organism part. Not changed.** The sample is tubulointerstitium, meaning tubules plus interstitium, so a tubule-only term such as UBERON "nephron tubule" or "renal cortex tubule" would overstate it. The record also does not say cortex vs medulla. I kept `kidney`. PubMed MeSH lists both "Kidney" and "Kidney Tubules", which is consistent with this.

The header gives the method instrument "Orbitrap Elite MS", tune file `Elite_nano_20130410`, method `TOP7_Labelfree_240K_...` (240K resolution is only possible on an Elite) and data folder `2013-10-05_OrbitrapElite_Kidney-FFPE-tubuli`. - Dissociation CID (`MS:1000133`), collision energy `35 NCE`, MS2 analyzer `radial ejection linear ion trap` (`MS:1000083`, ITMS scans), MS1 scan range `300m/z-2000m/z`. All from the raw method header.
- Trypsin `MS:1001251`. Sources: the PRIDE sample protocol ("Trypsin Porcine, Promega") and the mzIdentML (2 missed cleavages).
- Precursor tolerance 10 ppm and fragment tolerance 0.5 Da, from the mzIdentML `ParentTolerance` / `FragmentTolerance` (SearchGUI/PeptideShaker 0.38.0).
- Modifications are from the mzIdentML `ModificationParams`:
  - fixed: Carbamidomethyl C (UNIMOD:4)
  - variable: Oxidation M (UNIMOD:35), Acetyl protein N-term (UNIMOD:1), Gln->pyro-Glu N-term Q (UNIMOD:28), Glu->pyro-Glu N-term E (UNIMOD:27), and Ammonia-loss N-term C (UNIMOD:385; see the revision note above)

## Limitations

- **Paper full text unavailable.** The Nephron article is not open access, and the Karger page was bot-blocked. Sample facts come from the PRIDE protocols, the abstract, the PubMed MeSH headings, the raw-file names and the deposited mzIdentML/raw headers.
- **Sex and strain come from MeSH, not per-sample evidence.** `male` and `Wistar` come from the publication's PubMed MeSH headings, which are indexer-assigned and study-level. They are applied to every row because the study describes a single animal cohort. The sub-strain is not given: the sister paper PMC4240179 says Wistar Hannover, but it is not proven to share these animals.
- **Developmental stage is `not available`.** No age is given in the available record.
- **Disease terms.** `renovascular hypertension` (DOID:1591) for 2K1C rows matches the PRIDE disease field and the 2K1C model. `normal` for sham rows follows the run names and abstract. The analysed tissue is the *non-clipped* kidney, and its damage is secondary to hypertension, which the abstract calls "minimally damaged".
- **Organism part** is `kidney`. The tissue is microdissected tubulointerstitium, but the protocol does not say whether it came from cortex or medulla, and tubule-only terms would exclude the interstitium. FFPE preservation is not captured, because the template has no column for it. I omitted cell type: this is a tissue sample, and the PRIDE "epithelial cell" dropdown is not a per-sample statement.
- **Biological replicate numbering** (within group, 1-4 and 1-6) is my own convention. The rat numbers 1-10 are kept in the source names.
- **Instrument** is Orbitrap Elite, despite a "Velos Pro" configured name in the raw header and the sister paper (reasoning above).
- **The N-terminal C mod is interpreted.** The mzIdentML lists a variable N-terminal C modification of -17.026549 Da labelled UNIMOD:28 "Gln->pyro-Glu", a PeptideShaker 0.38 mapping artefact (UNIMOD:28 has no C site). It is encoded as Ammonia-loss (UNIMOD:385), the Unimod entry for this delta relative to carbamidomethyl-Cys.
- **Reduction/alkylation reagents are omitted.** The PRIDE protocol for this dataset does not name them. Carbamidomethyl C was searched as fixed, and the sister paper used DTT/iodoacetamide.
- **No fractionation.** Each sample is one raw file. Fraction identifier 1 and technical replicate 1 are set by `build`.
