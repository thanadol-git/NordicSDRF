# PXD022177 BLOCKED: TMT11 channel-to-sample map not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Department of Clinical Science, University of Bergen (lab PI Simona Chera). iPSC-derived pancreatic progenitors differentiated to stage 7 with 20 mM (standard), 25 or 30 mM glucose, compared with native human islets; one TMT11-plex, 24 basic-pH RP fractions (`m23166…m23190.raw`, 24 RAW), SPS-MS3 on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, in-house Sequest pipeline. Paper: Ghila L et al., Int J Mol Sci 2021, PMID 33918250, PMC8038174 (open access, read).

## Why it cannot be annotated
The conditions and group sizes are stated only as a figure legend ("n = 2,3,3,3": islets, 20, 25, 30 mM glucose; 11 channels) and the PRIDE text says "all 10 samples". Neither PRIDE nor the paper text gives which TMT channel (126-131N/C) carries which condition or replicate, nor which channel is unused. No sample sheet is deposited (files are 24 `.raw` plus 24 `.mzIdentML` search results). Assigning channels would be invention.

## Verified facts for a future annotation
Homo sapiens; hiPSC-derived pancreatic progenitors (stage 5 onwards exposed to 20/25/30 mM glucose) and human islets from deceased donors; TMT11plex; Orbitrap Fusion Lumos; Sequest: 50 ppm precursor, 0.9 Da product ion, fixed TMT (K, N-term) and carbamidomethyl C, variable Oxidation M. Possible unblock: author contact (simona.chera@uib.no) or the paper's Figure 1 source data.
