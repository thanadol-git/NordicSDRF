# PXD042238 — blocked annotation

The deposited protocol describes label-free co-immunoprecipitation of HeLa cells transfected with either pcDNA3 control or pcDNA-ZC3H11A(wt)-Flag. It also identifies three biological replicates and two gel fractions per sample.

However, the 18 RAW files are labelled only as `220512_{1,2,3}{A,B,C}-{1,2}.raw`. The deposited files and metadata contain no key relating A/B/C to the stated transfection conditions. Assigning those treatment labels would therefore be fabricated. A complete SDRF should be created after obtaining the sample sheet or a deposited A/B/C condition key.
