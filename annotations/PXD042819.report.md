# PXD042819 annotation report

## Evidence

- PRIDE deposits 28 label-free mouse Neuro-2a RAW files for ADAR3 cell-state profiling and ADAR3 co-immunoprecipitation.
- RAW filenames explicitly encode the N2a control/ADAR3 and `diff`/`un` groups, or the co-IP bait group (`T3` or `Ctrl`) and RNAse status, plus replicate number.
- PRIDE specifies DDA acquisition on Orbitrap Fusion for N2a cells and Orbitrap Eclipse for co-IP samples, processed with Proteome Discoverer/SEQUEST against mouse UniProt.

## Decisions

- Submitted group identifiers are retained literally; `diff`, `un`, `T3`, and `Ctrl` are not expanded beyond the deposited labels.
- All 28 deposited RAW files are represented. The deposit does not state digestion or modification parameters, so neither is inferred.

The two RAW files for `Ctrl-plusRNAse` biological replicate 2 have distinct deposited filenames, so the second source is suffixed `-rep2` to make the row coordinate unique while preserving the submitted group and biological replicate.
