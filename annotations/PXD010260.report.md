# PXD010260

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

- Title: "Muscular invasiveness in bladder cancer". Lab: Magnus Berle, Department of Clinical Medicine, University of Bergen (Bergen, Norway); samples were collected at Haukeland University Hospital, Bergen. Paper: Berle M et al., "Novel protein signatures suggest progression to muscular invasiveness in bladder cancer", PLoS One 2018;13(11):e0206475 (PMID 30419021, PMC6231613, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0206475).
- Study: label-free DDA proteomics (Q Exactive HF) of paired cold-cup biopsies from patients with primary non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC). Each patient gave one biopsy from the tumour (T) and one from macroscopically normal bladder on the opposite side of the bladder (N). A pooled internal control (QC, an equal mix of all samples) was injected six times.

## Templates

- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0: mass spectrometry (Thermo .raw, Q Exactive HF).
- `human` v1.1.0: human patients (PRIDE organism NEWT:9606, human SwissProt search, patients at Haukeland University Hospital).
- `oncology-metadata` v1.0.0: a cancer study (tumour vs. normal biopsies). It adds only optional columns. Per-patient tumour stage and grade were **not** added, because the paper's Table 1 cannot be tied to the raw files with certainty (see Limitations).
- Not used: `clinical-metadata` (no treatment arm), `dia-acquisition` (the acquisition is DDA).

## Design and file mapping

- 26 RAW files in PRIDE (all `projectAccessions` = PXD010260), each claimed once. The search/quant results are three .xlsx files.
- File names follow `20150528_MB_<T|N><n>.raw` for patients n = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 (there is no n = 6), plus `20150528_MB_QC_1..5.raw` and `20150528_MB_QC6.raw`.
- Sources: one source per patient and tissue (`patient<n>_tumor`, `patient<n>_normal`; 20 sources, 1 run each), and one `QC_pool` source with 6 injections (`comment[technical replicate]` 1-6, biological replicate `pooled`, pooled sample `pooled`).
- `characteristics[individual]` = `patient_<n>` links each patient's N and T biopsies. The pairing comes from the file names, and the paper itself uses the same convention ("the tumor samples T1 & T8" of "outliers N1 & N8").
- Which file IDs belong to the 8 included patients: the deposited `Bladdercancer_Progenesisquantification_Berle.xlsx` (the Q-value table the paper analyses) contains only T/N 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11. The full `Bladdercancer_AllIdentifications_Berle.xlsx` also contains T/N 4 and 10. So file IDs 4 and 10 are the "two additional patients ... excluded due to pathology not matching the inclusion criteria".
- Factor: `factor value[disease]` (tumour vs. normal biopsy):
  - `superficial urinary bladder carcinoma` (MONDO:0004200, "carcinoma of the bladder that has not invaded into the muscularis propria", i.e. NMIBC): tumour biopsies of the 8 included patients (8 rows).
  - `not available`: tumour biopsies of excluded patients 4 and 10 (2 rows). Their pathology did not match the inclusion criteria and is not described.
  - `normal`: the 10 macroscopically normal biopsies.
  - `not applicable`: the 6 QC-pool injections (a mix of tumour and normal material).
- Organism part `urinary bladder` for every row. Material type `tissue`.
- Technical values (from the PRIDE protocols and the paper's Methods, identical): Q Exactive HF (confirmed from the RAW header of `20150528_MB_T1.raw`: "Q Exactive HF - Orbitrap MS"); DDA, Top15; HCD (beam-type CID) at NCE 28%; Orbitrap MS2 (R = 30,000); MS1 375-1500 m/z; trypsin; DTT reduction in the lysis buffer; iodoacetamide alkylation (FASP); Carbamidomethyl C fixed and Oxidation M variable; precursor tolerance 5 ppm, fragment tolerance 0.05 Da (Proteome Discoverer 1.4.1, Sequest HT and MS Amanda, on Progenesis QI-exported MGF). Label free; no fractionation (fraction identifier 1).

## Limitations

- **Age, sex, ancestry: `not available` on every row.** The paper's Table 1 gives age, sex, stage (pTa/pT1), WHO grade and recurrence for patients numbered 1-8. The raw files use IDs 1-5, 7-11, so the paper renumbered patients and no deposited table gives the key.
  - The PCA in this run on the deposited Progenesis quantification puts T3, N1 and N11 apart from their groups. That matches the paper's outliers T3, N1 and N8, which suggests paper patient 8 = file ID 11 and supports an order-preserving renumbering (file 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11 → paper 1-8).
  - Because this is inference, not a deposited mapping, Table 1 demographics, stage, grade and recurrence were **not** written to the SDRF. A curator who confirms the key with the authors could add them.
- **Disease of excluded patients 4 and 10 (tumour rows): `not available`.** They were operated as bladder-tumour patients, but the paper says only that their pathology did not match the inclusion criteria.
- **Disease of normal biopsies = `normal`.** These are macroscopically normal bladder biopsies taken from cancer patients, not healthy donors. The paper's own finding is that two of them (paper N1/N8) carry cancer-like proteome signatures.
- **Tumour disease term**: MONDO:0004200 rests on "primary NMIBC" and the pTa/pT1 stages in Table 1. The histological type (urothelial) is not stated explicitly.
- `characteristics[developmental stage]` omitted. The included cohort is 55-85 years (Table 1; the Methods text says "55-76", which contradicts Table 1 patient 3 = 85), but no ages are given for excluded patients 4 and 10.
- `characteristics[cell type]` = `not available` (whole-tissue biopsies).
- `characteristics[sample type]` omitted (validator pitfall: `study sample` is rejected; no other PRIDE sample-type term fits).
- Treatment-naive status (an inclusion criterion: no prior mitomycin/BCG) was not added, because it is not established for excluded patients 4 and 10.
- The QC pool is described as "an equal mix of all samples". Whether excluded patients 4 and 10 contributed is not stated, so `characteristics[pooled sample]` = `pooled` without an SN= list.
- Modifications come from the PRIDE data-processing protocol and the paper. No machine-readable search-parameter file is deposited (only .xlsx exports), so `sdrf-tools search-params` could not be applied.
- No BioSamples accessions exist for this project.
- Mapping provenance: sample identity and tissue (T/N/QC) come from **file names**. Included vs. excluded patients come from the **deposited quantification table**. Paper-patient ↔ file-ID is an **inference** that is **not used** in the SDRF.
