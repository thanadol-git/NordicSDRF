# PXD019098 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** A systems approach delivers a functional microRNA catalog and expanded targets for seizure suppression in temporal lobe epilepsy
- **PRIDE record:** PXD019098
- **Publication:** Venø MT, … *PNAS* 2020;117(27):15977, DOI [10.1073/pnas.1919313117](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1919313117), PMID 32581127, PMC7355001

## Evidence recovered

- 102 RAW: TMTsixplex sets of rat hippocampus (`…_GroupC_T1F1…`, `…GroupE…`, `…grp_F_t1/t2_F1–17`), MaxQuant 1.5.3.30 `ProteinGroups_20180319_FinalDataset.txt` with reporter columns per group (`Reporter.intensity.corrected.0–5.Group0/B/C/E…`).
- Protocol: perforant-pathway stimulation time course (1 h, 24 h, 72 h, 10 d, 16 d, early and chronic epilepsy) plus controls; "Sets of 6 samples were labelled using the TMTsixplex"; each set normalised to a common day-16 channel.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Eight conditions are distributed over 6-plex sets, and the channel (0–5) → time point/animal assignment per group is given neither in the MaxQuant output (generic reporter indices), the PRIDE protocol, nor the main-text Methods (proteomics details are in the PNAS SI, which wasn't machine-readable here).

## Sources checked

PRIDE record and `files/all`; header of `ProteinGroups_20180319_FinalDataset.txt` (range read); PMC7355001 (NCBI efetch).

## Unblocking requirement

Per TMT group (0, B, C, E, F…): reporter channel 0–5 → time point / animal, and group → RAW prefix mapping.
