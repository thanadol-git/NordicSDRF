## Dataset

- **Title:** Use of hide glue in 4th millennium BC Europe
- **Lab / city:** Enrico Cappellini, Centre for GeoGenetics, Natural History Museum of Denmark, University of Copenhagen (Copenhagen, Denmark); MS acquisition in the Jesper V. Olsen group (raw method paths `C:\Xcalibur\methods\JVO\...`).

The paper is not indexed in PubMed or Europe PMC and no open-access copy was found (Unpaywall: none; Elsevier API refused). **The paper text was not read**; every value comes from the PRIDE record and the deposited files.
- **Study:** wood flakes from two areas under two different bark strips of a Neolithic bow (Parkhaus-Opéra, Zurich; 3175-3157 BC) were acid-extracted, trypsin-digested and analysed by LC-MS/MS to identify the animal glue; one extraction blank (negative control, no ancient material) was processed in parallel.

## Limitations
- **Revised after independent review:** `factor value[organism]` was removed. Species is the study's finding, not a condition, and encoding the bow-vs-blank contrast as Bovidae vs `not applicable` left the blank without a factor level. The natural encoding, `characteristics[sample type]` = study sample / negative control, cannot be used: the offline PRIDE ontology cache used by `parse_sdrf --use_ols_cache_only` (and by upstream CI) rejects `study sample` in both bare and NT/AC form. The SDRF therefore has no factor column (advisory only in CI); the extraction blank is identified by its source name and by organism `not applicable`. `characteristics[disease]` = `not applicable` was added. The blank was acquired separately (2011-08-10, 90-min QC method) from the bow samples (2010, 150-min method); the bow RAW files were originally acquired as `...Enrico-Parchments_S40` and `...Eske_Ancient_Skin_Sample_47` and renamed by the submitter.


- **Organism is inferred at family level.** `Bovidae` (NCBITaxon:9895) is the lowest common taxon of the two organisms the submitter declared in PRIDE (`Bos taurus`, `Ovis aries`). The paper, which would say which species each sample gave, could not be read; the per-sample species call was not re-derived from the search results (that would be a research result, not metadata). A curator with the paper may be able to replace `Bovidae` with a species per sample.
- **Organism part is `not available`** on the bow rows. The title says "hide glue" (i.e. skin), but that is the authors' conclusion, the paper could not be checked, and collagen type I alone does not prove skin over bone. The physical sample is wood flakes carrying an adhesive residue, not an anatomical tissue.
- **Acquisition names belong to other batch labels.** The RAW headers show the bow files were acquired as `...COLLAB_Enrico-Parchments_S40` (2010-10-15) and `...Eske_Ancient_Skin_Sample_47` (2010-12-15), i.e. inside other projects' queues. The submitter renamed them `Zurich_bowS40/S47` and searched them under those names, and the sample numbers are preserved, so the mapping follows the deposit; it cannot be verified further without the paper or the lab's sample sheet.
- **Blank acquired later.** The protocol says the negative control was "processed together with the samples", yet it was acquired on 2011-08-10 (method `JVO_90min_Top10HCD`) versus 2010 (`150min_Top10HCD`) for the bow samples. Annotated as deposited; the different gradient length is not captured in a column.
- **Instrument contradicts PRIDE** (Q Exactive in PRIDE, LTQ Orbitrap Velos in every RAW header); RAW header used.
- **Precursor tolerance** (6 ppm) comes from the protocol prose, not from the search files.
- **Collision energy** (40 NCE) is read from the method text in the RAW header (first 256 KB only).
- The extended searches (`*_ExtSearch_txt.zip`, per-species reference proteomes) were not opened; the protocol says they used the same enzyme and modification settings.
- `characteristics[disease]`, `cell type` and `sample type` are omitted (not meaningful for an archaeological residue / see the factor note above).
