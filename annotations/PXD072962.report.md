# PXD072962 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Spatio-temporal proteome remodelling of right and left ventricular free walls (FFPE) in a tamoxifen-inducible cardiomyocyte-specific Pkp2 knockout mouse model of arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy (7-21 days post induction vs Cre- controls), plus a TMTpro18 experiment of Honokiol-treated vs untreated knockout ventricles.

## Templates
ms-proteomics + vertebrates v1.1.0

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
All data files: SpatioTemporal_Raw.zip = 77 dia-PASEF runs (incl. QC pools) + 24 dda-PASEF library fractions (101 .d folders); Honokiol_Raw.zip = 24 TMTpro18 fractions x 18 channels (432 rows).

## Sample map
Deposited SpatioTemporal_meta.xlsx: run -> analytical id, time (dpi), region, block, animal id, group, genetics. Honokiol_meta.xlsx: TMT channel -> sample, region, Honokiol +/-, animal id. Folder names inside the zips matched every sheet entry (101/101; 24 TMT fractions).

## Key decisions
Data files are the .d folders / RAW files inside the two deposited zips (comment[file container]). Cre- controls: disease 'normal', time 7 dpi per sheet. Honokiol animals: genotype PKP2cKO assumed (record: Honokiol given to knockout animals); their time point is not in the sheet -> 'not available'. Strain, sex, age not stated. TCEP/CAA, trypsin + Lys-C; TMTpro K and N-term fixed; base mods CAM, Met-ox, N-term acetyl. diaPASEF dissociation CID by convention.

## Not available / not applicable
mouse strain, sex, age; Honokiol cohort time point and dose.
