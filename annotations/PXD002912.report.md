# PXD002912

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

**Expressed Proteome of Extraintestinal Escherichia coli Associated with Urinary Tract Infections.** This is a label-free DDA study of bacterial whole-cell lysates from the Gade Research Group (H. G. Wiker, V. K. Pettersen), Department of Clinical Science, University of Bergen, Norway. MS was run at PROBE, the Bergen proteomics unit. The study compares five clinical ExPEC isolates purified from blood cultures of sepsis patients at Haukeland University Hospital, together with reference UPEC strain 536. Strain 536 was grown in anaerobic and aerobic blood culture, on blood agar and on lactose agar.
Paper: Pettersen VK, Mosevoll KA, Lindemann PC, Wiker HG. *Mol Cell Proteomics* 2016;15(9). PMID 27364158, PMC5013306, doi:10.1074/mcp.M116.060582.

## Technical values
- **Instrument:** `NT=Orbitrap Elite;AC=MS:1001910`. The PRIDE dropdown says LTQ Orbitrap Velos (MS:1001742) and the paper says "LTQ Orbitrap", but the evidence points to the Elite:
  - The raw headers name the method instrument "Orbitrap Elite MS", and every acquisition folder is named `..._OrbitrapElite_...`.
  - The MS1 resolution of 240,000 at m/z 400 is only reachable on the Elite.
- **Cleavage agent:** Lys-C (`MS:1001309`) on `_L` rows and Trypsin (`MS:1001251`) on `_T` rows, from MED-FASP "using LysC and trypsin". The original names agree: `LF`/`_L` for the Lys-C digest and `TF`/`_T` for the tryptic one.
- **Modifications:** read from the deposited MaxQuant 1.5.3.28 `parameters.txt`, matching the paper Methods:

  - Variable: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Acetyl (K), Gln->pyro-Glu, Glu->pyro-Glu.
- **Tolerances:**
  - Precursor 20 ppm. This is the paper's initial (first-search) precursor tolerance; the main-search tolerance was not deposited.
  - Fragment 0.5 Da (`MS/MS tol. (ITMS)`).
- **Other run settings:**
  - Dissociation: CID.
  - MS2 analyzer: radial ejection linear ion trap.
  - MS1 scan range: 300–2000 m/z.
  - Acquisition: DDA, `PRIDE:0000627`.

## How the SDRF was produced
- `samples.tsv` was generated by `scratchpad/PXD002912/make_samples.py` from the PRIDE file list and the RAW-header original names. `technical.tsv` was written by hand.
- The SDRF itself was built with `sdrf-tools build -t ms-proteomics`.
- **One post-build step:** `build` gives one value per sample row, but the two digests of an injection used different enzymes. `scratchpad/PXD002912/fill_cleavage.py` therefore filled only `comment[cleavage agent details]` from the file suffix (`_L` gives Lys-C, `_T` gives Trypsin). The script asserts that `_L` is fraction 1 and `_T` is fraction 2. A diff confirmed that no other column differs from the `build` output.

## Limitations
- **No raw-file → experiment map was deposited.** There is no `mqpar.xml` or `evidence.txt`. Replicate structure therefore rests on the RAW-header original names, paper Table I (cultures per strain) and the MaxQuant experiment names.
- **The meaning of the second digit in the sample codes is inferred.**
  - C4n: Table I lists one culture per condition, so C11/C12 and C31/C32 are treated as two sub-samples of one culture (technical replicates).
  - E4n and UPEC 536 blood cultures: E11/E12 and W11/W12 are treated the same way.
  - D4n: D21/D22 are treated as the two anaerobic cultures in Table I. The paper's design statement ("two or four blood cultures from a patient") requires two analysed D4n cultures, and D21/D22 are the only distinct samples. A reading in which they are two sub-samples of one culture cannot be fully excluded.
- **G5m was acquired under the original names `C11`–`C14`** (2015-07-03, `Labelfree-randomized` sequence), not `G…`. The assignment to G5m rests on two things:
  - the depositor's renaming to GO11/GN12/GO13/GN14;
  - MaxQuant having exactly 8 C experiments (matching the 05-22/05-26 C4n files) plus 4 G experiments G1–G4.
  - The anaerobic/aerobic split for G (and for F) comes from the deposited N/O tokens only.
- **UPEC 536 groups were acquired under different codes.** Blood-culture files are `W*`, blood agar `U*` and lactose agar `L*`. The condition assignment follows the deposited names (536N/O, 536BA, 536LA). The first digit of the W codes (W1 = aerobic, W2 = anaerobic) follows the deposited 536O1x/536N2x names.
- **Technical replicate numbers flatten two levels.** For C4n they combine sub-sample and injection into 1–4.
- **`_L` = Lys-C and `_T` = trypsin is inferred** from the file names and the MED-FASP protocol.
- **The control mixed sample is pooled, but its exact list of bottles is not given.** The paper names its five strains (F4d is not included). The `SN=` list was rejected by the validator, so the cell reads `pooled`. The strain column is free text.
- **Organism:** clinical isolates are `Escherichia coli`, UPEC 536 rows are `Escherichia coli 536`, and the pool is `Escherichia coli`.
- **`characteristics[disease]` and `organism part` are `not applicable`**, because the samples are bacteria. Host context and patient age/sex (Table I) are not encoded.
- **Not annotated:**
  - Reduction and alkylation reagents (not stated, although Carbamidomethyl is fixed in the search).
  - Collision energy.
  - Acquisition dates. The header folder dates are sequence dates, not per-run timestamps.
- **`characteristics[sample type]` is omitted**, per campaign rules.
