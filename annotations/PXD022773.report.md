## Limitations

- Concentrations of GAKi/GAKc/DFP for the MS experiment not in the deposit (paper: DFP 1 mM 24 h typical; 10 uM GAKi in other experiments); omitted from values.
- red/blue/black in phospho file names unexplained; treated as technical replicates, order by date is arbitrary.
- Final_STY_mqpar.xml labels CJK_190918_Sachin_black_Gaki_pluss_190924143754.raw GAKI_Plus_3 (duplicate); assigned replicate 4 by pattern.
- Proteome and phospho replicates (esp. 5-8, acquired in different batches) may not share lysates; separate source names used.
- U2OS age 15Y, female, bone, osteosarcoma from Cellosaurus, not the deposit. Cell type, passage, ancestry, storage temperature not available. Reduction reagent not stated; alkylation reagent not named.
- Precursor tolerance 4.5 ppm from mqpar (paper says 6 ppm). Fragment 20 ppm is the MaxQuant FTMS default in the file.
- PRIDE lists submissionType PARTIAL; the deposited result files (proteinGroups, Phospho STY sites) were not used for sample mapping.
