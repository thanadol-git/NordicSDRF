# PXD004274 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Comparative ploidy proteomics of *Candida albicans* biofilms unraveled the role of AHP1 in the biofilm persistence against amphotericin B.

- Truong T et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 2016;15(11):3488-3500.
- DOI 10.1074/mcp.M116.061523, PMID 27644984, PMCID PMC5098045.
- The paper cites PXD004274 in its Abstract and Acknowledgments, so the PX record links the right paper.
- Lab: Seneviratne, Faculty of Dentistry, National University of Singapore.

## Scope, evidence and decisions
Status: **annotated**. The file has 8 rows (1 deposited peak-list file x 8 iTRAQ channels) and 29 columns. The only template is `ms-proteomics`.

### Deposited vs annotated
- ProteomeXchange PROXI (`resultType=full`) lists exactly two files: `RAW.mzml` (Associated raw file URI) and `SEARCH.xml` (search engine output). The record is flagged "Unsupported dataset by repository", i.e. a partial submission.
- Cross-checks failed:
  - The PRIDE v3 `files/all` endpoint returned an upstream connect error.
  - The PRIDE FTP over HTTPS returned 403.
- The campaign cache ("1 RAW / 2 files") matches PROXI.
- No vendor `.wiff` file is deposited. `RAW.mzml` holds all eight channels and is annotated 8 times, once per channel. `SEARCH.xml` is a search result, not a run, so it is not annotated.
- 1/1 run is annotated.
- The BigBio CI emits an advisory, `peak_list_data_file`, because the data file is an mzML rather than a vendor raw file. That is what was deposited.

### Samples
- **Organism:** *Candida albicans*, NCBITaxon:5476. The species is used rather than the strain taxon 237561 (SC5314), because the other three strains have no taxon of their own.
- **Strain:** goes in `characteristics[strain or breed]` and is the factor value.
- **Ploidy:** this is the study contrast, but no template-licensed column exists for it. It is carried in the source name (`<strain>_<ploidy>_biofilm72h_repN`).
- **Growth:** 72 h biofilms in GMM at 37 °C, in 96-well plates (Methods).

### Template licensing
`characteristics[strain or breed]` is formally licensed only by the vertebrates, invertebrates and plants templates, and none of these fits a fungus. I followed campaign and BigBio precedent for microbes, which is `ms-proteomics` plus strain or breed (e.g. PXD002168 *S. cerevisiae* BY4742, PXD001897, PXD002147). This is a deliberate exception to the licensing rule.

### Replicates
- The Methods say "Two biofilm samples of each strain ... were obtained as the technical replicates".
- The Fig. 1 title calls them "biological replicates".
- Each one is a separately grown biofilm in its own iTRAQ channel. I encoded them as distinct sources with `biological replicate` 1/2 and `technical replicate` 1. The paper itself conflicts here; see Review attack points.

### Technical values
- **Instrument:** TripleTOF 5600 (MS:1000932). PX and the paper agree. The mzML header could not be read because EBI FTP/HTTPS was unavailable.
- **Acquisition:** Information Dependent Acquisition, top 20, 15 s exclusion (Data-dependent acquisition, PRIDE:0000627).
- **MS1 scan range:** 400-1800 m/z (stated).
- **Dissociation:** MS:1000422, beam-type CID, **INFERRED**. The paper says only "rolling collision energy"; the 5600 fragments in a beam-type collision cell (CAD). Collision energy is omitted because it is rolling.
- **Enzyme:** trypsin, 1:20, 37 °C, 16 h (stated).
- **Reduction and alkylation:** TCEP (PRIDE:0000609) and MMTS (PRIDE:0000605), both stated.
- **Modifications:** from the ProteinPilot 4.5 / Paragon parameters stated in Methods ("Sample Type: iTRAQ 8-plex (Peptide Labeled); Cysteine Alkylation: MMTS"):
  - Methylthio C fixed (UNIMOD:39)
  - iTRAQ8plex K fixed (UNIMOD:730)
  - iTRAQ8plex N-term fixed (UNIMOD:730)
  - The PX record lists MOD:01549, iTRAQ8plex-116 reporter+balance reagent acylated residue, which is consistent.
  - Paragon's "ID Focus: Biological Modification" searches many variable modifications probabilistically without a fixed list. No variable modifications are enumerated, so none are annotated.
  - `SEARCH.xml` could not be read (EBI unavailable).
- **Fraction identifier:** 1. Only one data file is deposited.

### Values left `not available` / `not applicable`
- `comment[fractionation method]` = `not available`. The Methods say the peptides were "subjected to strong cation-exchange chromatography using iTRAQ method development kit" and then desalted. Fig. 1 shows "SCX and Desalting" as one step. No fraction count is given, and only one mzML is deposited. It is unknown whether SCX was a clean-up or a fractionation whose fractions were merged into one mzML.
- Precursor and fragment mass tolerance = `not available`. Paragon has no user-set tolerances and the paper states none.
- `characteristics[organism part]` = `not applicable` (unicellular organism, whole biofilm).
- `characteristics[disease]` = `not applicable`.

### Conflicts
- The paper's search parameters list "Database: Algae Transcriptome.fasta", but the same paragraph says UniProtKB *C. albicans* SC5314 (14,851 entries). This looks like a copy-paste error in the paper. It does not affect any SDRF column.
- Technical vs biological replicate wording (see Replicates above).

### Stated vs INFERRED
- **Stated:** channel map, strains, ploidy, instrument, acquisition, MS1 range, enzyme, reduction/alkylation reagents, iTRAQ 8-plex and MMTS search settings.
- **INFERRED:** dissociation type (beam-type CID); replicate encoding (bio rep 1/2 vs the paper's "technical").

## Review attack points
1. `ms-proteomics` + `characteristics[strain or breed]` for a fungus (licensing exception, precedent-based).
2. Replicate encoding: the paper calls the two biofilms per strain "technical replicates"; I annotated them as biological replicates 1/2.
3. The single `RAW.mzml` may be a merged SCX-fraction peak list; fraction/fractionation is unknown, and the mzML header was not read.
4. Dissociation = beam-type CID is inferred from the instrument. Variable modifications are unannotated (Paragon biological-modification focus).
