## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- Each HSPC replicate is pooled from several sorts (Supplementary Fig. 2a) of equal numbers of male and female mice, so sex = pooled; adult mice were 10–20 weeks old, fetal age is captured by the E14.5 developmental stage.
- The two thiol pools of one lysate are separate source names (…_SH, …_Sox); the pool is described in characteristics[treatment].
- reconcile reads only one of the two cleavage-agent columns (Trypsin and Lys-C are both annotated).
- The validator warns that 'pooled' is not in the installed vertebrates template's sex list; the SDRF spec (README, TERMS.tsv) allows 'pooled' for pooled samples.
