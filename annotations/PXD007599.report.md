## Design (PX description, submitter-authored)

Pitcher fluid of *N. ampullaria*, *N. rafflesiana* and the hybrid *N.* × *hookeriana*, collected
within 24 h of pitcher opening; 2 biological replicates per species, each a pool of 9 pitchers;
3 technical replicates each; trypsin; Easy-nLC + Orbitrap Fusion Tribrid.
