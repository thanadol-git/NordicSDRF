## Conservative choices

- **Templates:** `ms-proteomics` + `human`. Host-derived EV isolates searched against UniProt (bacterial + human proteins); metaproteomics not applied.
- **Organism:** `Homo sapiens` on EV rows. Controls are PBS-gradient blanks → `not applicable` (do not invent bacterial NCBITaxon).
- **Organism part / factor:** `amniotic fluid` (UBERON:0000173) and `feces` (UBERON:0001988); controls `not applicable`.
- **A1219a/b:** same source `A1219`, technical replicates 1–2 (two injections of one amniotic sample). Paper notes a twin pregnancy “designated A and B”; donor ID 1219 is still one mother (`F1219` matches).
- **Sex:** `female` (entire cohort is pregnant women). Controls `not applicable`.
- **Age:** cohort summary only (Table S1 not in evidence) → `not available` on every row (required human column; `not applicable` is not allowed for age).
- **Developmental stage:** `adult` from the pregnant-women cohort description; controls `not applicable`.
- **Disease:** `not available` on EV rows (no per-sample diagnoses in the local record); controls `not applicable`. Not broadcast as `normal`.
- **Cell type:** `not applicable` (EV isolates, not a CL cell type).
- **Ancestry:** `not available` (study samples) / `not applicable` (controls).
- **Sample type column dropped after round 1:** `study sample` / `negative control` are valid PRIDE children online (`PRIDE:0001013` / `PRIDE:0000905`) but `study sample` is missing from the local OLS cache. Factor is organism part only.
