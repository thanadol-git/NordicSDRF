# PXD035497 SDRF annotation report

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending

Dataset: PET and monomer degradation by Ideonella sakaiensis (Aalborg University, Denmark; Poulsen and Nielsen, J Proteomics 2023, PMID 36965770). No pre-existing annotation was found in sdrf-annotated-datasets (Step 0.5).

File: PXD035497.sdrf.tsv, 28 rows x 29 columns, template ms-proteomics v1.1.0. 28 RAW files, one row each, label-free, unfractionated, DDA, Q Exactive HF, HCD NCE 28, MaxQuant 1.6.10.43, trypsin (single protease), 4.5 ppm / 20 ppm.

Design: 7 groups x 4 biological replicates: control (C), ethylene glycol (EG), ethyl glycolate (EEG), sodium glyoxylate monohydrate (SGM), terephthalic acid (TPA), plus two numeric groups (1-4 and 1+-4+) that are presumably PET-exposed but whose identity could not be established. Factor: treatment.

Validation: parse_sdrf validate-sdrf passes with 3 warnings (treatment terms ethyl glycolate, sodium glyoxylate monohydrate, terephthalic acid not found in NCIT/EFO); tools check CLEAN; structure OK; reconcile clean.

Caveats
- Paper is paywalled; the file-name-to-condition legend is inferred from abbreviations (EEG = ethyl glycolate in particular is a guess). Groups 1-4 and 1+-4+ have treatment "not available"; the "+" meaning is unknown. Their RAW files are about 4x larger (~1.8 GB), suggesting a different gradient/method; not checked.
- Organism written as the current NCBITaxon label Pseudideonella sakaiensis (NCBITaxon:1547922) = PRIDE's Ideonella sakaiensis.
- Instrument taken from the PRIDE field, not RAW headers. Alkylation reagent inferred. Growth conditions, time points and sample-prep batches not captured.
- Independent review (Step 9.5) was not performed: subagents were forbidden for this run.
