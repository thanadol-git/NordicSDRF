# PXD032835 annotation report

## Evidence used

- PRIDE metadata and protocol describe a PISA experiment in BV2 cells: five flasks received 20 µM DEVD-FMK for two hours and five received DMSO control, followed by TMT10 labeling and 24 high-pH fractions.
- Deposited `BertrandJ_PISA_TMT-10_Labelling.xlsx` explicitly maps every channel to a submitted treated/control flask identifier and replicate number.
- The protocol specifies Lys-C/trypsin digestion, iodoacetamide alkylation, Q Exactive HF data-dependent acquisition, and 24 concatenated fractions.

## Annotation scope

The SDRF represents all 24 deposited RAW fraction files crossed with the 10 deposited TMT channels (240 assay rows). It preserves the workbook’s source IDs (T1–T5 and C1–C5) and represents their stated treatment as **DEVD-FMK** or **DMSO**.
