## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- PRIDE lists the organism part as 'Monocyte' and the disease as 'Lymphoma'; the paper names only the mast cell line HMC-1 for the exRNA isolates, so Cellosaurus values are used.
- The paper also cultured TF-1 cells, but the proteomics section and PRIDE describe HD/LD fractions from HMC-1 only.
- Samples were alkylated with MMTS but searched with carbamidomethyl C as a variable modification; the searched modifications are annotated. The search allowed semi-tryptic peptides.
- Only 18 and 16 of the 20 SCX fractions were deposited/searched; fraction numbers follow file order, not the SCX fraction numbers, which are not recorded. Velos_130122_98 was acquired after 72–74 (71 is absent) and may be a re-run; it is numbered LD fraction 16.
- The HD/LD distinction is a density-gradient fraction of conditioned medium, not a PTM/peptide enrichment, so it is recorded in a custom column characteristics[density gradient fraction] (values 'high density'/'low density') and the matching factor value; the gradient details are in this report.
- One isolate per density fraction was analysed (biological replicate 1).
