# PXD018051 merged annotation report

The five-row, 37-column SDRF combines the two independently reviewed conflict versions. It records all five deposited RAW runs: four archaeological human femoral cortical-bone samples (ar, ay, n, oo) and one digestion blank (bk). The PRIDE protocol identifies ar and n as the highest osteocalcin samples and ay and oo as the lowest; the merged file retains that sample group as both a characteristic and factor value. The blank has `not applicable` for organism, tissue, material type, individual and osteocalcin group.

The merged file also retains supported technical detail from the other version: Q Exactive HF-X, Top12 DDA, overnight trypsin digestion, 28 NCE, six variable modifications stated in the PRIDE protocol, and Orbitrap MS2 analyzer. HCD and the analyzer are instrument-based inferences. Individual age, sex and pathological status are not mapped to the four run codes and remain `not available`.

Templates: `ms-proteomics` and `human` v1.1.0. The official cache-only parser, structure check and offline term check pass. The fragment mass tolerance remains `not available` because it is not in the PRIDE protocol. Fresh independent review approved the merged SHA-256 `222f0f78370d54044bbd898da265ded7deeb0e2b58bb9e34dca2381eded904eb` with no blocking or important findings (score 94/100). The two prior approvals apply only to their original hashes.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD018051), [paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jasrep.2020.102573), and the adjacent evidence manifest.
