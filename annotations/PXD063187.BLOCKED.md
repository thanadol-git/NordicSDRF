# PXD063187 annotation blocked

The deposit contains 20 numbered RAW files and describes paired healthy and atretic follicular-fluid samples from ten patients. Neither the PRIDE record nor the accessible DIA-NN result parquet maps RAW numbers 1-20 to patient and follicle state.

Assigning healthy/atretic status or pairing patients from numeric order would be an unsupported inference and would risk reversing the principal biological factor.

To unblock: provide the sample key mapping each RAW number to patient identifier and healthy versus atretic follicle state.
