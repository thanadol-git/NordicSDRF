## Templates

- `ms-proteomics v1.1.0`: label-free LC-MS/MS (DDA) on an Orbitrap Velos Pro.
- `vertebrates v1.1.0`: the samples are rat (*Rattus norvegicus*) tissue. This adds `developmental stage`, `strain or breed` and `sex`.
- Not used: `dia-acquisition` (the runs are DDA, top 7), `cell-lines` (tissue), and `clinical-metadata` (animal experiment).

## Design and file mapping

- There are 20 female Wistar rats, one cerebral hemisphere per animal (frontal lobes and cerebellum excluded). Each sample was digested in solution and run once on the Orbitrap, giving one raw file per animal.
- The factor is `factor value[treatment]`, with the same value in `characteristics[treatment]`:
  - `fast decompression (1 bar/20 s)`: FD1–FD10, 10 rows.
  - `slow decompression (1 bar/10 min)`: SD1–SD10, 10 rows. These are the control group.
- The sample-to-file mapping comes from a **deposited table** and matches the **file names**:
  - The deposited Progenesis quantification table (`Bjorkum_DivingRats_Progenesissearchandquantificationresults.xlsx`) puts the sample columns FD1–FD10 under "Fast decompression rate (1 bar/20 s) after heliox saturation (FD)" and SD1–SD10 under "Slow decompression rate (1 bar/10 min) after heliox saturation diving (SD)".
  - Each raw file name starts with the same FD#/SD# label (for example `FD1_AH_6B_D1.raw` and `SD1_AH_1_K11.raw`).
  - The paper gives n = 10 per group.
- Biological replicate is the animal number within its group (1–10), restarting per condition as the spec states. Fraction and technical replicate are both 1.
- All 20 RAW files in the PRIDE list are used exactly once. Every file-list entry carries `projectAccessions = PXD006349`. The two .xlsx files are results, not runs.
- Instrument and method come from range-reading the raw headers of FD1, SD1 and FD9. All three show:
  - instrument "Orbitrap Velos Pro";
  - MS1 as FTMS over 300–2000 m/z;
  - MS2 as ITMS with CID at NCE 35, top 7 peaks.

  PRIDE's instrument dropdown ("LTQ Orbitrap Velos", MS:1001742) is less specific than this and was overridden.

## Limitations

- **Disease (FD rows):** written as `not available`. 12 of 21 FD rats showed decompression-sickness symptoms, but the paper does not report which of the 10 analysed FD animals did.
- **Disease (SD rows):** written as `normal`. This comes from the paper's statement that no SD rat showed symptoms (paper, group level, applies to every SD animal).
- **Developmental stage:** written as `not available`. Age is not reported; only body weight (~250 g) is given. An adult stage was not inferred.
- **Fragment mass tolerance:** the paper says "0.7" with no unit. The unit `Da` is **inferred** from the ion-trap (ITMS) CID MS2 scans.
- **Modifications:** no search-parameter file was deposited.
  - Carbamidomethyl (C, fixed) and Oxidation (M, variable) come from the paper and PRIDE.
  - The other variable modifications are **inferred from the deposited peptide table**, whose "Modifications" column reports them: Acetyl (protein N-term), Gln->pyro-Glu, Glu->pyro-Glu, Pyro-carbamidomethyl (all N-terminal) and Acetyl (K, 2 PSMs).
  - These five look like X!Tandem's built-in refinement modifications, which the paper does not mention.
- **Reduction and alkylation reagents:** written as `not available`. Alkylation is implied by the fixed carbamidomethyl. The DTT/iodoacetamide in the paper belong to the gel-based iontrap workflow, and the in-solution Orbitrap prep is only cited "as previously described".
- **Sampling time:** written as `7 day`. This comes from "one week after the dive the animals were euthanized" (paper).
- **Organism part:** written as `cerebral hemisphere` (paper). PRIDE's dropdown says "brain".
- **Ion-trap dataset not included:** the paper's second dataset (Agilent LC/MSD Trap XCT plus, 12 SDS-PAGE fractions, 240 files) is not deposited and is not annotated.
- **FD9 file size:** `FD9_AH_14B_10.raw` is ~212 MB, against ~830–850 MB for the other runs. Its header is intact (same instrument and method) and FD9 has values in the deposited quantification table. The reason for the size difference is unknown.
- **Raw headers:** only 3 of the 20 headers were read (FD1, SD1, FD9). The other 17 are assumed to have the same instrument and method.
- **Animal codes:** the extra codes in the file names (e.g. `AH_6B_D1`, `AH_18K_15`) are not decoded. Source names use the FD#/SD# labels from the deposited table.
- **Validation:** validation used the offline OLS cache only. Only warnings resulted, none from missing cache terms.
