# PXD035121 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: Cardiac perilipin 5 promotes physiological cardioprotective hypertrophy through increased calcium handling and cardiomyocyte contractility

Publication: Cinato M et al., Life Sci Alliance 2023 6(4):e202201690, PMID 36717246, PMC9887753 (scratchpad/PXD035121/manuscript.txt)

## Evidence collected

- 1 RAW file (Fusion_210511_72.raw, sample name '4202 IP TMT10C', Orbitrap Fusion, CID MS2 + HCD 65% SPS-MS3) and the PD 2.4 .msf/.pdResult
- Paper: FLAG co-immunoprecipitation from primary cardiomyocytes of WT and MHC-Plin5 mice, TMT10plex; 407 proteins, 79 >2-fold higher in MHC-Plin5 vs WT
- PD study definition (scratchpad/PXD035121/evidence/pd_study_definition.json): the only factor is a 'BiologicalReplicate' with values na1–na8, 9 and 12 across the ten channels; no genotype, condition or sample type is recorded
- The paper and PRIDE do not state how many WT and MHC-Plin5 samples were labelled or which channel holds which

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

A TMT SDRF needs one row per channel with its sample. No deposited or published source says which of the ten channels are WT and which are MHC-Plin5 FLAG-IPs (or controls), so the genotype, the only factor of the study, cannot be assigned.
