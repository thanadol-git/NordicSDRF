# PXD040583 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Penanes PA, Gorshkov V, Ivanov MV, Gorshkov MV, Kjeldsen F. "Potential of Negative-Ion-Mode Proteomics: An MS1-Only Approach". J Proteome Res 2023, doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.3c00307, PMID 37395192, PMC10407931 (SDU Odense, Kjeldsen lab). A methods study: HeLa digests made with trypsin, Lys-C, Asp-N or Glu-C, plus the commercial Thermo (Pierce) HeLa digest, each analysed by MS1-only acquisition (240K, no fragmentation) in positive (formic acid) and negative (imidazole) ion mode with 5, 10 and 15 min gradients, in quadruplicate, on an Orbitrap Eclipse; identification by ms1searchpy.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + human v1.1.0 + cell-lines v1.1.0.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 285 files: 120 RAW + 165 ms1searchpy result tables. All 120 RAW annotated. The full name list was transcribed and checked by SHA-256 against PRIDE (identical).

## Sample map
From the file names: digest (`Thermo_500ng`, `trypsin_Promega_1ug`, `LysC_1ug`, `AspN_1ug`, `GluC_1ug`), polarity/modifier (`positiveFA` / `negativeIMIDAZOLE`), gradient (`5/10/15min`), replicate `_1.._4`. 5 digests × 2 polarities × 3 gradients × 4 = 120. One source per digest. All 24 injections of one digest (2 polarities × 3 gradients × 4 repeats) are technical replicates of that digest, numbered 1–24 in run order within the digest, so each row has a unique sample coordinate; polarity and gradient are in their own comment columns. The protocol says each condition was "measured in quadruplicate".

## Key decisions
- Acquisition: MS1-only full scans. PRIDE has no MS1-only acquisition term (its "Proteomics data acquisition method" children are DDA, DIA, PRM and SRM), and the column forbids reserved words, so the parent term **Proteomics data acquisition method (PRIDE:0000659)** is used. Dissociation method `not applicable` (no fragmentation; the methods are `… MS1 positive 240K` / `… MS1 negative 4e5 240R`, per the RAW headers).
- Instrument Orbitrap Eclipse (MS:1003029): RAW headers of all 120 files.
- `comment[scan polarity]` = positive scan (MS:1000130) / negative scan (MS:1000129). Extra comments give the gradient length and mobile-phase modifier.
- Enzymes per digest: Trypsin (MS:1001251), Lys-C (MS:1001309), Asp-N (MS:1001304), Glu-C = glutamyl endopeptidase (MS:1001917).
- In-house HeLa lysates: TCEP (PRIDE:0000609) + chloroacetamide (PRIDE:0000600), parental HeLa (CVCL_0030). `HeLa_Thermo_500ng` = the commercial Pierce HeLa protein digest standard → **HeLa-S3** (CVCL_0058, EFO:0002791) per the annotator brief; its reduction/alkylation is `not available`. Donor attributes (female, 30Y6M, adult), disease cervical adenocarcinoma, organism part uterine cervix per Cellosaurus.
- Modifications: Carbamidomethyl C fixed (chloroacetamide alkylation; PRIDE lists iodoacetamide-derivatized residue). ms1searchpy variable modifications not read from the paper.
- Factors: scan polarity and cleavage agent.

## Not available / not applicable
tolerances (ms1searchpy settings not read); Pierce digest reduction/alkylation.

## Inferred vs stated
Inferred: Pierce digest = HeLa-S3 and trypsin (the vendor's digest; whether it is trypsin-only or trypsin/Lys-C wasn't checked); repeats as technical replicates; CAM fixed for the Pierce digest too.
