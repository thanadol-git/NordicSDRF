# PXD002896 — annotation blocked

PXD002896 deposits 156 vendor RAW files, a spectral library, and an open-access paper (PMCID: PMC4729823). The paper establishes a mouse tissue atlas and DIA plasma analysis of 26 Balb-C mice infected with four *Streptococcus pyogenes* doses or PBS control. It also documents the organ/cell sources used for the spectral library.

The deposited RAW filenames are generic operator/run codes such as `Erik_S1407_071` and `Simon_S1411_292`. Neither the PRIDE record, complete manifest, nor the accessible paper maps those files to plasma infection dose/control, organ/cell source, or biological sample. The study contains both tissue-atlas and plasma-DIA workflows, so this mapping cannot be inferred from the filenames.

Evidence checked: PRIDE PXD002896 project record, protocols, and complete file manifest; Malmström *et al.*, *Nature Communications* (2016), DOI 10.1038/ncomms10261 / PMCID PMC4729823.

Unblock with the deposited experimental-design table linking every RAW file to sample type, source tissue/cell, animal, and infection condition.
