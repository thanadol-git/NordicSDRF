## Study
"Increasing sustainability in palaeoproteomics by optimizing digestion times for large-scale archaeological bone analyses" (Mylopotamitaki et al., iScience 2024, doi:10.1016/j.isci.2024.109432, PMC10972796). 12 archaeological bone specimens (6 La Draga, Spain, Early Neolithic: LD_01-06; 6 Baishiya Karst Cave, China, Middle Pleistocene: BKC_07-12), each split into three digests (3, 6, 18 h trypsin), plus extraction blanks; LC-MS/MS on Evosep One + Orbitrap Exploris 480, DDA, label-free, MaxQuant.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `vertebrates` v1.1.0. MS technology, label-free DDA; animal (bovid/cervid) samples so `vertebrates` (adds developmental stage, strain/breed, sex). Not cell-lines, not human, no clinical/oncology (no disease). No DIA, no multiplexing.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
44 PRIDE files (v3 `files/all`, all with projectAccessions PXD045027): 42 RAW + 2 search-output zips. All 42 RAW annotated (one row each, 36 samples + 3 extraction blanks + 3 `Blank_00x` runs). The 2 zips (MQ_SPIN_output.zip, MQ_full_proteome_output.zip) are search outputs, not runs, and were used as evidence only. No files excluded.

## Key decisions and evidence
- **File to sample map is stated, not inferred**: paper supplementary mmc3.xlsx (columns Lab code, Set, Raw file) maps P0247-P0282 to LD_01..BKC_12 x 18h/6h/3h (P0247-58 = 18 h, P0259-70 = 6 h, P0271-82 = 3 h, specimens in order). It agrees with the MaxQuant `summary.txt` Experiment column (18h/6h/3h) for all 36 files.
- **Sources**: each digest is its own source (`LD_01_18h`, ...), biological replicate 1, because the three digests are separate preparations of sub-samples of one bone and rows must not collide on (source, replicate, technical replicate, fraction). The specimen link is kept in the source name. Digests are not technical replicates.
- **Factor value**: `factor value[digestion duration]` = 3 h / 6 h / 18 h (the compared variable). Blanks: extraction blanks carry their digest duration; `Blank_00x` carry `not applicable`.
- **Extraction blanks** P0244/P0245/P0246 = Blank_18h/6h/3h per MaxQuant summary.txt and paper ("laboratory blanks alongside the samples for each enzymatic digestion condition"). Sample type `negative control`.
- **Blank_004/005/006**: file names only; not mentioned in the paper, not in the MaxQuant searches. Annotated as `negative control` (generic blank); whether they are solvent/injection blanks is not documented. Reviewer should attack this.
- **Organism**: `Bovinae` for 11 specimens (ZooMS Bos/Bison; SPIN candidate sets also include Bubalus, so genus-level Bos would over-claim; matches PRIDE organism NEWT:27592). `Cervidae` for LD_02 (ZooMS and SPIN both Cervidae; the paper's "most likely Cervus elaphus" comes from fauna/context reasoning, so not used). Blanks: `not applicable`.
- **Organism part**: `bone tissue` (UBERON:0002481); paper says cortical bone, but a more specific UBERON term was not unambiguous. Tissue mass = per-extract weights from SI Table 2 (mmc1.pdf), ordered 18; 6; 3 h.
- **Modifications**: from the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` (SPIN search, 39 files): variable Oxidation (M), Oxidation (P) [MaxQuant "Hydroxyproline" = Oxidation on P], Deamidated N and Q, Gln->pyro-Glu, Glu->pyro-Glu; **no fixed modifications**. Conflict resolved: `mqpar.xml` inside MQ_SPIN_output.zip lists Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed but covers only 3 raw files and does not match summary.txt (empty Fixed column for all 39 files); the ZooMS-based protocol has no reduction/alkylation step. Carbamidomethyl was therefore NOT asserted. The second (full Bos taurus proteome) search used only Oxidation (M), Hydroxyproline, Deamidation (NQ), a subset; recorded search is the first. Semi-specific Trypsin/P cannot be encoded; cleavage agent = Trypsin (the enzyme added).
- **Tolerances**: precursor 4.5 ppm (mqpar mainSearchTol), fragment 20 ppm (parameters.txt FTMS).
- **Instrument**: Orbitrap Exploris 480 confirmed by Methods, PRIDE and raw-file headers (4 files checked). HCD, NCE 30 %, MS1 350-1400 m/z, Orbitrap analyser (MS2 resolution 15000).
- Accession check: PRIDE fields (bone, Bovinae, Exploris 480, MaxQuant) agree with paper; PRIDE `modifications` text was not relied on.

## Values left `not available` / `not applicable`
- `not available`: cell type, disease, developmental stage, strain or breed, sex (archaeological bones, no per-specimen biological attributes; no healthy/disease concept), reduction reagent, alkylation reagent (protocol silent; the search shows no carbamidomethyl but the paper does not say these steps were omitted).
- `not applicable`: all sample properties of blanks; digestion duration for `Blank_00x`.
- Omitted columns (optional): fractionation (none described), acquisition date, scan ranges other than MS1, batches.

## Inferred vs stated
Stated: file map, durations, instrument, mods, tolerances, collision energy, weights. Inferred: genus-family-level organism label (Bovinae as the rank covering Bos/Bison); `negative control` classification for Blank_00x; digest-level source naming; Oxidation (P) for MaxQuant "Hydroxyproline".
