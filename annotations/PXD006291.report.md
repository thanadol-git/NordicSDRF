# PXD006291 annotation report

## Scope

All 89 deposited RAW files are represented by 815 SDRF assay rows: 83 experimental HiRIEF fractions expanded over their explicit TMT10 channels, plus six deposited synthetic-peptide validation runs.

## Evidence and decisions

- The PRIDE description gives the complete TMT10 channel-to-sample map for the A431 Gefitinib time course and both histologically normal-tissue plexes. These mappings are retained for every deposited fraction.
- A431 channels encode untreated biological triplicates, Gefitinib-treated 2 h and 6 h duplicates, and 24 h biological triplicates. The deposited tissue maps encode the listed tissue, individual replicate, plex, and pooled internal-standard channel.
- Raw-file names provide the HiRIEF pH range and fraction identifier. `37-49` and `11-6` are normalized to the protocol’s stated ranges 3.7–4.9 and 6–11.
- Six deposited RAW files are explicitly named synthetic-peptide TMT126 validation runs. They remain one-channel validation assays rather than being assigned to an A431 or tissue channel map; the two files whose names include `H2O2_DMSO` retain that treatment label.
- PRIDE specifies FASP digestion and refers to the pooled internal standard as a mixture of tryptic peptides; Trypsin is therefore recorded. Fixed carbamidomethylation and TMT10plex labeling, plus variable methionine oxidation, come from the deposited search protocol.
