# PXD008213 — blocked: TMT RAW files are not mapped to biological subjects or channels

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD008213
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD008213/files/all
- Paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5932012/ (PMID: 29720585; DOI: 10.1038/s41467-018-04180-1)
- Supplement bundle: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC5932012/supplementaryFiles

## Evidence available

The paper and Supplementary Table 6 identify mouse KMT2A-MLLT3 leukemia subject IDs, experimental groups, activating mutations, and which subjects underwent quantitative proteomics. PRIDE lists 38 Orbitrap Fusion RAW files with `A`, `B`, and `C` aliases, consistent with multiplexed runs.

## Blocking gap

No checked source maps the `160430_JH_MLLAxel_A*`, `B*`, and `C*` RAW files to the paper's subject IDs, TMT channels, experimental groups, mutations, or technical replicates. The spreadsheet's subject-level proteomics flag is not a channel/run map. Assigning channels by A/B/C filename prefix would be unsupported.

## Unblock requirement

Provide the TMT design/sample sheet that links every RAW file and reporter channel to the subject ID, experimental group, mutation status, and replicate.
