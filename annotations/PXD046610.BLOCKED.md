# PXD046610 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE describes a DIA Orbitrap Exploris 480 study of tibialis anterior muscle and descending thoracic aorta from whole-body *Mustn1*-knockout and wild-type littermate mice. It reports technical triplicates for tibialis anterior. The deposit exposes four archive-level objects: `TAmuscletriplicates_rawPRIDE.7z` (41.6 GB), `AortarawPRIDE.7z` (23.4 GB), and paired search-result archives.
- **Why blocked:** PRIDE does not provide an external file manifest or individual RAW filenames for either 7z archive. The accession metadata identifies the tissues and genotype comparison, but does not link individual samples, biological replicates, or technical-triplicate RAW files to their source material. An SDRF cannot use only the archive names as `comment[data file]`, and extracting 65 GB of archives without a published manifest is not an evidence source for an annotatable sample map.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A deposited file manifest/run sheet listing the individual RAW files and mapping each to tissue, *Mustn1* genotype, biological replicate, and (for tibialis anterior) technical replicate; alternatively, an updated PRIDE deposit exposing those individual files.
