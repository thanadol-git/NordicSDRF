# PXD025358 — blocked SDRF annotation

## Evidence checked

- The PRIDE project record describes four TMTpro 16-plex sets: two skeletal-muscle sets and two adipose-tissue sets, each measured as 20 total-proteome fractions and 10 phosphopeptide fractions.
- The PRIDE inventory contains 120 opaque Thermo RAW filenames (`Fusion_200302_*`, `Fusion_200309_*`, and `Fusion_200316_*`), consistent only at aggregate level with four sets of 30 runs.
- All four deposited result workbooks were inspected:
  - `3299_muscle_TMTpro_Total.xlsx`
  - `3299_muscle_TMTpro_PhosphoPeptides.xlsx`
  - `3299_fat_TMTpro_Total.xlsx`
  - `3299_fat_TMTpro_PhosphoPeptides.xlsx`
- Their `sample`/`samples` sheets provide exact TMTpro16 channel-to-sample tables for sets 1–4, including the tissue-specific pooled references and sample identifiers 1–30.
- Workbook core metadata identifies only combined result groups such as muscle sets 1/2 and fat sets 3/4; it does not list the contributing RAW filenames or a per-file set/fraction assignment.
- The associated publication and its supplementary workbook were checked. They establish the cohort design, tissue types, total/phosphoproteomic workflows, and treatment comparisons, but do not provide a RAW-file-to-set crosswalk.

## Exact blocker

No deposited file, PRIDE field, publication table, or supplement maps each `Fusion_*.raw` file to TMT set 1, 2, 3, or 4 and to its total-proteome or phosphopeptide fraction number. The exact channel maps therefore cannot be connected to the RAW runs without inferring run blocks from acquisition dates and numeric filename ranges. That would create an unsupported sample/channel mapping, so no SDRF was written.

## Evidence that would unblock the accession

A Proteome Discoverer input-file table, instrument sequence/export, SDRF, or submitter-provided manifest that explicitly maps every RAW filename to tissue, set number, assay type (total or phosphopeptide), and fraction number.
