# PXD024630 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"ZNF524 directly binds telomeres and supports their integrity via TRF2/RAP1" (PX title). The dataset is the label-free BioID of **MYC-BirA*-ZNF524 WT (bait) vs MYC-BirA*-ZNF524 ZF2 mutant C144A (DNA-binding-deficient control)** in U2OS cells (Fig. 3f / Supplementary Fig. 3b). The paper is the same as for PXD043084: Braun H et al., "ZNF524 directly interacts with telomeric DNA and supports telomere integrity", Nat Commun 2023;14:8252, DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-43397-7, PMC10716145, PMID 38086788. The PX record says "publication pending", but the paper's Data availability lists "PXD024630, PXD043084 and PXD031416", which verifies the link. Submitter: Dennis Kappei, Cancer Science Institute of Singapore.

## Scope, evidence and decisions
The SDRF has 8 rows (8 RAW x label free) and 40 columns. Deposited (PROXI and the PRIDE FTP listing, both re-derived 2026-11-27): 10 files. These are 8 RAW (`20201113_LAB_HB_U2OS_BioID_ZNF524_100ng_WT_01..04.raw`, `..._100ng_Zcf2mut_01..04.raw`), the MaxQuant zip `..._100ng_WTvsZnF2mut.zip` (157 MB) and `checksum.txt`. All 8 RAW files are annotated and none is excluded. Evidence files are in `/home/claude/singapore2/scratch/PXD024630/`.

- **Bait vs control mapping (STATED):** `WT` = bait MYC-BirA*-ZNF524 wild type; `Zcf2mut` (sic; the zip says `ZnF2mut`) = control MYC-BirA*-ZNF524 ZF2 mutant. The Methods say "For the ZF2 mut, an additional C144A mutation was included". The Results say "We thus expressed a doxycycline-inducible MYC-BirA*-ZNF524 WT or the respective ZF2 mutant in U2OS cells". The Fig. 3f legend: "Volcano plot of BioID assay comparing proximity partners of ZNF524 WT versus ZNF524 ZF2 mut in U2OS cells", with n = 4 independently seeded and induced replicates. Suffix `_01..04` = biological replicate 1-4 within each arm. The numbering comes from the file names, and the arms are not claimed to be paired.
- **Not labelled:** label-free MaxQuant LFQ with match between runs, so there is no channel map. `comment[label]` = `label free sample`.
- **Induction (conflict, resolved):** the Methods (BioID) say "U2OS cell lines carrying the BirA*-ZNF524 WT or BirA*-ZNF524 ZF2 mutant were induced with 100 ng mL-1 doxycycline". The Fig. 3f legend says 300 ng ml-1. Every raw file name carries `100ng`. Raw file names and Methods rank above a figure legend, so **100 ng/mL** (48 h; 50 uM biotin for the last 6 h) is annotated in `characteristics[treatment]`, which is the same in both arms. The 300 ng/mL figure is the WT induction of the *NLS* comparison (PXD043084), which is probably the source of the legend error.
- **Cell line, medium, enrichment, prep, MS:** identical to PXD043084, from the same Methods. U2OS CVCL_0042 (Cellosaurus: female, 15Y, osteosarcoma, tibia), checked live. Tibia UBERON:0000979 and osteosarcoma MONDO:0009807 were checked on Ontobee. DMEM 4.5 g/L glucose, 10% FBS, 2 mM glutamine, pen/strep. Nuclear extract with streptavidin C1. In-gel digestion with DTT, IAA and trypsin. Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523): PX and paper agree. DDA top 20 HCD, MS1 60k, MS2 15k.
- **Search (STATED):** "for BioID ZNF524 WT vs ZF2 mutant: version 1.5.2.8" (MaxQuant). Trypsin, up to 2 missed cleavages. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and protein N-terminal acetylation (`PP=Protein N-term`) variable. FDR 0.01. Database UP000005640: the paper gives "v20210307" for both comparisons, although the RAW files date from 2020-11. This is noted but does not affect the SDRF.
