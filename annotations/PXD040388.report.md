The PRIDE dataset is the TMT secretome (conditioned medium) experiment: CM from ADRCs, MACS-sorted CD31+ and CD31- ADRCs, 3 donors, one TMT set (126 = pool), high-pH fractionated, Orbitrap, Proteome Discoverer 2.4. PRIDE submissionType is PARTIAL.
