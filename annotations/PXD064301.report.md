# PXD064301 annotation report

Status: approved after parser validation and independent hash-bound review.

The deposited Proteome Discoverer result sheets map TMT6 channels for two jacalin-enriched O-glycopeptide fractions (WT 126–128, KO 129–131) and one LWAC flow-through proteome run (WT 126–128, Hetero 129–131). The builder produced 18 SDRF rows from three RAWs; the two jacalin runs appear as separate fractions for each channel. The files preserve original fraction labels 10 and 11_12; SDRF fraction identifiers are ordinal within the two-file subset. PRIDE's structured organism field says human, but its sample protocol explicitly describes mouse liver and the deposited results contain mouse protein accessions.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD064301) and [file list](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD064301/files/all); exact deposited XLSX links are in the evidence manifest. Official parser, structure, reconciliation and offline term checks passed.
