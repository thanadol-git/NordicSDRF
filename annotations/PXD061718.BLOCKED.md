## Blocker

The available public view does not enumerate the complete raw-file inventory or provide a complete crosswalk from every run to differentiation state, IP/IgG condition, and biological replicate. The visible filename subset is insufficient to prove coverage of all deposited raw files. Creating source names or extending the visible naming pattern to the unseen files would be inference.
