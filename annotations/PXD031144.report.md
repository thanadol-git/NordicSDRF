# PXD031144 annotation report

Nascent proteome (AHA labelling, BONCAT) of skin fibroblasts from sporadic and LRRK2-G2019S Parkinson's disease patients and healthy donors, plus a PRM validation set. Paper: NPJ Parkinsons Dis 2023 (PMC9902458).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 95 rows: 64 AHA runs (LTQ Orbitrap XL, DDA) and 31 PRM runs (Q Exactive).
- **Clinical data:** disease, LRRK2 status, sex and age per donor come from Table 1 of the paper. The PRM input files independently confirm the group (`C_`, `S_`, `G_`) of each ID.

## Inferences to check
- **`A` vs `M`:** each AHA donor has an `A` and an `M` run. `A` is read as the AHA-labelled capture and `M` as a methionine (no-AHA) background control. The paper does not define the suffixes.
- **Missing donors:** IDs 372011, 592012, 30116 and 30159 have AHA runs but are not in Table 1. Their disease, sex and age are `not available`.

## Values
- **Cell type:** fibroblast from upper-arm skin. TUH donors are `PD1–13` and `C1–7`. NINDS/TNGB donors are the numeric IDs.
- **Acquisition:** data-dependent acquisition for AHA runs (CID); parallel reaction monitoring (PRIDE:0000629) for PRM runs (HCD). Instruments per the raw headers.
- **Search (AHA):** MaxQuant 1.6.0.16. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Met→AHA (UNIMOD:896) variable.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: one finding, a false positive: `cleavage_agent_unsupported`. The PRIDE text omits the enzyme, but the paper's methods state on-bead digestion with sequencing-grade trypsin.
