# PXD018233 annotation report

Status: validated SDRF; independent review PASS (`cf5aeb8b6308`).

The [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD018233), [deposited files](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD018233/files/all), and [publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7641822/) describe extracellular vesicles from primary human muscle fibroblasts (FibX), differentiating myoblasts (MyoX), and tendon fibroblasts (TenX). Four FibX, five MyoX and five TenX RAW files are annotated, with filename numerals as biological replicate identifiers. The F/M/T suffix mapping is supported by the publication group names and sample protocol. Source names and factor values identify the producing cell type; tissue terms describe its origin. The paper states that the three cell sources came from four shared biological samples, with a fifth supplying only tendon fibroblasts and myoblasts. The RAW suffixes align with these counts but are not explicitly documented as donor IDs; `characteristics[individual]` is therefore omitted. Donor-specific age, sex and disease were unavailable.

Templates: `ms-proteomics`, `human`. PRIDE specifies Orbitrap Fusion top-speed DDA/HCD, sequential Lys-C then trypsin digestion, and fixed carbamidomethyl C plus variable oxidation M, deamidation N/Q and protein N-terminal acetylation. The SDRF records final cleavage by trypsin; the preceding Lys-C is recorded here because the template has one cleavage-agent field. All fourteen deposited RAW files are claimed exactly once.
