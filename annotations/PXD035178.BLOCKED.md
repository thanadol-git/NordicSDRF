# PXD035178 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The dataset is two TMT 11-plex runs (LJ_1_, LJ_2_; 7 high-pH fractions each, 14 RAW files) and no source gives the TMT channel -> sample map. SDRF needs one row per (file x channel) with a sample identity. The paper says only that 13 aortas (7 XPro1595, 6 vehicle) were "randomly labeled" and that 131C was a pool of all AAA samples. It does not say which channel held which mouse or treatment, nor how 13 samples plus pool(s) were split over the two plexes (2 x 10 non-pool channels = 20 slots; unused/empty channels cannot be told from used ones).

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE v3 project record and complete `files/all` list (15 entries: 14 RAW + one 8.4 GB `Lasse_Stubbe_A-_1_.pdResult`); every entry carries PXD035178.
- Paper PMC9523116 (PMID 36186984, Front Cardiovasc Med 2022) full text, Methods, Data availability (PRIDE only; no Zenodo/figshare/GitHub link) and the Europe PMC supplementary bundle (Table_1, Table_2 cytokines; Table_3.XLSX).
- Supplementary Table 3: 5,469 proteins with n_CTRL/n_xPRO and 13 unlabelled per-sample ratio columns all headed `EA`. No channel, plex or group labels, so column order cannot be tied to channels.
- The `.pdResult` SQLite database, read by HTTP range: `AnalysisDefinition` has StudyDefinition samples named by default `LJ_1_ - [126]` ... `LJ_2_ - [131C]` (22 samples), empty `Factors`, empty `FactorValues`, quan method "TMT 11plex". This is the Proteome Discoverer "no map" default.

No deposited SDRF was used.

## Evidence needed to unblock

A submitter-provided channel/sample sheet (mouse ID, treatment, plex, TMT channel), or a labelled version of Supplementary Table 3 (column headers with plex and channel).
