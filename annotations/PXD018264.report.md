# PXD018264 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Lanigan LT, Mackie M, …, Cappellini E, Olsen JV, Taurozzi AJ, Welker F. "Multi-protease analysis of Pleistocene bone proteomes". J Proteomics 2020;228:103889, doi:10.1016/j.jprot.2020.103889, PMID 32652221 (not in PMC; abstract + PRIDE protocols used). One Late Pleistocene hominin bone from the Kleine Feldhofer Grotte (identified by collagen MALDI-TOF fingerprinting) and three Late Pleistocene Equidae bones were each extracted, reduced/alkylated, split into six aliquots and digested with chymotrypsin, elastase, Glu-C, Lys-N, pepsin or trypsin; each digest was StageTipped in triplicate and run on a Q Exactive HF-X. Extraction blanks were run per protease.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + vertebrates v1.1.0 (the hominin is recorded at genus level `Homo`, so the human template's donor fields don't fit).

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 93 files: 78 RAW + 15 MaxQuant result zips. All 78 RAW annotated: 54 Equidae (3 specimens × 6 proteases × 3), 18 hominin (6 × 3), 6 extraction blanks (one per protease). The generated file list matched PRIDE by SHA-256.

## Sample map
From file names `…_<run>_<specimen>_<protease><rep>.raw` (`Equid1-3`, `Homo`, `<protease>Blank`). One source per specimen × protease digest. The triplicates are separate StageTip preparations of the same digest, encoded as technical replicates 1–3 (the protocol: "immobilized on C18 Stage-Tips in triplicate"; MaxQuant kept them separate as "injection replicates"). Blanks: `sample type = blank`, organism/organism part/material `not applicable`.

## Key decisions
- Organism: Equidae (NCBITaxon:9788) and Homo (NCBITaxon:9605), as registered in PRIDE; the paper identifies the hominin only to genus in the available text. Organism part bone tissue (UBERON:0002481).
- Instrument Q Exactive HF-X (MS:1002877): RAW headers of first and last runs (`5ul_77min_60K_500bar-Working.meth`); DDA, HCD.
- Enzymes (one per run, also the factor value): Trypsin MS:1001251, PepsinA MS:1001311, Lys-N MS:1003093, glutamyl endopeptidase (Glu-C) MS:1001917, Chymotrypsin MS:1001306, elastase → leukocyte elastase MS:1001915 (the only elastase term in PSI-MS; the source enzyme isn't stated).
- TCEP (PRIDE:0000609), CAA (PRIDE:0000600).
- Modifications exactly as the stated MaxQuant search: all variable: Oxidation M, Deamidated N/Q, Oxidation P (hydroxyproline), Carbamidomethyl C. Tolerances 4.5 ppm (main search) and 20 ppm fragment.
