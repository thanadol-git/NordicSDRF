# PXD039805 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE describes one TMT-6plex Q Exactive HF run (`20220810_HF2_01_AS_220708_TMT6.raw`) of primary mouse brain endothelial-cell cultures exposed to *Plasmodium berghei* ANKA-infected erythrocytes. The protocol and article state that the experiment compared three IFNAR1-knockout and three wild-type cultures. The article's supplementary PDF reports the resulting IFNAR1-knockout/wild-type fold changes, but not reporter-channel identities.
- **Why blocked:** The two deposited Proteome Discoverer result workbooks contain result tables, not a TMT channel-to-sample/run sheet. PRIDE, the article, and its supplement do not identify which TMT6 reporter channels belong to each genotype or biological replicate. Assigning the six channels to the reported groups would fabricate the sample-to-assay relationships.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A TMT6 sample sheet or run sheet mapping every reporter channel in `20220810_HF2_01_AS_220708_TMT6.raw` to its wild-type or IFNAR1-knockout brain endothelial-cell biological replicate.
