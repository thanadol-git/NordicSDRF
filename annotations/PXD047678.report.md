## Conservative choices

- **Organism part:** `heart left ventricle` (UBERON:0002084) from the paper (left ventricle). PRIDE lists generic `Heart` (BTO:0000562); companion skeleton used `heart`.
- **Developmental stage:** `juvenile stage` as in the companion PXD035554 skeleton (3-month piglets). OLS routes that label to UBERON; the vertebrates contract asks for EFO. Validator accepted it.
- **Age:** `3M` (paper; same as companion). Extra characteristic, not in the vertebrates contract.
- **Sex / strain:** `not available`. Paper states mixed sexes and does not name a breed per animal.
- **Cell type:** `not available`. Whole-tissue homogenate, not isolated cardiomyocytes. Description text about SSM/IFM is the companion complexome experiment.
- **Disease:** `normal` (healthy piglets; HBO is the treatment, not a disease model).
- **Pellet vs supernatant:** Triton split of myofilaments vs other cellular proteins, encoded in source names. Not given a second organism-part or enrichment-process term (myofilament is GO/NCIT, not UBERON/BTO).
- **Cleavage:** PRIDE sample protocol is FASP/trypsin. `NT=Trypsin;AC=MS:1001251`. No deposited `mqpar` opened for this PXD.
- **Modifications:** from PRIDE `dataProcessingProtocol` (Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and protein N-term Acetyl variable).
- **Dissociation:** HCD on Q Exactive → `NT=beam-type collision-induced dissociation;AC=MS:1000422`. Collision energy not stated; omitted.
- **Tolerances:** not in the deposit record; `not available`.
- **Reduction/alkylation reagents:** omitted. Search uses carbamidomethyl, but the wet-lab protocol here does not name DTT/IAA.
