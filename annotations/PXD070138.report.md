## Decisions and limitations

- Sample/species mapping: R-number to species comes from the deposited search-file prefixes (R1_Aavi, R2_Bboe, R3_Coli, R4_Cnat, R5_Chos, R6_Hdic, R7_Lpar, R8_Xint, R9_Cgra, R10_Flan, R11_Hcer) and the matching RAW numbering; no explicit mapping file exists. Full species names for Hdic, Xint, Flan, Chos are expanded from abbreviations/tar names (R6_Hdictator, R8_Xintermedia, R10_Flanicius) and the paper text (Citharognathus hosei); treated as inferred.
- Organism column: written at the lowest rank present in NCBI Taxonomy, because species-level terms for Harpactira dictator, Xenesthis intermedia, Fufius lanicius and Citharognathus hosei do not exist there. Harpactira, Xenesthis, Fufius at genus level; R5 (C. hosei) as family Theraphosidae. Species identity is kept in the source name. Factor value[organism] mirrors the organism column.
- Cleavage agent: Trypsin (MS:1001251) from the sample-prep protocol; the MaxQuant setting Trypsin/P is a search specificity and was not used (reconcile blocker otherwise).
- Modifications read from deposited MaxQuant settings (R*_MQ-settings.xml, identical across all 11): Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed; Oxidation (M), Amidated (protein C-term), Glu->pyro-Glu variable. The PRIDE text also lists Acetyl (protein N-term) as variable, but the settings files only list it under restrictMods, so it is omitted. The paper says no fixed modifications for in-solution digests, but the settings and IAA alkylation both indicate Carbamidomethyl fixed.
- Tolerances: precursor 10 ppm (mainSearchTol), fragment 25 ppm (MaxQuant TOF MS/MS match tolerance).
- Dissociation method set to not available (not stated; not inferred). Reduction DTT and alkylation IAA from the PRIDE protocol.
- Developmental stage (adult or subadult, per specimen not stated), strain, sex and disease: developmental stage and strain not available; disease not applicable. Sex (female) is known only for H. cerberea and is not a template column here.
- Biological replicate 1 and technical replicate 1 for all rows: one injection per species, no replicates.
- Data files are the deposited .d.tar.gz archives (that is what PRIDE lists); assay names are the archive stems without .d.tar.gz.
- Paper full text (PMC13172734) read only for methods/species context; LC gradient and collision energy not stated, so omitted.
