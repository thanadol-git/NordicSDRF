# PXD071605 — evidence hold: TMT channel map

The [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD071605) and [publication](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.71214) describe a six-channel TMT experiment on three biological replicates each of two-week-old wild-type and `rak1-1` *Physcomitrium patens* tissue. The accession's [archive directory](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2026/04/PXD071605/) lists 30 RAW acquisitions: 20 input fractions, five acetyl-enriched pools and five phospho-enriched pools. The publication supports trypsin digestion and Orbitrap Eclipse analysis.

The publication and its described supporting tables do not identify which TMT reporter channel corresponds to each genotype and biological replicate. The archive filenames identify the fractions and enriched pools, not the six channel identities. The single deposited Proteome Discoverer `.pdResultDetails` is about 20 GB and has not been inspected for its reporter-channel metadata. No channel assignments have been inferred. A channel-to-source map from the depositor or that result file is required before constructing a truthful multiplexed SDRF.

No SDRF is approved or committed for this accession.
