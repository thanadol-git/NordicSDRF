# PXD017083 - BLOCKED (no SDRF written)

Status: not annotated; blocked on the TMT channel-to-sample map.

Dataset: urinary exosome TMT proteomics, primary aldosteronism patients, fludrocortisone suppression test (Aarhus; paper Wu A et al., J Hum Hypertens 2020, PMID 33173144, doi 10.1038/s41371-020-00439-7).

## What the record establishes
- Human, urine exosomes/uEVs, Q Exactive, TMT (9 channels used per set), 3 TMT sets, 8 fractions each (Exo_TMT_<set>-<fraction>.raw), PD 2.2 (Sequest+Mascot), carbamidomethyl C fixed; Ac-protein-N-term, Oxidation M, TMT N-term/K variable; 10 ppm / 0.02 Da; max 2 missed cleavages.
- Design: 12 participants (F4/M8), D0 and D4 samples = 24 samples; per set 8 channels (4 patients x D0/D4) + 1 universal control (pool of all 24).
- Caveat: PRIDE lists 48 RAW files (each Exo_TMT_s-f.raw also present as Exo_TMT_s-f_<timestamp>.raw, different sizes); the relation (re-uploads vs distinct runs) is undocumented.

## Why blocked (channel-map ladder, SKILL 6.1)
1. Paper and supplementary: full text closed access (Unpaywall oa_status closed, not in PMC); nature.com page returned only the reference list. Not checked: Table S1 behind paywall.
2. Deposited PRIDE files: only .raw, .dta.zip and PD .pdResult (6 GB each). An HTTP-range SQLite read of the .pdResult (to get StudyInformation/channel sample names) hung on the FTP server and was abandoned. Even if read, PD often holds generic "Sample" labels.
3. Europe PMC supplementary: no PMCID.
4. External repositories: none cited in the accessible PRIDE record.
5. Cross-validation: impossible without a map.

The PRIDE text gives no mapping of TMT channels to patient or to D0/D4, nor which channel is the universal control or which patients are in which set. Assigning these would be invented identities, and build would refuse an incomplete channel map.
