## Limitations

- No publication; relies on PRIDE protocol text and deposited MaxQuant summary/parameters (read by HTTP range from the zips).
- On-bead = "B" and FASP = "F" is inferred from protocol and naming, not stated in the deposit.
- 24 files cannot be tied to a preparation method (see above); marked not available.
- The relation of the two in-gel runs (here technical replicates 1 and 2, biological replicate 1) is an assumption.
- Age/sex/disease/cell type/organism part come from Cellosaurus (Jurkat CVCL_0065), not the deposit.
- Instrument taken from RAW headers (Q Exactive Plus) over PRIDE field (Q Exactive); headers of 6 of 72 files read.
- Tolerances: 6 ppm main search per protocol, 20 ppm fragment per parameters.txt. Search used MaxQuant 1.5.3.8 (protocol says 1.5.2.8).
- Not encoded: fractionation method, collision energy 25% NCE, alkylation/reduction reagents, LC details. The P3640 file uses a different gradient method name than most (2-30 vs 2-7-30).
