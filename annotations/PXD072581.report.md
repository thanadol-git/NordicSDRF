# PXD072581 annotation report

LFQbench-style mixture: E. coli lysate mixed at five dilutions into a constant HeLa background. Every mixture was run three times in DDA and three times in DIA on the same timsTOF. Submitted by PROMEC, NTNU (A. Sharma). No journal paper. The design is documented in the submitter's post "DIA-NN learned to search DDA" (fuzzylife.substack.com, Jan 2026), linked from the PRIDE record. Not in the community corpus.

## Files
- **`PXD072581-dda.sdrf.tsv`:** 15 DDA runs.
- **`PXD072581-dia.sdrf.tsv`:** 15 DIA runs (adds the `dia-acquisition` template).

DDA and DIA are split into two files, as upstream does for PXD005573, because the DIA template constrains the acquisition column.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0, plus `dia-acquisition` v1.1.0 for the DIA file.

## Design
- **Ratios:** `Hela_coli_<n>_1` is HeLa:E. coli n:1, i.e. E. coli diluted n-fold relative to the 1:1 reference (blog: 1:1, 2:1, 4:1, 5:1, 10:1).
- **Encoding:** following the corpus convention for mixed proteomes (PXD000279, PXD002952), the organism is the human background. E. coli is recorded as `characteristics[spiked compound]` = `CT=mixture;CN=Escherichia coli lysate;SP=Escherichia coli;QY=HeLa:E. coli n:1`, which is also the factor.
- **Replicates:** `a/b/c` are the triplicate injections of each mixture (technical replicate 1–3). One source per ratio.

## Values
- **Background cell line:** HeLa (CVCL_0030), from the uterine cervix of a 30-year-old woman with `cervical adenocarcinoma`.
- **Instrument:** `timsTOF Pro 2`, from `analysis.tdf` (serial 1875087.10477, acquired 2025-01-07). The PRIDE sample protocol says timsTOF Pro.
  - DIA scan range 100–1700 m/z is from PRIDE; the isolation window is `not available`.
- **Search** (`DDAreport.log.txt` / `reportDDA.log.txt` in the deposited zip; DIA-NN 2.3.1, same command for both modes):
  - `--unimod4`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ and N-terminal acetyl variable.
  - Trypsin (K\*, R\*), 2 missed cleavages.
  - `--mass-acc 15 --mass-acc-ms1 15`, so 15 ppm / 15 ppm. PRIDE says 20 ppm; the log wins.
  - Despite their names, `reportDDA.*` is the DIA search and `DDAreport.*` the DDA search, per the input files in each log.
- **Sample prep:** the PRIDE sample-processing text describes "synaptosome samples" (copied from another project), and the HeLa may be a commercial standard digest. Reduction and alkylation reagents are therefore left out.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass for both files.
- `tools reconcile` raises two flags, both expected:
  - "run names name a cell line" is a false positive: HeLa is annotated as the cell line, and its tissue of origin is the organism part.
  - The instrument conflicts with the protocol text; the raw file header wins.
