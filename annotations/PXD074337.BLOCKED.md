# PXD074337 — blocked SDRF annotation

## Evidence checked

- The PRIDE project record describes three *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* strains (CEN.PK113-11C, sZJD23, and sZJD28), each represented by three biological replicates, quantified in a TMT10plex experiment. It also describes a pooled reference spiked with UPS2 and measured label-free in ten fractions with three injections per fraction.
- The complete PRIDE inventory contains 50 Thermo RAW files. The accession's Proteome Discoverer result names and acquisition blocks distinguish 20 TMT fraction runs (`Lumos_200513_74`–`93`) from 30 label-free runs (`Lumos_200806_07`–`46`, in three ten-run blocks).
- The PRIDE sample-processing and data-processing records were inspected for channel assignments. They identify TMT10plex labelling, the nine strain replicates plus pooled reference design, SPS-MS3 acquisition, and the complete search parameters, but do not state which sample occupied each reporter channel.
- The full publication (DOI `10.1038/s41598-026-58495-x`) and both deposited supplementary files were inspected. They confirm the three strains and three biological replicates per strain, but provide no TMT reporter-channel table and no accession-specific channel-to-sample crosswalk.
- The deposited result files are multi-gigabyte Proteome Discoverer `.msf`/`.pdResult` databases rather than a small design manifest; no small deposited file supplies the missing channel metadata.
- No community SDRF is available for PXD074337.

## Exact blocker

The ten TMT10 reporter channels cannot be assigned exactly to the nine strain replicates and pooled reference. Although the set composition is known, no PRIDE field, publication table, supplement, or small deposited design file states the channel order. Inferring a conventional ordering would invent a sample/channel mapping. Because 20 of the accession's RAW files are TMT runs requiring that mapping, no SDRF was written.

## Evidence that would unblock the accession

The Proteome Discoverer study sample table or a submitter manifest listing reporter channel (126 through 131), strain, biological replicate, and pooled-reference channel for the TMT10plex set.
