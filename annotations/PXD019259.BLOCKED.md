# PXD019259 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Proteomic Analysis Enables Distinction of Early- Versus Advanced-Stage Lung Adenocarcinomas
- **PRIDE record:** PXD019259
- **Publication:** Kelemen *et al.*, *Clinical and Translational Medicine* (2020), DOI [10.1002/ctm2.106](https://doi.org/10.1002/ctm2.106)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits 75 RAW files named with opaque acquisition/sample numbers plus per-sample Proteome Discoverer `.msf` outputs.
- The study description reports paired normal and tumour lung tissue from early- and advanced-stage adenocarcinoma patients, but no reviewed source maps those clinical groups to the raw basenames.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

No public file-to-patient/tissue/stage crosswalk was found.  Assigning tumour versus normal, early versus advanced stage, or patient pairing from numeric filenames would fabricate metadata.

- `scratchpad/PXD019259/evidence/pride.json`

## Unblocking requirement

An author-provided sample sheet mapping each RAW filename to patient, paired tissue status, and disease stage.
