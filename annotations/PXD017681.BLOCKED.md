# PXD017681 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Molecular markers in the CSF proteome differentiate neuroinflammatory diseases (multiple sclerosis subtypes)
- **PRIDE record:** PXD017681
- **Publication:** *Sci Rep* 2021, DOI [10.1038/s41598-021-83591-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-83591-5), PMID 33603109, PMC7892884

## Evidence recovered

- 120 RAW (`QEHF3_04618_AN` … `QEHF3_04738_AN`, Q Exactive HF) in 17 TMT11plex sets; one PD `.msf` per set (`Set1.msf` … `Set17.msf`), whose input-file lists were read (6–8 RAW each).
- Paper: a pooled CSF sample on TMT 126 in every set; "mixed with ten other patient samples in a random manner (Supplementary Fig.)"; each set contains at least one sample of every patient group.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The channel → patient/diagnosis assignment for the 10 non-pool channels of each of the 17 sets is not in the `.msf` (PD study has no factors; quan method "TMT 11plex" only), not in the Methods, and not in PRIDE. The paper refers to a supplementary figure for the randomisation, which wasn't machine-readable here. Only the pool channel is known.

## Sources checked

PRIDE record and `files/all`; first 8 MB of all 17 `.msf` files; full Methods of PMC7892884.

## Unblocking requirement

The per-set channel table (Set1–17 × 127N…131C → patient ID and diagnosis group: RRMS, SPMS, PPMS, controls, etc.).
