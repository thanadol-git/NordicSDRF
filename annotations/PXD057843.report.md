## Limitations

- Run-to-sample mapping (run N = sample S N) is inferred: deposited plate layout lists S1-S24 in order, and the Spectronaut report shows more ant-derived protease groups in runs 1-6 and 13-18 (live) than 7-12 and 19-24 (dehydrated). No explicit deposited table.
- Digest was Trypsin/Lys-C mix; the cleavage column is single-valued, so only Trypsin is recorded (reconcile MAJOR finding).
- Modifications, precursor/fragment tolerances and dia method not deposited (Spectronaut directDIA defaults, no settings file): not available.
- Collision energy "32 NCE" and HCD are inferred from the paper ("collision energy set to 32") and the Orbitrap platform.
- Organism is the metagenome label `food fermentation metagenome`; organism part is not applicable. Environmental medium uses a FOODON label (`fermented dairy food product`) because ENVO has no yogurt term; the validator does not check it.
- Treatment values and the custom `ant collection season` column are free text. Season "spring" corresponds to ants collected end of May 2022 (sample names say June); autumn to start of October 2022. Date the yogurts were made/sampled is not stated.
- Reduction/alkylation reagents not stated; omitted.
