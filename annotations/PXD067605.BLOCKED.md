# PXD067605 — BLOCKED

Checked: 2026-09-26

## Evidence

- ProteomeXchange record and full indexed inventory: https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/?pxid=PXD067605
  - Six raw files: three `..._CircRNR2_<1-3>.raw` and three `..._IVC_<1-3>.raw`, plus `MS_CircMT-RNR2.xlsx.zip` and `checksum.txt`.
  - Human; instrument recorded as LTQ Orbitrap; iodoacetamide derivatization recorded.
- Publication: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13159150/
  - Describes label-free MS of a circMT-RNR2 junction-probe RNA pulldown versus a control probe in HDFa cells and confirms PXD067605.

## Blocker

Although all six raw names are visible, the public evidence never explicitly defines the filename code `IVC` or cross-walks it to the paper's control probe. The paper also omits the required digestion/search and acquisition settings, while the only deposited result is a zipped spreadsheet rather than an exposed search-parameter file. Mapping `IVC` to control and asserting a cleavage/acquisition method would therefore be unsupported. This is close to annotatable, but the required run-condition crosswalk is not explicit enough to treat as established.

No SDRF was written or validated.
