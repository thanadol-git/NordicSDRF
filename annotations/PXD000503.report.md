## Limitations

- Paper full text not retrievable (not open access; Europe PMC fullTextXML 500, PMC efetch gave only metadata). All sample/prep facts come from the PRIDE description, file names and RAW headers. PRIDE sample/data protocols are "Not available".
- Cleavage agent: Arg-C taken from the Mascot "semi-Arg-C" setting; true digest is presumably trypsin after lysine blocking in TAILS, not stated in the deposit.
- Modifications taken from the PRIDE text, not from search files (pep.xml header in first 60 KB had no search_summary; files are 65-80 MB and were not downloaded).
- Dissociation: spectra are CID/HCD pairs merged; SDRF carries CID only (dissociation method is a single-value column; a two-value form was not accepted by build). HCD (iTRAQ reporter quantitation) is noted here only.
- Instrument: from RAW headers (2011 LTQ Orbitrap XL, 2013 LTQ Orbitrap Velos); PRIDE lists LTQ Orbitrap and Velos. Headers read for one file per experiment only.
- 2011 PO runs 01-04 are named PO_1, PO_2, PO_2, PO_1. Interpreted as fractions 1 and 2 injected twice; the second injections (04, 03) are coded technical replicate 2, fractions 1 and 2. This is an inference from names.
- PO/NPO are coded as separate source names; fractions 1-8 per series assumed from run names (PO_n / NPO_n) and the TPP text on "peptide fractions". Fractionation method not stated (column omitted).
- Cell line: "Balb/c 3T3" has several Cellosaurus clones (A31 CVCL_0184, A31-1-1, A31-1-13); clone not stated, so Cellosaurus accession/name are `not available`. MMP10 experiment uses primary MEFs, so no cell-line template there.
- Organ part, sex, developmental stage: `not available` (culture supernatant has no clean UBERON term; not stated). Disease `not applicable`. Strain for MMP10 MEFs not available.
- Channel 118 in MMP10 is 12 h MMP10 treated; in GluC 118 is the 12 h buffer control (per PRIDE text).
- Biological replicate = 1 for all; replicate structure not described.
