# PXD025390 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The deposited RAW manifest identifies TMT set/fraction acquisitions but does not identify which mouse, treatment group, tissue sample, or biological replicate occupies each reporter channel. The publication and checked supplementary files do not supply a complete reporter-to-sample table that can be joined to those sets.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE project record: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD025390`
- PRIDE file inventory: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD025390/files/all`
- Publication: `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12007623/` (DOI `10.1096/fj.202403251rr`)
- Publication supplementary-file bundle
- Deposited `3270_raw_files.xlsx`

PRIDE lists 120 RAW files and declares TMT quantification. `3270_raw_files.xlsx` gives exact RAW-to-acquisition labels such as `3270 Total Placenta S1:1` and therefore supports material class, set, and fraction mapping. It does not contain reporter columns or per-channel biological identities. The publication describes the maternal-adiponectin mouse groups and placenta/liver analyses, but the checked article and supplements do not provide a lossless join from those groups and individual replicates to each TMT reporter in every deposited set.

## Evidence needed to unblock

The original TMT experimental-design sheet (or equivalent authoritative export) listing each set, reporter channel, exact biological sample/mouse, tissue, treatment/genotype group, and reference/empty channel. A run-to-set table alone is insufficient because it cannot establish channel identity.
