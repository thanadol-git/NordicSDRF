## Decisions and caveats

- Single protease (trypsin); no second cleavage-agent column.
- Label: `label free sample`; 13C incorporation is metabolic and recorded in `treatment`, not as a label.
- Tolerance conflict: PRIDE prose says OMSSA, 5 ppm / 0.01 Da; deposited idXML says MS-GF+, 10 ppm. Deposited output was used for precursor; fragment 0.01 Da is from prose only.
- Geographic location and dissociation method: `not available`. Biological replicate set to 1 (no replicates evidenced).
- `characteristics[treatment]` = `13C-cellulose` triggers an ontology warning (not in NCIT/EFO; NCIT "Cellulose" would drop the isotope label).
- Environmental medium `anaerobic digester sludge` (ENVO:00003965) is an inference from the reactor description.
- mzML/idXML derivatives are not assay files and were excluded.
