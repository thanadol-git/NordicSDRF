## Limitations (important)

- No publication/DOI exists yet and no file-to-sample mapping is deposited. The protocol says n = 3 biological replicates, samples at t0, 15, 30, 60 min post infection, but the 26 files (not 12 or 24) cannot be assigned to strain (WT vs possible CgpS variant), time point or replicate without inventing. Each file is therefore its own source (NP7...NP32) with biological replicate 1 as a placeholder (needed for unique row identity), and `factor value[sampling time]` = `not available` (a factor column is required). This SDRF should be revised once the authors supply the map.
- Strain/genotype not captured: PRIDE gives C. glutamicum MB001; not a column in the contract; genotype not stated per file.
- Mass tolerances `not available` (DIA-NN auto-optimised). Scan window limits `not available` (only MS1 range 400-1000 stated). `comment[dia method]` not available.
- Deposited DIA-NN report.log/pg_matrix refer only to NP6 (not in the deposit); modifications read from that log and lib.log (Carbamidomethyl C fixed, Oxidation M variable, N-term Acetyl variable; UNIMOD:1 at DIA-NN `*n` written as Any N-term).
- Reduction (TCEP), alkylation (iodoacetamide), HCD 30% NCE, isolation 6 m/z, MS1 400-1000 m/z are from the PRIDE protocol text only.
