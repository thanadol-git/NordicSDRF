# PXD008973 annotation report

Human cord-blood CD4+ T cells: naive (Thp), and Th0 vs Th17 at 24 and 72 hours. University of Turku. Paper: Tripathi et al., iScience 2019.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 75 rows: five donors × (Thp, Th0 24 h, Th0 72 h, Th17 24 h, Th17 72 h) × 3 technical replicates. The donor number in the file name is the individual and the biological replicate.

## Values
- **Organism part:** umbilical cord blood. Age, sex and ancestry are `not available`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF, per the raw file headers. PRIDE lists Q Exactive. HCD.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.5.5.1. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: every annotated value agrees with the deposit record, apart from the instrument (raw headers say Q Exactive HF; the record says Q Exactive).
