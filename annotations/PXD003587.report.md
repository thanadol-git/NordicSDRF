## Key decisions
- **Design**: biological replicate k = bioreactor pair k; `iTMT_k_R1`/`R2` are the two technical injections of replicate k (paper: duplicate injections, results merged per biological replicate). Label-free runs are one injection per replicate, numbering taken from the file name.
- **iodoTMT channel map** (the main judgement call): TMT126 control total Cys, TMT127 APAP total Cys, TMT128 control SNO, TMT129 APAP SNO, TMT130 control SOH, TMT131 APAP SOH. The paper normalises with ratios 127/126 (total cysteines), 129/128 (SNO), 131/130 (SOH) and states that APAP-only peptides show reporter "129 for SNO and 131 for SOH". 129 and 131 = APAP is explicit; 127 = APAP and 126/128/130 = control follow from the parallel numerator/denominator structure and are **inferred**. No per-channel table exists in the paper, PRIDE or the `.msf` (the PD quant method only defines tags 126-131). The SNO/SOH reduction chemistry per channel pair comes from the earlier method paper (ref 13), which was not read. The channel readout (totalCys/SNO/SOH) is encoded only in the source name, because no template-licensed column carries it.
- **Source names** are distinct per channel/readout and per experiment (`C3A_control_1_SNO`, `C3A_APAP_2_labelfree`, ...). The paper says the label-free aliquot came from each iodoTMT experiment but does not map bioreactor identities, so the label-free and TMT sources were not merged.
- **Modifications taken from the deposited Mascot search, not the prose**:
  - iTMT (`FUS02205_iTMT_1_R1.msf`): Oxidation (M), custom "TMT6-Cys" (C, 329.2266 Da = iodoTMT6plex, UNIMOD:1342) and Carbamidomethyl (C), all **dynamic**; no static mods.
  - Label-free (`FUS02231_C_1.msf`): Carbamidomethyl (C) static, Oxidation (M) dynamic.
  - The label-free rows have a third `comment[modification parameters]` column set to `not applicable` purely so one file has a single header (nothing else was searched).
- **Tolerances**: precursor 10 ppm. Fragment tolerance: iTMT 0.05 Da (deposited `.msf`) although the paper text says 20 mmu (0.02 Da); the deposited search was used. Label-free 0.6 Da (`.msf` and paper agree).
- **Instrument**: Orbitrap Fusion (MS:1002416) - paper, PRIDE and both inspected raw headers agree. Raw headers: ActivationType HCD; iTMT NCE 38, Orbitrap MS2 (30K); label-free NCE 32, ion trap rapid MS2. These differ by arm, so `collision energy`, `ms2 mass analyzer` and `fragment mass tolerance` are set per arm.
- **Cell line**: `C3A` (EFO:0002121), Cellosaurus Hep-G2/C3A `CVCL_1098`, ATCC CRL-10741. Donor age 15Y, sex male, disease hepatoblastoma are Cellosaurus properties, not statements in the paper (the paper calls the line hepatocellular-carcinoma derived).
- **Treatment**: `acetaminophen` vs `not applicable` for control; factor value[treatment] mirrors it. Dose (5 mg/mg) and 48 h are in the paper but there is no licensed bare-value slot for the dose; `sampling time` = `48 hour`.

## Conflicts and how resolved
- PRIDE/paper fragment tolerance 20 mmu vs deposited `.msf` 0.05 Da: deposited search wins (flagged).
- PRIDE keyword and paper say hepatocarcinoma; Cellosaurus says hepatoblastoma: Cellosaurus used (flagged).
- PRIDE lists `Mascot`, `Proteome Discoverer`, `MaxQuant`; no MaxQuant output is deposited (not used).
- `sdrf-tools reconcile` raised 4 findings, all judged acceptable: (1) trypsin "unsupported" - the PRIDE data-processing protocol and both `.msf` state trypsin; (2) "labelled annotated as label free" - both arms exist, the 6 label-free rows are genuinely label-free files; (3) organism part from cultured material - `liver` is the cell line's origin per Cellosaurus (corpus convention for HepG2); (4) disease with control arm - the "control" is untreated cells of the same cell line, not disease-free tissue, so the cell-line disease holds on every row.

## Stated vs inferred
- **Stated**: organism, cell line source (ATCC), treatment arms and 48 h, replicate structure, instrument, enzyme, search modifications/tolerances (deposited `.msf`), HCD/NCE from raw headers, DMEM.
- **Inferred**: TMT126/128/130 = control and TMT127 = APAP; label-free replicate numbering = file-name numbers; disease/age/sex from Cellosaurus; organism part and cell type (`liver`, `hepatocyte`, matching PRIDE `organismParts`).
- **Not examined**: only 2 of 12 raw headers and 2 of 12 `.msf` files were opened (1 per arm); other runs assumed to share settings. Supplementary tables and ref 13 were not read.
