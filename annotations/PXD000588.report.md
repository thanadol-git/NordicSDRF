# PXD000588 SDRF annotation report

## Study
A Quantitative Chemical Proteomics Approach to Profile the Specific Cellular Targets of Andrographolide, a Promising Anticancer Agent that Suppresses Tumor Metastasis. The approach is ICABPP: iTRAQ combined with clickable activity-based protein profiling. Lab of Qingsong Lin, Department of Biological Sciences, National University of Singapore. Paper: Wang J et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 2014;13(3):876-886, PMID 24445406, DOI 10.1074/mcp.M113.029793, PMC3945915. The paper's Acknowledgments cite PXD000588, so the PX record links the right paper.

## Scope, evidence and decisions
Status: annotated. The file has 4 rows (1 run x 4 used iTRAQ channels) and 40 columns. Templates: ms-proteomics, human and cell-lines (v1.1.0).

- **Deposited vs annotated.** The ProteomeCentral PROXI record (rev 2, 2024-10-22) lists two files: `121019_JG_Andro_1DLC_8plex_ITRAQ_2ul.mzml` (the "Associated raw file") and `121019_JG_Andro_1DLC_8plex_ITRAQ_2ul.group.xml` (ProteinPilot search output). The campaign cache said "1 raw". The single "raw" is in fact an mzML; no native SCIEX `.wiff` is deposited. The mzML is the only MS data file, so it is `comment[data file]` and it is annotated (1/1). The `.group.xml` is a search result and gets no row. PRIDE `files/all` and the FTP listing were unreachable today (upstream connect error), so the file list comes from the PX record only.
- **Channel map (STATED).** The Methods say the two biological replicates of the negative-control (DMSO) pull-down were labelled with iTRAQ reagents 113 and 114. The two biological replicates of the Andro-probe pull-down were labelled 116 and 117. The Figure 1 legend repeats 116/117 for the probe samples. All four were pooled and compared "within a single LC-MS/MS analysis". Channels 115, 118, 119 and 121 are not mentioned, so they get no rows.
- **iTRAQ 8plex, not 4plex.** The paper says only "iTRAQ Reagent kit (AB SCIEX)". Channel 113 exists only in the 8plex kit. The file name says `8plex` and the PX modification is MOD:01549 (iTRAQ8plex). So the 8plex reagent is used: UNIMOD:730 iTRAQ8plex, labels `iTRAQ113` etc.
- **Samples.** HCT116 colorectal carcinoma cells (ATCC) were used for target ID. HepG2, HeLa and MV4-11 appear only in proliferation assays and are not in this run. Treatment: clickable Andro analogue probe P2 at 100 uM for 4 h (1% DMSO), or DMSO alone as the control. Cellosaurus CVCL_0291 "HCT 116" gives male, 48Y, colon carcinoma (NCIt C4910), from colon (UBERON:0001155). Age and sex are therefore the cell-line donor values from Cellosaurus.
- **Prep (stated).** Prep steps, as stated:
  - Lysis by sonication in PBS.
  - CuAAC click to biotin-azide (TCEP/TBTA/CuSO4), then streptavidin pull-down.
  - On bead: TCEP at 65 C for 60 min, MMTS alkylation, and trypsin overnight.
  - iTRAQ in 0.5 M TEAB for 2 h, then pooling.
  - SCX cartridge cleanup and Sep-Pak C18 desalting. No fractionation, so fraction identifier is 1 and no fractionation-method column is written.
- **LC-MS (stated).** Eksigent nanoLC Ultra coupled to a TripleTOF 5600 in IDA mode (DDA). The paper and the PX record agree on the instrument (MS:1000932). The mzML header was not read because FTP was unreachable. Dissociation is written as beam-type CID (MS:1000422), the collision-cell CID of a Q-TOF. This comes from the instrument type, not from a stated value.
- **Search.** ProteinPilot 4.5 Paragon against IPI human v3.87. Settings stated: Cys alkylation MMTS, trypsin, instrument TripleTOF 5600, "biological modifications" ID focus. Modification parameters: Methylthio (UNIMOD:39) C Fixed, following MMTS. iTRAQ8plex (UNIMOD:730) K and Any N-term Fixed is INFERRED from how a ProteinPilot iTRAQ 8plex sample type works. Paragon has no explicit fixed/variable list, and the deposited `.group.xml` could not be read. The ~hundreds of "biological modifications" Paragon considers are not listed.

## Values left not available / not applicable
- `cell type`: not available (not stated; Cellosaurus gives none).
- `ancestry category`, `passage number`, `sample storage temperature`: not available.
- `culture medium` = `McCoy's 5A medium` (STATED). The Methods say HCT116 was kept in modified McCoy's 5A medium with L-glutamine and 10% FBS. This was wrongly left `not available` in the first version; review round 1 F1 caught it. The value is free text because no NCIT/EFO term resolved: an Ontobee NCIT search for "McCoy" found no terms, and OLS was rate-limited. The template's ontology check for this column is warning-level.
- `precursor/fragment mass tolerance`: not available. ProteinPilot Paragon does not take user tolerances and none are stated.

## Conflicts

- The paper does not name the plex. It is resolved to 8plex as described above.
