## Design
- Dataset 1 (Data_Set1_LCMS.zip, Evosep One): 15-day treatment of WT HEK293, EtOH / chloramphenicol (5 ug/ml) / actinonin (15 uM) / actinonin+chloramphenicol, 4 biological replicates each. Mapping of runs to conditions from MaxQuant summary.txt experiment names. factor value[treatment].
- Dataset 2 (Data_Set2_LCMS.zip, nanoElute): 3-day EtOH or 15 uM actinonin in Cas9 and ATP6AP1 KO cells, quadruplicates. No condition-to-file map is deposited, so treatment, genotype and factor are `not available`; biological replicate = run order 1-16.

## Limitations
- 9 of 25 Data_Set1 .d folders (Taru1_S1-A1, 2-4_S1-A2..A4, 9-12_S1-B1..B4, 18_S1-C2_1_941) were not in the MaxQuant search and have no identifiable condition; they are not in the SDRF. Dataset 1 coverage is therefore 16 of 25 runs.
- Dataset 2 conditions unrecoverable from deposit (analysis.tdf/SampleInfo.xml carry only run names; proteinGroups ATP6AP1 check inconclusive). Authors should supply the file map.
- Raw data are zip archives of Bruker .d folders; comment[data file] uses the .d folder name.
- Cellosaurus supplies sex (female) and kidney; age/cell type/passage/storage not stated. DMEM from paper.
- Precursor tolerance 10 ppm from mqpar (protocol text says 6 ppm); fragment 40 ppm is the MaxQuant TOF default. Enzyme recorded as Trypsin (search used Trypsin/P). CID dissociation inferred; instrument not verified from analysis.tdf.
- characteristics[genotype] is not in the template contract (extra column, as in other annotations); validator accepted it.
