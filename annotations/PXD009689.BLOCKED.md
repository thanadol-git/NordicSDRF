# PXD009689 BLOCKED: no TMT channel-to-sample map obtainable

Status: not annotated; no SDRF, report or evidence.json written. Independent review is moot.

Dataset: "Neuroproteomics in Brd1 knockout mice (frontal cortex _ synaptosome)", Aarhus (Paternoster / Palmfeldt).
Paper: Paternoster et al., Neurobiol Dis 2018, PMID 30590179, doi 10.1016/j.nbd.2018.12.011.
Design from PRIDE: female Brd1+/- mice, mouse (NEWT 10090), one TMT10plex pool of 10 samples, Pierce MCX into 8 fractions,
EASY-nLC 1000 / Q Exactive Plus, trypsin, PD 1.4 + Mascot. 16 RAW files (named F4-F8, "exclusion" and "3h-gradient" reruns, plus an unlabelled Q1533.raw).

## Why blocked
The SDRF needs one row per TMT channel, each with a source sample and genotype. Nothing available says which of the 10 channels is Brd1+/- or wild type, how many mice are in each group, or their age. Inventing this is not allowed (channel-map.md).

Rungs of the channel-map ladder checked:
1. Paper: not open access in Europe PMC (no PMCID, fullTextXML 500). The KCL Pure green AAM PDF returns HTTP 403 for scripted access, as does ScienceDirect (403). Only the abstract was readable; it gives no n or channel layout.
2. Deposited PRIDE files: 16 RAW, one PD .msf (3.9 GB) and one .mgf. No sample sheet. The .msf was not downloaded in full (rule); PD 1.4 only stores generic channel names, not sample identities.
3. Europe PMC supplementary: unavailable (no PMCID).
4. External repositories: none cited in the PRIDE record. Sibling projects (PXD009688, 9690, 9691, 9692, 9961) carry the same generic protocol text.
5. Cross-validation: not possible.

## Secondary gaps
- Mapping of Q1533.raw to a fraction is unstated (the other 15 are F4-F8 variants; F1-F3 are absent).
- Age and strain are not in the PRIDE record.
