Trypsin is also confirmed by MaxQuant summary.txt (Trypsin/P). Kept as Trypsin.

## Key inferences and limitations

- H/V to eye mapping is inferred: the protocol says BRVO was induced in the right eye and the left eye was the control, and file suffixes are H/V (Danish hoejre/venstre = right/left). The deposit never states the mapping. It is supported by deposited MaxQuant LFQ data: osteopontin (P14287, SPP1), reported upregulated in BRVO, is higher in H than V in 7 of 8 animals (mean log2 28.9 vs 28.2). Needs reviewer confirmation against the full paper, which was not retrievable (not open access).
- Disease uses the generic MONDO term "retinal vein occlusion"; the experimental model is branch-specific and laser-induced, 15 days post-induction (abstract). No treatment or sampling-time column was added.
- Sex, breed, age or developmental stage and cell type: not stated, written as not available.
- Precursor tolerance: absent from the deposited parameters.txt and the protocol, so not available. Fragment tolerance 20 ppm (FTMS) is from parameters.txt.
- Instrument is Q Exactive Plus per the protocol text; the PRIDE dropdown says Q Exactive.
- Single protease (trypsin), so one cleavage-agent column. MaxQuant used Trypsin/P; annotated as Trypsin.
- Modifications come from the deposited MaxQuant output (Cehofski_BRVO.zip, read by HTTP range; no RAW file was downloaded): fixed Carbamidomethyl (C); variable Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term). The BSA internal standard (20 fmol/uL) was not added as a spiked-compound column.
- Run names 8v_01..03 are lowercase v in the deposit and kept as-is in `comment[data file]`.
- Biological replicate numbers 1-8 map to animals 3-10; each eye is its own source (paired design).
