# PXD012153 — annotation blocked

Proteome of PANC-1 pancreatic ductal cells with persistent coxsackievirus B1 (CVB1) infection vs non-infected cells. University of Turku. Paper: Lietzén et al., iScience 2019.

## What is known
- **Runs:** samples 1–9, each injected 3 times. Q Exactive HF.
- **Design:** the paper compares mock-infected cells with cells persistently infected with two CVB1 strains (ATCC and 10796).
- **Search:** MaxQuant against human SwissProt plus enterovirus sequences. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Why it is blocked
The file names only give sample numbers. No deposited text maps samples 1–9 to mock, CVB1 ATCC or CVB1 10796. The `MaxQuant.7z` archive was not opened; its experimental design or summary file may contain the key.

## What would unblock it
A table mapping samples 1–9 to infection group, or the experimental design file from `MaxQuant.7z`.
