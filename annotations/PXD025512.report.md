# PXD025512 annotation report

*Lithobius forficatus* venom, telopodal defensive secretion and leg proteomes. The labs are Queensland (MS) and Greifswald/Oslo, with an NTNU (Centre for Biodiversity Dynamics) co-author. Paper: BMC Biology 2024, PMC11170834. TripleTOF 5600, ProteinPilot. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `invertebrates` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 9 rows, one per `.wiff`. Each `.wiff.scan` companion is part of the same acquisition and is not listed separately. The sample map comes from the paper's Methods and the file names:

| Files | Sample |
|---|---|
| `Oslo_Lf1–3venom` | venom from three individual adult males (Oslo) |
| `Oslo_Lf1–3glue` | telopodal defensive secretion from the same three males |
| `20170521_LforF` / `LforR` | front and posterior legs (TRIzol protein fraction), pooled from Greifswald specimens (3 males and 2 females) |
| `20160920_LithobiusTermL` | Greifswald telopodal defensive secretion, pooled |

**Inferred:** the paper lists four sample types (venom, defensive secretion, front legs, posterior legs). "TermL" (terminal legs) is read as the Greifswald defensive secretion because it is the only Greifswald secretion run deposited. No file or table states this explicitly.

The Oslo individuals keep their numbers 1–3 as `individual` and biological replicate. Greifswald pools are `pooled`.

## Values
- **Sample:**
  - Organism parts: `venom` (UBERON:0007113), `secretion of exocrine gland` (UBERON:0000456) and `leg` (UBERON:0000978).
  - Strain is `not applicable` (wild-caught).
- **Sample prep:** reduction and alkylation with DTT/IAA; trypsin.
- **Search:**
  - Carbamidomethyl C is the declared alkylation. The ProteinPilot Paragon search also allowed a large set of "biological modifications", which cannot be listed here.
  - Tolerances are `not available` (Paragon does not use user-set tolerances).
- **Acquisition:** DDA; dissociation is beam-type CID (QTOF).
