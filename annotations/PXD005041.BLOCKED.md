# PXD005041 — blocked

## Decision

An SDRF cannot be authored without inventing the mapping from the deposited RAW runs to the ten culture conditions and SILAC channels.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE project record: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD005041`
  - The project describes absolute quantification of the *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* proteome under ten environmental conditions.
  - Each analytical sample was mixed 1:1 with a common heavy `15N,13C` lysine-labeled internal standard; MaxQuant multiplicity was 2 with Lys8 as the heavy label.
  - The project protocol supplies preparation and search settings but no table translating run codes to conditions or biological replicates.
- Complete PRIDE file listing: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD005041/files/all`
  - It contains 48 RAW files with coded suffixes such as `C5U5`, `C3U6_R1`, `P11A`, and `P10B`.
  - The two non-RAW files are 988 MB and 485 MB `.rar` search bundles; no separate sample sheet, `mqpar.xml`, or other compact run map is deposited.
- Linked publication and both PMC supplementary PDFs were checked: Sánchez et al. (2017), DOI `10.15252/msb.20167411`, PMCID `PMC5572397`.
  - The paper is principally a metabolic-modeling study and refers to proteomic data from another study. It does not decode the PRIDE run identifiers or provide a run-to-condition table for PXD005041.
- Community annotation repository lookup returned no existing SDRF for this accession.

## Exact blocker

The public evidence does not map the 48 coded RAW filenames to the ten environmental conditions and biological replicates. It also does not identify, per run, the light experimental source and the heavy common-standard source in a form that can be translated to SILAC channel rows. Decoding tokens such as `C5U5` or `P11A` from naming patterns would be inference, not evidence.

## Needed to unblock

The MaxQuant experiment design/sample table from either search bundle (or an equivalent author-provided table) mapping every RAW file to condition, replicate, and light/heavy source identity.
