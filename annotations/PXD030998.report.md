## Key decisions
- Sources are the MaxQuant Experiment names from summary.txt (7Inter_A_I ... 7Norm_E_I, 7Inter_A_III ... 7Norm_C_III).
- Conditions: Inter = P. amylolyticus colony interacting with X. retroflexus, Norm = non-interacting colony, 48 h. Mapping is inferred from the names plus the paper's interacting vs non-interacting design; supported by identification counts (Inter ~420-640 protein groups, Norm ~1450-2000). Factor value[treatment] = this comparison.
- Instrument Q Exactive (RAW headers of both groups; paper) overrides PRIDE's Q Exactive HF.
- Collision energy 25 NCE from RAW method; paper says 28%. Raw wins; HCD MS:1000422.
- Cleavage agent Trypsin (record's digest reagent); the deposited search used Trypsin/P. `reconcile` reports a BLOCKER cleavage_agent_unsupported, a false positive (its regex misses "incubated overnight by trypsin"; the PRIDE text also says "tryptic peptides").
- Modifications: variable Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term from summary.txt; fixed Carbamidomethyl C from the PRIDE protocol (summary.txt lists no fixed mods). DTT and iodoacetamide from the protocol.
- Organism part, cell type, disease: not applicable (whole colony biomass; PRIDE "biofilm" overridden).
