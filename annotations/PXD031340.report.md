# PXD031340 annotation report

Bones / Nymark group (Dept. of Biology, NTNU, Trondheim), with MS at PROMEC. The samples are *Phaeodactylum tricornutum* WT and two CRISPR/Cas9 cpftsy knockout lines acclimated to medium light (ML). Paper: Nymark et al., Plant Cell Physiol 2023 (PMC10269134; Methods S1 read from the supplement). Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 only. A diatom fits neither `plants` nor `invertebrates`.

## Rows and files
15 rows, one per deposited `.raw`: WT, FTSY5 and FTSY6, each with replicates I–V (5 biological replicates per line, as in the paper). The extra `FTSY5-I__1_.mzML` and `.mzid` are not vendor raw files and are not listed.

## Values
- **Organism:** `Phaeodactylum tricornutum CCAP 1055/1` (NCBITaxon:556484), strain Pt1 8.6. Material type is `whole organism`.
- **Genotype:** `wild type` or `CpFTSY knockout (CRISPR/Cas9)` (factor).
- **Growth:** ML at about 200 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹, continuous light, 15 °C (paper).
- **Instrument and acquisition:** Q Exactive HF, confirmed from the raw header. HCD at 27 NCE. Tolerances are 10 ppm / 0.02 Da.
- **Sample prep and search:**
  - Reduction was TCEP and alkylation was chloroacetamide.
  - Modifications: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term, Met-loss+Acetyl and Deamidated NQ variable. These come from Methods, since no PD study file was deposited.

## Line identity (FTSY5 / FTSY6)
The paper names the two mutant lines cpftsy.1-25.7 and cpftsy.2-4.8, but the files are labelled FTSY5 and FTSY6. The deposited Proteome Discoverer export (`Nymark_FTSY_med_annotering_Phatr2.xlsx`) resolves this:
- Its normalized abundances reproduce the paper's Supplemental Table S3 exactly when F16–20 are taken as cpftsy.1-25.7, F21–25 as cpftsy.2-4.8 and F26–30 as WT. Over all 32 published proteins, the two lines' log2 ratios match with r = 1.000 and a mean absolute error of 0.003. Every other assignment of the three blocks fits badly (MAE ≥ 0.39).
- PD names the export sheet after its first input file, `200108_Nymark_FTSY5-I`, so F16 is FTSY5-I. WT occupies the last block, which matches alphabetical file order (FTSY5, FTSY6, WT).

`characteristics[individual]` therefore records FTSY5 as **cpftsy.1-25.7** and FTSY6 as **cpftsy.2-4.8**, and source names keep the deposited labels. The link from F-number to file name is inferred from PD's ordering, not written in the export. The block-to-line mapping is exact.
