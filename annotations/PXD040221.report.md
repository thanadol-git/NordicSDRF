## Key decisions

High-pH fractions as listed in the sheet (fraction 1 = pH-adjustment fraction). Faunal samples recorded as Bovidae (bovid database). Taxon names for extinct hominins are used as free text (no NCBITaxon exact match found for Paranthropus robustus, H. antecessor, H. erectus, Gigantopithecus blacki).
