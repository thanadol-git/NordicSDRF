# PXD009900 annotation report

Phosphoproteome and proteome of KRAS-mutant A549 lung cancer cells after siRNA knockdown of PP2A pathway genes, with or without the MEK inhibitor trametinib. University of Turku. Paper: Kauko et al., Sci Transl Med 2018.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human`, `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 36 rows: siRNA (scrambled control, PPP2R1A, CIP2A, PME-1) × trametinib (100 nM, 20 h, or none) × 3 replicates, each as a TiO2 phosphopeptide-enriched run and a non-enriched run. The key is the deposited `File_names_raw_msf.xlsx`.
- The replicates `_1–3` used different siRNA sequences, so they are annotated as biological replicates.

## Inferences to check
- **Modifications:** the protocol points to an earlier paper and does not list the search modifications. Carbamidomethyl C fixed and Oxidation M plus Phospho S/T/Y variable are assumed.
- **Vehicle:** the no-drug condition is annotated `no drug`; the vehicle is not stated.

## Values
- **Cell line:** A-549 (CVCL_0023), lung adenocarcinoma.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive. HCD.
- **Search:** Proteome Discoverer 1.4.1.14 / Mascot 2.4.1 with phosphoRS 3.0, plus a SpectraST spectral-library search.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: two findings, both reviewed and kept as annotated:
  - `disease_on_control_runs`: the control arm is scrambled-siRNA A549 cells, which are still lung adenocarcinoma.
  - `cleavage_agent_unsupported`: the record points to an earlier paper for sample preparation and never names the enzyme; trypsin is assumed from that standard workflow.
