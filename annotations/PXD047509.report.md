# PXD047509 annotation report

## Evidence used

- PRIDE PXD047509 metadata identifies a label-free LTQ Orbitrap immunoprecipitation experiment in *Caenorhabditis elegans*.
- The two deposited RAW filenames encode the matched strains: `112009tcriu171gbpsolndig.raw` and `112009tcrn2gbpsolndig.raw`.
- The PRIDE protocol specifies that HCF-1::GFP was immunoprecipitated from IU171 worms and that the same anti-GFP immunoprecipitation from wild-type N2 worms was the negative control.
- The deposited record specifies tryptic digestion and cites Emerson et al., bioRxiv 2023, DOI: 10.1101/2023.03.20.531974.

## Annotation decisions

- Each RAW file is represented by one label-free assay row and one source row.
- `HCF-1::GFP transgene` is the experimental factor: present for IU171 and absent for N2.
- Exact search modifications were not stated in the deposit, so the modification-parameters field is recorded as `not available` rather than inferred from the cited protocol.
