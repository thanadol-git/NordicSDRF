# PXD020561 annotation report

Secretome of intestinal smooth muscle explants from wild-type and *Mmp17*−/− mice (Oudhoff lab, CEMIR/NTNU; MS in Utrecht). Paper: Nat Commun 2021, PMC8602650. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `vertebrates` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 6 rows, one per `.raw`: WT1–3 and KO1–3. The deposited `evidence.txt` maps each run to its Experiment.

## Values
- **Sample:**
  - The sample is muscle-SN: 24 h DMEM-F12 conditioned medium from 2–4 mm strips of muscularis propria, with the epithelium removed (paper, "Mass spectrometry of muscle-SN").
  - Organism part is `muscle layer of intestine` (UBERON:0012367). The paper used either colon or the first 10 cm of duodenum for muscle preparations and does not say which for MS, so the generic term is used.
  - Strain is C57BL/6 background. Developmental stage is `adult` (experiments used 8–20-week-old mice); exact age and sex are `not available`.
- **Instrument:** **Q Exactive HF** (raw header; also the paper). PRIDE lists Q Exactive. HCD at 27 NCE.
- **Sample prep:** DTT/IAA; Lys-C, then trypsin.
- **Search:** from the paper and confirmed in `evidence.txt`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable; 20 ppm / 20 ppm.
- **Experiment type:** PRIDE calls this "Top-down proteomics", which is wrong; the samples were digested.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf` passes for both templates; the only warning is the free-text strain. `tools reconcile` reports a Lys-C/Trypsin blocker. It reads only the first cleavage column; both enzymes are declared, so this is a false positive.
