# PAD000044 annotation report

## Result

56-row affinity-proteomics SDRF for the three deposited NPX plates (`MAIT-Plate1.npx.csv`, `MAIT-Plate2.npx.csv`, `MAIT-Plate3.npx.csv`). 20 unique sources (16 donor×stimulus conditioned media + 4 cell-free media blanks). Templates: `affinity-proteomics` v1.0.0 + `human` v1.1.0. Factor is `factor value[treatment]`.

Not already in `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets`.

## Evidence

- PRIDE: PAD000044. García-Escribano et al., *Front Immunol* 2026, PMID 42433370, PMCID PMC13349841, DOI 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1852383. Submitter Juan Carlos López-Rodríguez, Universidad San Pablo-CEU (Madrid).
- Tetramer-enriched circulating MAIT cells from healthy-donor PBMCs (buffy coats, Centro de Transfusiones de la Comunidad de Madrid); 2×10^5 cells cultured 48 h with IL-2 + IL-7 plus unstimulated / IL-33 / IL-12p70 / IL-33+IL-12p70. Assayed material is culture supernatant (secretome), Olink Target 48 Cytokine, NPX Signature v2.2.1, calibrator-normalized. Paper n=4 donors matches NPX Donor1–Donor4.
- Dilutions in the deposit (non-diluted, 1:20, 1:50, 1:100) are hook-effect technical assays of the same supernatant, not extra biological conditions. Rows sharing a donor×stimulus source are technical replicates. Plate IDs: `MAIT_Alarmins1`–`3`; panel lots `932010`, `932007`, `932011`.
- `NoCells-*` wells are cytokine-containing media without cells (carry-over blanks on plate 2). Annotated as `buffer control`.

## Conservative choices

- Age and sex are not in the deposit or paper → `not available`. Developmental stage `adult` from blood-bank buffy coats. Ancestry `not available`.
- Disease `normal` for healthy donors; `not applicable` on cell-free blanks.
- Organism part / sample matrix `culture supernatant`; cell type `mucosal invariant T cell` (CL:0000940). PRIDE `blood` / `primary cell` organism-part fields were not used for the assayed matrix.
- `comment[platform]` is `Olink Target 48` (PRIDE CV is the parent `Olink Target`). Quantification unit `NPX` (template-allowed); the paper analyses pg/mL `QuantifiedValue`.
- 14 wells have 1–4 assays with a neighbouring SampleID (NPX export typos, including `Donor3-IL33 -IL12 1-20` and `Donor4-IL12 1-50` vs `Donor4-IL12  1-50`). Identity is the majority SampleID in that well (44–47/48 assays). Calibrators, negative controls and sample controls were not given SDRF rows.
