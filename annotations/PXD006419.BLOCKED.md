# PXD006419 — blocked: RAW aliases cannot be assigned to MSC sample groups

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD006419
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD006419/files/all
- Paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5570998/ (PMID: 28839187; DOI: 10.1038/s41598-017-09127-y)
- Supplement: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC5570998/supplementaryFiles

## Evidence available

The paper specifies DIA comparisons of lung-MSC and bone-marrow-MSC, with three biological donors per source and both cell-layer and conditioned-medium samples. It also describes Q Exactive Plus acquisition and the spectral-library workflow. The supplement confirms the four DIA sample groups: bone-marrow cell layer, lung cell layer, bone-marrow conditioned medium, and lung conditioned medium (n=3 each).

## Blocking gap

PRIDE contains 39 vendor RAW files named only `Emma_H1505_<number>.raw`. Neither the PRIDE record, the paper, nor the supplementary PDF maps those aliases to donor, MSC origin, sample material, DIA versus library acquisition, fraction, or technical replicate. The aggregate group counts cannot establish a file-level SDRF without unsupported inference.

## Unblock requirement

Provide the raw-file sample sheet or another authoritative mapping of the `Emma_H1505_*` files to source, material, assay type, and fraction/replicate.
