# PXD058323 annotation report

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

HepG2-derived cell membrane vesicles (Aarhus; FAIMS Exploris 480, DDA, MaxQuant 1.6.17). No publication is linked or findable, so the record is the PRIDE protocols, the file list, the deposited `mqpar.xml` and the RAW header.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0. Build: 12 rows x 38 columns.

## Rows and files
- 12 rows, one per `.raw` file: two groups (`CaCl`, `Trypsin`) x 3 biological replicates (R1-R3) x 2 raw files.
- The second file per replicate carries an acquisition timestamp in its name. MaxQuant pools both under one experiment, so they are annotated as technical replicates 1 and 2. This is inferred, not stated.
- The `HepG2_CMVs_MzXMLs.zip` members are FAIMS CV splits (-40/-55/-70) of the same RAW files and are not listed as data files.

## Values
- Cell line HepG2 (CVCL_0027). Disease hepatoblastoma, age 15Y and sex male come from Cellosaurus, not from the deposit.
- Organism part is `not available`: PRIDE says liver, but the sample is a cell line vesicle fraction and `tools reconcile` flagged a tissue claim.
- Instrument: Orbitrap Exploris 480 (protocol and RAW header).
- Digest: trypsin only (no second protease). One cleavage-agent column. MaxQuant used Trypsin/P.
- DTT reduction, chloroacetamide alkylation. Fixed Carbamidomethyl C; variable Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term (from `mqpar.xml`).
- Tolerances: 4.5 ppm (main search) and 20 ppm (FTMS fragment default).
- Dissociation method is `not available` (not stated).

## Caveats
- The factor `treatment` = `CaCl` or `Trypsin` is taken from run names. The deposit does not say what the two conditions are (all samples are trypsin-digested, so it is not the digest). The label is unverified and `CaCl` is not an ontology term (validator warning).
- Technical-replicate interpretation of the paired RAW files is inferred.
- The FAIMS compensation voltages are not captured in a column.

- `python -m tools check`: 11 of 11 terms verified, CLEAN. `structure`: OK. `reconcile`: all values agree with the record (after the organism part change).
- Independent review has not been run (no subagents allowed); status: validated annotation; independent review pending.
