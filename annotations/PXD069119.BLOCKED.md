# PXD069119 — BLOCKED

Checked: 2026-09-26

## Evidence

- ProteomeXchange record: https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/?pxid=PXD069119
  - HOLSER partial-proteolysis dataset; human; Orbitrap Exploris 480; TMT.
  - 70 files total: 60 raw files, five search outputs, and five other files. Visible raw examples are fraction files such as `20250603_Super-PELSA_fra_20.raw`.
- Publication linked by the record: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13150797/
  - Uses PXD069119 as HOLSER data in a reanalysis paper but does not publish a file×channel sample key in the web-visible text.

## Blocker

The deposit is multiplexed TMT and fractionated. The public record exposes fraction names but no channel-to-sample key for carrier, empty/reference, treatment, control, and replicate channels. The linked reanalysis paper cites the accession at project level and cannot supply that missing acquisition map. Fraction names alone cannot identify TMT channels.

No SDRF was written or validated.
