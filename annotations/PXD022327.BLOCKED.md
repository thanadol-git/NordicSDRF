# PXD022327 - BLOCKED (no SDRF written)

Status: blocked; no annotation produced. Nothing was committed.

## Why blocked
The dataset is one TMT10plex experiment (8 high-pH fractions, one plex, `TMT_IM_FRACTION01..08_MS3.raw`).
The paper reports n = 3 control vs 3 Dicer(AQP2Cre+) mice, i.e. 6 samples in a 10-channel plex. The record does
not say WHICH channels hold which samples, nor which 4 channels are empty or carry something else. PRIDE says only
"TMT labels were chosen randomly between the two sample groups". `sdrf-tools build` refuses an incomplete
channel map (SKILL.md 6.1) and inventing one would assign wrong genotypes to channels.
