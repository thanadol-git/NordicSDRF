# PXD010999 annotation report

Mouse mammary carcinoma 66cl-4 cells transduced with an empty vector, nephronectin (Npnt), or Npnt integrin-binding mutants. The samples are whole cells, small extracellular vesicles ("exo") and microvesicles ("mv"). Steigedal/Svineng labs with PROMEC, NTNU. Paper: J Proteome Res 2019, PMID 30707844 (not open access). Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `vertebrates` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 48 rows, one per `.raw`. Run names `170607_1…24_JIMITA` are numbers only. The deposited `txt48.zip` contains `Groups.txt` (Name / Batch / Category / Rep), which maps all 48 runs:
- 4 lines (EV, NPNT, RGD, AIA) × 3 materials (cell, exo, mv) × 4 biological replicates.
- Replicates 1–2 were run on 2017-06-07 and replicates 3–4 on 2017-09-04. The date is recorded as `comment[batch]`; the authors' MaxQuant analysis also treats it as a batch (two-way ANOVA).

## Values
- **Cell line:** `66cl-4` (Cellosaurus CVCL_9721), from a female mouse mammary tumour. Organism part `mammary gland`, disease `breast carcinoma`. Strain and age are `not available`.
- **Genetic modification:** the deposit labels are EV, NPNT, RGD and AIA. EV and NPNT are standard (empty vector, Npnt overexpression).
  - The same group's FEBS Open Bio 2018 paper (PMC6275265) describes 66cl4 lines overexpressing an RGD→RGE mutant and an RGE + EIE→AIA double mutant. I mapped deposit "RGD" to the RGE mutant and "AIA" to the RGE-AIA double mutant.
  - **This mapping is inferred:** the JPR paper is paywalled. The source names keep the deposit labels.
- **Material:** `characteristics[cellular component]` is `cell`, `extracellular exosome` (GO:0070062) or `microvesicle` (GO:1990742). `enrichment process` is `extracellular vesicle enrichment` for EV fractions; it is free text, so parse_sdrf warns.
- **Instrument:** **Q Exactive HF** from every raw header checked. PRIDE says Q Exactive, and the PRIDE protocol says LTQ-Orbitrap Elite with CID; both are wrong for these files. Dissociation is HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.5.8.3 `summary.txt`/`parameters.txt`):
  - Carbamidomethyl C is fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidation NQ are variable.
  - Tolerances are 20 ppm / 20 ppm.
- **Reagents:** reduction and alkylation reagents are not stated for these samples, so the columns were left out. The PRIDE protocol describes in-gel digestion of 80/60/20 kDa bands, which is not this experiment.
