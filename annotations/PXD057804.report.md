# PXD057804 annotation report

Murine PDAC spheroids of the UN-KC-6141 line (PROMEC/NTNU). Paper: Sci Data 2025, PMC11828881. Table 2 and Supplementary Table S1 link this PXD to "Murine PDAC spheroids". Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `vertebrates` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 3 rows, one per `.d.tar`. The deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` names the experiments `Spheroid15Kcells`, `Spheroid30Kcells` and `Spheroid60Kcells`, so each row is a hanging-drop spheroid seeded with 15 000, 30 000 or 60 000 cells and collected after 7 days. The spheroid size is the treatment and factor.

The PRIDE protocol mentions only 60 000 cells per drop, but the search output shows three seeding densities.

## Values
- **Cell line:** UN-KC-6141 (CVCL_1U11), passage 39 (paper). Donor age 50 weeks.
- **Instrument:** timsTOF Pro 2 (`analysis.tdf`), DDA-PASEF.
- **Sample prep:** SDC/TCEP/CAA; trypsin.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 2.0.3.0 with defaults plus Deamidation NQ. Fixed Carbamidomethyl C; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable. TOF MS/MS tolerance 40 ppm.

## Validation
CI-style `parse_sdrf` passes; warnings are free-text values only. The `tools reconcile` trypsin flag is a false positive: this PRIDE record's protocol text omits the digest, and the paper states trypsin.
