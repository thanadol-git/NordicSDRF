# PXD045589 annotation report

Zhyvolozhnyi et al., *Nanotheranostics* 2024 (PMC10750121). Sweat extracellular vesicles from eight healthy male volunteers; comparison of total concentrated sweat, ExoEasy EV isolate, and alginate skin-patch collection. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates

- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 (Orbitrap Fusion Lumos DDA, PEAKS search)
- `human` v1.1.0 (Homo sapiens sweat; entire analyzed cohort is human)

Metaproteomics was not used: bacterial proteins were identified, but the samples are human sweat.

## Rows and files

26 data rows, 36 columns. All 26 deposited RAW files claimed once (`comment[data file]` = vendor `.raw` names).

Label-free. R-56 ExoEasy has two injections (technical replicates 1 and 2). No fractionation.

## Conservative choices

- Organism part `sweat` (UBERON:0001089 / BTO:0001254). Cell type `not available` (secretion, not a cell type).
- Disease `normal`; sex `male` from the eight-man cohort; age `not available` (only 35 ± 10 as a cohort summary). Developmental stage `adult`. Ancestry `not available`.
- `R-0` kept as an individual id from the filename (not one of the eight volunteer tokens; few PEAKS features — possible unused-patch control, not stated).
- `R-AG` has no volunteer number; individual `not available`.
- `Blank` = paper negative control (PBS wash of unused gloves): organism part / disease / sex / developmental stage / individual `not applicable`; enrichment method `process blank`. Organism remains `Homo sapiens` because the search identified human contaminants in that run.
- Search modifications from PEAKS `protein-peptides_swissprot.txt` (and PRIDE `identifiedPTMStrings`): Carbamidomethyl C fixed, Oxidation M variable, Deamidated N,Q variable, Acetyl protein N-term variable. Paper Methods only name carbamidomethyl and oxidation; deposited search outranks the paper. Precursor 10 ppm, fragment 0.02 Da from PEAKS/PRIDE protocol.
- Acquisition DDA (`PRIDE:0000627`): 3 s top-N cycles, HCD+CID on precursors. Dissociation column is HCD (`MS:1000422`); CID was also used at equal priority and is not a second column (contract is not `multiple`).
- Reduction DTT / alkylation IAA from Methods. Instrument Orbitrap Fusion Lumos (`MS:1002732`). PRIDE `softwares` says MaxQuant; protocol and deposited identifications are PEAKS 10.6 — software is not written into the SDRF.
- `characteristics[sample type]` omitted after `parse_sdrf` rejected `study sample` against the OLS PRIDE cache (round 1). Role of the glove blank is in the enrichment-method factor.
