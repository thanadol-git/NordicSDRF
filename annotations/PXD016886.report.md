# PXD016886 annotation report

*Hadronyche infensa* (funnel-web spider) venom. The lab is Queensland, with an NTNU co-author. Paper: PNAS 2020 (PMID 32398368). It is not openly available here, so the PRIDE protocols were used. TripleTOF 5600. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `invertebrates` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 14 rows, one per `.wiff`. All come from venom pooled from three specimens:
- `HiOB-IAA-tryp10ul`: whole venom digest.
- `HiOB-fracs-IAA-tryp-01…12`: 12 RP-HPLC fractions, collected every 10 min. They share one source name with fraction identifiers 1–12 and fractionation method RP (PRIDE:0000563).
- `HiOB-u10kda-IAA-tryp`: the below-10 kDa filtrate. Its fraction rule is read from the file name; the PRIDE protocol does not describe the filtration.

## Values
- **Sample prep:** DTT/IAA; trypsin.
- **Search:**
  - The Mascot settings are used: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Amidated C-term and Gln->pyro-Glu variable; 10 ppm / 0.1 Da.
  - The PRIDE text says "carboxymethylation", but the protocol used iodoacetamide, which gives carbamidomethyl.
- **Not stated:** sex and developmental stage are `not available`.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf` passes for both templates, with free-text treatment warnings only. `tools reconcile` reports no disagreements.
