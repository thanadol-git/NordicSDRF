# PXD042321 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"Comparing extraction method efficiency for high-throughput palaeoproteomic bone species identification" (Sci Rep 2023, doi:10.1038/s41598-023-44885-y, PMC10603084). Twelve Late Pleistocene bone specimens (6 from Gruta da Companheira, Portugal: GdC-1, -6, -7, -9, -26, -193; 6 from Ilsenhoehle Ranis, Germany: R10300, R10329, R10340, R10357, R10359, R10363) extracted with six methods plus negative-control blanks, Evosep One 60 SPD DDA on an Orbitrap Exploris 480, MaxQuant 2.1.1.0. Submitter: D. Mylopotamitaki (Paris), extraction at CPR Copenhagen; Evosep method files were acquired under the CPR/Evosep workflow named in the RAW paths (JVO).

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + vertebrates v1.1.0. No cell-line, clinical or oncology template (archaeological bone, no donors). Not DIA.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
PRIDE file list read over all pages (page 0 = 100, page 1 = 6, page 2 empty): 102 RAW files, README.txt, SampletoData.csv, txt-Tryptic.zip and txt-Semitryptic.zip (MaxQuant outputs). All 102 RAW files are annotated (102 rows), including 16 blank runs and the runs the paper says were excluded (3a, 5a, 3b-Ranis). Headers of all 102 RAW files were range-read: Orbitrap Exploris 480, method 21min_LFQ_4e5_15000, HCD collision energy 30.

## Key decisions
- One source per specimen x extraction method (x peptide-solution pH for the two EDTA methods that were split into acidic "ac" and neutral "nt" halves): e.g. R10300_3EDTA_ac. Blanks are Blank01/Blank02 per method (sample type blank, PRIDE:0001016; other sample properties not applicable).
- Specimen identity from the deposited SampletoData.csv. The ID-to-specimen order differs between methods (e.g. ID01 = R10359 for methods 1-2 but R10300 for 3, 5, 6), so IDs were never inferred across methods.
- Extraction method is a custom characteristics[extraction method] with a matching factor value[extraction method] (the compared variable; includes ac/nt pH where applicable).
- File labels vs paper: files 3EDTA = EDTA + protease mix LysC/trypsin (paper method 4), 4EDTA = EDTA + LysC (paper 3), 5EDTA = EDTA/GuHCl, 6SPIN = PAC/SPIN. SampletoData.csv and file names were followed.
- Cleavage agent: two cleavage-agent columns. Trypsin only (1AmBic, 2Acid), Lys-C only (4EDTA), Lys-C + Trypsin (3EDTA, 5EDTA, 6SPIN); the second column of single-enzyme rows is not applicable. MaxQuant searched Trypsin/P (specific; semi-specific as second search) for all files, which does not describe the wet-lab digest.
- Reduction TCEP (PRIDE:0000609) and alkylation NEM (PRIDE:0000606) for SPIN only; other methods not applicable (none described).
- Modifications read from Tryptic/summary.txt per raw file: Oxidation (M), Deamidated (N,Q), Gln->pyro-Glu, Glu->pyro-Glu, Oxidation (P, hydroxyproline) variable for all files; Nethylmaleimide (C) fixed in the 14 SPIN files only (not applicable column in other rows). Both pyro-Glu terms are written with `TA` and `PP=Any N-term` on all 102 rows, the same encoding as the reviewed 19-row slices. MaxQuant lists the names without a position; `PP=Any N-term` is the position those names imply.
- DDA PRIDE:0000627 (OLS4: descendant of PRIDE:0000659), HCD 30 NCE, MS1 350-1400 m/z (paper), fraction 1, fractionation not applicable, label free.
- organism: the 8 specimens with a taxon in the paper's Table 1 (from prior ZooMS/SPIN analysis) carry it on all their rows (51 rows): Ursidae (GdC_1, R10340), Rangifer tarandus (GdC_9, R10329, R10363), Bovidae (GdC_26), Rhinocerotidae (R10300), Equidae (R10357); the other specimens (GdC_6 ambiguous, GdC_7 and GdC_193 not previously identified, R10359 Hyaenidae/Felinae ambiguous) stay Mammalia (class level, NCBITaxon:40674); organism part bone tissue (UBERON:0002481).

## Not available values
disease, developmental stage, strain, sex for specimens; precursor and fragment mass tolerance (not stated in deposit). Specimen identity for the 8 files 4EDTA ID01-ID06 (nt) and ID01-ID02 (ac): absent from SampletoData.csv, so source names end in "_unmapped".

## Conflicts / overrides
- PRIDE lists organisms by family (Hyaenidae, Rhinocerotidae, Bovidae, Felinae, Equidae, Ursidae, Capra, Ovis aries): these are per-specimen results/prior IDs, not one organism; per-specimen taxa are used where Table 1 gives an unambiguous one (see above), Mammalia otherwise.
- Paper says 3a, 5a and 3b-Ranis were excluded, yet their RAW files are deposited; annotated as acquisitions.
- Specimen ages (>50 kya GdC, 40-50 kya Ranis) and sites are not in a template column and are not recorded.

## Inferred vs stated
Stated: specimens/methods (CSV), instrument, HCD/NCE, enzymes, TCEP/NEM, modifications (MaxQuant output). Inferred: Mammalia as organism for specimens without an unambiguous prior identity; biological replicate 1 and technical replicate 1 placeholders; fraction 1; not applicable for reduction/alkylation outside SPIN.
