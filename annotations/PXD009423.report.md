- Disease 'acute myeloid leukemia' is taken from the project title/description (both cell lines are used in an AML study) rather than per-sample Cellosaurus annotation.
- No fixed modifications, carbamidomethylation or alkylation reagents are stated in the deposited search parameters or PRIDE text, so none are claimed. Precursor 6 ppm is the MaxQuant main-search value from mqpar.xml.
- txt.zip (377,852,379 bytes) was listed by HTTP range on its central directory: contents are txt/ MaxQuant tables (allPeptides, evidence, msms, peptides, proteinGroups, summary, parameters, etc.); no deposited run names inside, so only the 12 top-level RAW files are claimed.
