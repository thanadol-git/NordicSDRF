PRIDE title: 11q deletion or ALK activity curbs DLG2 expression to maintain an undifferentiated state in neuroblastoma

Publication: Siaw JT et al., Cell Reports 2020 32(12):108171, PMID 32966799, DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108171

## Evidence collected

- 20 Orbitrap Fusion RAW files (`Fusion_190123_06.raw`–`25.raw`) and one Proteome Discoverer `.msf` file
- The deposit describes FASP, trypsin digestion, TMT11 labeling, basic reverse-phase fractionation, and a 20-fraction acquisition
- Neither the PRIDE metadata, paper, nor accessible supplementary information gives a reporter-channel-to-cell-line/treatment/replicate map

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

The RAW names identify fractions only. Without the TMT11 reporter map, the neuroblastoma cell lines and experimental conditions cannot be assigned to channels without fabrication.

## What would unblock it

The TMT11 sample sheet for Proteomics Core Facility project 3062, including every reporter channel, cell line, treatment/genotype, replicate, and unused/reference channel.
