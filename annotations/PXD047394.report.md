# PXD047394 annotation report

Bruheim / Otterlei groups (NTNU, Trondheim). E. coli K-12 MG1655 in bioreactors was treated with 12 ng/mL ciprofloxacin or left untreated, and sampled at 1–120 min. The proteins analysed are an ATP/GTP-binding "signalome" captured by multiplexed inhibitor beads (MIB), run as LFQ DDA-PASEF. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 only. No organism-layer template covers bacteria.

## Rows and files
36 rows, one per deposited `.d.zip` (all covered). The mapping is taken verbatim from the deposited `Experimental_Design.csv`: sample letters A/K/X are ciprofloxacin and B/J/W are control, with biological replicates 1–3 and digits for time points. Each source name is arm × biological replicate × time point.

## Values
- **Organism:** `Escherichia coli str. K-12 substr. MG1655` (NCBITaxon:511145). Material type is `whole organism`. Organism part, cell type and disease are `not applicable`.
- **Treatment:** compound `ciprofloxacin`, dose `0.012 ug/mL` (= 12 ng/mL, written in a unit the column accepts), and `sampling time` in minutes. Factors are compound and sampling time.
- **Instrument:** timsTOF Pro 2, confirmed from the `analysis.tdf` InstrumentName in three batches (serial 1875087.10477). Dissociation is CID (DDA-PASEF).
- **Modifications:** Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidated NQ variable (Methods). The paper says "deamination (NQ)"; this is read as deamidation, the MaxQuant default.
- **Not stated:** reduction/alkylation reagents (MIB on-column digestion) and search tolerances. These columns are `not available` or omitted.

## Caveats
The MIB pull-down is not captured as an `enrichment process`, because no PRIDE/EFO child of EFO:0009090 fits a kinase-inhibitor-bead capture. It is described here instead.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf` (ms-proteomics, OLS cache) passes clean. `tools reconcile` reports no disagreements.
