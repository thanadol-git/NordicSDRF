## Design (from Data Brief Methods + Fig. S2 legend)

Leaves of untreated control (C) and MeJA-treated (T, 24 h after treatment) plants; three biological
replicates per group (C1–C3, T1–T3) × three technical replicates (Rep1–3), SWATH-MS on a TripleTOF 5600
(60 MS/MS windows of 12.5 Da over 350–1500 Da). IDA runs were acquired for the
ProteinPilot spectral library.

| Files | Rows | Mapping |
|---|---:|---|
| `Polygonum_{C,T}{1-3}Rep{1-3}_SWATH.wiff.zip` | 18 | source = biological replicate; technical replicate = Rep 1–3; DIA |
| `Polygonum_T{1-3}Rep{n}_IDA.wiff.zip` (T3Rep1 IDA not deposited) | 8 | same sources; technical replicate = Rep + 3 (4–6) so they do not collide with the SWATH injections of the same digest; DDA |
| `Polygonum_{Control,Treated}_IDA_Inj{1-3}` | **0 (excluded)** | which biological replicate (or pool) was injected is not stated; `characteristics[biological replicate]` forbids both reserved words, so these 6 library runs cannot be annotated without guessing |

Deposited 32 raw → 26 annotated + 6 excluded (above). T3 Rep1 (SWATH) was flagged an outlier by the
authors but is a deposited run and is annotated.
