# PXD017971 — annotation blocked

*Pseudomonas aeruginosa* PA14 wild type vs ΔcbpD (LPMO deletion) proteomes, plus spleen proteomes from infected and mock-infected mice. NMBU, Ås. Paper: Nat Commun 2021, PMC7902821 (open access). PXD021888 is the companion deposit.

## What is known
- **Bacterial runs:** 18 files (`190201_<group>-<rep>p`, groups 1, 2, 6, 7, 11 and 12 × 3 replicates). These are cell-pellet proteomes. The paper describes WT and ΔCbpD grown in LB, RPMI/HSA, and RPMI/HSA + 1% human serum, in triplicate.
- **Mouse runs:** 20 files (`190528_FA_1–20`). These are spleens of mice infected with WT or ΔcbpD, or mock-infected (paper Fig. 4).
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.6.3.3; Q Exactive; gel-slice in-gel digestion.

## Why it is blocked
- **Bacterial groups:** the numeric group codes (1, 2, 6, 7, 11, 12) are not defined anywhere in the deposit or the paper. Strain × medium cannot be assigned.
- **Mouse groups:** `FA_1–20` are not mapped to infection group or mouse.

## What would unblock it
The code key for `<group>-<rep>` (strain and medium) and for `FA_1–20` (infection group and mouse).
