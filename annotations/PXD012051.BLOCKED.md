# PXD012051 — annotation blocked

Plasma proteome of Atlantic salmon fed fishmeal (FM, D1), soybean meal (SBM, D2), SBM + 20% *Candida utilis* (SBM200CU, D6) or FM + 20% *C. utilis* (FM200CU, D7) for 30 days. NMBU, Ås. Paper: PLoS One 2019, PMC6936787 (open access).

## What is known
- **Design** (paper): 4 diets × 4 biological replicates (fish) = 16 plasma samples. In-solution trypsin digestion; Q Exactive.
- **Deposited files:** only 16 peak lists (`CJK_161122_Overland_Margarethe_01–16.mgf`, filed under RAW) and a Scaffold file (`171030_LagosLeidy.sf3`). No vendor raw files.

## Why it is blocked
- **No sample key:** nothing maps the sample numbers 01–16 to diet or fish. The paper only reports results per diet, and the Scaffold `.sf3` is a 268 MB proprietary binary.
- **No raw data:** with only MGF peak lists deposited, the dataset is also a poor candidate for reanalysis-oriented SDRF collections.

## What would unblock it
A sample sheet mapping `01–16` to diet (D1/D2/D6/D7) and fish ID, ideally together with the original `.raw` files.
