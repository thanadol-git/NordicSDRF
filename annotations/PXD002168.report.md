## Design and file mapping
The deposited MaxQuant search (`MaxQuantOutput.tar.gz`: `summary.txt`, `parameters.txt`, `proteinGroups.txt`, MaxQuant 1.3.0.5) contains exactly 12 RAW files in two experiments, `NormExcess` and `NormMinus`. Its settings are multiplicity 2, with Labels0 = light and Labels1 = Lys8.

| Source name | Label | Biotin in medium | Biol. rep | RAW files (fractions 1-6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXP1 normal biotin light | SILAC light | 2 ug/L (0.002 ug/mL) | 1 | `20121012_QE6_LC4_CTM_SA_Yeast_Proteome_NormExcess_{5,10,20,40,50,100}.raw` |
| EXP1 excess biotin heavy | SILAC heavy | 200 ug/L (0.2 ug/mL) | 1 | same 6 files |
| EXP2 normal biotin light | SILAC light | 2 ug/L (0.002 ug/mL) | 2 | `20120918_QE6_LC4_CTM_SA_proteome_bio_SCX_NormMinus_{5,10,20,40,50,100}.raw` |
| EXP2 biotin depleted heavy | SILAC heavy | none added (0 ug/mL) | 1 | same 6 files |

How the label-to-condition map was established:
- **Deposited table.** Supplementary Data 2 (`ncomms8726-s3.xls`, sheet `proteinGroups`) has the columns `Ratio H/L normalized Excess biotin Exp.1` and `Ratio H/L normalized depletion biotin Ex[p.2]`. So heavy = excess biotin (EXP.1) and heavy = biotin depletion (EXP.2), and light = normal biotin in both.
- **Cross-check from the deposited proteinGroups.** In experiment `NormMinus`, the biotin-metabolism proteins that the paper reports as upregulated on starvation have high H/L ratios: Bio2 (YGR286C) 9.93, Bio3 (YNR058W) 9.33, Bio4 (YNR057C) 8.56, Bio5 (YNR056C) 4.29, Vht1 (YGR065C) 11.6. This confirms heavy = depleted. `NormExcess` is mapped to EXP.1 (excess) by its experiment name and by the Supplementary Data 2 column.
- **Media concentrations** come from Methods: 2 ug/L biotin is normal and 200 ug/L is excess. Depletion is biotin-free YNB with no supplement, after two sequential overnight dilutions.
- **Fractions.** Fraction order is the numeric file suffix (5, 10, 20, 40, 50, 100 -> fractions 1-6). All six suffixes appear in both experiments.
- **Original filenames.** RAW headers were HTTP range-read for all 47 files. The six `NormMinus` files were acquired as `20120918_QE6_LC4_CTM_SA_proteome_bio_SCX_<n>.raw`; the deposit only inserted `NormMinus`, and the suffix (fraction) was preserved. The other 41 files keep their acquisition names. There are no swaps.
- **Biological replicates.** Each SILAC channel in each experiment is one independent culture. Within the "normal biotin" condition the two control cultures (EXP.1 and EXP.2) are numbered 1 and 2.

### RAW files left out (35 acetylome runs)
These are anti-acetyl-lysine enrichments with SCX microtip fractions:
- `20120814_QE5_LC2_CTM_SA_Yeast_BioAcetyl_SILAC_NormNothing_{40,45,50,60,84}` (5 files)
- `20130322_QE6_LC5_CTM_SA_Acetylome_H4_*` (6 files)
- `20130621_qe1_uplc7_ctm_sa_silac_ac_b_*` (6 files)
- `20130621_qe1_uplc7_ctm_sa_silac_ac_h4_*` (6 files)
- `20130712_QE6_UPLC5_CTM_SA_acetyl_WT_*` (6 files)
- `20130712_QE6_UPLC5_CTM_SA_acetyl_Hst4_*` (6 files)

They are left out because their per-channel sample identity is not described anywhere in the record:
- The deposit contains **no search output** for them; the only MaxQuant output covers the 12 proteome files.
- The paper and Supplementary Data 3/4 report site-level ratios for experiments named `NormHigh`, `NormLow`, `Hst2`, `Hst4` and `Sir2`. Nothing maps these names to RAW files.
- Five acetylome file groups compete for those experiments. Supplementary Figure 2B mentions two replica EXP.2 experiments, which could be label-swapped. There are no hst2Δ or sir2Δ files in the deposit, and the EXP.1 (excess) acetylome files cannot be identified.
- `acetyl_WT` and `acetyl_Hst4` are separate file sets, so it is not even established that every group is a two-channel SILAC mix, or what is in each channel.
- RAW headers carry no Sample ID or name, only vial positions.

Writing these files would require inventing channel identities and strain/biotin conditions. What would unblock them: the MaxQuant `experimentalDesign.txt`/`mqpar.xml` or `summary.txt` for the acetylome searches (file -> experiment map), plus the label orientation per experiment from the authors.

## Technical values
- Instrument: Q Exactive (`MS:1001911`). Confirmed in the RAW header of every group ("Q Exactive - Orbitrap MS"); matches PRIDE and the paper.
- Cleavage: Lys-C (`MS:1001309`). Deposited `summary.txt` gives Protease = Lys-C with max 2 missed cleavages. Methods say "strict Lys-C specificity", and the Lys8-only SILAC design implies Lys-C.
- Modifications, from the deposited search (`sdrf-tools search-params` on `summary.txt` + `parameters.txt`):
  - Carbamidomethyl (C) Fixed
  - Oxidation (M) Variable
  - Acetyl (Protein N-term) Variable
  - Label:13C(6)15N(2) (K) Variable, which is the Lys8 SILAC heavy label
- Fragment tolerance: 20 ppm (deposited `parameters.txt`, FTMS). Precursor tolerance: 7 ppm (paper Methods; not recorded in the deposited `parameters.txt`).
- Acquisition: DDA (`PRIDE:0000627`), per Methods. Dissociation: HCD as `MS:1000422` (Methods: "HCD fragment ion masses").
- Fractionation: SCX (`PRIDE:0000561`).

## Limitations
- **PARTIAL coverage:** 35 of 47 RAW files (all acetylome runs) are not annotated; the reasons are above.
- `comment[fractionation method]` = SCX:
  - NormMinus files: stated in the filenames (`proteome_bio_SCX`).
  - NormExcess files: **inferred**. Their names lack "SCX", but they share the identical six-fraction suffix scheme (5/10/20/40/50/100), LC system (LC4) and instrument (QE6), and were acquired one month later.
  - The paper does not describe proteome fractionation.
- `characteristics[strain or breed]` = BY4742: **inferred**.
  - Methods give BY4742 (MATα his3Δ1 leu2Δ0 lys2Δ0 ura3Δ0) as the parental WT background, and the proteome experiments are WT.
  - The lys2Δ0 auxotrophy is consistent with Lys8 SILAC.
  - The paper does not name the strain per experiment.
  - The PRIDE organism-part entry "diploid cell" conflicts with haploid BY4742, so `characteristics[cell type]` was not used.
- `characteristics[dose]`:
  - The media concentrations (2 and 200 ug/L) come from Methods and were converted to ug/mL to fit the dose unit list.
  - "0 ug/mL" for depletion means no biotin was added to biotin-free YNB. Residual intracellular biotin is not quantified.
- `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` = 7 ppm comes from the paper, which reports MaxQuant 1.2.6.20. The deposited search is MaxQuant 1.3.0.5 (files dated 2015-03-04, FASTA `yeast_orf_mitotrans_20150220`), and its `parameters.txt` does not record the precursor tolerance.
- `characteristics[organism part]` and `characteristics[disease]` = `not applicable` (unicellular yeast culture). The PRIDE "disease free" entry was not carried over.
- Sex and age are not applicable to yeast. PubMed MeSH was checked: *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* and "Organisms, Genetically Modified", with no strain given.
- Biological replicate numbering assumes each SILAC channel is one culture. The paper gives no further replication for the proteome experiments.
- Enzyme specificity was not independently re-verified from peptide C-termini. The deposited search was run with Lys-C specificity, so its peptide list cannot test it.
