# PXD035354 SDRF annotation report

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending

Dataset: Effects of the differential K+ diets on mouse kidney cortex tissue (Aarhus University; Fenton lab). Paper: Little et al., JCI Insight 2023, doi 10.1172/jci.insight.156437. No existing annotation was found in sdrf-annotated-datasets (Step 0.5).

## Output
- 384 rows x 34 columns, templates ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + vertebrates v1.1.0.
- Design: two TMTpro sets, each 8 high-pH fractions acquired twice (technical replicate 1 = plain-name RAW, 2 = timestamp-suffixed RAW, re-run about 24 h later; confirmed from msf WorkflowInputFiles). All 32 RAW files claimed.
  - Set1 (short-term, 4 days): TMT126 Ctrl, TMT127N-129N NS/NK x5, TMT129C-131C NS/HK x5 = 11 channels x 2 x 8 files.
  - Set2 (chronic, 21 days): TMT126 Ctrl, NS/NK x7, NS/HK x5 = 13 channels x 2 x 8 files.
  - Unused channels have no rows (build emitted none).
- Factor: factor value[treatment] (normal vs high potassium diet, with duration).

## Caveats
1. TMT channel to sample map is INFERRED: supplementary xlsx gives per-column group labels only. Set1 corroborated (11 active tags 126-131C in msf quant method = 11 columns); Set2 msf has all 18 tags active, so the first 13 channels (126-132C) in column order is an assumption. Verify before reuse.
2. The "Ctrl" column (normalised to 100 for every protein) occupies TMT126 in each set; whether it is a pooled reference or a control animal is undocumented. Its disease, treatment, sex, strain, stage, pooled sample and factor are `not available`.
3. Cleavage agent Trypsin comes from the deposited PD/Sequest/Mascot settings (Trypsin full, 2 missed cleavages); the PRIDE text and Supp Methods ("digested ... as described") never name the enzyme. FASP digests are normally trypsin; single protease, so one cleavage-agent column (no Lys-C step documented).
4. Sex male, C57BL/6J, adult come from the general Methods (male C57BL/6J, 10-12 weeks); not restated for the proteomics cohort (cohort 2). Disease set to `normal` for animal rows (healthy mice on diets).
5. Biological replicate numbers are within-group indices; whether Set1 and Set2 animals are distinct is inferred (4 d vs 21 d cohorts).
6. DDA, HCD, instrument model (Orbitrap Fusion, from PRIDE and msf) not checked against RAW headers; no ms2 mass analyzer, reduction or alkylation reagent columns given (not stated).
7. Organism part "cortex of kidney" (UBERON:0001225) used for kidney cortex homogenate.
8. PRIDE flags "No PTMs", but search used Acetyl (protein N-term) and Oxidation variable mods per the msf; SDRF follows the msf.
