# PXD043084 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"ZNF524 directly interacts with telomeric DNA and supports telomere integrity". The dataset is the label-free BioID of **MYC-BirA*-ZNF524 WT (bait) vs MYC-BirA*-NLS (control)** in U2OS cells (Supplementary Fig. 3a). Paper: Braun H, Xu Z, ... Butter F, Kappei D. Nat Commun 2023;14:8252, DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-43397-7, PMC10716145, PMID 38086788. The PX record still says "publication pending", but the paper's Data availability lists "PXD024630, PXD043084 and PXD031416", which verifies the link. Sibling dataset from the same paper: PXD024630 (BioID WT vs ZF2 mutant, annotated separately). Submitter: Dennis Kappei, Cancer Science Institute of Singapore.

## Scope, evidence and decisions
The SDRF has 8 rows (8 RAW x label free) and 40 columns. Deposited (PROXI and the PRIDE FTP listing, both re-derived 2026-11-27): 9 files. These are 8 RAW (`20230412_LAB_SZW_U2OS_BirAZnF524WT_BioID_01..04.raw`, `..._BirANLS_BioID_01..04.raw`) and one MaxQuant output zip (`20230412_LAB_SZW_U2OS_BirA_BioIDMS_ZNF524WTvsNLS.zip`, 202 MB). There is no checksum.txt. All 8 RAW files are annotated and none is excluded. Evidence files are in `/home/claude/singapore2/scratch/PXD043084/`.

- **Bait vs control mapping (STATED):** file token `BirAZnF524WT` = bait MYC-BirA*-ZNF524 wild type; `BirANLS` = control MYC-BirA*-NLS. The Results say "we repeated the BioID experiment using MYC-BirA*-NLS as a control". The Supplementary Fig. 3a legend (read from the SI PDF) says "Volcano plot of BioID assay comparing proximity partners of ZNF524 WT versus NLS in U2OS cells ... n=4 with each replicate seeded and induced independently". The zip name `ZNF524WTvsNLS` agrees. Suffix `_01..04` = biological replicate 1-4 within each arm. The n=4 independent replicates are stated; the numbering comes from the file names. Replicate N of WT and of NLS are not claimed to be paired.
- **Not labelled:** label-free LFQ (MaxQuant LFQ, match between runs), so there is no channel map. `comment[label]` = `label free sample`.
- **Induction (STATED, per arm):** Supplementary Fig. 3a and Methods: "BirA*-ZNF524 WT and BirA*-NLS were induced with 300 and 40 ng ml-1 doxycycline, respectively", for 48 h, with 50 uM biotin for 6 h after 42 h of induction. These go in `characteristics[treatment]`. The bait construct goes in `characteristics[genotype]` and `factor value[genotype]`. Using genotype requires declaring clinical-metadata, which adds no required columns. This follows the PXD054300 precedent. Constructs are in pTRIPZ MYC-BirA*, dox-inducible, lentiviral, with puromycin selection (Methods).
- **Cell line:** U2OS, CVCL_0042, checked live on api.cellosaurus.org: female, 15Y, osteosarcoma, "Derived from site: In situ; Bone, tibia; UBERON_0000979". Organism part `tibia` (UBERON:0000979) and disease `osteosarcoma` (MONDO:0009807) were both checked on Ontobee: not deprecated. Age and sex are Cellosaurus donor values. `cell type` = not available, because neither the paper nor Cellosaurus names one.
- **Culture medium (STATED):** DMEM 4.5 g/L glucose, 10% FBS, 2 mM glutamine, penicillin/streptomycin, 37 C / 5% CO2.
- **Enrichment:** nuclear extract, MyOne Streptavidin C1 beads, Laemmli elution. This is free text in `characteristics[enrichment process]`, which gives a validator warning only.
- **Prep (STATED):** in-gel digestion (4-12% Bis-Tris), DTT 56 C, IAA, trypsin (Promega), C18 StageTips. One RAW per sample, so `fraction identifier` = 1. The gel lane is cut into pieces but digested and measured as one sample, so no fractionation method is given.
- **MS (STATED):** EASY-nLC 1200, 25 cm C18, 2 h 2-40% ACN gradient, **Q Exactive HF** (MS:1002523, checked on Ontobee). The PX record and the paper agree. DDA, MS1 60k, up to 20 **HCD** MS/MS at 15k.
- **Search (STATED, Methods):** "for BioID ZNF524 WT vs NLS: version 2.0.1.0" (MaxQuant). Human UniProt UP000005640. Trypsin, up to 2 missed cleavages. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and protein N-terminal acetylation variable (`PP=Protein N-term`). FDR 0.01.

## Values left not available / omitted, and why
- `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` / `comment[fragment mass tolerance]` = not available. The paper gives no tolerances, and the deposited MaxQuant zip could not be opened, so MaxQuant defaults were not asserted.
- `characteristics[cell type]` = not available (see above).
- Unstated, so omitted: collision energy (NCE), MS1 scan range, passage number.

## Stated vs INFERRED; conflicts
- Every run→condition assignment is STATED by the file-name tokens together with the Supplementary Fig. 3a legend (n=4 per arm). Nothing material is inferred.
- The search modifications come from the paper's Methods, not from the deposited search output. The zip (202 MB) could not be opened through the browser or the shell. The PX `modifications` field ("No PTMs are included") is the usual default and was overridden by the Methods.
- Not checked live: PRIDE/PSI-MS/UNIMOD accessions other than MS:1002523 (OLS4 returned upstream errors and was rate-limited). Their labels match this campaign's validated files.
