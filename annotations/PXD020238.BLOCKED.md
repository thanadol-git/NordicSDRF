# PXD020238 — annotation blocked

## Reason

The deposit contains 44 RAW files from an MDCK cell-line study. The authors
provide two metadata tables, but they do not map every deposited FASP/run code.

- `metadata_notNaBut.txt` maps D6, E2, E3, and E6.
- Deposited RAW files also use E4, E5, E7, and E8, with no corresponding
  sample/condition records.
- `metadata_NaBut.txt` maps the separate 190403 runs (12_1 through 15_1),
  but cannot resolve the unmapped E-series files.

Creating an SDRF for only the mapped runs would silently omit 16 RAW files;
inferring their conditions from plate positions would fabricate a biological
mapping. A complete sample sheet or corrected metadata table is required.

## Provenance

- PRIDE project: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD020238
- Deposited metadata: https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2020/11/PXD020238/metadata_notNaBut.txt
