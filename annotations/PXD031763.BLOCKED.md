# PXD031763 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The deposited injection sheet maps RAW files to TMT set, phospho/total material, and fraction, but it does not map reporter channels to cell type, STK25-silencing/control condition, or biological replicate. Those channel identities cannot be recovered from acquisition order.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE project record: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD031763`
- PRIDE file inventory: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD031763/files/all`
- Publication: `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9018782/` (DOI `10.1038/s42003-022-03309-9`)
- Deposited `MS_injections.xlsx`

PRIDE lists 60 Orbitrap Fusion RAW files and declares TMT quantification. `MS_injections.xlsx` contains exact entries such as `4065 Set1 Phospho fr 01` and corresponding set/fraction series, including phosphopeptide and total-proteome acquisitions. The publication describes human aortic endothelial and smooth-muscle cell experiments, but the checked sources do not provide a reporter-by-set table connecting those biological conditions and replicates to the acquired multiplexes.

## Evidence needed to unblock

The TMT labelling design for every set, with columns for reporter, exact cell type, silencing/control treatment, biological replicate, reference pool or empty channel, and a join key matching the set names in `MS_injections.xlsx`.
