# PXD025319 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE declares TMT quantification and provides 24 vendor RAW files named only `J_01.raw` through `J_24.raw`, plus peptide/protein result tables.  The linked open-access eLife paper (PMID 35587260 / PMCID PMC9119677) identifies PXD025319 as the proteomics repository and describes the biology, but its publicly readable methods/data-availability text does not map those RAW aliases or reporter channels to genotype, tissue, stimulation, or replicate.
- **Why blocked:** The opaque `J_<number>` filenames contain no biological identity.  A complete channel-aware SDRF would require a reporter-channel/sample layout and its association with each raw-file set; assigning the article's mouse conditions to those aliases would be fabricated.
- **Unblocking requirement:** The original TMT experimental-design table or an author-confirmed crosswalk from the `J_01`–`J_24` RAW files and reporter channels to samples and conditions.
