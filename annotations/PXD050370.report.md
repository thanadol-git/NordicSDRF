# PXD050370 annotation report

Status: blocked after independent review. The draft SDRF is unapproved and must not be used as an established sample map.

Four RAW files comprise two Khudji tooth dentine subsamples and two extraction blanks. The deposited [metadata_hominin.txt](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2025/04/PXD050370/metadata_hominin.txt) assigns no cleaning to the June pair and bleach to the October pair. For the bleach pair it marks `Blank_P0581.raw` as Homo/khudji_bleach and `P0582.raw` as NA/blank. The deposited [MaxQuant bleach summary](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2025/04/PXD050370/khudji_bleach.zip) assigns the opposite identities: `Blank_P0581` to `Blank` and `P0582` to `Khudji1`. The deposited [PEAKS bleach reconstruction](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2025/04/PXD050370/bleach_PEAKS.zip) identifies its source file as `Blank_P0581.raw` and reports hominin collagen peptides. These accession-specific sources do not establish which October RAW is the tooth and which is the extraction blank. The June pair is unambiguous.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD050370) and [file list](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD050370/files/all). The `ms-proteomics` and `vertebrates` draft passes `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf`, structure, reconciliation and offline ontology checks, but independent review failed on the unresolved biological identity conflict. No approval receipt was issued. A curator-provided RAW-to-sample/blank mapping, including an explanation of the conflicting processed outputs, is needed before this accession can be approved.
