## Dataset

- Title: *Fingolimod and remyelination - Fingolimod downregulates brain sphingosine-1-phosphate receptor 1 levels but does not promote remyelination or neuroprotection in the cuprizone model*.
- Lab: Frode S. Berven (PI), Proteomics Unit at the University of Bergen (PROBE), Department of Biomedicine, Bergen, Norway. Submitter: Eystein Oveland.
- Paper: Nystad AE, Lereim RR, Wergeland S, Oveland E, Myhr KM, Bø L, Torkildsen Ø. *J Neuroimmunol* 2020;339:577091. PMID 31739156, DOI 10.1016/j.jneuroim.2019.577091. The journal version is closed (Elsevier; Europe PMC has no OA copy). The **full text, Supplementary methods and Table S2** are reproduced as Paper IV in the first author's open PhD thesis: Nystad AE, *Effects of calcitriol and fingolimod on remyelination in the cuprizone model*, University of Bergen, BORA handle [1956/22329](https://hdl.handle.net/1956/22329) (NVA registration 0198f2735c27-a6b6b0a4-1316-472e-a709-0323051e44ab, open file `archive.pdf`, 12,742,789 bytes, SHA-256 `c19c753b3e2cf8937205e1e4c3cedad15459b8bbfd8513cdc982625e7588e0db`). The PDF was fetched through the NVA `filelink` endpoint, and its text was extracted with `pdftotext` and searched for specific facts (`scratchpad/PXD012676/thesis/`).
- Design (thesis Paper IV §2.1, §2.2, §2.6, Table S2): 48 female, five-week-old c57Bl/6 mice from Taconic (Tornbjerg, Denmark) were acclimatised for 12 days. They were then randomised into healthy controls (n=6), cuprizone controls (n=6), cuprizone + fingolimod (n=18) and cuprizone + placebo (n=18). **All mice in the fingolimod and placebo arms (the only arms in the proteomics experiment) received 0.2% cuprizone in milled chow for six weeks**, followed by normal chow. Fingolimod (1 mg/kg in water) or placebo (water) was given by daily oral gavage from week 5. Mice were sampled at six weeks (DM, maximal demyelination) and after one (1RM) and three (3RM) weeks of remyelination. Frontal right hemispheres were lysed individually. For each condition, the n=6 lysates were randomised into 3 mini-pools of 2 mice each, giving 18 pools: 2 treatments × 3 time points × 3 pools (A, B, C). The pools were labelled with TMT10plex in two plexes of 9 pools each. Channel 131 of each plex holds a reference pool containing equal amounts of all 36 brain lysates. Each plex was fractionated by mixed-mode reversed-phase chromatography into 60 fractions, injected as 56 runs: fractions 1–3 and 58–60 were pooled into one injection each. The runs were acquired on a Q Exactive HF (HCD, NCE 32%) and searched in Proteome Discoverer 2.0 (Sequest HT and MS Amanda).

- How the mapping was established:
  1. **Channel → PD sample code**: from the column headers of the deposited `Fingolimod_TMT_Proteins.xlsx` (`Abundances (Scaled): F<n>: <channel>, Sample, <code>`).
  2. **Plex → file series**: from the PD StudyDefinition embedded in `Fingolimod_TMT.pdResult`. I range-read the first 4 MB only. FileSet `20151105_R1` (SampleFileSetNumber 1 = F1) contains the R files, and its samples (`20151105_R1 - [126]` = C6C, and so on) match the xlsx F1 codes. The G FileSet carries the F2 codes. This is consistent with the reference names REFR/REFG.
  3. **Code → treatment**: from the depositors' decoding row in the same xlsx sheet, which relabels every `C<t><x>` as `F<t><x>` (fingolimod) and every `F<t><x>` as `P<t><x>` (placebo). This was cross-checked against the data: S1PR1 (O08530) scaled abundance is 55–98 in all nine C pools and 107–140 in all nine F pools, matching the paper's main finding that fingolimod downregulates brain S1PR1.
  4. **Code digit → time point**: 6/7/9 → 6DM/1RM/3RM, i.e. 6, 7 and 9 weeks after the start of cuprizone. The thesis text (six weeks of cuprizone, then one or three weeks of remyelination) supports this. **Thesis Table S2 confirms both the time-point and the treatment decoding independently.** Its channel layout, read by row and condition, is: fingolimod DM/1RM/3RM in 126/127N/128N, then 127C/128C/129C, then 126/127C/127N; placebo DM/1RM/3RM in 129N/130C/130N, then 128C/129C/130C, then 128N/129N/130N; reference in 131. This reproduces the G-plex pools A, the B pools (G-plex fingolimod, R-plex placebo) and the R-plex pools C exactly, with code digit 6 = DM, 7 = 1RM, 9 = 3RM and C = fingolimod.
  5. **Original names**: RAW headers (range reads of R4, G4, G7_test) show the acquisition paths `...\2015-07-20_QE-HF_25cm_CPZ_FINGO_TMT\20151105_R4.raw` etc., identical to the deposited names, so no files were renamed.

## Limitations

- **Cuprizone is not a separate column.** `characteristics[treatment]` records the experimental arm (fingolimod / placebo). The shared 0.2% cuprizone exposure (6 weeks) is captured by `characteristics[disease]` = `demyelinating disease of central nervous system` (MONDO:0020800) and documented here and in the manifest. Fingolimod dose: 1 mg/kg, oral gavage, daily from week 5.
- `characteristics[sampling time]` 6/7/9 week is measured from the start of cuprizone exposure. It is derived from the thesis description (six weeks of cuprizone, then 1 or 3 weeks of remyelination) together with the Table S2 channel layout.
- `characteristics[developmental stage]` = `adult` is inferred from the reported age on arrival (five weeks) plus the 12-day acclimatisation and 6–9 weeks of experiment. Exact age at sampling is not stated.
- `characteristics[strain or breed]` = `C57BL/6` (thesis: "c57Bl/6", Taconic). The Taconic substrain (e.g. C57BL/6NTac) is not stated.
- Pool composition: Table S2 states that each mini-pool holds 2 biological samples (n=6 per condition), but not which mice. Pools are disjoint by construction ("randomized and divided into 3 mini-pools"). One mouse died during the study (47 analysed); Table S2 still lists 36 lysates in the reference, and which group lost the mouse is not stated in the proteomics section.
- Reference pool: modelled as one source (`reference_pool`) measured once per plex (technical replicates 1 and 2). Table S2 calls it "one reference pool … included in each TMT 10-plex", and §2.6 calls them "two identical reference samples". Treatment and sampling time are `not applicable` because the pool mixes both arms and all time points; `pooled` is not permitted in those columns.
- `characteristics[organism part]` = `cerebral hemisphere`, from "frontal right hemisphere"; the exact dissected region is not further specified.
- `characteristics[disease]`: the PRIDE disease "Multiple sclerosis" was not used, because these are cuprizone-treated mice.
- `characteristics[cell type]` = `not applicable` (whole-tissue lysate).
- Fraction identifiers 1 and 58 each stand for three pooled fractions (1–3 and 58–60). The paper says "58 fractions each 10 plex", while the deposit has 56 injections covering fractions 1–60.
- The cleavage agent could not be verified from peptide C-termini, because the pdResult is 31.7 GB and only a protein-level table is deposited.
- `comment[alkylation reagent]` is omitted (no alkylation step stated; carbamidomethylation was searched). The collision energy (NCE 32%) is reported in the Supplementary methods but is not added as a column in this repair.
