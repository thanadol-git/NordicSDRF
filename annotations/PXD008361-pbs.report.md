## Design and file mapping

Six AML patient samples (P1–P6) from the Bergen AML biobank were thawed and split into aliquots. Each aliquot was left unwashed or washed once or twice with PBS before SDS lysis. P1 and P4 have no second-wash aliquot: Table S1 shows "-" for their 2nd PBS wash, and no `PBS_P1_2xPBS` or `PBS_P4_2xPBS` file exists. A QC pool of all samples was injected three times.

| Source name | individual / biological replicate | treatment (factor) | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| AML patient P1 0x / 1x PBS | P1 / 1 | no PBS wash / 1 PBS wash | `PBS_P1_0xPBS`, `PBS_P1_1xPBS` |
| AML patient P2 0x / 1x / 2x PBS | P2 / 2 | no PBS wash / 1 PBS wash / 2 PBS washes | `PBS_P2_{0,1,2}xPBS` |
| AML patient P3 0x / 1x / 2x PBS | P3 / 3 | no PBS wash / 1 PBS wash / 2 PBS washes | `PBS_P3_{0,1,2}xPBS` |
| AML patient P4 0x / 1x PBS | P4 / 4 | no PBS wash / 1 PBS wash | `PBS_P4_0xPBS`, `PBS_P4_1xPBS` |
| AML patient P5 0x / 1x / 2x PBS | P5 / 5 | no PBS wash / 1 PBS wash / 2 PBS washes | `PBS_P5_{0,1,2}xPBS` |
| AML patient P6 0x / 1x / 2x PBS | P6 / 6 | no PBS wash / 1 PBS wash / 2 PBS washes | `PBS_P6_{0,1,2}xPBS` |
| AML patient QC pool | pooled / pooled | not applicable | `PBS_QC1..3` (technical replicates 1–3) |

- **How the mapping was established.** The deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` assigns each raw file to an experiment with the same name (`P1_0xPBS` … `P6_2xPBS`, `QC1`–`QC3`). The patient labels P1–P6 and the 0/1/2-wash design come from paper section 4.1, Figure 1a and Table S1. The QC composition comes from the PRIDE protocol ("A pool containing equal amounts of all the samples … analyzed three times") and from the Figure S1 caption ("QC1-3 are pools of P1–P6 samples").
- **Factor.** `factor value[treatment]` mirrors `characteristics[treatment]` (EFO:0000727): `no PBS wash`, `1 PBS wash` or `2 PBS washes`. The QC rows are `not applicable`, because the pool mixes aliquots from all three wash levels and the contract does not allow `pooled` for this column. NCIT and EFO have no term for a PBS wash count (OLS was searched for "PBS wash", "cell washing" and "wash"), so the values are free text.
- **Biological replicate** is the patient number (P1 = 1 … P6 = 6) in every wash group. Within one condition the six (or four) patients are independent samples, and numbering by patient keeps the pairing across washes. The QC rows are `pooled`.
- **Sample context.** Before this experiment, all patient cells had been cryopreserved in liquid nitrogen in 10% DMSO / 20% FBS (paper 4.1 and the PRIDE protocol). This is the same for every row, so it is not encoded as a column.
- **Row count.** 19 files × 1 row = 19 rows.

### Revision 2 (after independent review)

The independent review failed revision 1 with two important findings. I checked both against the evidence and accepted both.

1. **Biological replicate.** It was 1 on every patient row, although each wash group contains different patients. It is now the patient number (P1 = 1 … P6 = 6).
2. **Factor column.** `characteristics[number of pbs washes]` / `factor value[number of pbs washes]` is not an ontology-backed column name. It was replaced by `characteristics[treatment]` / `factor value[treatment]`, with values `no PBS wash`, `1 PBS wash` and `2 PBS washes`. The QC rows are `not applicable`, because the contract does not allow `pooled` for treatment.

- **`pooled` for QC age and sex:** rejected. The validator gave the errors "Value 'pooled' does not match required pattern" and "Invalid value 'pooled' - must be one of the allowed values", so these stay `not available`.

## Limitations

- **Demographics.** Age and sex are `not available`, and ancestry is omitted. Neither the paper nor Table S1 reports per-patient demographics.
- **Cell type.** `peripheral blood mononuclear cell` describes the density-gradient fraction, which the paper states is at least 95% leukemic blasts. No more specific CL term for AML blasts was used.
- **Organism part (inferred per patient).** `blood` follows the paper's general description of the biobank samples ("subjects with high peripheral blood blast counts"). The paper does not state the origin per patient.
- **QC pool.** It is encoded as one pooled source with technical replicates 1–3. Whether there was one mix or three separate mixes is not stated.
- **P2 second wash.** Table S1 gives P2's protein concentration after the 2nd wash as -0.01 µg/µL (below BCA detection), but `PBS_P2_2xPBS.raw` exists and is in the search. It is annotated as a normal 2-wash sample.
- **Not recorded.** Table S1 also gives the red-blood-cell contamination grade and the cell count per patient. These are not encoded.
- **Reagents.** Reduction and alkylation reagents for FASP are not named, so those columns are omitted. Carbamidomethyl is still searched as fixed.
- **Precursor mass tolerance** is not available (MaxQuant 1.5 `parameters.txt`).
- **Instrument settings** were read from 2 of the 19 raw headers. The other files are assumed to be identical: they share the acquisition folder and the MaxQuant search.
- **Treatment values.** `characteristics[treatment]` uses free-text values. No NCIT or EFO term exists for a PBS wash count, so the validator reports 3 ontology warnings.
- **QC treatment.** The QC rows' treatment is `not applicable`, because the pool mixes all three wash levels.
- **QC age, sex and pooled sample.** Age and sex stay `not available`, because the validator rejects `pooled` for them. `pooled sample` stays `pooled`, because an `SN=` list raises a warning.
- **Cryopreservation.** All patient cells were cryopreserved in DMSO/FBS before the wash experiment. This is recorded in the manifest, not as a column.
