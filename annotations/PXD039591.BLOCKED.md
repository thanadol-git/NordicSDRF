# PXD039591 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE identifies a human tumour-derived extracellular-vesicle proteomics study of TGF-β signalling and deposits 18 RAW files named only `sample1` through `sample18`. The deposited multiconsensus workbook contains quantitative result columns `A2` through `R2`.
- **Why blocked:** Neither the RAW filenames nor the result-workbook column labels map acquisitions to cell source, TGF-β condition, biological replicate, or experimental group. The workbook is a result table rather than a sample manifest, so assigning its columns to the stated study design would fabricate source-to-assay relationships.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A deposited experimental-design/run sheet mapping all 18 RAW files (or the A2–R2 result columns) to sample identity, TGF-β condition, and replicate.
