# PXD005442 annotation report

Proteome of outer membrane vesicles (OMVs) released by *Fibrobacter succinogenes* S85 growing on Avicel cellulose. NMBU, Ås. Paper: Environ Microbiol 2017, PMID 28447389 (not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 20 rows. The PRIDE protocol says OMV proteins were separated by SDS-PAGE "in two biological replicates". The deposited MaxQuant design gives:
- `Exp38`: 12 gel slices (`P1–P12`), biological replicate 1.
- `Exp98`: 8 gel slices (`_1_1–_1_8`), biological replicate 2.

Gel-slice numbers are used as fraction identifiers.

The 2D hrCNE/SDS-PAGE gel-spot analyses (Mascot) mentioned in the record are not part of these raw files.

## Values
- **Sample:** strain `S85 (ATCC 19169)`, material `cell`, cellular component `outer membrane vesicle`, growth substrate Avicel.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.4.1.2):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ and Gln→pyro-Glu variable.
  - FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags trypsin as not named; the PRIDE record does not describe digestion at all. Trypsin is the enzyme used throughout this lab's MaxQuant workflow and in all its sister deposits, but this deposit's summary does not state it, so it is an inference.
