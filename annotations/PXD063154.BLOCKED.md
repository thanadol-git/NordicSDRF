# PXD063154 — annotation blocked

Synaptosomes from ALS patient-derived motor-neuron networks ± tetraethylammonium (TEA). Department of Neuromedicine and Movement Science, NTNU; MS by PROMEC. Paper: Neurobiology of Disease 2025 (PMID 40945542), not open access.

## Why it is blocked
- **Missing key:** the 17 deposited runs are named `synaptosome_1`…`17`, and the deposited `proteinGroups.txt` uses the same numbers. The design (healthy vs ALS lines, TEA-treated vs untreated, time points, replicate lines) cannot be mapped to the numbers from any open source.
- **Extra run in the search:** `proteinGroups.txt` also contains a run `16_Slot2-16_1_8015` that is not deposited.
- **Instrument:** timsTOF Pro 2 (`analysis.tdf`). PRIDE says timsTOF Pro.

## What would unblock it
The paper's Methods or a sample sheet mapping 1–17 to donor line, disease status and treatment.
