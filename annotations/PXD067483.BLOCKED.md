# PXD067483 — BLOCKED

Checked: 2026-09-26

## Evidence

- ProteomeXchange record: https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/cgi/GetDataset?ID=PXD067483
  - Human HT-1080 fibrosarcoma cells exposed to NGA, NGA-Fe3+, NGA-Cu2+, elesclomol, and RSL3; Orbitrap Exploris 480.
- Linked publication: https://doi.org/10.1186/s12964-025-02558-5
  - Describes the elesclomol arm and confirms deposition under PXD067483.

## Blocker

The web-visible project record does not enumerate the complete raw-file list or map runs to the six treatment/control groups and replicates. The linked publication covers the elesclomol study but does not establish run-level assignments for the graphene-derivative and RSL3 arms named in the deposit. A complete mapping for the accession therefore cannot be supported.

No SDRF was written or validated.
