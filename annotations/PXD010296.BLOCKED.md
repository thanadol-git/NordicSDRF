# PXD010296 — blocked: no complete mixed-material workflow/run map

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD010296
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD010296/files/all
- Linked PubMed record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30462917/ (DOI: 10.1021/acs.jproteome.8b00792)

## Evidence available

PRIDE describes automated preparation workflows for rat spleen, human melanoma, human pancreatic tumour, and pancreatic tumour xenografts on a Q Exactive. The 104 RAW filenames explicitly identify some spleen and melanoma workflows, Barocycler/Bravo processing, reverse-phase fractions, and reruns.

## Blocking gap

The linked Journal of Proteome Research paper has no public PMC full text or supplementary run sheet. PRIDE does not provide a complete mapping for all files, particularly the pancreatic and xenograft material, to source species/tissue, individual, workflow/platform, fraction, and replicate. Individual filename hints cannot safely reconstruct the mixed-material design.

## Unblock requirement

Provide the master sample/run sheet assigning every RAW file to material/species, preparation technology, fraction, and biological/technical replicate.
