# PXD027187 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

No checked source provides the multiplex channel-to-sample mapping for the 80 RAW files. In addition, the PRIDE structured record declares iTRAQ while deposited result filenames explicitly refer to `TMT10plex` and `TMTpro`; the exact label chemistry cannot be resolved safely from metadata alone.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE project record: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD027187`
- PRIDE file inventory: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD027187/files/all`
- Publication: `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8613282/` (DOI `10.1038/s41467-021-27057-2`)
- Deposited RAW and result-file names

PRIDE lists 80 generically numbered RAW files and labels the quantification method as iTRAQ. The only non-RAW deposited artifacts are multi-gigabyte `.msf`/`.pdResult` files; their names include `TMT10plex` and `TMTpro`, contradicting the structured quantification field and distinguishing more than one search/acquisition grouping. The publication supplies biological context for ALK-driven neuroblastoma and ATR inhibition but does not expose an exact accession-level set/reporter/sample matrix that joins to these RAW basenames.

## Evidence needed to unblock

The original multiplex design table for every acquisition group, including label chemistry, set identifier, reporter, exact biological sample or pool, treatment, time point, biological replicate, and RAW-file range. It must also resolve the iTRAQ-versus-TMT discrepancy in the deposit record.
