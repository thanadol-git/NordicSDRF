# PXD069681 — annotation blocked

Somatic-cell proteomes of bovine quarter milk samples grouped by the bacterial species detected (S. aureus, NASM, streptococci, corynebacteria, …). NMBU, Ås. Paper: Vet Res 2026, PMC13181906 (open access).

## What is known
- **Runs:** 56 timsTOF `.d.zip` runs named by sample ID (`H042` … `H420`). The deposited MaxQuant 2.6.6.0 experiments use the same IDs.
- **Search:** Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Why it is blocked
The sample → cow, quarter, sampling time and pathogen-group key is not in the deposit or the paper text. The pathogen groups come from 16S data in a previous study.

## What would unblock it
A table mapping each `H###` to cow, quarter, lactation stage, SCC and pathogen group.
