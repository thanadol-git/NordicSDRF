# PXD013611 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"PRMT5 methylome profiling uncovers a direct link to splicing regulation in human acute myeloid leukemia" (Radzisheuskaya et al., Nat Struct Mol Biol 2019, PMID 31611688, PMC6858565). Submitter Pavel Shliaha, SDU (PI Ole N. Jensen). THP-1-cdCas9-KRAB AML cells transduced with a non-targeting sgRNA (4 replicates) or one of two PRMT5 sgRNAs (3 replicates each); lysis, LysC/trypsin digestion, TMT10plex labelling, one pooled 10-plex sample analysed on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos (MS3 multinotch SPS TMT method) after (a) high-pH RP hyperfractionation on three second-dimension LC systems (96 / 30 / 15 fractions) and (b) PTMScan MMA/SDMA immunoaffinity enrichment.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + human v1.1.0 + cell-lines v1.1.0. No clinical/oncology template (cell line only, no patient data).

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
PRIDE file list read over all pages (page 0 = 100, page 1 = 51, page 2 empty; 151 files). 148 RAW files, all annotated; non-RAW files not annotated: LUM_019.pep.xml and LUM_019.pdResult (search results), human_rev_can_uniprot_2017_11_06.fasta. Each RAW is one injection of the same pooled TMT10 sample, so 148 runs x 10 channels = 1480 rows, 50 columns.

## Key decisions
- One source per channel (sgNegCtrl_1-4, sgPRMT5-1_1-3, sgPRMT5-2_1-3); factor value[genotype] = sgRNA group (the paper's compared variable). characteristics[genotype] adds the THP-1-cdCas9-KRAB background.
- THP-1: Cellosaurus CVCL_0006 (`tools cellline lookup`: exact match, 1Y male). Disease written as "acute myeloid leukemia" from the paper (MLL-AF9-rearranged AML) rather than the curated subset's "childhood acute megakaryoblastic leukemia", which contradicts the paper. organism part blood (Cellosaurus tissue).
- Cleavage: Trypsin (MS:1001251) and Lys-C (MS:1001309) as two repeated comment[cleavage agent details] columns (the second was added by a deterministic column-duplication step after `build`, because build emits only one column for this field).
- Modifications from the deposited processing protocol (Mascot): fixed Carbamidomethyl C, TMT6plex K and N-term; variable Oxidation M, Deamidated N/Q, Dimethyl R, Methyl R. Tolerances 10 ppm / 0.6 Da. Reduction TCEP, alkylation chloroacetamide (from sample protocol).
- Dissociation CID, 35% NCE, ion-trap MS2 (supplementary methods and RAW method summaries). The quantified MS3 step is HCD 65% multinotch (not representable in the single dissociation column).
- Fractionation: high-pH RP (PRIDE:0000564) for the three hyperfractionation series; not available for the six ACN step fractions (method not described); not applicable for the two enrichment runs. characteristics[enrichment process] = antibody enrichment (PRIDE:0000954) for the two PTMScan runs, not applicable elsewhere.
- Acquisition DDA (PRIDE:0000627). Labels written as plain TMT126..TMT131 as the build tool/CHANNEL_RE and other repository files do.
