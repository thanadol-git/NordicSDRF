# PXD004863 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The 36 RAW filenames separate two acquisition series, but no deposited manifest or accessible primary-paper table identifies the biological source represented by each series or establishes whether filename `rep1`/`rep2` denotes technical injection, preparation replicate, pool, or distinct biological material. Decoding the abbreviations from filenames would be inference.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE project record: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD004863`
- PRIDE file inventory: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD004863/files/all`
- Primary publication metadata: DOI `10.1002/pmic.201600384`, PMID `28044435`
- Related open protocol article returned by the literature search: `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5755527/`
- Deposited RAW/MGF and search-result filenames

PRIDE lists 36 Orbitrap Fusion ETD RAW files. Twenty-four filenames form `hTau_CSF` fractions 1-12 with `rep1` and `rep2`; twelve form `RnA_FracA1` through `RnA_FracA12`. These names support fraction grouping only. The deposit contains no compact sample-design file, and the checked open protocol is methodological rather than an accession-specific mapping source. The primary publication was identifiable but did not provide an accessible deposited run table in the evidence gathered for this campaign.

## Evidence needed to unblock

An accession-specific sample/acquisition manifest, or a primary-publication supplement, defining `hTau_CSF` and `RnA`, the source material and disease/control status of each, pooling, and the exact meaning of `rep1` and `rep2`, with a direct link to the deposited RAW basenames.
