# PXD042517 annotation report

Quattrocolo lab (Kavli Institute / NTNU, Trondheim), with MS at PROMEC. Cajal-Retzius cells were ablated with Pde1c-Cre × AAV-flex-DTA (injected at P0). The tissue is CA1 stratum lacunosum-moleculare at P15 and P30. Paper: Glærum et al., Development 2024 (PMID 38095282). Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0
- `vertebrates` v1.1.0

## Rows and files
20 rows, one per deposited `.d.zip` (all covered). The mapping comes from the deposited `RawFile_Sample_assigment.txt`:
- `neg` = Pde1c-Cre−/−;DTA (control littermates)
- `pos` = Pde1c-Cre+/−;DTA (CR-cell ablated)
- `SLMn` is used as the biological replicate.

The group sizes as deposited are P15 neg 6, P15 pos 5, P30 neg 2 and P30 pos 7. The PRIDE processing text says P15 pos n=4, so one deposited P15-pos sample was apparently left out of the analysis. All deposited samples are annotated.

## Values
- **Sample:**
  - Organism part is `CA1 stratum lacunosum moleculare` (UBERON:0014557), from the PRIDE protocol.
  - The strain is the F1 of B6.FVB(Cg)-Tg(Pde1c-cre)IT146Gsat/Mmucd × C57BL/6JBomTac (paper; free text).
  - Sex is `not available`: the paper says both sexes were used but not recorded per animal.
- **Age and stage:** age is `15D` or `30D`. Developmental stage is `infant` (P15) or `juvenile days 30-44` (P30). These EFO stage choices are an interpretation; the age column carries the exact day.
- **Instrument:** **timsTOF Pro 2**, from `analysis.tdf` InstrumentName (serial 1875087.10477) in both acquisition batches. PRIDE and the protocol say timsTOF Pro, and `tools reconcile` flags this. The raw data wins.
- **Search:** modifications come from `summary.txt`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidated NQ variable. The PRIDE text and paper omit Carbamidomethyl, but the deposited search used it. The fragment tolerance of 25 ppm is MaxQuant's TOF MS/MS tolerance from parameters.txt; the precursor tolerance is `not available`.
- **Sample prep:** DTT/IAA (in-gel). Dissociation is CID.
- **Factors:** genotype and age.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf` (ms-proteomics and vertebrates, OLS cache) passes. The only warnings are free-text strain and treatment values.
