# PXD080484 annotation report

## Scope

All 98 deposited RAW files are represented.

## Evidence and decisions

- PRIDE describes human plasma TMT 10-plex proteomics for septic shock and acute pancreatitis, using tryptic FASP digestion and Orbitrap Fusion or Q Exactive HF acquisition.
- The deposited workbook contains reporter-abundance values and TMT sample names, but provides no raw-file-to-TMT-channel mapping. Therefore clinical group and individual channel assignments are intentionally not inferred.
- RAW names provide acquisition series, instrument family, fraction/run identifier, and repeated injections. The three timestamped repeat acquisitions are represented with technical replicate 2.
