## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- Mixed DDA and DIA: following the repo convention (PXD023708), the combined SDRF is kept plus `-dda` and `-dia` splits with technical replicates renumbered per split.
- Disease uses the Cellosaurus disease of each line: NCI-H841 and DMS 114 (CRL-2066) → thoracic SMARCA4-deficient undifferentiated tumor, NCI-H196 → lung sarcomatoid carcinoma, NCI-H1341 → cervical small cell carcinoma, although the paper treats them as SCLC lines; the paper's subtype is in phenotype.
- HLHE is not in Cellosaurus → accession/name/age/sex 'not available'.
- Runs marked 'Deleted…' in the design sheet (H661, H720, H460 reference lines; some re-measured DDA runs) are deposited and therefore annotated.
- reconcile flags: Trypsin/Lys-C not named in the PRIDE record (paper Methods state LysC then trypsin, S-Trap); organism part from cultured material (heuristic on 'cell line' wording; Cellosaurus derived-from site is used, as in repo precedent PXD070201).
- PRIDE lists the instrument as Q Exactive; RAW headers and paper say Q Exactive HF-X.
