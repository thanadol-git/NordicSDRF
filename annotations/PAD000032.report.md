# PAD000032 annotation report

## Result

500-row affinity-proteomics SDRF covering both deposited NPX tables. Templates: `affinity-proteomics` v1.0.0 + `human` v1.1.0.

- 405 cross-sectional study samples from `Main_analysis_NPX_long.npx.csv` (135 HIV+/Stroke+, 135 HIV+/Stroke−, 135 HIV−/Stroke−)
- 93 longitudinal study samples from `Longitudinal_validation_NPX_long.npx.csv` (37 HIV+/Stroke+ individuals; 18 with one pre-stroke sample, 19 with two)
- 2 pooled plasma sample controls, listed once from the main NPX file (the same control IDs are repeated in the longitudinal export)

Not already in `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets`.

## Evidence

- PRIDE: PAD000032, Chen et al., *Nat Commun* (2026), PMID 42265127, PMCID PMC13402305, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-74258-8. Fudan University / CHART cohort, Taizhou.
- EDTA plasma, Olink Target 96 Cardiovascular II and III (184 proteins), Intensity Normalization v.2, NPX. Readout on Fluidigm BioMark HD (no PSI-MS instrument term used).
- Group labels in the NPX files map the three main arms. Longitudinal groups `Ischemic_test_*` / `Hemorrhagic_test_*` plus C1/C2/C3 IDs match the paper’s 37 nested cases (C1=C3=37, C2⊂C1 n=19). test_1/test_2 treated as pre-stroke, test_3 as post-stroke; subtype applied only on post-stroke rows.
- Two `characteristics[disease]` columns: stroke status (`stroke disorder` / `ischemic stroke` / `hemorrhagic stroke` / `normal`) and HIV status (`HIV infectious disease` / `normal`). Matching factor values, plus `factor value[time point]` for the longitudinal arm.

## Conservative choices

- Age, sex, and ancestry are not in the deposit. The paper only reports cohort summaries (mean age 64.2 years; 82.2% male in HIV+/Stroke+), so those SDRF fields are `not available` (study samples) or `pooled` (controls). Developmental stage is `adult` from that cohort age.
- Main-arm HIV+/Stroke+ rows are `stroke disorder`, not ischemic vs hemorrhagic, because the main NPX group label does not split subtype (116 vs 19 in the paper).
- Longitudinal individuals are not joined to main-arm `G1_*` IDs (no sample map), even though the paper says they are nested in HIV+/Stroke+.
- Sample controls are `pooled`, not given a second copy from the longitudinal file.
