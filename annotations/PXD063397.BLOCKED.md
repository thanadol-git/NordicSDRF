# PXD063397 — annotation blocked

CD3+ T cells and CD14+ monocytes from common variable immunodeficiency (CVID) patients, with (CVIDCOMP) or without (CVIDIO, "infection only") autoimmune/inflammatory complications, and healthy controls. Bjørås/Jørgensen labs, NTNU/OUS. Paper: J Clin Immunol 2026, PMC13333567 (open access).

## What is known
- **Samples:** 63 timsTOF Pro 2 runs (instrument per the PRIDE protocol) (`220218_CVID_<1–63>_Slot…`). The paper reports a proteomics cohort of:
  - CD14+: CVIDCOMP 13, CVIDIO 5, controls 10.
  - CD3+: CVIDCOMP 13, CVIDIO 9, controls 10.
  - That is 60 samples; one patient was later excluded.
- **Sample prep:** SDC/TCEP/chloroacetamide; HILIC-bead on-bead trypsin digestion (KingFisher).
- **Search** (MaxQuant 2.0.3.0, deposited `summary.txt`/`parameters.txt`):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidation NQ variable.

## Why it is blocked
No key links sample numbers 1–63 to cell type (CD3+/CD14+) or clinical group:
- The deposited MaxQuant experiment names are just the numbers.
- The Perseus session (`session1.sps`) in `txt.tar` only holds a correlation analysis, with no categorical grouping.
- The supplementary tables give only RNA-seq sample groups (S01–S20) and DEP lists.

Cell type could be inferred from markers (CD14/CD3) in `proteinGroups.txt`, but the disease group, which is the study's primary factor, cannot be.

## What would unblock it
A sample sheet mapping `CVID_<n>` to cell type, clinical group (CVIDCOMP / CVIDIO / control) and patient ID.
