## Study
Coutu et al. 2021, "Palaeoproteomics confirm earliest domesticated sheep in southern Africa ca. 2000 BP", Sci Rep 11:6631 (PMC7988125, PMID 33758223). ZooMS (MALDI-TOF) plus LC-MS/MS on modern reference bone (springbok, grey rhebok, Namaqua Afrikaner sheep, grey duiker) and the archaeological collagen sample P4859C (Spoegrivier, OxA-3862). Corpus check: no existing directory for PXD021949 in sdrf-annotated-datasets (new).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `vertebrates` v1.1.0. Mass spectrometry; sheep and bovids are vertebrates, so the vertebrates organism layer (not human, not cell-lines, not DIA, no clinical/oncology layer) fits. The comparison is across species, so the only factor is `factor value[organism]`.

## Scope (deposited vs annotated)
Deposited: 10 files. Annotated: all 6 raw files (one row each: A, D, G, M, 4895C, SpringbokBK).
Excluded, by design:
- `ZooMSspectratxt.zip`: averaged MALDI-TOF peak-list TXT (mMass), not raw MS runs, no sample map deposited, MALDI ZooMS of many more specimens than the 6 LC-MS/MS runs.
- `SpringbokAMQtxt.zip`, `P4859CMQtxt.zip`, `Allsamplestxt.zip`: MaxQuant result bundles (search outputs). Used as evidence only (modifications, tolerance).
Note the MALDI-TOF instrument (ultraflex, listed in PRIDE) therefore does not appear in the SDRF.

## Key decisions and evidence
- Instrument: Q Exactive HF-X, confirmed from the raw header (A.raw) and Methods; PRIDE agrees. Raw file prefix "QE5" is a lab instrument id.
- Sample identity: species of A, D, G, M from paper Table 1. Raw `..._4895C.raw` is the paper's P4859C (Spoegrivier first phalanx, collagen); the raw file and MaxQuant experiment say 4895C while Table 1 and the zip folders say P4859C (Methods text says P4895C). Treated as one specimen; source name P4859C. Species Ovis aries is the paper's stated finding for this specimen.
- Cleavage: trypsin (MS:1001251) only. Methods say parallel trypsin and elastase digests were combined before each LC-MS/MS run, but `comment[cleavage agent details]` is not a multiple column in ms-proteomics v1.1.0; round 1 review rejected a hand-duplicated column, so elastase digestion is recorded here only (information loss). MaxQuant searches were semispecific.
- Modifications read from the deposited MaxQuant summary.txt: Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed; variable Oxidation (M), Deamidation (NQ), Gln->pyro-Glu, Glu->pyro-Glu, Hydroxyproline. Acetyl (Protein N-term) appears in the paper prose and PRIDE text but NOT in the deposited search, so it is omitted. Hydroxyproline is encoded as Oxidation UNIMOD:35 at P (inferred from MaxQuant's definition, not read from a file); Deamidated is UNIMOD:7 TA=N,Q.
- Fragment tolerance 20 ppm (parameters.txt FTMS). Precursor tolerance not recorded in the deposit: `not available`.
- Dissociation HCD (beam-type CID) with 28 NCE: NCE is stated in Methods; HCD is inferred from the instrument.
- Reduction/alkylation reagents `not available` (Methods silent); the deposited search declares Carbamidomethyl fixed, so `not applicable` would be wrong. PRIDE lists "iodoacetamide derivatized residue" as an identified PTM string.

## Values left as reserved words
- `not available`: sex, developmental stage (no per-sample data in Table 1), strain or breed for all except G, precursor tolerance, reduction/alkylation reagent. sample type and organism part for SpringbokBK.
- `not applicable`: disease (faunal bone), cell type.

## Judgement calls to attack hardest
1. **SpringbokBK.** Nothing in PRIDE or the paper describes this run. After round-1 review it is NOT asserted as a blank: organism Antidorcas marsupialis from the run name alone, organism part and sample type `not available`. Evidence it may be a blank: 2.5% MS/MS identified, 466 peptides sequenced vs 2.9-4.7k for the others, and the P4859C zip contains "Blank" searches. Not verifiable.
2. Only trypsin encoded although elastase was also used.
3. Hydroxyproline as Oxidation(P); HCD inferred.
4. P4859C vs 4895C identity.
5. `characteristics[sample type]` = study sample for modern references too (could be argued `reference`).
6. `organism part` = bone tissue for P4859C, which is collagen extracted from bone.
