# PXD001278 annotation report

Off-target proteome effects of transient transfection in HeLa cells, using SILAC. Heavy cells were untreated, treated with Fugene HD reagent alone, or transfected with an empty pECFP-N1 vector, and were compared with untreated light cells. Slupphaug lab, NTNU. Paper: BBA 2014, PMID 25448019 (not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 54 rows: 27 raw files × 2 SILAC channels.
- **Channel conditions** come from the run names:
  - `LN_H<x>` / `HeavyX_LightNormal` mean light = normal (untreated) and heavy = X.
  - X is `N`/`Normal` (untreated), `Tr`/`Reagent` (Fugene HD only) or `Ec`/`ECFP`/`Transfected` (Fugene HD + empty pECFP-N1).
- **Series:** the three acquisition dates (131019, 140129, 140508) each contain all three comparisons. They are annotated as experiment series = biological replicate 1–3.
- **Technical replicates:** `_1/_2/_3` (`_Normal`/`_Reagent`/`_Transfected` = 1) within a series are technical replicates 1–3.
  - **This split is inferred:** the paper is paywalled. The deposit could equally be one biological series with three replicates per date.
- **Label:** heavy is **Lys6 only** (L-lysine-13C6, arginine unlabelled; PRIDE protocol and PD search). Heavy rows carry `Label:13C(6)` K (UNIMOD:188) as Fixed; light rows say `not applicable`.

## Values
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Elite, from the raw header; CID, ion-trap MS2.
- **Search** (PRIDE processing text, PD 1.4 / SEQUEST HT):
  - 10 ppm / 0.6 Da, trypsin.
  - Oxidation M and N-terminal acetyl variable.
  - No carbamidomethylation, since no alkylation is described.
- **Deposited MaxQuant output:** the `maxquant_output.tar.gz` (MaxQuant 1.5.0) covers only the 9 ECFP runs and lists no fixed modifications.
- **Treatment:** 24 h; Fugene HD 56 µl / 19 µg plasmid per mix (PRIDE protocol).

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` raises two false positives:
  - "disease on control runs": the controls are untreated HeLa.
  - Trypsin: the search enzyme is trypsin and the extracts are tryptic.
