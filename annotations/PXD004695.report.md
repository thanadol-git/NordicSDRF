# PXD004695 annotation report

## Scope

All deposited MGF files are represented.

## Evidence and decisions

- PRIDE describes *Saprolegnia parasitica* developmental-stage proteomics with trypsin digestion, Xevo Q-Tof acquisition, and iTRAQ 4-plex quantification.
- The deposited Sampledetails.txt identifies BR1–BR3 as the quantitative iTRAQ sets, while wrong-labelled iTRAQ-only runs were used solely for identification. Raw MGF names identify stage-specific gel-band series and submitted BR groups.
- The deposit does not map iTRAQ channels to mycelium, primary cyst, secondary cyst, and germinated cyst. Consequently, stage is only assigned for explicit stage-named gel-band files; no channel-level stage inference is made.
