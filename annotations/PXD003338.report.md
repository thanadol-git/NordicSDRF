`sdrf-tools build` cannot express either change directly. A one-row file gets fraction 1, and the cleavage-agent column is single-valued in the contract. So the built file was passed through a deterministic script, `sdrf-skills/scratchpad/PXD003338/postprocess.py` (`python3 postprocess.py built_v2.sdrf.tsv annotations/PXD003338.sdrf.tsv`). The script:

- sets `comment[fraction identifier]` from 1 to 10 on the 10 m03545.raw rows, after asserting that they are technical replicate 3;
- inserts the Lys-C column directly after the Trypsin column, after asserting that every row has Trypsin.

A diff against the build output confirmed that the only cell changes are those 10 fraction cells and that the Lys-C column is the only new column. The first 240 built rows are identical to the previous version of the SDRF. The SDRF was never hand-edited.

## Design and file mapping

The design is a time course of hiPSC differentiation toward pancreatic progenitors:

| Time point | Stage | Replicates | TMT channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | hiPSC | 3 | TMT126, TMT127N, TMT127C |
| Day 5 | definitive endoderm | 2 | TMT128N, TMT128C |
| Day 12 | foregut endoderm | 2 | TMT129N, TMT129C |
| Day 17 | pancreatic progenitors | 3 | TMT130N, TMT130C, TMT131 |

All 10 samples sit in one TMT10-plex. The factor is `factor value[sampling time]` (0, 5, 12 or 17 day), which mirrors `characteristics[sampling time]`.

- **Channel-to-sample map.** Taken from the deposited result table `all_protein_identifications.xlsx` (sheet `t1_and_t2_all_search_engines_pr`). Its ratio headers read "Day 0 TMT 126", "Day 0 TMT 127C", "Day 0 TMT 127N", "Day 5 TMT 128C/128N", "Day 12 TMT 129C/129N" and "Day 17 TMT 130C/130N/131". The replicate counts match the paper's Methods and Figure 1 legend (triplicates for days 0 and 17, duplicates for days 5 and 12). This is the first rung of the channel-map ladder, the deposited files.
- **Run-to-fraction map.** The deposit has no fraction table, so the map comes from the Thermo raw-file headers, read with an HTTP range request on the first 128 KiB of each file.
  - m03490–m03501 carry sample IDs `hv_0016_T1`…`hv_0027_T1` (vials C5–D8): technical replicate 1, fractions 1–12.
  - m03503–m03514 carry `hv_0028_T2`…`hv_0039_T2` (vials B1–C4): technical replicate 2, fractions 1–12.
  - m03545 carries `hv_0037_T2` and was re-injected from vial A8 on 2014-11-29: technical replicate 3, fraction 10.
- **Replicate interpretation.** The paper describes 12 combined fractions, with about 5 µg dissolved per fraction and about 1 µg loaded per analysis. I therefore coded the T1 series as technical replicate 1 and the T2 series as technical replicate 2.
- **Row count.** 25 runs × 10 channels = 250 rows.

The deposit holds 26 raw files. One is not annotated: **m03515.raw**, sample ID `hv_0040_pg`.

- It ran immediately after m03514 (2014-11-26 11:09), from vial C5, the same tray position as T1 fraction 1 (m03490).
- It is about half size (194 MB).
- Neither the paper nor the deposit describes what "pg" contains, so it cannot be assigned to samples or fractions.

## Limitations

- **Technical replicates or two fraction sets.** Reading T1 and T2 as technical replicates is an inference from the sample-ID suffixes and the paper's statement of 12 fractions. The two series were loaded from different autosampler vials, so with the available evidence I cannot fully rule out a 24-fraction layout split across two series of 12. Either way, the channel-to-sample assignment is unaffected.
- **m03545.** It is coded as technical replicate 3, fraction 10. Whether it replaced or supplemented m03512 in the published search is unknown.
- **Fraction order.** Fraction numbers 1–12 are inferred from sequential sample IDs, vial positions and acquisition order. The deposit has no fraction table.
- **Biological replicate numbering.** Within a time point, replicate numbers are arbitrary and follow channel order (for example 126 = 1, 127N = 2, 127C = 3). The paper does not say which culture went into which channel beyond the time point.
- **Cell type.** Day 0 is `pluripotent stem cell` (CL:0002248). Days 5, 12 and 17 are the broader `endodermal cell` (CL:0000223), because CL has no definitive-endoderm, foregut-endoderm or pancreatic-progenitor cell term. The exact stage names are in this report and the paper.
- **Cell line.** `characteristics[cell line]` is the descriptive label `AG16102-derived hiPSC`. The paper reprogrammed AG16102 into two independent hiPSC lines (Teo lab, Sendai; Kulkarni lab, STEMCCA lentivirus) and does not say which one was profiled. `cellosaurus accession` and `cellosaurus name` are `not available`. Cellosaurus lists only Coriell AG28269 (CVCL_B5PL) as an AG16102-derived iPSC, and that is a different line.
- **Donor attributes.** `individual` = AG16102. `sex` = male, `age` = 69Y, `ancestry category` = European ancestry and `sampling site` = arm skin all come from the Cellosaurus record of the parental fibroblast AG16102 (CVCL_2G48), not from the paper. Cellosaurus says "Caucasian", and OLS maps that to HANCESTRO:0005.
- **Disease.** `disease` = normal: the paper calls AG16102 "commercial control fibroblasts". PRIDE's "diabetes mellitus" describes the study motivation, not a donor disease.
- **Organism part.** `organism part` = not applicable, because the samples are in vitro cultures. PRIDE's "pancreas" and "stem cell" entries describe the target lineage.
- **Technical values from the paper.** Fixed and variable modifications, tolerances, reagents (TCEP, iodoacetamide), fractionation (high-pH RP, 96 fractions combined into 12), MS1 range (400–1400 m/z) and MS2 CID at 35% NCE in the ion trap all come from the paper's Methods and the PRIDE protocol text. No search-parameter file was deposited. The raw-file method name (`..._ITMS2_..._CID35_..._FTMS3_10prec_HCD55_...`) is consistent with them.
  - MS3 reporter quantification used HCD at NCE 55 in the Orbitrap. This is not encoded because `dissociation method` is single-valued; CID (the identification MS2) is recorded.
- **Unannotated file.** m03515.raw (`hv_0040_pg`) is not annotated, as explained under Design and file mapping.
- **Post-processed values.** The fraction identifier on the m03545 rows and the Lys-C column were set by the documented post-processing script, not by `build`.
