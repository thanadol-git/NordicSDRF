## Design and file mapping
PRIDE protocol and preprint: three tissue types, each from wild-type controls and iab-6cocu mutants - accessory glands of unmated (virgin) males, accessory glands of males frozen right after copulation, and the reproductive tract (ovaries removed) of females frozen right after copulation with a wild-type or iab-6cocu male. Each sample pools tissue from five flies; five biological replicates per condition; each sample injected in technical triplicate. File names `<date>_KK_<cs|cocu>_<acg_nm|mated_male|mated_fem>_<replicate>_<technical>.raw` encode genotype (`cs` = control, `cocu` = iab-6cocu), tissue/mating state, biological replicate (1-5) and technical injection (1-3). 87 RAW files = 6 groups × 5 replicates × 3 injections − 3 (the wild-type mated male group has four replicates). All 87 are annotated. For female samples the genotype in the factor is that of the male mate (`wild type male mate` / `iab-6cocu male mate`); the female's own genotype is `not available`.
Factors: `factor value[genotype]` and `factor value[treatment]` (unmated vs mated).
**`cs` is read as the wild-type control** (the paper compares iab-6cocu to wild type; it is probably Canton-S, but no strain name is stated): strain `not available`.
