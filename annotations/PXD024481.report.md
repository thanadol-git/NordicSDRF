## Limitations

- Sample_Code.txt gives only cell type. Strain (C57BL/6 male/female, BALB/c, mT/mG), sex and age cannot be mapped to runs; strain, sex, developmental stage and disease are not available. The paper's full text is on PMC but does not tie runs to groups.
- Biological replicate 1-5 is the within-cell-type order of sample numbers (each sample is a separate source); it does not assert mouse identity.
- MaxQuant result archives (StdShotgun/Phosphoproteome_MQtxt.zip.gz) could not be read (HTTPS 404, FTP blocked), so modifications come from the PRIDE/paper prose: Carbamidomethyl fixed, Oxidation(M) and protein N-term Acetyl variable. The prep text says iodoacetate (would be Carboxymethyl, UNIMOD:6) while the search says only "alkylation on cysteines"; Carbamidomethyl is the MaxQuant default and is unverified. Alkylation reagent is therefore not available.
- DDA is assumed (MaxQuant LFQ on Q Exactive Plus); dissociation and both tolerances are not available (not verified from a deposited parameter file).
- The paper mentions 50,000-cell (1 h gradient) and 1-million-cell (2.5 h gradient) preps; which runs are which is not stated. Phosphoproteome result archive suggests an enrichment step that is not annotated.
- Independent review (Step 9.5) was not performed here, as no further subagents may be spawned.
