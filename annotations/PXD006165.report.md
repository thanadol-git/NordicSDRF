Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

## Description

- **Title:** Mice liver tissue LC-MSMS EPAC (PRIDE PXD006165, published 2019-09-12).
- **Lab:** Marit Bakke (PI), Department of Biomedicine, University of Bergen, Bergen, Norway. The data were acquired at PROBE, the University of Bergen proteomics unit; the submitter was Elise Aasebø.
- **Paper:** Sivertsen Åsrud K *et al.* "Mice depleted for Exchange Proteins Directly Activated by cAMP (Epac) exhibit irregular liver regeneration in response to partial hepatectomy." *Sci Rep* 2019;9:13789. PMID 31551444, PMCID PMC6760117, DOI 10.1038/s41598-019-50219-8. The proteomics Methods are in the Supplementary Information PDF (`41598_2019_50219_MOESM1_ESM.pdf`).
- **Study:** label-free DDA of liver from male C57BL/6J wild-type and Epac1/2 double-knockout (Rapgef3-/- Rapgef4-/-) mice. Liver was taken before 2/3 partial hepatectomy (PH), from the lobes resected at surgery, and 36 h after PH, from the regenerated lobes.

## Design and file mapping

The design is 2 genotypes × 2 sampling times, with 4 runs per group. The supplementary Methods name four groups (WTprePH, WTpostPH, Epac1/2-/-prePH, Epac1/2-/-postPH) and say that pre- and post-PH samples from the same mouse were compared with a paired t-test.

| group | runs (Sample #) | mouse IDs from file names |
|---|---|---|
| wild type, 0 hour (pre-PH) | 1, 3, 5, 13 | 84, 88, 93, not in name |
| wild type, 36 hour (post-PH) | 2, 4, 6, 14 | 84, 88, 93, not in name |
| Epac1/2 dKO, 0 hour | 7, 9, 11, 15 | 46, 47, 55, not in name |
| Epac1/2 dKO, 36 hour | 8, 10, 12, 16 | 46, 47, 55, not in name |

- **How the mapping was established:** from the deposited raw file names alone. Genotype comes from `WT`/`dKO`/`KO` in the name. Time point comes from `L1`/`L2`, read as the resected lobe at surgery (pre-PH), versus `regenerert` or `Reg` (Norwegian "regenerated", 36 h post-PH). Samples 13–16 (`1WT`, `2WT_Reg`, `3KO`, `4KO_Reg`) follow the same convention: no `Reg` means pre-PH. The deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` maps each raw file 1:1 to an experiment of the same name, which confirms 16 separate samples. No deposited sample table exists.
- The SDRF covers all 16 RAW files in the deposit. The FASTA and `txt.zip` are not data files.
- **Factors:** `factor value[genotype]` (`wild type` / `Epac1/2 double knockout (Rapgef3-/- Rapgef4-/-)`) and `factor value[sampling time]` (`0 hour` / `36 hour`).
- **Biological replicate** is numbered 1–4 within each genotype × time group. For the six mice with IDs, the number follows the mouse, so pre and post rows of the same mouse share it. `characteristics[individual]` carries `mouse <ID>` for those six mice.

## Limitations

- **Mapping of samples 13–16:** genotype and time come from the file names (`1WT`, `2WT_Reg`, `3KO`, `4KO_Reg`). These names carry no mouse ID, so `characteristics[individual]` is `not available`. The paired design and the balanced 4-per-group layout make it likely that 13/14 and 15/16 are each one mouse, but no deposited source says so. Biological replicate 4 in each group is a group-wise index, not a claim that those runs are paired.
- **Pre-PH time point:** reading `L1`/`L2` as pre-PH resected tissue is an inference from the file names. It contrasts with `regenerert` and matches the paper's design: the left lateral and median lobes are removed at surgery, and the paper reports only pre- vs 36 h post-PH proteomics. What `L1` vs `L2` stands for (probably a lobe or aliquot label) is not documented. Run 11 is `L1`; runs 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 are `L2`; runs 13 and 15 have no tag.
- **sampling time `0 hour`:** this is the resected tissue at the time of PH. It is the reference point, not a measured interval. `36 hour` is stated in the paper and the PRIDE protocol.
- **organism part:** written as `liver`. The paper says pre-PH tissue is the left lateral and median lobes and post-PH tissue is the right lateral and caudate lobes, but which lobe each run came from is not recorded per run.
- **disease = `normal`:** none of the mice had a disease. The knockouts are genetic models, recorded in `characteristics[genotype]`. Partial hepatectomy is a surgical procedure and is not a disease. I did not add a `characteristics[treatment]` column because every animal underwent PH; the pre/post contrast is in sampling time.
- **sex = `male`, developmental stage = `adult`, strain = `C57BL/6J`:** these come from cohort-level statements ("Male mice (C57BL/6J background, 8–12 weeks)"; "Male C57BL/6J mice" in the abstract). They are not per-animal records. The wild-type controls are described as littermates or C57BL/6JBomTac from Taconic.
- **genotype wording** is free text (`Epac1/2 double knockout (Rapgef3-/- Rapgef4-/-)`), from the paper. The Epac2 deletion removes all Epac2 isoforms.
- **precursor mass tolerance** is `not available`: it is absent from the deposited search outputs. The MaxQuant 1.5 defaults (20 ppm first search, 4.5 ppm main search) were not assumed.
- **Reduction and alkylation reagents** are not stated in the paper or deposit; the cited FASP protocol was not consulted for them. Those optional columns are omitted. Carbamidomethyl (C) is a fixed modification in the search.
- **Acquisition date:** the run names begin with `20151208`, but no per-run date was taken from the raw files, so the column is omitted.
- **Sample input:** the paper's "10–12 µg" of frozen liver is likely a typo for mg. No tissue mass column is written.
