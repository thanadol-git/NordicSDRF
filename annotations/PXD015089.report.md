# PXD015089 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"Proteomics of non-host response in barley" (Andrzejczak et al., Sci Rep 2020, PMID 32513937, PMC7280273). Barley (Hordeum vulgare) cv. Golden Promise, wild type (WT) vs HvHb1 phytoglobin overexpression (HO), mock vs wheat powdery mildew (Blumeria graminis f. sp. tritici) inoculated, leaves at 72 h after inoculation; 3 biological replicates per group; TMT labelling, two TMT sets, each high-pH RP fractionated into 5 fractions, Q Exactive HF.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + plants v1.1.0 (non-human plant; TMT, DDA). No single-cell/clinical templates apply.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
Deposited: 10 RAW files (2 TMT sets x 5 fractions), 3 FASTA/pep.xml search files, 4 Proteome Discoverer .msf files. Annotated: all 10 RAW files, 8 TMT channels each = 80 rows x 38 columns. Search outputs (msf, pep.xml, fasta) are not annotated as data rows.

## Key decisions
- Channel to sample map: the paper lists tag series per replicate and treatment order (WT, HO, WTi, HOi); the deposited Proteome Discoverer .msf study definition (M1 and M2 .msf headers read) assigns every channel a genotype and treatment and agrees with the paper in both sets: 126=WT mock, 127N=HO mock, 128N=WT inoculated, 129N=HO inoculated, 127C=WT mock, 129C=HO mock, 130C=WT inoculated, 130N=HO inoculated.
- Replicate assignment: tags 126/127N/128N/129N = replicate 1 (set M1) and replicate 3 (set M2); tags 127C/129C/130C/130N = replicate 2 in both sets (paper: "replicates one and two and replicates two and three were mixed into two sets"). So the replicate 2 samples are in both sets: same source name, technical replicate 1 (M1) and 2 (M2). This is inferred from the paper wording (the msf does not carry replicate numbers).
- Labels written as TMT126...TMT130C (8-plex of the TMT10 reagent, per paper); search used TMT6plex (UNIMOD:737) as the modification, as deposited.
- Instrument Q Exactive HF (paper and RAW name prefix QHF) over PRIDE's "Q Exactive".
- Factors: genotype and treatment. Genotype also in characteristics[genotype] (not in contract; added as a custom characteristic).
- Modifications from PRIDE data-processing protocol / paper (Carbamidomethyl C, TMT6plex K and N-term fixed; Oxidation M variable); the deposited .msf were not opened for parameters (large SQLite files), so search-params was not run.
