## Paper vs this deposit

The paper assigns **in vitro biotinylation proteomics to PXD033425** and **ex vivo granuloma proteomics to PXD039416**. This PXD contains both: timsTOF fleX biotinylation runs and Q Exactive granuloma RAWs.

Paper design (in vitro): biotin vs non-biotin surface labelling; methods also mention intact vs lysed biotinylation. PCA in the paper is 2 conditions × 3. MaxQuant has **four** groups A1–A4 × 3 replicates. No source names A1–A4 as biotin / non-biotin / intact / lysed.

Paper design (granuloma): 10 granulomas (replicates). Deposit has 9 RAW files and no sample sheet linking `P134xx` to a granuloma ID.

Organisms in the record: *Mycobacterium marinum* and *Danio rerio*. Instruments: timsTOF fleX + Q Exactive.

## Blocking gaps

1. Zip archive names do not match the inner `.d` folders. SDRF `comment[data file]` must be the deposited filename; writing `A1_1….d` would invent a file that is not in the PRIDE file list, and writing the zip name as 12 samples would invent a 1:1 map that is not evidenced.
2. A1–A4 are four MaxQuant experiment groups with no recovered mapping onto biotin / non-biotin × intact / lysed.
3. Nine granuloma RAWs vs ten granulomas in the paper, and the paper points granulomas at **PXD039416**, not this accession.
4. No checked source (PRIDE description, mqpar, `summary.txt`, proteinGroups column headers, preprint citation) is a file→condition table.

Assigning A1=biotin and A2=non-biotin (or any 2×2 layout) would invent the map.

## Unblock requirement

A sample sheet that (a) lists the Bruker `.d` members inside each zip and maps each of A1–A4 to biotinylation condition (and intact vs lysed if that is the four-group design), and (b) maps every `P134xx.raw` to a granuloma replicate — or states that the granuloma RAWs are out of scope here and belong with PXD039416.
