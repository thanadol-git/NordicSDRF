# PXD031143 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE identifies mouse serum, blood, lymph-node, and MIF haptenation experiments with TRITC. The public inventory exposes diverse RAW names including `HBloodTRITC*`, `LNTRITC*`, `HSA_MR*`, `Hb_MR*`, calibration curves, QC samples, and targeted PRM runs.
- **Why blocked:** Although high-level sample classes are suggested by filenames and the protocol, the deposited record provides no legend for the `MR` molar-ratio codes, no run-to-sample manifest, no control pairing, and no indication of which acquisitions are discovery versus targeted quantification. Mapping all runs to ratios, sample sources, and experimental role would require unsupported assumptions.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A deposited run sheet defining each RAW file’s biological material, TRITC molar ratio or control condition, experiment mode (discovery/PRM/QC/calibration), and replicate.
