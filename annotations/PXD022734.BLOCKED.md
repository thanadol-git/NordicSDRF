# PXD022734 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE deposits 24 fractionated TMT RAW files in two twelve-file series: `20191113_HF2_*_Shuijie_TMT12_F1–F12.raw` and `20200131_HF2_*_Shuijie_TMT_768_O_F1–F12.raw`.  Its two deposited workbooks give reporter-channel assignments, but label them as `F15` (TMT12) and `F16` (TMT 786), respectively.
- **Why blocked:** Neither workbook supplies a RAW/fraction-to-plex crosswalk.  In particular, its `F15` and `F16` identifiers do not occur in the deposited RAW filenames, whose two series instead use the TMT12 and TMT_768_O names.  Assigning either channel design to those files would therefore be unsupported.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A submitted experimental-design sheet linking each TMT channel map to its RAW/fraction series, or author confirmation of those two crosswalks.
