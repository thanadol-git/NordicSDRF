## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- Mixed DDA and DIA: following the repo convention (PXD023708), the combined SDRF is kept plus `-dda` and `-dia` splits with technical replicates renumbered per split.
- Disease follows the paper (Table 1), PRIDE and the bundled sdrf-skills cell-line snapshot (scratchpad evidence/cl-annotations-db_extract.tsv): small cell lung carcinoma for all SCLC lines, large cell carcinoma for NCI-H460/NCI-H661 and carcinoid for NCI-H720 (snapshot). The paper's subtype is in phenotype.
- Age/sex/sampling site come from the snapshot extract; GLC-4, NCI-N417 and NCI-H372 (absent from the snapshot) and NCI-H82 come from api.cellosaurus.org records saved in evidence/cellosaurus_api_extracts.txt. Precedence: a saved current Cellosaurus record wins over the snapshot, so NCI-H82 age is 41Y (the snapshot says 40Y); no other saved record conflicts with the snapshot.
- MS batch from the design sheet 'Batch number' (also the B1/B2 token of the vial ID) is in comment[lc batch] as 'MS batch 1' / 'MS batch 2'; the paper batch-corrects, and SCLC20–SCLC29 (incl. all three reference lines) were measured in batch 2 only.
- characteristics[culture medium] = RPMI 1640 (Methods: SCLC lines maintained in RPMI-1640 with 10% fetal calf serum); 'not available' for the NSCLC and carcinoid reference lines, whose medium is not stated.
- The dia-acquisition template is not declared for the -dia split (as for PXD023708), so DIA window columns are omitted.
- characteristics[sampling site] is the Cellosaurus derived-from site of each line (snapshot / API extracts). Paper Table 1 'Cell line origin' is coarser (Metastatic / Pleural eff. / Lung) and disagrees for three lines: NCI-H211 (paper Lung; Cellosaurus bone marrow), NCI-H378 (paper Lung; pleural effusion) and NCI-H1694 (paper Lung; ascites). The Cellosaurus site is kept because it records the specific sampled tissue; the paper's 'Lung' is consistent with the lung primary.
- SW1271 cell type = epithelial cell (snapshot); cell type is 'not available' for the other lines.
- Acetyl is annotated with PP=Protein N-term; the paper says only 'N-terminal acetylation' (Proteome Discoverer protein N-terminal modification is inferred).
- HLHE has no Cellosaurus accession in the snapshot and paper Table 1 gives no other ID (N/A) → accession/name/age/sex 'not available'.
- reconcile flags: Trypsin/Lys-C not named in the PRIDE record (paper Methods state LysC then trypsin, S-Trap); organism part on cultured material (heuristic on 'cell line' wording; see the dataset-specific organism part note).
- PRIDE lists the instrument as Q Exactive; RAW headers and paper say Q Exactive HF-X.
- Runs marked 'Deleted…' in the design sheet are deposited and therefore annotated: the H661, H720 and H460 reference-line runs (deleted after batch correction) and SCLC09_B2_DDA_R1 (deleted after normalization).
- Two SCLC02 DDA R3 files have RAW names whose B1/B2 token is opposite to the design sheet: 20200311_SN_SCLC2_B2_DDA_R3.raw is assigned to growth G1 and 20200311_SN_SCLC2_B1_D2_DDA_R3.raw to G2, following the design sheet's vial IDs (G1_D2 / G2_D1).
- characteristics[organism part] = culture medium (conditioned media); the line's derived-from site is in sampling site.
- RAW date prefixes per MS batch: batch 1 = 20191213, batch 2 = 20200311.
