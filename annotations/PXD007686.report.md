# PXD007686 annotation report

## Scope

All 90 deposited RAW files are represented as label-free assays.

## Evidence and decisions

- Submitted RAW names explicitly encode the cancer cell line, treatment, attached/detached state, and biological replicate. These values are retained as SDRF source characteristics and factors.
- `CTRL`, `5-FU`, `MTX`, and `PCTL` are expanded from the deposited study description to untreated control, 5-fluorouracil, methotrexate, and paclitaxel.
- The two submitted raw-file labels `HCT116L` and `CT116L` occur in the HCT-116 experiment described by PRIDE; their SDRF cell-line value is normalized to HCT116 while the original filename remains unchanged in `comment[data file]`.
- PRIDE specifies sequential Lys-C/Trypsin digestion, label-free acquisition on a Q Exactive Plus, fixed carbamidomethylation, and variable deamidation and methionine oxidation.
