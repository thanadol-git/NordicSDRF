# PXD051310 annotation report

## Result

14-row label-free SDRF covering every deposited RAW file. Five EOTRH incisor cementum samples (`D1`–`D5`, biological replicates 1–5) and five healthy controls (`H1`–`H5`, biological replicates 1–5) are mapped from the paper and from filename prefixes. `H1-1h` and `H1_30m` stay on source `Healthy_H1` as technical replicates 2 and 3 with filename-derived elution times (`1 h`, `30 min`). `startQC` and `test1_100_30` are quality-control injections with sample metadata marked not applicable.

Templates: `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `vertebrates` v1.1.0. Factor is disease (`equine odontoclastic tooth resorption and hypercementosis` vs `normal`).

## Evidence

- PRIDE project record: PXD051310 (United Kingdom; University of Liverpool).
- Publication: Jensen et al., Equine Veterinary Journal (2025), PMID 39777419, PMC12135756, DOI 10.1111/evj.14469.
- PRIDE `files/all`: 14 RAW files. Results text names EOTRH sample `D3`, confirming `D` = diseased and `H` = healthy.
- Methods: EDTA/GnHCl cementum extraction, DTT reduction, iodoacetamide alkylation, Trypsin/Lys-C digest; Mascot search enzyme trypsin, carbamidomethyl (C) fixed, oxidation (M) variable, 10 ppm / 0.01 Da; DDA HCD 30% NCE on an Orbitrap, 2-h gradient. Instrument model `Q Exactive HF` is taken from the PRIDE structured field.
- Disease term: OLS `MONDO:1014559` (equine odontoclastic tooth resorption and hypercementosis).

## Conservative choices

- Per-sample age is not deposited (cohort means only). Sex is `male` on diseased rows (all five diseased incisors were from geldings) and `not available` on controls.
- `H1-1h` / `H1_30m` are extra injections of `Healthy_H1` (same source name, technical replicates 2–3). Elution times `1 h` and `30 min` come from filenames, not from Methods.
- This PXD is a UK veterinary dataset, not an Icelandic lab; it was queued as a leftover Reykjavík keyword hit.

## Validation

`parse_sdrf validate-sdrf` against `ms-proteomics` and `vertebrates`: both passed. `sdrf-tools structure` OK.

The repeated Healthy_H1 injections are distinguished by technical replicate 2/3 and by their filename-derived elution conditions; the two QC injections remain separate sources with `not applicable` sample metadata. Methods used a Trypsin/Lys-C mix, while `comment[cleavage agent details]` is a single-cardinality column and records the Mascot search enzyme Trypsin (`MS:1001251`).
