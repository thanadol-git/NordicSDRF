# PXD076695: donor-to-run mapping unresolved

All 60 deposited RAW names match rows in the [experimental design](../scratchpad/PXD076695/experimental_design.txt): 19 DCM, 24 ICM and 17 CONTROL runs. The design also lists one S1 `3uL` run absent from the deposited RAW list. The [working SDRF](../scratchpad/PXD076695/draft.sdrf.tsv) passes deterministic validation but remains outside `annotations/`.

An [independent review](../scratchpad/PXD076695/review-fail.json) rejected the biological-replicate assignments. The [linked paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13519613/) reports only 20 ischemic cardiomyopathy tissue samples in its full 96-person cohort, while the deposited design labels 24 distinct RAW runs ICM. Neither the design nor available paper evidence identifies which ICM runs share a donor or whether some are technical repeats. The working file's 24 ICM biological-replicate ordinals therefore overstate the evidence.

Completion requires a run-to-donor or run-to-biological-source key for the 24 ICM runs, and reconciliation of any repeated preparations. Keep sample-level age and sex unavailable unless that key also links the clinical data to individual RAW runs.
