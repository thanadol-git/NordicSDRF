# PXD023018 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: Glycosaminoglycan domain mapping of proteoglycans

Publication: Persson A et al., Molecular & Cellular Proteomics 2021 20:100074, PMID 33757834, DOI 10.1016/j.mcpro.2021.100074

## Evidence collected

- 33 LTQ Orbitrap Elite RAW files, `.rep` result files, and `Data_output_summary.xlsx`
- Rat INS-1 832/13 conditioned-medium proteins were digested exhaustively with pronase, after which isolated glycosaminoglycan chains were depolymerized and analyzed by negative-mode LC-HCD-MS/MS
- Data analysis detects and quantifies glycosaminoglycan compositions rather than tryptic peptide/protein identifications

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

This deposition is glycosaminoglycan glycomics after pronase destruction of the protein backbone, not a bottom-up proteomics experiment represented by the repository's proteomics SDRF templates. Treating the RAW files as peptide samples would misdescribe the measured analytes.

## What would unblock it

A glycomics-specific SDRF template and campaign scope that can represent released GAG domains, depolymerization enzymes, ion-pairing chromatography, and negative-ion acquisition.
