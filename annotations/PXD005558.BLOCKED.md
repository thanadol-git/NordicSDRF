# PXD005558 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

TMT-10plex (and, per the paper, TMT-11plex for FtsZ mutants) experiment with no recoverable channel-to-sample map and no recoverable raw-file-to-plex/fraction assignment. The paper states the design only as 4:3:3 biological replicates (MI 16 h : MII 30 h : MII 65 h) but never states which reporter channel holds which replicate or condition. The deposited Proteome Discoverer results carry no sample/channel labels (single default "Sample" entry per raw file, empty SampleGroups). The 40 deposited RAW files (FUS03939-03962, FUS04146-04149, FUS04580-04585, QHF0721/22/25-28) cannot be assigned to plexes/fractions/phospho vs proteome from the record: the paper describes 12 HILIC fractions (proteome) and 30 high-pH fractions (FtsZ mutants), while one PD workflow also searched a non-deposited file (UHF2538.raw). The deposit is also flagged PARTIAL by PRIDE.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE v3 project record and full file list (55 files, all projectAccessions = PXD005558), under `scratchpad/PXD005558/`.
- Paper (Rioseras et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 2018, PMC6072539, PMID 29784711), full text via PMC HTML render: Methods, Experimental Design, Fig. 1 legend, Data Availability (only points to PXD005558; no Zenodo/figshare/GitHub).
- Deposited search outputs: `QHF0722.pdResult` (SQLite; WorkflowInputFiles, AnalysisDefinitionXML: Sample Name = raw stem, SampleType "Sample", no groups, no channel names) and `QHF0722.pep.xml` / `FUS04146.pep.xml` (workflow logs: Mascot, static Carbamidomethyl + TMT6plex K/N-term, quantifier method "TMT 10plex", no channel labels).
- Europe PMC fullTextXML/supplementaryFiles: not available (article not open access, HTTP 500 / error bean).
- Paper supplementary tables (three .xls, MCP): PMC serves them behind a JavaScript proof-of-work challenge; not retrieved (not circumvented).

No deposited SDRF was used as evidence.

## Evidence needed to unblock

Supplemental Tables S1/S2 (per-channel TMT abundance columns with sample names) or a submitter manifest giving, for each RAW file, its plex, fraction and phospho/proteome/FtsZ role, plus the TMT channel for each biological replicate (MI 16 h x4, MII 30 h x3, MII 65 h x3; FtsZ Ser-Ser x4, Glu-Glu x3, Ala-Ala x4). A human with browser access to the PMC supplements could supply S1/S2 for a retry.
