# PXD026774 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The TMTpro 16-plex channel-to-sample map (and the replicate-level assignment of the 3 multiplexed sets) is not stated in any deposited file, the paper text, or the paper supplement. An SDRF row per (source, channel) cannot be written without inventing it.

## What is established

- Study: Gockert et al., NAR 2022 (PMID 35048984, PMC8860595). HeLa:TIR1 cells; mAID-tagged ZCCHC8, ZFC3H1, EXOSC3 lines treated with 750 uM IAA for 0, 6 or 28 h; unedited HeLa:TIR1 control (0 and 28 h IAA merged as "ctrl"); biological triplicates.
- Deposit: 60 RAW files (all 1 page of PRIDE v2 listing, pageSize=100, page 1 empty) + mzTab.mzTab.gz (identification-only mzTab, MaxQuant 1.6.17). RAW names `Eclip_00926..00985_JSA_LJ_DegronR{1,2,3}F{01..20}.raw` = 3 TMTpro 16-plex sets x 20 concatenated fractions (paper: 50 fractions concatenated into 20).
- Methods: TMTpro 16-plex, 15 ug per sample labelled on-column, "reference samples pooled separately and mixed into the sample replicates in equal amounts as a common reference"; Orbitrap Eclipse SPS-MS3; MaxQuant 1.6.17; Cys carbamidomethyl fixed, TMTpro N-term/K, Met oxidation variable.

## Evidence checked (nothing maps channels to samples)

- PRIDE v2 project record and complete file list (61 files).
- Europe PMC full text XML: Methods, Results and Figure 2 legend give only the design (0/6/28 h, 3 cell lines, ctrl, triplicate); no TMT channel table.
- Figure 2A (image, gkac001fig2.jpg) is a schematic of conditions with no channel labels.
- Supplement (NAR_Supplement.pdf, figureS1-S5.pdf): no TMT/channel/plex content.
- mzTab.mzTab.gz header: identification-type, ms_run list and modification list only; no assay/study-variable or channel metadata.
- The stated conditions (3 mAID lines x 0/6/28 h = 9, plus unedited control at 0 and 28 h, plus a pooled reference) do not fix a unique 16-channel layout, so it cannot be deduced.
- Fraction identifiers F01-F20 are present in the RAW names and would be usable; the blocker is only the channel map.

No deposited SDRF was used as evidence.

## Evidence needed to unblock

A submitter-provided channel layout for the three TMTpro 16-plex sets (R1-R3), e.g. the MaxQuant experimental design / labeling template, the MaxQuant output (proteinGroups.txt reporter-intensity column names with an experimental design table), or a supplementary table. Also which channel(s) carry the common reference pool and whether the layout is identical across replicates.
