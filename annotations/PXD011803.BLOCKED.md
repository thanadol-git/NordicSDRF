# PXD011803 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Combined FGFR and Akt pathway inhibition abrogate growth of EGFR TKI-resistant NSCLC cells with upregulated FGFR1 expression
- **PRIDE record:** PXD011803
- **Publication:** Terp MG, …, Beck HC, …, Ditzel HJ. *NPJ Precis Oncol* 2021;5:65, DOI [10.1038/s41698-021-00208-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41698-021-00208-w), PMID 34267282, PMC8282882

## Evidence recovered

- 108 RAW (Q Exactive): three replicates (`KIR_REP1-3`) × two preparations (`BB…` and `BF…`), each 9 HILIC fractions (2–10) injected twice (plain and time-stamped re-injection). Six PD 1.4 `.msf` files (`KIR_REP<n>_2_control.msf` ↔ `BB` runs; `KIR_REP<n>_2_sample.msf` ↔ `BF` runs; input file lists read from the `.msf` headers by HTTP range).
- Quantification: iTRAQ 4plex (PD quan method "iTRAQ 4plex (Thermo Scientific Instruments)"), static Carbamidomethyl C, iTRAQ on K and N-term; dynamic Oxidation M, deamidation; 8 ppm / 0.05 Da.
- Paper: erlotinib-resistant clones ER10, ER20, ER30 derived from HCC827; lysates split into soluble and membrane-associated fractions before iTRAQ labelling and HILIC (22 fractions).

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The iTRAQ 4plex reporter channels (114/115/116/117) cannot be assigned to samples. The PD 1.4 `.msf` files carry no study factors or sample names (only the generic quan method), the paper's Methods and supplementary-material list do not state the label layout, and PRIDE has no sample sheet. It is also not stated which of `BB`/`BF` (deposited as "control"/"sample" searches) is the soluble vs the membrane fraction. Assigning HCC827/ER10/ER20/ER30 to channels would be fabrication.

## Sources checked

PRIDE project record and `files/all`; first 8 MB of all six `.msf` (workflow, quan method, input files); full-text Methods of PMC8282882 (NCBI efetch, title verified); supplementary-material captions.

## Unblocking requirement

The iTRAQ channel → cell line (and replicate) layout per `KIR_REP<n>` set, and confirmation of which prefix (`BB`/`BF`) is the soluble vs membrane-associated fraction.
