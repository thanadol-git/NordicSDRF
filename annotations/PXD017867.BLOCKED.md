# PXD017867 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Proteomic analysis of renal biomarkers of kidney allograft fibrosis – a study in renal transplant patients
- **PRIDE record:** PXD017867
- **Publication:** Mortensen LA, … *Int J Mol Sci* 2020;21(7):2371, DOI [10.3390/ijms21072371](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms21072371), PMID 32235494, PMC7177439

## Evidence recovered

- 324 RAW (`<set>_<well>.raw`) in seven TMT10plex file sets (PD study `Nyre01072016`: "Set of 46/61/45/45/39/42/43 files"), one `.msf`, `.pdResult`, `.pdResultView` and the `.pdStudy` (read in full).
- Paper: renal-transplant biopsies; "Peptide samples were randomly labelled with the 127N, 127C, 128N, 128C, 129N, 129C and 130N mass tags, whereas a pool of all samples were labelled with the mass tag 126 that served as an internal standard"; fixed TMT10plex K/N-term + Carbamidomethyl C; dynamic Oxidation M, deamidation; HCD 31.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The PD study definition has no factors and no sample names (every channel is unnamed `[127N]…`), the paper states only that patient samples were labelled randomly, and no sample sheet exists in PRIDE. Only the 126 pool channel is identifiable; the 7 patient channels per set (and their fibrosis status) are not. Annotating the 126 channel alone would describe none of the study design.

## Sources checked

PRIDE record and `files/all`; `.pdStudy` (full) and `.msf`/`.pdResult` headers; full Methods of PMC7177439 (NCBI efetch, title verified).

## Unblocking requirement

A per-set table: TMT set → RAW file block, channel → patient/biopsy ID, with fibrosis grade/time-point per biopsy.
