# PXD052078 — blocked on oxygen-condition conflict

The [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD052078) deposits `experimental_design.tsv` with 11 sample rows: six labelled `Oxygen level: 8%` and five labelled `Oxygen level: 20%`. The six 8% acquisition basenames contain `_8_`; the five 20% basenames contain `_20_`. A byte-range read of the `Raw_files.zip` central directory identified exactly these 11 Bruker `.d` acquisitions, each containing `analysis.tdf`. The deposited DIA-NN log also lists the 11 names.

The PRIDE sample-processing protocol states 7 days at **1% or 20% O₂**, as does the linked [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12355077/), which describes the proteomics contrast as 1% versus 20%. Thus the deposited run-level design and filenames disagree with the scientific methods on the key treatment value. The paper also describes three independent experiments with six replicates, while the deposited ZIP has 11 runs, so experiment identity cannot be assigned from the publication.

No SDRF was written. The raw-to-design mapping is verified, but a source-backed correction of the `_8_` group to 1% or an explanation that it represents a distinct 8% cohort is needed before assigning the oxygen factor.

Evidence cached in `scratchpad/PXD052078/`: accession-scoped `files.json`, `pride.json`, `experimental_design.tsv`, `DIANN_report.log.log`, and `raw-tail-256k.bin`.
