# PXD033510 annotation report

Patient-derived multiple myeloma lines TK12, TK13, TK14 and TK16, treated with ATX-101 (the APIM peptide targeting PCNA). The samples are MIB-enriched ATP/GTP-binding proteins (the "signalome"). Otterlei group, NTNU. Paper: Cancers 2024, PMC11640687. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 27 rows, one per `.raw`. Run names give the line and group: `CTR1–3` are untreated controls and `APIM4–6` are ATX-101-treated, mapped to biological replicates 1–3 within each group.

TK12 controls also have timestamped re-acquisitions (`CTR1/2/3_2020032…`), deposited next to the plain runs; these get technical replicate 2. MaxQuant searched the plain `TK12_CTR2` only through its re-run.

## Values
- **Cell lines:** TK12–14 come from one donor's pleural effusion and TK16 from bone marrow (paper, Table 1). Organism part is `pleural effusion` (UBERON:0000175) or `bone marrow`. Disease `plasma cell myeloma`; cell type `plasma cell`. The TK lines are not in Cellosaurus, so accession and name are `not available`.
- **Treatment:** ATX-101 vs untreated. The dose and time for the MIB samples are not stated; the paper's metabolomics used 10 µM for 4 h.
- **Enrichment:** multiplexed inhibitor beads (MIB) with on-column trypsin. No PRIDE/EFO enrichment term fits, so it is described here.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF (raw header), HCD.
- **Search:** from the deposited `mqpar.xml`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term, Deamidation NQ and Phospho STY variable; 4.5 ppm (main search) / 20 ppm.

## Related

## Validation
CI-style `parse_sdrf` passes. `tools reconcile` flags "disease on control runs"; that is a false positive, since the controls are untreated myeloma cells. Its trypsin flag is also a false positive (the search and Methods specify trypsin).
