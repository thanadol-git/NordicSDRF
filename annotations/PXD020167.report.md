# PXD020167 annotation report

*Phaeodactylum tricornutum* wild type vs CRISPR/Cas9 CpSRP54 knockout lines cpsrp54-8 and cpsrp54-11. Bones lab, NTNU. Paper: Plant J 2021, PMID 33372269 (not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 15 rows: 3 genotypes × 5 biological replicates (I–V), one `.raw` each. mzML and mzid copies are also deposited.

## Values
- **Sample:** strain `CCMP632 (Bohlin clone Pt1 8.6)`.
- **Factor:** `genotype`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF, from the raw header and the protocol. PRIDE's instrument field says Q Exactive. HCD at 27 NCE.
- **Search:** Proteome Discoverer 2.3 (SEQUEST), protocol:
  - Carbamidomethyl C static.
  - Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ and protein N-term acetylation variable.
  - 20 ppm / 0.02 Da.
- **Open question:** all files carry a `6t` tag; the deposited xlsx is named `…WT6tHL…`. This probably means 6 h of high light, but that could not be confirmed: the paper is closed and the Europe PMC abstract request was rate-limited. Because it is common to all samples it is not encoded as a factor.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: every annotated value agrees with the deposit record.
