# PXD030167 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE and the article describe serum extracellular-vesicle proteomics from male C57BL/6 mice after tibia surgery. One TMTpro run contains control and 6-hour post-surgery samples (`Mkrtchian_serum_EV_proteome_6h_TMT12.raw`); the other contains control, 24-hour, and 72-hour post-surgery samples (`Mkrtchian_serum_EV_proteome_24__72h_TMT16.raw`). The article specifies six surgical animals per timepoint (seven for C6h in mass spectrometry) and explains the two-run grouping.
- **Why blocked:** Neither the PRIDE result workbooks nor the public article and supplementary methods identify the reporter channel assigned to each control or surgical animal. Knowing the run-level groups and animal counts cannot establish individual TMTpro channel-to-sample relationships without inventing them.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A TMTpro sample sheet or run sheet mapping every reporter channel in each RAW file to its animal, surgical/control status, and timepoint.
