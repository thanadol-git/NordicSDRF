## Study design

Secretome (culture-supernatant proteins, 3 kDa MWCO-concentrated) of the marine bacterial predator
*Saprospira* sp. CCB-QB6 grown axenically for 9 h in artificial seawater medium (ASW) with either
0.05% w/v tryptone (low nutrient, LN — predation occurs) or 0.5% w/v tryptone (high nutrient, HN — no
predation). Three biological replicates per condition, one label-free Gel-LC-MS/MS DDA run each
(entire gel lane excised as one sample; no fractionation).

| Group | Source names | Files |
|---|---|---|
| low nutrient (ASW + 0.05% w/v tryptone) | CCB-QB6_secretome_LN_1..3 | LN1.mzML, LN2.mzML, LN3.mzML |
| high nutrient (ASW + 0.5% w/v tryptone) | CCB-QB6_secretome_HN_1..3 | HN1.mzML, HN2.mzML, HN3.mzML |

File → condition mapping was confirmed from inside the deposited files: pepXML `base_name` and the
mzML `sourceFile` are `LASWM_n-aMSMS-10ul` for LNn and `HASWM_n-aMSMS-10ul` for HNn
(L/H = low/high-nutrient ASW medium).

## Caveats / items for curator attention

1. **Data files are mzML, not vendor raw.** The submitter states the Agilent `.d` raw data were not
   available; mzData exports were converted to mzML with OpenMS FileConverter. PRIDE categorises the 6
   mzML files as RAW, so they are the only possible `comment[data file]` values.
2. **Instrument provenance.** The paper's LC-MS/MS paragraph names only the Agilent 1290 Infinity LC;
   the 6550 iFunnel Q-TOF comes from the PRIDE submitter protocol (free text and structured field agree).
   The mzML instrument-model cvParam is empty (mzData export), so it could not be confirmed from data.
3. **Reconcile finding overridden.** `sdrf-tools reconcile` raised BLOCKER `cleavage_agent_unsupported`
   because the PRIDE prose says only "conventional in-gel digestion". Trypsin is retained on the
   evidence of all six deposited pepXML searches (`sample_enzyme name="Trypsin"`, cut KR / not P) and
   the paper's section title "Sample Preparation and Tryptic Digestion"; the independent reviewer
   agreed this is a false positive.
4. `characteristics[growth condition]` is free text (the term is defined for the plants template; no
   NCIT/EFO term captures "ASW + x% tryptone"), used here as the experimental factor.
5. Optional details omitted: precursor selection range m/z 110–3000, 2 missed cleavages allowed.
