## Study
In vitro blood-brain barrier model (hCMEC/D3 endothelial cells + primary human astrocytes) exposed to PVP-stabilised 50 nm silver nanoparticles (AgNP, plasma-corona coated) for 24 h and 48 h; TMT 10-plex, 8 high-pH fractions per plex, Q Exactive HF, Proteome Discoverer 2.1 / Mascot. Khan et al., Nanotoxicology 2019, PMID 30623748 (doi 10.1080/17435390.2018.1540728). Full text was NOT obtainable (publisher blocks, not in PMC), so the annotation rests on the PRIDE record and deposited search results.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics v1.1.0` + `human v1.1.0` (sample-metadata layer is part of them). `cell-lines` deliberately NOT used: half the samples are primary astrocytes and `characteristics[cell line]` forbids both reserved words, so declaring it would force excluding or falsifying astrocyte rows. hCMEC/D3 is carried as cell type `HCMEC/D3 cell` (BTO:0003339, "Human cerebral microvascular endothelial cell line"; Cellosaurus CVCL_U985) with material type `cell line`.

## Scope

Non-RAW files (pdResult, pep.xml, prot.xml) are search outputs, not annotated. Channels 130C and 131 are unused (PD sample type "Blank") and have no rows.

## Channel map (key decision)
Not in PRIDE metadata and paper unavailable, but the deposited `.pdResult` (SQLite, `AnalysisDefinition` StudyDefinition) holds a non-default sample definition, identical in all four sets: 126, 127N, 127C, 128N = SampleType Sample; 128C, 129N, 129C, 130N = Control; 130C, 131 = Blank. Names are generic ("Astro24 - [126]"), so group identity is INFERRED: Sample = AgNP-exposed, Control = unexposed, from PD's ratio "(Sample)/(Control)" in prot.xml and the PRIDE protocol (four biological replicates per condition, unexposed controls). Replicate numbers 1-4 follow channel order within a group (arbitrary; no pairing between groups is claimed). Reviewer: attack this inference hardest.
