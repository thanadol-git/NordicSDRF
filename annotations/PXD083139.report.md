# PXD083139 SDRF annotation report

## Study
N-terminal shedding of the adhesion GPCR GPR125 in GPR125-GFP-overexpressing HEK293T cells: N-/C-terminomics (TrypN vs trypsin) of conditioned medium and lysate, plus a Glu-C buffer comparison (PMID 42827708).

## Templates
ms-proteomics + cell-lines + human v1.1.0

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
66/66 .d.zip runs (timsTOF Pro).

## Sample map
Deposited PRIDE_raw_file_manifest.xlsx (batch, group, code, digestion, fraction, biological and technical replicate per file) and the protocol. A submitter SDRF (GPR125.sdrf.tsv) is deposited; it was used as a cross-check but not copied.

## Key decisions
Rebuilt rather than copying the submitter SDRF: (1) TrypN (Protifi; cleaves N-terminal to K/R) is recorded as LysargiNase MS:1002708 (same specificity) instead of the submitter's Lys-N; (2) medium vs lysate (sample type) and enriched vs global proteome (enrichment process) were added from the manifest and protocol; (3) runs 40114-40119 repeat the codes of 40098-40103 (same samples re-acquired) and are given technical replicates 3-4 to avoid coordinate collisions. Tolerances are not stated in the protocol (the submitter values 40/10-20 ppm are not documented) -> not available.
