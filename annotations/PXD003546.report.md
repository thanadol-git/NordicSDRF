## Scope, evidence and decisions
Status: **annotated**. 4 rows (1 spectra file x 4 iTRAQ channels), 44 columns.

- **Deposited files (ProteomeCentral PROXI, `resultType=full`; Page URL checked as PXD003546):** 2 files.
  - `121019_JG_AHA_1DLC_8plex_ITRAQ_2ul.mzml` (PRIDE:0000404, "Associated raw file URI")
  - `121019_JG_AHA_1DLC_8plex_ITRAQ_2ul_Swissprot.group.xml` (ProteinPilot search export)

  The PX record is flagged "Unsupported dataset by repository" (a partial submission). No vendor .wiff is deposited: the one spectra file is a peak-list mzML, so it is the `comment[data file]` value (CI advisory `peak_list_data_file`). The campaign cache said "1 RAW / 2 files"; this matches, but the one "RAW" is the mzML. The PRIDE v3 `files/all` endpoint and the FTP/HTTPS listing were both unreachable today (upstream connect error, timeout), so the file list rests on the PX record alone.
- **Deposited vs annotated:** the 1 spectra file is annotated, with 4 rows. The group.xml is a search output, not a run.
- **Channel to sample map (STATED, Results, PMC5079677):** "iTRAQ reagents 113 and 114 were used to label the 2 DMSO-treated control samples, while iTRAQ reagents 116 and 117 were used to label the AHA-incorporated de novo proteins". The replicate-consistency ratios 114:113 and 117:116 confirm the pairs. Biological replicates are stated ("Biological replicates of AHA- or DMSO-treated samples were included"; "both biological replicates").
  - 113 = DMSO rep1, 114 = DMSO rep2, 116 = AHA rep1, 117 = AHA rep2. Numbering within each pair follows reagent order; it is a naming convention, and the conditions do not depend on it.
- **8plex reagents, 4 channels used:** the 8plex chemistry is shown by the file name (`8plex_ITRAQ`) and by the PX modification MOD:01549 (iTRAQ8plex), and 113 exists only in 8plex. The paper names only 113/114/116/117, so 115/118/119/121 get no rows: no sample was assigned to them.
- **Samples:** HeLa (ATCC CCL-2, stated) is CVCL_0030 (parental HeLa, not S3). Donor fields come from Cellosaurus CVCL_0030: female, 30Y6M, African American; adult is derived from the age. Disease is `cervical adenocarcinoma` (DOID:3702); Cellosaurus gives NCIt C27677 "HPV-related endocervical adenocarcinoma", and MONDO:0016275 is obsolete. Culture medium is DMEM (stated; NCIT:C185409).
- **Treatment:** both arms had 30 min L-methionine-free DMEM with 10% dialyzed FBS, then 2 h amino-acid-free medium to induce autophagy. The AHA arm got 50 uM L-azidohomoalanine and the control arm got DMSO. Both arms were then click-reacted with alkyne-biotin, enriched on streptavidin beads and digested on-bead. This is encoded as free-text `characteristics[treatment]` and `factor value[treatment]`. The free text needs the `sample-metadata` template; templates are declared on all rows.
- **Prep (stated):** TCEP 65 C 60 min, MMTS 15 min, trypsin (Promega) overnight, iTRAQ in 0.5 M TEAB. SCX on the iTRAQ Method Development Kit was used only for clean-up (stated "to remove any contaminants"), and the file name says 1DLC. So there is no fractionation: fraction identifier = 1 and no fractionation-method column.
- **MS (stated):** Eksigent NanoLC-Ultra, 75 um x 150 mm Reprosil-Pur C18, 300 nl/min. TripleTOF 5600 (paper and PX agree), DDA (top 20 precursors per cycle, 15 s dynamic exclusion), MS1 350-1250 m/z, MS2 100-1800 m/z, rolling collision energy.
- **Search (stated):** ProteinPilot 4.5 Paragon against SwissProt v2015.9 (40,406 entries), "Cysteine alkylation with MMTS; trypsin digestion; TripleTOF 5600; biological modifications", PSPEP decoy FDR.
  - Modifications encoded: Methylthio C (UNIMOD:39) fixed (MMTS), iTRAQ8plex K and any N-term (UNIMOD:730) fixed.

## Values left not available / not applicable / omitted
- `precursor/fragment mass tolerance` = not available: ProteinPilot Paragon takes no user tolerances, and the paper states none.
- `individual` = not available: HeLa has no donor identifier beyond the cell line.
- `collision energy` is omitted: only "rolling collision energy" is stated, with no value.
- The PX-listed modifications MOD:00398 (carbamoylated residue) and MOD:00425 (monohydroxylated residue) are omitted. They come from ProteinPilot's "biological modifications" ID-focus probabilistic search, which has no fixed/variable declaration and no stated residues. The group.xml could not be read to give them sites.
- `passage number` and `sample storage temperature` are not used.

## Stated vs INFERRED
- STATED: channel map, conditions, biological duplicates, cell line and source, medium, reagents, enzyme, instrument, LC/MS ranges, search software.
- INFERRED:
  - `dissociation method` = beam-type CID (MS:1000422): this is how the TripleTOF 5600 collision cell fragments, and the paper does not name the method.
  - `ms2 mass analyzer` = time-of-flight, from the instrument.
  - Fixed/variable status of Methylthio and iTRAQ8plex: ProteinPilot applies the Cys alkylation and the iTRAQ label as special factors. Treating them as fixed follows the usual campaign encoding.
  - Replicate numbering within each condition pair.
