# PXD041619 BLOCKED: TMTpro16 channel-to-sample maps not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Department of Clinical Science, University of Bergen (lab PI Simona Chera / Heike Scholz). Human pancreatic islets from donors, TMTpro16 (three plexes, 12 non-adjacent high-pH super-fractions each: 36 RAW `ea11986…ea12090`), Orbitrap Eclipse, in-house Comet pipeline; 36 `.mzIdentML`. Paper: Mathisen AF et al., Acta Physiol 2023, PMID 37621186, DOI 10.1111/apha.14037, PMC10592125 (full text not available through Europe PMC).

## Why it cannot be annotated
Neither the PRIDE record nor any reachable source says which donor/islet preparation is in which TMTpro channel of which plex, nor which RAW files belong to which plex (36 files = 3 x 12 assumed). No sample sheet is deposited (only `.raw` and `.mzIdentML`). The paper's full text could not be read (Europe PMC returns a stub). Assigning channels would be invention.

## Verified facts for a future annotation
Homo sapiens pancreatic islets; TMTpro16 (UNIMOD:2016); basic-pH RP fractionation (96 -> 24 super-fractions, 12 analysed); Orbitrap Eclipse; Comet: TMTpro on K/N-term static, UniProt human (March 2021). Possible unblock: paper supplementary table or author contact (simona.chera@uib.no).
