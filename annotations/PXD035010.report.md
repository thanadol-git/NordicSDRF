## Limitations

- The sample-to-condition mapping (what the letters P and S, and the suffix 'p' in 05p/06p, mean) is not in PRIDE, the deposit or the paper; treatment, factor value and group are therefore not annotated. The deposited community SDRF in the PRIDE file list was deliberately not consulted. The paper states two proteomic experiments of three LDR primed and three unirradiated control sEV samples; the 2017 series has only 4 sample codes (01, 03, 05p, 06p) and the 2019 series 6 (S1-3, P1-3), so the group assignment and which codes belong to which experiment are not decodable.
- Biological replicate numbers are ordinal from run names (Hanson digit; NinaEdin 1-4 by file order) and do not come from a mapping file.
- 191220_Hanson_P2.raw and 191220_Hanson_S2_191221023909.raw (and P3.raw / S3_191221045407.raw) are copies of the same acquisition (identical size and sampled bytes; internal acquisition name is the S*_timestamp file, vial RC5/RC6). They are listed as technical replicates 1 and 2 only to cover every file once; they are not independent injections. Full-file checksums were not obtained (download too slow); identity rests on equal size, identical 20 MB blocks at 7 offsets (0 to 750 MB) and identical headers.
- Instrument conflict: RAW methods show a Q Exactive (HCD, NCE 28, MS2 resolution 17500) whereas the paper and PRIDE protocol text state LTQ-Orbitrap XL with ion-trap CID. The RAW header was preferred; the paper description may be inherited text from older work.
- Paper says each gel lane was cut into 12 slices, but only one run (Hanson) or two same-vial repeat runs (NinaEdin) per sample are deposited; fraction identifier is therefore 1 for all and the gel-slice fractionation is not annotated.
- Age 54Y and sex female are Cellosaurus attributes of T-47D, not sample-specific; organism part and cell type are not available (sEV preparations from cultured cells); culture medium, passage number and reduction/alkylation reagents are not stated and omitted or not available. The sEV isolation (membrane affinity kit) is described in the paper but enrichment process is not annotated as no ontology term was verified.
- Acetyl N-terminal modification annotated as Any N-term (peptide level in the mzid), not Protein N-term. Search settings were read from the two deposited PEAKS mzid files, which are identical in parameters.
- SANDBOX ONLY (independent review FAIL): 191220_Hanson_P2.raw and 191220_Hanson_P3.raw carry embedded acquisition paths of the S2/S3 timestamped runs and are same-size copies (P2 first 4 MB byte-identical to S2_191221023909), so they are probably mislabelled S-run copies and the real P2/P3 acquisitions are probably not deposited; listing them as technical replicates 1 and 2 of Hanson_P2/P3 presents S-labelled data as P samples and one acquisition as two injections. P/S meaning remains undecoded and biological replicate numbers are ordinal. Instrument (Q Exactive, HCD NCE 28) was confirmed from RAW headers; the paper/PRIDE text (LTQ-Orbitrap XL, CID) is the wrong source.
