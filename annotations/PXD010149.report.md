# PXD010149 annotation report

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

## Result

50-row, 29-column SDRF (`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `human` v1.1.0), one row per RAW file. 24 patient sources, serum, label-free DDA on a Q Exactive Plus, 2 injections per patient (technical replicates 1-2) plus two extra runs for patient 628 (technical replicates 3-4). Not previously annotated (no PXD010149 directory in sdrf-annotated-datasets).

Study: Snipsøyr et al., Int J Infect Dis 2020 (PMID 32087365), serum proteomics in definite infective endocarditis (IE) versus excluded IE.

## Design

- definite infective endocarditis (C): 47, 162, 170, 178, 249, 289, 296, 373, 513, 566, 628, 761 (12 patients, `characteristics[disease]` = infective endocarditis, MONDO:0000565).
- excluded infective endocarditis (K): 50, 111, 156, 167, 407, 427, 434, 493, 537, 619, 638 (11 patients with raw files). `characteristics[disease]` = not available (suspected IE, diagnosis not stated).
- Patient 439 has raw files but is not on the case list; the list has 634 (K) with no raw files. Possible transcription mismatch; 439 is `not available`. Needs submitter or paper confirmation.

## Technical values

- Instrument: Q Exactive Plus (protocol text); PRIDE field says generic Q Exactive.
- Trypsin (single protease; in-gel digest). MaxQuant enzyme Trypsin/P, 2 missed cleavages.
- Modifications from deposited parameters/summary: Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed; Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (protein N-term) variable.
- Fragment tolerance 20 ppm (MaxQuant FTMS). Precursor tolerance and dissociation not stated: `not available`.

## Conservative choices and caveats

- Organism part is blood serum (title, protocol), overriding the PRIDE field "Blood plasma".
- Age and sex: not available (no per-sample data in the deposit).
- No depletion/enrichment column: depletion and ProteoMiner are described for the 2D-PAGE arm; the LC-MS arm is unclear.
- Extra 628 runs (`628_01_150420210739.raw`, `628_02_150420220915.raw`) are recorded as technical replicates 3 and 4; their purpose is not stated.
- Deposited MaxQuant version is 1.5.2.8, paper says 1.5.5.1. Parameters come from the deposit.
- Raw files were not inspected (no full downloads).
- Factor value has no matching characteristics column (score advisory) because control disease is unknown.
