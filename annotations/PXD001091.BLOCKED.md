# PXD001091 — annotation blocked

PXD001091 deposits 57 vendor RAW files for synthetic peptides spiked at different levels into a trypsin-digested *Streptococcus pyogenes* lysate. The raw-file names group measurements under `dilA_1` through `dilA_12`, but PRIDE provides no table giving the peptide identities or quantities associated with those dilution codes.

The spike identity and amount are the study's experimental factor. Recording arbitrary dilution codes as quantitative metadata would not be evidence-backed, so no SDRF is emitted.

Evidence checked: PRIDE PXD001091 project record, protocols, and complete file manifest.

Unblock with the dilution design/sample sheet identifying the spiked peptides and their quantities for every `dilA` group.
