## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- HCD written as MS:1000422 (beam-type collision-induced dissociation), the spec-canonical term.
- The B/G/L → group mapping is an inference from group sizes (unique solution), not stated in the deposit — highest-risk call.
- Per-subject age/sex not available (cohort summaries only).
- The deposit mixes DDA (library) and DIA runs. Following the repo convention, the combined PXD023708.sdrf.tsv is kept together with PXD023708-dda.sdrf.tsv (65 rows) and PXD023708-dia.sdrf.tsv (70 rows), with technical replicates renumbered within each split; sdrf-tools structure flags the combined file only.
- Acetylation site (protein N-term) assumed from Spectronaut defaults; PRIDE says only 'acetylation'.
- Early-stage OA is the authors' label for degenerative meniscal tear patients; disease annotated as 'osteoarthritis, knee' for both OA groups.
- Possibly empty or truncated runs are annotated because they are deposited: 190319_DIA_L441_nr1.raw (230 KB), 190401_DIA_pooled_SF_10.raw (0.24 GB; re-acquired as 190401_DIA_pooled_SF_10_20190406122152 and 190402_DIA_pooled_SF_10), and both 190405_DDA_pooled_SF_12 files (~0.5 GB each).
- The paper says 18 samples were run in duplicate; the deposit has 20 DIA _rep2 pairs, and the SDRF follows the files.
- sdrf-tools reconcile flags disease on the control arm; control (G*) rows carry disease = normal, so this is a tool false positive.
