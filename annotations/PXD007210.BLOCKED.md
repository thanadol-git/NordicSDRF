# PXD007210 — blocked: no cell-line/miRNA and assay mapping for RAW files

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD007210
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD007210/files/all

## Evidence available

PRIDE describes human MDA-MB-231 and MCF-10A cell-line proteomics after transient miR-200c expression or miR-203 inhibition, acquired on an LTQ Orbitrap. The manifest contains 125 vendor RAW files across several acquisition families, including `BCLisbon_*`, `Emila_Lisbon_*`, reruns, and a synthetic-peptide pool.

## Blocking gap

The PRIDE project has no linked publication, DOI, or deposited sample annotation. It does not map individual RAW aliases to cell line, miRNA perturbation, biological replicate, technical replicate, discovery versus SRM analysis, rerun status, or synthetic standard. These critical assignments cannot be inferred from the filenames.

## Unblock requirement

Provide a complete sample/run sheet linking every RAW file to cell line, miRNA condition, replicate, and assay role, with out-of-scope standards/reruns identified.
