## Study
"Confident identification of citrullinated peptides from P gingivalis" (Hojrup lab, SDU). Larsen et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 2019, PMID 31754044, doi:10.1074/mcp.ra119.001700. Outer membrane vesicles (OMV) of three P. gingivalis strains (wild type, PPAD active-site mutant C351A, PPAD knockout), 2D HFBA fractionation, label-free DDA on Q Exactive HF. The paper full text could not be retrieved (EPMC 500, PMC reCAPTCHA, publisher 403); the PRIDE record, raw-file headers, Data_Overview.docx and deposited X!Tandem XMLs were the evidence.

## Templates and why
- `ms-proteomics`: MS technology. No organism template fits a bacterium.
- `clinical-metadata`: chosen ONLY because it is the template that licenses `characteristics[genotype]` (needed to encode WT / C351A / knockout) without adding required columns beyond the base. This is a judgement call; it is not a clinical study. `cell-lines` rejected (no cell line).

## Key decisions and conflicts
- Instrument: raw headers say Q Exactive HF; PRIDE field says "Q Exactive". Raw wins.
- Modifications read from deposited X!Tandem XML input parameters (5 files sampled, identical): Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Deamidated N/Q/R variable (R deamidation = citrullination, all UNIMOD:7). Written as three Deamidated columns.
- Precursor tolerance 10 ppm (XMLs and protocol agree). Fragment tolerance `not available`: protocol says 0.02 Da, deposited XMLs say 0.10 Da (4 files) and 0.20 Da (1 file).
- Data_Overview.docx file ranges overlap/contradict for the QEHF3 series; raw-header sample IDs (WT_T1/T2/T3) were used instead.
- WT_T3_Rep2 QEHF3_06504-06513 (header "WT_T3_Frac01-10") treated as technical replicate 2 of fractions 1-10 (INFERRED).
- `fractionation method` = generic HPLC (PRIDE:0000565); no HFBA-specific term was verified.
