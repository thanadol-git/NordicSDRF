## Design and file mapping

Two samples, one LC-MS/MS run each, no fractions and no replicates:

| source name | genotype (factor) | RAW file |
|---|---|---|
| HAP1 control | wild type | H03892_5911_EXT392_ArnesenLab_TEST_SCX_RT_WT.raw |
| HAP1 NAA80 KO1 | NAA80 knockout | H03892_5911_EXT392_ArnesenLab_TEST_SCX_RT_KO.raw |

- The factor is `factor value[genotype]`, mirroring `characteristics[genotype]`.
- How the mapping was established: the SI Methods name exactly two samples, "HAP1 control and NAA80 KO1 cells", one injection each. The run-name suffixes `_WT` and `_KO` match them. The deposited Mascot `.dat` headers confirm it: their `FILE=` lines tie searches F134361 and F134362 to the WT raw file, and F134281 and F134282 to the KO raw file.
- The deposit's other 12 files are Mascot `.dat` files, mzIdentML exports and MGF peak lists, all derived from these two runs. They are not SDRF rows.
- Cell-line identity:
  - The control is parental HAP1 (Horizon C631), Cellosaurus CVCL_Y019.
  - KO1 is Cellosaurus CVCL_SZ97, "HAP1 NAT6 (-) 1". Its synonym is "NAT6 knockout cell line 17bp deletion", and it is a CRISPR knockout of NAA80 (HGNC:30252). This matches SI Fig. S2, where KO1 was made "by deleting 17 bp from exon 2". KO2 (404 bp insertion, CVCL_SZ98) was not measured by MS.
- Technical values were taken from the deposited Mascot parameters (tolerances and modifications), the SI and PRIDE protocols (DDA top-16, 28% NCE, scan ranges, trypsin), and the RAW headers (instrument).

## Limitations

- **comment[label]**: `label free sample` is inferred, as explained in the reconcile notes above. A reviewer may prefer another representation of the AcDC labelling, but no PRIDE label term exists for it.
- **Heavy N-terminal acetylation**: the variable modification "Acetyl:2H(3)C13(2) (N-term)" (+47.036 Da), the heavy component of the quantitation, is not listed. Unimod has no 13C2D3-acetyl entry, and no verified PSI-MOD N-terminal term was found.
- **Heavy lysine acetylation**: the fixed modification on K uses PSI-MOD `MOD:01965` ("2xC(13),3x(2)H labeled N6-acetyl-L-lysine", DiffMono 47.036106) because Unimod has no entry for it. The Mascot `.dat` files give 47.036105.
- **Two search passes in one row**: the modification list is the union of the two deposited Mascot passes. One pass used Gln->pyro-Glu (N-term Q); the other used light and heavy N-terminal Acetyl for quantitation. Both used Oxidation (M) as variable and Carbamidomethyl (C) plus heavy acetyl (K) as fixed.
- **Cleavage agent**: Trypsin comes from the protocol. The search enzyme was semiArgC/P, because lysines were blocked before digestion.
- **Dissociation method**: HCD is inferred from the Q Exactive HF and the "normalized collision energy of 28%". The paper does not name the method.
- **Donor values from Cellosaurus**: age 40Y, sex male, organism part and sampling site bone marrow, and disease are values for the parental HAP1/KBM-7 donor. Only "male" and "chronic myelogenous leukemia" are also stated in the SI.
- **KO1 to CVCL_SZ97 mapping**: this is inferred from the identical 17 bp exon-2 deletion. The paper says the authors "generated" the KO lines and does not cite a Horizon catalogue number. The SI does say "NAA80-KO and wild-type HAP1 cells (Horizon C631)", which supports `biorepository` = Horizon Discovery for both rows.
- **cell line**: `HAP-1` is the EFO label for the parental line. No ontology term exists for the knockout clone, which is identified by its Cellosaurus accession and name.
- **Unavailable values**: cell type, ancestry category and passage number are `not available`. Culture medium (IMDM with 10% FBS and 1% penicillin/streptomycin) is omitted because no NCIT term was found in OLS. Developmental stage is omitted.
- **Reagents not recorded**: reduction and alkylation reagents are not stated in this record (the protocol cites PMID 28315243), so they are omitted.
- **Replicates**: biological and technical replicate are both 1. There is one run per genotype and no replication.
- **Enrichment**: the SCX enrichment of N-terminally blocked peptides is not recorded in a column. It is a single enrichment step, not fractionation.
