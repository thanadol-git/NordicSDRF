## Study
Middle-Down Proteomic Analyses with Ion Mobility Separations of Endogenous Isomeric Proteoforms (Shliaha et al., Anal Chem 2020, PMID 31935065, doi:10.1021/acs.analchem.9b05011). Mouse embryonic stem cells (2i-LIF), H3 histone family purified by RP-HPLC, tails cleaved with Glu-C, WCX-HILIC ETD MS/MS with FAIMS on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos. The paper is closed access and its Methods were not read; PRIDE protocol text, RAW headers and the deposited Mascot reports are the evidence.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics v1.1.0` + `vertebrates v1.1.0` (mouse). `cell-lines` was not used: the protocol names no cell line (only "mouse embryonic stem cells"), and that template requires a cell line identity. No clinical/oncology template (no disease). Not human, not DIA, not labelled.

## Scope (deposited vs annotated)
27 files: 9 RAW, 9 Mascot report CSVs (FAIMS_1..9.csv, one per RAW, confirmed through the "Peak list data path" in each), 9 proteoformquant CSVs. All 9 RAW files are annotated (9 rows x 37 columns). The 18 CSVs are result files and carry no sample rows. No exclusions.

## Key decisions
- One source ("mESC histone H3 N-terminal tail"), biological replicate 1; the 9 RAW runs are separate injections of it and differ only in the FAIMS compensation-voltage pair in the filename (e.g. CV22or62). Encoded as `comment[technical replicate]` 1-9 (in sorted file-name order, which is an arbitrary ordering). The CV setting has no template column and is only recoverable from the file name. No factor value column was added: the CV is an instrument setting, not a sample variable.
- Modifications read from the Mascot 2.6.0 report headers (identical in all 9): variable Acetyl(K), Acetyl(N-term) (PP=Any N-term), Methyl(K/R), Dimethyl(K/R), Trimethyl(K). No fixed modifications; no reduction/alkylation reagent is stated, columns left out.
- Tolerances 2.05 Da precursor / 0.02 Da fragment from the Mascot header.
- Instrument from RAW header strings (all 9 "Orbitrap Fusion Lumos"), agreeing with PRIDE.
- Dissociation ETD (protocol "All ETD spectra"; Mascot instrument type ETD_deconvoluted).
- Cleavage agent glutamyl endopeptidase (MS:1001917) per protocol and Mascot "Glu_C".
