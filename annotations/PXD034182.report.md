# PXD034182 annotation report

Abdominal subcutaneous (SAT) and visceral (VAT) adipose tissue proteomes from people with severe obesity before and 6 months after bariatric surgery, and from non-obese controls. Paper: Am J Physiol Endocrinol Metab 2025 (not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 144 rows. All 144 raw files map one-to-one to the deposited `PXD034182_sample_list.xlsx`, which gives the group, case ID, time point (baseline or 6 months after surgery) and fat depot.

## Values
- **Disease:** `morbid obesity` for the Sleeve1 and Sleeve2 obese groups; `normal` for the non-obese SAT controls and the visceral-fat controls. The original group label is kept in `characteristics[group]`. PRIDE lists type 2 diabetes, but per-patient diabetes status is not deposited.
- **Organism part:** subcutaneous abdominal adipose tissue or visceral abdominal adipose tissue. Age and sex are `not available`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF, per raw headers (56 of 144 checked). HCD.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.6.5.0, run separately for SAT and VAT. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: one finding, a false positive: `organism_part_contradicted` objects that `visceral abdominal adipose tissue` is more specific than the title's "adipose tissue".
