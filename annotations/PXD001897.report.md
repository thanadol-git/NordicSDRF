# PXD001897 annotation report

Plate-based secretome method for filamentous fungi: *Trichoderma reesei* (*Hypocrea jecorina*) QM6a grown on Sigmacell cellulose, sugarcane bagasse, spruce wood, birch wood or glucose. NMBU, Ås. Paper: J Proteomics 2015, PMID 26477388 (not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 15 rows, one per raw file.
- **Substrate key:** the deposited MaxQuant `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` maps the numbered runs:
  - `2013101607–09` = Sigma1–3 (Sigmacell cellulose)
  - `10–12` = Bag1–3 (bagasse)
  - `13–15` = Spruce1–3
  - `16–18` = Birch1–3
  - `20140131_QM6A_1–3` = Gluc1–3 (glucose)
- **Replicates:** the trailing digit is the biological replicate.
- **Glucose runs:** they are in the design but have no LFQ column in the deposited `proteinGroups.txt`; they are annotated as deposited.

## Values
- **Sample:** organism `Trichoderma reesei`, strain `QM6a`, material `cell` (secretome collected from cell-free agar beneath the membrane).
- **Factor:** `growth substrate`.
- **Not encoded:** the PRIDE protocol mentions sampling after 2, 3, 4, 7 or 28 days, but which day applies to the comparative runs is not stated in the deposit. The paper is closed access.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.4.1.2):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ and Gln→pyro-Glu variable.
  - FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags trypsin as not named. This is a false positive: the PRIDE protocol says "trypsination" or "trypsinated", a spelling the checker does not match.
