# PXD005970 — annotation blocked

Human bronchial epithelial cells exposed for 13 weeks to two low doses of multi-walled carbon nanotubes (STAMI / PROMEC; Orbitrap Elite). Paper: Nanotoxicology 2018 (PMID 29350075), not open access.

## Why it is blocked
The 24 runs are named `STAMI_01`–`16`, `C1`–`C4` and `T1`–`T4`. The deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` repeats these codes. The open sources (PRIDE record and abstract) say only that there are two doses plus controls. Nothing maps the codes to dose, control or replicate, and the cell line is not named.

## What would unblock it
The paper's Methods or a sample sheet giving the cell line, the dose per code and the meaning of C/T versus 01–16.
