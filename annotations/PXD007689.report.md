## Templates and why

- **ms-proteomics v1.1.0:** the data are Thermo DDA LC-MS/MS runs on a Q Exactive HF and an Orbitrap Fusion.
- **vertebrates v1.1.0:** the samples are freshly isolated islets of Langerhans from *Rattus norvegicus* (Wistar) pups. This template adds the developmental stage, strain and sex columns.
- **Not chosen:**
  - cell-lines: the islets are primary tissue cultured for 9–10 days, not a cell line.
  - clinical-metadata: there are no patients.
  - dia-acquisition: every RAW method is data-dependent (QE HF Top-N; Fusion "OT-OT_MSMS_TopN_3sec").

## Design and file mapping

- **Comparison:** islets were stimulated with 20 mM glucose for 0 min (control, nontreated), 5 min, 10 min and 15 min. There are two biological replicates (`R1`, `R2` in the file names). Each replicate is one iTRAQ 4-plex pool of the four time points.
- **Channel map (from the paper Methods):** "Two biological replicates were made and labeling was performed as follows: control 114, 5 min glucose 115, 10 min glucose 116, and 15 min glucose 117."
  - In the SDRF: `iTRAQ114` = 0 minute, `iTRAQ115` = 5 minute, `iTRAQ116` = 10 minute, `iTRAQ117` = 15 minute.
  - The same map is applied to both replicates and all three peptide classes, as the Methods describe one labelling scheme.
  - The deposited MS-GF+/MASIC `Merged.tsv` files carry reporter columns `Ion_114`–`Ion_117` only. This was checked in nonmodified, phospho, multi-phospho and sialylation outputs, and confirms the 4-plex. Those files carry no sample names.
- **Sources:** 8 sources, named `rat islets <t> min glucose replicate <n>`. `characteristics[biological replicate]` is 1 or 2 within each time point.
- **Factor value:** `factor value[sampling time]` = `0 minute`, `5 minute`, `10 minute` or `15 minute`. Each value has 63 rows.
- **Peptide classes:** the modified TiSH protocol splits each labelled pool into three classes.
  - **Nonmodified peptides:** the TiO2 flow-through, HILIC-fractionated. 11 runs per replicate, `Nonmodification_R*_QHF*.raw`, on the Q Exactive HF.
  - **Mono-phosphopeptides:** HILIC-fractionated. 13 runs per replicate, `Phosphoryation_R*_FUS*.raw`, on the Orbitrap Fusion. There is also one multi-phosphopeptide run per replicate, `Phosphoryation_R1_QHF0903.raw` and `Phosphoryation_R2_QHF0904.raw`, on the Q Exactive HF.
  - **Formerly sialylated N-linked glycopeptides:** deglycosylated with PNGase F + Sialidase A, then HILIC-fractionated. 7 runs in R1 (`FUS04122`–`04128`) and 6 in R2 (`FUS04130`–`04135`), on the Orbitrap Fusion. There is no `FUS04129` in the deposit.
  - All 63 deposited RAW files are annotated (R1: 32, R2: 31).
- **Fraction numbering:** all runs of one replicate are fractions of the same labelled pool, so `comment[fraction identifier]` runs continuously within each replicate. The order is nonmodified (by run number), then mono-phospho HILIC (by run number), then the multi-phospho run, then sialylation (by run number). This gives 1–32 for R1 and 1–31 for R2. The peptide class of each row is given by `characteristics[enrichment process]` and `comment[fractionation method]`.
- **RAW headers:** the first 64 KB of every one of the 63 RAW files was read with HTTP range requests.
  - All `QHF*` files say "Q Exactive HF - Orbitrap MS" and all `FUS*` files say "Orbitrap Fusion" (host `BMB-FUSION`).
  - In every file, the original acquisition file name (`F:\Data\QHF….raw` or `D:\FUS….raw`) matches the deposited name, so no files were renamed or swapped.
  - The method names show HCD on both instruments: `2Column_*_iTRAQ_HCD30` on the QE HF and `*_OT-OT_MSMS_TopN_3sec_HCD40` on the Fusion. The gradient lengths run 43 → 103 → 73 → 43 min across each fraction series, which is consistent with the run-number order being the HILIC fraction order.

## Technical values and their sources

- **Instrument:** set per row from the RAW headers: `NT=Q Exactive HF;AC=MS:1002523` (24 files, 96 rows) or `NT=Orbitrap Fusion;AC=MS:1002416` (39 files, 156 rows). The paper mentions only a Q Exactive HF. PRIDE lists "Orbitrap Fusion" and "Q Exactive", and the RAW headers resolve the latter to Q Exactive HF.
- **Modifications:** read from the `AnalysisProtocolCollection` of three deposited mzIdentML files (MS-GF+ Beta v10089), one per class: `Nonmodification_R2_QHF0966`, `Phosphorylation_R2_FUS04121` and `Sialylation_R2_FUS04130`.
  - Carbamidomethyl (C) is Fixed.
  - iTRAQ4plex (K) and iTRAQ4plex (N-term) are **Variable** (`fixedMod="false"`). The paper says "fixed" for the iTRAQ tags, but the deposited search wins.
  - Oxidation (M) and Phospho (S, T, Y) are Variable.
  - The sialylation search adds Deamidated (N, Q) Variable. That column is `not applicable` on the nonmodified and phospho rows. In the first 400 KB of `Sialylation_R2_FUS04134Merged.tsv`, 536 of 993 PSMs carry +0.984.
- **Cleavage agents:** two `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns on every row, `NT=Trypsin;AC=MS:1001251` and `NT=Lys-C;AC=MS:1001309`. The Methods say the lysates were "predigested … with 2 μl Lys-C (Wako) … for 2 h", followed by "digestion with 2% (w/w) trypsin (Promega) overnight". The search enzyme was trypsin. Peptide C-termini cannot separate Lys-C from trypsin here, because both cut after K.
- **Tolerances:** precursor 20 ppm, from the mzid `ParentTolerance` (±20 ppm) and the PRIDE protocol. The paper text says ±10 ppm; the deposited search wins. Fragment tolerance is `not available`, because MS-GF+ sets it implicitly through `Instrument=HighRes`.
- **Other technical values:**
  - Dissociation is HCD, `NT=beam-type collision-induced dissociation;AC=MS:1000422`, from the RAW method names, the mzid `FragmentMethod=HCD` and the paper.
  - The MS2 analyzer is Orbitrap (`MS:1000484`), from the paper (MS2 at 30,000 FWHM on the QE HF) and the Fusion method summary ("MSn Level = 2, Detector Type = Orbitrap").
  - The acquisition method is DDA (`PRIDE:0000627`).
  - The reduction reagent is DTT (`PRIDE:0000608`) and the alkylation reagent is IAA (`PRIDE:0000599`), both from the paper Methods.
  - Fractionation is `NT=Hydrophilic interaction chromatography (HILIC);AC=PRIDE:0000554` on HILIC runs, and `not applicable` on the two multi-phospho runs.

## Post-processing after `build` (documented)

`build` takes one fraction list per source, so it cannot vary values by file within that list, and it emits a single `comment[cleavage agent details]` column. The SDRF was therefore built with placeholder cells and then finished by `scratchpad/PXD007689/postprocess.py`. That script does two things:

1. **Fills four cells per row from the data file:**
   - `characteristics[enrichment process]`: `not applicable` for nonmodified runs, `enrichment of phosphorylated protein` for phospho runs, and `enrichment of glycopeptides` for sialylation runs.
   - `comment[instrument]`: from the RAW header.
   - `comment[fractionation method]`: HILIC, or `not applicable` for the multi-phospho runs.
   - The last `comment[modification parameters]` column: Deamidated for sialylation runs, `not applicable` otherwise.
2. **Inserts a second `comment[cleavage agent details]` column** (Lys-C) directly after the first.

A diff check against the `build` output confirmed that only the 1,008 placeholder cells (252 × 4) changed and exactly one column was inserted.

## Limitations
- **Fractions and sources (revised after independent review):** `comment[fraction identifier]` restarts at 1 within each biological replicate and peptide group, in run order (nonmodified 1-11, mono-phospho 1-13, sialylated glyco 1-7 / 1-6); the unfractionated multi-phospho runs QHF0903/QHF0904 are fraction 1. Each peptide group is its own fractionation series of the same labelled pool, so the group is appended to the source name (`... nonmodified`, `monophospho`, `multiphospho`, `glyco`; 32 sources), as in PXD003312 from the same lab. The earlier draft numbered fractions continuously across groups (1-32 / 1-31) with 8 sources.



- **Channel map:** taken from the paper Methods (one sentence) and applied to both replicates and all peptide classes. No deposited file names the samples per channel; the result files carry generic `Ion_114`–`Ion_117` columns.
- **Fraction order:** inferred from run-number order within each peptide class. The gradient-length pattern in the RAW method names supports this order, but no deposited table gives HILIC fraction numbers.
- **Multi-phosphopeptide runs:** the claim that `QHF0903` and `QHF0904` are the unfractionated multi-phosphopeptide (SIMAC basic eluate) runs is inferred. They are the only Q Exactive HF runs in the phospho class (one per replicate), they use a different non-HILIC method (`2Column_133min_HT_Top10_AGC1e5`, `2Column_73min_Top10_200ms`), and the paper says multi-phosphopeptides went to "direct LC-MS/MS analysis" without HILIC. Their search output contains iTRAQ-labelled phosphopeptides.
- **Sex:** `not available`. The paper and MeSH headings (Animals, Newborn; Rats, Wistar) do not report pup sex, and each sample is islets pooled from many pups (~1,500 islets per condition).
- **Age and developmental stage:**
  - `characteristics[age]` = `4D-5D` is the pups' age at islet isolation ("4 to 5-days-old Wistar rat pups"). The islets were then cultured for 9–10 days in vitro before stimulation; that culture period is not encoded.
  - `characteristics[developmental stage]` = `neonate` (EFO:0001372) is the donor stage, from the same sentence.
- **Biological replicates:** each one is a separate islet preparation. The paper does not give the pup numbers per pool.
- **Compound and dose:** `glucose` and `20 mM` on the 5, 10 and 15 min rows; `not applicable` on the 0 min control rows, which the Figure 1 legend calls "nontreated islets".
- **Collision energy:** not encoded. The method names say HCD30 (QE HF iTRAQ methods) and HCD40 (Fusion), while the paper says "collision energy: 32V" for the QE HF. The two multi-phospho methods do not name a collision energy.
- **Enrichment process for sialylation rows:** free text, because no ontology term exists. The nonmodified rows (TiO2 flow-through) are `not applicable`.
- **mzid search settings:** read from one file per peptide class, not all 63 (each read needed a bisecting range search into a 28–110 MB file). The Deamidated assignment for every sialylation run assumes that all runs in a class share one search.
- **Instrument per file:** read from all 63 RAW headers, so it is not inferred from the file prefix.
- **Cell type:** omitted. The samples are whole islets (mixed endocrine cell types); the paper's "β-cell" wording describes the target biology, not a sorted population.
