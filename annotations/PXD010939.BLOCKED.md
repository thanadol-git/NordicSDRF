# PXD010939 — blocked

## Exact blocker

The study uses three TMT10-plex sets for 27 individual ovarian-cyst-fluid samples plus a common pooled reference, but no checked source provides the per-set reporter-channel-to-sample map. The 136 deposited RAW files also cannot be mapped safely to the three plexes and their ordered fractions/reanalyses from filenames alone. Creating an SDRF would therefore require inventing both biological channel assignments and part of the run/plex/fraction map.

## Evidence checked

- [PRIDE project PXD010939](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD010939): the project description defines three groups (nine benign serous, nine type 1 ovarian cancer, and nine type 2 ovarian cancer samples). The sample-processing protocol says that three samples from each group were pooled as a common reference, that the 27 samples were distributed over three TMT10-plex sets, that each set was fractionated into 17 fractions, and that fractions were analyzed repeatedly on Q Exactive and Orbitrap Fusion instruments. It does not assign any individual sample or disease class to a reporter channel or identify which RAW files belong to which biological plex/fraction coordinate.
- [Publication DOI 10.1016/j.jprot.2019.01.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jprot.2019.01.017), PMID 30710757: Europe PMC exposes the abstract but no full text or supplementary files. The available publication metadata confirms the 9/9/9 group design but contains no channel table.
- Complete [PRIDE file inventory](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD010939/files): 136 RAW files were enumerated. Their instrument/date/counter names do not encode a sample, TMT set, or unambiguous fraction number.
- Deposited `1788_QEandFusion_toPRIDE_FDR1_PEPTIDES.xlsx`, `1972set12runs141129_141219_FDR1_PEPTIDES.xlsx`, and `1972set22runs141205141219_FDR1_PEPTIDES.xlsx`: all three compact files were downloaded and inspected. Each has only one result sheet. They report channel ratios (`126` through `130C`, relative to `131`) and peptide/protein results, but no sample sheet, disease-class assignment, individual identifier, or RAW-to-fraction design table.
- The only remaining non-RAW deposited files are four Proteome Discoverer `.msf` databases of approximately 3.36-6.71 GB each. They were not downloaded under the campaign's no-giant-file rule. Their filenames distinguish analysis batches but cannot supply the absent biological reporter-channel assignment.

## What would resolve it

An author/depositor sample sheet or publication supplement giving, for each of the three TMT sets, the exact mapping of `126`, `127N`, `127C`, `128N`, `128C`, `129N`, `129C`, `130N`, `130C`, and `131` to the 27 individual samples and pooled reference, together with an explicit RAW-file-to-set/fraction/reanalysis table. Reporter intensities and filename order are not acceptable substitutes.
