# PXD011767 — BLOCKED

## Status

No SDRF was created because the public record does not assign the three
dimethyl channels to the named biological conditions.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE `PXD011767`: 11 fractionated raw files named
  `NTL_ScrI_shH_Rep1_{1..11}.raw`.
- Li et al. (2019), PMID `31371413`, PMCID `PMC7193482`, DOI
  `10.3324/haematol.2019.216648`: EGR1 perturbation in human primary bone
  marrow stromal cells.
- The deposited `txt.zip` MaxQuant output: one experiment (`Rep1`), fractions
  1–11, trypsin/P, and three dimethyl labels (`DimethLys0/DimethNter0`,
  `DimethLys4/DimethNter4`, and `DimethLys8/DimethNter8`).

## Blocking ambiguity

The raw-file stem names three conditions (`NTL`, `ScrI`, `shH`), but neither
the PRIDE metadata, the paper text, nor the submitted MaxQuant parameter and
summary tables states which condition was assigned to light, medium, or heavy
dimethyl labelling.  An SDRF would need that channel-to-condition mapping for
every fraction; inferring it from ordering would be fabrication.

A submitted experimental design table or author confirmation of the three
dimethyl channel assignments is required.
