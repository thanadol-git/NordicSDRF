# PXD043505 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"Analyses of the brown stain on the Parthenon Centaur head" (Gorshkov, Kjeldsen; University of Southern Denmark). Marble micro-samples from the rear of the Parthenon Centaur head (National Museum of Denmark, No. ABb13) were digested with trypsin and analysed by label-free DDA LC-MS/MS on a Q Exactive HF. Paper: Heritage Science, doi:10.1186/S40494-023-01126-9 (read from the publisher HTML; it is not in Europe PMC).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics v1.1.0` + `metaproteomics v1.0.0`. No organism template fits: the samples are an inanimate marble stain whose protein sources (bacteria, fungi, plants, vertebrates, human handling) are unknown a priori and were the research question, so the metaproteomics (environmental) scheme was the closest. Choice made without user confirmation (non-interactive run).

## Scope
Deposited: 12 RAW files plus Proteome Discoverer / FragPipe outputs (not annotated, not raw). Annotated: all 12 RAW files, 12 rows x 44 columns: 5 marble samples (P1-P5), 1 negative-control extraction (buffer and enzyme, processed like the samples), and the 6 blank (0.1% FA) injections run before each. One SDRF, no sub-experiments.

## Key decisions
- file_description.csv (deposited) maps each RAW file to P1-P5, negative control or blank injection. The 1C..5C file names correspond to P1..P5 (deposit description "P1 sample" for 1C, etc.).
- Blanks and negative control are kept as rows (so every RAW file is claimed) with organism `not applicable` and sample type `buffer control` / `negative control`. Marble samples: sample type `environmental sample`.
- organism `metagenome` (NCBITaxon) for marble samples, following the corpus convention for mixed-source samples. The real organisms are not established by the deposit.
- environmental medium `rock` (marble is a rock; a more specific ENVO term was not looked up).
- collection date 1999-09 (paper: samples removed September 1999).
- Modifications, tolerances and enzyme were read from the deposited Proteome Discoverer step-3 analysis file (Parthenon_step3_analysis.pdAnalysis): Trypsin (Semi), MS1 5 ppm, MS2 20 ppm, static Carbamidomethyl C, dynamic Oxidation M/P/S, Deamidated N/Q, Gln->pyro-Glu. Matches the paper's description of the final search. Enzyme recorded as Trypsin (semi-specific not expressible in the cleavage column).
- Reduction TCEP and alkylation chloroacetamide, HCD NCE 28, MS1 range 350-1400, charge 2-6 from the PRIDE protocol / paper. Instrument Q Exactive HF from PRIDE protocol and metadata; RAW headers not checked.
- No factor value column: the study has no formal comparison column in the deposit (brown-stained P1-P3 versus white marble P4/P5 is described in the paper but was not encoded as a factor).

## Values left not available / not applicable
geographic location (marble samples: not available; the paper gives provenance as Parthenon/Copenhagen but not a sampling location term), sample storage (not available), organism part / cell type / disease (not applicable), all environmental fields for controls (not applicable).

## Conflicts and notes
- Multiple searches were deposited with different settings: FragPipe open search (-150 to 500 Da precursor, 7 ppm), FragPipe unspecific (10 ppm), PD step 2 (only Ox M, Carbamidomethyl) and PD step 3. Step 3 was chosen as the final result; other searches are not representable in a per-row SDRF. Acetyl protein N-term (open search only) not included.
- Paper lists four KLR-numbered samples (P1-P4) for LC-MS/MS but five locations and five sample RAW files are deposited; P5 was annotated from the deposit.
- PRIDE organism "cellular organisms" (NEWT:131567) is not reflected; `metagenome` used.

## Stated vs inferred
Stated: instrument, enzyme, reagents, tolerances, modifications, collection date, sample-to-file mapping (file_description.csv). Inferred: `rock` as environmental medium, `metagenome` organism label, sample type categories, 1:1 mapping of 1C..5C to P1..P5 (also by deposit description).
