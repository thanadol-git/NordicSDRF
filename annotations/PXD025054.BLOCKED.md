# PXD025054 BLOCKED: TMT11 channel-to-sample maps not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Department of Clinical Science, University of Bergen (lab PI Simona Chera / Helge Ræder). hiPSC-derived β-like cells (HNF4A +/c811dupA MODY1 patient lines 6A/6D/6F and CRISPR-corrected isogenic lines) in 3D cell aggregates vs alginate capsules, plus human islets as positive control. Three TMT11plexes, each with the reference mix in two channels, 12 high-pH fractions per plex, Orbitrap Fusion, in-house Sequest pipeline; 36 RAW (`a14129…a14164`, 3 plexes x 12) plus 36 `.mzIdentML`. Paper: Carrasco M et al., Diabetes 2022, PMID 35043148, PMC8965667 (open access, read).

## Why it cannot be annotated
The paper and PRIDE give only "three TMT11plexes, with a reference mix of all samples in 2 of the 11 samples in each plex". Which cell line / environment / replicate sits in which channel of which plex, and which files belong to which plex, is not stated; the Supplementary Table 1/Figures describe cell lines, not channels, and no sample sheet is deposited. Assigning channels or plexes (even the file-to-plex split of `a14129…a14164`) would be invention.

## Verified facts for a future annotation
Homo sapiens; hiPSC-derived β-like cells (stage 7) in size-adjusted aggregates and alginate capsules, decapsulated before lysis (8 M urea/200 mM EPPS); trypsin, TMT11plex; Orbitrap Fusion; Sequest: 50 ppm precursor, 0.9 Da product ion, fixed TMT (K, N-term) and carbamidomethyl C, variable Oxidation M. Possible unblock: author contact (simona.chera@uib.no) or the paper's supplementary data.
