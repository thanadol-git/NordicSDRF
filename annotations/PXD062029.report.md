## Decisions and limitations

- Two cleavage columns (Trypsin, Lys-C): `tools build` emits one, so Lys-C was inserted after build with a script; parse_sdrf accepts it. Rebuilding from the tables alone would lose Lys-C. Protocol says Trypsin/LysC mix; MaxQuant used Trypsin/P.
- organism part `seed` for all groups is an approximation for BSG (spent malted grain, mostly husk/pericarp/residual endosperm). Developmental stage and growth condition are `not available`.
- Dissociation HCD is inferred from the instrument, not stated. Collision energy 28 eV in text is written as 28 NCE (assumed normalised, as for HF methods).
- The PRIDE processing text says N-terminal methylation as a variable modification, but mqpar.xml and summary.txt show Acetyl (Protein N-term); the deposited files were followed.
- Fragment tolerance 20 ppm is the MaxQuant FTMS MS/MS default (MatchTolerance).
- BSG versus BSG_cryo: crude BSG versus cryo-milled BSG per the paper; both come from the same mashing production. The paper mentions two BSG batches, but the mapping to file names is not stated.
- Biological replicate numbers come from the file-name index; the paper says all extractions were done in triplicate. Reduction/alkylation columns omitted (optional).
- The paper was read from the bioRxiv preprint.
