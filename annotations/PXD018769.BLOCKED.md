# PXD018769 — annotation blocked

PTM mapping of recombinant CbpD, the *P. aeruginosa* LPMO, digested in solution with trypsin or chymotrypsin and analysed by PEAKS. NMBU, Ås. No publication is linked.

## What is known
- **Files:** 4 raw files (`191001_OG1`, `OG1_2`, `OG2`, `OG2_2`) with matching MGFs and one PEAKS `mzid`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive.

## Why it is blocked
Which files are the tryptic and which the chymotryptic digests is not stated. `OG1`/`OG2` are not explained, and the `_2` suffix could be a re-injection or a second protein preparation. The cleavage agent is a required column, so it cannot be left as a guess.

## What would unblock it
The enzyme and replicate meaning of `OG1`, `OG1_2`, `OG2` and `OG2_2`.
