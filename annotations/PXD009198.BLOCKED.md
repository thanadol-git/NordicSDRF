## Evidence collected

- 45 RAW files (Orbitrap Fusion) and 6 Proteome Discoverer .msf files; the RAW sample names give set and fraction for every file: total proteome TMT sets 1–3 × 12 SCX fractions, phosphopeptide sets 1–3 × 3 runs (scratchpad/PXD009198/evidence/raw_sample_names.json)
- Paper: podocytes overexpressing MC1R stimulated with 10 nM BMS-470539 for 5, 10, 30 and 60 min, labelled with 6-plex TMT; pathways per time point
- Paper, supplement (tables S1–S4, blots) and PRIDE protocol do not assign TMT channels to conditions/time points or say what the three sets are (replicates or different conditions)

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

An SDRF for TMT data needs one row per channel with its sample. No deposited or published source says which channel carries which time point, control or replicate, so every characteristics and factor-value cell would be a guess.
