# PXD035661 evidence review

Status: blocked pending source evidence. The draft SDRF is in `scratchpad/PXD035661/PXD035661.draft.sdrf.tsv` and is not approved or committed.

The 28 deposited RAW filenames map to seven U2OS hydroxyurea exposure groups (untreated, 1, 4, 8, 12, 16 and 24 hours), with four numbered files each. The draft passes the official `parse_sdrf` validator and the offline `sdrf-tools check`, but two independent reviews did not approve it.

The remaining evidence gaps are:

- The 01–04 suffixes do not identify biological versus technical replication. The manuscript describes four independent experiments, but a per-file mapping of those experiments has not been verified.
- The PRIDE record and manuscript describe MS1/MS2 acquisition and MaxQuant processing without explicitly identifying the acquisition mode. The draft's DDA value is inferred.
- The PRIDE sample protocol identifies Af1521 ADP-ribose affinity enrichment and Lys-C pre-digestion before trypsin. These are not fully represented in the draft SDRF.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD035661), [manuscript](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40071-w), [U2OS Cellosaurus record](https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_0042).
