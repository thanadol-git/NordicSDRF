# PXD040431 — annotation blocked

Intestinal proteome of zebrafish fed a control diet or a *Cyberlindnera jadinii* yeast diet: one iTRAQ-8plex set, 10 high-pH fractions, TripleTOF 5600, ProteinPilot. NMBU, Ås. Paper: Heliyon 2024, PMC10925985 (open access).

## What is known
- **Labelling:** one iTRAQ set containing the control (C) and yeast (ACJ) diet samples. The deposited summary uses channels 113–118, with ratios against 113.
- **Raw files:** 10 `.wiff`/`.wiff.scan` fractions.

## Why it is blocked
Which diet and fish is in each iTRAQ channel (113–118) is not stated in the paper or the deposited ProteinPilot summary. Isobaric SDRFs need the label → sample map.

## What would unblock it
The channel map (113–118 → diet and fish).
