## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- comment[modification parameters] = Carbamidomethyl C (fixed) only, from the iodoacetamide step; no variable modifications are stated in the paper or PRIDE (the PX submission says no PTMs are included), and the deposited PQP library was not parsed.
- Adjuvant therapy flags appear only on the primary-tumour rows of SMPL_NFO.xlsx; they describe treatment after primary surgery and are carried on both the PT and LR rows of that patient. TUMOR.SIZE and lymph-node counts are not annotated because the size unit is not stated.
- Ki-67 (MKI67 high/low) is not annotated: the spreadsheet counts are the exact reverse of paper Table 1 (PT high 10 / low 13, LR high 8 / low 16 vs Table 1 with the labels swapped). Grade also differs by one recurrence (12 grade-2 LR in the sheet vs 13 in Table 1); the sheet is followed.
- sex = female is inferred from the cohort description (breast cancer, breast-conserving surgery); SMPL_NFO.xlsx has no sex column.
- dia-acquisition template not declared (its validator requires the bare literal instead of NT=/AC=).
