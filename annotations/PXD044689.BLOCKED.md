# PXD044689 — annotation blocked

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE exposes 24 Orbitrap Exploris 480 RAW files and matching mzML files.
- RAW-header byte-range inspection recovers author sample numbers such as `AE_1`; the deposited Proteome Discoverer workbook uses corresponding abundance identifiers `F1`, `F2`, `F4`, and so on.
- The publication establishes triplicate TurboID secretome samples across monoculture and spheroid culture, normoxia and hypoxia, with wild-type negative controls.
- Neither the RAW metadata nor the deposited workbook assigns each numeric sample identifier to one of those biological conditions.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The numeric RAW/sample identifiers cannot be mapped to culture format, oxygen condition, TurboID status, or replicate using primary evidence. Assigning conditions by acquisition order would be a guess.

## Unblocking requirement

The original 24-sample design sheet mapping `AE_1`/`F1`-style identifiers to monoculture or spheroid, normoxia or hypoxia, TurboID or wild type, and biological replicate.
