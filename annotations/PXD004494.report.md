# PXD004494 annotation report

iPOND (proteins on nascent DNA) in HEK293 cells, batch 2. Otterlei / PROMEC (NTNU). Paper: NAR 2017, PMC5737410. Orbitrap Elite. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 16 rows, one per `.raw`. The groups come from the run names and MaxQuant `summary.txt`:
- `5P` and `15P`: EdU pulse of 5 or 15 min.
- `P-30C` and `P-90C`: EdU pulse followed by a 30 or 90 min thymidine chase.
- `Negative`: negative control.
- `NegativeExtra`: one additional negative control.

Replicates `_01`–`_03` are biological replicates. The paper reports "six biological replicates (two batches)"; batch 1 is PXD004288. All runs used 0.25 % formaldehyde crosslinking (paper: the LFQ experiments used 0.25 % FA).

## Values
- **Cell line:** Flp-In-T-REx-293 (Cellosaurus CVCL_U427); the paper cites Thermo R780-07. Organism part `kidney`, sex `female` (Cellosaurus), age `not available` (fetal origin).
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Elite (raw header), with CID in the ion trap.
- **Sample prep:** in-gel DTT/IAA; trypsin.
- **Search:** from MaxQuant 1.5.3.30 `parameters.txt` and `summary.txt`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term, Deamidation NQ and Methyl KR variable; 10 ppm / 0.5 Da.

## Open points
- The paper describes two kinds of MS negative control: DMSO without EdU, and a no-CuSO4 click control. The files do not say which one `Negative` and `NegativeExtra` are, so both are annotated as `negative control`.
- The pulse length for the chase samples is not stated per run. The paper gives 5–15 min pulses.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf` passes for all three templates. Warnings are only free-text treatments and the cell-line name not being in CLO/BTO/EFO. `tools reconcile` reports no disagreements.
