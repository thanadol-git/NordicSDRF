# PXD021799 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE declares TMT quantification and deposits 33 fractionated Orbitrap RAW files in three `Min_Mito_<set>_Fract<n>` series.  The linked open-access paper (PMID 34215584 / PMCID PMC11057760) has no supplementary data package containing a proteomics design table.
- **Why blocked:** Neither source assigns reporter channels to samples, genotypes, or replicates, nor links a channel layout to each RAW fraction series.  The set and fraction numbers alone cannot supply that mapping.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A channel-to-sample worksheet with a RAW-series/plex crosswalk.
