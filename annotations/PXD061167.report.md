## Limitations

- Validator rejects the documented `NT=Data-independent acquisition;AC=PRIDE:0000450` in comment[proteomics data acquisition method] ("must be one of the allowed values"); plain `Data-independent acquisition` (documented as allowed) was used instead. `PRIDE:0000628` was also rejected.
- Cleavage agent column is single-valued: Trypsin recorded, Lys-C (initial digestion, also set in Spectronaut) not represented.
- Mass tolerances, isolation window width, DIA method variant: not available (Spectronaut settings not in the record; the 2 GB .sne archive was not opened).
- ND read as normal/chow diet (inferred; paper contrasts VAD with chow). Biological replicate numbers 1-5 are ours, ordered by file number; no deposited sample map. Wound day (paper: 5-day wounds) not confirmed per file.
- Sex female and strain C57BL/6 come from the paper Methods (study-level statement), not per-sample records. Age, developmental stage, cell type, disease: not available.
- Companion dataset PXD061256 (SureQuant targeted) is a different experiment and not covered.
