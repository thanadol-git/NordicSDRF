# PXD003286 annotation report

Gab2 phosphorylation and interactome in IL-2-stimulated Kit225 T-cells (double SILAC, Gab2 IP, with and without TiO2 phosphopeptide enrichment). Osinalde et al., Sci Rep 2016, PMID 27025927 (PMC4812247, CC BY). Kratchmarova lab, University of Southern Denmark (Odense).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0, `cell-lines` v1.1.0. The sample is the human cell line Kit225. `human` is used because it licenses the demographic columns. There is no label-free, DIA, or single-cell element.

## Scope (deposited vs annotated)
- PRIDE lists two ZIPs: `IPGAB2_rawfiles.zip` (40 `.raw`) and `IPGAB2searchfiles.zip` (MaxQuant output). Members were listed by HTTP-range of the zip central directory; nothing was downloaded in full.
- Annotated: 40 raw files x 2 SILAC channels = 80 rows x 42 columns. Every raw file is claimed.
- Raw files:
  - `..._IP27_GAB2_1..11` and `..._IP28_GAB2_1..11`: 22 files, Q Exactive.
  - `Qex03033..03041` and `Qex03042..03050`: 18 files, Q Exactive Plus (raw headers).

## Key decisions
- **Channel map (stated):** the paper says light (Arg0/Lys0) cells were unstimulated control, and Arg6/Lys4 cells were stimulated with 200 U IL-2 for 5 min. `mqpar.xml` confirms two labels: empty and "Arg6; Lys4". The PRIDE protocol also says "control (0/0) and IL-2-treated (6/4)".
- **Replicates (stated + file design):** `combined/experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` assigns the IP27 and Qex03033-41 files to SILAC_1, and the IP28 and Qex03042-50 files to SILAC_2. These are biological replicates 1 and 2.
- **Lane (inferred):** the paper describes two gel lanes of the same lysate. One was analysed directly (Gab2 IP) and the other after TiO2 enrichment. I assigned the 11-slice IP27/IP28 files to the direct lane and the Qex files to the TiO2 lane.
  - This split is inferred from file names, the instrument change (Q Exactive vs Q Exactive Plus), and the 11 vs 9 file counts. It is not written anywhere in the deposit.
  - The lane is encoded in `source name` (suffix `_IP`/`_TiO2`) and in `characteristics[enrichment process]` (free text).
  - Please attack this hardest.
- **Fraction identifier (inferred):** gel slice or file number in name order (1-11 for IP files, 1-9 for Qex files).
- **Label name:** Arg6/Lys4 is written as `SILAC heavy`, following the paper's wording ("heavy media"). By the usual convention it would be a medium label in a triplex design, so this is a judgement call.
- **Modifications (from the deposited `mqpar.xml`):**
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl C (UNIMOD:4). The paper says chloroacetamide, which has the same mass; the search wins.
  - Variable: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Deamidated N,Q (UNIMOD:7), and Phospho S,T,Y.
  - Labels on heavy rows: Label:13C(6) on R (UNIMOD:188) and Label:2H(4) on K (UNIMOD:481), written Fixed. Light rows say `not applicable`.
- **Tolerances:** precursor 6 ppm (`mainSearchTol` in `mqpar.xml`; the paper says 7 ppm), and fragment 20 ppm (FTMS). Deposit wins.
- **Cell line:** Kit225 = CVCL_5443 (Cellosaurus). The record gives male, 62Y, T-cell prolymphocytic leukemia, cell type T cell, derived from peripheral blood.
  - Age, sex, and disease are cell-line-donor facts taken from Cellosaurus, not from the paper.
  - Organism part is written as `blood`.
- **Instrument:** PRIDE says only Q Exactive. The raw headers show Q Exactive for the IP files and Q Exactive Plus for the Qex files. Dissociation is HCD (paper and raw header).
- **Acquisition:** DDA (top 10).
- **Factor value:** `treatment` (IL-2 stimulation vs unstimulated).

## Left as `not available`
- `ancestry category`: Cellosaurus says "Japanese", but I did not map it to the controlled vocabulary.
- `developmental stage` and `individual`: no per-sample evidence.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -t ms-proteomics -t human -t cell-lines --use_ols_cache_only` gives no errors. It warns that the free-text treatment and enrichment process values are not in the ontology lists.

## Limitations
- `sdrf-tools` is not installed here, so `contract`, `build`, `reconcile`, and the review gate were not run. The SDRF was generated by a private script, and the column set follows existing validated files (PXD000722 and PXD001278).
- No independent adversarial review was performed.
- `comment[data file]` holds the raw file name; the files sit inside a ZIP in PRIDE.
- The SEARCH zip was only listed. I read `mqpar.xml`, `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt`, and `fileInfo` from it.
