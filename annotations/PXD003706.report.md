# PXD003706 annotation report

## Scope

All 92 deposited RAW files are represented by one fraction-resolved assay row each.

## Evidence and decisions

- PRIDE describes three TMT6plex experiments: *Neptuniibacter* sp. MED92, *Phaeobacter* (*Roseobacter*) sp. MED193, and *Dokdonia* sp. MED134. The submitted RAW prefixes `JPDM01-06`, `JPDM13-18`, and `JPDM07-12`, respectively, identify these species and are preserved in the source mapping.
- The study compares three exponential-phase and three stationary-phase cultures per species, but no deposited resource maps those six samples to individual TMT6 reporter channels. Growth phase is therefore explicitly recorded as `not available`; no channel or replicate-to-phase assignment is inferred.
- RAW filenames identify the HiRIEF pH 3–10 separation and fraction. PRIDE specifies FASP with Trypsin, TMT6plex labeling, Q Exactive acquisition, fixed TMT6plex/carbamidomethyl modifications, and variable methionine oxidation.
