# PXD006299

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

- **Title:** Characterisation of protein ubiquitination using UbiSite technology. Blagoy Blagoev lab, Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Southern Denmark (Odense). Paper: Akimov V et al., "UbiSite approach for comprehensive mapping of lysine and N-terminal ubiquitination sites", Nat Struct Mol Biol 2018 (PMID 29967540, doi 10.1038/s41594-018-0084-y). The paper is not open access, so only the abstract and MeSH headings were used.
- **What this deposit holds:** the deep label-free proteome of the two cell lines, HEp-2 and Jurkat. The PRIDE file list has 280 RAW files plus one MaxQuant output, `txtproteome.zip`. The UbiSite enrichment data sit in two sibling deposits with the same title, PXD006201 and PXD009158. Those were not opened.
- **SDRF:** `annotations/PXD006299.sdrf.tsv`
- **Size:** 280 rows, 46 columns
- **SHA-256:** `8b1b6e47fbaa6aeaf5b8928e9dd7102c6506a3c63286f847f6b87d5ae0a4685b`

## Templates and why

- **ms-proteomics v1.1.0:** the data are Thermo Q Exactive HF DDA LC-MS/MS runs.
- **human v1.1.0:** both cell lines are *Homo sapiens* (PRIDE organism record, Cellosaurus, MeSH "Humans").
- **cell-lines v1.1.0:** every sample is a cultured cell line. This template adds the cell line, Cellosaurus accession and name, passage number and culture medium columns.
- **Not chosen:** clinical-metadata and oncology-metadata (no patients), and dia-acquisition (the RAW method is DDA, Top20).

## Design and file mapping

- **Comparison:** 2 cell lines × 3 replicate preparations (R1, R2, R3) × 46 high-pH reversed-phase fractions = 276 runs. There are also 4 re-injections of HEp-2 R3, fractions 41–44. Total: 280 runs.
- **Factor value:** `factor value[cell line]`, either HEp-2 (142 rows) or Jurkat (138 rows).
- **Sources:** 6 sources named after the MaxQuant experiments: `Hep2_R1`, `Hep2_R2`, `Hep2_R3`, `Jurkat_R1`, `Jurkat_R2`, `Jurkat_R3`. `characteristics[biological replicate]` is 1–3 within each cell line.
- **Source of the mapping:** the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt`, read from inside `txtproteome.zip` with HTTP range requests. It assigns each raw file an Experiment (`Hep2_R1` … `Jurkat_R3`) and a Fraction (1–46).
  - All 280 MaxQuant raw-file names match the 280 deposited RAW names exactly.
  - The experiment names agree with the cell line and replicate tokens in the file names (`Hep2`, `Jurkat` or `Jur`, `R1`–`R3`).
  - Each run's fraction equals the file-name suffix.
- **Re-injections:** the four files `20160215_QE3_UPLC8_AKP_Hep2_R3_Prot46F_41`–`44` are assigned by MaxQuant to experiment `Hep2_R3`, fractions 41–44. These are the same fractions already present as `20160211_..._Hep2_R3_Prot46F_41`–`44`. They are therefore encoded as `comment[technical replicate]` = 2 for the same source and fraction. The originals have technical replicate 1.
- **RAW headers:** read with HTTP range requests from 6 files on both instruments (QE5 and QE3, including one re-injection). Every header says "Q Exactive HF" and names the original acquisition file the same as the deposited file.
  - The method sections were read from 4 of those files (QE5 Jurkat R1 F1, QE5 Jurkat R2 F20, QE3 Hep2 R3 F10, and re-injection F42). All four show DDA Top20, MS1 at 60,000 resolution over 350–1400 m/z, MS2 at 15,000 resolution, a 1.3 m/z isolation window, and HCD at NCE 28.

## Technical values and where they came from

- **Search settings:** from the MaxQuant `summary.txt` (version 1.5.3.33 according to `parameters.txt`; the PRIDE prose says 1.5.5.31).
  - Enzyme Trypsin/P, specific, up to 3 missed cleavages.
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C).
  - Variable: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Gln->pyro-Glu.
  - "Multi modifications": Deamidation (NQ) and Phospho (ST).
  - All six are written as `comment[modification parameters]`.
- **Fragment tolerance:** 20 ppm, from MaxQuant `parameters.txt` "MS/MS tol. (FTMS)". MS2 was read in the Orbitrap.
- **Cleavage agents:** Lys-C and Trypsin, in two `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns.
  - The PRIDE sample protocol digests the whole lysate with Lys-C. The digested peptides are then either digested further with trypsin or analysed directly. The protocol states that the proteome peptides were HpH-fractionated.
  - The deposited proteome search used Trypsin/P.
  - Peptide C-termini in the deposited `peptides.txt` (a stream-read of the first 7,206 peptides) are 50.5% K and 48.4% R. That pattern requires trypsin; Lys-C alone gives almost only K.
- **Reagents:** DTT and chloroacetamide, from the PRIDE sample protocol. This agrees with the fixed Carbamidomethyl in the search.
- **Fractionation:** `High-pH reversed-phase chromatography (hpHRP)` (PRIDE:0000564), from the PRIDE protocol and the 46-fraction design.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523), from PRIDE and confirmed in the RAW headers.
- **Dissociation:** HCD, written as beam-type CID (MS:1000422), from the RAW method. Acquisition is DDA (PRIDE:0000627).

## Validation

- **Build:** `sdrf-tools build -t ms-proteomics -t human -t cell-lines` produced 280 rows × 45 columns.
- **Post-processing:** `scratchpad/PXD006299/postprocess.py` made two edits that `build` cannot express:
  1. It set `comment[fraction identifier]` to 41–44 on the 4 re-injection rows. `build` had numbered them 1–4 by list position.
  2. It inserted a second `comment[cleavage agent details]` column after the Lys-C column, with value `NT=Trypsin;AC=MS:1001251` on every row.
  - A diff against the build output, with the inserted column removed, shows exactly 4 changed cells, all `comment[fraction identifier]` on the 20160215 files. The (source, biological replicate, technical replicate, fraction) coordinate is unique across all 280 rows.
- **`parse_sdrf validate-sdrf`** with the three templates and `--use_ols_cache_only`: exit 0, "Everything seems to be fine. Well done." No errors and no warnings.
- **`sdrf-tools check --offline`:** exit 0, CLEAN (15 terms, 0 hallucinated, 0 wrong ontology, 0 UNIMOD swaps).
- **`sdrf-tools structure`:** OK.
- **`sdrf-tools reconcile --record project.json`:** one MAJOR finding remains, `cleavage_agent_incomplete` ("only Lys-C is declared, missing Trypsin"). The reconciler appears to read only the first of the two repeated `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns; Trypsin is in the second.
  - An earlier build raised two other findings, which were resolved. The reconciler flagged an anatomical organism part on cell-line material, so `characteristics[organism part]` is now `not applicable` and the Cellosaurus site of origin moved to `characteristics[sampling site]`. The other finding, "Trypsin/P vs record's Lys-C, Trypsin", was resolved by declaring both enzymes.
- **`sdrf-tools score`:** 99/100. The only gap is the missing optional `characteristics[developmental stage]`.

## Limitations
- **Reduction reagent:** after independent review, set to `not available`. The PRIDE sample protocol that named DTT is shared with the sibling UbiSite deposit and does not state how the deep-proteome samples were reduced; the cited Olsen-lab 46-fraction workflow uses TCEP.


- **Paper not read in full:** it is paywalled, and Unpaywall, Europe PMC and PMC have no open copy. Cell-line provenance (vendor, clone), culture conditions, passage numbers and any treatment are therefore unknown. `characteristics[passage number]` and `characteristics[culture medium]` are `not available`.
- **No treatment recorded:** the run names and MaxQuant experiments carry no treatment token. The keywords mention bortezomib and b-AP15, but those belong to the UbiSite experiments in the sibling deposits. No `characteristics[treatment]` column was added, and untreated status is not asserted.
- **R1–R3 inferred as biological replicates:** this comes from the file names and MaxQuant experiment names only. They were prepared and acquired on different dates and instruments (R1 and R2 on QE5 / UPLC1 in January 2016; R3 on QE3 / UPLC8 in February 2016). The paper text that would say whether they are independent cultures or technical repeats could not be read.
- **Jurkat clone not verified:** written as parental Jurkat (CVCL_0065) because the source of the cells is not stated. If the cells came from ATCC, the correct line is likely Jurkat E6-1 (CVCL_0367).
- **HEp-2 is a known HeLa derivative:** Cellosaurus records it as HeLa-contaminated. All donor values follow Cellosaurus: sampling site uterine cervix; disease "endocervical adenocarcinoma" (MONDO:0000554), the nearest MONDO term to Cellosaurus' NCIt C27677 "HPV-related endocervical adenocarcinoma"; female; age 30Y6M; ancestry African American (HANCESTRO:0568). `characteristics[cell type]` is `not available` because Cellosaurus gives none.
- **Jurkat donor values:** also from Cellosaurus. Sampling site blood; cell type T cell; disease "T-cell childhood acute lymphocytic leukemia" (MONDO:0000871); male; 14Y. Ancestry is `not available`: Cellosaurus says "Caucasian", which has no exact HANCESTRO equivalent, so it was not mapped.
- **Organism part:** `not applicable` for both lines, because they are cultured cell lines. The tissue of origin is in `characteristics[sampling site]`.
- **Reagents and digest assumed shared with the UbiSite workflow:** DTT, chloroacetamide and the Lys-C pre-digestion come from the PRIDE sample protocol. That protocol mainly describes the UbiSite workflow, so it is assumed the proteome used the same lysate prep. Trypsin is confirmed by the search settings and the peptide C-termini.
- **Precursor mass tolerance:** `not available`. MaxQuant 1.5.3 `parameters.txt` and `summary.txt` do not record it, and `mqpar.xml` was not deposited.
- **Instrument settings sampled, not exhaustive:** only 4 of the 280 RAW method sections were read. Scan ranges were therefore not written; NCE 28 is applied to all runs on the strength of the 4 identical samples.
- **Validation used the offline OLS cache:** no term was rejected. The MONDO, HANCESTRO and PRIDE terms were also confirmed in live OLS during annotation.
