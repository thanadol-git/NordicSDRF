# PXD067648 annotation blocked

PXD067648 is a Netherlands (Utrecht) MCF10A PIK3CA-mutant proteome/phosphoproteome deposit, not an Icelandic study. It entered the Reykjavík candidate list because the sample-processing protocol names Waters Sep-Pak cartridges manufactured in Reykjavik.

The paper describes three cell lines (MCF10A wild-type, PIK3CA E545K, PIK3CA H1047R) × four treatments (control, insulin, alpelisib, combination) with 20 min and 60 min points for the three drug conditions, in biological triplicate: 63 study samples, each measured as DIA proteome and DDA phosphoproteome. The 138 deposited RAW files are labelled only with plate-style codes (`A01`–`A63`, `C01`–`C63`, `D01`–`D63` on the 115 min DIA and phospho sets; `A0x`/`B0x`/`C0x`/`D0x` on a 12-file 85 min DIA set). Assigning those codes to cell line, treatment, or time would fabricate the source-to-data map.

Sources checked for a map:

1. Paper and Methods (PMID 42204977 / PMC13339826) — design counts only; no file or well key.
2. Europe PMC supplementary `pr5c00907_si_001.pdf` — figures S1–S9, no sample table.
3. Deposited DIA-NN `Prot.zip` (`report.pg_matrix.tsv`, `report.stats.tsv`) — columns are raw filenames, not sample names. The search also contains a full `B01`–`B63` series that was not deposited as 115 min RAW files.
4. Data Availability — PRIDE PXD067648 only; no Zenodo/figshare/OSF record.
5. `Phos.zip` (3.7 GB, 16 MaxQuant tables: `allPeptides.txt`, `evidence.txt`, `Phospho (STY)Sites.txt`, `proteinGroups.txt`, etc.). No `experimentalDesign.txt`, no sample names, no plate-to-condition map.

No SDRF is emitted.

Unblock with the original plate map, MaxQuant `experimentalDesign.txt` that names cell line/treatment/time per RAW file, or author confirmation of the `A`/`C`/`D` × `0–6` × `1–3` code.
