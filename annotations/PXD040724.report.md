## Limitations

- "Control" versus "4440" group meaning is not stated in the deposit (paper uses PL4440 empty-vector control RNAi); age and strain for 4440 group are `not available`.
- Group sizes are uneven and differ from the 5 replicates per condition in the paper; replicate numbers are ours (order of file number).
- Strain N2 and developmental stage adult are inferred from the paper and ages.
- Acetyl position (Protein N-term) is our reading of an unspecified mzid entry; dissociation method not verified; acquisition DDA assumed; reduction/alkylation reagents omitted.
- Search settings come from Scaffold mzid export (5 ppm / 0.01 Da), which may differ from the original Mascot run.
