# PXD037269 annotation status: organism assignment unresolved

The deposited README maps four RAW files unambiguously: one extraction blank and three Roman Egyptian painting samples (AEIN711, AEIN685, AEIN686-687). The protocol gives sequential Lys-C/trypsin digestion and Orbitrap Exploris 480 Top10 DDA. Its structured PRIDE organism field is Bos taurus, but the study searched mixed artistic-material and SwissProt databases for protein binders from different potential species. The available paper text did not establish a defensible organism assignment per painting sample. Broadcasting Bos taurus to all three paintings would overstate the evidence; using `not applicable` for biological protein sources also needs curatorial judgment. No SDRF was authored.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD037269), [deposited README](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2023/11/PXD037269/README.txt), [paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/00393630.2023.2256132).
