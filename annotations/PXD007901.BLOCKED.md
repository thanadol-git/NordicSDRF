## Evidence available

PRIDE describes proteome and phosphoproteome analysis of ten patient-derived xenografts from one neuroblastoma tumour, with both human and mouse signal in scope. The file manifest has two ten-file RAW series: `170120_FL_Sample1-10` and `161028_LA_1-10_FASPC18_x10`.

## Blocking gap

The linked article has no public PMC full text or supplementary sample table. PRIDE does not map Sample 1–10 to PDX identity, specimen/passaging context, proteome versus phosphoproteome workflow, species contribution, or replicate/fraction design. The two RAW series cannot be assigned by filename alone.

## Unblock requirement

Provide the PDX/sample manifest mapping Sample 1–10 across both RAW series to xenograft identity, molecular workflow, and any replicate/fraction metadata.
