## Validation

- `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -t ms-proteomics --use_ols_cache_only`: passes with two warnings (the free-text substrate values are not NCIT/EFO terms).
- tools check: 8 terms verified, CLEAN.
- reconcile: one BLOCKER "cleavage_agent_unsupported" (trypsin). False positive: the PRIDE record does not name trypsin, but the paper Methods state trypsin digestion (1 ug per 50 ug protein, 37 C, 16 h).

## Limitations

- Sample-to-group mapping (P17/P18/P19/P24 methanol; P20/P42/P43/P44 TMB) is from the PRIDE sample processing protocol. Source names (MeOH_P17 etc.) and biological replicate numbers 1-4 within each group are ours.
- Modifications (carbamidomethyl fixed, oxidation M variable) are from the paper text, not read from the deposited MaxQuant output (MQ_output.zip, 269 MB, not downloaded). MaxQuant default variable protein N-term acetylation, if used, is not recorded.
- Mass tolerances not stated: `not available`.
- Treatment values are free text (no ontology term); the paper gives 10 mM TMB while the PRIDE protocol says 150 mM methanol / 10 mM TMB for this proteome (paper cultivation text mentions 10 mM MeOH for other cultures); PRIDE values used.
- Paper says harvested in exponential phase (OD600 0.2-0.4); not encoded. Cultivation 65 C. Reduction with TCEP and alkylation with iodoacetamide are from the paper (in-solution digest).
- Single protease (trypsin), so one cleavage agent column. Organism part, cell type, disease recorded as `not applicable` (pure culture).
- Biological replicates are assumed independent cultures ("quadruplicate measurements"); not confirmed.
