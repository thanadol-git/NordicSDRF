# PXD057607 — annotation blocked

Human PDAC tumour tissue (PROMEC/NTNU; timsTOF Pro 2). Paper: Sci Data 2025, PMC11828881.

## Why it is blocked
- **What the paper documents:** Supplementary Table S1 lists exactly three files for "Human PDAC tumour tissues": `TN_PDAC_P1`, `TN_PDAC_P2` and `TN_PDAC_P4`, matching the paper's three patients.
- **What is deposited:** seven runs. The other four (`TN_PD_P1–3` and `TN_PDAC_P3`) are not described anywhere, and "PD" vs "PDAC" is unexplained (it could be another tissue or condition). The deposited MaxQuant search includes all seven.
- **Why not annotate only three:** an SDRF covering only the three documented runs would leave four deposited runs unannotated, which the repository audit treats as a blocker.

## Evidence that is solid
- Instrument timsTOF Pro 2 (`analysis.tdf`).
- SDC/TCEP/CAA, trypsin.
- MaxQuant 2.4.14.0 with defaults plus Deamidation NQ.

## What would unblock it
The submitters explaining `TN_PD_*` and `TN_PDAC_P3`.
