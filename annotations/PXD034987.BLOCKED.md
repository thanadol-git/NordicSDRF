# PXD034987 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: The proteomic responses of carbapenemase-producing and non-producing carbapenem-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa strains to subminimal-inhibitory concentrations of meropenem revealed by quantitative tandem mass spectrometry

Publication: Salvà-Serra F et al., Frontiers in Microbiology 2022 13:1089140, PMID 36845973, PMCID PMC9948630, DOI 10.3389/fmicb.2022.1089140

## Evidence collected

- 40 Orbitrap Fusion Lumos RAW files in four ten-fraction ranges and four large Proteome Discoverer `.msf`/`.pdResult` files
- Two resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa strains, each grown without meropenem and at three sub-MIC levels, with four technical replicates per condition
- For each strain, two TMT11 sets contain two replicates per condition and a PAO1 control/reference
- The paper gives the group design but neither it nor PRIDE maps those groups to reporter channels; no human-readable sample sheet or quantitative workbook is deposited

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

Four separate TMT11 reporter maps are required. The experimental group order does not prove the label order, and the RAW names identify fraction ranges only.

## What would unblock it

The four TMT11 design tables for Proteomics Core Facility projects 3329 and 3360: reporter channel → strain, meropenem concentration, replicate, PAO1 reference, and unused channel.
