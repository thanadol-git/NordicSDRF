## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- Four TMT channels (127C, 128N, 129C, 131) have no named sample in the deposited study design; they are annotated as 'unassigned_TMT<channel>' with 'not available' characteristics (phenotype included) rather than as empty channels, because the design does not say they were unused.
- The paper names the controls C1-3, the PD study definition C1, C2 and C4; the PD names are used.
- Controls are cultured empty-vector-transduced fetal-liver cells ('controls were FL cells transduced with empty vector'); they are annotated as organism part liver, embryonic day 14.5, C57BL/6 (fetal livers from E14.5 C57Bl/6 embryos) and were never transplanted. The PD name C4 is the third control (biological replicate 3).
- All labelled samples are anti-HA immunoprecipitates; this is recorded in the source names (HA-IP_) and here, because no enrichment-process term for antibody immunoprecipitation exists under EFO:0009090.
- Sex, age and strain of the leukemic bone marrow are 'not available' (recipients were NBSGW mice transplanted with C57BL/6 fetal-liver-derived MNX1-transduced cells; recipient age and sex are not stated, and the cells and host differ).
- Each MS2 cycle uses CID (identification) and HCD MS3 (quantification); both are annotated. FAIMS compensation voltages are in this report only.
- Multiplexed rows of one RAW file share one assay name (the file name without extension).
