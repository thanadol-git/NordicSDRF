# PXD052116 annotation report

## Scope

The SDRF covers all 27 deposited RAW files for major ampullate gland and silk samples from *Larinioides sclopetarius*.

## Evidence and decisions

- The deposited experiment reports identify the major ampullate gland triplicates, the native-silk HFIP, 8 M urea, and 9 M LiBr triplicates, and the six formic-acid-resolubilized silk preparations at 2, 4, or 8 M urea.
- `silk_sample_7.raw` through `silk_sample_15.raw` are listed in the deposited urea report but the sample-number-to-treatment mapping is absent. The deposited sample identifiers (7–15) are retained as biological-replicate identifiers; the unmapped solubilization treatment is recorded as `not available`, without inference.
- Acquisition was DDA on a Q Exactive HF. The reported digestion workflow was Lys-C followed by trypsin; the machine-readable cleavage-agent field uses the terminal trypsin specificity.
