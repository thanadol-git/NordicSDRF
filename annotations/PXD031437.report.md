# PXD031437 annotation report

*Aliivibrio salmonicida* LFI1238 wild type and LPMO deletion mutants (ΔAsLPMO10A, ΔAsLPMO10B, double), with and without 1 h exposure to Atlantic salmon serum. Paper: BMC Microbiol 2022, PMC9361615 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 24 rows: 4 strains × ± serum × 3 replicates.
- **Raw files:** the raw data is one `Raw_files.zip` holding 30 timsTOF `.d` folders. `comment[data file]` uses the `.d` names inside it.
- **Strain codes:** the paper defines `A` = AsLPMO10A and `B` = AsLPMO10B (ΔA, ΔB, ΔAB). `_serum` marks serum-exposed samples.
- **Excluded:** the four `Blank_*` runs and two `HeLa200_*` QC runs in the zip are not samples and are not annotated.

## Values
- **Sample:** strain `LFI1238`.
- **Factors:** `genotype` and `treatment`.
- **Instrument:** timsTOF Pro (record and paper).
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.6.17.0, deposited `parameters.txt`): MaxQuant defaults, i.e. Carbamidomethyl C fixed and Oxidation M plus Acetyl protein N-term variable. Tolerances not stated.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags trypsin. This is a false positive: the record says "in-gel digested" without naming the enzyme, while the deposited MaxQuant search uses Trypsin/P.
