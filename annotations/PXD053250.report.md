## Limitations and caveats

- RAW archive LC_MS_file.7z (330.8 GB) is truncated relative to its own header (next-header offset points past EOF), so its member list could not be read. The 138 `.d` names come from `mqpar.xml` (filePaths/experiments); they match the Intensity columns of the deposited proteinGroups table in txt.7z. Not verified against archive members.
- Source name = "mouse <leading number of run name>" (our inference). Seven IDs occur twice (35033, 38319, 44998, 34243, 35967, 37201, 19786) and are one source with technical replicate 2. 130 sources vs 29 mice in the paper: the deposit evidently covers more animals/cohorts than the paper describes. Biological replicate numbers are only a running index.
- No per-run genotype (APPPS1 vs APPPS1-hA7ko), sex or age mapping is deposited: sex, developmental stage and strain are `not available`. Run-name prefixes `f`/`m` (86 of 138 runs) probably mean female/male but are undocumented, so not used.
- Disease "Alzheimer's disease" is applied to all rows (all mice are APPPS1-based per the paper); no control arm is evident. `factor value[disease]` merely mirrors it to satisfy the template; it is not a true comparison. A genotype factor should be added if the submitter supplies the mapping.
- Instrument: protocol text (timsTOF pro2) used over the PRIDE dropdown (timsTOF Pro).
- Precursor tolerance 10 ppm and fragment 25 ppm are from mqpar (protocol prose says 4.5 ppm main search). Carbamyl (N-term) variable mod in the protocol is absent from mqpar and omitted. Enzyme written as Trypsin (mqpar Trypsin/P = no proline restriction, matches the protocol).
- Dissociation CID is inferred for PASEF; reduction/alkylation reagents, collision energy not stated by name and omitted.
