`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `vertebrates` v1.1.0 (Rattus norvegicus, label-free DDA, fractionated).

## Why two SDRFs
The 4-day cohort (rats 1-12) was acquired on a Q Exactive and the later 2-day cohort (5 rats, added in revision) on an Orbitrap Fusion with a different HCD energy ("high K 2day data was acquired on a different instrument", PRIDE protocol; confirmed from RAW headers).

## Design and file mapping (stated)
The deposited `README.docx` states: normal diet = EXOSOMES-1…6 (uEV) and KIDNEY-1…6; high-K diet for 4 days = EXOSOMES-7…12 and KIDNEY-7…12; each sample was fractionated into eight fractions and each fraction run twice. File names are `<EXOSOMES|KIDNEY>-<rat>-<fraction>[_<timestamp>].raw`. Sources are `rat<n>_uEV` / `rat<n>_kidney` (same animal, two sample materials; `individual` = `rat<n>`); `biological replicate` counts within diet group (1-6).
- Each (rat, fraction) exists as a plain file and a timestamped file: written as `technical replicate` 1 (plain) and 2 (timestamped). **Which is the "first run" is not stated;** the numbering is arbitrary. All 384 RAW files of this cohort are annotated.
- Factor: `factor value[treatment]` (standard diet vs high potassium diet (potassium citrate)); `sampling time` 4 day.

## Technical values
Q Exactive (PRIDE lists Orbitrap Fusion and Q Exactive; the RAW headers of `KIDNEY-2-2.raw` and `EXOSOMES-4-4.raw` read "Method of Q Exactive", HCD normalized energy 30); beam-type CID (HCD), 30 NCE. DDA. Trypsin (FASP digestion; the enzyme is not named in the PRIDE text but is the MaxQuant default used with "all other parameters default"). MaxQuant 1.6.3.4: fixed Carbamidomethyl C; variable Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M, Carbamyl K and peptide N-term, Phospho STY. Tolerances not stated: `not available`.
