# PXD050545: TMT channel layouts required

The [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD050545) lists 44 RAW acquisitions in three series: twelve `S1`–`S12` runs, eight `X758` fractions, and 24 `X760` fractions. The [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11264841/) and its [supplements](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11264841/supplementaryFiles) establish an 11-sample bulk ventricular phosphoproteome (five controls and six insulin-treated mice) and 18 cardiomyocyte samples (wild type and Tbc1d4 knockout crossed with vehicle and insulin). The supplementary Methods specify TMT 11-plex for the bulk experiment and TMTpro 18-plex for cardiomyocytes.

Supplementary Tables S1–S3 name individual sample columns (`Control_1` through `Insulin_6`, and `WT_Veh1` through `KO_Ins5`). The deposited normalized result tables instead use `sample-01`–`sample-11` or `sample-01`–`sample-18`. Neither those tables, the supplementary workbooks and Methods, nor the PRIDE project file list explicitly assigns biological samples to reporter channels such as TMT126 or to the three RAW series. The TMT-Integrator sample index cannot be treated as a channel identity without a deposited layout or equivalent verified crosswalk.

No SDRF is approved. The sample-to-reporter layout for each plex and its association with the three RAW series is needed. Any later reconstruction should compare the per-channel values against the published sample tables before approval.
