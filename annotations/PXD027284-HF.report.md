## Limitations

- Which of the 21 experiments is primary NK / NK-92 / KHYG-1, and which is IL-15, IL-12/15/18 or anti-CD16 stimulated, is not recorded in any deposited file (mqpar.xml and proteinGroups.txt use numeric experiment names 1-21 only; RAW headers carry no sample names). The paper describes 7 groups x triplicates but not the run order. Therefore cell line, cellosaurus, treatment, organism part, disease, age, sex are not in the SDRF (or are `not available`) and there is no factor value. The cell-lines template was not declared because primary cells and cell lines cannot be assigned per row (cell line column has no reserved word).
- characteristics[biological replicate] is 1 for every row as a placeholder because the triplicate grouping is undecodable; do not read it as replicate identity. Distinct experiments are distinguished by source name.
- The paper states Q Exactive HF for the analysis, but RAW headers show the E* series was acquired on a Q Exactive (NCE 25) and EH* on a Q Exactive HF (NCE 28); the deposit is split into -QE and -HF SDRFs. The pairing of E* and EH* files as the same sample is inferred only from shared MaxQuant experiment names 1-21 in mqpar.xml (one combined search), not from a deposited mapping.
- Sample is EVs isolated by precipitation (Total Exosome Isolation kit) from culture supernatants; no enrichment/material-type column was added because the contract has no honest single value across rows.
- Both series were searched together in one MaxQuant run; tolerances and modifications are identical for both SDRFs. Fragment tolerance 20 ppm is the mqpar FTMS default for the Orbitrap analyzer.
- Age, sex, ancestry and individual are `not available`: donors were anonymous healthy buffy-coat donors without per-sample data; cell lines are not assigned. Developmental stage omitted (optional).
- SANDBOX ONLY (independent review FAIL): run-to-sample mapping is undecodable (mqpar.xml experiments 'experiment 1-21' appear in both series, proteinGroups columns are numbered only, RAW headers carry no sample names, paper supplements are figures), so cell line (primary NK / NK-92 / KHYG-1) and treatment (IL-15, IL-12/15/18, anti-CD16) are missing and biological replicate = 1 is a placeholder that contradicts the triplicate design (the ms-proteomics/human templates reject 'not available' for it). Precursor tolerance: mqpar.xml 4.5 ppm vs PRIDE text 6 ppm. Instrument split confirmed from all 42 RAW headers (E* = Q Exactive NCE 25, EH* = Q Exactive HF NCE 28).
