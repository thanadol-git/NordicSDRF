# PXD012802 — blocked

## Exact blocker

The 30 label-free pooled-mitochondria runs have an explicit fraction/technical-replicate map, and the 40 TMT runs have an explicit two-plex/fraction map, but no checked source maps the ten reporter channels in either TMT plex to the nine biological samples and reference. The result workbooks replace reporter-channel identities with sample aliases (`M1`-`M9`, `C10`-`C18`, and reference), so assigning those aliases to TMT channels—or assigning individual biological replicates/growth phases by alias order—would be inference.

## Evidence checked

- [PRIDE project PXD012802](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD012802): the protocols describe yeast whole-cell and isolated-mitochondrial proteomes across fermentative growth, diauxic shift, and respiratory growth; three biological replicates per stage; separate pooled references; TMT labeling; and pooled-mitochondria label-free quantification. They do not state reporter-channel assignments.
- [Publication DOI 10.1073/pnas.1918216117](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1918216117), PMCID PMC7132131: the Methods confirm three time points (9, 13, and 20 hours), biological triplicates, and TMT10-plex analysis. The full article, supplementary methods PDF, and all seven supplementary data workbooks were checked; none gives a reporter-channel table.
- Complete [PRIDE file inventory](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD012802/files): all 70 RAW files were enumerated.
- Deposited `2965_ProteomeXChange_File_List.pdf`: this gives an exact run map for TMT set 1 mitochondria (20 ordered fractions), TMT set 2 cells (20 ordered fractions), and the pooled label-free mitochondria sample (three injection replicates of ten ordered fractions). It contains no channel map.
- Deposited `2965_TMTs1_Mitochonrial_1mc_HighConfidentProts_UniqueQuan.xlsx`: the sole result sheet reports nine ratios named `M1` through `M9` against `Ref_2965_Mito`, but does not preserve the reporter-channel identity of any sample alias.
- Deposited `2965_TMTs2_Cells_1mc_HighConfidentProts_UniqueQuan.xlsx`: the sole result sheet likewise reports `C10` through `C18` against `Ref_Cells_2824`, without reporter-channel identities.
- Deposited `2965_Mito_LFQ_IBAQ_1mc_AllPeptQuan.xlsx` supports the pooled label-free technical replicates but cannot resolve the TMT channel map.

## What would resolve it

The original TMT sample sheet or Proteome Discoverer study-design export giving, separately for the mitochondrial and whole-cell plexes, the exact reporter channel assigned to each of `M1`-`M9`/reference and `C10`-`C18`/reference, plus the biological replicate and growth stage behind each alias. Ratio-column order is not an acceptable substitute for that mapping.
