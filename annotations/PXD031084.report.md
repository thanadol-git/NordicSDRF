# PXD031084 annotation report

## Study
"Proteomics couples electrical remodelling to inflammation in a murine model of heart failure with sinus node dysfunction" (Cardiovasc Res 2024, doi:10.1093/cvr/cvae054, PMC11218694). Sinus nodes of 8-week-old male C57BL/6N mice 8 weeks after transverse aortic constriction (TAC, HF) or sham surgery (CTRL); 3 sinus nodes pooled per sample, 5 pools per group. TMT10-plex, one plex; the pool gives a proteome (38 fractions) and a phosphoproteome (12 fractions). Not in community corpus.
