# PXD061849 — BLOCKED

Checked: 2026-09-26

## Evidence

- ProteomeXchange record and complete indexed file inventory: https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/ui?pxid=PXD061849
  - Human T cells from treatment-naive (n=6) and MTX-inadequate-response (n=3) rheumatoid-arthritis patients, treated with TH9619, methotrexate, or DMSO.
  - 111 files total: 107 raw files, three result matrices, and `KEY_RA.xlsx`.
  - Raw names are numeric run identifiers such as `18948_1.raw`, `18950_2.raw`, and `18945_2_X.raw` and do not encode patient cohort or treatment.
  - The project lists no resolved publication.

## Blocker

The required file-to-patient/cohort/treatment crosswalk is not present in the web-visible record. `KEY_RA.xlsx` is evidently the necessary deposited key, but its contents are not exposed by the indexed public page. Without that key, the 107 numeric raw names cannot be assigned to the nine patients and three treatment arms without fabrication.

No SDRF was written or validated.
