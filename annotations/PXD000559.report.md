# PXD000559

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

- Study: "Arginine methylation during transcriptional arrest". Michael Lund Nielsen lab, Novo Nordisk Foundation Center for Protein Research, University of Copenhagen (Copenhagen, Denmark). Paper: Sylvestersen KB, Horn H, Jungmichel S, Jensen LJ, Nielsen ML. Mol Cell Proteomics 2014;13(8):2072-2088 (PMID 24563534, PMC4125738, doi:10.1074/mcp.O113.032748). The study immunoenriches arginine mono-methylated (MMA) peptides, fractionates them by microcolumn SCX at four pH steps and analyses each fraction on a Q Exactive.
- SDRF: `annotations/PXD000559.sdrf.tsv`, 76 rows x 44 columns (header + 76 data rows).
- SHA-256: `e83e0423b9875b5fdb38a68cf1c0ce9eebb956286509293e76968cda3bdd02e7`
- Evidence manifest: `annotations/PXD000559.evidence.json`

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `human` v1.1.0 + `cell-lines` v1.1.0.
- ms-proteomics: this is DDA LC-MS/MS on a Thermo Q Exactive (paper Methods; RAW headers).
- human: every sample is a human cell line (PRIDE organism NEWT:9606; paper).
- cell-lines: the samples are cultured HEK293T, PADI4-inducible HEK293T and U2OS cells. Using this template adds cell line, Cellosaurus accession and Cellosaurus name.
- dia-acquisition does not apply (the paper says data-dependent acquisition), and clinical/oncology templates do not apply (no patients). U2OS is an osteosarcoma line, but its disease is a cell-line property here, not a clinical cohort.

## Design and file mapping
All 44 deposited RAW files are annotated. Each pull-down was split into 4 SCX fractions, and each fraction is one RAW file. The fraction order follows the elution pH, which is the number in the file name's last token: fraction 1 = pH 4.5 (`_40`, `_4-12`, `_412`), fraction 2 = pH 6.5 (`_65`), fraction 3 = pH 8.0 (`_80`), fraction 4 = pH 11 (`_11`).

Within each condition, `characteristics[biological replicate]` counts the independent cultures (separate SILAC experiments). The three label-free injections come from one lysate, so they share biological replicate 1 and are told apart by `comment[technical replicate]`.

How the mapping was established:
- **RAW file to MaxQuant experiment:** read from the deposited MaxQuant `evidence.txt`, using the `Raw file` and `Experiment` columns streamed out of `MaxQuantOutput.tar.gz`. It covers 40 of 44 files. The four ActDON files are not in the deposited search.
- **Original acquisition names:** read from the RAW headers by HTTP range reads of the first 64 KB. 41 of 44 files were read; three reads returned nothing. Every header matches its deposited name, except that the deposited `_412` files were acquired as `_4+12`, which is also how MaxQuant lists them.
- **SILAC channel to condition:**
  - Paper Methods: heavy U2OS cells were treated with ActD (5 µg/ml) for 1, 3, 8 or 16 h, and light cells with DMSO. The paper's Results state that the DOX-inducible PADI4 cells were grown heavy and the uninduced cells light, with an additional reversed experiment.
  - Deposited quantification check:
    - In `modificationSpecificPeptides.txt`, the H/L ratios of MMA peptides in ActD2hr (the `rev-pull` files) are anti-correlated with every forward ActD experiment (r = -0.31 to -0.46 over all sites; r = -0.72 to -0.92 for sites changing more than 1.7-fold). The forward experiments correlate positively with each other.
    - For the Table I hnRNPDL MMA peptide ASRGGGNHQNNYQPY, H/L is 0.63 at 1 h, 0.29 and 0.38 in the two forward 3 h experiments, 0.10 at 8 h, and 3.34 in ActD2hr. This confirms that ActD2hr is the label swap.
    - In `proteinGroups.txt`, PADI4 protein has H/L = 0.047 in PADIr, so PADI4 is expressed in the light cells there. This confirms that PADIr is the reverse experiment.
- **ActDON = 16 h:** this comes from the file name ("ON" = overnight) together with the paper's 16 h time point. ActDON is the only fifth ActD time point among the files. Its label direction (heavy = ActD) comes from the paper Methods; the deposited search cannot check it.

## Technical values
- Instrument: Q Exactive (`MS:1001911`). PRIDE, the paper, the RAW headers ("Q Exactive - Orbitrap MS") and MaxQuant `summary.txt` all agree.
- Cleavage: two `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns, Trypsin (`MS:1001251`) and Lys-C (`MS:1001309`). The paper Methods say proteins were "digested using endoproteinase Lys-C (1:100 w/w) and modified sequencing grade trypsin". MaxQuant searched with Trypsin. Peptide C-termini in `evidence.txt` are 99.3% K/R.
- Modifications (MaxQuant 1.2.6.20 `summary.txt`/`parameters.txt`, matching the paper):
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl C.
  - Variable: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Methyl K/R (`UNIMOD:34`).
  - SILAC labels: Arg10 (`UNIMOD:267`, R) and Lys8 (`UNIMOD:259`, K), following this repository's SILAC convention (from `sdrf-tools search-params`). They are set to `not applicable` on the 12 label-free rows, because MaxQuant multiplicity is 1 for those runs.
- Tolerances: precursor 7 ppm and fragment 20 ppm (paper and PRIDE protocol; `parameters.txt` FTMS MS/MS tolerance is 20 ppm).
- Dissociation: HCD (`MS:1000422`). The paper states 20 ppm "for HCD fragment ion masses".
- Collision energy: 25 NCE. This comes from the instrument method names in the RAW headers (`MLN_CE25_...`, `1.9um_90 min_CE25_...`).
- Acquisition: DDA (`PRIDE:0000627`). Fractionation: SCX (`PRIDE:0000561`).
- Reagents: DTT (`PRIDE:0000608`) and chloroacetamide (`PRIDE:0000600`), from the paper Methods.

## Limitations
- **Fraction "4-12" / "4+12":** the 0621 ActD3hrs files and the Padi4 files have a `4-12`/`4+12` (deposited `412`) fraction in place of `40`. It was assigned to fraction 1 (the lowest-pH SCX step, pH 4.5), because it occupies that slot in each 4-file set. Its exact composition (possibly a pooled low-pH eluate) is not described in the paper. This is an inference from file names.
- **SCX pH values:** Methods say pH 4.5, 6.5, 8.0 and 11; the Fig. 1 legend says pH 4. The fraction order is taken from the file-name pH tokens.
- **ActDON (16 h):** not in the deposited MaxQuant search, so its channel direction rests only on the paper's statement that heavy cells received ActD. Mapping "ON" to 16 h is an inference from the file name plus the paper's time-point list.
- **PADI4 doxycycline duration:** `characteristics[sampling time]` is `not available` for the induced PADI4 rows. The paper shows PADI4 expression after 24 h of DOX (Fig. 5E) but does not explicitly state the duration used for the MS samples.
- **PADI4-inducible HEK293T:** annotated with the parental line HEK293T (CVCL_0063). The transgenic inducible derivative (a gift from the Kouzarides lab) has no Cellosaurus entry that could be confirmed; the source names carry "PADI4".
- **HEK293T demographics:** age is `not available` (Cellosaurus "Fetus", no numeric age) and ancestry is `not available`. U2OS ancestry "European ancestry" is translated from Cellosaurus "Caucasian".
- **Cell type:** `not available` for both lines (not given by Cellosaurus or the paper).
- **Enrichment:** no `characteristics[enrichment process]` term exists for anti-MMA antibody peptide immunoprecipitation (children of EFO:0009090 cover phospho and cell sorting only), so the column is omitted.
- **Collision energy:** 25 NCE comes from the RAW method file names, not from the scan headers. Two Padi4 `_65` files used a different method (`1.9um_90 min_CE25_...`), also CE25.
- **Precursor tolerance:** 7 ppm is the paper's stated maximum. MaxQuant 1.2 `parameters.txt` does not report it.
- **Instrument:** confirmed in 41/44 RAW headers; three range reads (ActD1hrs_65, ActD1hrs_80, ActD3hrs_forward_11) returned no data.
- **Paper access:** the full text came from the PMC HTML page (the Europe PMC fullTextXML endpoint returned HTTP 500). The supplementary PDFs/XLSX sit behind a proof-of-work gate and were not read (supplemental Fig. S1C and S4C, which show the SILAC set-ups). The SILAC directions were instead checked against the deposited quantification, as described above.
