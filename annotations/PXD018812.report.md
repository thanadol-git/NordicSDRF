## Decisions and limitations

- Condition-to-file mapping comes from the experiment names and file order inside the five deposited mqpar.xml files, cross-read with the PRIDE processing protocol text. DJ34phospho2 lists "Treated" runs (P2318-20) before "DMSO Ctrl" (P2322-24).
- Sub-experiments built per modification set and merged, because the builder collapses repeated per-row columns. Consequence: the first `comment[modification parameters]` column holds `not available` for the DJ34-set rows (no fixed modification was searched) and the last holds `not available` for proteome rows (no Phospho searched). These are placeholders for "not searched", not missing data.
- Precursor tolerance is the MaxQuant main search tolerance (6 ppm, or 4.5 ppm for the two DJ34 phospho searches); fragment 20 ppm from FTMS MatchTolerance.
- Instrument Q Exactive Plus from the PRIDE protocol text (structured field says Q Exactive); RAW headers not checked. Dissociation (HCD) and DDA are assumed, not stated in the deposit.
- Organ part (bone marrow) and cell type (pro-B cell) come from Cellosaurus CVCL_0161, not the deposit. Strain, sex, developmental stage and disease are `not available` (Cellosaurus notes a BALB/c vs C3H origin dispute; the cells are BCR-Abl-transformed, but no disease term was asserted).
- `characteristics[treatment]` / `factor value[treatment]`: DMSO, DJ34 and Imatinib. Dose and duration of the MS experiments are in the paper's Supporting information, which was not read. `DJ34` has no NCIT/EFO term (validator warning, kept as the compound's name).
- Replicates 1-3 are treated as biological replicates (triplicate experiments); no technical-replicate structure is documented. No fractionation.
- Reduction/alkylation reagents, collision energy, passage number, culture medium not stated.
