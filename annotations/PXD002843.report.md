# PXD002843 annotation report

Plate-method secretome of *Cellvibrio japonicus* Ueda107 grown on α-chitin, β-chitin or glucose. NMBU, Ås. Paper: Proteomics 2016, PMID 27169553 (not open access; abstract: growth on α- and β-chitin).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 18 rows: 3 conditions × 3 biological replicates × 2 injections.
- **Condition key:** the deposited MaxQuant design maps `a_6d` → α-chitin 6 days, `b_4d` → β-chitin 4 days and `glc_23h` → glucose 23 h.
- **Replicates:** experiments `A6d_B1–3`, `B4d_B1–3` and `G23h_B1–3` give biological replicates 1–3.
- **Re-injections:** files ending `_2` belong to the same MaxQuant experiment and are annotated as technical replicate 2.

## Values
- **Sample:** strain `Ueda107`, material `cell` (plate secretome).
- **Factor:** `growth substrate`; harvest time is in `characteristics[time]`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.4.1.2):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ and Gln→pyro-Glu variable.
  - FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm.
