# PXD035328 — annotation blocked

Somatic-cell proteome of quarter milk samples from four Norwegian Red cows with high (H) or low (L) somatic cell count (SCC). Each quarter was sampled before and after milking (32 samples). NMBU, Ås. Paper: Vet Res 2023, PMC10074679 (open access).

## What is known
- **Runs:** 30 timsTOF `.d.zip` runs `JT1–JT32` (JT17 and JT18 missing). The deposited MaxQuant experiments are `JT01–JT32`.
- **Partial key from the paper:**
  - `JT25–28` are cow L4 before milking and `JT29–32` are L4 after milking. This implies blocks of 8 per cow.
  - `JT24` contained blood.
  - The H cows are H3 and H5.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.6.17.0. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Why it is blocked
The paper does not state:
- which cow is in each of the first three blocks;
- which quarter and before/after status each JT number has;
- which quarters had high SCC (that is only shown in Fig. 1).

The study's main factor (high vs low SCC per quarter) therefore cannot be assigned per run.

## What would unblock it
A table mapping `JT1–JT32` to cow, quarter, before/after milking, SCC and IMI status.
