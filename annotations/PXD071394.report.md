## Caveats and items for the reviewer
1. Modification parameters, mass tolerances, dissociation method, DIA window columns are `not available`. The only search output is a 37 GB Spectronaut `.d.sne`, not read; the paper says "default settings". No RAW content inspected (no range reads of the zipped `.d`).
2. Digest: trypsin only (PAC, Opentrons OT-2); a single cleavage-agent column. Reduction/alkylation reagents not stated, columns omitted.
3. `-n` in `PRIS_<patient>-<n>` is read as the visit (PRIS 1-4 date); this matches the overview dates except for 7 files with missing or malformed dates. Inferred, not stated.
4. Nine sources have two runs (technical replicate 1, 2): 310-1, 315-1, 320-1, 327-1, 327-2, 331-2, 357-2, 363-1, 381-2. The paper says three samples were collected in two tubes and measured twice, so tube replicate versus repeat injection is unclear. Suffixes `-x/-xx/-xxx` (317, 320) are kept as separate sources; their meaning is undocumented.
5. `pic1-4` are undescribed (possibly QC/pool); all sample properties `not available`. Patient 323 is absent from the overview sheet.
6. The final diagnosis is applied to every visit of a patient; early draws may precede the diagnosis. Aseptic failure is encoded as `aseptic loosening` (EFO:0010725), the label used by the deposited code table.
7. Patients 394 and 428 are the same person (overview note); `individual` is PRIS_394 for both.
8. Paper group sizes (n=18 chronic pain, 18 infection, 41 aseptic) are patient-level subsets and do not map directly to the 281 runs here.
9. Acquisition method is written as plain `Data-independent acquisition`: the installed validator rejects the recommended `NT=Data-independent acquisition;AC=PRIDE:0000450` and `diaPASEF` (PRIDE:0000650) forms. DIA-PASEF is stated in the paper only.
10. Ancestry not stated; left `not available`.
