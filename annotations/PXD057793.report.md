# PXD057793 annotation report

Murine PDAC cell line UN-KC-6141, 6 cultures. PROMEC/NTNU. Paper: Sci Data 2025, PMC11828881. Its Table 2 links this PXD to "Murine PDAC cells". Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `vertebrates` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 6 rows: `Cell_1`–`Cell_6`, treated as biological replicates.

`Cell_1` caveat: the shared MaxQuant output (`txt.tar`, identical in PXD057795) searched `Cell_1_redo`, not `Cell_1`. The deposited file's internal path is `20160505_Cell_1.raw`, so the deposit may be the original acquisition rather than the redo. The same search also covers `MediumBefore`, `MediumAfter`, `SerumControl` and `SerumTreated` runs, which are not deposited here.

## Values
- **Cell line:** UN-KC-6141 (Cellosaurus CVCL_1U11; KrasG12D; Pdx1-Cre; C57BL/6). Disease `pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma` (MONDO:0005184); organism part `pancreas`.
  - Age is `50W`, the donor mouse's age at tumour harvest (paper). Sex is `not available`.
- **Instrument:** the paper says all samples were run on a timsTOF Pro, and PRIDE says Q Exactive HF. The deposited 2016 `.raw` files are Thermo, and their header says **Q Exactive** (method `20160505_240min_FullMS_MS2_Top20`). The raw data wins: Q Exactive, HCD.
- **Search:** from MaxQuant 1.5.3.30 `parameters.txt` and `summary.txt`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidation NQ variable; 4.5 ppm (main search) / 20 ppm.
- **Sample prep:** the paper's SDC/TCEP/CAA protocol accompanies its timsTOF description, which does not match these files. Reduction and alkylation reagents are therefore not written.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf` passes for all three templates; warnings are only the cell-line name and strain. `tools reconcile` flags trypsin as unsupported because this PRIDE record's protocol text describes only the animals. The paper and the MaxQuant search (Trypsin/P) support trypsin.
