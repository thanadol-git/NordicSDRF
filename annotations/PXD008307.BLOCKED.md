# PXD008307 — blocked: no TMT channel-to-AML sample map

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD008307
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD008307/files/all
- Linked PubMed record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29144447/ (DOI: 10.1038/nature24294)

## Evidence available

PRIDE describes TMT quantitative proteomics of human AML stem-cell and non-stem-cell populations from bone marrow on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos. The manifest contains 172 RAW files named with donor-like aliases (`DD08`, `DD13`, `HD20`, `HD48`, `B06`, `DD06`) and multiple extraction/fraction suffixes.

## Blocking gap

The linked Nature paper has no public PMC full text or supplementary sample table. PRIDE does not map RAW files or TMT channels to donor, stem/non-stem cell population, biological replicate, fraction, or experimental batch. Filename aliases alone are insufficient for these clinical and multiplex assignments.

## Unblock requirement

Provide the TMT design/sample sheet linking every RAW file and reporter channel to AML/healthy donor, cell population, replicate, fraction, and batch.
