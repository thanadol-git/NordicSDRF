## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- The paper performs proteomics on inhibitor-treated HEK293T; whether the NPR-H-001 α-syn FRET reporter derivative or parental cells were used is not stated — parental HEK293T (CVCL_0063) annotated as in PRIDE.
- Inhibitor concentration/duration for the proteomics samples is not stated in Methods (PRIDE says 48 h) — no dose column.
- GF109203X has no ChEBI/NCIT label in the offline cache (validator warning).
- HeLa.raw: HeLa vs HeLa S3 cannot be resolved; cellosaurus accession 'not available'.
- Search modifications from paper/PRIDE text; the 12 GB PD .msf files were not read.
