# PXD012235 — partial coverage: three bead-only BUB1B controls

The accession-scoped PRIDE file list and deposited `BUB1B-IPs_summary.txt` contain 24 BUB1B RAW acquisitions: 21 cell-derived BubR1 immunoprecipitations and three `beadsonly` controls (`_1`, `_2`, `_3`). The controls are buffer-treated beads without a biological source, as described in the [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6443202/). They are excluded from `PXD012235-bub1b-cells.sdrf.tsv`.

The active human SDRF template requires `characteristics[biological replicate]` as an integer starting at 1 or `pooled`. Assigning a biological replicate, HeLa cell line, donor properties, or disease to a bead-only control would be false. A separate control SDRF needs a schema-approved way to represent an acellular control before these three acquisitions can be covered. Do not treat this accession as fully annotated or contribute its SDRFs as complete coverage.

The BUB3 series has 12 additional RAW acquisitions in a separate SDRF. Its review status is tracked in `results/denmark/copenhagen_seventh20_batch.md`.
