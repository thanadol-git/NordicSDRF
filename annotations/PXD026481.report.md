# PXD026481 annotation report

## Evidence used

- PRIDE project metadata identifies a human TMT experiment acquired on an Orbitrap Fusion and specifies tryptic digestion, iodoacetamide alkylation, and TMT-10plex labeling.
- The deposited Proteome Discoverer workbook `20181214_F_Sasha_TMT_F1_12.xlsx` supplies the normalized-abundance column headers that map every populated TMT channel to sample/control status, replicate number, and the submitted treatment label.

## Annotation scope

The SDRF represents the 11 deposited RAW fractions and 10 populated channels (110 assay rows). It preserves the submitted condition labels **Acute**, **Untreated**, **Resistant**, and **Pooled**, rather than expanding them into unverified compound or cell-line identities. The channel map is repeated for each fraction because all fraction files derive from the same multiplexed TMT set.
