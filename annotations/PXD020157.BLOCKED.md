# PXD020157 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Clinical proteomics workflow for high quality quantitative proteome and PTM analysis of biologically relevant FFPE tissue samples
- **PRIDE record:** PXD020157

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits 67 RAW files spanning several experiments: fractionated lung TMT6plex, lung method optimisation, FFPE tonsil digestion, acetylation, frozen tissue, and phosphotyrosine-enrichment runs.
- The project description identifies broad study arms (three lung adenocarcinoma samples, FFPE tonsil, and comparison material), but supplies no file-level design matrix.
- In particular, the 24 `20190614_Lung_TMT6plex_F*.raw` fractions have no public TMT-channel-to-sample assignment; other raw basenames encode partial procedural labels but not a complete biological sample map.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The accession combines multiple workflows and sample types without a complete RAW-to-sample and TMT-channel mapping. Deriving clinical identity, sample pairing, or reporter-channel assignments from filenames would fabricate metadata.
