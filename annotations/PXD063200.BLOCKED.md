# PXD063200: blocked after independent review

The 12 acquisition names in the deposited RAW ZIP match the deposited experimental design and MaxQuant settings. The design maps analytical sample IDs S1–S6 to TurboID-VMAT and S7–S12 to free-TurboID controls. The [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD063200) and [paper](https://rupress.org/jcb/article/225/3/e202507071/281358/A-Syd-and-RUFY-dynein-adaptor-complex-mediates) describe pan-neuronal labeling in adult *Drosophila* followed by homogenization of whole flies.

The [working SDRF](../scratchpad/PXD063200/draft.sdrf.tsv) passes deterministic validation, but two independent review rounds failed. The design does not establish whether the 12 analytical sample IDs represent distinct biological sources or repeated preparations, so the biological-replicate and source identity fields are unsupported. The draft also assigns `neuron` as sample cell type, whereas the material analyzed was whole-fly homogenate; neuronal expression is an experimental condition, not an isolated sample cell type. The [second review report](../scratchpad/PXD063200/review-fail.json) records the findings.

The draft is withheld from `annotations/`. Resolve with a deposited run-to-biological-source key or author clarification; correct the whole-fly sample description before a new annotation review cycle. The deposited [experimental design](../scratchpad/PXD063200/experimental_design.txt), [MaxQuant settings](../scratchpad/PXD063200/mqpar.xml), and [evidence draft](../scratchpad/PXD063200/draft.evidence.json) remain available for that work.
