# PXD010554 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** System-wide identification of enzyme substrates by thermal analysis (SIESTA)
- **PRIDE record:** PXD010554
- **Publication:** Mateus *et al.*, *Molecular Systems Biology* (2020), PMID [33637753](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33637753/)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits 95 RAW files.  Most encode enzyme/cofactor condition, replicate, and fraction (for example, `TXNRD1_substrate_NADPH_Rep1_Frac1.raw`).
- The protocol describes paired lysate aliquots, TMT labelling, and eight-fraction peptide separation for the TrxR1 and PARP10 experiments.
- No deposited design file, paper supplement, or result export maps TMT reporter channels to the individual enzyme/cofactor conditions.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Raw filenames establish the fractionated experiment arms but cannot identify which TMT channel carried which biological condition.  Inferring channel assignment from condition names or acquisition order would fabricate multiplex metadata.
