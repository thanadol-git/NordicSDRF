## Study
"Downregulation of antigen presentation-associated pathway proteins in poor outcome triple-negative breast cancer patient tumors" (Pedersen et al., Oncoimmunology 2017;6(5):e1305531, PMID 28638726, PMC5468003). Laser-microdissected FFPE TNBC tumours, trypsin digestion, TMT 6-plex (TMTsixplex; 126 = pooled internal standard), HILIC fractionation (7 pooled fractions), nanoLC Q Exactive (Thermo), PD 1.4 with Mascot+Sequest. Discovery cohort 34 primary tumours (15 recurrence, 19 recurrence-free); second cohort 10 primary + matched metastases.

5. Cross-validation: nothing to cross-check.

## Facts established (stated, from paper and msf)
- Instrument per paper: Q Exactive (PRIDE agrees: MS:1001911); not verified from RAW headers.
- Search (Plex1 .msf): trypsin, 2 missed cleavages, fixed TMT6plex K and N-term, variable Oxidation (M) and Deamidated (N,Q), fragment tolerance 0.05 Da; paper states 8 ppm precursor. No carbamidomethyl in the search despite MOD:00397 appearing in PRIDE's PTM list.
- PRIDE disease field (breast cancer) is project-level; there is no evidence per channel, so it must not be broadcast.
