# PXD006770 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The deposited RAW-file workbook maps 194 of the 197 deposited RAW files, but it contains no rows for `161210_QEx_LA_MS581_01_targ.raw`, `161210_QEx_LA_MS581_02_targ.raw`, or `161210_QEx_LA_MS581_05_targ.raw`. The workbook and deposited Skyline archive instead contain similarly named `161212_*` runs, which are also separately present in the PRIDE file list. Treating the three `161210_*` files as repeats of samples MS581_01, MS581_02, and MS581_05 would therefore be a filename-based inference rather than a deposited mapping.

## Mapping facts that were resolved

- The workbook maps 194 unique deposited files to source name, sample type, organism, and either data-dependent or targeted acquisition.
- Those rows cover mouse whole BAL fluid, BAL supernatant, mucus plugs, and epithelial cells, plus human BAL fluid from never-smokers, asymptomatic smokers, and patients with COPD.
- The project protocols resolve the Q Exactive methods, Lys-C/trypsin preparation, MaxQuant search settings, and heavy-peptide targeted quantification.

## Why an SDRF was not written

All 197 deposited RAW files must be represented. Assigning the three unmapped `161210_*` files to the three similarly numbered `161212_*` samples, or marking them as technical repeats, would invent the source/run relationship. A supportable annotation requires an accession-specific correction or export that explicitly identifies those three files and their source samples.
