# PXD007991 — evidence hold: acquisition method and digest scope

The [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD007991) lists 37 RAW files and distinguishes two experiments. Nineteen `20151117_*_TiO2_*.raw` files are the siKALIP kinase assay on HEK293 6E lysate, using sequential Lys-C and trypsin digestion. The other 18 RAW files are consistent with the MASTL phosphorylation-map experiment: purified recombinant full-length, Bonsai and 450 constructs, including kinase-dead, dephosphorylated and mitotic-extract conditions. The [paper](https://doi.org/10.1074/mcp.RA119.001879) describes in-gel trypsin digestion for the phosphorylation map, Q Exactive HF analysis and MaxQuant search settings. The PRIDE project description explains the construct abbreviations in the filenames.

The required proteomics acquisition-method term for the Q Exactive RAW files is not specified in the accessible PRIDE protocol or publication. The paper separately describes a Waters Synapt DIA method for hydrogen/deuterium exchange experiments, which must not be applied to the Q Exactive files. No DDA term has been inferred from the instrument or search engine. A RAW method header or depositor protocol identifying the acquisition mode is needed to complete an SDRF for the 18 recombinant-protein runs. The 19 kinase-assay runs additionally use both Lys-C and trypsin; the active template accepts one cleavage-agent value, so they require a schema-compatible representation.

No SDRF is approved or committed for this accession.
