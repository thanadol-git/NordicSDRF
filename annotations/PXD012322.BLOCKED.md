# PXD012322 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Matrisome properties of scaffolds direct fibroblasts in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis
- **PRIDE record:** PXD012322
- **Publication:** Elowsson Rendin *et al.*, *International Journal of Molecular Sciences* (2019), DOI [10.3390/ijms20164013](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms20164013), PMID [31426504](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31426504/), PMCID [PMC6719040](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6719040/)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE lists 59 Thermo RAW files: 12 named `Emma_S1801_*` and 47 named `Emma_S1803_*`.
- The paper establishes two analyses: decellularized healthy/IPF lung scaffolds, and scaffolds repopulated with heavy-SILAC fibroblasts.  It reports four biological replicates per clinical group; the repopulated series covers days 1, 3, and 9 with two technical replicates per group.
- `SILAC2.resultfiles.zip` contains MaxQuant summaries for `txt.scaffold` and `txt.repopulated`.  These link individual raw basenames to numeric `Experiment` values and establish the label chemistry, but do not attach those experiments to the paper's healthy/IPF donors, time points, or technical replicates.
- The article's public PowerPoint supplement and Table 1 describe cohort-level design and donor attributes, but contain no raw filename, instrument-ID, or MaxQuant-experiment crosswalk.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The raw filenames are opaque acquisition IDs.  No public source reviewed maps them to **healthy versus IPF**, the individual donor, or (for the repopulated runs) day 1/3/9 and technical replicate.  Assigning those properties based on file order, numeric experiment labels, or the `S1801`/`S1803` prefixes would be invented metadata.

## Unblocking requirement

An author-provided sample sheet or MaxQuant experimental-design table that explicitly maps every raw basename to the scaffold/repopulated sample, clinical group, donor, time point, and replicate.
