# PXD004305 annotation report

Plate-method secretome of *Chitinophaga pinensis* grown on konjac glucomannan, carob galactomannan or glucose. NMBU, Ås. Paper: J Proteomics 2017, PMID 28069559 (not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 18 rows: 3 substrates × 3 biological replicates × 2 injections.
- **Grouping:** the deposited MaxQuant design groups `<substrate>_t2_<n>` with `<substrate>_t2_<n>_2` into one experiment, so `_2` files are technical replicate 2.
- **Substrate codes:** `kGM` = konjac glucomannan and `cGM` = carob galactomannan; these are the mannans named in the abstract. `Glc` = glucose.
- **Time point:** `t2` denotes the sampling time point. Its absolute time is not stated in the deposit and is not encoded.

## Values
- **Sample:** strain `DSM 2588` (the type strain; the record names the species only), material `cell` (plate secretome).
- **Factor:** `growth substrate`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.4.1.2):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ and Gln→pyro-Glu variable.
  - FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags trypsin as not named. This is a false positive: the PRIDE protocol says "trypsination" or "trypsinated", a spelling the checker does not match.
