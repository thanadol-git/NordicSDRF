DOI 10.1186/s13059-025-03577-8, PMID 40296153, PMC12036290. The paper's Data availability section cites PXD062330. The PROXI record still says "publication pending"; the PRIDE v3 record links the paper.

## Scope, evidence and decisions
Status: annotated. The file has 12 rows (2 RAW x 6 TMT6plex channels) and 38 columns. It declares the templates ms-proteomics, vertebrates and cell-lines (v1.1.0) on every row.

**Deposited vs annotated.** I re-derived the file list from the PRIDE FTP directory listing (cloud browser) and from `submission.px_1`. It holds 2 RAW (`MIR22-MIR23-set1_TMT6_20211125.raw` 561 MB, `MIR22-MIR23-set2_TMT6_20211125.raw` 569 MB), the PD exports `_Proteins.txt` and `_PeptideGroups.txt`, `Samples-Data_metadata.txt` and `checksum.txt`. This matches the campaign cache (2 RAW / 6 files; `submission.px_1` is the PX tool file). Both RAW files are annotated, so 2/2 runs have samples.

**Channel map (STATED).** The map comes from two independent deposited sources that agree on every channel:
1. `Samples-Data_metadata.txt`, written by the submitter. The coordinator transcribed it, and I re-read it in the cloud browser; the two copies are identical. It lists 12 samples. Run 1 = set1 and Run 2 = set2. In each set the channels are TMT126/127/128 = sgGal4 / MIR sg22 / MIR sg23 of the first biological replicate, and TMT129/130/131 = the same three sgRNAs of the second biological replicate. That gives BR1-2 in set1 and BR3-4 in set2. All samples are E14 embryonic stem cells, digested with trypsin and run on the Orbitrap Eclipse.
2. The PD 2.4 study factors in the `_Proteins.txt` header ("Found in Sample: [Sxx] F8/F9: <channel>, Sample, BR<n>, <Gal4|MIR22|MIR23>"). Here F8 holds BR1/BR2 and F9 holds BR3/BR4, with the same channel order. That fixes F8 = set1 and F9 = set2, consistent with source 1.

The biological replicate numbers 1-4 are the submitter's own numbers. Each (sgRNA, BR) pair has a unique source name `E14_dCas9_<sgRNA>_BR<n>`.

**Sample columns.**
- Cell line: ES-E14, CVCL_C320. Cellosaurus (live API) lists "E14" as a synonym of this entry and gives strain 129P2/Ola, male, blastocyst-derived, CL_0002322.
- Cell type: embryonic stem cell. Disease: normal. Organism part: not applicable (cell line).
- Developmental stage: "blastula stage", meaning the stage of the donor embryo (a blastocyst).
- Treatment and factor value[treatment]: the CAPTURE2.0 dCas9-biotin pull-down with sgGal4 (control), MIR sg22 or MIR sg23. sgRNA names follow the metadata file and the paper.

**Search and acquisition columns.** These come from `submission.px_1` and the PRIDE protocol, and agree with the paper Methods:
- Instrument: Orbitrap Eclipse. MS:1003029 is the submitter's own term, and the paper says the same.
- Acquisition: DDA (submitter `experiment_type` PRIDE:0000627).
- Digestion: trypsin, full specificity, up to 2 missed cleavages; S-Trap micro digestion.
- Tolerances: 10 ppm / 0.6 Da.
- Modifications: Carbamidomethyl C fixed (UNIMOD:4); TMT6plex K and peptide N-term fixed (UNIMOD:737); Oxidation M variable (UNIMOD:35). PD/Sequest HT. The UNIMOD ids were checked against the UNIMOD XML bundled with sdrf-pipelines.
- No fractionation: one pooled run per TMT set, so fraction identifier is 1 and fractionation method is `not applicable`.

**Values left not available / not applicable.**
- `comment[dissociation method]` = not available. Neither the paper nor PRIDE states HCD, CID or SPS-MS3. The 0.6 Da fragment tolerance suggests ion-trap MS2, but that is not evidence of the activation type. I did not read the RAW header (561 MB file, and the browser cannot range-read it).
- `comment[reduction reagent]` = not available. No reducing agent is named anywhere; the paper's "as previously described" points to a reference I did not read.
- No collision energy or MS1 scan range column, because neither value is stated.
- `comment[alkylation reagent]` = iodoacetamide (IAA). This is INFERRED from the submitter's PX modification term "iodoacetamide derivatized residue" (MOD:00397) together with the fixed carbamidomethyl search. It is not stated as a wet-lab step.

## Conflicts
- **ESC vs RA design.** The paper's Results say the MIR-proteome of ESC was contrasted with RA-induced differentiated cells (Fig. 3H, "ESC-specific MIR-associated proteins comparing to RA"). The deposited metadata and the PD study factors contain only E14 ESC samples (sgGal4/sg22/sg23 x BR1-4), with no RA state and no RA channel. The RA-differentiated CAPTURE2.0 MS data are therefore not in this PXD. I annotated what was deposited (ESC only) and did not add an RA factor. A reviewer should confirm that the RA arm is deposited elsewhere or not at all; this does not change the 12-row mapping.
- **Cell line identity.** The paper and the metadata both say "E14". Many labs' "E14" is actually the E14TG2a subclone (CVCL_9108). I used the stated name, ES-E14 (CVCL_C320).
- **Search engine.** PRIDE's text names only "Proteome Discoverer 2.4 with Percolator". The paper and the Proteins.txt header name Sequest HT. These do not conflict.
