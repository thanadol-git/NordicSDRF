Status: validated annotation; independent review pending

Dataset: Circulating microvesicles and exosomes in small cell lung cancer (Aalborg University Hospital, Denmark; Pedersen et al., Clin Proteomics 2022, PMID 34996345). No prior annotation existed in sdrf-annotated-datasets.

Templates: ms-proteomics v1.1.0, human v1.1.0, clinical-metadata v1.0.0, oncology-metadata v1.0.0.

Only the 20K microvesicle pellet is deposited; treated-patient and 100K runs in the paper are not in this PARTIAL submission.

Validation: parse_sdrf validate-sdrf PASS; tools structure OK; tools check --offline CLEAN; tools reconcile consistent.
