# PXD071573 annotation evidence report

Status: Blocked: 15 Bruker RAWs are named S2-A1 through H1 and S2-A2 through G2, while the study compares RAK1-GFP, MPK4b-GFP and 35S-GFP controls with and without wounding. The deposited SEARCH and RESULT ZIPs link S01-S15 to those run IDs but do not assign the run IDs to bait or wounding state. The associated publication confirms the experiment but does not supply the missing per-file design. The S01 RAW archive's SampleInfo.xml confirms DDA PASEF on a timsTOF Pro and autosampler position S2-A1; it does not identify bait or wound state.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD071573) and [file list](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD071573/files/all). No SDRF has been approved for this accession.
