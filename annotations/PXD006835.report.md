## Evidence and choices

- Search settings read from parameters.txt and summary.txt inside the deposited UK4_Fap.rar / EColi_Curli.rar (HTTP range reads, no full download): MaxQuant 1.5.8.3, fixed Carbamidomethyl (C), variable Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term), enzyme Trypsin/P, 2 missed cleavages, FTMS MS/MS tolerance 20 ppm, LFQ + match between runs.
- Cleavage agent recorded as Trypsin (the paper: tryptic in-gel digest); MaxQuant searched with Trypsin/P. Single protease, so one cleavage-agent column.
- Instrument: Q Exactive Plus (paper Methods; RAW paths `D:\QEplus\...`). The embedded method template is named "Q Exactive HF" and PRIDE lists "Q Exactive", so this is a minor discrepancy. HCD NCE 27, MS2 Orbitrap at 15,000 resolution, read from the RAW header (first 300 kB by range read).
- Biological replicate 1-4 follows the paper's "four biological replicates"; the Methods say 50 uL aliquots of one combined lysate were used as replicates, so they may be technical in effect.
- Strain: SM2258 (E. coli MG1655 derivative) and UK4 (the "WT" in file names). `characteristics[strain or breed]` and `[treatment]` are not in the template union but passed validation.

## Limitations

- Precursor tolerance, reduction and alkylation reagents are `not available`: not in the deposit parameters or paper (alkylation is only implied by the fixed carbamidomethyl).
- Organism part, cell type and disease are `not applicable` (bacterial culture lysate).
- Single short SDS-PAGE band per sample, so fraction 1 and no fractionation method. No independent review done yet.
