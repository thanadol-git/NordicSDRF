# PXD009644 annotation report

## Scope

All 48 deposited RAW files are represented as fraction-resolved TMT10 validation assays.

## Evidence and decisions

- The deposited filenames explicitly identify three validation multiplexes (`Rep1`–`Rep3`) and their 16 concatenated fractions. These source and fraction mappings are retained directly.
- The parent study contains A549 cells treated with many anticancer compounds, but PRIDE does not deposit the TMT channel-to-compound assignment for these validation files. The SDRF therefore records the TMT10 validation multiplexes without invented channels or drug identities.
- PRIDE specifies A549 human adenocarcinoma cells, sequential Lys-C/Trypsin digestion, TMT10plex labeling, Q Exactive HF acquisition, and fixed/variable modifications used for the search.
