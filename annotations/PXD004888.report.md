## Templates

- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 only. The samples are bacterial (two oral bacteria grown as in-vitro biofilms). No organism template fits bacteria, and this is a defined laboratory culture, not an environmental or host-associated microbiome, so `metaproteomics` was not used. The data are label-free DDA, so there is no `dia-acquisition` template. There are no cell lines, clinical subjects or human material.

## Design and file mapping

- There are 3 biofilm types × 4 biological replicates, giving 12 label-free LC-MS/MS runs with one raw file each and no fractionation:
  - F. nucleatum ATCC 25586 mono-species EPM: `Fn_ECM1-4.raw`
  - P. gingivalis ATCC 33277 mono-species EPM: `Pg_ECM1-4.raw`
  - Dual-species EPM: `FnPg_ECM1-4.raw`
- Group membership comes from the raw file names, and matches the MaxQuant experiment names in the deposited `peptidesFn.txt` / `proteinGroupsFn.txt` (`FnECM1-4`, `FnPgECM1-4`) and `peptidesPg.txt` / `proteinGroupsPg.txt` (`PgECM1-4`, `FnPgECM1-4`).
- The count agrees with the protocol ("prepared in four biological replicates"). The replicate number is the digit in the file name.
- All 12 RAW files in PRIDE are claimed exactly once. The other files (MaxQuant txt tables and FASTA files) are not rows.
- Factor: `factor value[growth condition]`, which mirrors `characteristics[growth condition]`. Values are `Fusobacterium nucleatum mono-species biofilm`, `Porphyromonas gingivalis mono-species biofilm`, and `Fusobacterium nucleatum and Porphyromonas gingivalis dual-species biofilm`.
- The deposit holds two MaxQuant searches: the F. nucleatum database over Fn and FnPg runs, and the P. gingivalis database over Pg and FnPg runs. Their parameters are identical except for the FASTA, so one SDRF with each raw file listed once was written rather than per-search files that would duplicate the dual-species runs.

## Technical values and sources

- Instrument: `NT=Orbitrap Elite;AC=MS:1001910`. PRIDE lists the generic `LTQ Orbitrap` (MS:1000449), and the protocol says "LTQ-Orbitrap". The raw-file method header (byte-range read of Fn_ECM1, Pg_ECM1 and FnPg_ECM4) names the instrument `Orbitrap Elite MS`, with tune file `Elite_nano`, folder `2014-11-20_OrbitrapElite_P14-47`, and method `TOP10CID_240K`. The 240,000 survey resolution stated in the protocol is only reachable on the Elite in that family.
- Acquisition: DDA (`PRIDE:0000627`); top-10 CID per protocol and raw method.
- Dissociation: CID (`MS:1000133`); collision energy `35% NCE` (raw method: "Normalized Coll. Energy: 35.0").
- MS2 analyzer: linear ion trap (`MS:1000291`), from the protocol and the ITMS MS2 scan event.
- MS1 scan range: `300m/z-2000m/z`, from the protocol and the raw method `FTMS + p norm o(300.0-2000.0)`.
- Cleavage: Trypsin (`MS:1001251`), from the protocol.
- Reduction and alkylation: DTT (`PRIDE:0000608`) and iodoacetamide (`PRIDE:0000599`), from the protocol.
- Modifications, from the deposited MaxQuant 1.5.2.8 `parametersFn.txt` / `parametersPg.txt` (identical apart from the FASTA):
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C), from "Fixed modifications".
  - Variable: Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term), from "Modifications included in protein quantification: Acetyl (Protein N-term);Oxidation (M)". Oxidation (M) is corroborated by the `Oxidation (M)Sites.txt` site table and the `Oxidation (M) site IDs` columns.
- Fragment tolerance: `0.5 Da`, the MaxQuant ITMS MS/MS tolerance in parameters.txt. MS2 was acquired in the ion trap.
- Precursor tolerance: `not available` (see Limitations).

## Limitations

- **Dual-species organism (representation choice).** `characteristics[organism]` is single-valued in `build`, which cannot emit the repeated organism column that community files such as PXD006003 and PXD052080 use for mixed-species samples. The dual-species rows therefore carry the common ancestor `Bacteria` (NCBITaxon:2). This is true but less specific. The two species and strains are named in `characteristics[growth condition]` and the factor value. A curator may prefer two `characteristics[organism]` columns, the first `Fusobacterium nucleatum subsp. nucleatum ATCC 25586` and the second `Porphyromonas gingivalis ATCC 33277`, with `not applicable` in the second column for mono-species rows.
- **Strains.** Strain-level taxa come from the PRIDE organism/sample attributes (NEWT:190304, NEWT:431947) and the data-processing text, which names the "type strain ATCC 25586 / ATCC 33277" search databases. The strain used for culture is inferred to be the same, because the full text was unavailable.
- **`characteristics[growth condition]`.** This EFO:0000523 term is listed in TERMS.tsv for the `plants` template. It is used here as an extra characteristic to carry biofilm composition. The wording of the values is the annotator's, based on the title and abstract. The 4-day static biofilm age is stated in the protocol but not encoded.
- **Replicate numbers.** These come from the file-name digit. The file names and MaxQuant experiment names do not say whether `Fn_ECM1` and `FnPg_ECM1` were grown in parallel or form a batch. No batch columns were added.
- **Inferred and unavailable values.**
  - `organism part`, `cell type` and `disease` are `not applicable` (in-vitro bacterial culture).
  - `material type` and `sample type` were omitted: no allowed value fits extracellular polymeric matrix, and `study sample` is rejected by CI.
  - Precursor mass tolerance is `not available`. The deposited MaxQuant 1.5 parameters.txt does not record it, and the protocol only says "default settings". The MaxQuant default would be 20 ppm first search and 4.5 ppm main search, but that is not asserted.
- **Acetyl (Protein N-term).** This variable modification is read from the "Modifications included in protein quantification" line, not from a "Variable modifications" field, which MaxQuant 1.5 parameters.txt lacks. It agrees with MaxQuant defaults and the stated "default settings", but no `mqpar.xml` or `summary.txt` was deposited to confirm it directly.
- **Instrument.** The model is taken from the method block in the raw-file header. The run-header instrument-ID record was not located with range reads. An `Orbitrap Velos Pro` string also appears inside the embedded method's OLE configuration block. Every method-level field (instrument name, tune file, folder name, 240K resolution) points to the Orbitrap Elite.
- **Paper.** The full text (Elsevier, not OA; Unpaywall found no OA copy) was not read. Sample facts rest on the PRIDE protocols, the abstract and the deposited search files.
