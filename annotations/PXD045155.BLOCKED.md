# PXD045155 — annotation blocked

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE exposes 61 individual Orbitrap Exploris 480 RAW files, matching the publication cohort total of 11 people living with HIV-1, 23 with HIV-2, and 27 HIV-seronegative participants.
- RAW-header byte-range inspection recovers internal sample identifiers such as `EJ_S219` and `DDBS329755`.
- The publication further divides HIV-2 participants into nine viremic and fourteen aviremic individuals and documents plasma preparation, DIA acquisition, and search parameters.
- The deposit contains no sample sheet assigning the internal identifiers to HIV status or viremia group.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The central experimental factor—HIV-1, HIV-2 viremic, HIV-2 aviremic, or seronegative—cannot be assigned to each RAW file. Matching 61 files to 61 participants by order would be unsupported and would risk exposing incorrect clinical metadata.

## Unblocking requirement

The de-identified clinical design table mapping every `EJ_S...`/`DDBS...` identifier to HIV type, viremia class, and the corresponding deposited RAW filename.
