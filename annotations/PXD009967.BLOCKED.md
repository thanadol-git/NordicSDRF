# PXD009967 annotation blocked

PXD009967 is a seed-beetle reproductive-proteomics study with 19 deposited RAW files. The public PRIDE record describes several biological material classes (15N-labelled female reproductive tracts, male accessory glands, testes, and ejaculates), but the RAW files are only named with opaque `MS-15-051c-*` and `MS-15-051d-*` acquisition codes. No accessible sample sheet or plain manifest maps those codes to the described material classes.

Evidence checked: PRIDE / ProteomeXchange PXD009967 public project and archive views; Bayram *et al.*, *Insect Biochemistry and Molecular Biology* (2019), PMID 30529580, DOI 10.1016/j.ibmb.2018.12.002; and the archive's 19 RAW, 19 peak, 19 search, and 20 auxiliary files.

Unblock with the original experiment-design sheet or an author-confirmed mapping from every `MS-15-051c-*` / `MS-15-051d-*` RAW file to biological material and replicate.
