## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- HCD written as MS:1000422 (beam-type collision-induced dissociation), the spec-canonical term.
- INFERRED: 'Set_2_Patient_N' (stroma) is the same person as 'Patient_N' (parenchyma, set 1). Basis: 5 + 9 (PC_06–PC_14) = 14 patients, matching the deposited P1-5 / P6-14 protein lists; if Set 2 were different people the cohort would be 19.
- Benign-appearing compartments are annotated disease = normal with phenotype 'tumour-adjacent…'; the donors all have PDAC.
- Search settings are not stated in PRIDE and the paper is closed. Carbamidomethyl C (fixed) is annotated from the IAA alkylation and the PRIDE PTM list; PRIDE also lists carbamoylated residue (MOD:00398), but whether carbamylation or other variable modifications were searched is unknown, so none is added.
- Age and sex per patient not available.
- 'Tube_11_HealthyAcinarHNE' (patient 1) is kept as a second benign-parenchyma source; the meaning of 'HNE' is unknown.
