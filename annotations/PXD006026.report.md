## Design choices

- Jurkat: control and cisplatin x 3 biological replicates (1, 3, 4), each acquired at 25 and 35 NCE; the two energies are recorded as technical replicates 1 and 2 with `comment[collision energy]`.
- `factor value[treatment]` = cisplatin vs `not applicable` (HeLa, LNCaP and Jurkat control). The factor is only meaningful within Jurkat.
- `organism part` is `not applicable` for cell lines because `sdrf-tools reconcile` flags a tissue value as a BLOCKER; the tissue of origin is not lost from cell line / disease columns.

## Limitations

- Paper full text not read (not open access); abstract only.
- Age and sex come from Cellosaurus, not the deposit. HeLa disease uses a generic label (cervical adenocarcinoma).
- K = control and Cis = cisplatin, and replicate numbers (R3, R4, "1st"), are inferred from run names. `CJK_150824_Hela_4h_3` treated as a 4th HeLa digest; "4h" unexplained.
- Phosphopeptide enrichment of Jurkat not annotated (no verified enrichment term); Phospho and Carbamidomethyl are not in the deposit's stated search parameters, so omitted. Raw/Mascot .dat headers not inspected, so DDA and HCD dissociation (for HeLa/LNCaP) are assumed.
- Collision energy for HeLa/LNCaP, passage number, culture medium and storage temperature not stated.
