## Evidence collected

- 9 RAW files (Orbitrap Elite, negative-mode glycan methods NEG_Glycan_… / NEG-NOexcl_HCD80…)
- Deposited SampleListPRIDE.txt: 5 runs are IDURON disaccharide standards (ΔUA-GalNAc 4S/6S …) and 4 runs are chondroitinase ABC digests of XylNap/XylNap-d7-primed GAGs from CCD and HCC70 cells
- PRIDE data processing: 'Data was assessed manually using the Xcalibur 3 software'; no peptide search

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

The data are LC-MS/MS of glycosaminoglycan disaccharides and oligosaccharides, not peptides: no protease digestion, no protein database search, no proteomics acquisition. The SDRF-Proteomics templates (cleavage agent, modification parameters, label) do not describe these runs.
