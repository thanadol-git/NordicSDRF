# PXD036514 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE contains two multiplexed *Caulobacter crescentus* experiments: a Lon pulldown (`20180515_LJKJDO_pulldownTMT10_3hrgrad_r1.raw`) and a TMTpro 16-plex LarA-overexpression experiment (`20210324_KGKJDO1-16_minipool_relabel_3hrgrad_r1.raw`). The associated paper describes the pulldown's no-Lon, Lon-WT, and inactive Lon-TRAP conditions, and the LarA experiment's vector-control versus 3xFLAG-LarA cultures, with non-induced, 2-hour, and 4-hour timepoints.
- **Why blocked:** The deposited result workbooks, PRIDE metadata, and published methods do not map any TMT/TMTpro reporter channel to a condition or biological replicate. Each study has more than one condition and multiple replicates; translating the reported design into individual channel rows would invent their source-to-assay links.
- **Unblocking requirement:** TMT run sheets mapping every reporter channel in both RAW files to its sample, including pulldown bait/control and replicate for the first run, and vector/LarA status, xylose timepoint, and replicate for the second run.
