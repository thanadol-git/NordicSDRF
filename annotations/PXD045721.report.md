# PXD045721 annotation review

The six-run draft matches the deposited design table and `raw.zip` vendor directories and passes the official SDRF validator. Independent review did **not** approve it. The deposited design repeats `ATR_BR1_LAD2` and `control_BR1_LAD2` twice each, while PRIDE calls the experiments triplicates. There is no source-level evidence that each pair is a reinjection of the same preparation, so the draft's technical replicate assignments are unsupported.

The reviewer also found that the LAD 2 line has a [Cellosaurus record](https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_0387) supplying its accession and donor metadata; those should be incorporated in any corrected annotation. The draft and evidence manifest have been moved to `scratchpad/PXD045721/` and are **not approved for contribution or commit**. The independent review is `scratchpad/PXD045721/independent_review.json`.

Resolution requires a run-level clarification of the repeated BR1 labels or a source-backed way to represent their unresolved biological and technical replicate relationship.
