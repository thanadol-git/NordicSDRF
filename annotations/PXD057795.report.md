# PXD057795 annotation report

Murine PDAC tumours: orthotopic allografts of UN-KC-6141 cells in C57BL/6 mice, 6 tumours. PROMEC/NTNU. Paper: Sci Data 2025, PMC11828881. Its Table 2 links this PXD to "Murine PDAC tumour tissues". Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `vertebrates` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 6 rows: `Tumor_1`–`Tumor_6`, one tumour per mouse (the paper used six PDAC mice).

The matching normal pancreas tissues are in PXD057798, which is not part of this batch.

## Values
- **Sample:**
  - Organism part `pancreas`; disease `pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma`; material type `tissue`.
  - Treatment: orthotopic injection of 2.5×10⁵ UN-KC-6141 cells at passage 32 (paper).
  - Mice were 5–8 weeks old at injection, and both sexes were used, but not per sample. Age, sex and developmental stage are therefore `not available`.
- **Instrument and search:** same as PXD057793. The raw header says **Q Exactive** (method `20160505_240min_70ul-PCE_FullMS_MS2_Top20`), not the Q Exactive HF in PRIDE or the timsTOF Pro in the paper. The MaxQuant search is shared with PXD057793.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf` passes for both templates; warnings are free-text treatment and strain. The `tools reconcile` trypsin flag is the same false positive as in PXD057793.
