## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `human` v1.1.0 (label-free DDA on an Orbitrap Exploris 480 with FAIMS). Treatment and sampling time columns come with these templates.

## Design and file mapping
Kidney organoid spheroids (20 per sample) at day 25 of differentiation treated with 5 ng/mL TNFalpha or vehicle for 24 h or 48 h; three biological replicates per condition = 12 samples. File names `<date>_FD_Organoids_Set2_<24h|48h>_<TNFa|VC>-<rep>.raw` encode the condition directly (stated in PRIDE description: TNFalpha 24 h / 48 h vs vehicle control (VC)). Each sample has two RAW files, from acquisition dates 210702 and 210704, written as `technical replicate` 1 and 2. **The deposited MaxQuant search (`Set2_Pellet-MaxQuant-txt.zip`, mqpar.xml) only includes the 210702 files**, so the 210704 files may be re-injections not used for the published analysis; they are annotated because they are deposited runs with a clear sample. 24 RAW files, all annotated, no fractionation (FAIMS CVs -40/-55/-70 V were split into mzXML for the search; the SDRF lists RAW files).
Factors: `factor value[treatment]` and `factor value[sampling time]`.

## Technical values
- Orbitrap Exploris 480 (PRIDE record and RAW header of `210702_..._24h_TNFa-1.raw`).
- DDA (cycle-time driven MS2). HCD at NCE 28: **inferred from the acquisition method file name in the RAW header** (`2.5h_3CVs_2col_HCD28_CV40_55_70_optLC.meth`), not stated in the PRIDE text.
- Trypsin (PRIDE protocol; MaxQuant `Trypsin/P`). TCEP reduction and chloroacetamide alkylation (PRIDE protocol).
- From mqpar.xml / parameters.txt read by HTTP range from the deposited zip (MaxQuant 1.6.17.0; PRIDE text says 1.6.7.0): fixed Carbamidomethyl (C); variable Oxidation (M), Acetyl (protein N-term); main-search tolerance 4.5 ppm (first search 20 ppm), FTMS MS/MS tolerance 20 ppm; max 2 missed cleavages.
- No RAW file was downloaded in full (first 400 KB only).

## Limitations
- The iPSC/ESC line is not stated for this deposit (the paper uses UM77-2 female ESC and NEPTUNE 19A male iPSC for different experiments), so cell line, sex, age and cell type are `not available` (same as PXD029696).
- `organism part` is the free-text `kidney organoid` (organoid, not a native organ); disease `not available`.
- The "Set2" label and the two acquisition dates are the only hints of batch structure; whether 210704 repeats the same lysate or a new preparation is not stated.
