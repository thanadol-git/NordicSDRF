## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- The RAW files are deposited only inside TOP3A_BioID_prox_proteins_raw_data.7z; comment[data file] gives the .raw names inside the archive.
- Only the first RAW file's header could be read (the archive is one solid LZMA2 stream); the other five are assumed to share its instrument method, consistent with the single MaxQuant run and the paper.
- Precursor mass tolerance is not stated in the paper or the saved MaxQuant parameters.txt (MaxQuant default main-search 4.5 ppm), so the column is omitted.
- Flp-In T-REx 293 is HEK293-derived (female, fetal kidney per Cellosaurus); age and developmental stage are 'not available'.
- The BioID Methods do not name the cell line. Flp-In T-REx 293 is inferred: 'Cell culture and transfection' lists Flp-In T-REx 293 and U2OS cells, the BioID constructs are doxycycline-induced (the Flp-In T-REx system, pcDNA5/FRT/TO) and the paper's other inducible TOP3A experiments use stably transfected Flp-In T-REx 293. That the COX8 control is likewise a stable Flp-In line is assumed.
