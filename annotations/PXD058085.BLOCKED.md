# PXD058085 — blocked after two independent review rounds

The deposited `MSfiles.xlsx` maps 20 Bruker `.d` acquisitions in two RAW ZIPs: 10 liver and 10 mesenteric artery runs. Both draft SDRFs pass `parse_sdrf` template validation, structure, offline ontology checks, and project-record reconciliation. Neither has an independent PASS receipt.

The [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD058085) states that all samples were digested with a Trypsin/LysC mix. The active `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 template permits one `comment[cleavage agent details]` value. In the first review round, both drafts encoded only Lys-C and reviewers identified the absent trypsin as an important omission. In the second round, both drafts encoded the two enzymes as repeated `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns; reviewers found that violates the template's single-value cardinality, despite the official validator passing.

The two unapproved second-round drafts and evidence manifests are in `scratchpad/PXD058085/`. Resolution requires a schema-compatible way to represent the mixed digest, an authoritative template change permitting repeated cleavage-agent values, or an explicit decision to represent a single enzyme with the co-digest documented outside the controlled column. Any revised SDRF needs a fresh independent review and hash-bound gate receipt before commit.

The project structured organism is `Rattus rattus` (taxid 10117), but its processing protocol says the search used a *Rattus norvegicus* database, the deposited FASTA filename carries taxid 10116, and the [publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39653302/) indexes Sprague-Dawley rats. The drafts use *Rattus norvegicus* and document the conflict. The workbook and both Bruker `SampleInfo.xml` files label the last two treated artery runs `mA_P4`; no `mA_P5` acquisition is deposited, so the drafts do not infer one.

The project specifies DIA-PASEF, but the active offline `parse_sdrf` validator rejects its documented `PRIDE:0000650` term and plain label. The drafts use the accepted generic data-independent acquisition value and document the precise method in evidence.
