# PXD030521 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Aggregation as a host defense mechanism
- **PRIDE record:** PXD030521
- **Publication:** Petrlova J. et al., iScience 2023, DOI [10.1016/j.isci.2023.107951](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2023.107951), PMCID [PMC10561040](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10561040/)

## Evidence recovered

- 35 RAW files (Q Exactive in RAW headers; PRIDE lists Orbitrap Fusion): 31 `181005_SUP_A<x><y><A|B>` and 4 `181011_Pellet<n>_<A|B>` (Pellet1_B, Pellet2_A, Pellet2_B, Pellet3_A).
- Methods: LPS-aggregated pellets and supernatants of acute wound fluids from four patients, each lane cut out after SDS-PAGE and digested in-gel; 'four biological replicates and their technical duplicates'.
- The deposited Mascot export summarises merged searches SUP1–4 and Pellet1–4.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The deposition does not match the described design: the paper implies 16 pellet and supernatant runs, but 31 supernatant runs with an undocumented second index (`<y>` = 1–4) and only 4 pellet runs are deposited, and Pellet4 is missing entirely although it was searched. Assigning `<y>` to gel slices and A/B to duplicate injections would be a guess, and half the pellet data are absent.
