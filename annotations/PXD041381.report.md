# PXD041381 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"Mouse liver sinusoidal endothelial cell responses to the glucocorticoid receptor agonist dexamethasone" (Front Pharmacol 2024, doi:10.3389/fphar.2024.1377136, PMID 39439887, PMC11494038). Tromsø (K.K. Sørensen lab, UiT). The study cultures primary mouse LSECs (CD146 MACS) for 1 h (untreated), and for 10 h or 48 h with or without 1 µM dexamethasone. There are 5 samples per biological replicate, with one TMT6plex mix per replicate. Each replicate pools LSECs from 4–5 male C57BL/6J livers. Each mix is fractionated by high-pH RP into 21 pooled fractions and acquired with SPS-MS3 on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + vertebrates v1.1.0.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
- PRIDE lists 33 RAW files and `proteinGroups.txt`. **All 33 RAW files are annotated**, giving 165 rows (33 files × 5 channels).
  - TMT7 (replicate 1): fractions A1–A12 only. The other 9 fractions are not deposited.
  - TMT8 (replicate 2): A1–A12 and B4–B12, i.e. 21 fractions, numbered 1–21 in that order.
- **Not deposited:** replicate 3 (MaxQuant experiment TMT9) has no RAW files. It is present only in proteinGroups.

## Channel map (how it was derived)
1. **MaxQuant channel → condition.** Each deposited `Reporter intensity corrected <i> <TMTn>` column was correlated with the condition-named columns of Supplementary Table S1 (`NT_1h_TMT_Exp1`, ...) after centring each protein within its run. Every run gave a unique match with mean r = 1.000: TMT7 = Exp1, TMT8 = Exp2, TMT9 = Exp3. The layouts differ between runs:
   - TMT7: idx0 NT 1h, idx1 NT 10h, idx2 Dex 10h, idx3 NT 48h, idx4 Dex 48h.
   - TMT8: idx0 NT 48h, idx1 Dex 48h, idx2 NT 1h, idx3 NT 10h, idx4 Dex 10h.
2. **MaxQuant index → TMT mass.** MaxQuant reports only 5 channels. The MS3 reporter ions in one RAW file per run were summed with ThermoRawFileParser. The empty channel differs between the runs:
   - TMT7 (`TMT7_A6`): 127 is empty (226 spectra vs about 16,500 for the others), so idx0–4 = 126, 128, 129, 130, 131.
   - TMT8 (`TMT8_A5`): 129 is empty (329 vs about 20,000), so idx0–4 = 126, 127, 128, 130, 131.
   - The relative per-channel totals in proteinGroups follow the RAW profile in both runs (e.g. the dip at 128 in TMT8).

## Key decisions
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Fusion Lumos (MS:1002732), from the RAW header, protocol and PRIDE. Method `TMT6_TKO_6plex_SPS_140min`.
- **Acquisition:** dissociation is recorded as CID, the MS2 identification step (RAW header `Fragmentation types=CID`). MS3 reporter fragmentation is not a separate column. DDA, charge 2–7 (RAW metadata).
- **Digestion and reduction:** Lys-C then trypsin, stated. Reduction is DTT, stated. Alkylation is not stated, so it is `not available`.
- **Search parameters (as stated in the PRIDE data protocol and paper):** carbamidomethyl C, TMT6plex K and N-term fixed; oxidation M and protein N-term acetyl variable; 4.5 ppm precursor and 20 ppm fragment tolerance.
- **Fractionation:** high-pH reversed-phase. Fraction order is inferred from the well names (A1–A12, then B4–B12).
- **Sample properties:** liver; endothelial cell of hepatic sinusoid; material type cell; male; C57BL/6J from Charles River (proteomics). Age is 6–12 weeks at use, a range, so developmental stage is `not available`. `pooled` because each replicate is 4–5 livers.
- **Factors:** treatment (untreated / dexamethasone) and sampling time (1 / 10 / 48 hour).
