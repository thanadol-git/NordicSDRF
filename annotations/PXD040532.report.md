## Design and file mapping
Twelve breast cancer cell lines (BT474, BT549, HCC1143, HCC1187, HCC1428, HS578T, MDA-MB-231 [`MB231`], MDA-MB-468 [`MB468`], MCF7, T47D, ZR-75-1 [`ZR751`], ZR-75-30 [`ZR7530`]) under normoxia (`N`) or 24 h hypoxia (`H`), two replicates each = 48 RAW files `<line>_<H|N><rep>.raw`. The deposited `groups.txt` (MSstats group table) lists `Normox`/`Hypox` and PAM50-like subtype per file key and confirms the N/H reading. Conditioned medium (secretome) was concentrated (3 kDa filters), in-solution trypsin digestion. Factor: `factor value[treatment]` (normoxia vs hypoxia, 24 h). Subtype per line (Luminal/other) from `groups.txt` is not written as a column.
