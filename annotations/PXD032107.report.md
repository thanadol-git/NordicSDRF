# PXD032107 SDRF annotation report

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

## Study
"Proteomic analysis of cultured human podocytes stimulated with TNFa - cell pellet" (Rinschen lab, Aarhus; submitted by M. Lasse). Publication: Lasse et al., Nat Commun 2023, PMID 37580326. Conditionally immortalized human podocytes, 5 ng/mL TNFa vs PBS vehicle, 24 h and 48 h, 6 replicates each; label-free DDA, Q Exactive, MaxQuant 1.6.17.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0, human v1.1.0, cell-lines v1.1.0 (human cell line, MS, label free).

## Scope
24 RAW files deposited, 24 rows annotated (24 rows x 40 columns). Non-sample files (txt.zip, fasta, design template) are not rows. File-to-condition map comes from the deposited experimentalDesignTemplate.txt (files 1-6 TNFa 24h, 7-12 vehicle 24h, 13-18 TNFa 48h, 19-24 vehicle 48h). Factors: treatment and sampling time.

## Key decisions
- Instrument: raw header reads "Q Exactive - Orbitrap MS" and protocol says QExactive, so MS:1001911 was used; PRIDE metadata says Q Exactive HF. Reviewer should weigh this.
- Modifications read from MaxQuant parameters/summary: Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed; Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (protein N-term) variable. Enzyme Trypsin/P, single protease (no Lys-C), so one cleavage-agent column.
- Fragment tolerance 20 ppm (MaxQuant FTMS). Precursor tolerance not in deposited parameters: not available (the "default for orbitraps" is stated only in prose).
- Cell line CIHP-1 / CVCL_W186 (synonym AB8/13): inferred from the cited Saleem podocyte line; not stated by name in the deposit.
- disease = normal (healthy-kidney-derived line; inferred). age, sex, storage temperature: not available. Passage number is not stated and the column is omitted.
- Sampling time written as "24 hour"/"48 hour" (validator unit list); treatment written as free text.
