# PXD017420 — annotation blocked

Metaproteome of extracellular vesicles (EVs) from a pig faecal microbial community grown in vitro with spruce β-mannan (AcGGM) or without an added carbohydrate (negative control). NMBU, Ås. Paper: Microorganisms 2020, PMC7409281 (open access).

## What is known
- **Runs:** 4 raw files (`180112_Leidly_01–04`), Q Exactive at the UiO core.
- **Design:** two biological replicates of EVs per treatment (paper); in-solution trypsin; MaxQuant 1.4.1.2.
- **Search database:** pig-colon MAGs plus *Sus scrofa*.

## Why it is blocked
Nothing maps runs 01–04 to treatment (β-mannan vs control) or replicate:
- No MaxQuant design or summary is deposited; only a Perseus session (`.sps`), whose readable strings contain protein IDs only.
- The paper does not list the run order.

## What would unblock it
The run → treatment/replicate map.
