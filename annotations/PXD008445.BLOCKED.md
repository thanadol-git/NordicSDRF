# PXD008445 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Patients with early stage oropharyngeal squamous cell carcinoma can be identified with label-free serum proteomics
- **PRIDE record:** PXD008445
- **Publication:** Jethwa *et al.*, *Molecular & Cellular Proteomics* (2018), PMID [29961760](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29961760/)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits 97 compressed vendor RAW archives (`*.raw.zip`).
- The submitted `samples.csv` supplies serum identifiers and clinical groups for 84 rows.
- Matching the sheet's `File name` values to the underlying `*.raw` basenames maps only 70 of the 97 deposited RAW archives.
- Twenty-seven archived RAW basenames have no sample-sheet row, while 14 sample-sheet rows have no deposited matching archive.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The submitted mapping is incomplete and internally inconsistent at the accession level.  Creating rows for only the 70 matched files, or assigning groups to the other 27 by acquisition numbering, would produce incomplete or fabricated metadata.

## Source artifacts

- `scratchpad/PXD008445/evidence/pride.json`
- `scratchpad/PXD008445/evidence/files.json`
- `scratchpad/PXD008445/evidence/samples.csv`

## Unblocking requirement

A reconciled sample manifest that maps every deposited RAW archive to a serum sample and states whether unmatched sheet rows are intentionally absent or should have corresponding files.
