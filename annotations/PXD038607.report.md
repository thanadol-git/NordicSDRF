# PXD038607 annotation report

## Evidence used

- PRIDE metadata identifies a human neuronal-cell PISA experiment on simulated traumatic brain injury.
- The deposited protocol explicitly maps channels 1–8 in each of three TMTpro sets to control, 312, 625, 1250, 2500, 5000, 10,000, and 20,000 g samples, respectively.
- RAW filenames encode all three multiplex sets as Rep01–Rep03 and each of their 12 concatenated fractions. The protocol specifies Lys-C/trypsin digestion, iodoacetamide alkylation, Q Exactive HF data-dependent acquisition, and 12 fractions per set.

## Annotation scope

The SDRF represents all 36 deposited RAW files crossed with the eight study channels (288 assay rows). Channel numbers 1–8 are represented by their sequential TMT reporter labels 126, 127N, 127C, 128N, 128C, 129N, 129C, and 130N. The remaining 16-plex channels are not assigned by the protocol and are omitted.
