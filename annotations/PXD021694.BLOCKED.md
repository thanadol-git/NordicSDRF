# PXD021694 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The deposited Proteome Discoverer export resolves the sample-to-TMT-channel layouts for three sets, but neither it nor the publication maps those sets (`F1`, `F2`, and `F3`) to the 60 deposited RAW filenames. All RAW files share the prefix `Fusion_181018_` and form one acquisition sequence with gaps. Dividing the sorted filenames into three consecutive blocks of 20 would be a plausible reconstruction, but it is not stated by a primary source and therefore cannot be used as a sample/run mapping.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE project record and protocols: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD021694`
- Complete PRIDE file listing: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD021694/files/all`
- Deposited `PDexport_results_Isolationandcharacterizationofextracellularvesiclesfromtissues.xlsx`: `https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2020/11/PXD021694/PDexport_results_Isolationandcharacterizationofextracellularvesiclesfromtissues.xlsx`
- Open-access publication: `https://doi.org/10.1080/20013078.2020.1722433` (PMCID `PMC7034452`).
- The only other deposited non-RAW files are the 9.85 GB `.pdResult` and 13.88 GB `.msf` databases; there is no standalone file/set mapping or acquisition sequence table.
- Community annotation directory: absent (GitHub directory returned 404).

## Mapping facts that were resolved

- The workbook identifies three patient-specific TMT sets, a TMT126 reference pool in each set, the populated reporter channels, and all 18 study samples.
- It maps samples to larger/smaller EV fractions and low-density/high-density subfractions for patients 1-3.
- PRIDE and the paper resolve Orbitrap Fusion MultiNotch MS3 acquisition, trypsin, search tolerances, and TMT modification settings.

## Why an SDRF was not written

Without a source connecting each `Fusion_181018_*.raw` file to set `F1`, `F2`, or `F3`, every row's reporter-channel identities would depend on an inferred 20-file boundary. A supportable SDRF requires the Proteome Discoverer study/file table, acquisition log, or equivalent export that explicitly links all 60 RAW filenames to the three TMT sets.
