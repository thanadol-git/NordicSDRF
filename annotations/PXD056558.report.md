## Design

## Limitations

- Disease / fibrosis group not assigned (not available) and no factor value: abstract defines control/moderate/severe (9 each = 27 mares) but 35 mares were deposited, the paper full text and supplements are paywalled, and the deposit has no map. This is the key gap; a reviewer with paper access should add it.
- Sex female from "mares"; age, breed, developmental stage not available.
- Mare IDs from file names; same ID in tissue and plasma assumed to be one animal; S1-S3 treated as biological replicates (protocol: "biological triplicates").
- Instrument verified from RAW header (Orbitrap Eclipse). Modifications from protocol prose, not search results (Spectronaut .sne archives 71 GB/3.8 GB not downloaded); N-term acetyl as protein N-term is assumed.
- Tolerances not available. FAIMS (-45 V CV), Evosep gradient, tissue mass not captured.
