# PXD009426 — blocked: preparation labels are not mapped to tissue/species and digestion arms

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD009426
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD009426/files/all
- Linked PubMed record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29812944/ (DOI: 10.1021/acs.jproteome.8b00228)

## Evidence available

PRIDE describes a urea Lys-C/trypsin digestion-method study on Q Exactive data involving human melanocytes and rat pancreas/spleen. The manifest contains 74 RAW files with labels such as `XG_RT`, `XG_37`, `SP_RT`, `SP_37`, and `LysC-Tryp_25C`.

## Blocking gap

The linked Journal of Proteome Research paper has no public PMC full text or supplementary sample table. PRIDE does not define `XG`, `SP`, or the numbered Lys-C/trypsin aliases; map them to species/tissue; or specify the exact urea, temperature, enzyme, and replicate assignments. The partial filename labels are insufficient to annotate a mixed-species study safely.

## Unblock requirement

Provide a sample/run sheet connecting every RAW file to source material, organism, digestion condition, temperature, enzyme sequence, and biological/technical replicate.
