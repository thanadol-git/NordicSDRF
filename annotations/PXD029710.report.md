## Design and mapping

Human serum fractionated by nanodiamond solid-phase extraction plus Triton X-114 cloud-point separation
at pH 3, 7 and 11, each split into two phases, compared with unfractionated crude serum (PX
description). `summary.txt` names seven MaxQuant experiments — `Control`, `A3`, `A7`, `A11`, `D3`,
`D7`, `D11` — which match the seven deposited RAW names one-to-one (`Control Sample.raw`,
`3A fraction.raw` … `11D fraction.raw`). The MaxQuant raw-file names (`CTS_180917`, `F1–F6_180817`)
differ from the deposited names; the mapping is by the experiment codes, not by those names.
