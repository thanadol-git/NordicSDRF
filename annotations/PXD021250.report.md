# PXD021250 annotation report

Kandasamy lab (CEMIR / IKOM, NTNU, Trondheim). Resting vs anti-CD3/CD28-activated primary CD4+ T cells from two healthy blood donors (St Olav's Blood Bank). Orbitrap Fusion, 6 SCX fractions × 3 injections. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0
- `human` v1.1.0

## Rows and files
74 rows, one per deposited `.raw` (all 74 covered). The donor and replicate mapping comes from the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt`:
- Files named `CD4_T_naive_SCX_*` (no donor tag) belong to Experiment `Naive_D1_*`. They are annotated as donor D1.
- `SCX_n` maps to `comment[fraction identifier]`. MaxQuant wrote fraction 32767 for `naive_D2_SCX_5_R3`; the file name gives 5, and 5 was used.
- `R1`–`R3` map to `comment[technical replicate]` 1–3. These are repeat runs of the same SCX fractions; summary.txt groups them as separate Experiments within each donor.
- Four deposited runs carry a suffix. The timestamp suffixes are Xcalibur's automatic rename when a run is re-acquired under an existing file name. All four are in the instrument folder `2018/June/CD4_Naive_R2`, per the raw headers.
  - `naive_D2_SCX_2_R2_180609174641` and `naive_D2_SCX_5_R1_newcolumn` are deposited **alongside** their plain runs. They get technical replicate **4** so the row key stays unique.
  - `naive_D2_SCX_1_R1_180608223021` and `naive_D2_SCX_4_R2_180613013832` are the **only** deposited copies, so they keep R1 and R2. MaxQuant's summary.txt also lists a plain `naive_D2_SCX_1_R1` with only 525 MS/MS, apparently an aborted run. That file is not in the PRIDE file list and returns 404 on the FTP, so it is not annotated.

## Values
- **Sample:** organism part `blood` (PBMC-derived) and cell type `CD4-positive, alpha-beta T cell` (CL:0000624). Disease is `normal`. Developmental stage is `adult`, at cohort level (blood donors). Age, sex and ancestry are `not available`.
- **Instrument and acquisition:**
  - The instrument is Orbitrap Fusion, confirmed from the raw header.
  - Dissociation is HCD, from `ActivationType = HCD` in the raw method.
  - Fractionation is SCX (PRIDE:0000561).
- **Sample prep and search:**
  - Reduction was DTT and alkylation was IAA (paper Methods).
  - Tolerances are 20 ppm / 20 ppm (paper; parameters.txt FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm).
  - Modifications come from `summary.txt`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.
- **Factor:** `factor value[treatment]` = activated vs unstimulated. The values are free text, and parse_sdrf warns that they are not NCIT/EFO terms.

## Caveats
- The paper (PMID 33383959) cites PXD015872 for the CD4+ data. PXD015872 is a different study from the same lab (PMA-differentiated THP-1 cells, its own paper PMID 34234780) and contains 26 `THP1_DML_*` files and no CD4 runs. PRIDE links both PXD021250 and the SUP-T1 dataset PXD021272 to PMID 33383959, and PXD021250's run names match the paper's design. The citation in the paper is wrong; PXD021250 is the CD4+ T-cell deposit.
- `comment[collision energy]` was left out. The paper says 35% NCE, but the raw method text could not be parsed unambiguously.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf validate-sdrf` passes for ms-proteomics and human (OLS cache). The only warnings are the free-text treatment values. `tools reconcile` reports no disagreements. Adversarial review was not run.
