# PXD050187 BLOCKED: TMT10 channel-to-sample map not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Department of Biomedicine, Aarhus University (lab PI Bent Honoré). Mouse model of systemic chronic immune activation: female C57BL/6 mice infected with LCMV clone 13 (2 x 10^6 PFU i.v.) vs naive age-matched controls, sacrificed at 1, 8 and 28 weeks (day 7/56/196); neuroretina, RPE/choroid, kidney and spleen analysed. Per tissue and time point one TMT10plex (five infected + five control mice), high-pH fractionation into 8 fractions, each fraction injected twice, Orbitrap Fusion SPS-MS3 (12 plexes x 8 fractions x 2 = 192 RAW, named `<1-16>_<Retina|RPE_choroid|Kidney|Spleen>_<1w|8w|28w>_Fr<1-8>_<1|2>.raw`). Paper: Frontiers in Immunology 2024, DOI 10.3389/fimmu.2024.1374617, PMC11043527 (open access, read).

## Why it cannot be annotated
A row needs (file x TMT channel) -> infected or control, and which mouse. Both PRIDE and the paper say only "five infected mice and five control mice for each experiment were labelled with TMT10plex". Which of the ten reporter channels (126-131) carries infected vs control, and whether the assignment is the same in all twelve plexes, is not stated. Ladder checked:
1. PRIDE record and 204-file list: 192 RAW plus 12 MaxQuant `proteinGroups_<tissue>_FP58xx.txt` (no sample sheet, no readme).
2. The deposited `proteinGroups` headers: reporter columns are `Reporter intensity corrected 1..10` plus MaxQuant experiment names such as `Kidney LCMV FP5868_1`, `FP5869 LCMV_1`, `Kidney FP5911AB LVMV` - experiment labels per plex/fraction, no channel-to-group labels.
3. Europe PMC full text (PMC11043527): design and counts only; per-channel layout is not in the text. Supplementary Tables S1-S4 (protein lists) and Figure S1 (SRM) could not be checked from here and are unlikely to hold the layout.
4. No Zenodo/figshare/GitHub repository is cited in Data Availability beyond PRIDE.
Assigning 126-130 = infected / 131 = control (or any other split) would be invention.

## Verified facts (for a future annotation once the map is found)
Mus musculus, female C57BL/6 (Taconic), 6-10 weeks at start; tissues retina, RPE/choroid, kidney, spleen (S-Trap digestion with trypsin, TCEP/iodoacetamide); TMT10plex; Orbitrap Fusion (PRIDE), MS1 350-1500 m/z at 120,000, SPS-MS3.
