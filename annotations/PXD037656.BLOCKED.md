# PXD037656 BLOCKED: group/channel definitions not recoverable (method-comparison deposit)

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Department of Biomedicine, University of Bergen (Hernandez-Valladares / Berven; PROBE). "Mass spectrometry-based proteomics workflows in cancer research: the relevance of choosing the right steps" (Cancers 2023, DOI 10.3390/cancers15020555, PMC9856946, open access, read). HeLa cells ("HeLa_AXL"), SDS lysis, SP3 digestion with trypsin, Ti-IMAC phosphopeptide enrichment; 111 RAW from an Orbitrap Eclipse comparing DDA, DDA-library (high-pH fractions), cDIA and HRMS1-DIA for proteome and phosphoproteome (5 `C20-24` and 5 `T20-24` runs per mode, `_020322` proteome and `_090322` phospho dates), plus fractionated TMT10 proteome (`F1…F12`) and TMT10 phosphoproteome (`A…`, unfractionated) runs.

## Why it cannot be annotated
1. **What `C` and `T` are is not stated.** PRIDE, the deposited file names and the (review) paper text never define the two HeLa groups behind `C20-C24` / `T20-T24` (the text mentions no AXL experiment at all), so a factor value cannot be written without guessing (control vs treated is an assumption).
2. **TMT10 channels.** The TMT10 proteome (12 fractions) and phospho runs have no channel-to-sample table anywhere (the `.msf` results were not opened; PD defaults to generic channel names in this campaign).
3. The method-comparison design (the same HeLa digests injected with DDA/cDIA/HRMS1-DIA, plus library fractions `HeLa_AXL_DDA_library_DIA_fraction_*` and phospho library `…PHOSPHOlibrary_DIA_B*/C*`) would need a per-run acquisition-parameter record (DIA windows, FAIMS/cDIA settings) that is only in the Spectronaut `.sne` archives (1.7-19 GB, not opened).

## Verified facts for a future annotation
Homo sapiens, HeLa (SDS lysis, SP3, trypsin); phosphopeptides by MagReSyn Ti-IMAC HP; only TMT and phospho samples fractionated by offline high-pH RP; Orbitrap Eclipse; analyses with Proteome Discoverer (DDA/TMT) and Spectronaut (DIA). Possible unblock: author contact (maria.hernandez@uib.no) or the Spectronaut condition setups inside the `.sne` files.
