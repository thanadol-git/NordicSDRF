# PXD008676 — blocked: labelled RAW aliases lack experimental-condition mapping

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD008676
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD008676/files/all
- Linked PubMed record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29628141/ (DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2018.03.008)

## Evidence available

PRIDE describes dimethyl-label proteomics connected to pseudouridylation/tRNA-derived fragments in human embryonic stem-cell systems, acquired on an Orbitrap Fusion. The manifest contains 12 RAW files labelled with 293T or H9, S1–S3, and run number.

## Blocking gap

The linked Cell paper has no public PMC full text or supplementary data. PRIDE does not define the meaning of S1–S3, map files to PUS7 perturbation or control states, identify dimethyl-label channels, or establish biological/technical replicate structure. Filename labels alone are inadequate evidence.

## Unblock requirement

Provide the proteomics sample sheet defining cell line, PUS7/tRF condition, label channel, and replicate for each of the 12 RAW files.
