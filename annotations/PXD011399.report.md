## Design and file mapping

Rats with leukemia from an AML-like model were compared with healthy rats. Efferent lymph was collected by cannulating lymphatic vessels from the spleen and the liver, and pooled plasma was collected from each group. Each sample was digested with trypsin and run once.

| sample number | group | organism part | sampling site | biological replicate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 4, 7, 13 | normal | lymph | spleen | 1, 2, 3, 4 |
| 10, 16 | normal | lymph | liver | 1, 2 |
| 28, 34, 43, 46 | acute myeloid leukemia | lymph | spleen | 1, 2, 3, 4 |
| 31 | acute myeloid leukemia | lymph | liver | 1 |
| 49 | normal | blood plasma | blood | pooled |
| 203 | acute myeloid leukemia | blood plasma | blood | pooled |

- **The sample-to-file map comes from the deposit itself.** The deposited `readme.txt` lists each sample number with its group (for example "1 lymph from healthy spleen", "31 lymph from leukemic liver", "49 - pooled plasma, healthy"). Each RAW file is named `20150825_Eli_10ug_<number>_4ul.raw`.
- **Two cross-checks.** The MaxQuant `summary.txt` gives one experiment per file, named with that same number (1 … 203). The original acquisition path stored in every RAW header matches the deposited file name, so no file was renamed or swapped.
- **All 13 RAW files are annotated.** Nothing was left out.
- **Factor values.** `factor value[disease]` compares acute myeloid leukemia with normal. `factor value[sampling site]` compares spleen lymph, liver lymph and plasma. The abstract reports differences between leukemic and control lymph in each organ, and between lymph and plasma.

## Technical values

- **Instrument: Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523).** This comes from the RAW headers. All 13 files carry "Q Exactive HF - Orbitrap MS" and the method `Sensitive_TOP12HCD_120K-60K_2mmSH_IT5e4_90minRUN.meth`, recorded in the data folder `2015-08-25_QE-HF_P`. This contradicts the PRIDE record (see Limitations).
- **Acquisition.** DDA, Top12. HCD at NCE 28, with MS2 read in the Orbitrap at 60,000 resolution. MS1 range 375–1500 m/z; MS2 range 200–2000 m/z. Precursors of charge 2–5 were selected (the method excludes unassigned, 1 and 6 or higher). Source: RAW method block. MaxQuant `msms.txt` independently shows HCD/FTMS for every PSM read.
- **Cleavage agent: Trypsin (MS:1001251).** Source: the protocol; MaxQuant used Trypsin/P on every run. In `peptides.txt`, 98.6% of target peptides end in K or R, and the rest are protein C-termini.
- **Modifications.** Carbamidomethyl (C) is fixed; Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (protein N-term) are variable. Source: MaxQuant `parameters.txt` and `summary.txt`, consistent with the data processing protocol.
- **Tolerances.** Precursor 20 ppm (the protocol says "20 ppm in MaxQuant"). Fragment 20 ppm (MaxQuant "MS/MS tol. (FTMS)", which applies because MS2 was read in the Orbitrap).
- **Reduction and alkylation.** DTT (PRIDE:0000608) and iodoacetamide (PRIDE:0000599), from the PRIDE sample processing protocol.

## Limitations

- **The paper's full text was not read.** The Wiley article is open access under CC BY-NC-ND, but Wiley returns a Cloudflare challenge and the article is not in PMC or any repository that was found. Only the abstract and the PubMed MeSH headings were used.
- **The PRIDE protocol does not match the RAW files.** PRIDE `instruments` lists LTQ Orbitrap Velos (MS:1001742). The protocol text describes an LTQ-Orbitrap Velos Pro with ion-trap CID, a top-7 method, a 300–2000 m/z range and a 0.5 Da fragment tolerance. The RAW files show Q Exactive HF, Orbitrap HCD, top 12 and 375–1500 m/z. All acquisition values were taken from the RAW headers.
- **DTT and IAA rest on that protocol alone.** They come from the digestion part of the same protocol. Trypsin and carbamidomethylation are confirmed independently by the MaxQuant output, but DTT and IAA are not.
- **Disease is written as `acute myeloid leukemia`.** This follows the abstract ("rat model known to mimic human AML") and the MeSH major topic. PRIDE's broader "acute leukemia" (DOID:12603) was not used. Healthy rats are `normal`, following the readme's "healthy".
- **Strain, sex and developmental stage are `not available`.** None of the PRIDE record, the abstract or the MeSH headings (Animals, Humans, Rats, …) states them. The full text may contain them.
- **Biological replicates are partly inferred.** The readme maps every number to a group. That each numbered lymph sample comes from a different rat is inferred from the distinct sample numbers; no animal key is deposited. It is also unknown whether spleen and liver lymph came from the same animals. Replicates are therefore numbered 1..n within each disease × site group, in sample-number order.
- **The plasma pools are only partly described.** Samples 49 and 203 are `pooled` (biological replicate and pooled sample). The number of animals and their identities are not stated, so no `SN=` members are listed.
- **Sampling site `blood` for the plasma pools is inferred** from the material (plasma is derived from blood). The draw site is not stated.
- **The 20 ppm precursor tolerance may be the first-search value.** It comes from the protocol's statement about MaxQuant. MaxQuant 1.5.3.17 `parameters.txt` does not record the first or main search tolerances, so the 20 ppm may refer to the first search only.
- **Progenesis/SearchGUI is not described.** The protocol also mentions a Progenesis LC-MS analysis searched with SearchGUI (10 ppm / 0.5 Da), but only the MaxQuant search was deposited. The SDRF describes the MaxQuant search.
- **Two optional columns were omitted.** `comment[acquisition date]` was left out because the `20150825` prefix is the batch or folder date, and 13 runs of 90 minutes cannot all fall on that day. `characteristics[cell type]` was left out because it is not stated whether the lymph was cleared of cells before digestion.
