## Design decisions

- Instrument per file group: QE_160621_* = Q Exactive (MS:1001911), QEHF_171005_* = Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523). PRIDE lists only Q Exactive; both verified from UTF-16 strings in the first 3-4 MB of a RAW file (range read). Built as two tables and concatenated (identical headers).
- Acquisition DDA: RAW method names (Top10 ...meth) and MaxQuant search; not stated in the deposit text.
- Samples: one source per MaxQuant "Experiment" (summary.txt, read from txt.rar by range-reading the non-solid RAR entries parameters.txt and summary.txt). QE_160621_39 and _40 share Experiment 17, so they are one source with fractions 1 and 2.
- QEHF_171005_100/103/106 carry MaxQuant experiment names WT/TA/TE, mapped to gfp-stkP / gfp-stkP-7TA / gfp-stkP-7TE (paper: gel-band analysis of GFP-StkP-7TA and -7TE). That WT = gfp-stkP is an inference.
- Modifications from deposited parameters.txt/summary.txt: Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed; Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Phospho (STY) variable. Fragment tolerance 20 ppm (FTMS) from parameters.txt. Cleavage agent recorded as Trypsin (PRIDE protocol: trypsin digest; MaxQuant search enzyme was Trypsin/P, summary.txt).
- characteristics[genotype] is not a column of ms-proteomics (TERMS.tsv lists it for other templates); it was added so that the strain difference is captured, and it also feeds factor value[genotype]. The validator accepted it.

## Limitations

- The file-to-condition mapping for the 18 QE_160621 files (GFP-StkP grown with or without ampicillin, 1 ug/mL 15 min) is NOT in the deposit or the main text (it is in the supplement/Figs S2-S3, not retrievable). treatment is "not available" and the factor value[genotype] is constant (gfp-stkP) for these rows; the real compared variable (ampicillin) is not encoded. Replicate status of the 17 experiments is unknown; biological replicate = 1 for each source.
- Treatment of the QEHF samples is not stated (not available); enrichment process for QEHF not stated (gel band; not available). Whether the QEHF samples were phospho-enriched is not documented.
- Fractions 1,2 for exp17 follow MaxQuant's Fraction field; they could be repeat injections.
- Precursor tolerance and dissociation method not in the deposited files read (MaxQuant first/main search tolerances not in parameters.txt); recorded as not available. Not read from msms.txt (compressed, 23 MB).
- Organism part / cell type not available (bacterial cells, GFP-StkP purified by IP and SDS-PAGE gel band); disease not applicable. Strain R6 is in PRIDE organism NEWT:171101 but ms-proteomics has no strain column.
- Reduction DTT and alkylation iodoacetamide are from the PRIDE sample protocol.
- Supplementary methods (mbio.03799-24-s0001.pdf) were not available through Europe PMC; paper main text read.
