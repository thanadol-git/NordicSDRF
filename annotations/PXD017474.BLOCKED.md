# PXD017474 — annotation blocked

MIB-enriched signalome of haematological cells treated with the PCNA-targeting peptide ATX-101 (Otterlei group, NTNU; Q Exactive). Paper: Oncogene 2023, PMC9937922.

## Why it is blocked
- **Run names:** `MB41`–`46`, `MCCAR1`–`6` and `MONO5`–`10`, many with timestamped re-acquisitions, plus a QC BSA run.
- **What the paper says:** MC/CAR cells and primary monocytes from three donors were analysed. It defers treatment, replicate and time details to Supplementary Table S1, which is not available here. "MB41–46" is not explained anywhere.
- **Not good enough:** reading `1–3` as controls and `4–6` as treated (as in PXD033510) would be an inference, not evidence.
- **PRIDE metadata is wrong:** it lists the organism as *Mus musculus* and the tissue as embryonic kidney. The samples are human.

## What would unblock it
Supplementary Table S1 or a sample sheet giving the treatment and replicate for each code, and the identity of MB41–46.
