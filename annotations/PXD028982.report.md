# PXD028982 annotation evidence report

Status: Blocked: the 8 deposited RAWs mix four intact MUC1 mass measurements with four Asp-N glycopeptide MS/MS runs. Filenames identify glycoengineered reporter groups, but the intact runs use a different acquisition and preparation workflow from the peptide runs. The public record does not give a validated acquisition term and full per-run method mapping for a single consistent SDRF. A split by workflow and the raw acquisition methods are needed.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD028982) and [file list](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD028982/files/all). No SDRF has been approved for this accession.
