# PXD035331 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Aboo C, Lambertsen KL, …, Stensballe A, Hejbøl EK, Frich LH. "The effect of the nonselective TNF inhibitor etanercept and of the selective TNF inhibitor XPro1595 on lesioned supraspinatus muscle". Biosci Rep 2025;45(12):BSR20253559, doi:10.1042/BSR20253559, PMID 41230865 (Odense / SDU; MS at Aalborg, Stensballe). Female mice with an experimental supraspinatus tendon tear, sacrificed 5 days after surgery; ipsilateral (lesioned) and contralateral supraspinatus muscle; groups: saline, etanercept, XPro1595, untreated wild type, and TNF-KO. Label-free DDA on a timsTOF Pro (PASEF), MaxQuant 1.6.0.1. Sources: PRIDE protocols and the submitter's `PROTEOMICS_LHF.xlsx`.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + vertebrates v1.1.0. `characteristics[individual]` is used for the mouse ID because each mouse contributes two tissues (ipsi/contra).

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 96 files: 93 Bruker `.d.zip` RAW, MaxQuant txt zip, FASTA, sample sheet. The transcribed file list matched PRIDE by SHA-256. **Annotated: 77 runs.** **Not annotated: 16 runs from mice LHF3-2, -3, -4, -5 and -6**, which are absent from `PROTEOMICS_LHF.xlsx`; their genotype and treatment are unknown, so they are left out rather than guessed.

## Sample map
File names `<mouse><side>_<plate well>_01_<run no.>`: side `i/I` = ipsilateral (lesioned), `c/C` = contralateral. Sample sheet: LHF3-7…12 C57BL/6 with no treatment entered (the Treatment cell is empty; read as untreated), LHF3-13/14 TNF-KO, LHF4-1…5, 7 XPro1595, LHF4-8…14 etanercept, LHF4-15…20 saline; all female, 5 days survival. Where a tissue has two runs, they are technical replicates 1/2 (`comment[technical replicate]`). `LHF3-71_GC4_01_373` is read as a typo for `LHF3-7I` (LHF3-7 has only one other ipsilateral run and the `1` sits where I/C goes). biological replicate = mouse index within genotype × treatment.

- Strain C57BL/6, sex female, developmental stage adult; genotype wild type / Tnf knockout; treatment saline / etanercept / XPro1595 / no treatment.
- Factors: disease (side), genotype, treatment.
