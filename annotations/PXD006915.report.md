# PXD006915 annotation report

AY-27 rat bladder cancer cells, SILAC. Three treatment series:
- Bleomycin alone (BLM).
- Bleomycin delivered by photochemical internalisation with fimaporfin + blue light (BLMPCI).
- Fimaporfin + light alone (PDT).

The deposit also has endosome-enriched fractions. Gederaas/Slupphaug/Hagen, NTNU. Paper: Mol Omics 2023, 19:585 (doi 10.1039/d2mo00337f, open access CC BY-NC). Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `vertebrates` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 54 rows covering all 28 raw files. SILAC runs have two rows (light, heavy); heavy rows carry Lys8/Arg10 as Fixed.
- **BLM series:** `160822_SILAC2/3/4_bleomycinH_Odrun_tech1/2`, i.e. three experiments × 2 injections. The "bleomycinH" tag is read as bleomycin in the heavy channel, so heavy = bleomycin 0.1 µM for 4 h and light = untreated control. The paper does not state the channel orientation; this comes from the file names.
- **BLMPCI and PDT series:** `Exp1–3_PCI_*` and `Exp2–3_PDT_*`. This matches the paper's three BLMPCI experiments and two PDT experiments.
  - **Channel orientation is not reported** in the paper, and the file names carry no H/L tag, so `characteristics[treatment]` is `not available` on these rows.
  - I tried to infer it from proteins the paper reports changing (Blmh, mitochondrial and lysosomal proteins) in the deposited `proteinGroups.txt`. The H/L direction was inconsistent between experiments, so I did not use it.
- **Endosome vs total:** `ENDOSOME` runs carry `cellular component` = `endosome` and `enrichment process` = `density gradient centrifugation` (PRIDE description: gradient ultracentrifugation). The paper itself only analyses total extracts.
- **Technical replicates:** `tech1/tech2`, `-redo` and Xcalibur timestamped re-acquisitions; a re-acquisition gets technical replicate 2.
- **Incorporation tests:** two SILAC label-incorporation test runs (2015-11-09 and 2015-11-11) are single heavy-only rows with treatment `none`.

## Values
- **Cell line:** `AY-27` (CVCL_S825), male F344 rat, urinary bladder, `bladder transitional cell carcinoma`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD at 30 NCE.
- **Sample prep:** reduction with DTT, alkylation with iodoacetamide, trypsin (paper Methods).
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.6.0.1 `summary.txt`, multiplicity 2 with Arg10/Lys8):
  - Carbamidomethyl C is fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidation NQ are variable.
  - Tolerances are 20 ppm / 20 ppm.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate both pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags trypsin as unsupported by the PRIDE record. That is a false positive: the paper states trypsin digestion.
