# PXD019910 — BLOCKED

Checked: 2026-09-26

## Evidence

- PRIDE project: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD019910
  - Human cerebrospinal-fluid study of Alzheimer disease and cognitive-impairment groups; Orbitrap Fusion; DDA; TMT11plex.
  - The processing protocol states 12 concatenated fractions per TMT set.
- Complete PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD019910/files/all
  - 606 vendor RAW files plus one mzML and one mzIdentML file.
  - RAW names identify set and fraction (for example `s09_f09`) but not reporter-channel sample identities.
  - No sample sheet, Proteome Discoverer study-design export, or other channel map is deposited.
- Linked open-access reuse publication and supplements: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12741918/
  - The supplementary workbook contains cohort summaries and downstream protein analyses, not a `(TMT set, reporter channel) -> source sample` map.

## Blocker

The 49 multiplexed sets and their fractions are identifiable, but no public evidence maps the 11 reporter channels in each set to individual sources or diagnostic groups. Authoring an SDRF would require inventing channel identities.

No SDRF was written or validated.
