# PXD016288 annotation report

Gill and gut proteomes of the deep-water clam *Acesta excavata* from Haltenbanken coral reefs, used to confirm protein expression by the gill symbiont '*Ca.* Acestibacter aggregatus'. NMBU, Ås. Paper: FEMS Microbiol Ecol 2021, PMC8755941 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `invertebrates` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 14 rows.
- **Samples:** the paper says three clams were analysed by gill and gut proteomics, "plus a gut sample of clam Ae24".
  - `Gill_1–3` = gill of clams 1–3.
  - `GI_1–3` = gut of clams 1–3.
  - `GI_Ac24` = gut of clam Ae24.
- **Technical replicates:** the `_1`/`_2` suffix (two injections per sample) is used as the technical replicate.
- **Clam identity:** clams 1–3 are assumed to be the same three animals for gill and gut, as the paper implies.

## Values
- **Organism:** `Acesta excavata`. Symbiont proteins are searched from the binned genome.
- **Sample:** organism part `gill` or `gut`; individual clam ID. Sex and age are not available.
- **Factor:** `organism part`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; PRIDE only says "Thermo Scientific instrument model". HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.6.0.13):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.
  - FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: every annotated value agrees with the deposit record.
