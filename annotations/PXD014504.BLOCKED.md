# PXD014504 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: Glycosaminoglycan Domain Mapping of cellular chondroitin/dermatan sulfates

Publication: Persson A, Vorontsov E, Larson G, Nilsson J. Sci Rep 2020 10(1):3506, PMID 32103093 (scratchpad/PXD014504/manuscript.txt)

## Evidence collected

- PRIDE protocols: xyloside-primed chondroitin/dermatan sulfate GAGs from CCD-1095Sk and HCC70 cells, depolymerised with chondroitinase ABC, AC-I/II, B or heparinase II/III, analysed by negative-mode HCD MS/MS with dibutylamine ion-pairing LC
- Data processing: 'Glycomics data were processed using the XCalibur software … and interpreted manually'; no protein or peptide search
- 43 files: RAW files named <cell line>_<enzyme>_<replicate>Elite_GAG_… plus disaccharide and oligosaccharide standard runs, and manual search summaries (pdf/xlsx)

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

This is a glycomics (GAG oligosaccharide) dataset, not proteomics. There is no protease digestion of proteins, no peptide identification and no protein modifications; the SDRF-Proteomics required columns (cleavage agent, modification parameters, label) have no meaningful value. It is out of scope for SDRF-Proteomics.

A glycomics SDRF template (or community decision to annotate glycan-only MS datasets with SDRF-Proteomics); no further evidence is missing.
