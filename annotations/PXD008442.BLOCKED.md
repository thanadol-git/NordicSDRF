# PXD008442 — blocked: no patient/disease/extraction mapping for RAW files

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD008442
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD008442/files/all
- Linked PubMed record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29501846/ (DOI: 10.1016/j.jprot.2018.02.027)

## Evidence available

PRIDE describes human lung tissue SWATH/DIA analysis comparing COPD and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, using sequential extraction and a Q Exactive. The manifest contains 133 RAW files with `Emma_*` date/number aliases.

## Blocking gap

The linked Journal of Proteomics paper has no public PMC full text or supplementary sample sheet. PRIDE does not associate raw aliases with patient, disease group, clinical context, extraction fraction, technical replicate, or assay-library versus DIA run. Those clinical and experimental assignments cannot be inferred from aliases.

## Unblock requirement

Provide the clinical and raw-file annotation sheet mapping every RAW file to patient, disease group, tissue extraction fraction, replicate, and assay role.
