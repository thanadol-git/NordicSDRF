# PXD022368 dental-calculus subset: evidence blocker

The publication's Table 1 maps two deposited GASP raw files exactly: sample 8786 is SK358 and sample 8836 is SK520, both human dental calculus from St Helena. A two-row SDRF was assembled from these names, the paper's contamination-experiment protocol, and the accession-scoped PRIDE file list. The other 11 RAW files were not included because their sample and workflow mappings need separate review.

The first official validation passed with one warning: the source-literal `dental calculus` is not resolved by the offline UBERON/BTO cache for `characteristics[organism part]`. Independent review failed because the proposed data-dependent acquisition value was inferred from general shotgun LC-MS/MS language rather than run-specific method evidence. A second build recorded acquisition as `not available`, but the official validator rejected that value because the template requires a specific PRIDE acquisition term. The draft and evidence manifest are preserved in `scratchpad/PXD022368/` as unapproved; no SDRF remains in `annotations/`.

To finish this subset, verify acquisition mode from the two RAW method headers or another source explicitly tied to those runs. The literal dental-calculus organism-part term also needs an accepted ontology term or a reviewed documented exception to its warning.

Source: [Ramsøe et al., Scientific Reports 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-87125-x.pdf), [PRIDE project record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD022368), accession-scoped file list at `scratchpad/PXD022368/files.json`, and independent review at `scratchpad/PXD022368/adversarial_review_gasp_dental.json`.
