## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- The five 290818GR<kinase> runs (29 Aug 2018; 425,984-byte .wiff2 each, no search results, no LBD/enzyme tag) are NOT in this SDRF. They match the paper's 'Intact mass' experiment (undiluted protein, deconvoluted, Table 3 'intact mass and peptide mapping'), which has no peptide search and no proteomics acquisition method the template can express. 10 of the 15 runs are annotated.
- The exact SCIEX instrument model is not stated in the saved evidence (the MS methods are in the Supporting Information, which could not be read); the generic parent term MS:1000121 is annotated.
- The substrate is a recombinant human protein fragment: material type 'synthetic' (recombinant proteins, SAMPLE-GUIDELINES), organism part, cell type and disease 'not applicable'; the construct (GR F602S N514-K777 from E. coli) is described here.
- The chymotrypsin and trypsin+chymotrypsin digests of the same kinase reaction share one source name; they are distinguished by comment[technical replicate] (1 = chymotrypsin, 2 = trypsin+chymotrypsin) and comment[cleavage agent details]. They are two digests of one sample, not repeated injections of one digest.
