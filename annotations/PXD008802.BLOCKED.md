# PXD008802 — annotation blocked

Collagen proline hydroxylation in skin of prolyl 4-hydroxylase mutant mice. University of Turku. Paper: Sipilä et al., J Biol Chem 2018 (PMID 29615493).

## What is known
- **Runs:** raw files are named `C1–C27` (skin collagen samples).
- **Search:** Proteome Discoverer 1.4 / Mascot 2.4, 5 ppm / 0.02 Da. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, K and P variable. Q Exactive.

## Why it is blocked
Nothing deposited says which mouse genotype (wild type or each P4H mutant) each C-number is. The paper is not open access, so its methods could not be checked for a key. The study's main factor therefore cannot be assigned per run.

## What would unblock it
A table mapping `C1–C27` to genotype, mouse and replicate.
