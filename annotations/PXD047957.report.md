## Study

Liver of 10 female Lal-/- C57BL/6J mice (6 vehicle, 4 lanifibranor 30 mg/kg, daily gavage 21 days), PAC digestion, Evosep ONE, timsTOF Pro 2 diaPASEF, DIA-NN 1.8.1 library-free. Not in community corpus.

## Key decisions
- Arms: L1-L4 lanifibranor, C1-C6 vehicle. Inferred from file-name letter and group sizes matching the paper (4/6); biological replicate numbers are placeholders (1-4, 1-6).
- Disease: lysosomal acid lipase deficiency (MONDO:0800449) on all rows; vehicle controls are also Lal-/-. reconcile flags this (control arm) - deliberate override.
- Cleavage: Trypsin only; Lys-C also used but column is single-valued (reconcile flag retained).
- Instrument and DIA windows from analysis.tdf of L1 and C6: timsTOF Pro 2, 32 windows x 26 m/z over 400-1201 (paper says 25 Da; header used). CE ramps 20-57.2 eV; not encodable as one value, so `not available`.
- Mods from DIA-NN log: Carbamidomethyl C fixed, Oxidation M variable. Tolerances 20 ppm (MS1) and 10 ppm (MS2) from the DIA-NN command.
- Acquisition method written as plain `Data-independent acquisition`: the NT;AC form is template-listed but rejected by cache-only parse_sdrf. dia method diaPASEF (PRIDE:0000650).
- Factor: treatment.
