# PXD019044 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"Effects of Canavanine on Human Glioblastoma Cells under Arginine Deprivation" (Karatsai et al., Cells 2020, PMID 33008000, PMC7600648). Proteomics was done on U251MG cells only (U87MG was used in other assays, not proteomics). Cells were SILAC-labelled with heavy 13C6 15N4-Arg for seven passages, then moved to: heavy Arg medium (48 h), light Arg medium (48 h), Arg-free medium (48 h), or Arg-free medium with 50 uM canavanine (24 h and 48 h). LysC digestion, nanoAcquity + Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, 3 biological replicates per condition.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + human v1.1.0 + cell-lines v1.1.0. SILAC single-run design, DDA; no clinical/oncology template (no patients).

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
PRIDE lists 33 files over one page (page 0 = 33, page 1 empty): 15 RAW, 15 .pep.xml, FASTA, sample_explanation.csv, PD_results.zip (49.7 GB, not opened). All 15 RAW annotated = 15 rows x 48 columns (one RAW per sample, unfractionated). Runs LUM02043 and LUM02053 are not deposited (numbering gaps; sample_explanation.csv also omits them).

## Key decisions
- Run to sample map: the deposited sample_explanation.csv (raw_file, condition, replica) gives condition and replicate for every RAW; used as is.
- Label: each run is one sample. Heavy Arg condition = SILAC heavy; light Arg condition = SILAC light; Arg-free and canavanine conditions = SILAC heavy (cells were pre-labelled heavy for 7 passages per the paper and received no light Arg). The other SILAC channel of each run is marked unused (empty-source row, emits no SDRF row). This label choice is a judgement (see inferred).
- Dissociation: RAW method header (LUM02040 and LUM02056) says ActivationType = CID, Collision Energy 35%, ion trap rapid detector, so CID (MS:1000133), 35% NCE, ion trap MS2; not HCD.
- Instrument Orbitrap Fusion Lumos: agrees with PRIDE, paper and RAW method header.
- Disease, organ: PRIDE says brain cancer / brain (DOID/BTO); annotated as glioblastoma / brain per paper. Cell line U251MG = Cellosaurus U-251MG CVCL_0021 (cellline lookup); written as `U-251 MG` so the BTO/EFO check matches.
- Cleavage agent Lys-C (MS:1001309): paper and PD workflow (LysC, full) in deposited pep.xml.
- Mass tolerances 3 ppm / 0.6 Da (paper and deposited pep.xml search workflow). The pep.xml also embeds a template with 20 ppm / 0.5 Da; the final search values were taken.
- Modifications from the deposited PD workflow in pep.xml: Carbamidomethyl C fixed, Oxidation M variable, Label:13C(6)15N(4) R variable (UNIMOD:267). The user-defined canavanine (+1.979 Da on R) has no Unimod/PSI-MOD entry and is NOT in modification parameters.
- Factor: treatment (medium/canavanine). sampling time = 24 hour (canavanine 24 h) or 48 hour.

## Round-1 additions
- Culture medium filled: `DMEM with 5% dialyzed fetal bovine serum` (paper: DMEM Sigma D9443 with or without 0.4 mmol/L Arg, 5% dialyzed serum F0392). Free text, so the NCIT check warns. Paper conflict: Methods/PRIDE say complete medium with heavy/light Arg (0.4 mM), Results say AFM with 0.1 mM Arg; the Arg level is not encoded.
- SILAC label is an approximation of a mixed state. Paper Table 1 (% identifications, heavy/light/canavanine): ArgH 48h 97.2/2.8/0.06; ArgL 48h 33.9/65.9/0.2; CAV 24h 83.6/6.5/9.9; CAV 48h 76.9/6.3/16.8. So SILAC light runs LUM02044-46 are about one third heavy, and canavanine runs carry about 6% light plus 10-17% canavanine. One row per run is kept (Table 1 is aggregate, not per sample); the unused other channel is not asserted absent. Heavy for the no-Arg runs (LUM02040-42) has no paper support (condition named only in sample_explanation.csv; Table 1 and PRIDE list 4 conditions).
- Modifications do not include canavanine (+1.97927 Da on R, no Unimod accession), the study's central search target.
- Reduction/alkylation reagents are not stated in the paper text. Disease glioblastoma is from the paper (Cellosaurus says astrocytoma). RAW headers: all 15 read by the reviewer (Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, CID, 35%, ion trap); acquisition dates not recoverable (every file carries method creation time 2/9/2017).
