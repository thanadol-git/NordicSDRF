# PXD008724 annotation report

MIB kinome enrichment of T24 and UM-UC-3 bladder cancer cells treated with the PCNA-targeting APIM-peptide (ATX-101), cisplatin, or both. Otterlei lab, NTNU. Paper: Oncotarget 2018, PMC6126690 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 24 rows, one per `.raw`.
- **Line:** `T` = T24, `U` = UM-UC-3. The search FASTA is human, and the paper's MIB assay used these two lines.
- **Biological replicates:** the digit 4/5/6 is the replicate. The 2017 runs `UA–UD` are UM-UC-3 replicate 5, per the deposited res-U `Groups.txt` (Bio = 5).
- **Condition letter:** the deposited `t24umuccombi.xlsx` gives % of control per replicate ("t4 cis", "t4 atx", "t4 comb", …). Its columns were matched against ratios computed from the deposited MaxQuant LFQ values. Using MAPK3:
  - T4 B/A = 64.0% = "t4 cis"; C/A = 69.3% = "t4 atx"; D/A = 50.1% = "t4 comb". T5 and T6 agree.
  - For UM-UC-3: U4 B/A = 75.0% = "u4 cis", U6 = 88.6%, U(2017) = 88.2%.
- **Result:** `A` = untreated, `B` = cisplatin 10 µM, `C` = APIM-peptide (16 µM for T24, 8 µM for UM-UC-3), `D` = both, all for 24 h (paper).

## Values
- **Organism is corrected:** PRIDE lists rat (the paper also has a rat in-vivo model), but these MIB runs are human cell lines.
- **Cell lines:**
  - T24 (CVCL_0554): female, 82Y.
  - UM-UC-3 (CVCL_1783): male.
  - Both: urinary bladder, `urinary bladder carcinoma`.
- **Enrichment:** MIB kinase enrichment (free text).
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant):
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl N-term and Deamidation NQ variable; no fixed modifications.
  - 20 ppm / FTMS 20 ppm.
  - The earlier search of the 2015 runs (`T-U.zip`, MaxQuant 1.5.3.30) also had Dimethyl KR variable. The res-U search (1.5.7.0) did not; only the shared modifications are listed.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` raises three flags:
  - The organism conflicts with the record; the correction is explained above.
  - Trypsin is a false positive.
  - The organism part on cultured material is the cell line's tissue of origin, by convention.
