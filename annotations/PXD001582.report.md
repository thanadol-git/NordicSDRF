# PXD001582 annotation report

A-431 epidermoid carcinoma cells after hexyl-aminolevulinate photodynamic therapy (HAL-PDT) vs untreated controls. Reversibly oxidised cysteines are blocked with NEM, reduced with DTT and labelled with biotin-maleimide. Slupphaug/Hagen labs, NTNU. Paper: Mol Biosyst 2016, PMID 26742548 (not open access). Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 95 rows for 95 of the 98 deposited files. Three files are left out: `130319_LINDA_T20.raw`, `130319_LINDA_T20_130326122707.raw` and `130419_LINDA_T12.raw`. Each is about 33 kB, header-only aborted acquisitions with no spectra. Each has a full-size re-acquisition in the deposit.

The run names `<date>_LINDA_<K|T><n>` were decoded as follows. **The paper was not available, so this is inferred** from deposit-internal evidence:
- **K / T = control / HAL-PDT.** The deposited `Multiconsensus_from_T_K_N.xlsx` has seven Proteome Discoverer result columns. Its "Check" formula keeps proteins found in ≥2 of the first three (T) series and in none of the next three (K) series or the N series. That is the logic for "oxidised only after treatment", so T = treated and K = control ("kontroll"). The N series is not deposited.
- **n = SDS-PAGE gel slice 1–20** (`comment[fraction identifier]`). The deposited `searchresult.pep.xml` (for a different, undeposited series, `130604_Linda_serie3`) shows the same K1–K20 / T1–T20 scheme. PSM counts are lowest in slices 1 and 20, the pattern of a gel lane cut top to bottom.
- **Dates 130319 / 130405 / 130419 = independent experiment series 1–3** (`biological replicate`). Some slices are missing in each series; the files are annotated as deposited.
- **Reruns:** timestamped re-acquisitions deposited next to a plain run get technical replicate 2 (`130419_LINDA_T10_130422141850`). Where the timestamped file is the only usable copy, it keeps technical replicate 1.
- **Gel runs:** `GEL1/GEL2/gel1/gel2` (5 files) could not be decoded. They are annotated with treatment `not available` and fraction 1.

## Values
- **Cell line:** `A-431` (CVCL_0037), from the skin epidermis of an 85-year-old woman with `skin squamous cell carcinoma` (Cellosaurus disease).
- **Treatment:** HAL-PDT is 20 µM HAL for 3 h, then red light at LD100, with harvest 30 min later (PRIDE protocol).
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (pep.xml search summary and the PRIDE processing text): Proteome Discoverer 1.4 / SEQUEST HT, trypsin, 10 ppm / 0.02 Da. Variable modifications are `Biotin:Sigma-B1267` (UNIMOD:993) and `Nethylmaleimide` (UNIMOD:108) on C; there is no fixed modification.
- **Reagents:** reduction is DTT; the alkylation (blocking) reagent is N-ethylmaleimide (PRIDE:0000606).

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate both pass.
- `tools reconcile` raises two false positives:
  - It flags trypsin as unsupported, but the processing text names trypsin as the enzyme.
  - It flags an organism part on cultured material; the value is the cell line's tissue of origin, as used throughout the corpus.
