## Limitations

- Only the 16 Thermo RAW files (OB-CM secretome, Q Exactive HF) are annotated. The cell-lysate timsTOF fleX .d files exist only inside the 35 GB LC_MS_files.zip (not listed as individual PRIDE files) and are not covered; mqpar_cell_lysate.xml describes 8 .d runs with no sample mapping in the deposit. A separate SDRF was not built for them.
- The paper describes OB-CM from 14-day differentiated cells (3 of 4 samples reported); the BASAL/DAY3/DAY14/DAY21 x 4 labels in mqpar.xml are not explained in the paper. Time-point meaning (osteoblast differentiation stage) is taken from the labels at face value and may not be exact; replicate numbers are the label suffixes and it is unknown whether they are independent biological replicates.
- Cell line recorded as paper name hBMSC-TERT; Cellosaurus has no entry by this name (hMSC-TERT CVCL_Z015 and clones exist but the clone was not stated), so cellosaurus accession/name, age, sex, passage number and disease are `not available`. parse_sdrf warns that hBMSC-TERT is not in CLO/BTO/EFO and tools check flags it as unverified; unavoidable.
- organism part `culture fluid` (BTO:0002233) is an approximate term for conditioned medium.
- Dissociation method not available (not stated); reduction/alkylation reagents not named. DDA inferred.
- Precursor tolerance 4.5 ppm from mqpar.xml differs from 6 ppm in paper text.
- Paper full text was read (Europe PMC PMC10669731); SKM-CM data are in PXD033025, not this deposit.
