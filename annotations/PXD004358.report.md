# PXD004358
Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

**Human O-Mannosylation independent of POMT1/2.** Copenhagen Center for Glycomics, University of Copenhagen, Denmark (PI Henrik Clausen; data by Ida Signe Bohse Larsen and Adnan Halim). Paper: Larsen ISB *et al.*, "Mammalian O-mannosylation of cadherins and plexins is independent of protein O-mannosyltransferases 1 and 2", *J Biol Chem* 2017;292(27):11586-11598, PMID 28512129, PMC5500819, doi:10.1074/jbc.M117.794487. The paper's data-availability statement names PXD004358 as the human-cell dataset (PXD004341 is the CHO companion). This is an O-Man and O-GalNAc glycoproteomics study: tryptic digests, PNGase F-treated, enriched by lectin weak affinity chromatography (ConA, and VVA for the dimethyl sample), on isogenic HEK293 knockout lines and a POMT1-deficient patient fibroblast.

- SDRF: `annotations/PXD004358.sdrf.tsv`
- Rows x columns: 38 x 47 (28 RAW files: 18 label-free runs, and 10 dimethyl-labelled runs with 2 channel rows each)
- SHA-256: `7150bb473c421f6d05f2d1a08b1276749ecc777d84f3526d9296a4e5730e2406`
- Built with `sdrf-tools build` from `scratchpad/PXD004358/samples.tsv` + `technical.tsv`, then `postprocess.py` (documented below).

## Templates
- `ms-proteomics v1.1.0`: mass-spectrometry DDA data.
- `human v1.1.0`: every sample is human (HEK293 and a human skin fibroblast).
- `cell-lines v1.1.0`: every sample is a cultured cell line (HEK293 CVCL_0045, and Coriell fibroblast GM23363 CVCL_JF24, a Cellosaurus "finite cell line").
- Not used: `dia-acquisition` (all runs are DDA), `clinical-metadata` and `oncology-metadata` (no patient cohort, only one patient-derived cell line).

## Design and file mapping
The factor is `factor value[genotype]`, copied from `characteristics[genotype]` (EFO:0000513, free text). The knockout genotypes and the dimethyl channel assignment come from the paper (Results, Table 1, Fig. 1, and the Methods "Dimethyl stable isotope labeling" section). The paper's abbreviations are SC = COSMC KO (SimpleCell) and SC/PGNT1 = COSMC + POMGNT1 KO; the run names use "SCSC" for the latter. Fraction numbers are the position in each lectin elution series, ordered by the LWAC fraction number in the file name.

**Dimethyl series.** Per the paper, the light (HEK293SC/PGNT1) and medium (HEK293SC/PGNT1/POMT1/POMT2) digests were mixed 1:1 and processed as one sample in a serial workflow: ConA LWAC elution, then the ConA flow-through on VVA LWAC, then the VVA flow-through. Each RAW file therefore gets one assay name shared by its two channel rows:
- `151125_..._LWAC25`-`30`: ConA elution, fractions 1-6.
- `151218_..._Method1`: VVA elution, fraction 7, technical replicate 1.
- `151218_..._Method2` and `_Method3`: the same VVA elution re-injected with the paper's MS methods 2 and 3. These are fraction 7, technical replicates 2 and 3.
- `160104_..._ConA(FT)VVA(FT)_3h_10HCD`: VVA flow-through (unbound total digest; the paper's Fig. 2A 1:1 check), fraction 8.

`comment[sample preparation batch]` (free text) records the lectin step and date on every row, so rows from different enrichment steps can be told apart. No PRIDE fractionation term (children of PRIDE:0000550) or EFO enrichment term (children of EFO:0009090) describes lectin LWAC.

**Biological replicate numbering.** Within a genotype, independently prepared samples are numbered 1, 2 in date order. There are two such pairs: COSMC/POMGNT1 KO (Oct 2014 label-free; Nov 2015 dimethyl light) and COSMC/POMGNT1/POMT1 KO (May 2015 Velos; Aug 2015 Fusion). They are separate cell-pellet preparations with separate LWAC runs, shown by different dates, instruments and file naming. The paper does not describe them as replicates.

### Technical values
- Instrument (per row). Velos files: `Orbitrap Velos Pro` (MS:1003096); the RAW header model is "Velos Pro". Fusion files: `Orbitrap Fusion ETD` (MS:1002417); the RAW header model is "Orbitrap Fusion", but ETD MS2 was acquired, and PRIDE lists MS:1002417. PRIDE also lists "LTQ Orbitrap Velos" (MS:1001742); the RAW headers say Velos Pro.
- Dissociation (per row, two `comment[dissociation method]` columns), taken from the instrument method names in the RAW headers:
  - HCD + ETD (`5HCD_5ETD`, `top5ETD_top5HCD`): 32 rows.
  - ETD only (`top7ETD`, Method2/3): 4 rows.
  - HCD only (`top10HCD_27%`, 160104): 2 rows.
  - Single-mode rows carry `not applicable` in the second column. HCD = MS:1000422 and ETD = MS:1000598.
- Cleavage: Trypsin (MS:1001251). The `.msf` workflows use a fully specific search followed by a semi-specific second search.
- Tolerances: 10 ppm / 0.02 Da. Both the PRIDE protocol ("In all cases") and the `150602 ..._ETD.msf` Sequest parameters state these values.
- Modifications (7 columns, per row), read from the deposited Proteome Discoverer 1.4 `.msf` parameters:
  - Every row: Carbamidomethyl C (fixed) and Oxidation M (variable).
  - ConA searches (label-free runs and 151125): Hex S,T.
  - 151218 VVA searches: HexNAc S,T,Y.
  - Dimethyl runs (151125, 151218, 160104): Dimethyl (UNIMOD:36) and Dimethyl:2H(4) (UNIMOD:199), each on K and peptide N-term. Unused slots are `not applicable`.
- ms2 mass analyzer: orbitrap (paper: Orbitrap HCD/ETD MS2 at 7,500/15,000 on the Velos and 60,000 on the Fusion). MS1 scan range: 355-1700 m/z (paper and PRIDE).
- Labels: DIMETHYL0 (PRIDE:0000848, -N(CH3)2, light) and DIMETHYL4 (PRIDE:0000850, -N(CHD2)2 from CD2O + NaBH3CN, medium), per the paper's Methods.

### Post-processing (`scratchpad/PXD004358/postprocess.py`, diff-verified)
`build` cannot express dimethyl channels, per-row repeated columns, or a re-injection's fraction number. The script applied the following changes, and a cell-by-cell diff against the `build` output confirmed nothing else changed:
1. Dimethyl rows were built as `SILAC light/medium` placeholders. Their `comment[label]` was replaced by DIMETHYL0/DIMETHYL4, and the `-SILAC light/medium` suffix was stripped from `assay name` (20 + 20 cells).
2. `comment[fraction identifier]` was set from 1 to 7 for the Method2/Method3 rows (4 cells).
3. Modification column 3 was set to HexNAc on the 151218 rows and to `not applicable` on the 160104 rows (8 cells). Modification columns 4-7 (the dimethyl terms) were set to `not applicable` on the 18 label-free rows (72 cells).
4. `comment[sample preparation batch]` was rewritten for the 151218 Method1 and 160104 rows (4 cells).
5. A second `comment[dissociation method]` column was inserted after the first.

## Limitations
- **150828 HEK_WT_POMT1_POMT2_KOKO (5 files): genotype background conflict.** The embedded acquisition path in each RAW header reads `HEK_SC_POMT1_POMT2_KOKO`, but the deposited file name reads `HEK_WT_...`. The raw-file paths recorded inside the deposited `.msf` searches also use the `WT` name, so the lab renamed these files before searching. In the same batch, `HEK_SC_POMT1KO` was renamed to `HEK_SC_SC_POMT1KO`. Only this set can be the paper's HEK293POMT1/POMT2 group ("generated in HEK293 WT cells", Table 3 column POMT1/POMT2). I therefore annotated it as POMT1/POMT2 KO in a WT background, as the deposited name and the paper indicate. This is inferred from naming plus the paper; no table maps files to genotypes.
- **150828 ConA_21_25:** annotated as COSMC/POMGNT1/POMT1 KO from the deposited and search-time name `HEK_SC_SC_POMT1KO`. The acquisition-time header reads `HEK_SC_POMT1KO`.
- **Fibroblast identity.** The run names say "Fibroblast_GM3363_POMT1KO". GM3363 is not in Cellosaurus. Coriell GM23363 (CVCL_JF24) carries exactly the paper's POMT1 variants (p.Ser29Arg and p.Arg622Ter, heterozygous), so "GM3363" was read as GM23363. The fibroblast is patient-derived (partial POMT1 deficiency), not a knockout, despite "POMT1KO" in the file name.
- **`characteristics[cell line]` for the fibroblast** is the BTO class `skin fibroblast cell line` (BTO:0001619), not the Cellosaurus name. GM23363 has no CLO/BTO/EFO term (checked in live OLS), and `sdrf-tools check` rejected it. The specific identity is kept in `characteristics[cellosaurus accession]` CVCL_JF24 and `characteristics[cellosaurus name]` GM23363.
- Disease for the fibroblast: `autosomal recessive limb-girdle muscular dystrophy type 2K` (MONDO:0012248, the POMT1-related LGMD), from Cellosaurus (NCIt C133730 / ORDO 86812). The paper only says "POMT1-deficient patient". HEK293 rows are `normal` (Cellosaurus lists no disease).
- Sex: female for both lines, from Cellosaurus. Age: `not available`. Cellosaurus gives "Age unspecified" for GM23363, and the age of the HEK293 source fetus is not recorded. Developmental stage: `embryo stage` for HEK293 (embryonic kidney origin, per `cellline.md`) and `not available` for GM23363.
- Ancestry and passage number: `not available` (not reported). Culture medium: DMEM for HEK293 (paper Methods); `not available` for the fibroblast (not stated). Cell type: `not available` for HEK293 (Cellosaurus gives none) and `skin fibroblast` for GM23363 (Cellosaurus).
- Biological replicate numbers 1/2 for the repeated genotypes are inferred from separate preparation dates. The paper does not call them biological replicates.
- Fraction identifiers are positions within each source's lectin series, not the lab's LWAC fraction numbers (22-27, 25-30, 26-30, 14-17). The mapping is in the design table above and in the data-file names.
- Treating Method1/2/3 as technical replicates of one VVA elution fraction is inferred. The file names share the sample tag `ConA(FT)VVA(151215)`, and the paper describes methods 1-3 as alternative acquisitions of the VVA-enriched sample.
- Assigning 160104 (`HEK_SCSC_POMT1_2_KO_ConA(FT)VVA(FT)`) to the dimethyl mixture is inferred. Its deposited search includes Dimethyl (+28.031) and the +32.056 medium-label mass, and the paper says the VVA flow-through of the labelled mix was measured (Fig. 2A).
- Modifications were read from the first 6 MB of each `.msf` (HTTP range reads) and the full `150602_..._ETD.msf`. The PRIDE protocol also lists Asn deamidation, but it does not appear in any deposited search, so it was omitted. The medium-label mass appears in the quantification settings strings rather than as a named dynamic modification. It is declared as a variable `Dimethyl:2H(4)` to match the light declaration.
- The 4xHex / 8xGalNAc `.msf` searches used precursor mass offsets on extracted MGFs, not separate modifications. They are not represented in the SDRF.
- Search-result (`.msf`) files are not listed as data files; only the 28 RAW files are annotated.
