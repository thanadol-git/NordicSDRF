# PXD030695 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE identifies an ion-based PISA study spanning several anticancer-drug experiments. It deposits 24 `ZnPISA_lysate` fractions, 12 `ZnPISA_cell` fractions, 12 `PISA_comparison` fractions, and 12 `CuPISA_MTX` fractions, together with result/parameter tables. Six standalone RAW files identify DMSO or MTX replicates, but the multiplexed groups have no deposited channel sheet.
- **Why blocked:** The protocol describes both TMT10plex and TMTpro-16plex workflows but does not map their reporter channels to drug, dose, cell/lysate context, or replicate. The result tables are experiment-specific summaries rather than a reporter-channel manifest. Annotating the many multiplexed acquisitions from the broad study description would fabricate sample assignments.
- **Unblocking requirement:** Deposited design sheets mapping every reporter channel in the ZnPISA, PISA-comparison, and CuPISA multiplexes to sample identity, drug/dose, experimental context, and replicate.
