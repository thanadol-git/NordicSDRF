# PXD047603 — annotation blocked

DIA proteome of postnatal day 1 hearts from mice with an exonuclease-deficient POLG (mutator) combined with Twinkle overexpression (PolgMutTwOE), vs wild type. Paper: Nat Commun 2024 (PMC11401880).

## What is known
- **Runs:** 12 runs named by mouse number (`N38`, `N63`, ..., `N97`). Orbitrap Fusion Lumos with FAIMS, DIA, per the raw header.
- **Design:** the paper's main comparison is PolgMutTwOE vs WT (n = 6 + 5). It also mentions N-acetylcysteine supplementation and other genotypes.
- **Search:** Spectronaut 15.0.2 directDIA. Trypsin/P; Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Why it is blocked
Nothing deposited (outside the 1.9 GB Spectronaut `.sne` project) or in the paper maps the mouse numbers to genotype or treatment.

## What would unblock it
A table mapping `N38–N97` to genotype, treatment and sex.
