# PXD037472 annotation report

## Evidence used

- PRIDE metadata and protocol describe HEK293 phosphoproteomics following vehicle, semaglutide, or danuglipron stimulation, acquired in DIA mode on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos. The protocol specifies 0, 5, 15, 30, and 60 minute timepoints and biological triplicates.
- Deposited RAW filenames encode a 45-file time course with treatment, timepoint, and replicate A–C.
- A second 21-file deposited naming scheme records submitted groups `i`, `U`, and `GLP`; each is crossed with the submitted treatment codes `C`, `Sem`, and `PF` and replicates 1–3.

## Annotation scope

The SDRF represents all 66 deposited RAW files. The protocol expands the treatment codes to vehicle, semaglutide, and danuglipron. The ambiguous `i`, `U`, and `GLP` codes are retained verbatim in the **submitted sample set** factor; no meaning or timepoint is inferred for that second naming scheme.
