# PXD061447 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Palaeoproteomic (SPIN) taxonomic identification of bone fragments and sediment in resin-embedded micromorphology blocks from Bacho Kiro, La Ferrassie and Quincay, plus a modern resin-impregnation experiment (PNAS Nexus 2025, PMID 41098883).

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
67 RAW (all deposited runs). Metadata rows without a raw file (resin-only samples 19, 43) are not data and are skipped.

## Sample map
Deposited metadata.txt: one row per sample with site, block, layer, tissue type, extraction batch, StageTip/Evotip id and raw_file; SPIN species result per sample.

## Key decisions
Organism: archaeological samples recorded as Mammalia (the taxon is the study output; SPIN result kept in comment[spin taxonomic assignment]); resin-experiment specimens use the known species (Canis lupus, Bos taurus); blanks not applicable. Material: bone-containing samples = tissue, matrix/resin-only samples = not applicable (sediment; recorded in comment[sample type description]). Acid demineralisation + trypsin; no reduction/alkylation. Instruments: P04xx runs Exploris 480 (protocol), TIMS6 runs timsTOF Pro 2 (analysis.tdf InstrumentName).

## Not available / not applicable
Age (geological), element for archaeological fragments.

## Conflicts / overrides
