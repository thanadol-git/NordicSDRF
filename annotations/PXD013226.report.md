# PXD013226 annotation report

Exercise training in cancer cachexia. Two models:
- Plantaris muscle and serum from Walker 256 tumor-bearing **rats**, with and without aerobic interval training.
- Pooled plantaris and serum from B16F10 tumor-bearing **mice**.

Brum/Ferreira labs (São Paulo) with PROMEC, NTNU. Paper: Mol Metab 2020, PMC7283151 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `vertebrates` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 72 rows, one per `.raw`.
- **Rat runs:** `plantaris|serum_<n><group>_tech1|2`, with groups `con` (healthy control), `W` (Walker 256, sedentary) and `hit` (Walker 256 + aerobic interval training).
  - Animals n = 1–5 are the biological replicates.
  - Paper: male Wistar rats, Walker 256 cells injected into the femoral bone marrow, samples at 11 dpi, technical duplicates of 5 biological samples.
  - The organism is from the deposited `plantarisratlfq.xml`/`serumratlfq.xml` (rat FASTA). PRIDE lists only mouse.
- **Mouse runs:** `*_POOL_*` are male C57BL/6 mice: `con`, `B16` (B16F10 sedentary) and `HIT` (B16F10 + AIT). Each is a pool of 8 mice (paper), with mouse FASTA in `mqpar.xml`. They are marked `pooled`.
  - The plantaris `con_POOL_tech1` run is not deposited.
- **Technical replicates:** `tech1/tech2`. A timestamped re-acquisition next to a plain run of the same name gets technical replicate 3; otherwise the timestamped copy keeps its number.

## Values
- **Disease:** `carcinosarcoma` for Walker 256 tumor-bearing rats, `melanoma` for B16F10 mice, `normal` for controls. Treatment/exercise is free text and is the factor.
- **Organism part:** `plantaris` (UBERON:0011905) or `blood serum`. Animals are adult males; age `not available`.
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Elite, from the raw header; CID, ion-trap MS2.
- **Search** (deposited MaxQuant 1.5.0 `summary.txt`/`mqpar.xml`):
  - Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M and Carbamidomethyl C all variable; no fixed modifications.
  - Main search 4.5 ppm; ITMS MS/MS 0.5 Da.
- **OxICAT:** the PRIDE protocol also describes an OxICAT redox experiment, but those raw files are not in this deposit (only result tables are). The deposited runs were searched label-free, so `comment[label]` is label free.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` raises two false positives:
  - The OxICAT label: see above.
  - "disease with a control arm": control rows are `normal`.
