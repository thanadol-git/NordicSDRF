## Decisions and caveats

- comment[proteomics data acquisition method] is required and restricted to DDA/DIA/PRM/SRM; none fits MALDI imaging. `Data-dependent acquisition` is a placeholder forced by the template, not a true description.
- Data files are the deposited `.zip` archives of Bruker imaging data, not individual RAW files; contents not inspected (0.7-1 GB each, not downloaded).
- Instrument: UltrafleXtreme (MS:1001544) from the protocol text; PRIDE metadata lists generic `ultraflex` (MS:1000201).
- Dissociation, tolerances and modifications set to not applicable (no search; PRIDE:0000398). MaxQuant listed in PRIDE metadata is not supported by the protocol and was ignored.
- Mouse age/developmental stage and cell type: not available. Mapping Tg1/3/4 to biological replicate 1/2/3 is an assumption. Whether matrices were applied to adjacent sections of one mouse is inferred from the protocol.
- The deposit also includes LCM/LIFT experiments described in the protocol, but no corresponding files were deposited as RAW.
- Not machine-reviewed by an independent context (this run was told not to spawn subagents); the review gate was not run.
