# PXD058697 - BLOCKED (no iTRAQ channel-to-sample map)

Status: not annotated. No SDRF was written, because the channel map cannot be established from any accessible source.

Dataset: Aarhus (Lange lab; PRIDE submitter M. Ghassemian, UCSD). Mouse (Mus musculus) heart, iTRAQ 8-plex, TripleTOF 5600, 18 .wiff files (Stephan_1_1 to Stephan_1_18, each with a .wiff.scan). Paper: Fujita et al., Circ Heart Fail 2025, PMID 40066567, PMC11995854.

## Why blocked
- The deposit has 18 .wiff/.wiff.scan files and a single search export, protein-peptides.csv (PEAKS). The export has iTRAQ8-113/114/115/116/117/118/119/121 columns but no sample names. PRIDE metadata (sampleProcessingProtocol, dataProcessingProtocol, keywords) states "labeled with sample-specific isobaric tags (iTRAQ8)" and gives no channel assignments.
- The paper (Fig 5 legend) says 4 groups (CTL, Obsc KO, Obsl1 KO, Obsc/Obsl1 dKO) with 3 biological samples per group, so 12 mice. Eight channels cannot hold 12 samples, so there are at least 2 sets, or an unstated design. The text says only "each set of samples was pooled and fractionated". Which of the 18 fractions belong to which set, and which channel holds which genotype, is not stated anywhere accessible.
- Channel-intensity density in the search export is uneven (113-117 about 311 peptide rows each, 118 about 229, 119 about 71, 121 about 40). Some channels are therefore probably low-input or empty, which is another reason not to guess.
- Not accessible: the Supplemental Methods, Tables S1-S3 and Supplemental File 3 (proteome table) are not in the open-access full text (Europe PMC reports the article as not open access, and the PMC efetch gives the body only). GEO GSE283745 is RNA-seq and holds no proteomics channel map.
