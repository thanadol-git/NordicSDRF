# PXD054499 annotation status: blocked on run-to-state map

The accession has one 6.4 GB `raw.zip` containing 22 distinct RAW files, S1–S22. I verified those names against the ZIP64 central directory and the deposited `mqpar.xml`. The project and paper compare left-ventricle tissue from active and hibernating grizzly bears, but the deposited filenames and MaxQuant experiment labels are only S1–S22. Neither source identifies which runs came from active versus hibernating animals or whether multiple runs came from one animal. An SDRF assigning the comparison factor or biological replicate would therefore require a run-level map from the submitter.

Technical details are available: Orbitrap Ascend Top10 DDA, trypsin, and deposited MaxQuant modifications including fixed carbamidomethylation and variable oxidation, N-terminal/K acetylation, and STY phosphorylation. No SDRF was authored because the source-to-run coordinates are unresolved.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD054499), [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11732570/), [deposited MaxQuant settings](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2025/05/PXD054499/mqpar.xml).
