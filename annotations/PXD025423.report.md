## Limitations

- No deposited or published mapping assigns the 16 RAW files to Ctrl, low dose (LD) or high dose (HD) exposure. The deposited GroupsForThisStudy.txt and txt.zip Lab.xlsx label runs NM1, NM4, media and UnTcell (runs 01-08 as NM1 in duplicate injections, C1-C4 media, T1-T4 UnTcell), which does not correspond to the Ctrl/LD/HD design in the paper; they appear to come from a shared batch. Treatment is therefore `not available` and no factor value is given.
- Paper design is two biological replicates per group (Ctrl, LD, HD) = 6 samples, versus 16 RAW files; the relation between samples and runs (and whether some runs belong to other projects) is undecodable. Source name = RAW file stem and biological replicate = ordinal 1-16 are placeholders; technical replicate 1 throughout.
- txt.zip (MaxQuant txt output, 24 runs) was searched by range requests only; it includes runs 20160930_STAMI_09 to _16 that are not deposited as RAW and are not claimed. The search also covers the unrelated-looking NM4/media/UnTcell conditions.
- Age 65Y and sex female come from Cellosaurus CVCL_X491 (HBEC3-KT), not from the study. Organism part (bronchus) and cell type (bronchial epithelial cell) are curator assignments from the tissue of origin.
- HBEC3-KT is not found in CLO/BTO/EFO, so the cell line value yields a validator warning and a tools check 'hallucinated' flag; it is the Cellosaurus recommended name (study text uses HBEC-3KT).
- Dissociation method and fragment tolerance not stated (mqpar has both FTMS 20 ppm and ITMS 0.5 Da); set to not available. Exposure time (13 weeks) and doses are not recorded in the SDRF because they cannot be attached to specific runs.
- SANDBOX ONLY (independent review FAIL): replicate numbers 1-16 are placeholders; deposited GroupsForThisStudy.txt pairs runs 01+02, 03+04, 05+06, 07+08 as technical replicates (samples NM1C11, NM1C12, NM1C21, NM1C22) and labels C1-C4 'media' and T1-T4 'UnTcell', which may not be HBEC3-KT lysate, so the blanket cell-line/organism-part/cell-type assignment is not source-supported; treatment (control, 0.96 and 1.92 ug/cm2 TiO2) cannot be mapped to runs. Instrument (Orbitrap Elite) was confirmed from the RAW header; the method path (top20CID) suggests CID dissociation, not recorded.
