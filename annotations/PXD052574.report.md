# PXD052574 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Human mucosal-associated invariant T (MAIT) cell proteome. Weerakoon H, Miles JJ, Hill MM, Lepletier A. "A shotgun proteomic dataset of human mucosal-associated invariant T cells", Data in Brief 2024;56:110786, doi:10.1016/j.dib.2024.110786, PMC11367653, PMID 39224509. The paper cites PXD052574 (Specifications table and Table 1), so the PX publication link is correct. (Submitter affiliation is Rajarata University, Sri Lanka; the samples were collected and measured at QIMR Berghofer, Brisbane.)

## Scope, evidence and decisions
- Deposited (PROXI rev 2 datasetFiles and PRIDE FTP listing agree): 9 Thermo .raw files (3.2 to 4.0 GB each), plus Parameters.txt, search.zip, the MaxQuant proteinGroups export, the FASTA and checksum.txt. Annotated: 9/9 raw files, giving 9 rows (label-free, unfractionated, 1 run per sample). Nothing was excluded.
- Run to sample (STATED, paper Table 1): Rep{n}_MAIT.raw = MAIT cells, biological replicate n; Rep{n}_nonMAIT_CD3.raw = non-MAIT CD3+ T cells, replicate n; Rep{n}_nonMAIT_CD8.raw = non-MAIT CD8+ T cells, replicate n. The three subsets were sorted in matched sets from each of 3 healthy donors.
- Cell type: MAIT = CL "mucosal-associated invariant T cell" (CL:0000940, label checked live on Ontobee); non-MAIT CD3 = "T cell"; non-MAIT CD8 = "CD8-positive, alpha-beta T cell". Factor value[cell type] carries the comparison. The sort gates are in the paper (MAIT CD3+CD161+TCRVa7.2+; the non-MAIT subsets are CD161-Va7.2-) but were not encoded in a column.
- Organism part blood (PBMC), disease normal (healthy volunteers), material type cell, developmental stage adult, age 30Y-35Y (the paper gives only the cohort range).
- Templates: ms-proteomics + human. Primary cells, so no cell-lines template.
- Instrument Orbitrap Fusion (MS:1002416), stated by both the paper and PX. The raw headers were not read: the shell has no network, and the files are 3+ GB.
- Prep (paper 4.2): reduction with TCEP (PRIDE:0000609) and alkylation with chloroacetamide (PRIDE:0000600); trypsin. DDA, HCD, MS1 380-1500 m/z.
- Modifications come from the deposited MaxQuant Parameters.txt: fixed Carbamidomethyl (C), and Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term) as variable. The paper's Methods agree.
- Tolerances: precursor 20 ppm (paper). Fragment 20 ppm comes from the deposited Parameters.txt (MS/MS tol. FTMS 20 ppm), which overrides the paper's "product 40 ppm". MS2 was acquired in the Orbitrap at 30k, so the FTMS setting applies.
- Fractionation method is not applicable: single-shot runs, with fraction identifier 1. The SDS-PAGE term was not used because no gel was run (the SDS was only in the lysis buffer).

## Values left not available / not applicable
- characteristics[sex] not available: the cohort is 2 male and 1 female, but the paper does not say which donor/replicate is which sex.
- characteristics[ancestry category] not available: not stated.
- comment[fractionation method] not applicable: no fractionation.

## Stated vs INFERRED
- STATED: the file to cell type and replicate map, all prep and MS parameters, the modifications, and the instrument.
- INFERRED: characteristics[individual] donor1-3 = Rep1-3. Table 1 calls the files "Biological Replicate n", and the sorts were matched per donor. That replicate n is the same donor across the 3 subsets is the natural reading, but it is not literally stated.

## Conflicts
- MaxQuant version: the paper says 1.6.0.16, but the deposited Parameters.txt says 1.5.8.3 (written 02/08/2018). Not encoded; informational only.
- Fragment tolerance: the paper says 40 ppm and Parameters.txt says 20 ppm (FTMS). The deposited file wins.
- Parameters.txt lists a second FASTA (Chicken_Ovalbumin_proteome_Ref) that the paper does not mention. This matches the stated 200 ng ovalbumin internal standard. It was not encoded as characteristics[spiked compound]; a reviewer may want it added.
- PX modifications (acetylated residue, iodoacetamide-derivatized residue) are consistent with the Carbamidomethyl search, but the reagent was chloroacetamide, not IAA.
- Tooling note: Europe PMC and NCBI idconv queries made through WebFetch returned unrelated papers (a substitution artefact). The paper was resolved via NCBI idconv in the cloud browser and verified on the PMC page itself. EBI OLS was unreachable, so CL:0000940 was verified on Ontobee. The other terms (T cell, CD8-positive alpha-beta T cell, blood, normal) are plain labels in common use across this campaign and could not be re-checked live today.
