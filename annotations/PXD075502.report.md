# PXD075502 annotation report

Approved `PXD075502.sdrf.tsv`: 31 rows × 25 columns, covering purified porcine elastase treated with eight oxidant conditions: six HOSeCN dose levels (0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50-fold), 50-fold decomposed HOSeCN, and 50-fold HOCl. The deposited `Description_of_uploaded_files.pdf` maps every acquisition to its condition and repeat number. Templates: `ms-proteomics` and `vertebrates` v1.1.0.

The official parser passed the pre-review build. After independent review identified unsupported biological-replicate assignments and an incomplete modification list, the tables were corrected: the documented repeats are technical/reaction replicates of the single evidenced purified-protein source, and the incomplete modification column was removed. The rebuilt artifact passed `sdrf-tools structure`, and a fresh independent reviewer reran the official parser and `sdrf-tools check` successfully. The hash-bound PASS receipt covers SHA-256 `9b1b46c60e53b058141e35049d0b1b84572559d64e6ddecafb979ac7d2a54bf1`.

Remaining limitation: the PRIDE protocol lists complex variable oxidation and chlorination searches, but exact controlled-vocabulary mappings for all were not reconciled, so `comment[modification parameters]` is omitted. This is a minor review finding; see the evidence manifest.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD075502), [deposited run description](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2026/06/PXD075502/Description_of_uploaded_files.pdf).
