# PXD038570 — BLOCKED

Checked: 2026-10-01

## Evidence

- ProteomeXchange deposit: ten RAW files named `AM_1.raw` through `AM_10.raw` and `AM_1_10.csv`.
- Publication: DOI 10.3389/fped.2022.1091308 (PMCID PMC9869058).
- The paper describes two pooled plasma groups, each containing three juvenile-idiopathic-arthritis patients, separated by uveitis status and analyzed after immunoprecipitation from WISH-cell extracts.

## Blocker

Neither the paper nor deposited CSV maps the ten generic RAW identifiers to the two plasma pools or to technical replicates; the CSV exposes only generic `F1`–`F10` columns. A condition assignment would therefore be unsupported.

No SDRF was written or validated.
