# PXD016395 evidence status

The deposit contains four `.baf` RAW runs from four pooled Acromyrmex echinatior colony fecal-fluid samples. The paper identifies colony IDs Ae263, Ae322, Ae356, and Ae372, and describes trypsin digestion and a Bruker Maxis Impact QTOF. The RAW filenames `001`, `992`, `995`, and `998` do not encode the colony IDs.

**Blocked: required file-to-colony and acquisition-method maps remain unresolved.** Neither the PRIDE protocol nor the paper specifies which numbered RAW run belongs to which colony or explicitly names the acquisition method. Assigning DDA from instrument type or MaxQuant defaults would be unsupported.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD016395), [paper](https://elifesciences.org/articles/61816).
