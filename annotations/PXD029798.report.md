## Limitations

- The deposited TMT table maps reporter channels to source codes but does not connect those codes to the publication's Woman 1-44 clinical table. Age, HIV status, HPV status/type, cytology, longitudinal time, disease group, and individual identity are therefore not assigned.
- Cohort-level properties for the 1,200 non-reference rows are limited to human adult women and uterine-cervix brush material. They are not propagated to the 80 undocumented reference rows. Age and ancestry remain `not available` at source-code resolution.
- The single dissociation field records CID used for MS2 peptide identification. The supplementary method also documents 55% HCD at MS3 for reporter-ion quantification.
- TMT126 is labelled only `Ref`; its physical composition and pooling status are unavailable. Reference rows therefore use `not applicable` for organism and `not available` for organism part, material type, sex, developmental stage, and pooling status.
- The supplied independent-review finding was repaired. The prior FAIL receipt is bound to the superseded hash and no review-gate state was written because this campaign forbids touching git state.
