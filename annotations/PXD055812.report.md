## Validation

- parse_sdrf validate-sdrf (4 templates, --use_ols_cache_only): only a warning, N/TERT-1 not found in CLO/BTO/EFO (it is a valid Cellosaurus line, CVCL_CW92; no CLO/BTO term exists).
- tools structure: OK. tools check: CLEAN (0 hallucinated).
- Validator discrepancy: with dia-acquisition in the union, the documented form `NT=Data-independent acquisition;AC=PRIDE:0000450` is rejected ("must be one of the allowed values"), although the template text lists it and ms-proteomics alone accepts it. I used the plain label `Data-independent acquisition`, which the template also allows. Revert to NT/AC when the validator is fixed.
- tools reconcile flagged cleavage_agent_unsupported (BLOCKER) for Trypsin; false positive, the protocol states Lys-C followed by trypsin (Promega). Only trypsin is recorded because the column takes one enzyme.

## Limitations and inferences

- B11 and D2 are the two KO clone IDs in the RAW file names. The supplement labels the groups "DMKN BG KO" and "DMKN AB KO" (beta/gamma and alpha/beta), but no source read maps B11/D2 to BG/AB, so genotype is "dermokine knockout (DMKN-/-), clone X" without isoform. Needs submitter confirmation.
- Replicate numbers 1-3 come from file suffixes _1/_2/_3 (inferred); source names are our own labels.
- Lysate is the whole 3D culture (keratinocytes on dermal fibroblasts in Matrigel); only keratinocyte is annotated as cell type. Organism part "skin".
- Disease "normal": N/TERT-1 derived from clinically normal foreskin (paper), not the PRIDE disease field (Wounds and Injuries), which describes the paper's theme.
- Age not available (Cellosaurus gives <1M, not the SDRF pattern); sex male from paper. Passage, culture medium for the 3D cultures and storage temperature set to not available.
- Mass tolerances not available (Spectronaut defaults). Modifications read from the PRIDE processing text; the .sne search archive was not opened. Scan window limits use the MS1 range 350-1650 m/z; isolation window 38 m/z; collision energy 30% NCE; dia method not available.
- No fractionation; EvoSep 44-min gradient and FAIMS CVs (-35/-55/-75 V) have no dedicated column.
- Related datasets PXD050151 and PXD074943 (2D proteome/phosphoproteome) are separate and were not annotated here.
