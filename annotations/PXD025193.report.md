# PXD025193 annotation report

Palvimo lab (University of Eastern Finland, Kuopio). AR ChIP-SICAP in SILAC-labelled VCaP cells. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates

- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0
- `human` v1.1.0
- `cell-lines` v1.1.0 (VCaP / CVCL_2235)

## Rows and files

12 assay rows (6 RAW × SILAC light + heavy). Channel map from the protocol: light (Arg0, Lys0) = ethanol vehicle; heavy (Arg10, Lys8) = 10 nM R1881. The deposited `proteinGroups.txt` dropped H/L columns, but the accompanying xlsx still has `Ratio H/L` / iBAQ L / iBAQ H, confirming the SILAC design.

Antibody (AR vs IgG) and replicate (R1–R3) come from the RAW filenames.

## Factors

- `factor value[treatment]`: ethanol vs R1881
- `factor value[antibody]`: AR vs IgG

## Conservative choices

- Disease `prostate carcinoma` on every row: VCaP is a prostate-cancer line; ethanol is a vehicle treatment, not a healthy-tissue control (reconcile `disease_with_control_arm` is a false positive).
- Cleavage recorded as Trypsin. Protocol used a Trypsin/Lys-C mix; the cleavage column is single-cardinality so Lys-C is not a second column.
- SILAC label mods (Arg10 / Lys8) written as variable on every row, matching MaxQuant.

## Validation

`structure` OK; `check` CLEAN. Adversarial review-gate was not run (Oulu protocol).
