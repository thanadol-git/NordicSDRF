# PXD029241 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"Changes in the proteome and secretome of rat liver sinusoidal endothelial cells during early primary culture and effects of dexamethasone" (Li et al., PLoS One 2022 17(9):e0273843, PMID 36054185, PMC9439253). Tromsø (K.K. Sørensen lab, UiT). The study cultures primary rat LSECs for 2 h or 24 h, with no treatment (NT), dexamethasone (1 µg/ml), IL-1β (100 U/ml) or Dex + IL-1β. Cell lysates (5 groups) and 24 h culture supernatants (4 groups) are TMT6plex-labelled, with one TMT run per biological replicate. Each replicate pools LSECs from two male Sprague Dawley rat livers.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + vertebrates v1.1.0.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
- PRIDE lists 5 RAW files and 1 search table (`20191227_TMT_Raw_data.txt`).
- **4 of 5 RAW files are annotated**, giving 18 rows across two SDRFs (lysate: 10 rows; supernatant: 8 rows). Unused channels are not emitted.
  - `5plex_CLys_22_26`: lysate, replicate 1.
  - `5plex_CLys_40_44`: lysate, replicate 3.
  - `4plex_CLys_36_39`: supernatant, replicate 2.
  - `4plex_CLys_45_48`: supernatant, replicate 3.
- **Not annotated:** `170126_RLi_TMT_5plex_CLys_17_21.raw`. The search table has a matching "mixRat1" experiment, but its 6 channels carry no sample names. Samples 17–21 do not appear in the paper's Table S1 either, so no channel map exists for it.
- **Not deposited:** the RAW files for lysate replicate 2 (samples 31–35) and supernatant replicate 1 (samples 27–30). They appear only in the search table.

## Channel map (how it was derived)
1. **Sample identity per run.** The deposited search table renames each MaxQuant `Reporter intensity corrected <i> <experiment>` column to a sample number (`22_Rat2`, `36_sRat3`, ...). Unused channels keep their original MaxQuant name, and each leftover sits at its own index position (e.g. `... 5 cRat2` after 22–26, and `0 sRat3`, `36`, `37`, `3 sRat3`, `38`, `39`). So the column order is the channel order.
2. **Sample → condition.** Each deposited column was correlated with the condition-named columns of the paper's Table S1 (`pone.0273843.s001.xlsx`: `NT_2h_Exp1`, ...) after centring each protein within its run. Every column matched exactly one condition with r = 1.00, and all other candidates scored below 0.7. Rat2/Rat3/Rat4 = Exp1/Exp2/Exp3. Lysates are numbered NT 2 h, NT 24 h, Dex, IL-1β, Dex + IL-1β. Supernatants are numbered NT, Dex, IL-1β, Dex + IL-1β.
3. **Channel index → TMT mass.** Index 0–5 = TMT126–131, the standard MaxQuant TMT6 order. This was checked against RAW reporter ions in two files:
   - `5plex_22_26`: 131 is empty (112 spectra vs >91,000 for the others).
   - `4plex_36_39`: 126 and 129 are empty.

   Both match the leftover-column positions. `40_44` and `45_48` were not downloaded; they follow the same verified convention.
4. The two `4plex_CLys_*` files are supernatants despite the "CLys" (cell lysate) in their names. This comes from the search-table experiment names (`sRat3`, `sRat4`) and the Table S1 supernatant match.

## Key decisions
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive (MS:1001911), from the RAW headers (`Instrument model = Q Exactive Orbitrap`), the PRIDE protocol and the paper. The PRIDE structured field says Q Exactive HF; it is overridden.
- **Acquisition:** HCD (RAW header), DDA Top10.
- **Digestion and reduction:** Lys-C then trypsin, both stated. Reduction is DTT, stated.
- **Modifications (round 1 review fix):** carbamidomethyl C, TMT6plex K and Any N-term fixed; oxidation M and protein N-term acetyl variable. Alkylation is iodoacetamide. The evidence is PRIDE's identified PTMs (iodoacetamide derivatized, TMT6plex acylated, monohydroxylated and acetylated residue) and the TMTsixplex kit protocol, which the paper followed. Sites and fixed/variable status are inferred, since no search parameter file was deposited. Tolerances are `not available`.
- **Charge (round 1 review fix):** the ms min/max charge columns were dropped. The values were observed RAW ranges, not method settings.
- **Sample properties:** liver; endothelial cell of hepatic sinusoid; material type cell; male; Sprague Dawley (Crl:CD (SD)), 6 weeks at purchase plus ≥1 week acclimatisation, so developmental stage is `not available`. `pooled` because each replicate is 2 livers.
- **`characteristics[specimen]`:** cell lysate vs cell culture supernatant.
- **Factors:** treatment and sampling time.
