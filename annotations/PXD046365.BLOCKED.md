# PXD046365 — annotation blocked

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits acquisition data only in three large RAR archives: human PExA, pig PExA, and combined pig tissue/BALF.
- Byte-range reads of the DIA-NN reports recover 22 pig tissue run identifiers, 22 pig BALF identifiers, 23 pig PExA identifiers, and 13 human PExA identifiers, including apparent control/QC runs.
- The publication defines pig PGD versus non-PGD groups (9 versus 13 recipients) and human PGD versus non-PGD groups (5 versus 6 recipients), and reports two excluded pig PExA and two excluded BALF samples.
- No primary design table maps the recovered run identifiers to PGD grade, exclusion status, or human/pig biological participant.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Run names recover the matrix and numeric subject code but not the study arm. Assigning PGD status from report order, missing numbers, or heat-map order would be unsupported. The archive contents also include controls/QCs that cannot be classified without the original manifest.

## Unblocking requirement

The sample manifest mapping every archived `.d` acquisition to organism, participant/pig identifier, matrix, PGD grade, inclusion/exclusion status, and any control/QC role.
