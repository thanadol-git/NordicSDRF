# PXD063461 — annotation blocked

Duodenal biopsies from CVID patients with and without intraepithelial lymphocytosis, untreated coeliac disease patients and healthy controls. Kaarbø/Jørgensen (Oslo) with PROMEC, NTNU. Paper: J Allergy Clin Immunol 2023;151:767, PMID 36220400 (not open access).

## Why it is blocked
- **Missing key:** the 37 runs are named only by biobank number (`20210309_CVID_1170` … `_2132`, with `1180_Again` and `1482_Again` re-injections). Nothing in the deposit or open sources maps a number to a group (CVID_IEL, CVID_N, coeliac, control), sex or age.
- **Deposited search has no design:** the `txt.tar` holds a MaxQuant 1.6.17 search whose Experiment names are the same numbers. It has no Groups/design file; `log2LFQ.xlsx` and the clustering CSVs use the same number labels.
- **Supplement not reachable:** the paper's "Online Repository" supplement may describe the cohort, but the article is paywalled.

## Already established
These values are ready for when the sample key turns up:
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF (raw header).
- **Search:** Trypsin/P; Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidation NQ variable; MaxQuant 1.6.17, human UniProt Oct 2020.
- **Sample prep:** proteins from the Qiagen AllPrep kit.

## What would unblock it
A sample sheet mapping biobank number → diagnosis group (and ideally sex/age), from the submitters (M. M. L. de Sousa / A. Sharma, PROMEC) or from the JACI online supplement.
