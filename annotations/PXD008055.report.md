## Decisions and limitations

- Instrument per file from RAW headers (range read): BR files = Orbitrap Elite (MS:1001910; PRIDE label "LTQ Orbitrap Elite" is a synonym), the three others = Q Exactive. PRIDE lists both; paper says only "Elite". Two builds were concatenated (identical headers) because instrument/fragment tolerance differ by run group.
- Fragment tolerance from deposited MaxQuant parameters.txt: ITMS 0.5 Da (Elite, CID ion trap MS/MS), FTMS 20 ppm (Q Exactive). Precursor tolerance not in parameters.txt (not available).
- Dissociation: CID for Elite (paper: CID, collision energy 35); Q Exactive fragmentation not stated, `not available` (not assumed HCD).
- Cleavage agent: digest was Lys-C then trypsin (MaxQuant: Trypsin/P;LysC/P, 3 missed cleavages). The template column comment[cleavage agent details] is single-valued, so only Trypsin/P (MS:1001313) is declared; reconcile flags Lys-C as missing. Kept knowingly; a second column would break the template.
- Modifications from parameters.txt/summary.txt: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Acetyl K variable.
- Sample design: four paper samples = growth phase (mid-log OD600 0.5 vs stationary OD600 1.2) x 2 biological replicates, factor value[growth phase]. Three Q Exactive files (2.5 mg, 7.5 mg Immunechem antibody, 7.5 mg PTM Biolabs) are the "preliminary experiment" appended to the search; the paper does not state their growth phase, so growth phase/factor are `not available`. They are given biological replicate 1 as separate sources; differences (starting protein amount, antibody vendor) are in source names only.
- characteristics[growth phase] is not in the ms-proteomics contract; it was added as an extra column and the validator accepted it.
- organism: "Vibrio cholerae" (strain V52, NCBITaxon 345076 per PRIDE, is not captured: no strain column in template). organism part/cell type/disease `not applicable` (whole bacterial culture; PRIDE disease Cholera describes the pathogen, not the sample). material type `cell`.
- Anti-acetyl-lysine immunoenrichment (Immunechem ICP0388 agarose) is not encoded: no verified enrichment-process term was looked up (column is optional; not in ms-proteomics proper).
- Reduction (DTT) / alkylation (iodoacetamide) optional columns omitted. LC gradient, collision energy not encoded. Whether mid-log/stationary BR files were run on the Elite per the paper while preliminary files are Q Exactive confirmed only from RAW headers.
- No existing annotation in sdrf-annotated-datasets (no directory).
