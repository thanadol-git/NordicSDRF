# PXD033081 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE identifies a human ion-based PISA experiment and deposits 24 RAW files arranged as 12 fractions for `repA` and 12 fractions for `repB`. The deposited `proteinGroups.txt` contains anonymous `Reporter intensity corrected 1` through `16` columns for each replicate; `parameters.txt` supplies processing settings but no experimental design.
- **Why blocked:** The project description covers several anticancer-drug experiments, but no deposited file maps either 16-channel multiplex to a drug, control, cell/lysate context, or source sample. The `repA`/`repB` RAW names identify acquisition batches only. Assigning a channel or treatment would fabricate the source-to-assay relationship.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A deposited multiplex/run sheet mapping all 16 reporter channels in `repA` and `repB` to sample identity, treatment, experimental context, and replicate.
