# PXD006361 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Munk S, Sigurðsson JO, Xiao Z, Batth TS, ... Olsen JV. "Proteomics Reveals Global Regulation of Protein SUMOylation by ATM and ATR Kinases during Replication Stress", Cell Reports 21(2):546-558 (2017), PMID 29020638, doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2017.09.059 (Olsen lab, NNF Center for Protein Research, Copenhagen). Human U-2 OS cells, synchronised with a 24 h thymidine block and released for 8 h into mitomycin C (MMC), or treated with hydroxyurea (HU) for 3 h, with or without ATR (ATR-45), ATM (KU55933) or CHK1 inhibitors. Three readouts: SUMO2 protein IP (FLAG-SUMO2-Q87R), SUMO-peptide enrichment (His10-SUMO2-K0-Q87R, Ni-NTA) and TiO2 phosphopeptides. Q Exactive HF, MaxQuant 1.4.1.4 / 1.5.x. The full text came from the open Leiden repository copy of the paper (PDF); the Supplemental Experimental Procedures and Figures S1-S5 are not reachable (Cell and ScienceDirect return 403, no PMC copy).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `human` v1.1.0 + `cell-lines` v1.1.0 (the latest versions in `spec/sdrf-proteomics/sdrf-templates/templates.yaml`). Human U-2 OS cell line, DDA LC-MS/MS. `dia-acquisition`, `clinical-metadata` and `oncology-metadata` do not apply (no DIA, no patients).

## Sample map
`comment[data file]` is the RAW name inside the archive; `comment[file container]` (added after the build, see Key decisions) is the PRIDE archive.

**A. `OriginalMMC.zip`, SILAC FLAG-SUMO2 IP (40 files) and phospho (10 files).** The channel map follows Fig 1B of the paper (Light = parental U-2 OS + MMC, Medium = FLAG-SUMO2 untreated, Heavy = FLAG-SUMO2 + MMC). The figure carries a "Label swap" arrow between the parental and the untreated sample, and the quantification confirms the swap in experiment 2.

| Files | MaxQuant experiment | Light | Medium | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `..._SUMO_Exp1_{A,B}_01-10` (20) | SUMO E1 Flag | parental + MMC | FLAG-SUMO2 untreated | FLAG-SUMO2 + MMC |
| `..._SUMO_Exp2_{C,D}_01-10` (20) | SUMO E2 Flag | FLAG-SUMO2 untreated | parental + MMC | FLAG-SUMO2 + MMC |
| `..._Phospho_Exp1_01-09, _1-4` (10) | Phospho E1 Flag | MMC | untreated | MMC (cell construct not available) |

- Per file: 3 channel rows, each its own source. Bio replicate 1 = experiment 1, 2 = experiment 2. A/B and C/D are two portions of the same mixed sample (MaxQuant lists A and B under one experiment with the same fractions 1-10), so they are technical replicates 1 and 2 and the fractions are 1-10.
- Cross-checks against the deposited quantification (`MMCFig1txt.zip`, `proteinGroups.txt` and `Phospho (STY)Sites.txt`):
  - SUMO IP: the least-enriched channel (SUMO1/2/3 ratios 10-23 fold lower, PML 4-18 fold) is Light in E1 and Medium in E2, so that channel is the parental control.
  - E2 MMC direction: MMC-up proteins in E1 (H/M) have higher E2 H/L (top-quartile median log2 -0.45 vs bottom -0.74; Spearman 0.23, n = 301 targets), which keeps Heavy = MMC in E2.
  - Proteome runs: the SUMO3/SUMO2 group shows the same pattern (E1 Light 14x lower; E2 Medium 14x lower).
  - Phospho: S/T-Q (ATM/ATR motif) sites are lowest in the Medium channel in E1 and in the Light channel in E2. That fits the same swapped design, but cannot say which treated channel is the parental cell. So the phospho rows get MMC/untreated, not the cell construct.

**B. `MMCATRiATMi.zip` (105 files, 20 sources).** 5 conditions x 4 biological replicates, labelled by file name: `CTRL_NOMMC` / `Replicate_CTRL` (untreated), `MMC_DMSO` / `Replicate_MMC`, `MMC_ATMi`, `MMC_ATRi`, `MMC_ATRi+ATMi`. Bio replicate 1 = the 20150720 series (files 01-06, fractions 1-6), replicates 2-4 = `Replicate_<cond>_01/02/03` (fractions 1-5). The deposited `evidence.txt` Experiment column assigns the same pairing (Rep0-Rep3) and puts file `_06` of Rep0 in a separate "x" experiment.

**C. `HUCHK1iATMiSUMO.zip` (24 files, 12 sources).** 3 HU conditions (`3hHU`, `3hHU-SCH`, `3hHU-SCH-ATMi`) x 4 bio replicates `EXP1-4`. Each sample was run twice: 20160226 files = technical replicate 1, 20160229 `_2` files = technical replicate 2. This matches the paper ("Four biological replicates ... each sample was analyzed twice") and the MaxQuant experiments `3hHU-E1`/`E1B`.

**D. `HUCHK1iATMiPhospho.rar` (48 files, 24 sources).** 24 TiO2 files (`TIO2-3hHU*-EXP1-4-1ST/2ND_01`) plus 24 `SUMO-PHOSPHO` files (`20160326` 1ST_2 and `20160405` 2ND_2). 1ST/2ND = first/second injection of one sample (MaxQuant lists them as experiments `...-1ST` / `...-2ND`), so technical replicate 1/2. TiO2 and SUMO-PHOSPHO series are kept as different sources because the record does not say they share cultures.

## Key decisions
- **Channel map, not invented.** For OriginalMMC the map comes from the paper's Fig 1B plus the quantification checks above. The Phospho Exp1 rows carry only the evidenced facts (MMC/untreated, from the S/T-Q induction pattern); the FLAG/parental identity of the treated channels is `not available`.
- **File container.** Every row carries `comment[file container]` (the PRIDE archive). `sdrf-tools build` does not emit this column, so it was added to the built table with a short script, after the last `comment[...]` column and before the `factor value[...]` columns. Run names in `comment[data file]` are the names inside the archive.
- **Instrument.** Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523): confirmed from the UTF-16 instrument strings in the RAW headers of the first RAW of `OriginalMMC.zip` (QE3), `MMCATRiATMi.zip` (QE5) and `HUCHK1iATMiSUMO.zip` (QE3). The RAR cannot be range-read; its files carry the same QE3/QE5 instrument codes, so HF is inferred. Dissociation = beam-type CID (HCD), implied by the instrument; the record does not state it.
- **Digest, modifications, tolerances** from the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` / `parameters.txt` of each search (Trypsin/P, 2 missed cleavages, Carbamidomethyl-C fixed, FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm):
  - Series A (`OriginalMMC`): Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Gln->pyro-Glu, Phospho (STY), QQTGG (K) UNIMOD:2082, Pyro-QQTGG (K) UNIMOD:2083.
  - Series B: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Deamidated (NQ), Gln->pyro-Glu, QQTGG, Pyro-QQTGG.
  - Series C: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Phospho (STY), Deamidated (NQ), Gln->pyro-Glu, Carbamyl (K and Any N-term), QQTGG, Pyro-QQTGG.
  - Series D: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Phospho (STY).
  - The paper says the His10-SUMO2 peptides were digested with Lys-C then trypsin, so series C lists Lys-C + trypsin; all other series trypsin.
- **Cell line.** U-2 OS = Cellosaurus CVCL_0042 (`sdrf-tools cellline`: female, 15Y, osteosarcoma). Organism part `bone`. The PRIDE field "bone marrow (BTO)" was not used (see Conflicts).
- **Treatment text** is free text; it keeps what the paper states: 8 h MMC release after a 24 h thymidine block, 3 h HU, named inhibitors ATR-45 (ATRi) and KU55933 (ATMi), and an unnamed "CHK1 inhibitor". No doses (Supplemental Procedures not reachable). The file-name code `SCH` is probably SCH900776 but that is not written in the paper, so it is not used.
- **Factors.** `factor value[treatment]` (MMC or HU +/- inhibitors) and `factor value[genetic modification]` (parental vs FLAG-SUMO2-Q87R vs His10-SUMO2-K0-Q87R; it is constant within series B-C).
- **Enrichment.** `anti-FLAG immunoprecipitation of SUMOylated proteins`, `enrichment of SUMOylated peptides (Ni-NTA, His10-SUMO2)`, `TiO2 phosphopeptide enrichment`. No `no enrichment` rows exist (the proteome runs were not deposited).
- **Fractions.** FLAG-IP runs `_01-10` / `_01-06` / `_01-05` are numbered 1..n. They are probably gel slices (the Methods say in-gel digestion) but this is not stated, so `fractionation method` is `not available`. Phospho Exp1 `_01-09` = 1-9 (high-pH RP per the paper); the pooled file `_1-4` was given fraction 15 as a placeholder outside the 1-14 range.
- **Row identity.** One row per (source, file, channel); `assay name` = file stem, shared by the 3 SILAC channels.

## Conflicts / overrides
- PRIDE `instruments`: "Q Exactive"; PRIDE protocol: "Q exactive plus or HF"; paper: "Q-Exactive high-frequency (HF)". RAW headers: Q Exactive HF. -> HF (`sdrf-tools reconcile` flags this as `instrument_contradicted` because it reads "Q-exactive plus"; the record itself is ambiguous).
- PRIDE `organismParts`: "bone marrow" (BTO:0000141). U-2 OS is an osteosarcoma line of tibial bone, so `bone` is used.
- The `20160405_..._SUMO-PHOSPHO-2hHU-*` file names say 2 h HU, while MaxQuant experiments (`3hHU-EXPn-2ND_2`) and the paper say 3 h. They pair with the `20160326 ... 3hHU ... 1ST_2` files as injection 2 of the same sample, so 3 h is used. If the 2 h in the file names is real, these 12 rows are wrong.
- PRIDE's description gives "Quantitation with SILAC", but Series B-D are label-free, as MaxQuant `summary.txt` (Multiplicity 1) and the paper both state.
- The deposit has MMC + ATMi (without ATRi) runs in Series B, a condition not in the paper's Fig 4B (4 conditions); it is annotated from the file names.
- `reconcile` also notes that the record does not describe the digest (MINOR); the digest comes from the MaxQuant output and the paper's Methods.

## Reviewer attack points
- Medium confidence: the E2 SILAC map. The parental channel (Medium) is strongly supported (SUMO enrichment 10-20 fold); that MMC = Heavy and untreated = Light is supported by the paper's "Label swap" arrow and by a weak (rho 0.23) agreement of ratios, not by a stated table.
- The Phospho Exp1 channels (L/H both MMC, M untreated) rely on S/T-Q motif induction only; `genetic modification` is not available there.
- A/B and C/D as technical replicates of one mixed sample, and `_06` of the 20150720 series as the sixth fraction of replicate 1, are interpretations of file naming plus MaxQuant experiment names, not stated in the paper.
- The 2hHU file-name conflict (12 rows) and the placeholder fraction 15 for `Phospho_Exp1_1-4`.
- HF for the 48 RAR files is inferred, not read from the headers.
- `SUMO-PHOSPHO` enrichment recorded as TiO2 although the workflow is described only by the file name.
- The 138 searched but undeposited files; no rows exist for them and none were invented.
