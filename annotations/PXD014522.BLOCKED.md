# PXD014522 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: Discovery of species-unique peptide biomarkers of bacterial pathogens by tandem mass spectrometry-based proteotyping

Publication: Karlsson R et al., Molecular & Cellular Proteomics 2020 19(3):518–528, PMID 31941798, PMCID PMC7050107, DOI 10.1074/mcp.RA119.001667

## Evidence collected

- 44 Q Exactive/Q Exactive HF RAW files and paired Proteome Discoverer `.msf` searches
- PRIDE states that the deposition contains representative clinical samples; the publication describes nasopharyngeal/nasal swabs, including frequent coinfections, analyzed directly after human-biomass depletion
- Hi/Mc/Sa/Sp filename prefixes identify the targeted pathogen peptide list, not necessarily the full organism composition of the clinical specimen
- The paper used both data-dependent acquisition (on QE and QEHF) and proof-of-concept PRM (on QEHF), but no deposited sample sheet maps the 44 RAW files to clinical specimen, study phase, or acquisition mode

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

Assigning each run to a pure single bacterial species would misrepresent mixed clinical swabs, and assigning every QEHF run to either DDA or PRM from the instrument token alone is unsupported. Both sample composition and acquisition method are required SDRF fields.

## What would unblock it

A run manifest mapping each RAW file to clinical specimen or culture/spike status, detected/targeted organism(s), study phase, and acquisition mode (DDA with inclusion list or PRM).
