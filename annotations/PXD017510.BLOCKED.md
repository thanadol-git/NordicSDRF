# PXD017510: source and replicate map required

The [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD017510) contains 39 RAW files from several biological arms: OVCAR-3 and SKOV-3 cell lysate or secretome, HEK 293 controls, ovarian tumor material with opaque codes `BOLDR`, `LUCC`, `RICC`, `VIC` and `NIC`, and an explicitly named mouse-kidney run. A single human ovarian-cancer SDRF would misassign the mouse acquisition and obscure the other arms.

The [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7600217/) describes four untreated high-grade serous ovarian tumor samples, OV1–OV4, and the [supplementary Tables S3–S5](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7600217/supplementaryFiles) label the same OV1–OV4 groups and cell-line categories. Neither source links the five deposited opaque codes to those four tumors or identifies which coded runs belong to the reported study. Several RAW names use suffixes such as `_1`/`_2`, `P20`/`P80`, and `LWAC10`–`LWAC13`; no accession-specific key establishes whether these are biological replicates, technical reinjections, fractions, or different preparations.

The paper's Methods identify Orbitrap Fusion and sequential HCD/ETD acquisition, resolving the malformed `LTQ-Orbitrap Velos ProFusion` wording in PRIDE's protocol against its structured Fusion instrument field. That technical detail is available, but source assignment and replicate identity are still needed before the 39 runs can be grouped into defensible SDRFs. No SDRF is approved.
