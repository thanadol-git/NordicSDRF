# PXD016146 — annotation blocked by current SDRF scope

## Dataset

- **Title:** MALDI rat liver anticancer drug spiked-in dataset (imzML)
- **PRIDE record:** PXD016146
- **Publication:** Eriksson *et al.*, *Analytical Chemistry* (2019), DOI [10.1021/acs.analchem.9b02637](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.9b02637)

## Evidence recovered

- One MALDI mass-spectrometry-imaging acquisition is deposited as Thermo RAW, imzML, ibd, and MALDIPos files, together with a ground-truth compound list.
- The PRIDE description documents five drug mixtures applied to rat liver tissue and refers to the supporting spreadsheet for their compositions.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

This is an imaging-MS dataset, not a conventional bottom-up proteomics experiment.  The available local SDRF templates and validation profile target `ms-proteomics` sample/assay metadata and do not represent imaging pixels, coordinates, or imzML/ibd relationships.  Emitting a peptide-proteomics SDRF would misrepresent the data model.
