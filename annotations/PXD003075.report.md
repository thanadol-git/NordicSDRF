# PXD003075 annotation report

## Outcome

Supported and annotated from the accession-specific PRIDE record, complete PRIDE file listing, and open-access publication. The SDRF contains 180 rows: six TMT6 channels for each of 30 RAW files, representing 15 participant multiplexes acquired in two technical replicates.

## Evidence used

- PRIDE project record and protocols: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD003075`
- Complete PRIDE file listing: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD003075/files/all`
- Open-access publication: `https://doi.org/10.1186/s13195-016-0178-x` (PMCID `PMC4780148`). Table 1 gives the exact reporter-to-time mapping, and the Methods describe participant-level TMT6 multiplexing and duplicate LC-MS analysis.

## File and channel mapping

- The complete PRIDE listing contains 30 RAW files. Each of the 15 deposited `LFAM` participant identifiers occurs exactly once in the `20130822` acquisition series and once in the `131108` series.
- The publication states that the six samples from each participant were combined into one TMT6 set and that each set was analyzed in two technical replicates. The matching `LFAM` identifiers therefore provide an exact participant-to-run pairing for all 30 RAW files.
- Publication Table 1 maps TMT126, TMT127, TMT128, TMT129, TMT130, and TMT131 to 0, 3, 9, 12, 18, and 36 hours, respectively.
- Technical-replicate numbers are deterministic local identifiers: the `20130822` series is replicate 1 and the `131108` series is replicate 2. The numbering carries no chronological or biological meaning.

## Limitations

- Neither the PRIDE deposit nor the publication provides a participant-ID-to-treatment-group crosswalk. Placebo, 140 mg, and 280 mg semagacestat factors are intentionally omitted rather than inferred.
- Participant-level age, sex, ancestry, and developmental stage are unavailable and are recorded as `not available`.
- The PRIDE overview says baseline plus six subsequent time points, but the detailed Methods and Table 1 explicitly define six total analyzed time points. The SDRF follows the exact table: 0, 3, 9, 12, 18, and 36 hours.
- The sources do not explicitly name a dissociation method or collision energy, so those fields are omitted.
- A fresh-context adversarial reviewer was unavailable in this session. No review-gate state was written because the campaign explicitly forbids touching git state.
