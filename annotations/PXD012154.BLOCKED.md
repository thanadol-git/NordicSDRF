# PXD012154 — annotation blocked

Secretome of PANC-1 pancreatic ductal cells persistently infected with coxsackievirus B1 (strains ATCC and 10796) vs non-infected cells. University of Turku. Paper: Lietzén et al., iScience 2019 (PMC6699423).

## What is known
- **Runs:** samples `S1–S9`, each injected 3 times (27 runs). Q Exactive HF, per the raw header.
- **Design:** the paper collected samples at three time points (300, 315 and 322 days post-infection). This gives 3 groups × 3 biological replicates.
- **Search:** MaxQuant against human SwissProt plus enterovirus sequences. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Why it is blocked
No deposited file or paper text maps `S1–S9` to non-infected, CVB1 ATCC or CVB1 10796. I opened `MaxQuant.7z`: its `summary.txt` and `parameters.txt` use the raw file names as experiment names, so there is no key.

## What would unblock it
A table mapping samples S1–S9 to infection group and collection day.
