# PXD047125 — blocked SDRF annotation

## Evidence checked

- The PRIDE project record describes additional label-free, data-dependent proteomics data for the cardiac-amyloidosis study deposited primarily as PXD033168. It identifies human endomyocardial biopsy material, Orbitrap Fusion Lumos acquisition, trypsin digestion, and the associated publication (DOI `10.1080/13506129.2022.2127088`).
- The complete PRIDE file inventory contains 113 Thermo RAW files.
- Both deposited sample-list files were inspected:
  - `3169_Lumos_190517_21_27_amyloid_samples.csv`
  - `3169_Lumos_190517_21_27_amyloid_samples.xlsx`
- The deposited sample list maps every RAW stem to an internal identifier, preparation replicate, and injection replicate. Most identifiers are numeric (for example, `3169 1:1 Inj1` and `3169 35:2 Inj3`); two late runs are labelled `3169 S05 R1` and `3169 S28 R1`.
- The publication and its online tables/supplement links were checked. The paper defines a 75-patient cardiac-amyloidosis cohort plus 12 controls, reports duplicate preparations where material allowed and triplicate injections, and refers to cases using identifiers such as `S01`, `S05`, and `S28`. It states that the principal study data were deposited as PXD033168.
- No community SDRF is available for PXD047125.

## Exact blocker

The accession-specific sample list does not state that its numeric internal identifiers (for example, `1`, `2`, `3`, and `35`) are the publication's clinical case identifiers (`S01`, `S02`, `S03`, and `S35`), nor does it map those numeric identifiers to patient/control status or amyloid subtype. The publication and its supplementary material describe the broader PXD033168 cohort but do not explicitly cross-reference the `3169` identifiers used for the 113 PXD047125 RAW files. Converting the numeric labels to publication case IDs, or assigning disease/control and subtype values from similarly numbered publication rows, would therefore be an unsupported sample mapping. No SDRF was written.

## Evidence that would unblock the accession

An accession-specific manifest or submitter confirmation explicitly mapping every `3169` sample identifier to the publication case/control identifier and biological group (including amyloid subtype where applicable), while preserving the deposited preparation and injection replicates.
