## Study

Cell Metab 2025;37(4):971-986.e8, doi:10.1016/j.cmet.2025.01.022, PMID 40037362 (SDU Odense). Not in PMC; PRIDE protocols and the abstract were used. Label-free DIA of mouse serum from wild-type vs hepatic stellate cell-specific Plvap-knockout mice on control (CD) or high-fat diet (HFD).

## Sample map

## Key decisions
- Instrument Orbitrap Exploris 480 (MS:1003028): RAW headers of all 22 files (method `DIA_120Knew_56win_54ms_120min`) and the protocol; DIA, HCD.
- Lys-C + trypsin; DTT (PRIDE:0000608), iodoacetamide (PRIDE:0000599).
- Modifications from the stated DIA-NN 1.8.1 search: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and N-terminal Acetyl (protein N-term) variable. Precursor tolerance 10 ppm (stated).
- Organism part **blood serum** (UBERON:0001977): the sample protocol says "100 µg isolated serum"; PRIDE's organism-part field says blood plasma.
- Genotype: KO = hepatic stellate cell-specific Plvap knockout (PRIDE description: "HSC-specific ablation of PLVAP"); WT = wild type. The Cre driver isn't named in the available text.
- Factors: genotype and treatment (diet).
