# PXD001996
Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

## Dataset
- **Title:** Multi-layered proteomics of EGFR signaling _Rab7 data
- **Lab:** Jesper V. Olsen lab, Novo Nordisk Foundation Center for Protein Research, University of Copenhagen, Denmark (submitter Chiara Francavilla). Copenhagen (Denmark) queue.
- **Paper:** Francavilla C et al. *Multilayered proteomics reveals molecular switches dictating ligand-dependent EGFR trafficking.* Nat Struct Mol Biol 2016;23(6):608-618. PMID 27136326, PMC7618131, doi:10.1038/nsmb.3218. The paper's data-availability statement names PXD001996 as "the RAB7-GFP dataset". PRIDE lists no reference; the paper was found by a Europe PMC search on the accession.
- **Experiment:** HeLa cells, triple-SILAC labelled and transfected with GFP-RAB7A (wild type) or GFP-RAB7A Y183F. GFP-trap immunoprecipitation, then a 1:1:1 SILAC mix, SDS-PAGE (7 gel slices), in-gel trypsin digestion, and Q Exactive Plus DDA. Two biological replicates. This is the RAB7 interactome shown in Supplementary Fig. 6.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| medium | Lys4/Arg6 | GFP-RAB7 wild type, EGF 8 min | GFP-RAB7A wild type | epidermal growth factor |
| heavy | Lys8/Arg10 | GFP-RAB7 Y183F, EGF 8 min | GFP-RAB7A Y183F | epidermal growth factor |

There are 6 sources: 3 channels in each of 2 biological replicates, named `HeLa_<condition>_rep<n>`.

File-to-replicate and fraction mapping comes from the deposited `MaxQuantOutput/Rab7_ExpDesign.txt`:
- **Biological replicate 1** is MaxQuant experiment `rab7mu1`, made up of the `Exp1_*` files: F1–F7 are fractions 1–7 (technical replicate 1), and F1b is a re-injection of fraction 1 (technical replicate 2).
- **Biological replicate 2** is MaxQuant experiment `rab7mu2`, made up of the `Exp2_*` files: F1–F7 are fractions 1–7 (technical replicate 1). F2b, F4b and F7b are re-injections of fractions 2, 4 and 7 (technical replicate 2), and F4c is a second re-injection of fraction 4 (technical replicate 3).
- **RAW header check:** I range-read all 19 RAW files. The 8 `Exp1_*` files carry their deposited names. All 11 deposited `Exp2_*` files were acquired as `Exp3_*`, with the same date, fraction and injection suffix. This is a consistent rename with no swaps, and the MaxQuant design groups exactly these 11 files as `rab7mu2`.
- All 19 RAW files are in the SDRF. The only other deposited file is `MaxQuantOutput.tar.gz` (search output).

Technical values:
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive Plus (MS:1002634), from the RAW headers ("Q Exactive Plus - Orbitrap MS") and the paper. The PRIDE instrument field says Q Exactive; I overrode it.
- **Acquisition:** DDA top 10, HCD at NCE 25, MS1 at 70,000 resolution over 300–1750 m/z, MS2 at 35,000 resolution in the Orbitrap. All from the instrument method stored in the RAW header.
- **Cleavage agent:** Trypsin/P (MS:1001313). The paper says the gel slices were digested with trypsin. In the deposited `peptides.txt`, MaxQuant counts a K/R before P as a missed cleavage (2084 such peptides), which it only does under Trypsin/P.
- **Modifications** (from the deposited `Rab7_parameters.txt`, MaxQuant 1.4.1.4):
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C).
  - Variable: Acetyl (Protein N-term), Gln->pyro-Glu and Oxidation (M), all from the "Modifications included in protein quantification" list.
  - Variable: Phospho (STY), GlyGly (K) and Tyr->Phe (Y), from the site tables, which match the site-ID columns in `peptides.txt`. Tyr->Phe lets the search identify the Y183F mutant peptide.
  - SILAC labels Label:2H(4) K, Label:13C(6) R, Label:13C(6)15N(2) K and Label:13C(6)15N(4) R, written as variable, taken from Supplementary Fig. 6a.
- **Fragment tolerance:** 20 ppm (MaxQuant FTMS MS/MS tolerance).
- **Fractionation:** SDS PAGE (PRIDE:0000568).

## Post-processing
`build` numbers fractions by their position in a row's file list, so it cannot give a re-injection its real fraction number. After building, `scratchpad/PXD001996/postprocess.py` set `comment[fraction identifier]` on every row to the Fraction column of `Rab7_ExpDesign.txt`. A diff against the built file showed exactly 12 changed cells, all in `comment[fraction identifier]`. They are F2b 1→2, F4b 2→4, F7b 3→7 and F4c 1→4, each in 3 channels. The (source, biological replicate, technical replicate, fraction) coordinate and the (source, assay, label) identity are both unique.

## Limitations
- **Channel map:** taken from Supplementary Fig. 6a/6e, a figure image I read at 300 dpi. It is not a deposited table, and the Supplementary Tables were not opened.
- **Biological replicate grouping:** taken from the deposited MaxQuant experiment design, cross-checked against the RAW headers. The rename from Exp3 to Exp2 at deposition is documented above.
- **Technical replicate numbers for re-injections:** inferred from the b/c file-name suffixes, the later acquisition dates and the MaxQuant fraction assignments.
- **`characteristics[sampling time]`:** "8 minute" means time after EGF addition at harvest, an interpretation of "+ EGF 8 min". Unstimulated rows are `not applicable`.
- **`characteristics[genotype]`:** describes the transiently transfected construct, GFP-RAB7A wild type or Y183F (EFO:0000513, free text). The column is not in the chosen templates' contract but is defined in the spec (TERMS.tsv).
- **Cell-line properties:** disease (endocervical adenocarcinoma, MONDO:0000554), age 30Y6M, sex female, organism part uterine cervix and cell type epithelial cell come from Cellosaurus CVCL_0030. The paper says only that the cells were bought from ATCC.
- **`characteristics[ancestry category]` = `not available`:** Cellosaurus gives no population annotation.
- **`characteristics[passage number]` = `not available`:** not stated in the paper.
- **`comment[precursor mass tolerance]` = `not available`:** MaxQuant 1.4 `parameters.txt` does not record it, and no `mqpar.xml` or `summary.txt` was deposited.
- **Reduction and alkylation reagents:** not stated. The columns are omitted even though Carbamidomethyl (C) was a fixed search modification.
- **GFP-trap immunoprecipitation:** no fitting term exists under EFO:0009090, so `characteristics[enrichment process]` is omitted.
- **SILAC label modifications:** taken from the paper figure, not from a deposited `mqpar.xml`, which does not exist here. The ratio columns (M/L, H/L, H/M) confirm triplex SILAC.
