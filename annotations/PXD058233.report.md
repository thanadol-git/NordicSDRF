## Decisions and limitations

- Two cleavage columns (Trypsin, Lys-C): build emits one (column non-multiple), so the Lys-C column was inserted after build with a script; parse_sdrf accepts it. Rebuilding from tables alone would lose it. Digest per protocol is Trypsin/LysC; MaxQuant searched Trypsin/P, recorded as Trypsin.
- Modifications from mqpar.xml: Carbamidomethyl (fixed), Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term (variable). The deposit's protocol prose says protein N-terminal methylation, contradicting mqpar; the deposited parameters were used.
- Fragment tolerance 20 ppm is the MaxQuant FTMS default in mqpar; precursor 4.5 ppm (main search).
- Organism part "shoot" for all fractions (aerial harvest; PRIDE lists leaf/plant cell, not adopted); material type "organism part". The sample-level meaning of juice and pulp is carried only by factor value[fraction].
- Two cultivation batches (48 and 50 days) are mentioned but the mapping to replicates A/B/C is not stated; A/B/C treated as biological replicates 1-3 and batch not recorded. Developmental stage not available. Disease not applicable (PRIDE says disease free; plant).
- Dissociation HCD from mqpar fragmentation parameters. Paper full text and RAW headers were not read.
