The paper is open access. Tables 1 and 2 and the supplement File S1 (Text S1, Table S2) were used.

  - 12 archaeological skin objects from Danish bogs.
  - 3 modern reference skins (domestic sheep, goat and cattle).
  - 2 blank negative controls.
- **SDRF:** `annotations/PXD001029.sdrf.tsv`
- **Size:** 23 rows, 36 columns

## Templates and why

- **ms-proteomics v1.1.0:** the data are label-free DDA LC-MS/MS runs on Thermo Orbitrap instruments.
- **vertebrates v1.1.0:** the source animals are sheep, goat and cattle, which are non-human vertebrates.
- **Not chosen:**
  - human: the human proteome was searched only as a contaminant and reference database.
  - dia-acquisition: both methods are DDA Top10.
  - clinical and cell-line templates: they do not apply.
- **One SDRF, not split by instrument:** the two instruments correspond to the paper's two preparation procedures within a single study and a single search strategy. The instrument is recorded per row instead.

## Design and file mapping

- **Factor value:** `factor value[organism]`, the species of the skin. This is what the study determines.
  - *Ovis aries*: 10 rows (7 sources).
  - *Capra hircus*: 6 rows (4 sources).
  - *Bos taurus*: 4 rows (3 sources).
  - *Caprinae*: 1 row (Roum, which the paper could assign only as sheep/goat).
  - `not applicable`: 2 blank rows.
- **Procedure A:** paper samples 1, 4, 5, 6, 10 and 12, marked * in Table 1.
  - Instrument: LTQ Orbitrap Velos.
  - Text S1 says each object was split into two subsamples that were processed independently. Each object therefore has two runs: `..._S3x` from 2010-10-15 and `..._4x` from 2010-12-15.
  - These are encoded as one source with `comment[technical replicate]` 1 and 2. Table S2 reports their spectra as sums of two runs, for example 16513+15217.
- **Procedure B:** paper samples 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 and 11, run on the Q Exactive (2012-07-17), one run each.
- **Modern references and blanks:** modern sheep CN3213, modern goat CN3196 and modern cattle, plus blanks B01 and B02. All were run on the Q Exactive (2012-07-17), one run each.
- **Sources:** 17 sources, named after the museum numbers and the MaxQuant search names, for example `Baunso_NM_D11103a`, `modern_sheep_CN3213` and `blank_B01`. `characteristics[biological replicate]` numbers independent objects from 1 within each species. The order follows the paper's sample numbers, with the modern reference last. The blanks are B01 = 1 and B02 = 2.
- **Source of the mapping:** the deposited MaxQuant searches together with the RAW headers.
  - The `summary.txt` of every one of the 17 search zips was read with HTTP range requests. Each search contains exactly the raw files that this SDRF maps to that source.
  - The search names give the identity, because the original Velos acquisition names carry only sequence numbers: `COLLAB_Enrico-Parchments_S34`–`S39` and `Eske_Ancient_Skin_Sample_41`–`46`. For example, `Baunso_NM_D11103a_main_search` contains S34 and 41, and `Mogelmose_NM_16316_main_search` contains S39 and 46.
  - The RAW headers of all 23 deposited files give the same original names, so the deposited file names are consistent with the searches.
  - The two QE blanks were originally `ANCIENT136_B02` and `ANCIENT148_B01`.
- **Organism per object:** from the "MS" column of paper Table 2.

## Technical values and where they came from

- **Instrument:** LTQ Orbitrap Velos for the 12 Velos rows and Q Exactive for the 11 QE rows. Both come from the RAW headers ("LTQ Orbitrap Velos MS", "Q Exactive - Orbitrap MS") and agree with PRIDE.
- **Acquisition:** DDA. The Velos method is `150min_Top10HCD_JESPER_Special`. The QE method is `120minTop10Sensitive`, and Text S1 says it ran in data-dependent mode with up to 10 MS/MS.
- **Fragmentation:** HCD (`MS:1000422`), with MS2 read in the Orbitrap. The `msms.txt` files show Fragmentation=HCD and Mass analyzer=FTMS for a Velos search (Baunso a) and for QE searches (Karlby b, blank).
- **Search settings:** MaxQuant 1.2.2.5, identical in all 17 searches.
  - Protease: Trypsin/P, 2 missed cleavages.
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C).
  - Variable: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (K), Deamidation (NQ), Gln->pyro-Glu, Glu->pyro-Glu and Pro->Hyp. Pro->Hyp is written as Oxidation on P, UNIMOD:35.
  - Text S1 agrees with these settings.
- **Tolerances:** precursor 6 ppm (Text S1, "default values"). Fragment 20 ppm (Text S1 and `parameters.txt` FTMS).
- **Reduction and alkylation:**
  - Procedure B (Text S1, FASP): DTT (`PRIDE:0000608`) and 2-chloroacetamide (`PRIDE:0000600`).
  - Procedure A: the protocol has an HCl extraction and trypsin digestion but no reduction or alkylation step, so these rows are `not applicable`.
- **`comment[sample preparation batch]`:** `procedure A`, `procedure B`, or `not available` for the modern references and blanks.
- **`comment[acquisition date]`:** taken from the date prefix of the original acquisition names.

## Limitations
- **Revised after independent review:** `factor value[organism]` was removed. Species is the study's finding rather than an experimental condition, the Caprinae level overlaps sheep and goat, and the blanks had no level; `characteristics[sample type]` = study sample cannot be used because the offline PRIDE cache used by upstream CI rejects it. The SDRF therefore has no factor column (CI advisory only), and `characteristics[biological replicate]` now numbers the 17 sources 1-17 (both runs of a procedure-A object share one number) instead of restarting per organism. Modern reference tissue as skin is stated in the paper ("two historic skin samples ... and from a cattle skin").


- **Organism is the authors' MS result:**
  - The organism of each archaeological object is the authors' MS-based species assignment (paper Table 2), not an a-priori known identity.
  - Sample 11 (Roum) could be resolved only to sheep/goat and is written as *Caprinae* (NCBITaxon:9963), the narrowest NCBI taxon that contains both *Ovis* and *Capra*.
  - Sample 1 (Baunsø a) and sample 12 (Haraldskær) are goat by MS but are called differently by microscopy.
- **Modern references:**
  - Their organism part, `skin of body`, is inferred from the paper's "modern comparable material" and the PRIDE organism part "Skin". The tissue is not named explicitly.
  - The preparation procedure is not stated for the modern references or the blanks. `comment[sample preparation batch]`, `comment[reduction reagent]` and `comment[alkylation reagent]` are therefore `not available` on those 5 rows.
- **Procedure-A replicates:** subsample 1 versus subsample 2 is not labelled anywhere. Technical replicate 1 and 2 follow acquisition order (October versus December 2010). That the two runs are two independently processed subsamples, rather than re-injections, comes from Text S1 and Table S2.
- **Blanks:** B01 and B02 are the "negative controls" the paper mentions. Their exact composition (extraction or LC blank) is not described. Organism, organism part, disease, sex and developmental stage are `not applicable`. `characteristics[sample type]` was omitted because the accepted PRIDE list would force `study sample` on the skin rows, and the validator rejects that value.
- **Sex, developmental stage, disease:** `not available` for every animal row, because the paper, supplement and PubMed MeSH have no per-sample data. The paper interprets foetal haemoglobin peptides in sample 10 (Møgelmose) as calfskin from an animal a few months old. This was not encoded as a developmental stage.
- **Context kept only in source names:** archaeological dating, bog locality and museum numbers appear only in the source names. No ontology-backed column exists for them.
- **Cleavage agent:** MaxQuant searched with Trypsin/P, while the SDRF records the digestion enzyme as Trypsin (`MS:1001251`).
- **Precursor tolerance:** 6 ppm is the paper's statement of the MaxQuant default. MaxQuant 1.2.2.5's `parameters.txt` does not list it, and no `mqpar.xml` was deposited.
- **Mapping provenance:** the file-to-sample mapping comes from the deposited search tables and the RAW headers. The sample-to-species mapping comes from paper Table 2. No values were taken from file names alone.
