# PXD010265 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The deposit is multiplexed (iTRAQ 4-plex and TMT 10-plex) and no source gives the channel-to-sample map, nor which of the 66 RAW files belong to which plex or enrichment/fraction series. The four biological groups are known (normal, normal + 5.6 mM glucose, db/db, db/db + 5.6 mM glucose), but not which reporter channel carries which group or replicate. Without that map there is no sample identity for any (file x channel) row, and channel identities may not be inferred.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE v3 project record and the complete `files/all` inventory (132 files: 66 RAW, 66 SEARCH; every entry's `projectAccessions` contains PXD010265).
- Deposited search outputs: the `*Merged.tsv` files (MS-GF+ combined with MASIC). Header and rows of `QHF1572Merged.tsv`, `FUS04002Merged.tsv` and `LUM2_01177_MRLMerged.tsv` were read. They show reporter columns only (`Ion_114..117` for QHF/FUS, `Ion_126.128..131.138` for LUM2) and carry no sample names. There is no mzid, mqpar, PD study or sample sheet in this deposit.
- Publication of record: Kang et al., J Proteome Res 2019 (PMID 31317746, doi 10.1021/acs.jproteome.9b00160). It is closed access (Unpaywall `oa_status: closed`, no PMCID), and the publisher page is behind a Cloudflare challenge.
- Companion open paper: Kang et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 2020 (PMC7261816, PMID 32265294), whose Data Availability statement says the proteomics data (PXD010265) were published previously (ref 10, the JPR paper). Methods and Fig. 1 were read. They give the design (4 groups, 4 proteomics biological replicates) and say samples were tagged with the iTRAQ 4-plex or TMT 10-plex kit, but give no channel assignment. Supplementary Tables S1-S4 and the Supplementary Figures on PMC returned a reCAPTCHA page, and Europe PMC reports the article as not open access for supplementary files, so they could not be read.
- Companion PRIDE project PXD017469 (phospho/sialylome of the same design): record and file list checked, no sample sheet or manifest.
- Data Availability links: Panorama Public (PRM validation only, not the discovery plexes). No Zenodo, figshare or GitHub repository is cited.

No deposited SDRF was used as evidence.

## Evidence needed to unblock

A channel-to-sample and file-to-plex table from the submitter, or the JPR 2019 supplement or the MCP 2020 Supplementary Tables S1-S4 if one of them contains it. Inferring channels from reporter-ion intensity patterns was rejected as fabrication.
