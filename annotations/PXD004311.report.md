## Design and file mapping

Juvenile Atlantic cod received an intraperitoneal injection of either vehicle or methylmercury chloride (CH3HgCl) at 0.5 or 2 mg/kg body weight, given as two half-doses on day 1 and day 7. Livers were sampled at day 14. Each liver was digested separately with trypsin, and 1 µg of peptides was injected once (no fractionation).

| Group | Files | n | `factor value[dose]` |
|---|---|---|---|
| vehicle control | `OAK_K1.raw` … `OAK_K10.raw` | 10 | 0 mg/kg |
| 0.5 mg/kg MeHg | `OAK_05_1.raw` … `OAK_05_9.raw` | 9 | 0.5 mg/kg |
| 2 mg/kg MeHg | `OAK_2_1.raw` … `OAK_2_9.raw` | 9 | 2 mg/kg |

- **How the mapping was established:** from a deposited table. The deposited Progenesis export (`MeHg_Ensemble_MSGF_Xt_Amanda_Progenesis_export_Peptides.csv`) has a condition header row that groups OAK_K1–K10 under "Kontrol", OAK_2_1–2_9 under "2" and OAK_05_1–05_9 under "0,5". The group sizes (10/9/9) match the paper and the PRIDE protocol exactly. The 8 mg/kg group in the paper did not survive and has no data.
- Source names are `cod_liver_<fish id>` (e.g. `cod_liver_K1`, `cod_liver_05_1`, `cod_liver_2_1`). `characteristics[biological replicate]` is the fish index within its group.
- `characteristics[compound]` is `methylmercury chloride` (CHEBI:28216) for treated fish and `not applicable` for vehicle controls. `characteristics[dose]` mirrors the factor value.
- Factor value: `factor value[dose]` is the compared variable. The paper analyses dose groups against controls with ANOVA and Dunnett's test.

## Technical values and sources

- Instrument: `NT=Orbitrap Velos Pro;AC=MS:1003096`. The `OAK_K1.raw` header says "Orbitrap Velos Pro" and the paper says "LTQ-Orbitrap Velos Pro". PRIDE's structured field says "LTQ Orbitrap Velos" (MS:1001742), which is less specific, so it was overridden.
- Acquisition, dissociation, collision energy, MS2 analyzer and MS1 range were read from the raw method header: data-dependent acquisition (PRIDE:0000627); CID (MS:1000133) in the ion trap (MS:1000264); 35 NCE (the paper also says 35%); MS1 FTMS 300–2000 m/z.
- Cleavage agent and tolerances come from the paper and the PRIDE data-processing protocol: trypsin (MS:1001251), 10 ppm precursor, 0.5 Da fragment.
- Modifications:
  - The Methods list Carbamidomethyl (C, fixed, UNIMOD:4) and Oxidation (M, variable, UNIMOD:35).
  - The deposited PeptideShaker export also contains PSMs with variable Acetyl (Protein N-term, UNIMOD:1), Gln->pyro-Glu (N-term Q, UNIMOD:28), Glu->pyro-Glu (N-term E, UNIMOD:27) and Pyro-carbamidomethyl (N-term C, UNIMOD:26).
  - Those extra modifications come from the X!Tandem refinement pass. The deposited search outranks the Methods, so all six are declared.
- `sdrf-tools search-params` does not read SearchGUI/PeptideShaker output, so the export's modification strings were tallied directly.

## Limitations

- **SRM data not annotated.** `SRM_MeHg.wiff` and its `.wiff.scan` sidecar (QTRAP 5500, MS:1000931) are left out. They hold a single multi-sample wiff for 28 Skyline replicates (K1–K10, 0.5-1…9, 2_1…9 in `SRM_Skyline_MeHg.xlsx`). One file cannot be assigned to 28 label-free sources with `sdrf-tools build`, and the sample order inside the wiff was not verified. A separate SRM SDRF would need the wiff's internal sample list.
- **Sex:** `not available`. The paper says only "mixed gender" and gives no per-fish sex.
- **Strain or breed:** `not available`, because no stock or population is stated.
- **Developmental stage:** `juvenile stage` comes from cohort-level text ("juvenile Atlantic cod", 260–530 g) that applies to all fish. It is not a per-fish measurement.
- **Disease:** `not applicable`. These are healthy fish in a toxicant-exposure study, and no disease state is studied.
- **Reduction and alkylation reagents:** `not available`. Sample prep is cited to Berle et al. 2013 and not restated. The fixed carbamidomethyl implies alkylation took place.
- **Biological replicate numbering** is the fish index taken from the file names within each group (inference from file names). Group membership itself comes from the deposited Progenesis table.
- **Compound and dose for controls:** controls received the vehicle (20% acetone and 80% soybean oil). They are encoded as `compound = not applicable` and `dose = 0 mg/kg`, and the vehicle itself is not encoded.
- **`characteristics[compound]` and `characteristics[dose]`** are columns from the clinical-metadata vocabulary, used here as extra columns. The `clinical-metadata` template was not applied because this is not a clinical study.
- **Undeposited runs:** the PeptideShaker export includes PSMs from six inclusion-list runs (`OAK_EO_Incl_*`, `OAK_HG_Incl_*`, `OAK_HGKontr_Incl`) whose raw files were not deposited. They are not in the SDRF.
- **Instrument header check:** the raw method header was read from `OAK_K1.raw` only. All 28 files are assumed to share that method, as the paper states a single method.
