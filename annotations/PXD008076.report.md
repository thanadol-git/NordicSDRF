# PXD008076 SDRF annotation report
## Scope
- **Accession:** PXD008076
- **Title:** Systematic Analysis of the Cerebrospinal Fluid Proteome of Fibromyalgia patients
- **Organism:** *Homo sapiens*
- **Assay:** label-free shotgun LC-MS/MS of cerebrospinal fluid
- **Instrument:** 7 T hybrid LTQ FT mass spectrometer
- **Data files annotated:** 77 deposited RAW mzML files
## Sample-to-file mapping
The deposited RAW filenames directly identify the clinical group:
- `raw_Control_1.mzML` through `raw_Control_38.mzML`: 38 non-pain controls.
- `raw_Fibromyalgia_1.mzML` through `raw_Fibromyalgia_39.mzML`: 39 fibromyalgia cases.
The associated publication reports that all fibromyalgia participants were female. It gives only group-level age summaries and control sex totals, not participant-level mappings; unsupported age and control-sex fields are therefore recorded as `not available`.
## Methods captured
Each cerebrospinal-fluid sample was depleted of seven abundant proteins, digested with a trypsin/Lys-C mixture, and analysed by nanoLC-MS/MS. PRIDE records LTQ FT, label-free acquisition, and carbamidomethyl, deamidated, and oxidation modifications. The deposited shotgun LC-MS/MS workflow is represented as data-dependent acquisition in the SDRF. Each single-run sample uses fraction identifier `1`.
