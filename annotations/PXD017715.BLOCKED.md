# PXD017715 — blocked

## Exact blocker

The study used TMT-labelled pooled thyroid and plasma samples, but no checked source gives the reporter-channel assignment for any plex. The publication defines five biological groups (untreated control and four iodine-131 activity groups) plus a pooled reference, while the PRIDE protocols mention TMT10-plex and the publication/search parameters describe TMT6-plex. Without an explicit channel table, assigning the five groups and reference to reporter channels—or assuming channel order from activity order—would be inference. The exact RAW-to-plex/fraction membership is also not fully exposed outside the four multi-gigabyte Proteome Discoverer `.msf` databases.

## Evidence checked

- [PRIDE project PXD017715](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD017715): the project describes male Sprague Dawley rats exposed to 0.5, 5, 50, or 500 kBq iodine-131 plus untreated controls; thyroid and plasma collection after nine months; pooling within groups; TMT labelling; basic-reversed-phase fractionation; and Orbitrap Fusion analysis. It provides no reporter-channel table.
- [Publication DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0244098](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244098), PMCID PMC7775064: the full article and its supplementary-table captions were checked. The Methods state that four rats per group were pooled, that an equal-all-samples reference pool was prepared, and that each sample/reference in a set received a unique TMT6-plex or TMT10-plex tag. The identity of each tag is not reported.
- The PRIDE record also cites [DOI 10.1038/s41598-022-06071-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-06071-4), which reuses the accession for an age-related comparison; the accession still contains no deposited channel-design file.
- Complete [PRIDE file inventory](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD017715/files): all 101 RAW files were enumerated. The only non-RAW deposits are four Proteome Discoverer search databases: two plasma sets and two thyroid sets. Their filenames indicate broad set/fraction groupings but not biological reporter-channel identities.
- The four `.msf` files are 2.6–3.6 GB each and were not downloaded. They are search-result databases, not an explicit deposited sample sheet, and inspecting them would not repair the absent publication/deposit channel map.

## What would resolve it

The original TMT sample sheet or Proteome Discoverer study-design export giving, for every thyroid and plasma plex, the exact reporter tag assigned to control, 0.5, 5, 50, and 500 kBq pools and the pooled reference, together with an explicit RAW-to-plex and fraction-number table. It should also resolve whether each set used TMT6-plex or TMT10-plex chemistry.
