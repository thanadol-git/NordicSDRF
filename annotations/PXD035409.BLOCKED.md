# PXD035409 BLOCKED: TMT16plex channel-to-sample map not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Department of Clinical Medicine / Gade Laboratory, University of Bergen (Furriol, Marti groups). Whole kidneys of adult (6-month) gla-/- (mutant) and wild-type AB/TU zebrafish (Fabry disease model); 16 samples (8 per genotype, each a pool of 2 fish; 4 male + 4 female per genotype) in one TMT16plex, 12 fractions (`Jessica_Furriol_ZF_TMT_16plex_F1…F12.raw`), Q Exactive HF (PRIDE) / Orbitrap Exploris per the paper; PD 2.5. Paper: Elsaid HOA et al., J Transl Med 2023, PMID 37670295, PMC10478213 (open access, read).

## Why it cannot be annotated
Which of the 16 TMT channels (126-134N) holds which genotype / sex / replicate is not stated in the PRIDE record or the paper. The deposited Proteome Discoverer export (`Jessica_Furriol_ZF_TMT_16plex_F.xlsx`) labels all channels generically (`Abundance: F1: 126, Sample` … `134N, Sample`; samples S1-S16, no groups). No Zenodo/other repository is cited. Assigning genotype/sex to channels would be invention.

## Verified facts for a future annotation
Danio rerio kidney, 16 pooled samples (8 gla-/-, 8 WT; 4 male/4 female each), SP3 digestion with trypsin, TMT16plex, 12 high-pH fractions; DTT/iodoacetic acid; PD 2.5 Sequest HT/MS Amanda. Possible unblock: author contact (jessica.furriol@uib.no) or the paper's supplementary data.
