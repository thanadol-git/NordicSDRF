## Limitations

- Sample-to-group mapping comes from Supplementary File S2 (MOESM1 sample ID list) cross-matched to EV numbers in RAW file names. EV8 (a control) was not analysed, so Con_8 does not exist.
- Age and sex are `not available` per sample; only group means/counts are published (Table 1). Ancestry, cell type, enrichment process also `not available`.
- Technical replicate = injection order (_2 suffix). Biological replicate is 1 for all sources.
- Mass tolerances not stated and mqpar.xml not deposited (only proteinGroups.txt), so tolerances are `not available`; modifications are taken from the PRIDE protocol text (carbamidomethyl fixed; oxidation M and protein N-term acetyl variable), not from search parameter files.
- Collision energy "35 %" written as `35% NCE` (Thermo normalised collision energy assumed; the paper gives no unit). DDA, CID, ion-trap MS2 inferred from the described method.
- Single protease (trypsin); no Lys-C step is described, so one cleavage-agent column only.
- Seven of ten MCI patients progressed to AD within two years; this is not encoded per sample.
- Technical metadata was not verified from RAW headers (no raw reads performed).
- Independent review (Step 9.5) not run; this is a producer-validated annotation.
