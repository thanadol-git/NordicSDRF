# PXD055190 annotation report

Thylakoid proteome of lettuce (*Lactuca sativa* cv. Hilde White Boston) after 4 h of combined low temperature and high light (LT/HL) and during recovery. University of Turku. Paper: Physiol Plant 2025 (PMC12130749).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `plants`, `dia-acquisition` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 22 rows. The codes are read against the paper's design (Figure 1):
- `Ctrl`, `Ctrl1h`, `Ctrl24h`: 4 h at growth temperature and growth light (GT/GL), then no recovery, 1 h darkness or 24 h darkness.
- `CHL`, `CHL1h`, `CHL24h`: 4 h LT/HL (13 °C, 1500 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹), then the same recoveries.
- `CHL24hGL`: LT/HL followed by 24 h in growth light.

## Inferences to check
- **`CH1B`:** read as replicate 1 of `CHL` (there is no `CHL_1` file).
- **Replicate count:** `CHL24h` has four replicates; all other groups have three.

## Values
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Fusion Lumos with FAIMS, DIA. 395–1005 m/z, variable windows. HCD.
- **Search:** Spectronaut 16.1 directDIA against a curated lettuce FASTA. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl N-term, Acetyl K and Phospho S/T/Y variable.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: every annotated value agrees with the deposit record.
