# PXD045602 — evidence hold: combined trypsin and Glu-C digestion

The [PRIDE accession](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD045602) lists 28 Bruker `.d.zip` RAW archives. The [publication](https://portlandpress.com/biochemj/article/481/5/387/234077/Mesenteric-artery-smooth-muscle-cells-from) states that the proteomics samples were digested with both trypsin and Glu-C to detect alpha-tubulin Lys40 acetylation. It describes timsTOF Pro DDA-PASEF acquisition and 20 ppm precursor and fragment search tolerances.

The active `ms-proteomics` template allows one `comment[cleavage agent details]` value. Recording only trypsin or only Glu-C would omit an actual digestion reagent; repeating the column would violate its single-value cardinality. The RAW archive names also use `RA`–`RE` codes whose condition and replicate meanings should be verified against deposited processing metadata before sample rows are assigned.

No SDRF is approved or committed for this accession. A schema-compatible combined-digest representation and a file-to-sample key are needed.
