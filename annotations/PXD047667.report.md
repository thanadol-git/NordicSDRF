# PXD047667 evidence status

The six RAW files unambiguously encode three GFP-CC1 bait and three GFP-Lti6b control Arabidopsis immunoprecipitation samples. PRIDE and the paper describe the plant lines, GFP-Trap enrichment, on-bead trypsin digestion, and Q Exactive Plus instrument.

**Blocked: acquisition-method evidence is insufficient.** The deposited protocol and paper call the analysis a “shotgun method” without explicitly specifying DDA, DIA, or another acquisition mode. The required `comment[proteomics data acquisition method]` cannot be assigned from “shotgun” alone. A previously drafted DDA assignment was rejected by independent review, so no approved SDRF is claimed.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD047667), [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11606495/).
