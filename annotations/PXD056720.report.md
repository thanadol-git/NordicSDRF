## Limitations

- H1823.raw (NV2_R3) is in Sample_Name.xlsx and mqpar but not deposited, so no row.
- Group labels NV2, V1, V2, EGF come from Sample_Name.xlsx; their meaning is not stated. The paper's MS section names only SNX10-EGFP WT, Y32S and EGFP control in triplicate, so the mapping (e.g. V = variant, EGF = EGFP control) was NOT inferred. The factor value[sample group] column carries the raw labels (WT, NV2, V1, V2, EGF).
- Age 15Y, sex female, disease osteosarcoma and organism part bone come from Cellosaurus, not the deposit. Cell type set to not available.
- Genetic modification (inducible SNX10-EGFP) and GFP-trap enrichment are not encoded as columns, since the sample-to-construct mapping is unknown.
- Precursor tolerance 4.5 ppm from mqpar (paper text says 6 ppm). The paper mentions PTY as a variable modification; mqpar lists only Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term), which were used.
- Instrument from protocol text, not verified from RAW headers. Passage, culture medium (DMEM, 10% FBS in paper), storage temperature not annotated.
