# PXD012524 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Human hippocampal spheroids for modelling AD
- **PRIDE record:** PXD012524
- **Publication:** Pomeshchik *et al.*, *Stem Cell Reports* (2020), DOI [10.1016/j.stemcr.2020.06.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stemcr.2020.06.001), PMID [32589876](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32589876/), PMCID [PMC7363942](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7363942/)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE contains 24 RAW files.  They form four cell-line prefixes (`12F`, `17F`, `36C`, `37N`), each with three biological preparations (`1`–`3`) and two LC-MS/MS technical replicates (`01` and `02`).
- The submitted Proteome Discoverer export explicitly maps all 24 raw files to acquisition file IDs and confirms a Q Exactive HF-X, 150-minute label-free DDA workflow.
- The article describes the four genotypes as an APP p.V717I patient, a PS1 p.R278K patient, and their age- and gender-matched controls.  It identifies `CSC-37N` as a control line, but does not state which raw-file prefixes correspond to APP, PS1, or the two controls.
- Public supplements describe the cohort-level experimental design, but no reviewed source contains a raw-basename-to-cell-line/genotype crosswalk.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Although the filename structure supports replicate and technical-replicate annotation, assigning the `12F`, `17F`, `36C`, and `37N` prefixes to **APP variant, PS1 variant, or matched control** would require guessing.  The source record must support those disease and genotype characteristics per raw file, not merely at study level.
