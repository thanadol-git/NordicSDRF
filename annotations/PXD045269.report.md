# PXD045269 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Bendixen SM, …, Blagoev B, Ravnskjaer K. "Single cell-resolved study of advanced murine MASH reveals a homeostatic pericyte signaling module". J Hepatol 2024;80(3):467-481, doi:10.1016/j.jhep.2023.11.001, PMID 37972658 (SDU Odense). Not in PMC; only the PubMed abstract and the PRIDE protocols were available. The proteomics arm is label-free DIA of whole mouse liver from a diet-induced advanced MASH ("Fibrotic") model vs control ("Normal") livers.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + vertebrates v1.1.0. No `dia-acquisition`: the protocol gives the 56-window scheme only as first/last target m/z, not the full window table.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 19 files (all `projectAccessions` = PXD045269): 17 RAW + 2 Spectronaut exports. All 17 RAW annotated (8 Fibrotic A–H, 9 Normal 1–9), one row each. The spectral-library DDA runs (high-pH fractions of the pool) were not deposited, so they aren't annotated.

## Sample map
File names: `…_Fibrotic_<A-H>.raw` and `…_Normal_<1-9>.raw`. Each file = one animal (the protocol: "the trypsin-digested peptides from all samples were analyzed independently by … DIA"; only proteins present "in at least 3 animals in each group" were kept). biological replicate = animal index within group.

## Key decisions
- Instrument **Q Exactive HF-X** (MS:1002877) from the RAW headers of all 17 files (method `DIA_olsenNEW_120min_56wind.meth`) and the protocol; PRIDE's structured field says Q Exactive HF, overridden.
- DIA (PRIDE:0000450), HCD at 28 NCE, MS1 300–1300 m/z (protocol).
- Lys-C + trypsin (two cleavage-agent columns), DTT (PRIDE:0000608), chloroacetamide (PRIDE:0000600), PAC/HILIC-bead digestion (protocol).
- Modifications: Carbamidomethyl C fixed (chloroacetamide alkylation); Oxidation M and protein N-terminal Acetyl variable, assumed from Spectronaut defaults (not stated).
- Disease: Fibrotic = metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MONDO:0007027; the PRIDE title says NASH, the paper MASH); Normal = normal (PATO:0000461). Organism part liver (UBERON:0002107); material tissue.
- Factor: disease.
