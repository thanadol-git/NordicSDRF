# PXD031280 annotation report

## Study
"Palmyrene Polychromy: Investigations of funerary portraits from Palmyra in the collections of the Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek" (Di Gianvincenzo et al., Heritage 2022, doi:10.3390/HERITAGE5020063). Two paint micro-samples (red, haematite; yellow, lead-based pigment) from the headdress of IN 2795 analysed palaeoproteomically (LysC then trypsin, label free DDA). Each sample was split into two aliquots analysed in 2016 (Q Exactive HF) and 2021 (Orbitrap Exploris 480), each with a blank. The paper full text was not retrievable (not in Europe PMC; MDPI blocked), so PRIDE protocol text and deposited MaxQuant outputs are the evidence. Not in community corpus.
