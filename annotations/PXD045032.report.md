# PXD045032 annotation evidence report

Status: investigation started; no SDRF approved.

The accession has six discovery (DD-MS2) RAWs and six PRM RAWs. Each acquisition type uses mouse IDs 689, 693, 695, 812, 814 and 818; sample 812 has a discovery rerun. The [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD045032) and [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10605996/) identify bone-marrow cells from Kank1 wild-type and knockout mice, but do not explicitly map those six IDs to genotype. The deposited `COR012_Marwa.sky` lists the PRM filenames, and `KANK1_PRM.xlsx` reports KANK1 peak areas by ID; an abundance difference alone is insufficient to assign genotype.

The acquisition types require distinct run-level methods: the discovery runs use Orbitrap Exploris 480 DD-MS2 and sequential Lys-C/trypsin digestion, while the PRM runs use a targeted method and trypsin. A curator-provided ID-to-genotype map, or an accession-specific output that states it explicitly, would allow both subexperiments to be annotated without inference.
