## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- characteristics[biological replicate] = 1 on every row: the factor (non-return rate) is a property of the bull, so each bull is one biological unit. Repeated seminal-plasma samples of a bull are distinguished by source name (<bull>_<sample>), characteristics[individual] (bull) and comment[sample preparation batch] (design 'Batch'); no claim is made about how many independent collections each bull contributed.
- comment[sample preparation batch] = design 'Batch' 1/2/3. The abstract says the bulls were 'sampled across three seasons' and PRIDE says 'different time points', so the three batches are the sampling seasons; each was also acquired separately (Elite_170424 / 180117 / 181205).
- Sv39–Sv42 (bulls 17, 19, 21, 12) are flagged IsTechrep=TRUE in the design without naming a parent sample (the design's TechRep column maps each to itself). They are kept as their own sources. The deposited intensities show they are not repeat injections of another batch-2 sample of the same bull (0.70–0.85 vs 0.95–0.96 for true repeat injections; evidence/sv39_42_correlation_check.json), but what IsTechrep denotes is not documented.
- Breed not stated anywhere reachable (strain or breed = not available).
- MS-GF+ was also used; its tolerances were not read (only the X!Tandem block).
- Paper full text not open access; the per-bull high/low fertility groupings in the design (HighFert, ThreeGroupFert) and age at first collection are not annotated.
- The design lists 21 bulls; the abstract says 20 — not reconciled (bull7 appears only in batch 1).
