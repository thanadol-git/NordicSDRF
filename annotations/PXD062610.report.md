# PXD062610

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

- Crypt compartment proteomics (laser capture microdissected FFPE duodenal biopsies) in celiac disease: untreated (UCeD), treated (TCeD) and non-CeD controls; Q Exactive HF, DDA, label-free, MaxQuant.
- SDRF: `annotations/PXD062610.sdrf.tsv`; rows 109, columns 34; SHA-256 `2de6e88a1b3181bd6d5e23876b65b10bfb3f6751fb6e052bb8c2984020edb3a5`
- Templates: ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + human v1.1.0 + clinical-metadata v1.0.0 (patient cohort with age/sex/individual/treatment status; no cancer, so no oncology; not DIA or cell lines).
- Scope: one SDRF. PXD062684 (mouse crypt, timsTOF) is a separate dataset, not covered.

## Sample mapping
EH number to sample comes from the paper's Supplemental Table 9 (LFQ sheet header labels GROUP_patient), group/age/sex from Supplemental Table 1. 109 files = 60 biopsies (20 UCeD, 20 TCeD, 20 controls) with one or two LCM captures each (captures numbered as biological replicate 1/2 by EH order; the numbering is our inference). Source name = authors' label (CTR/TCD/UCD + patient ID). Factors: disease and treatment status.

## Limitations
- Cleavage agent written as Trypsin (digest, per protocol); MaxQuant search used Trypsin/P (mqpar).
- Cell type not available (crypt epithelium incl. stem and Paneth cells; no single CL term); ancestry not available.
- Supplemental Table 1 rows for patients 1390 and 1438 have Diagnosis column TCeD/UCeD instead of CeD; group taken from Group column and consistent with the LFQ labels.
- Age is age at biopsy for that group. Instrument not verified from RAW headers; fragment tolerance taken from MaxQuant FTMS setting. Reduction/alkylation reagents (DTT, IAA) are stated in the protocol but optional columns were omitted.
- Independent review (Step 9.5) not performed here.
