# PXD005225 annotation report

Plate-method secretome of *Serratia marcescens* BJL200 on chitin (two preparations) or glucose over a time course. NMBU, Ås. Paper: BBA Proteins Proteom 2017, PMID 28130068 (not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 54 rows, one per raw file. The deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` and `proteinGroups.txt` define 27 experiments:
- `A` at 30, 48 and 72 h; `B` at 20, 30 and 48 h; `Glc` at 8, 20 and 30 h; each with biological replicates 1–3.
- Files ending `_2` are re-injections of the same sample (technical replicate 2).

**Interpretation (please check):** `A` = α-chitin and `B` = β-chitin. This is inferred from the same lab's naming in PXD002843 (`a_6d` / `b_4d` = α-/β-chitin); this deposit and its abstract only say "chitin".

## Values
- **Sample:** strain `BJL200`, material `cell` (plate secretome).
- **Factors:** `growth substrate` and `time`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.4.1.2):
  - **No fixed modification** (`Fixed modifications` is empty).
  - Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ and Gln→pyro-Glu variable.
  - FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm.
