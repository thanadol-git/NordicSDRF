# PXD005286 annotation report

Modified multiplexed inhibitor bead (MIB) kinome enrichment, with on-column trypsin elution. Three human cancer lines (A549, NCI-H460, U2OS) were treated with cisplatin or docetaxel. Petrovic/Otterlei, NTNU.
- Methods paper: Anal Biochem 2017, PMID 28167071 (not open access).
- **Data in Brief companion:** "Changes in cellular signaling proteins in extracts from A549, H460, and U2OS cells treated with cisplatin or docetaxel", PMC5362150 (open access). This is the key source.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 32 rows, one per `.raw`. The run names `<rep><line><cond>` were decoded as follows:
- **Line:** `A` = A549, `H` = NCI-H460, `U` = U2OS. This matches the Data in Brief's 3 lines × 3 replicates design.
- **rep:** 1–3 is the biological replicate.
- **cond:** the column order of the Data in Brief table (mmc2.xlsx: "A549 Cis 1–3, A549 Doc 1–3, H460 …, U2OS …", % of control) was matched against ratios computed from the deposited `proteinGroups.txt`. Using MAPK3:
  - `C` = **cisplatin**: 1AC/1AA = 66.13% = "A549 Cis 1"; 2AC/2AA = 176.2%; 3AC/3AA = 52.0%; 1HC/1HA = 100.65% = "H460 Cis 1"; 1UC/1UA = 54.3% = "U2OS Cis 1".
  - `D` = **docetaxel**: 1AD/1AB = 208.2% = "A549 Doc 1"; 2AD/2AB = 88.9%; 3AD/3AA = 18.5%.
  - **`A` and `B` are both untreated controls.** B is the parallel control for docetaxel where it exists (replicates 1–2); otherwise A serves both.
  - **Missing runs:** the 4 runs absent from a full 36 are all `B` runs (rep-3 B for every line, H460 rep-2 B).
- **Doses** (Data in Brief), all for 24 h:
  - Cisplatin 5 µM for A549/U2OS and 0.5 µM for H460.
  - Docetaxel 5 ng/mL.

## Values
- **Organism is corrected:** PRIDE lists *Hordeum vulgare* (barley), which is wrong. The cells and the search FASTA (`uniprot-human-canonical-Mar16`) are human.
- **Cell lines:**
  - A-549 (CVCL_0023): male, 58Y, lung, lung adenocarcinoma.
  - NCI-H460 (CVCL_0459): male, age unspecified, pleural effusion, lung large cell carcinoma.
  - U2OS (CVCL_0042): female, 15Y, tibia, osteosarcoma.
- **Enrichment:** MIB kinase enrichment (free text; no ontology term).
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD at 30 NCE.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.5.3.30 `summary.txt`):
  - No fixed modifications.
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl N-term, Deamidation NQ and Dimethyl KR variable.
  - 20 ppm / FTMS 20 ppm. The Data in Brief gives a 0.02 Da fragment tolerance.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` flags trypsin. That is a false positive: elution is on-column trypsinisation.
