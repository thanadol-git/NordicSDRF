# PXD013182 — annotation blocked

- **Title:** Proteome Integral Solubility Alteration (PISA): a high-throughput proteomics assay for target deconvolution
- **Evidence:** PRIDE deposits 93 RAW files and parameter/peptide outputs for separate PISA experiments. The protocol describes TMT10 labelling, pooled plexes, and fractionation, without a channel-to-sample or run-to-plex map.
- **Why blocked:** Experiment names in the RAW files cannot determine the reporter-channel assignment, treatment, or replicate for each multiplexed run.
- **Sources:** `scratchpad/PXD013182/evidence/pride.json`, `scratchpad/PXD013182/evidence/files.json`.
- **Unblocking requirement:** TMT10 experimental-design files linking channels, samples, treatments, and RAW fractions.
