# PXD010138 — blocked: no patient/brain-region/stage mapping for RAW files

## Sources checked

- PRIDE project record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD010138
- PRIDE file manifest: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD010138/files/all
- Linked PubMed record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31207390/ (DOI: 10.1016/j.nbd.2019.104509)

## Evidence available

PRIDE describes nanoLC-MS/MS profiling of 103 human Alzheimer’s brain samples across entorhinal, parahippocampal, temporal, and frontal cortex, on a Q Exactive. The manifest contains 373 RAW files with donor-like aliases, FASP labels, and repeat/run suffixes.

## Blocking gap

The linked Neurobiology of Disease paper has no public PMC full text or supplementary clinical sample table. PRIDE does not map RAW aliases to patient, brain region, Alzheimer/tau stage, extraction/processing method, fraction, or technical replicate. The filename patterns cannot safely reconstruct those clinical assignments.

## Unblock requirement

Provide the clinical sample sheet and RAW-file run map linking every file to patient, region, disease/tau stage, preparation method, and replicate.
