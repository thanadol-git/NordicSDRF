## Design (paper §4.1–4.4)

18 two-year-old tissue-cultured oil palms, six per group: untreated control (C), wounded by drilling
(W), artificially infested with red palm weevil larvae (I). At weeks 1, 3 and 6 three trees per group
were sampled (leaf, 10th frond); each extract was injected in three technical replicates.

`WK<week>_<group><tree>_<ddmmyy>_<inj>.raw` → sampling time / treatment / biological replicate
(tree 1–3) / technical replicate. 3 weeks × 3 groups × 3 trees × 3 injections = 81 = deposited.
Source names are per week×group×tree, because the paper samples three trees at random at each
time point (the same tree may or may not recur).

Irregularity: `WK6_C1` has `_061119_02`, `_101119_01`, `_101119_02` (no `_03`); the three files are
numbered technical replicates 1–3 in acquisition-date order.
