# PXD032677 — annotation blocked

SARS-CoV-2 (hCoV-19/Norway/Trondheim-S15/2020, MOI 0.1) infection time course in Calu-3 cells, analysed with TMT6plex. Kandasamy lab, NTNU. Paper: iScience 2022, PMC9794516 (open access).

## What is known
- **Design:** 6 time points (0, 3, 6, 12, 24 and 48 h) × 3 biological replicates. Each replicate is one TMT6plex (6 channels = 6 time points).
- **Runs:**
  - Total proteome (`CAL_TP_SCX1–10_R1–3`): SCX fractions.
  - Phosphoproteome (`CAL_P_SCX1–10_R1–3`): Fe-NTA, then SCX.
  - Acetylome (`CAL_AcK_SCX1–3_R1–3`): anti-acetyl-K IP, then SCX.
  - Some extra re-injections (`_t`, `_rep`, `_2`, `R2_`).
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF, from the protocol and the instrument recorded in the `.pdResult` file metadata; HCD at 32 NCE.
- **Search:** Proteome Discoverer 2.4 / SEQUEST HT, 10 ppm / 0.05 Da.
  - Carbamidomethyl C, TMT6plex K and peptide N-term fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ and Phospho STY variable; Acetyl K for the acetylome.

## Why it is blocked
The **TMT channel → time point** assignment is not given anywhere:
- not in the paper (Methods only say the samples were labelled with TMT6plex "following the manufacturer's instructions");
- not in the PRIDE protocol;
- not in the deposited Proteome Discoverer results. The `.pdResult` study definition was range-read; it lists only the `Quan Channel` (126–131) per sample, with no time-point factor.

Assuming 126 = 0 h … 131 = 48 h would be a guess.

## What would unblock it
The authors' channel map (126–131 → 0/3/6/12/24/48 h), and confirmation that it is the same in all three replicates.
