## Limitations

- Main caveat: which condition each group S1-S6 represents is not stated in the deposit (mqpar.xml only gives S1_A...S6_C). Paper describes rhIL1B stimulation and control vs miR-140 electroporation (and non-electroporated cells), but no mapping to S1-S6 was found. `characteristics[treatment]` and `factor value[treatment]` are therefore `not available`; the factor column exists only to satisfy the template. Fixing this needs the supplementary dataset or the submitter.
- Group-to-file mapping comes from mqpar.xml file/experiment order; donor A/B/C assumed to match the suffix (individual = A/B/C; biological replicate 1-3). Validator rejected "donor A" for individual, so bare letters used.
- Age, sex, ancestry not stated. Reduction/alkylation not stated; MaxQuant search had no fixed modification, so only Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term) variable were annotated. Dissociation HCD and 25% NCE from paper (not annotated as collision energy column).
- txt.zip not opened; RAW headers not checked for instrument (Q Exactive from PRIDE and paper).
- Disease "osteoarthritis" is taken from the cells' origin (OA cartilage) for all rows.
