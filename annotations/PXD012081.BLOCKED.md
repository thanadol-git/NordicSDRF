# PXD012081 — BLOCKED

Checked: 2026-10-27

**Blocker:** TMT11-plex dataset with no channel-to-sample map anywhere in the public record. Each plex holds 11 samples, but no source says which reporter channel (126…131C) carries which sample. An SDRF would have to invent 33 channel identities, so none was written.

## Dataset

- Title: *Wnt-modulated hiPSC-derived insulin-producing cells, adult human islet proteome*
- Lab: Helge Ræder, Department of Clinical Science, University of Bergen, Bergen, Norway (submitter Heidrun Vethe, UiB)
- Paper: Vethe H et al., *The Effect of Wnt Pathway Modulators on Human iPSC-Derived Pancreatic Beta Cell Maturation*. Front Endocrinol (Lausanne) 2019;10:293. PMID 31139151, PMC6518024, doi:10.3389/fendo.2019.00293
- Existing annotation (Step 0.5): none. `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets` `datasets/PXD012081` and `sandbox/PXD012081` both return 404.

## What was established (and would go into an SDRF once the map exists)

- **Files:** 72 files: 36 Thermo `.raw` and 36 per-run `.mzIdentML`. Every entry's `projectAccessions` contains PXD012081 (PRIDE v2 `/files` and v3 `/files/all` agree).
- **Plex/fraction layout, from the sample IDs in the RAW headers (HTTP range read of all 36 files):**
  - Plex 1: `a04000`–`a04011` = `HV_01_01`…`HV_01_12` (fractions 1–12; mzIdentML suffix `_HV_wt`)
  - Plex 2: `a04013`–`a04024` = `HV_02_01`…`HV_02_12` (fractions 1–12; suffix `_HV_wt`). `a04012` is not deposited and is not part of the sequence.
  - Plex 3: `a04064`–`a04075` = `HV_03_01b`…`HV_03_12b` (fractions 1–12; suffix `_HV_set3B`)
- **Design, from the paper's Methods and Statistics sections:** three hiPSC lines from healthy donors:
  - Coriell ND41866 (retroviral reprogramming)
  - a Cellartis/Takara Sendai-reprogrammed line
  - an in-house episomal line

  The hiPSC samples per line are S6d7, S7d7, S7d14, and S7 cells treated with WNT3A (200 ng/mL), WNT4 (100 ng/mL), WNT5A (400 ng/mL), WNT5B (80 ng/mL in Supp. Table 1; the Methods say 400 ng/mL), WNT5A+5B (400/80 ng/mL) or the tankyrase inhibitor G007-LK (5 µM). That makes 9 samples. Each TMT11 plex adds 2 aliquots of a homogenised pool of islets from 6 donors (3 female, 3 male, Oslo islet facility), so 9 + 2 = 11 channels. The paper says the plexes are normalised to the islet aliquots. It implies, but never states, that one plex corresponds to one hiPSC line, and it never says which line is in which plex.
- **Instrument:** the RAW headers say **Orbitrap Fusion Lumos** (`MS:1002732`). PRIDE and the paper both say Orbitrap Fusion (`MS:1002416`). Method `150min_MS3_TMT10_10notch_4Z_50tub.meth`, SPS-MS3, CID MS2 in the ion trap, HCD MS3 in the Orbitrap, EASY-nLC 1000.
- **Search settings, from the mzIdentML `SpectrumIdentificationProtocol`:**
  - SEQUEST, Trypsin (`enzyme_info Trypsin 1 1 KR`)
  - precursor tolerance 50 ppm, fragment tolerance 0.9 Da
  - fixed mods: TMT6plex (`UNIMOD:737`) on K and peptide N-term, Carbamidomethyl (`UNIMOD:4`) on C
  - variable mod: Oxidation (`UNIMOD:35`) on M
- **Fractionation:** offline basic-pH reversed-phase HPLC, 96 fractions concatenated to 12.

## Channel-map ladder (`references/channel-map.md`): every rung checked

1. **Paper and supplementary:** no per-channel table. The full text (Europe PMC fullTextXML) only gives the conditions and the "n = 2 islets per plex" design. Figure 1 is a schematic of the conditions, not a TMT layout. The Supplementary Material (Europe PMC `supplementaryFiles` zip, the same set Frontiers links) contains:
   - Table_1.pdf: viability per modulator
   - Table_2.pdf: IPA pathway z-scores
   - Image_1/Image_2 (Supplementary Figures 1–2): protein plots and cell counts

   None of them is a channel map.
2. **Deposited PRIDE files:** only RAW and mzIdentML. The mzIdentML files are identification-only, with no reporter-ion quantities and no sample/channel names. The RAW header sample fields hold only fraction IDs (`HV_0n_xx`) and `Sample355`/`Sample368…379`, with no channel information.
3. **Europe PMC supplementary:** same as rung 1.
4. **External repositories in Data Availability:** the paper cites only PXD012081. There is no Zenodo, figshare, GitHub or OSF link. Europe PMC finds one other article mentioning PXD012081 (PMID 31872528, Acta Physiol 2020, xenotransplanted progenitors). It is a different experiment and does not describe this deposit's channels.
5. **Cross-validation:** not possible, because there is no map to check.

## What would unblock it

Any one of these:

- the authors' TMT11 channel-to-sample sheet for plexes 1–3 (channel → hiPSC line × condition, or islet aliquot)
- the in-house protein/peptide quantification export with channel headers
- a corrigendum or supplementary table giving the layout

With that sheet, the annotation is straightforward: 3 plexes × 12 fractions × 11 channels = 396 rows, using templates `ms-proteomics` + `human` + `cell-lines` (hiPSC-derived cells alongside primary islet tissue), with factor values for treatment / differentiation stage. The cell-line template's fit for the islet rows would also need checking.

No SDRF, report or evidence manifest was written. No validation was run. Scratch files are in `sdrf-skills/scratchpad/PXD012081/`.
