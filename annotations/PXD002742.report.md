## Key caveats
- **Channel map unknown.** The paper was not retrievable (Cloudflare 403, no PMC copy), and PRIDE files, mzTab and XML header give no channel to sample/genotype assignment (PD ratios are vs channel 114). Sources are named "iTRAQ channel N" and `characteristics[biological replicate]` is just the channel index; `factor value[genotype]` is `not available` (the template requires a factor column). The comparison control vs ETHE1-deficient is therefore NOT encoded. Fill from the paper before reuse.
- Channels 119 and 121 are included because the PD method defines all eight; PD ratios shown were 113-118 vs 114, so their occupancy is unconfirmed.
- 'excl' RAW files (excl-1..12) are treated as a second injection (technical replicate 2) of fractions 1-12, inferred from file numbering; file 7_...110217144931 and 8_re are fractions 7 and 8.
- Fragment tolerance 0.5 Da is the Mascot parameter in the deposited XML; the prose says 0.7 Da.
- Dissociation PQD inferred from the PD quantifier ActivationTypeFilter, not confirmed from RAW headers. DDA assumed.
- Disease, sex, developmental stage, strain, cell type are `not available` (per-sample evidence missing; PRIDE "hepatocyte" and "brain disease" fields are submitter dropdowns and were not used).
- RAW headers were not examined; no RAW file was downloaded.
