# PXD021828 BLOCKED: RAW file names carry no sample identity and no sample key is deposited

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
University of Bergen, Department of Clinical Science (lab PI Martha Chekenya). Secretome (conditioned medium) of glioblastoma cells (P3 patient-derived cells and cell lines, with/without bortezomib and temozolomide, ATG5 knockout) in nutrient-deprived medium; label-free, trypsin, MaxQuant 1.6.1.0, Q Exactive HF; 39 RAW files named `ME1`…`ME32` (`ME7` twice, the second time-stamped), `Column_control_1…5` (`Column_control_3` twice). Paper: Rahman MA et al., Front Cell Dev Biol 2022, PMID 36619857, PMC9814514 (open access, read).

## Why it cannot be annotated
Which cell line / genotype / drug treatment each `ME<n>` run corresponds to is not stated in the PRIDE record, the deposited `mqpar.xml` (experiment names are the file names) or `proteinGroups.txt` (columns `Intensity ME<n>`), or in the paper's main text (it says two independent experiments and defers to supplementary methods and Supplementary Figure S3; the supplementary files could not be consulted). `Column_control_*` are presumably blank/column control runs but are not described. Assigning conditions from file order would be invention.

## Verified facts for a future annotation
Homo sapiens, conditioned medium after 1 h serum-free wash and 24 h in unsupplemented medium, ultrafiltration, trypsin 1:50 (DTT/iodoacetamide); Q Exactive HF, MS1 375-1500 m/z at 60,000; MaxQuant: fixed Carbamidomethyl C; variable Oxidation M and protein N-term Acetyl; main-search tolerance 4.5 ppm (first search 20 ppm), MS/MS 0.5 Da. Possible unblock: the supplementary methods/tables or author contact (martha.enger@uib.no).
