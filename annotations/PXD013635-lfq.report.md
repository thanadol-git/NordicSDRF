# PXD013635 (label-free secretome arm)

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

- SDRF: `annotations/PXD013635-lfq.sdrf.tsv`; 8 rows, 28 columns; SHA-256 `3c265d88aaf4b78f6c0b92cd121ffd92952781c810300ad289a2e607de8a8441`
- Dataset: Tendon degradome (Wunderli et al., Matrix Biol 2020, PMID 31917255), DTU. Mouse tail tendon fascicles, 12 d unloaded culture in a low niche (3% O2, 29 C; SecLo) vs high niche (20% O2, 37 C; SecHi), then 48 h secretome collection, LFQ on Q Exactive, 4 biological replicates per condition.
- Templates: ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + vertebrates v1.1.0 (mouse; no cell-line/clinical layer).
- Scope: 8 secretome RAW files (SecLo_1-4, SecHi_1-4), one each. The 2 TMT-TAILS RAWs are in `PXD013635-tmt.BLOCKED.md` (no channel map). The 4 search files (.msf/.pdResult) are not RAW.

## Validation
- parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -t ms-proteomics -t vertebrates: no errors; 1 warning (strain C57BL/6J not found in NCBITaxon list; free-text column, kept).
- tools structure OK; tools check CLEAN.
- tools reconcile: BLOCKER labelled_annotated_as_label_free and MAJOR Lys-C missing. First is expected (record lists TMT for the other arm; these 8 runs are label free per paper/PD Minora LFQ). Second: cleavage column is single-valued, so only Trypsin is given though digest was LysC + trypsin.

## Limitations
- Sample size/design from the paper; per-file mapping (SecLo/SecHi, replicate 1-4) from file names plus Fig 6 legend (n=4). Source names are ours.
- Organism part "tendon" describes the source tissue; the analysed material is conditioned medium.
- Factor value[treatment] is free text describing the niche; not ontology-backed.
- Precursor/fragment tolerance and variable modifications not read (deposited .msf is 8 GB, not downloaded); only fixed Carbamidomethyl (protocol: chloroacetamide) given. Reduction reagent (TCEP) not annotated (accession unverified); alkylation reagent not available as the validated CHEBI term was not confirmed.
- Developmental stage not available (12-13 week old mice; no verified EFO term chosen). Dissociation HCD per protocol; NCE 25% for secretome not encoded.
- Paper read from bioRxiv preprint PDF, not the published version.
