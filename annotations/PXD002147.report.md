## Design and file mapping

Five sample types, each in two biological replicates. Per the PRIDE sample-processing protocol, "one biological replicate of protein extracts was resolved in SDS-PAGE gel and subjected to in-gel tryptic digestion. The other replicate was subjected to in-solution tryptic digestion and fractionated by SCX chromatography." So the SDS-PAGE archive is written as biological replicate 1 and the SCX archive as biological replicate 2 of each sample type.

| Sample type | organism part | growth condition | rep 1 (SDS-PAGE) | rep 2 (SCX) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CDB mycelium | mycelium | Czapek-Dox broth (nutrient-limiting medium) | `CDBSDS.zip` | `CDB_SCX.zip` |
| CDB supernatant | culture fluid | Czapek-Dox broth | `CDBsupernatantSDS.zip` | `CDBsupernatantSCX.zip` |
| PDB mycelium | mycelium | potato dextrose broth (nutrient-rich medium) | `PDBSDS.zip` | `PDBSCX.zip` |
| PDB supernatant | culture fluid | potato dextrose broth | `PDBsupernatantSDS.zip` | `PDBsupernatantSCX.zip` |
| In planta | leaf | in planta (infected wheat leaves, late infection stage) | `InPlantaSDS.zip` | `InPlanta_SCX.zip` |

- Factor values: `factor value[growth condition]` (CDB / PDB / in planta) and `factor value[organism part]` (mycelium / culture fluid / leaf — intracellular vs extracellular subproteome).
- One row per PRIDE RAW entry. PRIDE exposes only the 10 ZIP archives as RAW files; each archive holds ten Thermo `.raw` fraction runs (gel slices or SCX fractions `_1` … `_10`). This follows the repository precedent `PXD001870`. Exact PRIDE RAW coverage: all 10 RAW entries are claimed, each by one row.
- Archive-to-sample mapping was established from (a) the archive names, (b) the zip central directories (HTTP range reads; inner names `CDB1-2_*`, `CDBS1-S2_*`, `PDB_*`, `PDB_supernatant_*`, `In_Planta_*`), (c) the first RAW header of every archive (range read inside the zip): the original acquisition path contains `\SDS\` or `\SCX\` matching the archive in all 10 cases (e.g. `...\SCX\CDB1-2\CDB1-2_1.raw`, `...\SDS\P140590\P140590_1.raw` in `PDBSDS.zip`), and (d) the titles of the 10 deposited PRIDE XML / mzTab search results (`CDB fungal tissue_gel`, `CDB supernatant_SCX`, `In planta_gel`, …), which name the sample type and separation for each search.

## Technical values

- Instrument: Q Exactive (MS:1001911) — PRIDE record, and "Q Exactive - Orbitrap MS" in the RAW header of all 10 archives.
- DDA (PRIDE:0000627; Xcalibur method `IDanalysis_1h_20130404_tq.meth`, Mascot search); HCD (MS:1000422, the only dissociation mode on a Q Exactive); MS2 analyser orbitrap.
- Trypsin (MS:1001251): PRIDE protocol (in-gel and in-solution tryptic digestion) and `enzyme digestion = Trypsin` in the SDS result files; PSM C-termini in the PDB_SDS mzTab head are K/R.
- Fractionation method: SDS PAGE (PRIDE:0000568) on the 5 SDS archives and Strong cation-exchange chromatography (PRIDE:0000561) on the 5 SCX archives.
- Tolerances 20 ppm precursor / 0.6 Da fragment, from the PRIDE XML search parameters of `PDB_SDS` and `In_Planta_SCX` (identical in both).
- Modifications, identical in all 10 deposited mzTab headers: no fixed modifications; variable `MOD:00397` iodoacetamide-derivatized residue → Carbamidomethyl (UNIMOD:4) on C, `MOD:00425` → Oxidation (UNIMOD:35) on M, `MOD:00400` → Deamidated (UNIMOD:7) on N,Q, `MOD:01160` deaminated residue → Gln->pyro-Glu (UNIMOD:28) at peptide N-terminal Q. Target residues were read from 4,900 PSMs in the PDB_SDS mzTab head (all 55 `MOD:01160` sites are position 0 on a peptide starting with Q).

## Limitations
- **Revised after independent review:** organism part `mycelium` (CDB and PDB cell samples) was changed to `cell culture` (BTO:0000214, as in the submitter's mzTab files), because *Z. tritici* grows yeast-like in rich medium and the sources say only 'fungal tissue'; source names now read `_cells_` instead of `_mycelium_`. `characteristics[host organism]` was added: `Triticum` on the two in planta (infected wheat leaf) rows, `not applicable` on culture rows; the sources say only 'wheat', so genus level is used.


- **Paper closed.** Only the abstract and MeSH were readable; Methods details (culture duration, wheat cultivar, inoculation protocol, gel slicing, SCX gradient) could not be checked.
- **Fractions inside archives.** Each row stands for a ZIP of ten fraction runs; `comment[fraction identifier]` is 1 per archive because the archive is the PRIDE file unit. The inner `.raw` names are identical between each sample type's SDS and SCX archives, so naming inner files as rows would create duplicate data-file names.
- **Replicate numbering is from the protocol, not from file tokens.** The PRIDE protocol says SDS-PAGE and SCX used different biological replicates. But the inner names `CDB1-2` / `CDBS1-S2` and the shared facility sample IDs (`P140590` for both PDB-mycelium archives, `P140574` for both PDB-supernatant archives) could also be read as one pooled extract split between the two methods. The protocol statement was followed; this could not be resolved without the paper.
- **Mycelium vs supernatant for PDB** rests on the archive names and mzTab titles (`PDB fungal tissue_*` vs `PDB supernatant_*`). The RAW headers only give facility IDs (P140590, P140574), which do not name the material.
- **Organism part.** Mycelium (BTO:0001436) is written for the "fungal tissue" samples and culture fluid (BTO:0002233) for the culture supernatants; in planta samples are written as leaf (BTO:0000713, as in the PRIDE record). These in planta samples are infected wheat leaves, so they also contain host (wheat) protein. The deposited searches used only a *Z. tritici* database. The host species/cultivar is not recorded in a column: the abstract says only "wheat" (MeSH: *Triticum*), and `characteristics[host organism]` belongs to the `human-gut` template.
- **Strain IPO323** comes from the submitter's PRIDE organism (NEWT 336722, strain CBS 115943 / IPO323) and the species field of all 10 PRIDE XML results. It is consistent with the JGI *Z. tritici* gene index used for the search, but the paper text could not be checked.
- **Sampling time.** "25 day" for in planta rows is from the PRIDE protocol ("25-d-Z. tritici-infected wheat leaves"). Culture duration is `not available`.
- `characteristics[disease]`, `cell type`, `material type`, `age`, `sex` and `developmental stage` were left out (fungal cultures/infected tissue; no source-level evidence).
- Reduction/alkylation reagents are not stated in the protocol and were not written. Iodoacetamide is implied only by the searched `MOD:00397`.
- Tolerances were read from two of the ten result files (PDB_SDS, In_Planta_SCX). The other eight were not range-read for these settings, but their modification sets are identical.
