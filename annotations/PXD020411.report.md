# PXD020411 annotation report

Venom proteomes of the spitting cobras *Naja mossambica* and *Naja nigricincta nigricincta*. University of Namibia / Turku Proteomics Facility. Paper: Toxins 2020 (PMC7472217).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `vertebrates` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 68 rows. The species of every run comes from the "List of samples" sheet in the deposited `Supplementary_identifications.xlsx`.
- **`Zeb_6` vs `NnN`:** despite the names, `Zeb_6` is *N. n. nigricincta* and `NnN_1`/`NnN_2` are *N. mossambica*. This follows the deposited sheet.
- **Silver-stained gel lanes:** `Zeb_6`, `NnN_1` and `NnN_2`, each cut into 16 slices. Slices are fractions 1–16.
- **Concentrated unstained gel lanes:** lanes 1 and 2 are *N. mossambica*; lanes 6 and 7 are *N. n. nigricincta*. Each lane was run three times: initial, then with exclusion lists 1 and 2. These are technical replicates 1–3.
- **Acetonitrile-precipitation supernatants:** samples 3 and 4 are *N. mossambica*; samples 8 and 9 are *N. n. nigricincta*. Each was run digested (trypsin) and undigested (cleavage agent `unspecific cleavage`, because it was searched with no enzyme).

## Values
- **Material:** each species' venom is one milking of one adult snake, so the biological replicate is 1. Sex and age are `not available`.
- **Instrument:** varies per run, as the paper says and the raw headers confirm. In-gel slices were run on Q Exactive HF; unstained gels and supernatants on Q Exactive. HCD.
- **Search:** Proteome Discoverer 2.2 / Mascot 2.6.1, 8 ppm / 0.02 Da. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: one finding, kept as annotated: `cleavage_agent_contradicted` flags the undigested runs, which were searched with no enzyme and so are annotated `unspecific cleavage`.
