# PXD010527 — acquisition method needs direct evidence

The deposited `ExperimentalDesignTemplate.zip` maps all 33 RAW files to WT, F133V mutant and negative-bait pull-down groups with fractions. A 20-run draft for the four complete five-fraction WT and mutant groups is preserved at `scratchpad/PXD010527/draft.sdrf.tsv`; the evidence manifest is alongside it. Official cached-ontology validation, structure and offline checks passed.

Independent review rejected the draft because all rows asserted data-dependent acquisition while neither the cited PRIDE protocol nor the publication explicitly gives the acquisition mode. The draft has no hash-bound approval and is not committed as an SDRF. It needs a run-level RAW method or an explicit deposition/publication statement before another review. The 13 other RAW files also remain to be annotated, including incomplete fraction series and the negative-bait group.
