# PXD004572

Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

PRIDE title: "CSF comparison MS and controls" — global CSF proteome of relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis (RRMS) versus other-neurological-disease (OND) controls. Proteomics Unit at the University of Bergen (PROBE) and KG Jebsen Centre for MS research, Bergen, Norway (lab PI Frode S. Berven; submitter Ann Cathrine Kroksveen). Paper: Kroksveen AC, Guldbrandsen A, Vaudel M et al., "In-depth cerebrospinal fluid quantitative proteome and deglycoproteome analysis: presenting a comprehensive picture of pathways and processes affected by multiple sclerosis", J Proteome Res 2017;16(1), DOI 10.1021/acs.jproteome.6b00659, PMID 27728768. The paper states that PXD004572 holds the global data and PXD004540 the deglycoproteome data. Only the global experiment is annotated here.

## Templates

- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 — DDA LC-MS/MS on a Q Exactive HF.
- `human` v1.1.0 — human CSF donors.
- `clinical-metadata` v1.0.0 — a patient case-control cohort (RRMS vs OND), carrying `characteristics[treatment status]`. The paper states that RRMS patients were untreated at lumbar puncture.

Not used: `dia-acquisition` (DDA per Methods), `cell-lines` (body fluid), `oncology-metadata` (not cancer).

## Design and file mapping

- Donors: 21 untreated RRMS patients (revised McDonald criteria, all OCB+) and 21 OND controls (OCB−, judged by a neurologist to have no risk of MS). All had diagnostic lumbar puncture at Haukeland University Hospital.
- Pools: the donors were split by controlled randomisation into 3 RRMS pools and 3 OND pools of 7 donors each, with 1 male per pool and 200 µg protein per donor. Each pool was MARS Hu-14 depleted, trypsin digested and labelled with TMT6plex. The six labelled pools were combined and fractionated into 100 mixed-mode fractions, giving one LC-MS/MS run per fraction.
- Channel map (source of truth: paper Table 1, row "TMT label global proteome analysis"):

  Table 1 also has a different label row for the deglycoproteome. That row belongs to PXD004540 and was not used.
- Files: PRIDE lists 201 files (fetched over 3 API pages; `/files/count` = 201): 100 RAW, 100 MGF and one PeptideShaker `TMT_MS_OND.mzid`. Every entry was checked to belong to PXD004572. The RAW names carry a fraction number from 1 to 100, each exactly once, in several naming styles: `MM_F1`, `MMF13`, `MM25`, `MM_F15_rerun`, `MM_F51_normgrad_2ul`, `MM_F60_newgrad`. `comment[fraction identifier]` is that number. Technical replicate is 1 throughout. The 100 `InputSpectra` in the deposited mzid are exactly these 100 stems. MGF and mzid files are not data files.
- Rows: 6 channels × 100 fractions = 600. Every RAW file is claimed by all 6 channels.
- Factor: `factor value[disease]` (relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis vs nervous system disorder).

## Technical values

- Instrument: Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523). The RAW header (`MM_F1.raw`, range read) says "Q Exactive HF - Orbitrap MS", and the Methods say Q-Exactive HF. The PRIDE dropdown says Q Exactive; it is superseded.
- Acquisition: DDA (top 12), HCD at 32% NCE, MS2 in the Orbitrap at R = 60,000, MS1 range 375–1500 m/z (Methods).
- Search (from the deposited mzid `AnalysisProtocolCollection`, located by HTTP byte-range binary search in the 7.3 GB file):
  - Enzyme: Trypsin (MS:1001251), 2 missed cleavages.
  - Tolerances: 10 ppm precursor, 10 ppm fragment.
  - Fixed modifications: TMT6plex on K and on the peptide N-terminus (UNIMOD:737), Carbamidomethyl on C (UNIMOD:4).
  - Variable modifications: Oxidation on M (UNIMOD:35), Acetyl on the protein N-terminus (UNIMOD:1), Glu->pyro-Glu (UNIMOD:27), Gln->pyro-Glu (UNIMOD:28), and Ammonia-loss on N-terminal C (UNIMOD:385).
  - The paper's Methods list only the first four modifications and say "all other settings were left to default". The other four come from the deposited search, which outranks the prose.
- Fractionation: `High-performance liquid chromatography` (PRIDE:0000565). The column was a mixed-mode Sielc Promix MP on an Agilent 1260, and PRIDE has no mixed-mode child term.
- Reduction and alkylation: DTT (PRIDE:0000608) and IAA (PRIDE:0000599), inferred (see Limitations).

## Validation

- `sdrf-tools build ... -t ms-proteomics -t human -t clinical-metadata`: wrote 600 rows × 45 columns.
- `sdrf-tools structure`: OK. `sdrf-tools check --offline`: CLEAN (16 terms checked, 0 hallucinated, 0 UNIMOD swaps).
- `sdrf-tools reconcile --record project.json --accession PXD004572`: exit 1 with one finding, `cleavage_agent_unsupported` (the PRIDE record never names Trypsin). This is a false positive: the PRIDE protocol text is a single line. Trypsin is stated in the Methods ("trypsin was used in a 1:40 ratio") and is the `Enzyme` in the deposited mzid. The value is kept.
- `timeout 900 parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -s PXD004572.sdrf.tsv -t ms-proteomics -t human -t clinical-metadata --use_ols_cache_only`:
  - Round 1: exit 1, 2 errors: `Value 'pooled' does not match required pattern` (`characteristics[age]`) and `Invalid value 'pooled' - must be one of the allowed values` (`characteristics[sex]`). The column for each error was identified by substitution with `--skip-ontology`. The spec's TERMS.tsv permits `pooled` in both columns; the installed validator does not.
  - Fix: age → `not available`, sex → `not applicable`, changed in `samples.tsv` and rebuilt.
  - Round 2: exit 0, **0 errors, 0 warnings** ("Everything seems to be fine").

## Limitations

- **Channel map**: taken from the paper's Table 1 (accepted-manuscript text). It is not cross-checked against a deposited table, because the mzid carries no channel-to-sample names and the supplementary tables were not reachable.
- **OND disease term**: the controls are "other neurological diseases", with individual diagnoses only in Table S1 (ACS site, HTTP 403, not read). The generic MONDO `nervous system disorder` (MONDO:0005071) is inferred as the honest pool-level value, not `normal`.
- **Age / sex**: pools are 1 male / 6 female with mean ages of 34.1–38.3 years (Table 1). No per-sample value exists, and `pooled` is rejected by the validator, so age is `not available` and sex is `not applicable`.
- **Biological replicate**: the pool index within each diagnosis group (1–3), assigned by the annotator.
- **Treatment status**: `treatment naive` for RRMS, from the Methods ("None of the RRMS patients received medication prior to the LP"). It is `not available` for OND, where nothing is stated.
- **Ancestry**: `not available` (not reported per donor). Cell type: `not applicable` (pooled CSF proteome).
- **Fraction identifiers**: inferred from the number in each file name (one file per number 1–100, matching "100 fractions"). The `_rerun`, `_newgrad` and `_normgrad_2ul` suffixes are taken to be the single deposited acquisition of that fraction. Per-fraction LC methods (Table S3) were not read and are not annotated.
- **DTT / IAA**: inferred from the reagent list in "Chemical, reagents and solutions" plus the fixed Carbamidomethyl in the deposited search. The digestion step itself is described only "as in ref. 25".
- **Paper version**: the paper was read as the accepted manuscript, not the publisher version.
- **Deglycoproteome**: the companion experiment (PXD004540) is out of scope for this SDRF.
