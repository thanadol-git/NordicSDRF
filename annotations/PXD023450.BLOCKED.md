# PXD023450 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE deposits twelve `SARS_TMT_F1–F12.raw` and twelve `MERS_TMT_F1–F12.raw` files, together with `SARS_TMT.xlsx` and `MERS_TMT.xlsx`.  The workbooks identify the channels and infection/time/replicate labels, but name their plexes `F19` and `F18`, respectively.
- **Why blocked:** No deposited source links workbook plex `F19` to the SARS RAW fractions or `F18` to the MERS fractions.  Treating their similarly named file series as that crosswalk would be an inference rather than a documented RAW-to-plex mapping.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A submitted design manifest that associates `F19` and `F18` with the respective RAW fraction series, or author confirmation of that association.
