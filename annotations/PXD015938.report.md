## Limitations

- Group labels come only from SampleIDlist.xlsx (Healthy CON 6, NEC CON 6, ORA 9, Healthy SYS 7 = 28 pigs). The other 20 raw files (09,10,14,15,17,19,21,27,28,29,30,36,38,39,40,42,43,44,47,48) have treatment and disease as not available. The sheet lists sample 3-14 but no such raw file is deposited; the 47 deposited files do not include 03.
- ORA = enteral antibiotics (ENT) and SYS = parenteral (PAR) is an inference from the paper naming, not stated in the sheet. ORA pigs have no NEC/healthy split, so disease is not available.
- Sex, litter (3 sows), NEC score and age per pig are not in the deposit; sex is not available. Developmental stage "neonate" is inferred (day 5 of life, born preterm at day 106).
- Precursor tolerance and dissociation method are not recorded (not available). Paper says MaxQuant 1.5.2.8, deposited parameters report 1.5.5.1. Only one cleavage agent (trypsin); no Lys-C step.
- Reduction/alkylation: paper says chloroacetamide while MaxQuant fixed mod is Carbamidomethyl (UNIMOD:4), recorded as searched.
- PRIDE metadata lists instrument Q Exactive and disease sepsis; the paper (Q Exactive HF, NEC) was used instead, and sepsis is not asserted.
- Raw files were not downloaded; MaxQuant parameters read via HTTP range from the deposited zip.
