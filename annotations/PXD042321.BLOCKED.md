# PXD042321 — remaining runs with unresolved sample and method mapping

The accession-scoped PRIDE file list contains 102 RAW files. Deposited `SampletoData.csv` identifies 94 of them: 78 specimen runs and 16 blanks. The other eight are 4EDTA series whose filenames encode method and specimen ID but are absent from the deposited map. The publication describes six extraction methods: trypsin for methods 1 and 2, Lys-C for method 3, and mixed Lys-C/Trypsin digests for methods 4–6. The active SDRF template accepts one cleavage-agent value. The acellular blanks also have no truthful biological-replicate value under the active contract. Some specimen taxa are uncertain in the paper's Table 1.

The [six-run reindeer SDRF](PXD042321-reindeer-trypsin.sdrf.tsv) and [ten-run family-level SDRF](PXD042321-mammal-family-trypsin.sdrf.tsv) cover eight identifiable specimens under the AmBic and Acid methods, both explicitly trypsin-digested. Independent review approved both subsets. The other 86 RAW files remain outside the approved SDRFs; full coverage needs resolved specimen identities, a schema-compatible mixed-digest representation and a blank convention. The deposited numeric method prefixes disagree with the paper's narrative numbering, so method names should be used when mapping further subsets.
