# PXD040913 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE describes plasma proteomics for venous-thromboembolism biomarker discovery and deposits 94 DIA RAW files identified only by plate coordinates (for example, `A2`, `E1`, and `H12`), plus three separate CFHR affinity-experiment RAW files. The protocol specifies tryptic digestion and the data-processing protocol distinguishes DIA and affinity/DDA analyses.
- **Why blocked:** No deposited clinical/sample manifest maps the plate-coordinate DIA runs to participant, cohort, VTE status, or replicate. The three affinity runs are a distinct experiment and likewise lack their sample associations. The publication's cohort-level description cannot safely be assigned to individual files.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A run sheet mapping every coordinate-labelled DIA file to its plasma sample and cohort, and mapping the three CFHR affinity files to their respective experimental samples.
