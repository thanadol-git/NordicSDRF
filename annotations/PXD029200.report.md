# PXD029200 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Frandsen HS, …, Rogowska-Wrzesinska A. "Mapping Proteome and Lipidome Changes in Early-Onset Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease Using Hepatic 3D Spheroids". Cells 2022;11(20):3216, doi:10.3390/cells11203216, PMID 36291085, PMC9600727 (Methods read via NCBI efetch; the titles matched). SDU Odense. HepG2/C3A spheroids grown 21 days in rotary bioreactors, then exposed to 65 µM oleic acid + 45 µM palmitic acid (or no fatty acids), sampled in triplicate at days 0, 1, 2, 4 and 7. Label-free DDA on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, MaxQuant 1.6.8.

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + human v1.1.0 + cell-lines v1.1.0.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 58 files: 51 RAW, MaxQuant/Perseus tables, mzTab, FASTA, and two sample sheets (`Raw_file_sample_list.xlsx`, `Pride_explanatory_sample_file.xlsx`). **Annotated: 27 of 51 RAW.**
**Not annotated: 24 RAW** whose sample codes have middle digit 2 or 4 (`121–123, 141–143, 221–223, 241–243, 321–323, 341–343, 421–423, 441–443`; files 3795–3797, 3800, 3801, 3804, 3809, 3810, 3814, 3816, 3820–3822, 3826, 3831–3833, 3837, 3839, 3843, 3849, 3850, 3852, 3854). `Raw_file_sample_list.xlsx` lists these codes, but the submitter's explanatory sheet defines only codes `x1y` (low glucose, low fat) and `x3y` (low glucose, high fat), and the paper describes only ± fatty acids at 5.6 mM glucose. A 2×2 glucose × fat design (2 = high glucose / low fat, 4 = high glucose / high fat) is likely but stated nowhere, so these runs are left out rather than given invented treatments. If the submitter confirms the code, they can be added with the same row template.

## Sample map
From `Pride_explanatory_sample_file.xlsx` (file number → code, day, treatment, triplicate): code `11/12/13` = day 0 (replicates 1–3); otherwise `<timepoint><treatment><replicate>` with timepoint 1–4 = day 1, 2, 4, 7 (protocol: "day 0, 1, 2, 4 and 7"), treatment 1 = low glucose / low fat, 3 = low glucose / high fat (65 µM OA + 45 µM PA). The file number maps to `LUM2_0<number>_LES.raw`. The submitter notes that day-1 spheroids were not in the same fasting state as the other days.

## Key decisions
- Cell line C3A (HepG2/C3A; EFO:0002121, Cellosaurus CVCL_1098, ATCC CRL-10741 per the paper). Disease hepatoblastoma (MONDO:0018666; Cellosaurus classifies HepG2 as hepatoblastoma); donor male, 15Y (Cellosaurus HepG2 donor). Cell type hepatocyte; organism part liver.
- Instrument Orbitrap Fusion Lumos (MS:1002732): RAW headers of first and last files, method `…160min_labelfree…CE32…`; DDA; HCD.
- TCEP (PRIDE:0000609), chloroacetamide (PRIDE:0000600), trypsin (MaxQuant).
- Modifications from the MaxQuant settings in the protocol: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term, Deamidated N/Q variable.
- Factors: treatment and time (days of fatty-acid exposure).
