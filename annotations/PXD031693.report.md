## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- The paper is not open access. The six condition labels are annotated verbatim as 'condition label …' without interpreting the tokens (e.g. which is the affinity matrix and which the eluent, or whether EtOH is a control matrix), because that is not stated in any deposited or open source.
- Mouse background strain, age, sex, pooling, reduction/alkylation reagents and search tolerances are not annotated (not in the deposit).
- Each of the 24 runs is its own source with biological replicate 1; whether the six runs of a genotype come from one retinal extract is not documented.
- Modifications are those observed in the deposited mzIdentML (a lower bound of what was searched); Acetyl is placed on the protein N-terminus because every acetylated peptide starts with Met.
- HCD and 30 % NCE are read from one RAW header and assumed for all 24 runs.
- reconcile flags trypsin as not named in the PRIDE record; the mzIdentML enzyme is Trypsin.
