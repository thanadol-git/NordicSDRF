# PXD004095 annotation report

## Result

SDRF covering the 50 deposited RAW files. The 24 h and 36 h fraction sets use the explicit filename mixture codes: MLAH is light mock plus heavy Ad2-infected material, and MHAL is heavy mock plus light Ad2-infected material. The 11 older `20140312_Sara_SILAC_*` runs are retained with time point and labeling left as `not available`.

## Evidence

- PRIDE / ProteomeXchange record: PXD004095.
- Submitter and PI: Sara Lind, Uppsala University, Sweden.
- Study system: primary human IMR-90 lung fibroblasts, mock or human adenovirus type 2 infection, SILAC with swapped labeling.
- The record states that cells were evaluated at 24 and 36 hours post infection and that two biological replicates used swapped labeling.
- Instrument: Q Exactive; processing: MaxQuant 1.4.1.2 and Andromeda.
- Reference: Zhao et al., “Posttranscriptional Regulation in Adenovirus Infected Cells,” *Journal of Proteome Research* (2017), DOI 10.1021/acs.jproteome.6b00834.
- PRIDE FTP directory: `https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2016/12/PXD004095/`.

## Conservative choices

The generic 20140312 run names do not expose time point, fraction, or label orientation, so those fields are `not available`. No patient age, sex, or individual-level metadata were claimed. The SDRF records the mixed SILAC sample rather than incorrectly assigning each fraction to one infection state.

## Validation

Structural validation: `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf --sdrf_file annotations/PXD004095.sdrf.tsv --template ms-proteomics --template human --skip-ontology`.
