# PXD012573: acquisition method needs direct evidence

The accession-matched PRIDE record gives complete TMT11 channel maps for both the FGF4/MEK-inhibition and analog-sensitive ERK2 experiments. All 24 RAW files form two twelve-fraction sets. The [source table](../scratchpad/PXD012573/samples.tsv) maps 22 biological sources to the channels and would expand to 264 SDRF rows. The [working SDRF](../scratchpad/PXD012573/draft.sdrf.tsv) passed parser and structural validation, and independent review confirmed all file and channel assignments.

The working SDRF records data-dependent acquisition (DDA), but neither the [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD012573) nor the [linked paper's Mass Spectrometry methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618169/) specifies DDA for these runs. The `ms-proteomics` template requires a specific acquisition-method descendant and does not permit `not available`. The independent [review report](../scratchpad/PXD012573/review-fail.json) therefore has verdict **FAIL**. The working SDRF is kept in the accession scratchpad and is **not an approved annotation**.

To complete this accession, inspect an accession-matched RAW method or deposited search/method file for acquisition mode, then rebuild, validate, and obtain a fresh independent review. Deposited search modifications and mass tolerances are also not yet available locally; their working values are `not available`.
