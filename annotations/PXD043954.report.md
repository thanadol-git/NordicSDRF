# PXD043954 — draft annotation (bangkok-1 re-review, 2026-11-25)

Brevibacillus sp. SPR20 wild type vs ARTP mutant M27, label-free proteomics.
Songnaka N et al. Int J Mol Sci 2023;24(15):12016, doi:10.3390/ijms241512016, PMID 37569391, PMC10419081.

## From the paper (section 2.9 and 4.9)
- Design: 3 biological x 2 technical replicates, 12 LC runs (WT control vs M27 treatment).
- Digestion: trypsin (Promega) 1:50, 37 °C, 6 h; 5 mM DTT reduction (90 °C, 15 min); alkylation 25 min RT in the dark (agent not named).
- LC-MS: EASY-nLC1000 + Orbitrap HF (= Q Exactive HF, matches PX record), DDA.
- Search: Proteome Discoverer 2.4, UniProt Brevibacillus db; trypsin; Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M variable; 20 ppm precursor, 0.05 Da fragment; 1% FDR.
