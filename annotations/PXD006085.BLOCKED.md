# PXD006085 annotation blocked

PXD006085 has 80 deposited RAW files: eight gel fractions for each of ten lanes named `S1-2`, `S1-3`, `S1-4`, `S1-5`, `S1-C`, `S2-2`, `S2-3`, `S2-4`, `S2-5`, and `S2-C`. The PRIDE README preserves those files and their search-result mappings, but has no experimental-design table.

The associated study establishes five treatment groups (control, controlled mechanical ventilation for 1, 5, or 10 days, and 10 days plus BGP-15) and two SUMO enrichments (SUMO1 and SUMO2/3). It does not map the deposited lane codes `S1`/`S2` or numeric suffixes `2`–`5` to those biological conditions and enrichments. Inferring that mapping from the filenames would create an unsupported sample-to-data assignment, so no SDRF is emitted.

Evidence checked: PRIDE / ProteomeXchange PXD006085; the deposited FTP README and 80 RAW-file manifest; and Lind *et al.*, *Molecular & Cellular Proteomics* (2017), PMID 28373296 / PMCID PMC5461539. The public article text and its accessible supplemental references did not expose the lane-code map.

Unblock with the original sample sheet, a lane map from the MSF files, or confirmation from the authors that assigns each `S1`/`S2` lane and suffix to a SUMO enrichment and ventilation condition.
