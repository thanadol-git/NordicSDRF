# PXD011044 annotation report

MIB kinome enrichment of 67NR mouse mammary carcinoma cells treated in vitro, and of orthotopic 67NR tumours from treated BALB/cfC3H mice. Treatments were the APIM-peptide ATX-101, the EGFR/HER2/VEGFR inhibitor AEE788, or both. Otterlei lab, NTNU. Paper: Oncotarget 2020, PMC6944453 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `vertebrates` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 35 rows, one per `.raw`.
- **Condition letters:** the PRIDE sample protocol states the key: "A = Untreated for cells/vehicle (PEG300) for tumors, B = APIM-peptide 6 µM for cells/6 mg/kg for tumors, C = AEE788 1 µM for cells/25 mg/kg for tumors, D = APIM-peptide + AEE788".
  - **Discrepancy:** the deposited MaxQuant `Groups.txt` labels C as "Cisplatin", apparently carried over from the sister dataset PXD008724. The paper and PRIDE both describe AEE788, so AEE788 was used.
- **Cells:** `N<rep><cond>` (2017-01-18) are 67NR cells, 3 biological replicates × 4 conditions, treated for 24 h (paper: n = 3).
- **Tumours:** `T<cond><mouse>` are tumours, one per mouse (biological replicates). They were harvested 24 h after the third treatment (paper: 6–7 mice/group).
  - Deposited: A1–6, B1–6, C1–5 and D1–6, run on 2016-08-26 and 2017-01-12.
  - Tumour doses: vehicle 97% PEG300 in DMSO orally, ATX-101 6 mg/kg i.p., AEE788 25 mg/kg orally.
  - `material type` = `tissue`. The tumours are grown from 67NR, so `cell line` = 67NR (CVCL_9723).

## Values
- **Mouse:** female BALB/cfC3H mice (8 weeks at inoculation). 67NR is Cellosaurus CVCL_9723. Organism part `mammary gland`, disease `breast carcinoma`.
- **Enrichment:** MIB kinase enrichment (free text).
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.5.7.0, mouse FASTA):
  - Deamidation NQ, Oxidation M and Acetyl N-term variable; no fixed modifications.
  - 20 ppm / FTMS 20 ppm.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` raises two false positives: trypsin, and "disease with a control arm" (the controls are untreated or vehicle-treated tumours/tumour cells).
