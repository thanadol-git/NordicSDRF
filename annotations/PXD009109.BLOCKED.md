# PXD009109 — annotation blocked

OxICAT redox proteomics of high- vs low-capacity runner rats (HCR/LCR). PROMEC/NTNU; Orbitrap Elite. Paper: Sci Rep 2018, PMC6289973.

## Why it is blocked
The deposited runs cannot be mapped to the samples the paper describes.

- **The paper:** redox proteomics of **plantaris and cardiac (left-ventricle) muscle**, with biological triplicates of pooled samples per group (n = 10, 5, 5).
- **The deposit:** 18 runs named `HCR|LCR × P1|P2|Serum × #3 × _1.._3`. "P1", "P2" and "Serum" appear nowhere in the paper or the PRIDE protocols. The paper never mentions serum, and no file is labelled cardiac or plantaris.
- **Protein evidence:** marker intensities in the deposited MaxQuant `proteinGroups.txt` were checked.
  - **P1** and **P2** are both skeletal-muscle-like: Myh4, Tnni2, Mylpf and Acta1 are high, and Myh6 is absent.
  - **Serum** runs are plasma-dominated: Alb, Hp and Apoa1 are high, and muscle proteins are low.
  - **No run looks cardiac.** So the cardiac samples the paper analyses appear not to be deposited, and the meaning of P1 vs P2 and of `#3` is unknown.
- **Replicates:** whether `_1.._3` are the three biological pools or technical injections cannot be confirmed.

## What would unblock it
A sample sheet from the submitters (Brum lab / PROMEC) explaining P1, P2, Serum and `#3`, and saying whether the cardiac runs exist elsewhere.

## Evidence that is solid (for a future SDRF)
- Instrument Orbitrap Elite (raw header), CID.
- MaxQuant 1.6.0.16 search: variable Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term, Deamidation NQ, ICAT-C and a custom "Copy of ICAT-C" (the heavy label); no fixed modifications; trypsin/P.
- OxICAT labels each sample with light ICAT (reduced Cys) and heavy ICAT (oxidised Cys) in the same run.
