# PXD037675 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE identifies a human neuronal-cell PISA study of simulated traumatic brain injury and deposits 16 fraction RAW files named `Hans_Mat_Frac01.raw` through `Hans_Mat_Frac16.raw`, plus protein and peptide result tables. The protocol specifies TMTpro-16plex processing but does not state a TMT channel allocation, injury condition, or replicate map.
- **Why blocked:** The study title and project description describe several impact conditions, but no public deposit file connects any TMT reporter channel to those conditions or to a source sample. The fraction filenames contain no sample identity. An SDRF would therefore fabricate the source-to-assay relationships.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A deposited TMT multiplex sheet mapping all populated reporter channels to neuronal sample identity, impact condition, and biological replicate.
