## Study
Heo S, Kang T, Bygrave AM, Larsen MR, Huganir RL. "Experience-Induced Remodeling of the Hippocampal Post-synaptic Proteome and Phosphoproteome". Mol Cell Proteomics 2023;22(11):100661, doi:10.1016/j.mcpro.2023.100661, PMID 37806341, PMC10652125 (Experimental Procedures read). MS at SDU Odense (Larsen). C57BL/6 mice (Charles River, delivered at 8 weeks, tested 2 weeks later): naïve, walk-through, shock-only and inhibitory-avoidance (IA) groups; hippocampal PSD preparation; iTRAQ 8plex, 3 biological replicates, proteome and phosphoproteome arms; PD 3.0 / Sequest HT.

## Key decisions
- Instrument per RAW header: QHF* = Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523), FUS* = Orbitrap Fusion (MS:1002416), LUM* = Orbitrap Fusion Lumos (MS:1002732). PRIDE lists Lumos + Q Exactive.
- DDA, HCD. Labels ITRAQ113–ITRAQ121 (corpus convention).
- Modifications from the PRIDE data-processing protocol: fixed Carbamidomethyl C, iTRAQ8plex K + peptide N-term (UNIMOD:730); variable Oxidation M, Phospho STY; 10 ppm / 0.02 Da.
- Organism part hippocampal formation (UBERON:0002421); `comment[subcellular fraction]` = PSD vs the PD factor "Hippocampus" (the non-PSD channel per set). Factor: treatment (behavioural group). biological replicate = SET number (one animal per group per replicate: "PSD fractions were isolated from individual mice").
