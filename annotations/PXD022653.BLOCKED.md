# PXD022653 BLOCKED: TMT channel-to-sample map not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Department of Biomedicine, Aarhus University (lab PI Robert A. Fenton). TMT-labelled proteome of urinary extracellular vesicles (uEV) from 13 hypertensive patients (8 F / 5 M; 10 primary aldosteronism, 3 low-renin essential hypertension) before and after a seated saline suppression test (SSST). Four TMT sets (S1-S4), each 9 channels = 8 samples (4 patients x pre/post) + one universal control channel (paper: "TMT 126"); 8 fractions per set, each run twice (64 RAW). Q Exactive Plus; PD 2.2 Sequest + Mascot. Paper: Wu A et al., Kidney360 2022, PMID 36128481, DOI 10.34067/KID.0000362022, PMC9438418.

## Why it cannot be annotated
A row needs (file x TMT channel) -> patient, pre/post, diagnosis, sex. The PRIDE record and paper text give only the design (four patients pre/post per set, universal control in 126) and cohort totals; they do not say which patient/time point sits in which of channels 127-131 and the remaining channels, nor which patients are in which set, nor per-patient sex/age/diagnosis (cohort sex and diagnosis counts only). That layout is in the paper's Supplemental Appendix 1 (PDF) and/or Appendix 2 (CSV), which could not be retrieved: PMC serves a bot-check page to scripted clients, a WebFetch of the CSV returned only a "Preparing to download" placeholder, and a further attempt to download it directly was blocked. The 4 `.pdResult` files (6.8-7.1 GB each) were not range-read (PD studies of this kind default every channel to a generic sample name).
Inventing the channel identities is forbidden, so no SDRF was written.

## Verified facts (for a future annotation once the map is found)
Homo sapiens, urine, uEV; TMT (9 of 10 channels used per set, universal control TMT126); Q Exactive Plus; precursor 10 ppm, fragment 0.02 Da, 2 missed cleavages; carbamidomethyl C fixed; variable protein N-term acetyl, Met oxidation, TMT on peptide N-term and K; files `SST-Exosomes-S<1-4>-<fraction 1-8>[_<timestamp>].raw`. Possible unblock: open Supplemental Appendix 1/2 of the paper in a browser and place the layout table next to this record, or contact the authors (robert.a.fenton@biomed.au.dk).
