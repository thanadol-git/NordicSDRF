# PXD006122 annotation report

## Scope

All 97 deposited RAW files are represented as 970 channel-aware TMT10 assays.

## Evidence and decisions

- The deposited protocol gives a complete set/channel/disease table for all four TMT10plexes: 8 older adults without dementia, 8 Parkinson’s disease with dementia (PDD), 7 dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB), and 9 Alzheimer’s disease (AD) donors. Channels 126 and 131 in each plex are pooled internal standards made from all 32 samples.
- Every RAW filename identifies its TMT set and HiRIEF fraction, allowing each mapped TMT channel to be retained per fraction.
- PRIDE does not disclose donor identifiers. The biological-replicate values are therefore reproducible within-disease indices derived from the explicit TMT set/channel table; source names retain the exact deposited set and channel identity and do not claim participant identities.
- The protocol explicitly specifies Lys-C followed by Trypsin digestion, TMT10plex and carbamidomethyl fixed modifications, and variable methionine oxidation.
