## Choices and limitations

- Single protease (trypsin), so one cleavage-agent column.
- Instrument: Q Exactive (protocol text) over the PRIDE dropdown "LTQ Orbitrap".
- Cysteine modification recorded as Carboxymethyl (UNIMOD:6) as written in the deposit text; it may be a wording slip for carbamidomethyl, unverifiable (TOPPAS_out.zip not inspected).
- Tolerances are from the main OMSSA search (5 ppm / 0.5 Da); the survey search used 20 ppm / 0.05 Da.
- Acquisition method DDA is inferred. Dissociation not available. Organism "anaerobic digester metagenome" (NCBITaxon:1263854) and medium "anaerobic digester sludge" are closest-match choices; PRIDE lists only Methanoculleus sp. 10.
- Paper full text not open access; time-point suffix = hours and R13 = reactor 13 are inferred from the abstract/protocol and file names. Sampling at 32, 96, 144 h and other reactors are not deposited. Deposit is PARTIAL.
