- Paper: 6 outpatient isolates = 3 ESBL producers (2 blood, 1 sputum; all blaNDM-1) + 3 non-ESBL (urine). Six runs = six isolates: consistent.
- Isolate codes K02/K04/K39/K69/K75/K90 come from the file names. The follow-up WGS paper (Enany et al. PLoS One 2022, e0265884, PMID 35320327) names the four ST627 isolates K04, K69, K75, K90, which matches four of the six file numbers (STATED cross-reference). K02 and K39 are therefore the ST39 (ESBL-1) and ST1634 (non-ESBL-1) isolates, but which is which is NOT stated (K39 = ST39 is tempting but unproven).

It is therefore NOT annotated (no phenotype/ESBL column, specimen source per isolate not annotated). Factor value is the isolate (strain or breed). Unblock: per-isolate key from the authors, or the deposited .xls headers / PRIDE FTP once reachable.
- Instrument: paper TripleTOF 5600+ (MS:1002584) over PX "TripleTOF 5600"; paper is more specific, raw headers unread.
