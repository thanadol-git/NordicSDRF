# PXD051824 annotation report

A549 lung adenocarcinoma cells treated with pemetrexed (PMX), the HDAC inhibitors MS-275 (entinostat) and SAHA (vorinostat), and their sequential combinations, label-free. Slupphaug lab, NTNU. Paper: Sci Rep 2025, PMC11736037 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 55 rows, one per `.d.tar`.
- **Run names:** `<group>_<n>[B|C]` covers six groups (CTR, PMX, MS275, PMXMS275, SAHA, PMXSAHA) × biological replicate n = 1–3 × three injections. The paper says "three biological replicates analysed in triplicate".
- **Injections:** the plain name, `B` and `C` are technical replicates 1, 2 and 3.
- **Extra control run:** CTR_1 has a second plain run (`…_1_1260`, about 10.6 GB, with far fewer MS/MS in `summary.txt`). It gets technical replicate 4.

## Values
- **Cell line:** A-549 (CVCL_0023), male, 58Y, lung, `lung adenocarcinoma`.
- **Treatments** (paper Fig. 2 schedule; the PRIDE text says PMX 60 h, MS275 48 h):
  - PMX 0.5 µM for 60 h.
  - MS-275 5 µM for 48 h.
  - PMX → MS-275: PMX 12 h, then MS-275 added for 48 h.
  - The SAHA groups are deposited but not described in the paper's LFQ section, so the SAHA dose is "not reported".
- **Instrument:** `timsTOF Pro 2`, from `analysis.tdf` (serial 1875087.10477, acquired 2022-03-11). The paper and PRIDE say timsTOF Pro. Acquisition was DDA-PASEF.
- **Search** (deposited MaxQuant 2.0.3.0 `summary.txt`/`parameters.txt`):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidation NQ variable.
  - TOF MS/MS 40 ppm; precursor `not available`.
- **Reagents:** the LFQ lysis reagents are not given, so those columns are left out.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` raises two false positives: "disease on control runs" (the controls are untreated A549) and trypsin.
