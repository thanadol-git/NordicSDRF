# PXD027749 annotation report

## Evidence used

- PRIDE metadata identifies TMT proteomics on the peripheral-blood CD4+CCR6+ compartment, acquired on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos.
- The deposited `CCR6_TMT_11plex_B1B2B3_fractions_HIV_Proteins.xlsx` result workbook supplies all three multiplex-specific channel maps. Its abundance-column headers explicitly list TMT channel, participant identifier, submitted `Pos`/`Neg` condition, and multiplex set.

## Annotation scope

The SDRF represents 24 deposited RAW files (eight fractions for each of B1, B2, and B3) crossed with their 11 deposited channels, giving 264 assay rows. Cohort identifiers **ART**, **EC**, **HC**, and **Norm**, plus the submitted condition labels, are preserved verbatim from the workbook. The annotation intentionally does not expand these labels to clinical interpretations not stated in the channel map.
