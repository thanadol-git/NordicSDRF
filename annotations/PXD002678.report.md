## Limitations

- Sample-to-file mapping comes from deposited Rawfilesamplenames.txt. Several input/control files serve more than one antibody (3229: 1002-1E01 and 1130-3B01; 4230 and 4209: 1114-1G01 and 1130-3A02; 6567 and 6565: 1130-2A02 and 1050-5B05; 3227: 1002-1E01 and 1130-3B01). Each is one row, so the antibody-to-control link is only in the source name/file mapping, not a column.
- Antibody identity is only in source names; no template column carries it. `characteristics[sample type]` was dropped because `study sample`/`negative control` failed PRIDE ontology validation.
- Cleavage: the peptides are a chymotrypsin/pepsin/trypsin/elastase/carboxypeptidase digest; MaxQuant used no enzyme. Recorded as `unspecific cleavage` (MS:1001956); the reconcile blocker reflects that the record never names it as such.
- Organism part, developmental stage, strain, growth condition: not available (grocery-store flour). Disease: not applicable.
- Two LC set-ups (Dionex nLC and EASY-nLC 1000, different scan ranges/exclusion) were used; per-file assignment is not deposited, so no LC or scan-range columns. Instrument model not verified from RAW headers. Collision energy 25 NCE from paper for all files.
- Reduction/alkylation columns omitted (iodoacetamide only used to stop TG2 reaction). Some mqpar files list raw names not deposited; ignored.
- MALDI-TOF experiments mentioned in the paper are not part of this deposit.
- Factor value: enrichment process (pull-down vs control vs input); antibody is not a factor column.
