# PXD012632 — blocked

## Exact blocker

The deposited MaxQuant design maps all 117 RAW files to numeric experiment identifiers, but no checked source maps the 111 colonic experiment identifiers to the publication's 36 active-ulcerative-colitis, 28 remission, and 47 control sample aliases. The supplementary abundance workbook replaces those identifiers with sequential aliases (`UCA_1`–`UCA_36`, `UCR_1`–`UCR_28`, and `C_1`–`C_47`) without a crosswalk. Assigning disease state by numeric-ID order, result-column order, or abundance-pattern matching would be an inferred sample mapping and is not acceptable.

## Evidence checked

- [PRIDE project PXD012632](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD012632): the project describes mucus collected from sigmoid-colon biopsies from 36 patients with active ulcerative colitis, 28 in remission, and 47 controls, plus six ileal samples from people without inflammatory bowel disease. It does not provide per-run clinical assignments.
- [Publication DOI 10.1136/gutjnl-2018-317571](https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2018-317571), PMCID PMC6850676: the full article and online supplementary methods define the cohorts and the label-free LC-MS/MS workflow but do not cross-reference patient/sample aliases to RAW filenames or MaxQuant experiment identifiers.
- Complete [PRIDE file inventory](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD012632/files): all 117 RAW files were enumerated. Their names contain acquisition dates and numeric codes but no diagnosis label.
- Deposited `Results.7z` (5.4 MB) was downloaded and inspected. `Results/Colon/experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` explicitly maps 111 RAW basenames to numeric experiment IDs, and `Results/Ileum/experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` maps six RAW basenames to IDs 736, 737, 738, 739, 740, and 742. Neither file contains clinical group assignments.
- Publication supplementary workbook `gutjnl-2018-317571supp002.xlsx` reports per-sample normalized intensities under the aliases `UCA_1`–`UCA_36`, `UCR_1`–`UCR_28`, and `C_1`–`C_47`, but omits the numeric MaxQuant experiment IDs. Supplementary tables provide cohort summaries rather than the missing crosswalk.

## What would resolve it

The original clinical sample sheet or an explicit crosswalk from each MaxQuant experiment identifier/RAW filename to `UCA_n`, `UCR_n`, or `C_n`, including whether the six ileal samples should be linked to any colon samples from the same individuals. Per-patient age, sex, inflammation, or other clinical columns would additionally require individual-level mappings rather than cohort summaries.
