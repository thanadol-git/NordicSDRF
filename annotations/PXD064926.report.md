## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- Three SDRF files: PXD064926-tmt.sdrf.tsv (2 runs of 3164 sample 01, cell-derived TOP3A), PXD064926-sample02.sdrf.tsv (2 runs of 3164 sample 02) and PXD064926-peptides.sdrf.tsv (21 runs, synthetic peptides); together all 25 RAW files.
- 3164 sample 02 (QEHF_190506_43 and _66) is listed by the submitters under '3164 Labelling N-terminal with TMT' but its search file ('3164_NtermTr_semitrypsin') has no TMT and the paper does not describe it; its sample attributes, reagents and TMT status are 'not available' (only oxidation is annotated), in a separate file because the cell-lines template does not allow an unknown cell line. The paper's Methods (Strep-tag II/3xFLAG pulldown) and Results (BirA*-HA) differ on the construct; the Methods text is used.
- Run QEHF_190506_63 is a re-run of sample 01 after an extra HiPPR detergent clean-up (technical replicate 2).
- The TMT reagent labels a single, non-multiplexed sample (N-terminal derivatisation), so comment[label] is 'label free sample' and TMT is a variable modification.
- Peptide A/B/C = wild-type / KGRQ>AAAA / KGRQF>AAAAA is inferred from the summary sheet ('4860 KGRQ, AAAA and AAAAA peptides') and the paper's PRM of wild-type and KGRQ peptides (PRM files exist for A and B only).
- 4860 runs without 'low'/'high' are read as no-MPP controls; 4754 controls S7/S8 do not say which peptide they contain. MPP amount for 4754 (3.48 or 10.44 µg) is not stated per run.
- Reduction/alkylation for the 4754 peptides is not stated; only oxidation is annotated there. The 4860 heavy-Arg internal standard is given as a variable Label:13C(6)15N(4) modification.
- In the -peptides file, the DDA and PRM runs of the same 4860 reaction share a source name and are technical replicates 1 (DDA) and 2 (PRM).
- Precursor tolerance is 10 ppm for all 4860 runs (deposited search results are named '4860_peptideA/B/C_10ppm…'; peptide C has DDA runs only) and 5 ppm for the 4754 Lumos runs. factor value[peptide] and factor value[treatment] are both given.
