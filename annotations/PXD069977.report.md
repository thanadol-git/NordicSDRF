## Decisions and limitations

- Sample-to-file mapping: Experimental_design.xlsx gives sample_ID F1..F93; mapped to Prot146_N.raw by number. Not stated explicitly in the deposit, but consistent with mzTab ms_run order.
- Two cleavage columns (Trypsin, Lys-C): `tools build` emits only one column because the contract marks the column non-multiple, so the second column was inserted after build with a script; parse_sdrf accepts it. Existing examples (PXD000987) use the same repeated form. Rebuilding from tables alone would lose Lys-C.
- Time points are given as "N hours" in the design file; the zero point (inoculation) is not stated in the deposit. Written as "N hour".
- Strain: PRIDE organism field says NBRC 107763, not corroborated by protocol text, so strain or breed is `not available`; developmental stage `not available`; dissociation method `not available` (not stated). Genotype carries wild type or the deletion name.
- Modifications taken from mzTab header; Acetyl/Met-loss are protein N-terminal in PD defaults, position annotated accordingly (inference).
- Reduction (TCEP) and alkylation (CAA) columns omitted (optional). Paper full text was not read.
