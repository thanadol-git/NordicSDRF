# PXD013113 annotation report

Insoluble (matrix) proteins from 3D cultures of HaCaT keratinocytes and three ras-transformed HaCaT clones, alone or with fibroblasts. University of Turku. Paper: Matrix Biology 2020 (laminin-332 in cutaneous SCC; not open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 27 rows. The 10 blank runs are excluded.
- **Groups:** HaCaT, A5, II-4 and RT3 monocultures; each line co-cultured with the fibroblast line `ANTMAR`; and `ANTMAR` alone.
- **Replicates:** `nr1–3` are annotated as biological replicates (separate cultures).
- **Re-injections:** three files carry a timestamp suffix (`..._nr2_1706...`). These are re-injections of `nr2` and are annotated as technical replicate 2. The deposited MaxQuant summary confirms the groups.

## Inferences to check
- **Disease and phenotype:** HaCaT and fibroblasts are `normal`. The ras clones are annotated `skin squamous cell carcinoma`, with a phenotype column giving the published behaviour: A5 benign, II-4 invasive, RT3 malignant. Calling A5 a carcinoma line is a simplification.
- **ANTMAR:** treated as a primary dermal fibroblast line with no Cellosaurus entry. The deposit does not describe it.

## Values
- **Cell lines:** HaCaT (CVCL_0038), HaCaT A-5 (CVCL_XK16), HaCaT II-4 (CVCL_3868), HaCaT RT3 (CVCL_XK15).
- **Enzyme:** Trypsin and Lys-C (LysC/Trypsin mix).
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, per the raw header and PRIDE. HCD.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.5.2.8. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and hydroxylation of K and P variable (hydroxylation is UNIMOD:35).

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: one finding, a false positive. `cleavage_agent_incomplete`: Lys-C is declared in a second cleavage column, which the tool does not read.
