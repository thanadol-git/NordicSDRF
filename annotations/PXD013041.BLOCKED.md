# PXD013041 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Study of the *Salmonella enterica* strains using the TMT-based proteomics
- **PRIDE record:** PXD013041
- **Publication:** Knöppel *et al.*, *Frontiers in Microbiology* (2020), DOI [10.3389/fmicb.2020.00340](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2020.00340)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits 50 RAW files, and `RAWfilelist22342290.pdf` assigns every one to one of three TMT10 experiments (2234 Set 1, 2234 Set 2, or 2290).
- `SamplesLabelingTemplate22342290.pdf` maps the ten TMT channels to sample IDs for every plex and maps all 2290 sample IDs and 18 of the 20 2234 sample IDs to strain identifiers.
- In both 2234 TMT10 sets, the TMT126 channel is sample `1A`.  The submitted strain table has no strain identity or other sample metadata for `1A` in either set.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Each fractionated TMT run requires a complete channel-to-sample map.  The two unassigned TMT126 channels recur across all 41 RAW files of the 2234 sets.  Assigning a strain, pooled reference, or control identity to `1A` would be fabricated, so a complete accession-level SDRF cannot be supported.
