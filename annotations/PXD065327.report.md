# PXD065327 annotation report

IDH1-mutant vs wild-type glioma models, label-free:
- Isogenic U-87MG (wild type vs heterozygous IDH1 R132H).
- Patient-derived cells: PD-AS (IDH1 R132C astrocytoma, grade 3) and PD-GB (IDH1-wild-type glioblastoma, grade 4).

Slupphaug lab / PROMEC, NTNU. Paper: IJMS 2025, PMC12470816 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0, `human` v1.1.0 and `cell-lines` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 12 rows, one per `.d.zip`. These are the untreated controls of two larger acquisitions; only the controls are deposited and used in the paper.

| Batch | Run names | Group key | Assigned to | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 220202 | `1b–3b`, `28b–30b` | `WTCtrl`, `MUTCtrl` | **U-87MG** WT and IDH1 R132H | NES about 70-fold lower and ABCC2 about 35-fold higher in MUT; the paper reports about 78-fold and about 28-fold for U87MUT vs U87WT. |
| 231223 | `WT1–3`, `mut1–3` | `WTCtr`, `MutCtr` | **PD-GB** and **PD-AS** | PTEN detected only in mut and CD70 about 90-fold lower in mut; the paper reports both for PD-AS vs PD-GB. |

- **Group keys:** each batch's MaxQuant `Groups.txt` (in the deposited zips) gives the group of every run.
- **Batch-to-model mapping:** neither the group files nor PRIDE say which batch is which model. I assigned them from protein markers in each batch's deposited `proteinGroups.txt` (Evidence column), matched against the paper's reported differences.

## Values
- **Cell lines:**
  - U-87MG ATCC (CVCL_0022) and U-87MG ATCC IDH1 p.R132H (CVCL_UE09): male, `glioblastoma`.
  - PD-AS and PD-GB have no Cellosaurus entries. They are `material type` = `cell`, with age and sex `not available`.
- **Genotype** (factor): IDH1 wild type / p.R132H heterozygous / p.R132C.
- **Instrument:** `timsTOF Pro 2` for both batches, from `analysis.tdf` (serial 1875087.10477). The paper and PRIDE say timsTOF Pro.
- **Sample prep:** SDC lysis with TCEP and chloroacetamide, then trypsin.
- **Search** (deposited `summary.txt`/`parameters.txt`):
  - **220202:** MaxQuant 2.0.3.0, TOF MS/MS 40 ppm, Phospho STY also variable.
  - **231223:** MaxQuant 2.4.3.0, TOF MS/MS 25 ppm.
  - Both: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl N-term and Deamidation NQ variable.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile` raises two flags, both expected:
  - "disease on control runs" is a false positive: these are untreated tumour lines.
  - The instrument conflicts with the protocol text; the raw file header wins.
