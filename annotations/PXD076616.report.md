# PXD076616 annotation report

Quattrocolo lab (NTNU, Trondheim). Growth-cone particles were isolated from hippocampus (HP) or dentate gyrus (DG) of wild-type C57BL/6JBomTac mice at P1, P3 and P5, with 5 biological replicates each. Paper: Development 2026 (doi 10.1242/dev.205342, PMC13354955). Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0
- `vertebrates` v1.1.0

## Rows and files
30 rows, one per deposited `.d.zip` (all covered). The mapping comes from the deposited `Rawfile_sample_assignment.txt` (`GC_P{day}_{DG|HP}{rep}`), and `summary.txt` agrees.

## Values
- **Tissue:**
  - HP is annotated as `hippocampal formation` (UBERON:0002421). The paper dissects the whole hippocampus as HP, and DG is a separate dissection.
  - DG is `dentate gyrus of hippocampal formation` (UBERON:0001885).
  - Material type is `organism part`. Cell type is `not applicable`, because the sample is a subcellular growth-cone fraction.
- **Age and sex:** stage is `neonate` and age is 1D/3D/5D. Sex is `not available`: the paper says both sexes were used but not recorded.
- **Instrument:** **timsTOF Pro 2** in both acquisition batches (2024-02 and 2024-11). This is confirmed from `analysis.tdf` and HyStarMetadata (serial 1875087.10477). PRIDE lists the instrument as "proteomics" and the protocol says timsTOF Pro.
- **Sample prep and search:**
  - Reduction was TCEP and alkylation was chloroacetamide (SDC buffer).
  - Modifications come from `summary.txt`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term and Deamidated NQ variable.
  - Fragment tolerance is 25 ppm (TOF, parameters.txt). Dissociation is CID.
- **Factors:** organism part and age.

## Validation
`parse_sdrf` (ms-proteomics and vertebrates, OLS cache) passes. The only warning is the free-text strain. `tools reconcile` reported a "trypsin unsupported" blocker, but that was against an abbreviated record I built by hand. The real PRIDE protocol states trypsin digestion overnight at 37 °C, so it is a false positive.
