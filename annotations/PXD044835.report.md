# PXD044835 annotation report

The PRIDE record describes two distinct Retron-Eco1 MS workflows. The qualitative (QL) digest used trypsin and has four runs in a 2 × 2 WT/E106A by NAD+ exposure design. The quantitative (QN) digest used Asp-N followed by Lys-C and has eight runs, four per WT exposure condition. Separate SDRFs preserve the different cleavage agents and MS1 ranges.
