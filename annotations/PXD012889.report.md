# PXD012889
Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

## Dataset

- Title: *Distinct protein signature of hypertension-induced damage in the renal proteome of the two-kidney, one-clip rat model*.
- Lab: Hans-Peter Marti (PI), Department of Clinical Medicine, University of Bergen, Norway; submitter Kenneth Finne (University of Bergen).
- Paper: Vethe H, Finne K, et al. *J Hypertens* 2015;33(1):126-135, PMID 25304470, DOI 10.1097/HJH.0000000000000370. Not in PMC and the publisher page is behind Cloudflare, so only the abstract and MeSH headings were used. The PRIDE sample- and data-processing protocols are a detailed copy of the paper Methods.
- Design: label-free DDA of renal cortex tissue fluid from 4 two-kidney, one-clip (2K1C) hypertensive male Wistar-Hannover rats and 6 sham-operated controls. Two regions were taken per rat: juxtamedullary (inner) cortex (IC) and outer cortex (OC). For 2K1C rats the non-clipped kidney was analysed (abstract).
- Not already annotated: `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets` returned 404 for `datasets/PXD012889` and `sandbox/PXD012889`.

## Design and file mapping

| Group | Rats (file IDs) | biological replicate | disease | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2K1C | 1, 3, 4, 5 | 1-4 | renovascular hypertension | `2K1C_<n>IC.raw`, `2K1C_<n>OC.raw` |
| Sham | 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 | 1-6 | normal | `SHAM_<n>IC.raw`, `SHAM_<n>OC.raw` |

- Factors: `factor value[disease]` (renovascular hypertension vs normal) and `factor value[organism part]` (juxtamedullary cortex vs outer cortex of kidney). The study compares 2K1C against control within each cortex region.
- Source name is one per physical tissue sample, for example `2K1C rat 1 juxtamedullary cortex`. The two regions of one rat share the rat's biological-replicate number.
- How the mapping was established:
  1. Deposited file names encode the group (`2K1C_` / `SHAM_`), the animal number and the region (`IC` / `OC`).
  2. Original acquisition names were read from the RAW headers (HTTP range read of all 20 files). They carry the same animal number and region with no swaps: `1ICny`, `1OC`, `3IC`, `3OC`, `4IC_130227004736`, `4OC`, `5IC`, `5OC`, `6IC` ... `11IC`, `11OC`, all in `2013-02-22_Orbitrap_nyre`.
  3. The deposited `Progenesis-export_Protein-measurements.txt` header places samples 1IC-5OC under `2K1C` / `treated` and 6IC-11IC under `Control`, with `Inner cortex` / `outer cortex` labels. This confirms the group and region of every file.
- All 20 RAW files are annotated. `SHAM_11OC.raw` is the one control outer-cortex run that the authors excluded from Progenesis quantification for "technical failure of the MS read-out". It is absent from the Progenesis export but still deposited, so it is kept.

## Validation

- `timeout 900 parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -s annotations/PXD012889.sdrf.tsv -t ms-proteomics -t vertebrates --use_ols_cache_only`: exit 0 with no errors and 1 warning. The warning is `Term: Wistar Hannover in column 'characteristics[strain or breed]', is not found in the given ontology list ncbitaxon`. NCBITaxon has no rat-strain terms, and the contract and TERMS.tsv define this column as free text. The value is taken verbatim from the abstract.
- `sdrf-tools structure`: Structure OK.
- `sdrf-tools check --offline`: CLEAN (0 hallucinated, 0 UNIMOD swaps).
- `sdrf-tools score`: 100/100.
- `sdrf-tools reconcile --record project.json --accession PXD012889`: exit 1, with one BLOCKER `disease_on_control_runs` ("every row asserts 'renovascular hypertension'"). This is a **false positive**. The reconciler reads only the first non-empty `characteristics[disease]` value (`record_reconcile.py`, `next(v for v in col(...) if v)`) and assumes it applies to all rows. In the file, all 12 `SHAM_*` rows carry `normal` and only the 8 `2K1C_*` rows carry `renovascular hypertension`; this was checked by printing the column per row.
- Terms checked in live OLS: MS:1003096, UBERON:0005271, UBERON:0002189, MONDO:0006947, PATO:0000461, NCBITaxon:10116, MS:1000133, MS:1000083, PRIDE:0000608, PRIDE:0000599, PRIDE:0000627, MS:1002038, MS:1001251.

## Limitations

- **Paper full text unavailable.** Everything comes from the PRIDE protocols (which reproduce the Methods), the abstract, the MeSH headings, the RAW headers and the Progenesis export.
- **Disease, inferred mapping.** The 2K1C rows carry `renovascular hypertension` (MONDO:0006947). This rests on the 2K1C (Goldblatt) model definition and the MeSH heading `Hypertension, Renovascular`. PRIDE's disease field `Essential hypertension` (DOID:10825) was not used: 2K1C hypertension is renovascular, not essential, and the field cannot be true of the sham rows. The analysed kidney of 2K1C rats is the non-clipped one, and its side (left/right) is not stated in the available evidence, so it is not encoded.
- **Sham disease.** `normal` comes from the file prefix `SHAM_`, the Progenesis `Control` group and the abstract's "normotensive controls".
- **Sex.** `male` for all rows comes from the abstract ("2K1C male Wistar-Hannover rats") and the MeSH heading `Male`. There is no per-animal table. The sham rats' sex is inferred from the study-level description.
- **Strain.** `Wistar Hannover` is free text from the abstract; there is no ontology term.
- **Developmental stage.** `not available`: age and weight are not in the PRIDE record or abstract.
- **Cell type.** `not applicable`: the analysed material is tissue fluid, not a defined cell population.
- **Material.** The protocol extracted *tissue fluid* (interstitial fluid) by centrifuging dissected cortex pieces through nylon mesh, then depleted abundant plasma proteins. `characteristics[organism part]` records the cortex region (the compared variable). No column states "interstitial fluid", because `material type` has no fluid value in its fixed list. A reviewer may prefer an additional representation.
- **Modifications** come from the dataProcessingProtocol prose. No search-parameter file (SearchGUI/PeptideShaker) was deposited; the only search output is a Progenesis protein table.
- **Biological replicate** numbers (1-4 for 2K1C, 1-6 for sham) are assigned in ascending order of the authors' animal IDs.
- **Run names.** `1ICny` (in the RAW header of `2K1C_1IC.raw`) suggests a re-acquisition ("ny" = new). Only one file per sample is deposited, so there is one row each.
- **Excluded run.** `SHAM_11OC.raw` is annotated even though the authors excluded it from quantification; nothing in the SDRF marks the exclusion.
