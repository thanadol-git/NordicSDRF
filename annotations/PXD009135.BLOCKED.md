# PXD009135 — annotation blocked

Plasma proteins adsorbed to alginate microspheres (PROMEC/NTNU; Orbitrap Elite). Paper: Materials Today Bio 2022, PMC9676213.

## Why it is blocked
- **What the design should be:** the paper describes three microsphere types (sulfated SA, high-G HiG, PLL-coated AP) × 5 replicates × E (eluate) and T (on-bead) fractions, plus a saline control and a plasma control. Many samples were re-injected.
- **What is deposited:** 83 runs named only by number (`20160313_AnneMari_01`…`53`), with `_redo`, `-t1/-t2` and `_t1–t3` variants.
- **Missing key:** the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` uses the same numbers as experiment names. No file, table or supplement maps numbers to microsphere type, fraction or replicate.

## What would unblock it
A sample sheet from the submitters mapping 1–53 to microsphere, fraction and replicate, and saying which `_redo` runs were used.
