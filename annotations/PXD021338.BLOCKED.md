# PXD021338 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Proteome of fluid from human ovarian small antral follicles
- **PRIDE record:** PXD021338
- **Publication:** Pla I. et al., Hum Reprod 2021, DOI [10.1093/humrep/deaa335](https://doi.org/10.1093/humrep/deaa335), PMCID [PMC7891813](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7891813/)

## Evidence recovered

- 16 RAW files (Q Exactive HF-X in RAW headers; PRIDE says Q Exactive): 13 individual follicular-fluid runs `FF_M_43…83_DDA` and 3 depleted-pool runs `FF_Dep_small_R3–R5`.
- The paper's HF-X experiment is 13 paired samples from six women, comparing follicles whose oocyte reached metaphase II after IVM (n = 7) with those that stayed immature (n = 6).
- All 11 supplementary tables (Europe PMC zip) and the deposited `Output_MRMquantification_Vimentin_2020.csv` (its Condition and BioReplicate columns are empty) were checked; none links the F43…F83 codes to a woman or a maturation outcome.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The compared variable (oocyte maturation) and donor identity cannot be assigned to any of the 13 runs. The deposited `.msf` is 61 GB and its study-factor table was not read.
