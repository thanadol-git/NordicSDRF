# PXD034106 queen-cell remainder

Fifteen of the seventeen deposited acquisitions have approved SDRFs: eleven historical beeswax samples and blanks in `PXD034106-beeswax.sdrf.tsv`, and four polymer-contamination blanks in `PXD034106-polymer.sdrf.tsv`. The remaining queen-cell extraction and its extraction blank are identified in the accession's deposited `Raw_File_Sample_Key.txt`.

The PRIDE sample protocol says both queen-cell preparations were digested sequentially with Lys-C and Trypsin. The current `sdrf-tools build` contract emits one `comment[cleavage agent details]` value. Encoding only one enzyme would omit an essential preparation step, so these two runs are not represented in an SDRF yet. Their omission does not change the approved 15-run subsets.

Sources: [PRIDE project record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD034106), deposited `scratchpad/PXD034106/Raw_File_Sample_Key.txt`.
