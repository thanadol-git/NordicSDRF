# PXD005340 annotation report

## Result

SDRF covering the 28 deposited RAW files: feline serum samples, four human IGF-I standard runs, and sample runs A–G.

## Evidence

- PRIDE project: PXD005340, targeted analysis of IGF proteins in cat serum.
- Submitter/PI: Margareta Ramström, Uppsala University, Sweden.
- Material: *Felis catus* blood serum with human QPrEST internal standards.
- Assay: targeted PRM; sample/run naming identifies the repeated measurements.
- Instrument: LTQ Orbitrap Velos Pro ETD; Skyline quantification.
- Reference: Sundberg et al., *PLOS ONE* (2016), PMID 27907059, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0167138.
- FTP archive: `https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2017/01/PXD005340/`.

## Conservative choices

Age, sex, and clinical metadata were not reported. Sample numbers are retained as file-encoded biological replicate identifiers; standard runs are assigned replicate `1` and clearly identified by their source names.
