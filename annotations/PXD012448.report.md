# PXD012448 annotation report

*Roseburia intestinalis* L1-82 grown on glucose or acetylated galactoglucomannan (AcGGM): intracellular, cell wall-associated and secreted proteomes. NMBU, Ås. Paper: Nat Commun 2019, PMC6385246 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 66 rows, one per raw file, named `<fraction type>_<Glc|Mannan>_<replicate>_<slice>`.
- **Replicates:** triplicate cultures per substrate; the third token is the biological replicate.
- **Gel slices:** the last token is the gel slice and is used as the fraction identifier. The paper describes five slices for the intracellular lanes; the CWA and secretome lanes have three.
- **Design-file swap:** the deposited MaxQuant `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` swaps the CWA experiment names (`CWA_Glc_*` → `CWAMan*`, `CWA_Mannan_*` → `CWAGlc*`). The intracellular and secretome rows are consistent. The file names are followed.

## Values
- **Sample:** strain `L1-82 (DSM 14610)`.
- **Growth substrate:** `glucose` or `acetylated galactoglucomannan`.
- **Cellular component:** `cytoplasm` (intracellular), `cell wall` (CWA) or `extracellular region` (secretome).
- **Factors:** `growth substrate` and `cellular component`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header; HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.5.3.30 per deposited `parameters.txt`; the paper says 1.4.1.2):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M, Deamidation NQ, Gln→pyro-Glu and Glu→pyro-Glu variable.
  - FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm.
