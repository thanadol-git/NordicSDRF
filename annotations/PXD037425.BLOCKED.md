# PXD037425: mixed-workflow run design required

The [PRIDE record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD037425) contains 23 RAW acquisitions in distinct arms: 16 HeLa ulilysin immunoenrichment method tests with varying ETciD/EThcD/HCD settings, three HeLa SILAC/PRMT5-inhibitor runs, two HCC827 ulilysin runs, one HeLa trypsin run, and one older ulilysin/PTMScan run. The sample protocol describes both trypsin and ulilysin digests; its generic ulilysin search paragraph cannot be broadcast to the trypsin acquisition.

The [paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jproteome.3c00724) and [supplementary data](https://acs.figshare.com/articles/dataset/ETD-Based_Proteomic_Profiling_Improves_Arginine_Methylation_Identification_and_Reveals_Novel_PRMT5_Substrates/25066711) establish the PRMT5-inhibitor comparison and three SILAC runs. Supplementary Table S1.1 contains heavy and light abundances for F4, F5 and F6, but it does not say which isotope label corresponds to inhibitor-treated or control HeLa cells. The RAW names provide `IAP_1`–`IAP_3` but no label-to-treatment key. Mapping by relative methylpeptide abundance would be an inference from the experimental result, not an experimental-design record.

The paper's method-development figures explain the fragmentation comparisons but do not identify the biological source and reinjection relationships for the 16 corresponding RAW names. The two HCC827 acquisitions and older PTMScan run also lack a run-level purpose and sample linkage in the accessible study material. A source/replicate map for these arms and a SILAC heavy/light treatment key are required before defensible companion SDRFs can cover all 23 files. No SDRF is approved.
