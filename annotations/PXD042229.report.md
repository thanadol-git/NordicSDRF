# PXD042229 annotation report

## Scope

The annotation covers the 48 graft RAW files: eight mice, two graft sides, and three technical injections. The 21 post-mortem RAW files are excluded because the public deposit does not crosswalk sample numbers 1–7 to Alzheimer-disease versus control donors. Graft rows distinguish APP p.V717I and control human iPSC-derived hippocampal grafts in mouse hippocampus.
