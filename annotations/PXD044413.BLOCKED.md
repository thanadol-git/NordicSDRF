# PXD044413 — annotation blocked

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits the acquisition data only inside the 19.2 GB `Cytokine_EVLP.rar`; individual Bruker `.d` acquisitions are not listed by the archive API.
- Byte-range reads of the deposited DIA-NN reports recover 18 EVLP run names and 18 transplantation-end run names.
- The EVLP names distinguish six baseline (`LPS`/`Lav`), six cytokine-adsorber (`EVLP_Cyto`), and six untreated-control (`EVLP_Con`) samples.
- The publication states the transplantation endpoint contains six baseline, six untreated, and five treated samples, but its numeric recipient identifiers are not assigned to those arms.
- The deposited `samples_EVLP.xlsx` and `samples_LTx.xlsx` contain only a `sample_index` header and no mapping rows.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The transplantation-end run identifiers cannot be assigned to treated or untreated recipients from primary evidence. Inferring arm membership by pairing odd donor numbers with even recipient numbers would be unsupported. A complete SDRF would also need the authoritative list of acquisitions inside the RAR archive.

## Unblocking requirement

The original sample sheet mapping every archived `.d` acquisition to biological pig, baseline/EVLP/transplant timepoint, and cytokine-adsorber or untreated arm, plus an archive file manifest.
