# PXD011722 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Proteomic And Phosphoproteomic Study Of Placenta And Fetal Liver In Mice With Diet-Induced Obesity
- **PRIDE record:** PXD011722
- **Publication:** Scott *et al.*, *Scientific Reports* (2019), PMID [30670847](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30670847/)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE deposits 88 Thermo RAW files and eight Proteome Discoverer results named for TMT sets 1–8.
- The submitted `2360TMTLabelingSchemeTotalandPhospho.pdf` provides the complete ten-channel scheme for placenta/liver total-protein and phosphoproteome sets, including treatment group, sample IDs, reference channels, and unused 127C channels.
- Deposited RAW filenames are generic `Fusion_160628_<number>.raw` names and no source maps any filename or fraction series to one of the eight TMT sets.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The channel map alone is insufficient: every fractionated RAW run must be linked to its corresponding TMT set before channel rows can be emitted. Assigning runs to sets from acquisition-number ordering would fabricate a set-to-file map.

## Unblocking requirement

A run manifest mapping each deposited RAW filename to its TMT set and fraction, or an equivalent Proteome Discoverer export containing that linkage.
