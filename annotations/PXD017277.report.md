# PXD017277 annotation report

Intracellular proteomes of *Bifidobacterium longum* subsp. *longum* strains 25 and 71 grown on glucose, cellobiose, lacto-N-neotetraose (LNnT) or 2'-fucosyllactose (2'-FL). NMBU, Ås. Paper: iScience 2020, PMC7390879 (open access).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 75 rows: 15 cultures × 5 gel slices.
- **Cultures:** strain 25 on glucose, cellobiose and LNnT; strain 71 on glucose and 2'-FL; triplicate cultures each.
- **File names:** `<strain>_<substrate>_<replicate>_<slice>.raw`. The slice is used as the fraction identifier.
- **Cellobiose code:** `Glc2` is cellobiose. The deposited MaxQuant design maps `B25_Gl2_*` to `B25Cellobiose1–3`; it spells the files `B25_Gl2`/`B25_Gl`, while the deposited names are `B25_Glc2`/`B25_Glc`.

## Values
- **Sample:** strain `subsp. longum 25` or `subsp. longum 71`; whole cells.
- **Factors:** `strain or breed` and `growth substrate`.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, from the raw header. PRIDE says Q Exactive HF. HCD.
- **Search** (MaxQuant 1.6.1.0, deposited `mqpar.xml`):
  - Carbamidomethyl C fixed.
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term, Deamidation NQ and Gln→pyro-Glu variable.
  - Main search 4.5 ppm; FTMS MS/MS 20 ppm.

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: every annotated value agrees with the deposit record.
