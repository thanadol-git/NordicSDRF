## Limitations

- Protease taken from file names; conditions (temperature, enzyme:protein ratio, time) are in source names for the 2024 Legumain/Pepsin/LysC/Trypsin/Krakatoa BSA files only. For the numbered BSA series (Chymotrypsin, Papain, Elastase, Thermo, GluC, ProtK 1-18, ie 3 ratios x 3 times x 2 temperatures) and the numbered Dec-2024 series no run-to-condition map is deposited, so run number is the only identifier.
- Protein identity is inferred: 2024-03..05 files = BSA (protocol); nanobodies and binders from file names; Dec-2024 "Sample" (50 ng), "Jubba" (100 ng; 1,2,3,PA,PB, rerun) and "Extra Krakatoa/Vesuvius" (K15-17, V15-17) files have no stated protein (possibly antibodies/binders) and use organism = not applicable. Organism of nanobodies (phage display) and mAbs (human IgG1 from CHO) not asserted. Nanobody/binder numbers are plate positions; mapping to Nb1-10/miBd1-3 not verified.
- Suffix 1/2/3 on Jubba and Dec-2024 files and 1H2/1H3 Legumain were treated as technical replicates of one source; PA/PB and rerun treated as separate sources. Whether these are technical or biological replicates is not stated.
- No elastase, papain, legumain, Krakatoa or Vesuvius term in PSI-MS: written as unspecific cleavage (MS:1001956); the real enzyme is in factor value[protease]. Glu-C encoded as glutamyl endopeptidase (MS:1001917), proteinase K as peptidase K (MS:1003981); elastase here is likely porcine pancreatic (not stated), so leukocyte elastase was not used.
- Modifications, 25 NCE and HCD are taken from the PRIDE protocol text (PD 2.4 / SequestHT, BSA and antibodies); nanobody/binder files were de novo sequenced (InstaNovo) and their search settings are not stated, so the same list is applied uniformly. Mass tolerances not available; 15 .pdResult files (up to 5 GB) not opened. Dissociation HCD is inferred from the Q Exactive NCE setting. Different LC gradients (16, 35, 70 min) exist by file name; not annotated.
- 15 SEARCH (.pdResult) files are not in the SDRF.
