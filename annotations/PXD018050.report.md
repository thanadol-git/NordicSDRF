# PXD018050 annotation review

Status: partially approved. The four archaeological bone-point RAW files from the Q Exactive HF-X are represented in `PXD018050-hfx.sdrf.tsv`, which passed independent review. The four Q Exactive HF reference runs remain blocked.

The [paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-74258-8) states that the reference specimens P220, P221, X4787 and X4997 were digested with trypsin, elastase and chymotrypsin. The [PRIDE deposit](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD018050) has one LC-MS RAW file per reference specimen and does not map a particular protease to each file. An independent reviewer rejected the draft's assay-level trypsin assignment. The reference draft and review are retained under `scratchpad/PXD018050/` pending a supported digest map.

For the approved HF-X subset, VHM13821 and A37811 are Preboreal elk, and A40894 and A42422 are Boreal red deer. The paper and PRIDE protocol explicitly support these specimen-to-species and instrument mappings. The archaeological period factor is confounded with species in this subset, as noted by the reviewer.
