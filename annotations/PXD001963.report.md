# PXD001963 annotation report

Super-SILAC proteomics of one patient's malignant CD138+ plasma cells at the multiple myeloma (MM) stage and four years later at secondary plasma cell leukaemia (sPCL). Myeloma group / PROMEC, NTNU. Paper: Oncotarget 2017, PMC5386695. Not in the community corpus.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 and `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 48 rows: 24 `.raw` files × 2 SILAC channels.
- **Light channel:** the patient sample. Female; MM cells from bone marrow (2009), sPCL cells from blood (2013).
- **Heavy channel:** the super-SILAC standard (Lys8/Arg10), pooled from six myeloma lines (OH-2, IH-1, KJON, INA-6, LR5, VOLIN) and two lymphoma lines (RAMOS, KARPAS-466). The pool members are recorded in `pooled sample` as `SN=` entries. Heavy rows carry the Label:13C(6)15N(2) K and Label:13C(6)15N(4) R modifications as Fixed, following corpus convention. Because the same standard is measured in every run, its rows are numbered as technical replicates 1–24 in file order, which keeps every row coordinate unique.

The raw headers show the patient and standard were combined in each run, for example `PCL37347+SS`, `SS+2009MM`.

## Sample mapping and uncertainty
- **`20140604/0615_MM_2009_{1,2}_rep1–4`:** MM stage, two CD138+ aliquots × four technical replicates. This matches the paper exactly.
- **`140211_PCL{37347,37348,37435,37436}SS_{1,2}`:** sPCL blood samples, identified by these four sample IDs. The paper describes six blood samples, each with two aliquots × two technical replicates (24 runs); only these 8 runs are deposited. The `_1`/`_2` suffix is annotated as technical replicate, but it may denote aliquots instead.
- **`141225_SS2009MM_run1` and `SS2013PCL_run1`:** 4 timestamped runs each, of the MM-2009 and sPCL-2013 material. The paper does not describe these, so they are their own sources, with technical replicates numbered by acquisition time.

## Values
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Elite (raw header), CID with ion-trap MS/MS.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.5 defaults (Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable) with multiplicity 2 (Arg10, Lys8). ITMS MS/MS 0.5 Da. The paper swaps the FTMS/ITMS wording, and the precursor tolerance is not stated.
- **Disease:** `plasma cell myeloma` (MONDO:0009693) or `plasma cell leukemia` (MONDO:0018689), as the factor.
