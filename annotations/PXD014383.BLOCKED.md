# PXD014383 BLOCKED: TMT10 channel-to-sample map not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
InterPrET Center, Department of Biomedicine, Aarhus University (lab PI Robert A. Fenton). Isolated mouse distal convoluted tubules (parvalbumin-eGFP mice, FACS) and total kidney from mice on low-NaCl or high-K+ diet for 4 days; 6 animals per diet; TMT10plex (3 animals per diet per plex plus a reference mix), two plexes (S1, S2) per origin; Q Exactive; PD Mascot/Sequest. 60 RAW files.

## What is established
The deposited `samples-readme.xlsx` maps every RAW file to experiment (exp1/exp2), origin (total kidney vs DCT), fraction (1-8 or unfractionated) and replicate (injection 1/2). It does not say which TMT channel is which animal or diet.

## Why it cannot be annotated
Channel -> diet/animal is not stated in PRIDE, the readme or file names; no publication is linked. The `AnalysisDefinition` of `Marleen-S1.pdResult` was read by HTTP range: its samples are generic (`Marleen-S1 - [126]` ... `[131]`, no factors); only channel 131 is flagged `Control` (the reference mix, consistent with PRIDE text). Assigning diets to channels 126-130 would be invention.
