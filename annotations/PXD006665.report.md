## Result

7-row, 30-column SDRF (templates `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `vertebrates` v1.1.0). Not previously annotated (no PXD006665 directory in `sdrf-annotated-datasets`).

Dataset: Burmese python (*Python bivittatus*) gastric juice (6 runs) and blood plasma (1 run); Aarhus University (Duan, Wang). TripleTOF 5600 DDA, label-free, trypsin, Mascot search. Paper: Duan et al., GigaScience 2017, PMC5597892.

## Choices

- One row per vendor `.wiff` run; `.wiff.scan` companions and `.mgf` peak lists are not data files. Factor: `factor value[organism part]` (gastric juice vs blood plasma).
- Modifications read from the deposited `Python_plasma.mzid`: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation on M and on P variable (two columns, UNIMOD:35). Trypsin only (single cleavage-agent column). Tolerances 15 ppm / 0.2 Da from Methods.
- DTT (PRIDE:0000608) and iodoacetamide (PRIDE:0000599) from Methods.
- Disease `not applicable`; sex, developmental stage, strain `not available` (not described).

## Caveats

- Dissociation method not recorded in the deposit (`not available`).
- The mouse-database search (`Digestive_fluid_searching_against_mouse_database.mzid`) was not used for sample metadata.
