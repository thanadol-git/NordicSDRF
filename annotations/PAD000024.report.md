# PAD000024 annotation report

## Result

33-row affinity-proteomics SDRF for the deposited Olink NPX table (`MDD_rearranged_NPX.npx.csv`). Templates: `affinity-proteomics` v1.0.0 + `human` v1.1.0. Factor is `factor value[disease]` (`major depressive disorder` vs `normal`).

Not already in `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets`.

## Evidence

- PRIDE: PAD000024, He et al., *Mol Psychiatry* (2026), PMID 41673463, PMCID PMC13112381, DOI 10.1038/s41380-025-03383-5. Submitter Molly Estill, Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai.
- Sample processing (PRIDE + paper Methods): fasting serum from Vacutainer Gold Top silica-gel tubes; Olink Proseek multiplex; 353 proteins on four Target 96 panels (Inflammation, Cardiovascular II, Cardiovascular III, Neurology).
- NPX file: 33 unique `SampleID`s, all four panels, `IPC Normalized`, QC Pass, plate IDs `20210558-003`–`006`.
- Per-sample table: `adjustedmetadata_fordeposit.csv` (108 subjects: 30 AD, 32 HC, 25 MDD, 21 PSO). Only the 33 NPX `SampleID`s were annotated; 32 match `MDD_ID` exactly (25 MDD + 7 HC). NPX leftover `DF8001` was mapped 1:1 to leftover metadata `D6001` (HC, male, White, 34Y).
- PRIDE structured organism part (`blood` / `blood cell`) was not used. Protocol and paper say serum (`blood serum`).

## Conservative choices

- AD and psoriasis metadata rows were not given SDRF rows: they are not in the deposited NPX.
- Ancestry follows deposit `Race` at the HANCESTRO parent (`European ancestry`, `African ancestry`, `Asian ancestry`, `Hispanic or Latin`); `Unknown` → `not available`. `Black` was not narrowed to African American.
- Two MDD patients were on antidepressants per the paper; treatment is not per-sample in the deposit, so no treatment column.
- Mouse CSDS experiments in the paper are not part of this PRIDE deposit.
