# PXD019051 BLOCKED: SILAC light/heavy-to-treatment map not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Institute of Biomedicine, Aarhus University (lab PI Robert A. Fenton). "Acute effects of Salbutamol in Mouse Kidney Distal Convoluted Tubules (mpkDCT) Cells": SILAC (Lys/Arg light vs 13C6 Lys / 13C6 15N4 Arg heavy) mpkDCT cells, 10 uM salbutamol for 5, 15 or 30 min, 5 biological replicates per time point, light + heavy lysates pooled, high-pH fractionation, TiO2 phosphopeptide enrichment plus flow-through, Q Exactive, PD 2.1. Paper: Poulsen SB et al., Kidney Int 2021, PMID 33940111, DOI 10.1016/j.kint.2021.04.021 (closed access).

## Why it cannot be annotated
Each RAW file contains both SILAC channels; a row needs (file x channel) -> treatment. The PRIDE protocol says salbutamol was added to "the experimental group" and "differentially labelled control or treated samples were pooled", but never says whether light or heavy was the salbutamol arm, or whether the assignment was swapped between the 5 replicates or the 3 time points. File names (`mpkDCT_Salphos_<5|15|30>_<rep 1-5>_<fraction>[_<timestamp>].raw`, 15 phospho fractions, and `mpkDCT_SalFT_...` 10 flow-through fractions, 375 RAW) carry time and replicate only.
Ladder checked: PRIDE record and 390-file list (375 RAW, 15 PD `.msf` of 8-10 GB, one per flow-through sample; no readme or sample sheet); Europe PMC (closed, no PMCID); publisher full text (HTTP 403); `.msf` files not range-read (PD SILAC quantification nodes carry ratio definitions, not sample names). Sibling record PXD010032 (same lab, aldosterone) has the same gap. Assigning heavy = salbutamol to every file would be invention.

## Verified facts (for a future annotation once the map is found)
Mus musculus, mpkDCT (Cellosaurus CVCL_R773); Q Exactive; HCD NCE 30%, top 10, 17,500 MS2 resolution; precursor 10 ppm, fragment 0.02 Da; Lys-C pre-digestion then trypsin; carbamidomethyl C fixed; variable acetyl protein N-term, oxidation M, heavy Arg/Lys labels, phospho STY; time points 5, 15, 30 min. The RAW files appear twice for some fractions (plain and timestamped names). Possible unblock: the paper's supplementary methods or author contact (robert.a.fenton@biomed.au.dk).
