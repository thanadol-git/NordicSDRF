## Study
"Proteomic characterisation of lysosomal and mitochondrial perturbations in human iPSC-derived neurons with PARK2 mutation" (Univ. Southern Denmark, Larsen lab). Paper: Bogetofte et al. 2019, Front Cell Neurosci 13:297 (PMC6624735, PMID 31333417). Mitochondria-enriched lysates of isogenic control vs PARK2 KO iPSC-derived neuronal cultures (3 independent differentiations), trypsin, TMT 6-plex, phosphopeptide enrichment (TiO2), HILIC fractionation, Q Exactive HF DDA.

## Templates
`ms-proteomics` + `human` + `clinical-metadata` (all v1.1.0 / v1.0.0 as in the file). `clinical-metadata` was chosen only because it licenses `characteristics[genotype]`, which carries the KO design. `cell-lines` was NOT used: the XCell Science control/KO iPSC lines have no verifiable Cellosaurus identity and the template forbids reserved words for cell line; the material is differentiated neuronal culture.

## Scope (deposited vs annotated)
24 files: 22 RAW + 2 MSF. All 22 RAW annotated x 6 TMT channels = 132 rows. The 2 MSF search outputs are not data files (used as evidence). Phospho set: QHF05025-31 (7). Non-modified set: QHF2_02250-02260 (10, no 02253 deposited) and QHF05032-36 (5).

## Key decisions
- Channel map is from the deposited PD .msf StudyDefinition (identical in both): 126/127/128 = Control, 129/130/131 = PARK2. Labels TMT126..TMT131 (TMT6plex).
- Source names are per peptide pool and replicate (`PARK2_control_1_phosphopeptides`, `..._nonmodified`) because phospho-enriched and flow-through peptides are separate pools and (source, replicate, fraction) must be unique.
- Modifications from the .msf: Carbamidomethyl C, TMT6plex K and N-term fixed; Phospho STY variable only in the phospho search (non-modified rows carry `not applicable` in that column). The paper also mentions N-terminal acetylation, absent from both deposited workflows; not annotated.
- Instrument Q Exactive HF (raw headers + Methods) overrides PRIDE "Q Exactive".
- Fragment tolerance 0.03 Da from the Mascot node; paper/PRIDE text say 0.02 Da (conflict; deposited search used).
- factor value[genotype] = wild type / PARK2 knockout.

Inferred: biological replicate index (channel order within group; the 3 independent differentiations are stated but not mapped to channels); fraction identifier = acquisition order within each set; cell type "neuron" (cultures have "a high proportion" of DA neurons, not pure).
