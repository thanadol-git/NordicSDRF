# PXD017743 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: Molecular profiling of driver events in metastatic uveal melanoma

Publication: Karlsson J et al., Nature Communications 2020 11:1894, PMID 32313009, DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-15606-0

## Evidence collected

- 20 Orbitrap Fusion Lumos/FAIMS RAW files (`Lumos_191014_FAIMS_71.raw`–`90.raw`) and one Proteome Discoverer `.msf` file
- The paper describes three independently grown UM22 BAP1-reintroduced samples and three empty-vector controls, digested with trypsin and labeled with TMT10plex
- PRIDE and the paper provide no reporter-channel assignment; no deposited sample sheet or human-readable quantitative export is present

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

A multiplexed SDRF requires the exact channel-to-source mapping. Assigning the six biological samples to TMT channels from their order in the paper would be an unsupported guess, and the remaining channels cannot be identified as unused or pooled without evidence.

## What would unblock it

The TMT10 design for Proteomics Core Facility project 3278: reporter channel → UM22 BAP1 or empty-vector sample, biological replicate, and any pooled/unused channels.
