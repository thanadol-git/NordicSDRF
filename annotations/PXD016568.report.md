## Design and file-to-sample mapping

Histone PTM profiling of sorted GFP+ Lp30 AML cells, Kdm5c knockdown (shKdm5c-I) vs non-targeting shRNA control, 4 biological replicates each, label-free.

The file-to-sample mapping is not stated in PRIDE or the paper text. It was derived by value-matching: the deposited epiProfile output (`histone_ratios_single_PTMs.xls`, columns `1,mette1` ... `8,mette8`) is numerically identical, row by row, to Supplementary Table 8 (columns `shControl_rep1-4`, `shKdm5c_I_rep1-4`) (checked on H3K4me1/me2/me3, H3K9me3, H3K14ac, all 8 columns identical to 6 decimals). So mette1-4 = shControl rep1-4, mette5-8 = shKdm5c_I rep1-4. This is evidence-based but is an inference through a derived table, not a sample sheet; reviewer should challenge it.

Source names are `shControl_rep1..4` and `shKdm5c_I_rep1..4`; `factor value[treatment]` mirrors `characteristics[treatment]`.
