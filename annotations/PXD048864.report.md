## How the sample mapping was obtained

The deposit has only 7z archives (two raw, two DIA-NN result). The 7z end-header directories were read by HTTP range requests (no archive downloaded) to list the 47 ISF and 49 CSF `.d` run folders. The PRIDE record gives no run-to-sample map, but the authors' OSF project (doi 10.17605/OSF.IO/VWQ58, linked in PRIDE Data Availability) holds `OSF.io upload.zip` (30 MB) with the group/sex/age/sample-ID tables keyed on the leading run number: ISF_DIA_2023.xlsx maps all 47 ISF runs; the CSF B6 vs APP 200d workbook plus the Perseus CSF export map 39 of 49 CSF runs. Overlaps between these tables agree (0 conflicts).

## Design and caveats

- The paper describes only B6 (N=6) vs APPtg (N=6) per fluid; the deposit also contains 50-day and 200-day animals and hA7ko / APP-hA7ko lines, i.e. a larger batch than the paper's 12 per fluid. All deposited runs are annotated.
- 10 CSF runs (47801, 47805, 47847, 47852, 47955, 47969, 47976, 50337, 50514, 50779) have no deposited mapping: genotype, age, sex, disease and strain are `not available`.
- `characteristics[genotype]` and `characteristics[age]` are not columns of the selected templates; they are added as extra columns (validator accepts) and mirrored as `factor value[genotype]` and `factor value[age]`. Genotype labels are the authors' (`APPswe wt/+`, `h-A7flx 0/0`); the meaning of hA7ko (human ABCA7 allele) is not defined in the deposit, so its disease and strain are `not available`. APP-hA7ko strain also `not available`.
- Paper says APPtg mice were male; deposit tables show both sexes. The tables were followed.
- Sample IDs (animal numbers) were used for source names (ISF_/CSF_ prefix); `biological replicate` is a running count within fluid/genotype/age group (our numbering). The paper says ISF and CSF came from separate animals, yet two IDs (47781, 47990) occur in both fluids with identical genotype/age/sex; kept as separate sources.
- Developmental stage `not available` (no ontology term asserted for 50 d / 200 d). Age written as 50D / 200D from the author tables.
- Modification: only Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed, from protocol text; the DIA-NN result archives were not unpacked. Mass tolerance 15 ppm applied to both precursor and fragment (protocol states one "mass accuracy"; assumption). Dissociation CID (MS:1000133) assumed for timsTOF PASEF, as in other timsTOF annotations here; not stated in the deposit. Reduction/alkylation reagents not named; omitted. Collision energy ramp (20-59 eV) not encoded.
- DIA window: protocol states 25 Da, MS/MS 475-1000, IM 0.85-1.27 (MS 0.85-1.30); recorded as written. True diaPASEF windows may be variable-width (not verified from method files).
- `comment[data file]` is the `.d` folder name; the files exist only inside the two 7z archives.
- Cell type `not applicable` (biofluids); the PRIDE record has no disease or organism part fields to reconcile.
