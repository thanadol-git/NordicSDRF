## Caveats

- Evidence: MaxQuant parameters.txt and summary.txt were read by HTTP range reads of the zip members; RAW header (AT06 only, first 60 kB) used to confirm Q Exactive HF (PRIDE field says Q Exactive; overridden).
- Biological replicate numbering 1-6 is ours: the paper says the six samples came from three horses, but no deposited file-to-horse mapping exists, so no donor/individual column is given.
- Developmental stage "adult" is inferred from donor age 13-17 years; sex and breed from the paper.
- Disease is `not available` (donors euthanized for unrelated reasons; no health statement).
- Precursor tolerance, dissociation method and collision energy are `not available` (not in parameters.txt or the methods text). Dissociation is likely HCD but is not stated.
- No `enrichment process` column: "cell surface biotinylation" is not an EFO/PRIDE term (validator warned); the enrichment is described only here.
- Gradient length differs between the paper (35 min) and the RAW method name (30min); not captured in the SDRF.
- Single cleavage agent (Trypsin); MaxQuant used Trypsin/P, recorded as Trypsin.
- Factor value is organism part (BM vs AT).
