## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- Per-patient age not available (Table S1 in the ACS PDF supplement not parsed).
- ER/PgR status and IntClust from the deposited Sample.information.xlsx are carried in characteristics[clinical data] (V20, V21: PgR not available).
- Sex is 'not available': the Patients section's 'collected from women' refers to set 1 (21 specimens); for set 2 (these 24 tumours, from a 109-sample study) neither the paper nor the deposited sheet states sex.
- HCD written as MS:1000422 (beam-type collision-induced dissociation), the spec-canonical term.
- dia-acquisition template not declared: its validator requires the bare literal 'Data-independent acquisition' instead of NT=/AC= form; the four recommended DIA-window columns are therefore omitted.
- Modifications are from the paper's library-generation description, not from a parsed search file.
- sdrf-tools reconcile flags Trypsin as absent from the PRIDE record; the paper's Methods state trypsin digestion for sample sets 1 and 2, so the flag is a false positive.
- Supplementary Table S2 (xlsx, clinical characteristics) was not retrieved; it may hold per-patient age for set 2.
