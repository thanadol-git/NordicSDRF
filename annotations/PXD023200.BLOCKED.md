# PXD023200 — annotation blocked

Denitrification proteome of *Bradyrhizobium* sp. HAMBI 2125 during the oxic→anoxic transition. NMBU, Ås. Paper: Environ Microbiol 2021, Yuan et al. (not open access).

## What is known
- **Design** (PRIDE protocol): samples from the aerobic inoculum and at 22, 27, 32 and 36 h post-inoculation, three flasks each. That is 15 samples.
- **Runs:** the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` assigns `190614_Yuen_<n>_<1|2>` to experiment `<n>` (1–15), with `_1`/`_2` as fractions.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.6.2.6. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl protein N-term variable.

## Why it is blocked
Nothing maps sample numbers 1–15 to time point and flask. The numbering could be by time point or by flask, and the closed paper could not be checked.

## What would unblock it
The map from sample 1–15 to time point (inoculum/22/27/32/36 h) and flask.
