## Output

- Skin biopsies: Control1-5 (normal) and AD1-4 (atopic dermatitis, patient in active treatment per PRIDE abstract). DDA (20230603_Skin_*_DDA, 18 files) and PRM (20230512_Skin_*_PRM45min, 18 files), each with TR1/TR2 injections. Skin rows carry Met->Hse and Met->Hsl C-terminal variable mods (from the msf); other rows `not applicable` for those two.
- Factor: factor value[disease] (skin only; `not available` for fibroblast/control rows).

## Caveats
1. Fibroblast conditions (control vs in vitro AD / asthma model; likely R1-3 vs R4-6 from msf grouping) are not stated in the deposit; disease, treatment and factor are `not available`. Whether fibroblasts are primary or a cell line is unknown.
2. Skin "Patient1-4" (DDA) is assumed to be the same four patients as "AD1-4" (PRM); the same assumption is made for Control1-5.
3. Age, sex, ancestry not in the record: `not available`. Biological replicate numbers are within-group indices.
4. Search settings (10 ppm / 0.6 Da, Carbamidomethyl fixed, Oxidation, protein N-term Acetyl, Met->Hse/Hsl) are read from the PRM msf files; no msf exists for the DDA runs, so they are assumed identical. PRIDE reports no PTMs.
5. Dissociation method, reduction/alkylation reagents not stated: dissociation `not available`. Instrument Orbitrap Eclipse confirmed by msf WorkflowInputFiles.
6. Positive-control rows: organism Homo sapiens taken from the deposit; sample is a recombinant/synthetic standard so organ, cell type, disease are `not applicable`. msf also references 20230521_FL_PN_1umol_PRM.raw, which is not deposited.
7. Acquisition method: PRM = PRIDE:0000629; DDA = PRIDE:0000627. Label-free assumed (no labelling in the protocol).
