# PXD060125 SDRF annotation report

## Study
ANGPTL3/8 is an atypical unfoldase that regulates intravascular lipolysis by catalyzing unfolding of lipoprotein lipase, PNAS 2025, doi:10.1073/pnas.2420721122, PMID 40112106 (SDU Odense, Jørgensen lab). Hydrogen/deuterium-exchange MS (HDX-MS) on purified recombinant proteins: human ANGPTL3, ANGPTL3/8, GPIHBP1 and bovine LPL, alone and in complexes; continuous labelling time courses, temperature-pulse experiments and sub-stoichiometric unfoldase kinetics. Waters Synapt G2 HDMS (MSE), DynamX; peptide IDs from separate Orbitrap Eclipse DDA runs (MGF/CSV only deposited).

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 only (no organism template fits purified recombinant protein mixtures).

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 397 files: 383 RAW (`.raw.zip`, Waters), 7 DynamX state-data xlsx, peptide CSVs/MGFs and `Rawfile_Overview.xlsx`. All 383 annotated, one row per run. The list was regenerated from the naming scheme and matched PRIDE by SHA-256 (one missing run, `Exp.A_ANGPTL3-8_bLPL_50s_Rep_1`, and the `Controls_Rep_3` spelling are reproduced).

## Sample map
From file names (confirmed by `Rawfile_Overview.xlsx`, which lists each RAW with its experiment): `Exp.<A–G>_<state>_<time|Control|90H>_Rep_<n>`. Experiments: A imprint of LPL on ANGPTL3/8; B LPL thermal unfolding (180 s at 20/30/40 °C, then 10 s pulse label); C imprint of ANGPTL3/8 on LPL·GPIHBP1; D imprint of LPL on ANGPTL3; E imprint of ANGPTL3 on LPL·GPIHBP1; F/G sub-stoichiometric ANGPTL3 or ANGPTL3/8 on LPL (± GPIHBP1) at 25/37 °C, pre-incubation time then 10 s pulse label. `Control` = undeuterated reference; `90H` = maximally deuterated reference. Rep = technical (labelling) replicate; one source per experiment × protein state × labelling condition (each HDX reaction is a separate aliquot).

## Key decisions
- `characteristics[organism]` = organism of the protein whose deuterium uptake is reported: Homo sapiens for Exp A/D (ANGPTL3/8, ANGPTL3), Bos taurus for B/C/E/F/G (LPL). Every protein in the mixture is listed in `characteristics[compound]` with its species.
- Acquisition DIA-type MSE (PRIDE:0000450), CID (MS:1000133); instrument Synapt G2 HDMS (MS:1001777, as PRIDE). Online pepsin digestion (PepsinA MS:1001311); TCEP in the quench.
- `time` = continuous labelling time (A, C, D, E), pre-incubation time before the 10 s pulse (F, G), or 180 s (B); `not applicable` for undeuterated and maximally deuterated controls. `temperature` = incubation temperature. Factors: compound (protein state) and time.
