# PXD037734 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** A seven-transmembrane methyltransferase catalysing N-terminal histidine methylation of lytic polysaccharide monooxygenases
- **PRIDE record:** PXD037734
- **Publication:** Nat Commun 2023, doi 10.1038/s41467-023-39875-7

## Evidence recovered

All runs are inside 9 figure-level ZIP archives (TMT-11 Figure 1, fractionated in-depth, LFQ single-shot, PRM sets for Figures 2/4/5/S1/S10) from A. nidulans and Pichia pastoris, on Q Exactive HF-X and Exploris 480 instruments.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Sample codes in the run names are not explained in the record (e.g. LFQ CE/MM/PD, HOPE_Anidulans_01-52, SECRETOME LSAA9_MTe/MTn, AmySP/NativeSP L/LM), the TMT-11 channel-to-strain layout is not deposited, and the PRM runs need the Skyline documents to tie targets to samples. Annotating would mean guessing most sample identities.

## Sources checked

PRIDE record and protocols; ZIP central directories of all raw-file archives.

## Unblocking requirement

A sample sheet mapping each run (and each TMT channel) to strain/genotype, growth condition and figure panel, e.g. from the paper supplement or the authors.
