G4A4 biotinylated RNA pulldown from K562 nuclear extract in K+ (folded G-quadruplex) or Li+ (destabilised) buffer, on-bead trypsin elution, Evosep ONE / timsTOF Pro DDA-PASEF, MaxQuant LFQ.

## Key caveats
1. Run-to-condition mapping is inferred, not stated. The deposit and paper do not map runs C1..C8 to buffers. Odd runs (C1,C3,C5,C7) = K+, even runs (C2,C4,C6,C8) = Li+, inferred from proteinGroups.txt: nucleolin (K+-dependent G4 binder per the paper) has log2 LFQ 19.0/16.5/18.7/16.3/18.6/16.6/19.0/16.7 across runs 1-8. This is a clean alternating pattern but remains an inference; the reviewer should weigh it.
2. Replicate count: paper says MS "in triplicate"; deposit has 8 runs (4 per condition). Biological replicate numbers 1-4 follow the C1/C2 ... C7/C8 pairing, which is also inferred.
3. Instrument: PRIDE dropdown says timsTOF SCP; analysis.tdf GlobalMetadata (run 1, range read) and the paper say timsTOF Pro (MS:1003005). timsTOF Pro used.
4. Sex (female) and disease (chronic myeloid leukemia) come from Cellosaurus K-562, not the deposit. Organism part, cell type, age, individual, ancestry, enrichment process: not available. PRIDE organismParts (bone marrow, lymphoblast) are submitter dropdowns and were not adopted.
5. Dissociation method not stated: not available. Precursor 10 ppm and fragment 40 ppm (TOF) from mqpar.xml; modifications from mqpar (Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M, Acetyl protein N-term variable).
6. Single protease (trypsin); no second cleavage-agent column needed.
7. Independent review (Step 9.5) was not run, because this task forbids spawning subagents. No review-gate approval exists.
