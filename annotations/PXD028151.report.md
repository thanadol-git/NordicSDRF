# PXD028151 evidence status

The five RAW files are fractions of a TMT10 phosphoproteomics experiment. PRIDE describes cells under p38/JNK-related stress-response conditions, sequential Lys-C/trypsin digestion, TMT labeling, and TiO2 phosphopeptide enrichment. The five RAW names identify fractions, not the ten channel identities.

**Blocked: TMT channel-to-sample map unresolved.** The article describes the biological comparisons, but the exact TMT126–TMT131 channel assignments for each condition and replicate are needed before any channel rows can be built. I inspected the `MxQ_results.zip` index and extracted its `mqpar.xml` and `parameters.txt` entries directly. They confirm TMT10 reporter channels and the five RAW fractions but do not assign channels to conditions. The paper's supplemental ZIP was inaccessible through the open-access mirror tested. No channel identity has been guessed.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD028151), [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8431686/).
