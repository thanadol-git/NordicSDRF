# PXD068367: run-level treatment and labeling map needed

The accession contains six `.d.zip` acquisitions. Its accession-matched `combined_peptide.tsv` and `combined_modified_peptide.tsv` each have six sample columns matching the RAW basenames exactly: three `aSN_WT` and three `aSN_1_4` runs. The search table records modified peptides with a +89.9702 Da tyrosine adduct, matching the PRIDE search protocol's variable dinitration setting. The protocol also states that some peptides were optionally dimethyl-labeled to assist cross-link detection.

These sources do not define what `1_4` means biologically, which runs received tetranitromethane at which concentration, or which runs received optional dimethyl labeling. The project-level Parkinson's disease tag describes the biological relevance of α-synuclein, not a donor diagnosis for these recombinant protein samples. Assigning it to every run would misstate the experiment. A local PSI-MOD ontology search did not find an exact dinitrotyrosine term, so the +89.9702 Da search modification also needs a reviewed representation.

No SDRF was added. To complete the six-run map, obtain a depositor design table or run-method notes identifying the `WT`/`1_4` conditions, TNM exposure, and dimethyl-labeled aliquots, then confirm a controlled term or explicitly reviewed free-text representation for dinitrotyrosine.

Sources: [PRIDE project record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD068367), accession-scoped `scratchpad/PXD068367/files.json`, `combined_peptide.tsv`, `combined_modified_peptide.tsv`, and the [published study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41386361/).
