# PXD070680 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Skeletal muscle proteomics reveals metabolic phenotype-specific adaptations to exercise training in health, obesity and type 2 diabetes
- **PRIDE record:** PXD070680
- **Publication:** Sci Adv (10.1126/sciadv.aeg8071)

## Evidence recovered

407 data files: 400 timsTOF Pro 2 .d.zip (164 proteome diaPASEF S01-S164, ~200 phospho diaPASEF, post-IP runs) and 7 Orbitrap Ascend RAW (primary myotubes S01-S13); Spectronaut reports with run-named columns only.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

Runs are named only by sample number (S01...S164) and plate well. Nothing deposited maps a sample to participant, group (lean / obese / type 2 diabetes), time point (pre / post 8-week HIIT) or condition (basal / insulin-stimulated), which are the study factors. Same situation as the companion adipose-tissue submission PXD040434 (blocked earlier).

## Sources checked

PRIDE record and protocols; RAW name patterns; column headers of directDIA_Proteome_Report_Protein.tsv and PrimaryMyotubes_Proteome_Report.tsv.

## Unblocking requirement

A sample -> participant / group / time point / insulin condition table for the proteome, phosphoproteome, post-IP and myotube runs.
