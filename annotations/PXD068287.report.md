## Limitations

- Raw files are only inside LC-MS_files.zip; file names were read from the zip central directory by HTTP range request. PRIDE lists no individual RAW files.
- Run-to-group mapping (T2D vs NGT) is not deposited or in the paper; MaxQuant experiments sample1-12 carry no group info. Disease is therefore 'not available' and no factor value was added. Do not assume sample1-6 = one group.
- Paper states 6 T2D and 6 NGT donors and that two proteomic samples were excluded (n=5 per group), yet all 12 sample1-12 have LFQ data in the deposited search; which were excluded is unknown.
- Mapping of the 12 sEV samples to donors is not stated; biological replicate 1-12 equals the MaxQuant sample number and individual is omitted. Age is only given as group means (NGT 44.0, T2D 49.2 years), so age is 'not available'.
- 15471.raw is an extra RAW not searched in the deposited MaxQuant run; it is kept as a separate source with all sample properties 'not available' (could be a QC or excluded sample), biological replicate 13 is a placeholder ordinal.
- Paper and PRIDE text list carbamyl (N-term) as a variable modification, but deposited mqpar.xml and summary.txt show only Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term); the SDRF follows mqpar.xml.
- Fragment tolerance, dissociation method (HCD likely but not stated), reduction/alkylation reagents (carbamidomethylation implies alkylation) are not stated and set to 'not available'/omitted. Primary myotube cultures are not a cell line, so the cell-lines template was not used.
- Acquisition method DDA is inferred from the MaxQuant LFQ shotgun workflow, not explicitly stated.
