# PXD006563 annotation report

ECM proteins from 3D cultures of a primary prostate fibroblast line, DU145 prostate cancer cells, and the two cultured together. University of Turku. Paper: Ojalill et al., Prostate 2018 (PMID 29520855).

## Templates
`ms-proteomics`, `human` v1.1.0.

## Rows and files
There are 19 rows. The blank runs are excluded. Files are `170419_PekkaR_<group>_<FEB|APR>_<n>`.

## Inferences to check
- **Group codes:** `WT` is read as DU145 monoculture, `SL24` as the patient-derived fibroblast line, and `WT-SL24` as the co-culture. The deposit does not define the codes; this reading comes from the project title (fibroblasts, DU145 cells and mixed cells).
- **Batches:** `FEB` and `APR` are annotated as biological replicates 1 and 2 (independent culture batches). `_<n>` is the technical replicate.
- **Disease:** all rows are `prostate carcinoma`. The fibroblast line comes from a cancer patient, but whether it is cancer-associated is not stated.

## Values
- **Cell line:** DU145 (CVCL_0105) for the WT and co-culture rows. The fibroblast line has no Cellosaurus entry, so its cell line is `not applicable`.
- **Enzyme:** Trypsin and Lys-C (two cleavage columns), because the protocol used a LysC/Trypsin mix.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, per the raw file headers and PRIDE. The protocol text says Orbitrap Velos Pro, which appears to be copied from PXD006562. HCD.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.5.2.8. Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and hydroxylation of P and K variable (hydroxylation is the same mass shift, UNIMOD:35).

## Validation
- `parse_sdrf` (CI style, warnings only) and the review gate pass.
- `tools reconcile`: three findings, all reviewed and kept as annotated:
  - `disease_on_control_runs`: `WT` is the parental DU145 carcinoma line, not a healthy control.
  - `instrument_contradicted`: the raw headers say Q Exactive; the Velos Pro text in the protocol appears to be copied from PXD006562.
  - `cleavage_agent_incomplete`: Lys-C is declared in a second cleavage column, which the tool does not read.
