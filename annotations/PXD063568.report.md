# PXD063568 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Proteomic Landscape of Porcine Induced Neural Stem Cell Reprogramming and Differentiation (Mahidol University). Label-free comparison of porcine tail fibroblasts (PTFs), two Sendai-virus piNSC lines (VSMUi002-B, VSMUi002-E) and the neuronal/glial progeny of each line (piNSC-NG), by DDA discovery plus SWATH-DIA, on a Bruker compact Q-TOF.
Paper: Ploypetch et al., PeerJ 13:e20120 (2025), DOI 10.7717/peerj.20120, PMC12499565. The PX record still says "publication pending"; the paper was found by title search. Donor details come from the cited PTF/piNSC derivation paper: Chakritbudsabong et al., Front Vet Sci 2022, DOI 10.3389/fvets.2021.806785, PMC8790232.

## Scope, evidence and decisions
Annotated 30 RAW (label-free) in **two files**:

| File | Rows x cols | Templates | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PXD063568-fibroblast.sdrf.tsv` | 6 x 35 | ms-proteomics, vertebrates | PTF R1-R3, DDA + SWATH |
| `PXD063568-pinsc.sdrf.tsv` | 24 x 40 | ms-proteomics, vertebrates, cell-lines | VSMUi002-B/-E piNSC and piNSC-NG R1-R3, DDA + SWATH |

**Why split (round 3).**
- Round 2 declared cell-lines and dia-acquisition per row. The BigBio CI gate (`.github/scripts/sdrf_review.py`) validates every declared template against the whole file, so that file failed with "[cell-lines] There were validation errors".
- cell-lines forbids both reserved words in `characteristics[cell line]` and `[cellosaurus accession]`. The PTF primary fibroblasts are not a cell line, so they cannot share a file that declares cell-lines. They are split along the cell-line vs primary-cell axis (precedent: PXD040999-elute/-flowthrough).
- The paper's main contrast, PTF vs piNSC vs NG, now spans two files. It is still recoverable from `factor value[cell type]`, which is present in both.
- dia-acquisition was **dropped** rather than splitting DDA from SWATH. Splitting would have given four files: two of 3 rows, and DDA and SWATH runs of the same sample in different files.
  - What was lost: its own columns (scan window lower/upper limit, isolation window width). They are not licensed otherwise, so the SWATH geometry (150-2200 m/z, 25 Da windows) is recorded in this report only.
  - What was kept: the SWATH runs still carry `NT=SWATH MS;AC=PRIDE:0000447` in `comment[proteomics data acquisition method]`, which ms-proteomics accepts.
- The old single file `PXD063568.sdrf.tsv` was deleted.

**Deposited vs annotated.** The FTP listing and the PROXI `datasetFiles` both give 60 files plus `submission.px_1`:
- 30 Bruker `.d.zip` RAW: 15 DDA (`<stem>_R1-3.d.zip`) and 15 SWATH (`<stem>_R1-3_SW.d.zip`).
- 15 Mascot `.dat` and 15 `.mgf`, one per DDA stem.

The stems are PTF, VSMUi002_B, VSMUi002_E, VSMUi002_B_diff and VSMUi002_E_diff. All 30 RAW files are annotated, with none missing and none extra (checked by script). The `.dat` and `.mgf` files are not data-file rows.

**Design.**
- Five groups x R1-R3. The paper says there were three independent biological replicates per group, processed individually, so R1-R3 = `biological replicate` 1-3.
- Each DDA run and its `_SW` counterpart share one `source name`. The submitter protocol says the SWATH "Samples from the same subjects as in the discovery phase were used". The pairing by identical R index is INFERRED from that statement plus the file naming.
- **Line identity (round-1 F1).** `characteristics[cell line]` = VSMUi002-B or VSMUi002-E on all piNSC and piNSC-NG rows. The NG rows are the differentiated progeny of that named line: the paper analyses VSMUi002-B-NGs and VSMUi002-E-NGs as separate groups.
  - `characteristics[cellosaurus accession]` = not available (Cellosaurus "VSMUi002": 0 hits).
  - `characteristics[passage number]` = 3 (PTF), 20 (piNSC) and 21 (NG), as stated.
  - The PTF file has no cell-line columns, because primary fibroblasts do not declare cell-lines. That includes `passage number`, which only cell-lines licenses, so the PTF passage (3) is recorded in this report only.
- **Factors.**
  - pinsc file: `factor value[cell type]` + `factor value[cell line]`, giving 4 groups.
  - fibroblast file: `factor value[cell type]` = fibroblast.
  - Across both files, the paper's five groups are recovered: PTF, VSMUi002-B, VSMUi002-E, VSMUi002-B-NG and VSMUi002-E-NG.
- **Replicate key (round-1 F2).** `comment[technical replicate]` = 1 for the DDA injection and 2 for the SWATH injection of the same source. This makes (source name, biological replicate, technical replicate, fraction identifier) unique across all 30 rows, and so within each file; the build script asserts it.
  - Rationale: the DDA and SWATH runs are two separate LC-MS injections (1 ug each) of the same biological sample ("Samples from the same subjects as in the discovery phase"). That matches the spec's definition of technical replicate, repeated measurements of the same sample.
  - Caveat: the numbers 1/2 mark injection order by acquisition mode, not replicate injections in the same mode. The paper does not say whether the same digest was re-injected or a fresh digest was made.
  - No fractions were invented: fraction identifier = 1 throughout.
- `factor value[cell type]`: fibroblast (CL:0000057) for PTF; neural stem cell (CL:0000047) for piNSC; neural cell (CL:0002319, "a cell that is part of the nervous system") for piNSC-NG. The NG groups are described as mixed neuronal and glial progeny (TUJ1, MAP2, SYP, TH, GFAP). That is why no neuron-only term is used.
- piNSC-NG rows also carry `characteristics[treatment]`: 14 days in EGF/bFGF-free medium.

**Donor.** All PTFs and both lines come from one animal, "a three-day-old crossbred piglet", Large White/Landrace x Duroc (PMC8790232). This paper says the PTF isolation is described in that previous work. So:
- `developmental stage` = neonate stage (donor stage at isolation)
- `strain or breed` = Large White/Landrace x Duroc crossbred
- `sex` = not available (not reported in either paper)

`organism` = Sus scrofa domesticus (NCBITaxon:9825), as registered in PX and consistent with a domestic crossbred piglet. The campaign cache said "Sus scrofa"; 9825 is its subspecies child.

**Sample prep.** Submitter protocol:
- 50 ug protein, DTT 100 mM, then IAA 100 mM in the dark, then a DTT quench
- Trypsin Gold (Promega), 16 h at 37 C
- C18 ZipTip clean-up
- no fractionation

**LC-MS.**
- Dionex Ultimate 3000 RSLCnano, CaptiveSpray, "Q-ToF Compact, Bruker" (protocol) / "Bruker compact QTOF" (paper), giving MS:1002280 `compact`. PRIDE's instrument field agrees.
- DDA: AutoMSn **CID**, top-2, m/z 150-2200, giving MS:1000133 (campaign QTOF convention).
- SWATH: 25 Da windows over 150-2200, 32 overlapping windows, 100 ms.
  - Acquisition method: `NT=SWATH MS;AC=PRIDE:0000447`, an is_a child of PRIDE:0000450. This follows round-1 F3: the paper says "DIA was performed in SWATH mode", and the dia-acquisition template names SWATH MS as an allowed DIA descendant.
  - Caveat: the PRIDE CV definition of PRIDE:0000447 describes the ABSciex implementation, whereas this is a Bruker QTOF. If a reviewer prefers the generic term, the fallback is PRIDE:0000450.
  - Window geometry (scan window 150-2200 m/z, isolation width 25): **report only**. dia-acquisition was dropped (see "Why split").

**Search.** MaxQuant 1.6.2.10 / Andromeda, Sus scrofa UniProt.
- Modifications: Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M variable; "N-terminal acetylation" variable.
- Trypsin/P, 2 missed cleavages.
- Precursor 0.5 Da (first search) / 0.25 Da (main search). The main-search value is recorded.
- Fragment (TOF MS/MS) 0.5 Da.
- SWATH extraction in Skyline 19.1.0.193.

**Accession verification.** OLS4 was unreachable today (WebFetch rate-limited; browser returned "upstream connect error"). Every term was checked live elsewhere:
- Ontobee: NCBITaxon:9825, UBERON:0002415, UBERON:0007221, CL:0000057, CL:0000047, CL:0002319, MS:1002280, MS:1001251
- hegroup SPARQL: PATO:0000461
- psi-ms.obo on GitHub: MS:1000133
- pride_cv.obo on GitHub: PRIDE:0000450 / 0000447 (SWATH MS, is_a 0000450) / 0000627 / 0000599 / 0000608
- UNIMOD 1 / 4 / 35: checked against the `unimod.xml` bundled with sdrf_pipelines, because the live unimod.org fetch failed.
- Cellosaurus "VSMUi002": 0 hits.

## Values left not available / not applicable

- `organism part`: tail for PTF rows. **not applicable** for piNSC and piNSC-NG rows: they are in-vitro reprogrammed/differentiated derivatives, not tail tissue (the same convention as cell-line rows in this campaign).
- `treatment`: not applicable for PTF and piNSC rows.
- `fractionation method`: not applicable. Single-shot runs.
- `precursor/fragment mass tolerance` on the 15 SWATH rows: not available. Skyline extraction tolerances are not stated; the MaxQuant tolerances apply to the DDA search only.
- Omitted columns:
  - Collision energy: not stated.
- `cellosaurus accession`: not available on line rows, because the lines are not registered.

## Stated vs INFERRED
- STATED: the group identity of each run (self-describing file names plus the five groups in the protocol); three biological replicates; instrument; DDA/SWATH; CID; enzyme; DTT/IAA; MaxQuant modifications and tolerances; donor breed and age.
- INFERRED:
  - R index = biological replicate number.
  - DDA R*n* and SWATH R*n* are the same biological sample. As a consequence, technical replicate 1 = DDA and 2 = SWATH.
  - The NG rows carry the parental line name in `characteristics[cell line]`.
  - The acetylation position is `Protein N-term`, the MaxQuant default "Acetyl (Protein N-term)". The paper only says "N-terminal acetylation".
  - The donor described in PMC8790232 is the donor of these PTFs. This rests on the paper citing that work for PTF isolation, and on that paper deriving VSMUi002-B/E.

## Conflicts / caveats
- **Deposited search output vs Methods.** The deposited search files are **Mascot `.dat`** (445-702 MB each), but the paper and protocol describe only MaxQuant/Andromeda. The `.dat` parameter headers were not read: there is no shell internet, and the browser would have had to download the whole 600 MB file. So `comment[modification parameters]` comes from the MaxQuant prose, not from the deposited search. The PX modification list (MOD:00425 monohydroxylated, MOD:00397 iodoacetamide derivatized) supports Ox(M) and CAM(C). It does not list acetylation.
- **Instrument.** The model was not confirmed from the raw files: `.d.zip` archives of 0.6-4.1 GB cannot be range-read here. Paper, protocol and PX all agree on compact, so there is no conflict.
- **SWATH geometry.** "32 overlapping windows" of 25 Da cannot span m/z 150-2200 (that would need about 82). The geometry is reported as stated and not encoded.
- **Organism part (round-1 F4, kept).** `not applicable` stays on the derived rows; the tail origin is recorded on the PTF rows and here.
- **Treatment (round-1 F5, kept).** The NG treatment is still free text, accurate to the paper; no ontology term was mapped.
- **Validation (round 3).** Both files report `[OK]` from `python3 /home/claude/sdrf-annotated-datasets/.github/scripts/sdrf_review.py` (2/2 clean, exit 0).
  - `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf --use_ols_cache_only` was run once per declared template on the whole file, and every run passed:
    - fibroblast file: ms-proteomics and vertebrates.
    - pinsc file: ms-proteomics, vertebrates and cell-lines.
  - Validation is structural only. The installed sdrf-pipelines lacks the OLS extras, so terms were checked by hand as listed above.

SDRF files: PXD063568-fibroblast.sdrf.tsv and PXD063568-pinsc.sdrf.tsv. Both are built by `/home/claude/bangkok3/scratch/PXD063568/build_PXD063568.py`; the earlier versions are kept as `build_PXD063568.round1.py` and `.round2.py`. Evidence files are in the same scratch directory.
