## Design and file mapping

Female C57BL/6 mice were fed 0.2% cuprizone for 7 weeks and then taken off it so they could remyelinate. From week 6 they received intraperitoneal calcitriol (1,25-(OH)2D3) or placebo (vehicle). Brains were collected at week 7 (demyelinated), week 8 (1 week of remyelination) and week 10 (3 weeks of remyelination). For each of the six conditions, protein from the right frontal brain of 4–6 mice was pooled. The six pools were TMT6plex-labelled, mixed, and fractionated by mixed-mode HPLC into 54 fractions, each run once.

The deposited Proteome Discoverer table agrees independently: its ratio columns are `129 D7/126 Pl7`, `130 D8/127 Pl8` and `131 D10/128 Pl10`.
- **Files:** PRIDE lists exactly 54 RAW files (`TMT6_frac_1to3.raw`, then `TMT6_frac_8.raw` to `TMT6_frac_60.raw`). This matches the protocol: fractions 1–3 were pooled and fractions 4–7 were discarded, leaving 54. Every file is one fraction of the same TMT6plex mix, so each file has six rows, one per channel.
- **Factor values:** `factor value[treatment]` (calcitriol vs placebo) and `factor value[sampling time]` (7 / 8 / 10 week). The study reports VitD/Ctr ratios at each time point.

## Technical values

- Instrument: Orbitrap Elite (MS:1001910). Acquisition is DDA, with HCD at 40% NCE; MS2 is read in the Orbitrap and the MS1 range is 300–2000 m/z. Source: PRIDE protocol.
- Trypsin; precursor tolerance 10 ppm; fragment tolerance 0.05 Da. Source: data processing protocol.
- Modifications are Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed, TMT6plex (K) fixed, TMT6plex (any N-term) fixed and Oxidation (M) variable. These come from the data processing protocol and were confirmed against the deposited PD peptide table. Its Modifications column contains only these four, across 60,880 peptides.
- Fractionation: `High-performance liquid chromatography` (PRIDE:0000565). See Limitations.

## Limitations

- **Disease.** `demyelinating disease of central nervous system` (MONDO:0020800) is written on every row. Every mouse in all six groups was demyelinated with cuprizone. PRIDE gives `multiple sclerosis` (DOID:2377), but that is the human disease being modelled, not a diagnosis of these mice, so it was not used. This value is a judgement made from the protocol.
- **Sex = female.** This comes from the Nystad 2014 APMIS abstract, which the PRIDE protocol cites for the animal work. The PRIDE record does not state sex, and the Oveland 2017 full text was not accessible.
- **Strain = C57BL/6.** Stated in the PRIDE protocol ("C57Bl/6") and in the Nystad 2014 abstract. The substrain is not given.
- **Organism part = right frontal lobe** (UBERON:0002810). The protocol says "right frontal including some corpus callosum". The corpus callosum part is not recorded separately.
- **Developmental stage = `not available`.** The age of the mice is not stated in any accessible source.
- **Pooling.** Each channel is a pool of 4–6 mice, so `characteristics[pooled sample]` and `characteristics[biological replicate]` are both `pooled`. Individual mouse identifiers are not deposited, so no `SN=` members are listed. Pool sizes come from the protocol.
- **Sampling time** is counted in weeks from the start of cuprizone feeding (7, 8, 10). This follows the project description.
- **Treatment.** Values are `calcitriol` (NCIT:C330) and `placebo` (NCIT:C753). Cuprizone was given to every group and is not recorded as a treatment value, since it does not vary between groups.
- **Fraction identifiers** are ordinal (1–54). Fraction 1 is `TMT6_frac_1to3.raw` (pooled HPLC fractions 1–3); fractions 2–54 are `TMT6_frac_8.raw` to `TMT6_frac_60.raw`. The original well numbers survive in the file names.
- **Fractionation method.** The protocol describes mixed-mode HPLC (Sielc Promix column, Agilent 1260). PRIDE has no mixed-mode term under PRIDE:0000550, so the generic HPLC term is used.
- **TMT labels** are written `TMT126`–`TMT131`, as in the protocol. The reagent was TMT6plex; N/C isotopologue suffixes are not given in the deposit.
- **Omitted.** Reduction and alkylation reagents are not stated, so those columns are omitted. Carbamidomethyl in the search implies alkylation, but the reagent is not named.
- **Software mismatch.** PRIDE `softwares` lists MaxQuant, but the protocol and the deposited table show Proteome Discoverer 1.4.1.14 (MS Amanda, Mascot, SequestHT). This does not affect any SDRF column.
- **Paper access.** Only the abstract of the primary paper (Oveland 2017) was available; it is not open access. All design details come from the PRIDE record, the deposited PD table and the Nystad 2014 abstract.
