# PXD015955 annotation blocked

PXD015955 is "in vivo environment swiftly restricts human pancreatic progenitors towards monohormonal identity via a HNF1A/HNF4A mechanism" from Simona Chera's lab, Department of Clinical Science, University of Bergen, Norway. The paper is Legøy TA et al., Front Cell Dev Biol 2020;8:109 (PMID 32161757, PMC7052484, doi:10.3389/fcell.2020.00109). It is a TMT 11-plex, SPS-MS3 study of hiPSC-derived pancreatic progenitors (stage 5) that were alginate-encapsulated and xenotransplanted intraperitoneally into NSG RIP-DTR mice for 2 weeks, then retrieved. Not yet annotated upstream: `bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets` `datasets/PXD015955` and `sandbox/PXD015955` both return 404.

## Blocker

There is no TMT channel-to-sample map anywhere in the record. The record does not even say which 11 samples went into the plex. An SDRF for this deposit needs 11 rows per RAW file, one sample per reporter channel. Assigning sample identities to TMT126…TMT131C without evidence would be invention, so no SDRF is emitted.

The available sources only bound what the plex might contain, and they do not settle it:
- Paper Methods: the in vivo transplanted cells were processed for TMT 11-plex. Three mice were used per group (normoglycaemic and diphtheria-toxin diabetic). "The hyperglycemia samples from this dataset were not analyzed in this work. Two human islets samples were excluded from analysis due to islet quality problems."
- The PRIDE sample-processing protocol also mentions human islets ("Human islets were processed as previously described"). Islets came from seven deceased donors.
- Supplementary Figure 1e clusters the samples with n = 2, 2, 2, 3, 5 (S7enc, S5, S7, 2w_postTX, human islets) "over two plexes". The in vitro conditions belong to the companion deposit PXD012704.
- These counts are compatible with several different 11-channel compositions, for example 3 normoglycaemic + 3 hyperglycaemic grafts + 5 islets, or other mixes including bridge or excluded islet channels. None is stated, and no channel order is given.

## What was established

- **Files:** 14 Thermo RAW files, each with a matching mzIdentML (Sequest + in-house LDA; no sample or channel section). All entries' `projectAccessions` contain PXD015955.
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, confirmed from RAW headers and matching PRIDE `MS:1002732`.
- **Acquisition:** Range-read RAW headers show two acquisition series of the same pooled TMT sample, matching the paper's "analyzed in duplicate, one with advanced peak determination (APD) activated and a second run with this option off":
  - APD on: method `Iso07_100u_120min_TMT10_60_NoLM_cosmo.meth`; runs a13002, a13003, a13004_20190318165355, a13005, a13006, a13007 (autosampler rows 173–178) and a13016 (row 188).
  - APD off: method `Iso07_100u_120min_TMT10_60_NoLM_NoAPD_cosmo.meth`; runs a13026–a13031 (rows 198–203) and a13041 (row 213).
  - The paper reports 6 combined high-pH fractions, but each series has 7 runs. The identity of a13016 and a13041 (a seventh fraction or a re-injection) is not documented.
- **RAW sample field:** generic `Sample048` in every file. It carries no channel information.
- **Search settings (PRIDE data-processing protocol):** TMT (+229.163 Da) on K and peptide N-term, fixed; carbamidomethyl C, fixed; oxidation M, variable; 50 ppm precursor tolerance; 0.9 Da fragment tolerance; human UniProt. The cleavage agent is not stated in the PRIDE text.

## Channel-map ladder checked

1. **Paper and supplementary:** full text (Europe PMC PMC7052484), Figure 1, Supplementary Figure 1 (Data_Sheet_1.PDF), and Tables 1–3. Tables 1–3 are DEP gene lists and an abbreviation list. No per-channel table.
2. **Deposited PRIDE files** (v2 `/files`, v3 `files/all`: 28 files, only `.raw` and `.mzIdentML`): no sample sheet or design file. mzIdentML header and tail hold only software and `search_id` user params. RAW headers carry a generic sample name.
3. **Europe PMC `supplementaryFiles`** for PMC7052484: the same 12 files as rung 1, with no layout file.
4. **Data Availability statement:** cites only PXD012704 and PXD015955. There is no Zenodo, figshare, GitHub or other repository. The companion PXD012704 ("Alginate encapsulation of differentiating hiPSCs", Sci Rep 2020) holds only RAW/mzIdentML files, with no sample sheet. A Europe PMC full-text search for "PXD015955" returns only this paper.
5. **Cross-validation:** not possible, because nothing could be reconstructed.

## What would unblock it

A per-channel table (TMT126…TMT131C → sample) for the PXD015955 plex from the submitters (thomas.legoy / tle088@uib.no, simona.chera@uib.no) or from Joao A. Paulo (Harvard, who ran the samples). For each sample it should give the condition (2w_postTX normoglycaemic, hyperglycaemic graft, human islet donor, other), the mouse or donor number, and which channels were excluded. A note on what a13016 and a13041 contain (a seventh fraction vs a re-injection) would also be needed. With that, the deposit maps to one SDRF of 14 runs × 11 channels = 154 rows. Templates would be `ms-proteomics` + `human`, with APD on/off recorded per run.
