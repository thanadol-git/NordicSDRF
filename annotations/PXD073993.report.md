# PXD073993 SDRF annotation report

## Study
"Integrated Single-Myofibre Multi-Omics Correlates Inflammatory State to Myosin Energetics in ICU-Acquired Weakness". J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle 2025, doi:10.1002/jcsm.70407, PMID 42896625, PMC13680048 (full Methods read via NCBI efetch; the titles matched). Proteomics at SDU Odense. Single human quadriceps (rectus femoris) myofibres from 8 ICU-acquired weakness (ICU-AW) patients and 8 orthopaedic controls (total hip replacement for hip arthritis) were profiled sequentially for Mant-ATP chase, RNA-seq, and proteomics on the same fibre. Label-free diaPASEF on a timsTOF SCP with an Evosep One (60 SPD).

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.1.0 + human v1.1.0 + clinical-metadata v1.1.0 (patient cohort, licenses `individual`). No `single-cell`: these are whole single myofibre segments, not single-cell isolations. No `dia-acquisition` (window table not deposited).

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
`files/all`: 180 files (all `projectAccessions` = PXD073993): 178 RAW (`.d.zip`), the submitter's `metadata.csv` and a DIA-NN gene matrix. All 178 runs annotated, one row each.

## Sample map
`metadata.csv` (178 rows) maps `Analytical Sample ID` S1–S178 to `Subject ID` and plate position; each RAW name carries `_E0000048x_S<n>_S2-<well>_`, and every file's S-number and well matched the metadata (0 misses). Subject IDs read `<donor>f<fibre>`: donors `c14,c15,c17,c18,c19,c22,c24,c25` (8 controls) and `s5_2,s8_2,s14_2,s15_2,s20,s21,s23,s26` (8 ICU-AW), matching the paper's n = 8 + 8. `characteristics[individual]` = donor, `biological replicate` = fibre number within the donor, `source name` = the full subject ID (one fibre = one source).

## Key decisions
- The donor prefix `c` = control and `s` = ICU patient is inferred from the 8/8 split, which matches the paper's group sizes exactly.
- Disease: ICU-AW donors = critical illness polyneuropathy (MONDO:0001957), the term PRIDE uses; ICU-AW has no MONDO term of its own. Controls = osteoarthritis, hip (MONDO:0006629), because they were biopsied during hip replacement for hip arthritis, so `normal` would be wrong.
- Organism part rectus femoris (UBERON:0001378, PRIDE + paper); cell type skeletal muscle fiber (CL:0008002); developmental stage adult (cohort age range 19–88).
- Instrument timsTOF SCP (MS:1003231); DIA (PRIDE:0000450) in diaPASEF mode; CID (MS:1000133); MS1 100–1700 m/z.
- Trypsin + Lys-C (paper 2.7).
- Factor: disease.
