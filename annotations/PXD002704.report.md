# PXD002704 - annotation report

## Study
CysPAT (Cysteine-specific Phosphonate Adaptable Tag) enrichment of Cys peptides plus TiO2, applied to (a) total Cys residues in HeLa lysate and (b) simultaneous Cys-peptide / phosphopeptide co-enrichment from SILAC-labelled HeLa cells stimulated with EGF for 5 min. Huang H, Pedersen MH, Vea MI, Lassen PS, Larsen MR, Palmisano G. Mol Cell Proteomics 2016 (PMID 27281782, PMC5054350). Submitter H. Huang, PI M. R. Larsen (SDU).

## Outcome
ANNOTATED. 98 rows x 45 columns. `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -t ms-proteomics -t human -t cell-lines`: exit 0, 1 warning (custom SIA modification has no UNIMOD accession). `sdrf-tools structure`: OK.

## Templates and why
`ms-proteomics` + `human` + `cell-lines` (v1.1.0). MS technology; human; HeLa cell line (cell-lines makes cellosaurus columns required). No clinical/oncology template: a cell line, no patient. No `dia-acquisition` (DDA).

Conflicts: PRIDE lists LTQ Orbitrap Velos for the whole project; the paper says Orbitrap Fusion for the SILAC arm. Raw files show neither for QHF.
- Modifications read from each `.msf` Mascot node, not from prose. Search arm 1 (HelaSIA270114): SIA (C), Oxidation (M), Acetyl (K), Acetyl (Any N-term), Deamidated (N,Q).
