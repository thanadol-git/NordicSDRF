## Limitations
- Two cleavage-agent columns (Trypsin, Lys-C) is a deviation from the single-valued contract column, added after `build` (as in PXD013363-lysc-gluc); a reviewer may prefer only the final protease.
- Cell line E14Tg2a.IV is Cellosaurus CVCL_Y481 (name "E14TG2a.4"); sex male (paper); strain (129/Ola ancestry) not stated -> `not available`. Passage number (16 per PRIDE protocol) is not written.
- Disease for the two knock-in lines is `intellectual disability, X-linked 106` (MONDO:0030907, the OGT-CDG disorder modelled), asserted on the cell lines, not donors; control `not applicable`.
- TMT label set (TMT11 vs TMT10) as above. Unused channels 129C/130N are not represented.
