# PXD021004 BLOCKED: iTRAQ 4plex channel-to-sample map not recoverable

Status: blocked annotation; no SDRF produced. Independent review not applicable.

## Dataset
Western Norway University of Applied Sciences / University of Bergen (lab PI Alvhild Alette Bjørkum). Serum of eight females at 04:00 after a control night of sleep and after 6 h of sleep deprivation (paired, 16 samples); MARS-14 depletion, trypsin, iTRAQ 4-plex, four experiments (Ex1-Ex4; 4 channels each), 50 mixed-mode fractions per experiment, LTQ Orbitrap Elite; 203 RAW files (`AB_Ex<1-4>_<fraction>.raw`, 50/50/50/51 files, plus `Blank1_AB.raw` and `Blank2_AB.raw`). Paper: Sleep Science and Practice 2021, DOI 10.1186/s41606-021-00066-2.

## Why it cannot be annotated
Which subject (P1-P8) and condition (control `_0` vs sleep deprivation `_1`) sits in which of the four iTRAQ channels (114-117) of which of the four experiments is not stated in the PRIDE record. The deposited `Search_results.txt` gives per-subject columns (`P1_0`…`P8_1`) and which experiments each protein was seen in, but no experiment/channel layout. The paper could not be obtained in full text (not in Europe PMC; publisher and repository pages redirect to login or handle resolvers). Inventing the channel identities is forbidden.

## Verified facts for a future annotation
Homo sapiens, blood serum, female, 8 subjects, paired design; MARS Hu-14 depletion; trypsin 1:20; iTRAQ 4-plex; mixed-mode (Sielc Promix) fractionation, first 10 min discarded; LTQ Orbitrap Elite. Possible unblock: the paper's methods/supplementary table or author contact (Alvhild.Alette.Bjorkum@hvl.no).
