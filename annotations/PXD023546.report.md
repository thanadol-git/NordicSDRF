## File → sample map (recovered, not guessed)

`2085-N` vial codes are **not** named hypoxia/normoxia in RAW filenames. The map was reconstructed by joining:

1. Europe PMC / Nature supplementary PD protein tables
   - MOESM2.xlsx (`lumos200928_Renca HN EV repeat.pdResult`) — EV study, PD sample factors `pair, hypoxia|normoxia`

3. Protein “Found in Sample: High” bit-patterns in the Excel vs PSM file ids in the mzid (greedy co-occurrence). Singleton proteins independently confirm several F-ids. Every `2085-N` pair of technical replicates received the **same** pair number and oxygenation.

## Conservative choices

- Organism part EV = `extracellular vesicle` (UBERON search hits GO:1903561; validator accepted). SN = `culture fluid` (BTO:0002233). Blanks = `not applicable`.
- Disease `renal cell carcinoma` (MONDO:0005086) on study samples. Cellosaurus disease is NCIt “Mouse kidney carcinoma” (no MONDO hit); paper/PRIDE say RCC. Blank rows: `not applicable`.
- Cell line required by template and does not allow `not applicable`; blanks still carry `RenCa` / `CVCL_2174`. Sampling site `kidney` likewise.
- Sex `male` and strain `BALB/c` from Cellosaurus, not from per-sample paper text. Developmental stage / passage / storage temperature `not available`. Donor age 6W not written as sample age.
- Culture medium NCIT term `Dulbecco's Modified Eagle Medium`; paper used DMEM/F-12 (no NCIT F-12 mix term).
- Characteristics `treatment` omitted: `normoxia` is not in NCIT/EFO; oxygenation lives only in the factor column.
- Sample type `study sample` vs `not applicable` on blanks.
- Modifications from PRIDE `dataProcessingProtocol` + paper Proteomics methods + peptide-level Excel (Carbamidomethyl / Oxidation / Deamidated / Acetyl). Deposited mzid `SpectrumIdentificationProtocol` has trypsin (2 missed) but no `SearchModification` block; no `.pdResult`/`.msf` was deposited. UNIMOD: Carbamidomethyl C fixed (4); Oxidation M variable (35); Deamidated N,Q variable (7); Acetyl protein N-term variable (1).
- Precursor 10 ppm; fragment **0.02 Da** (orbitrap) as specified. Protocol also searched ion-trap MS/MS at 0.6 Da; that analyzer is not written.
- Dissociation `beam-type collision-induced dissociation` (HCD, MS:1000422). Methods also used CID at equal priority; contract is not `multiple`.
- Reduction DTT / alkylation IAA from Methods. Instrument Orbitrap Fusion Lumos (`MS:1002732`). DDA `PRIDE:0000627`.
- `comment[sdrf version]` = `v1.1.0`; `comment[sdrf annotation tool]` = `manual curation`.
