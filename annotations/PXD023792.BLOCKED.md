- The publication describes four decellularized myxoid-liposarcoma PDX scaffolds analyzed by mass spectrometry.

## Exact blocker

No checked source explicitly maps F1, F2, or F3 to individual RAW filenames or identifies the non-fraction acquisition files. Three 20-fraction TMT sets would account for 60 RAW files, but PRIDE deposits 70. Ten files (`Fusion_180514_52.raw`–`56.raw` and `Fusion_180514_77.raw`–`81.raw`) are much smaller than the 60 apparent analytical files, yet neither PRIDE, the workbook, the publication, nor its supplements labels them as blanks, standards, quality controls, or members of a TMT set. Assigning the three 20-file numeric blocks to F1–F3, or classifying those ten intervening files, would therefore be an inferred run/set map.

The only remaining deposited containers that may hold file-level processing metadata are `2876_3sets_0mc_Fusion_180514_32_101.msf` and the associated processing result, each about 14.4 GB. They were not downloaded because the campaign explicitly forbids giant raw/result downloads, and the deposited compact workbook does not expose their run-to-set table.

Without an exact RAW-to-TMT-set map and identities for all 70 RAW files, a complete SDRF cannot be authored without inventing sample/channel assignments.
