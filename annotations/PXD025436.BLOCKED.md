# PXD025436 — BLOCKED

## Exact blocker

The accession combines 24 Q Exactive runs with 61 Orbitrap Fusion runs, and the checked files do not provide a complete run/sample design for the first group or a reporter-channel-to-sample design for the TMT groups. The result tables contain quantified comparisons but not the lossless acquisition/channel join required for SDRF rows.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE project record: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD025436`
- PRIDE file inventory: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD025436/files/all`
- Publication: `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11357550/` (DOI `10.3390/microorganisms12081725`)
- Deposited result workbooks `2498_TargetDecoy_Multiconsensus_from_24_Reports.xlsx`, `2748_set1to3.xlsx`, and `2762_Set1andSet2.xlsx`
- Deposited small Q Exactive `.msf` search files and all RAW/result filenames

PRIDE declares TMT quantification and lists 85 RAW files: 24 `QE_...` acquisitions and 61 `Fusion_...` acquisitions. The publication describes surface-shaving experiments on multiple *Staphylococcus aureus* strains, and the result workbooks group quantified outputs by study/set. None of the checked sheets states, for each TMT set, the exact reporter-to-strain/sample/replicate assignment and reference/empty channels. The Q Exactive filenames and per-run search files likewise do not establish the biological identity of each run. Result-column order is not a safe substitute for an experimental-design table.

## Evidence needed to unblock

An authoritative design manifest covering both acquisition families: exact RAW-to-sample/strain/replicate mapping for the Q Exactive runs, and set/reporter-to-sample/strain/replicate mapping (including pools and unused channels) for every Fusion TMT set.
