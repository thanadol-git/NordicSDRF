# PXD024786 — blocked SDRF annotation

## Evidence checked

- The PRIDE project record describes rat thyroid tissue from young and adult animals exposed to 0 (control), 50 kBq, or 500 kBq iodine-131, digested with trypsin, labelled across four TMT10plex sets, fractionated into 20 concatenated fractions per set, and measured by SPS-MS3 on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos.
- The complete PRIDE inventory contains 102 Thermo RAW files named only by acquisition sequence (`Lumos_190613_06.raw` through `Lumos_190613_119.raw`, with gaps).
- The deposited `TMT_sample_list.xlsx` was inspected. It gives an exact channel-to-sample table for TMT sets 1–4, including pooled references and the control, 50 kBq, and 500 kBq young/adult replicates.
- The PRIDE processing record identifies a single combined Proteome Discoverer result spanning files `06-119`; neither that record nor the sample-list workbook lists the RAW files contributing to each TMT set or fraction.
- The associated publication metadata and available supplementary evidence were checked; no RAW-file-to-set crosswalk was found.
- No community SDRF is available for PXD024786.

## Exact blocker

The four exact TMT10 channel maps cannot be connected to the 102 opaque RAW files. The experimental protocol predicts 80 primary fraction runs (four sets × 20 fractions), while the accession contains 102 RAW files, and no deposited source identifies which runs are primary fractions, repeats, or other acquisitions. Assigning TMT set and fraction from numeric acquisition ranges would be inference and could also silently include repeated or non-primary runs. No SDRF was written.

## Evidence that would unblock the accession

A Proteome Discoverer input-file table, instrument sequence/export, or submitter manifest mapping each RAW filename to TMT set, fraction number, and run role (primary, repeat, QC, or other).
