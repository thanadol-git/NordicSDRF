## Design and file mapping
The paper describes 8 patients with PTB sampled at baseline (0M), 2 months (2M) and 6 months (6M) of treatment. The RAW names are `<run>_<patient code>_<ddmmyy>[suffix]` with 13 distinct codes (`42-14`, `50-14`, `79-14`, `83-14`, `128-14`, `S20-14`…`S42-14`), i.e. **more codes than the 8 patients in the paper, and no table links codes to patients or to 0M/2M/6M**. The annotation therefore keeps the codes verbatim as individuals (`patient_<code>`), uses the date in the name as `comment[acquisition date]` (assumed to be the acquisition date) and writes `sampling time` as `not available`; the 0M/2M/6M assignment is not inferred from date order. Each (code, date) is one source; extra files for the same (code, date) (`AfterCAL`, `Low-Intensity`, `R5_…`, `R6_…`, `…afterCAL`) are technical replicates. All 38 RAW files annotated (34 sources, 13 codes).
