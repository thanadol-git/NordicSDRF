## Evidence and mapping

- PRIDE `PXD011295` identifies rat spleen and a Q Exactive instrument; it
  deposits 18 raw files named `20180822_Rat_{Bioruptor|Barocycler}{1..3}_R{1..3}.raw`.
- Kuras et al., PMID `30462917`, DOI `10.1021/acs.jproteome.8b00792`, states
  that the three LC-MS/MS replicates were configured as one experiment.
- The public `proteinGroups.zip` and `peptides.zip` archives contain six
  MaxQuant experiment groups: Barocycler 1–3 and Bioruptor 1–3.  This supports
  the source-to-raw map used in the SDRF: the trailing `R1`–`R3` is the
  technical replicate for each numbered preparation sample.
- The paper documents the Lys-C/trypsin digest and the MaxQuant modifications:
  fixed carbamidomethyl-C; variable oxidation-M and protein N-terminal acetylation.
