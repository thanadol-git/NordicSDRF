# PXD001808 annotation report

## Scope

All 40 directly deposited RAW files are represented. A separate MGF with a .raw.mgf suffix is not an additional raw acquisition.

## Evidence and decisions

- PRIDE describes bronchoalveolar-lavage exosomes labelled in three iTRAQ 8-plex experiments, with trypsin digestion, SCX fractionation, and Orbitrap Velos DDA.
- RAW names unambiguously identify the Exo I, Exo II, and Exo III plex series and their SCX fractions. The EXOTRAQIII filenames ending in r are represented as technical replicate 2.
- The required supplement mapping iTRAQ channels to controls, Löfgren sarcoidosis, and regular sarcoidosis is not deposited; individual clinical/channel assignments are not inferred.
