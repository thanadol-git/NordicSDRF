# PXD062585 — blocked

## Decision

An SDRF cannot be authored without inventing the TMT channel-to-sample mapping.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE project record: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD062585`
  - The sample-processing protocol states that CSF peptides were labeled with TMT 16-plex reagents and fractionated into 24 concatenated fractions.
  - The linked publication is Weiner et al. (2025), DOI `10.1186/s13024-025-00841-0`, PMCID `PMC12057231`.
- Complete PRIDE file listing: `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD062585/files/all`
  - It contains 360 Thermo RAW files.
  - RAW names resolve to 15 multiplexes (`S01`–`S15`) with 24 fractions each (`F01`–`F24`).
  - The only non-RAW deposited file is a project-level mzTab result; no sample sheet, Proteome Discoverer study-information export, or labeling table is deposited.
- Publication full text and all four PMC supplementary files were checked.
  - Supplementary material describes cohort demographics, analysis subsets, protein-level results, and enrichment results, but does not map TMT126–TMT134N channels to source samples for `S01`–`S15`.
  - The Data Availability statement says normalized abundances with clinical data must be requested from the cohort owners; it does not expose a public channel map or a public supplementary repository containing one.
- Community annotation repository lookup returned no existing SDRF for this accession.

## Exact blocker

For each of the 15 TMT16 multiplexes, the identities of the samples assigned to the reporter channels—and any unused, pooled-reference, or bridge channels—are not present in PRIDE, the paper, the PMC supplements, or the public Data Availability links. The RAW filenames identify only multiplex and fraction. Creating SDRF rows would therefore require guessing sample/channel identities, which is prohibited.

## Needed to unblock

A labeling sheet or Proteome Discoverer study-information export that maps every channel in each of `S01`–`S15` to its source sample, including reference/bridge and unused channels.
