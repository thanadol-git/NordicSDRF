# PXD011546 — BLOCKED

## Status

No SDRF was created.  The public records establish methods and raw-file
families, but not a complete sample/condition mapping for all 52 raw files.

## Evidence checked

- PRIDE `PXD011546`: 52 raw files in three families — 26 `Anahita_*`, 20
  `CK_*`, and 6 `TM_*` — plus submitted result matrices.
- Mohanty et al. (2019), PMID `30971685`, PMCID `PMC6458182`, DOI
  `10.1038/s41467-019-09040-0`: human CSF clinical groups and the DDA/DIA
  proteomics methods (trypsin, Q Exactive Plus).
- `CSF_franklin_spc.tsv` maps the 26 `Anahita_*` result columns, and
  `WF312082_matrix.xlsx` maps the 20 `CK_*` columns; both are abundance
  matrices, not phenotype or donor maps.

## Blocking ambiguity

The paper gives aggregate CSF groups (pneumococcal, neuroborreliosis, viral,
and subarachnoid hemorrhage) but has no filename/sample-alias crosswalk.  It
also describes several in-vitro and rat experiments without connecting their
conditions to the `CK_*` and `TM_*` raw series.  Assigning organisms, disease,
or treatment groups to these raw files would therefore be invented.

A submitted sample sheet or author-provided crosswalk covering all three run
families is required before an SDRF can be produced.
