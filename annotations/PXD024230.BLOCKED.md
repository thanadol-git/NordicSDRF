# PXD024230 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: Mass spectrometric analysis of glycosaminoglycan-protein linkage regions

Publication: Persson A et al., Glycobiology 2021, DOI 10.1093/glycob/cwab035

## Evidence collected

- 20 Orbitrap Fusion/Q Exactive HF RAW files with deposited result filenames that partially indicate MIN6, NIT-1, INS-1 832/13, human islets, cell lysate, or conditioned medium
- The publication reports three human-islet preparations with 80%, 52%, and 40% islet purity, but four human-islet RAW runs and no run-to-preparation mapping
- Chondroitinase ABC and heparinase were applied during technical GAG depolymerization after enrichment, not as biological treatments
- The defining searches include residual GAG structures at 993.2809, 1073.2377, 1153.1945, 614.169429, and 694.135760 u; these have no stable controlled-vocabulary accessions accepted by the strict SDRF modification field
- One deposited result association for `Fusion_180521_30.raw` conflicts between media/ChABC and cells

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

The deposit does not map the human-islet runs to the three biological preparations, so run basenames cannot safely stand in for biological sources. The enzyme steps are technical processing rather than experimental factors, and omitting the study-defining residual GAG search modifications would make the technical annotation materially incomplete.

## What would unblock it

A RAW-to-biological-source/sample sheet (including the three islet preparations and the ambiguous Fusion run), a structured search-parameter export, and either accepted MOD/UNIMOD accessions or an SDRF extension capable of representing the five residual GAG modifications.
