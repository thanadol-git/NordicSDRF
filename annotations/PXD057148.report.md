## Limitations and caveats

- PRIDE lists a single 47.6 GB LCMS_file.zip; the 9 .d names come from the DIA-NN log, not the zip listing.
- Interpretation of NT (non-transfected control) and ETOH (ethanol vehicle) is inferred from names; NT_ETOH 3474 and 3475 are both named IP_1 but in different slots and were treated as two biological replicates.
- Acquisition method written as plain `Data-independent acquisition`: the validator rejects the `NT=;AC=` forms (PRIDE:0000450 and diaPASEF PRIDE:0000650) even though the template documents them; diaPASEF is kept in `comment[dia method]`.
- Organ part kidney and sex female come from Cellosaurus (HEK293, CVCL_0045), not the deposit. Age, passage, medium, storage not stated.
- DIA-NN search used a mouse reviewed FASTA although the cells are human; not captured in SDRF, flagged for submitter.
- Only DIA-NN modifications (Carbamidomethyl fixed) listed. Dissociation, collision energy (ramp 20-59 eV) and reduction reagent set to `not available`. Alkylation reagent iodoacetamide inferred from the PRIDE PTM string.
- Whole-cell lysate DDA samples mentioned in the protocol are not in this deposit (PARTIAL).
