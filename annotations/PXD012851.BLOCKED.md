# PXD012851 — blocked

## Exact blocker

The 27 RAW filenames and project protocol identify solvent conditions, three negative controls, and paired most-intense/least-intense acquisition runs, but no checked source maps the coded positive-sample prefixes (`18`, `20`, `21`, `23`, `24`, `25`, `26`, `27`, and `28`) or the first-session runs (`1`, `2`, and `3`) to biological source material and technical-replicate relationships. It is therefore not possible to determine whether those prefixes are separate CSF donors, aliquots of one pool, or replicate preparations. Assigning source names, donor identities, diagnoses, or replicate links from filename order would be inference.

## Evidence checked

- [PRIDE project PXD012851](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD012851): the protocol states that human CSF was analyzed in two sessions. The first used three samples dissolved in 0.1% formic acid; the second used triplicate positive samples and single negative controls for each of 50 mM ammonium bicarbonate, 0.1% formic acid, and 20% acetonitrile, with most-intense and least-intense acquisitions. It does not identify the biological source behind any coded filename.
- [Publication DOI 10.1186/s13195-019-0533-9](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13195-019-0533-9), PMCID PMC6745076: the full article, article XML, and six supplementary files were checked. They describe the discovery workflow and a later targeted clinical study, but provide no RAW-to-source or discovery-sample alias table. The clinical disease groups cannot safely be assigned to these discovery RAW files.
- Complete [PRIDE file inventory](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/projects/PXD012851/files): all 27 RAW files were enumerated. Filenames encode solvent (`FA`, `ABC`, or `ACN`), negative controls (`Neg`), and acquisition mode (`Most` or `Least`), but not an unambiguous biological source.
- Deposited `20NegACN.pep.xml`, `22NegFA.pep.xml`, and `24NegABC.pep.xml` were checked. They corroborate the three negative-control aliases but contain no positive-sample source map.
- No deposited sample sheet, study-design export, or other compact design-bearing file was present in the PRIDE inventory.

## What would resolve it

The original sample sheet or study-design export mapping every RAW filename to its biological source or pool, preparation replicate, solvent condition, and technical acquisition pair, including an explicit explanation of the numeric aliases. If the material was pooled CSF, that must be stated explicitly; if it represented individual donors, the donor-to-run map is required.
