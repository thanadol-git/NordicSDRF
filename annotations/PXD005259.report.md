# PXD005259
Status: validated annotation; independent review pending.

**Dataset:** PXD005259, "Proteomics of Diverse Clonal Groups of Enterotoxigenic Escherichia coli Underline Variations in Surface Antigens and Similarities in Metabolism". Gade Research Group for Infection and Immunity, Department of Clinical Science, University of Bergen, Norway (PI Harald G. Wiker; submitter Veronika Kucharova/Pettersen); LC-MS/MS at the Proteomics Unit at the University of Bergen (PROBE). Paper: Pettersen VK, Steinsland H, Wiker HG, J Proteome Res 2017;17(1):325-336, PMID 29185342, doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.7b00593 (closed access; only the abstract was readable).

Label-free comparison of 7 human ETEC strains (5 lineages) against the non-pathogenic E. coli B strain BL21(DE3), with 3-4 biological replicate cultures per strain. Each culture lysate was digested by MED-FASP: a Lys-C digest and then a tryptic digest of the same filter, each injected separately. MaxQuant combined the two digests of each culture as fractions 1 and 2 of one experiment.

- **SDRF:** `NordicSDRF/annotations/PXD005259.sdrf.tsv`
- **Rows x columns:** 54 x 33. There are 27 source names (cultures), each with one `_L` row (fraction 1, Lys-C/P) and one `_T` row (fraction 2, Trypsin/P).
- **SHA-256:** `23f605fa0f0bc645346631611b9fd748dc24603622b86b34e9278249be36bbde`

## Templates
- `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 only. This is MS-based shotgun proteomics (DDA, label-free). The organisms are bacterial isolates, and no organism template fits bacteria (`human`, `vertebrates`, `invertebrates` and `plants` do not apply). These are pure cultures, not a microbial community, so `metaproteomics` does not apply either. This follows the campaign rule for bacteria. No `cell-lines`, `dia-acquisition` or clinical template applies.

How the mapping was established. It comes from the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt` (inside `MaxQuantOutput.zip`), whose columns `Raw file`, `Experiment`, `Fraction` and `Enzyme` agree with the deposited file names. Every `<strain>_L<n>` / `<strain>_T<n>` pair is one MaxQuant experiment, for example `BL21_L1`/`BL21_T1` -> `B-1` and `TW10598_L1`/`TW10598_T1` -> `5-1`. `_L` is always Fraction 1 with LysC/P, and `_T` is always Fraction 2 with Trypsin/P.

I cross-checked the SDRF row by row against `summary.txt`. All 54 runs agree on fraction and enzyme, and the 27 experiments map one-to-one onto the 27 source names. The raw-file headers show that both runs of each culture were acquired in the same instrument session. All 54 raw files in the deposit are claimed exactly once. The 6 FASTA files and `MaxQuantOutput.zip` are not runs.

## Technical values (source)
- Instrument `NT=Orbitrap Elite;AC=MS:1001910`. Source: PRIDE `instruments` (LTQ Orbitrap Elite) and the raw headers of BL21_T1, H608_L1 and H104_L1, which read "Orbitrap Elite MS". The OLS label for MS:1001910 is "Orbitrap Elite".
- Cleavage agent `NT=Lys-C/P;AC=MS:1001310` on `_L` rows and `NT=Trypsin/P;AC=MS:1001313` on `_T` rows. The deposited search's per-file `Enzyme` is `LysC/P` and `Trypsin/P`. The PRIDE protocol agrees: "enzyme specificity was defined in group-specific parameters as either to trypsin or LysC, allowing N-terminal cleavage to proline". The `/P` terms are therefore the right ones for both enzymes, and plain Lys-C/Trypsin would describe a different specificity from the one searched.
- Modifications. The variable modifications come from `summary.txt` `Variable modifications`: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Acetyl (K), Gln->pyro-Glu and Glu->pyro-Glu. The fixed modification, Carbamidomethyl (C), comes from the PRIDE data processing protocol, because MaxQuant 1.5 `summary.txt` has no fixed-modification column.
- Tolerances 20 ppm precursor and 0.5 Da fragment, from the PRIDE data processing protocol (MaxQuant 1.5.3.30).
- DDA, CID, ion-trap MS2 and MS1 300-2000 m/z, from the PRIDE sample processing protocol (Orbitrap survey at R = 240,000, top-10 CID in the linear ion trap).
- Collision energy `35 NCE`. All 54 raw headers name an instrument method `..._240K_10minDelay_Trig1000ctns_195minRUN_CE35_Preview...meth`: 48 use `04_TOP10CID_...`, and the 6 runs of the January 2014 session use `TOP10_Labelfree_...`, both with CE35. This is read from the method name, not from the protocol prose.
- `comment[lc batch]` is the instrument session folder recorded in each raw file's header, for example `2014-05-20_OrbitrapElite_P14-13`. There are 8 sessions between January 2014 and February 2015. Strains are spread across them, and both runs of a culture always share a session.

## Build and the one post-build step
- `sdrf-tools build --samples samples.tsv --technical technical.tsv --files files.json -t ms-proteomics` produced 54 rows x 33 columns. Each `samples.tsv` row is one culture with `files` = `<strain>_L<n>.raw,<strain>_T<n>.raw`, so `build` set fraction 1 on `_L` and fraction 2 on `_T`.
- `build` writes one value per sample row, so it put the `technical.tsv` cleavage agent (Lys-C/P) on both digests. `scratchpad/PXD005259/fill_cleavage.py` then replaced only `comment[cleavage agent details]` on the 27 `_T` rows with Trypsin/P. Before writing, it asserts three things: every `_L` row is fraction 1, every `_T` row is fraction 2, and no other cell differs from the `build` output. A cell-level diff against the `build` output confirmed 27 changed cells, all in that column. This is the same approach as the sibling Bergen MED-FASP deposit PXD002912.

- `timeout 900 parse_sdrf validate-sdrf -s PXD005259.sdrf.tsv -t ms-proteomics --use_ols_cache_only`: "Everything seems to be fine. Well done." (0 errors, 0 warnings).
- `sdrf-tools structure`: Structure OK. `sdrf-tools check --offline`: CLEAN (12 terms checked; no unverified, mislabelled or swapped terms).
- `sdrf-tools reconcile --record pride.json --accession PXD005259` returned 2 BLOCKER `cleavage_agent_contradicted` findings ('Lys-C/P' and 'Trypsin/P' versus the record's 'Trypsin, Lys-C') and 1 MAJOR `cleavage_agent_incomplete` finding ("missing: Trypsin, Lys-C"). All three are lexical. The record names both enzymes, and both are declared, as their `/P` forms. The proline rule waived by `/P` is stated in the record and recorded in the deposited search. The checker reports "missing: Trypsin, Lys-C" even though `Trypsin/P` and `Lys-C/P` are both present, which shows it is matching names, not enzymes.
- OLS (live) confirmed every accession used: MS:1001910 Orbitrap Elite, MS:1001310 Lys-C/P, MS:1001313 Trypsin/P, MS:1000133 collision-induced dissociation, MS:1000264 ion trap, PRIDE:0000627 Data-dependent acquisition, NCBITaxon:562 Escherichia coli, UNIMOD:4/35/1/28/27.

## Repair after independent review (round 1)
- **Important: the enzyme split misrepresented the design.** This was accepted. The two split files (`PXD005259-lysc`, `PXD005259-trypsin`) are replaced by this single SDRF. Each culture now has its `_L` row as fraction 1 and its `_T` row as fraction 2, as in `summary.txt`. The split files and their review reports were deleted.
- **Minor: H608 aliases.** These are now documented below.
- **Minor: H104 replicate-2 internal names.** These are now documented below.
- **Minor: collision energy.** `35 NCE` was added after confirming CE35 in all 54 raw headers.

## Limitations
- **The paper is closed access.** Europe PMC and Unpaywall report it closed, and the ACS page returned 403. Only the abstract was read. Every value comes from the PRIDE record, the deposited MaxQuant `summary.txt`, the deposited FASTA names and the raw-file headers.
- **`H10407` is inferred from the file stem `H104`.** The deposited files and MaxQuant experiments say `H104`. The full name comes from the PRIDE keyword "H10407", the deposited FASTA `H10407_AA.fas`, and H10407 being the reference ETEC strain the group profiled earlier (Pettersen et al. 2015, cited in the protocol). This is a strong inference, but no table states it.
- **H104 replicate 2 has different internal names.** In the raw headers, `H104_L2.raw` and `H104_T2.raw` carry the acquisition names `LH104_1.raw` and `TH104_1.raw`. The deposited file names and the MaxQuant experiment (`H104-2`, Fraction 1/2) both say replicate 2, and the session folder (`2015-01-23_OrbitrapElite_Unknown`) differs from those of replicates 1 (`2014-11-07`) and 3 (`2015-02-02`). The deposited and MaxQuant numbering is used, and the internal `_1` is treated as a per-session counter.
- **`H608` is the depositor's label, not a verified strain designation.** The aliases are `HA608` in MaxQuant (`summary.txt` raw files `HA608_L<n>`/`HA608_T<n>`, experiments `HA-1..HA-3`) and `VK_608L.raw`/`VK_608T.raw` as the replicate-1 raw acquisition names; replicates 2 and 3 were acquired as `H608_L.raw`/`H608_T.raw`. No NCBI BioSample/nuccore record and no deposited FASTA matches any of these names, so its official name, clonal lineage and serotype are not established. The abstract's count of 7 ETEC strains plus BL21(DE3) shows it is the seventh ETEC strain.
- `BL21(DE3)`: the PRIDE description says "E. coli B strain BL21". The published abstract says "BL21(DE3)", and the more specific published name was used.
- `characteristics[organism]` = `Escherichia coli` (NCBITaxon:562) on every row, for consistency. Strain-level taxa exist only for H10407 (NCBITaxon:316401) and BL21(DE3) (NCBITaxon:469008), so the strain is carried in `characteristics[strain or breed]` instead.
- `characteristics[disease]` = `not applicable`. These are pure bacterial cultures. PRIDE's "diarrhea" is the human disease ETEC causes, not a property of the sample, and BL21(DE3) is non-pathogenic, so broadcasting it would be wrong on 8 rows. `organism part` and `cell type` are also `not applicable` for bacterial cultures.
- The ETEC lineage and colonization-factor profile per strain are in the paper but were not readable, so they are not annotated. The culture conditions (sheep blood agar, 37 °C, about 16 h) are the same for all samples and are not captured as a column.
- Reduction and alkylation reagents are not stated in the readable record. The lysis is "as previously described" and the paper is closed, so the optional columns are omitted. The Carbamidomethyl fixed modification shows alkylation was searched for.
- `comment[collision energy]` comes from the instrument method name in the raw headers, not from scan-level metadata.
- `comment[lc batch]` is the session folder name read from the raw headers. It is evidence of when runs were acquired, not a documented preparation batch. The paper's statistics do not model it, and it is given for reuse only.
- `comment[technical replicate]` = 1 on all rows, because there are no repeated injections.
