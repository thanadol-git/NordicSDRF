## Key decisions
- **Partial on purpose:** LFQ is sandbox-ready; TMT left out (Copenhagen mixed-deposit pattern). Do not invent TMT channels.
- **One source per RAW:** each FACS gate is a distinct prepared sample even when from the same mouse.
- **Cell type:** `Purkinje cell` only for YFP-high; Low/Neg = `not available` (debris / mixed non-PN).
- **Disease `normal`:** proteomics ages are 3–8 weeks; calbindin loss starts ~12 weeks.
- **Sex `not available`:** proteomics cohort sex not stated (surgery methods mention males and females generally).
- **Fragment tolerance 0.5 Da** from MQ ITMS settings (paper: fragments in the ion trap). Precursor **4.5 ppm** from MaxQuant `mqpar.xml` `mainSearchTol` (recalibrated).
- **Instrument Orbitrap Fusion** for LFQ (Lumos + FAIMS is the TMT arm only).

## Excluded / not deposited
- TMT astrocytes: 32 RAW; R archive has `Experimental_design.csv` with Reporter intensity 2–9 → CTRL/KO only (no channels 1/10, no age). Still insufficient for faithful multiplex SDRF without further tables; left blocked.
- Some MQ-searched LFQ RAW in `summary.txt` (e.g. EM264, EM256 Ctrl) are **not** in the PRIDE file list; only the 58 deposited LFQ RAW are annotated.
