## Key decisions
12-week cerebral artery runs come in two series, CN and CO, per animal number; the sheet labels both only 'SHR/WKY 12-weeks' and does not say whether CN and CO are the same animals (two preparations) or two cohorts. Rows therefore use distinct source names per series, individual 'not available', series code in comment[sample group code]. CY = 6-week cerebral arteries. Instrument recorded as timsTOF Pro (PRIDE: 'timsTOF'; not verifiable inside .rar). TCEP/CAA, Trypsin/Lys-C mix, FASP per record; DIA-NN defaults for mods. diaPASEF -> CID convention.
