# PXD020756 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Proteomic analysis of dexamethasone effects on cartilage breakdown
- **PRIDE record:** PXD020756

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE describes a bovine cartilage-explant model of post-traumatic osteoarthritis with cytokine treatment, injury, and dexamethasone interventions.
- The deposit contains 98 RAW files whose names encode experiment, bovine preparation, acquisition date, and plate-style positions (for example, `150911_exp24_bov1_C12.raw`).
- The submitted `OAC_open_Exp24_all_area.xlsx` defines abundance columns for internal sample IDs such as `F1`–`F121`, including control, cytokine, injury, dexamethasone, and combination arms across bovine preparations.
- The workbook does not map those `F` IDs (or conditions) to the deposited RAW filenames and there are 121 workbook sample columns versus 98 RAW files.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The public workbook establishes the study design but lacks the required crosswalk from each RAW filename to its internal sample ID and condition. Matching plate-style filenames to `F` IDs would be an unsupported inference.

## Source artifacts

- `scratchpad/PXD020756/evidence/pride.json`

## Unblocking requirement

The experiment manifest linking every deposited RAW file to its `F` sample ID, bovine preparation, treatment arm, and biological/technical replicate.
