## Why the deposit is split into two SDRFs

The deposit holds two independent sub-experiments with different designs and searches:

- TMT: 5 condition pools, TMT6plex, mixed-mode fractionation, 15 cm column, Proteome Discoverer 1.4 with SEQUEST + MS Amanda, fragment tolerance 0.05 Da, TMT6plex fixed modifications, CID+HCD.
- Label-free: 34 individual mice, Progenesis + SearchGUI/X!Tandem, fragment tolerance 0.7, CID only.

A single file would force one modification set and one tolerance on every row, so each sub-experiment gets its own SDRF.

## Design and file mapping

- Mice: female C57BL/6. CPZ-42d had a 0.2% cuprizone diet for 6 weeks, with matched controls (CTR-CPZ). EAE was induced with rhMOG1-125, and mice were sacrificed at day 16 (EAE-16d) or day 32 (EAE-32d) post-immunisation, with matched controls (CTR-EAE). Tissue was the frontal cortex of the right hemisphere.
- For TMT, equal protein from the individual animals of each condition was pooled into one channel per condition. The 5 channels were combined 1:1:1:1:1. TMT131 was not used.
- Channel map (source of truth: the deposited `Sampleoverviewlabel-freeandTMT.xlsx`, sheet "TMT"):

| channel | source name | animals pooled (`characteristics[pooled sample]`) |
|---|---|---|
| TMT126 | pool CTR-CPZ | CPZ_C1_ctr, C3, C4, C5, C6 |
| TMT127 | pool CPZ-42d | CPZ_A4, A5, B4, B5, B6 |
| TMT128 | pool CTR-EAE | EAE_A1/C1/D1/E1/F1/B1_ctr |
| TMT129 | pool EAE-16d | EAE_A3, B3, C3, D3, E3, F3 |
| TMT130 | pool EAE-32d | EAE_A4, B4, C4, D4, E4, F4 |

- Fraction runs: the TMT mix was fractionated into 60 mixed-mode fractions. Fractions 1–3 and 7–9 were pooled, and 4–6 were discarded. That gives 53 RAW files, `CPZ_EAE_TMT_MP1_3`, `MP7_9`, `MP10` … `MP60`, all deposited. `comment[fraction identifier]` runs 1–53 in that order (MP1_3 = 1, MP7_9 = 2, MP10 = 3 … MP60 = 53), with technical replicate 1.
- Unfractionated runs: 10 files (`TOP10_*`, `TOP7_*`) are described in the deposited sheet as "Unfractioned sample, parameters optimization 1–10". Each one is a separate injection of the unfractionated TMT mix, annotated as technical replicates 2–11 (in the sheet's order) with fraction identifier 1 and `comment[fractionation method]` = `not applicable`.
- Rows: 5 channels × 63 files = 315. Every TMT RAW file in the PRIDE file list (102 files over 2 API pages) is claimed exactly once. The other 34 RAW files belong to the label-free SDRF. The 5 xlsx result files are not data files.
- Factors: `factor value[disease]` (normal / demyelinating disease / experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis) and `factor value[sampling time]` (42 day / not available / 16 day / 32 day). Together they separate the five conditions: CTR-CPZ = normal + 42 day; CTR-EAE = normal + not available; CPZ-42d = demyelinating disease + 42 day; EAE-16d = EAE + 16 day; EAE-32d = EAE + 32 day.

## Technical values

- Instrument: Orbitrap Velos Pro (MS:1003096). Methods and Supplementary Methods say "LTQ-Orbitrap Velos Pro". The PRIDE dropdown lists LTQ Orbitrap Velos, but the Methods are more specific.
- Acquisition: DDA (Supplementary Methods: "operated in the DDA-mode"). Cleavage agent: Trypsin.
- Modifications, tolerances and enzyme come from the PRIDE data-processing protocol and Supplementary Methods: TMT6plex (K) fixed, TMT6plex (any N-term) fixed, Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed, Oxidation (M) variable; 10 ppm precursor tolerance, 0.05 Da fragment tolerance. The deposited TMT result tables are Excel exports from Proteome Discoverer. They contain no search settings, and no `.msf` was deposited, so the Methods are the best available source.
- Reduction: TCEP (Methods: "the TCEP reduction was done at RT"). Alkylation: `not available` (see Limitations).
- Fractionation: `High-performance liquid chromatography` (PRIDE:0000565) for the 53 fraction runs. Mixed-mode HPLC on a Sielc Promix column has no more specific PRIDE child.

## Limitations

- **TMT channel map conflict.** Three of the authors' own artifacts give one map, and the Methods prose gives another. The map used here comes from the deposited `Sampleoverviewlabel-freeandTMT.xlsx` (sheet "TMT": 126 = CTR-CPZ, 127 = CPZ-42d, 128 = CTR-EAE, 129 = EAE-16d, 130 = EAE-32d). The paper's Supplementary Data 1 (`41598_2021_86191_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx`, sheet "TMT raw data files") repeats it word for word. But the paper's Methods and the PRIDE sample-processing protocol both say: CTR-CPZ = TMT130, CTR-EAE = TMT127, CPZ-42d = TMT126, EAE-16d = TMT128, EAE-32d = TMT129.
  - The sheet's map was chosen because the deposited quantification itself cross-validates it. The Proteome Discoverer protein and peptide exports (`TMT6plex_EAE_CPZ_PDun-normalized_proteins.xlsx`, `TMT_EAECPZ_PDun-normalized_peptides.xlsx`) report exactly three ratios: 127/126, 129/128 and 130/128. Supplementary Data 1 labels its three TMT ratio columns "CPZ-42d / CTR-CPZ", "EAE-16d / CTR-EAE" and "EAE-32d / CTR-EAE".
  - Under the sheet's map those PD ratios are exactly disease/control: 127/126 = CPZ/CTR-CPZ, 129/128 = EAE-16d/CTR-EAE, 130/128 = EAE-32d/CTR-EAE. Under the Methods' map they would be CTR-EAE/CPZ, EAE-32d/EAE-16d and CTR-CPZ/EAE-16d, which are not the study's comparisons.
  - If the Methods prose is right instead, every channel identity here is wrong. A reviewer should weigh this; the authors could confirm it.
- **CTR-EAE timepoint and count.** The paper says healthy EAE controls (N = 5) were sacrificed at p.i. day 32, and the label-free Methods say CTR-EAE N = 5. Both deposited sheets and Supplementary Data 1 and 2 instead list 6 animals ("CTR for EAE, 36 days"), and 6 `EAE_*1_ctr` RAW files exist. The TMT pool is annotated with the 6 animals the sheet lists, and `sampling time` is `not available` for CTR-EAE because day 32 and day 36 conflict.
- **Sampling time reference differs by model.** CPZ and CTR-CPZ count days on diet (6 weeks = 42 days). EAE counts days post-immunisation. The column holds the number of days only.
- **Disease for cuprizone mice.** `demyelinating disease` (MONDO:0002562) is used for CPZ-42d. Cuprizone causes toxic demyelination, which the paper confirmed histologically (Supplementary Fig. 1). No ontology term exists for "cuprizone-induced demyelination", and cuprizone has no NCIT/EFO term for `characteristics[treatment]`, so no treatment column is written. The PRIDE dropdown disease "multiple sclerosis" was not used: these are mouse models, not MS.
- **Developmental stage `adult`** is inferred. Methods say CPZ and EAE were induced in eight-week-old female mice, and the CPZ controls came from the same randomly assigned group of 10. The EAE controls' age is not stated; they are assumed to belong to the same cohort described under "Mice".
- **Alkylation reagent `not available`.** The Methods say the TMT samples were prepared "as outlined in the manual for the TMTsixplex Isobaric Label Reagent Set". That kit manual uses iodoacetamide, and the search has Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed, but no primary source of this study names the reagent.
- **No dissociation method, collision energy or MS2 analyzer column.** The 53 fraction runs used back-to-back CID (ion trap, NCE 35%) and HCD (Orbitrap, NCE 40%) on the same precursors. `comment[dissociation method]` is single-valued in the current contract, so `build` cannot emit both. The optimisation runs varied the method (the `TOP10_HCD_*` and `TOP7_HCD_*` names suggest HCD only, `TOP7_CID*HCD*` both), and their settings are documented only through file names.
- **Optimisation runs as technical replicates.** The 10 unfractionated runs are annotated as technical replicates 2–11 of each pool. They are re-injections of the same TMT mix with different MS settings, not identical replicates. The paper does not say whether they entered the Proteome Discoverer search.
- **Biological replicate** is 1 for every pool (one pool per condition); pool membership is in `characteristics[pooled sample]`.
- **Sex** (female), **strain** (C57BL/6) and **organism part** (frontal cortex) come from the paper's Methods ("Female C57Bl/6 mice"; "frontal cortex of the right hemisphere anterior to bregma + 1.0 mm").
- Raw files were not opened (no techsdrf run). Instrument, acquisition mode and tolerances come from the Methods, not from the RAW headers.
