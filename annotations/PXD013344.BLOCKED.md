# PXD013344 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Proteome of fluid from human small antral follicles reveals insights in folliculogenesis and oocyte maturation
- **PRIDE record:** PXD013344
- **Publication:** Pla *et al.*, *Human Reproduction* (2021), DOI [10.1093/humrep/deaa335](https://doi.org/10.1093/humrep/deaa335), PMID [33313811](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33313811/), PMCID [PMC7891813](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7891813/)

## Evidence recovered

- PRIDE contains 99 RAW files with several distinct workflows, including pooled and individual follicular-fluid samples, depleted and non-depleted preparations, SCX and basic-RP fractions, and targeted runs.
- The paper establishes that pooled material came from 15 follicles, 10 follicles were individually analysed, and a further paired oocyte-maturation experiment used two or three follicles from each of six women.
- The filename prefixes distinguish several workflow families (for example, `FF-IND_LF`, `FF-IND_DepLF`, `FF_SCX`, `FF_FASP_RP`, and pooled/depleted-pool runs), but no public source maps individual raw basenames to the patient, follicle, pool composition, oocyte maturity outcome, or paired woman.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

An SDRF for this mixed study needs a source-supported map for each raw file, including the biological origin and processing branch.  The available methods describe group-level design only.  Inferring sample identities from the `S`/`F`/`RP` numbers or treating all 99 files as equivalent would lose the study's biological and technical structure.

## Source artifacts

- `scratchpad/PXD013344/evidence/pride.json`
- `scratchpad/PXD013344/evidence/files.json`

## Unblocking requirement

An author-provided sample manifest that maps every raw basename to follicle/pool, donor or paired participant, oocyte maturity outcome where applicable, depletion/fractionation branch, and technical replicate.
