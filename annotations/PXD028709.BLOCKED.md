# PXD028709 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Mapping the Melanoma Plasma Proteome (MPP) using Single-Shot Proteomics and Custom Protein Databases
- **PRIDE record:** PXD028709
- **Publication:** Almeida N. et al., Cancers 2021, DOI [10.3390/cancers13246224](https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers13246224), PMCID [PMC8699267](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8699267/)

## Evidence recovered

- 28 RAW files (Q Exactive HF-X, verified in RAW headers) plus two Proteome Discoverer .msf files (`…PoolDep_DepSuperMix_10PlasmaMMsamples…`, `…10POOLPlasmaMM…`).
- Ten runs `PlasmaMM_SUDnnn_DDA_R1` match the ten early-stage melanoma patients of Table 2 by number (PTP007, 027, 028, 029, 039, 044, 048, 050, 054, 068), with age, sex and stage per patient.
- The other 18 runs are `PlasmaMM_POOL1/2/3` (10 runs), `H14` (3), `PlasmaDepMM_Pool` (3) and `PlasmaDepMM_SuperMix` (2).
- Methods: the custom database was built from a pool of plasma from healthy individuals (n = 30) depleted with human-7 (top7), human-14 and Seppro SuperMix columns, plus undepleted pools; a separate pool of 57 melanoma patients was analysed undepleted.
- Supplementary tables S1–S9 (MDPI zip via Europe PMC) report proteins per depletion level and per 'MM pool' / 'MM primary tumor patients', never per RAW file.

## Why no SDRF was emitted

18 of 28 RAW files are pools whose identity is not recorded anywhere: POOL1, POOL2 and POOL3 could be the healthy (n = 30) or the melanoma (n = 57) pool, `PlasmaDepMM_Pool` could be the top7-depleted pool or a depleted melanoma pool, and the `MM` token in the SuperMix file names contradicts the Methods, which say the healthy pool was depleted. Disease, pool composition and depletion method therefore cannot be assigned to those rows, and a partial SDRF of the ten patient runs would drop most of the deposition.

## Unblocking requirement

A file-to-sample table from the authors stating, for each POOL1/2/3, H14, PlasmaDepMM_Pool and PlasmaDepMM_SuperMix run, the source pool (healthy n = 30 or melanoma n = 57) and the depletion (none, top7, top14, SuperMix). With that, the ten patient runs (SUDnnn ↔ PTPnnn) and the pools can be annotated from the evidence already collected.
