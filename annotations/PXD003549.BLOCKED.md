# PXD003549 — annotation blocked

Triple-SILAC study of mRNA-binding proteins (UV cross-linking and oligo(dT) capture) and input extracts in HeLa cells after methyl methanesulfonate (MMS). Slupphaug lab, NTNU. Paper: J Transl Med 2021, PMC8254245 (open access).

## What is known
- **Labels** (paper): light = Lys0/Arg0, medium = Lys4 (2H4)/Arg6, heavy = Lys8/Arg10.
- **Mixes:** two triple-SILAC mixes, (no-UV / PBS / 0 h MMS) and (PBS / 4 h MMS / 15 h MMS), across three biological replicates "with label swap".
- **Fractions:** input extracts were cut into 5 gel slices (`Ext*_1–5`) and oligo(dT) eluates into 3 (`IP*-1–3`).
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Elite, from the raw header; CID.
- **Search:** PD 2.0 (Mascot/SEQUEST), 10 ppm / 0.6 Da, Carbamidomethyl C fixed.

## Why it is blocked
- **Channel orientation:** which condition is in which channel is never given, and the label-swap experiments mean it differs between replicates. Each of the 3 channels per run therefore cannot be assigned a treatment.
- **Mix and replicate mapping:** it is not stated which mix is `Ext1`/`IP1` and which is `Ext2`/`IP2`, or how the run batches map to the three biological replicates. The batches are 140801 + 140805 B3, 20150714 B5/B6, 150213 and 150318.
- **Deposited MaxQuant output does not match:** `MaxQuantOutput.zip` (MaxQuant 1.5.2.8) uses different experiment names (`B1_IP1_120ms`, `B1_IP1_50ms`, … `B5_IP2`) that do not correspond to the deposited raw files.

## What would unblock it
A sample sheet from the authors giving, per raw file, the mix, the biological replicate and the condition in each of the L/M/H channels.
