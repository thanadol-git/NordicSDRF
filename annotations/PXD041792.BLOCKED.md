# PXD041792 — blocked: numbered RAW files do not encode the 2×2 BioID design

Status: blocked locally; no SDRF was produced.

## Evidence available

PRIDE title and description: BioID screen for proteins linked to human PHYHD1 in HEK293T cells transiently expressing **BirA\*** or **BirA\*-tagged PHYHD1**, each **with or without biotin** (2×2 = four conditions). Label-free DIA on a Q Exactive HF; on-bead trypsinolysis; Spectronaut DirectDIA (`20220628_100634_220627.sne`, ~510 MB — not downloaded).

Deposited RAW files (vendor names only):

- `220627_PHYHD1_1.raw`
- `220627_PHYHD1_2.raw`
- `220627_PHYHD1_3.raw`
- `220627_PHYHD1_4.raw`

## Blocking gap

The experimental variable is construct × biotin. Filenames are a shared date/bait stem plus a numeric suffix **1–4**. They do not say which run is BirA\* vs PHYHD1-BioID, nor biotin vs no biotin. Four files matching four conditions is not a map.

An SDRF needs a real `factor value[...]` for that comparison. Filling `factor value[construct]` / treatment as `not available` on every row, or treating 1–4 as `factor value[biological replicate]`, would either hide the design or invent that the numbers are replicates rather than the unmapped 2×2. Guessing file 1–4 → condition is not allowed.

## Unblock requirement

A sample sheet (or equivalent from the authors / paper supplement) that assigns each of `220627_PHYHD1_{1,2,3,4}.raw` to construct (BirA\* vs BirA\*-PHYHD1) and biotin (±) — and replicate if those are not one-file-per-condition.
