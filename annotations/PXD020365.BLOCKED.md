# PXD020365 — BLOCKED

PRIDE title: Spleen proteomics data from high fat diet fed mice

Publication: Svahn SL et al., Data in Brief 2020 32:106110, PMID 32904176, PMCID PMC7451803, DOI 10.1016/j.dib.2020.106110

## Evidence collected

- 87 LTQ Orbitrap Velos RAW files and four Proteome Discoverer `.msf` files
- Twelve C57BL/6J mice: four low-fat/chow, four saturated-fat HFD, and four polyunsaturated-fat HFD samples
- The paper states that a pooled reference and one sample from each diet were labeled in each of four iTRAQ4plex sets, each fractionated into 22 SCX fractions
- The publication and deposit do not map set/channel to mouse; one of the expected 88 fraction files is also absent

## Why this cannot be annotated yet

The four iTRAQ sets require 16 exact channel assignments. The group counts do not establish which mouse or diet occupies iTRAQ114–117 in each set, and RAW numbering identifies fractions rather than samples.

## What would unblock it

The four-set iTRAQ4 sample sheet (set and channel → diet and mouse replicate), plus confirmation of the intentionally missing fraction file.
