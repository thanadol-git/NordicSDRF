## How the sample map was recovered

The channel-to-sample map was recovered from the deposit, so nothing was invented. `MaxQuant_Output.zip` is 1.08 GB. Only its central directory and about 30 small members (about 1.2 MB in total) were read by HTTP range:

- **`Index_of_Raw_Data.xlsx`** maps 604 of the 612 RAW files to an experiment, a SILAC scheme (e.g. `L=control, H=A485 16h`; `L=control, M=30m, H=240m`) and a MaxQuant output folder.
- **Each folder's `summary.txt`** lists all 612 RAW files with Experiment, Multiplicity and Labels. It was used for the 8 files missing from the Index (`20171121_…CBP112-1_acK_SCX_1a…5`, MaxQuant experiment E2 in `MEF CBP112 Ac`).
- **Each folder's `parameters.txt`** gives the fixed modifications and the MS/MS tolerance.
- **File-name check.** The time-course channel times in the Index (`L-0_M-1h_H-8h`, `M-30m_H-4h`, `M-0-5h_H-1h`, `M-10m_H20m`) match the names of all 93 time-course RAW files. The `hs`/`mm` and `293FT`/`C8` tokens match the organisms.

- **Modifications** (`sdrf-tools search-params` on `summary.txt` and `parameters.txt`, per folder):

  - Variable modifications are Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term) and the SILAC label modifications. Acetyl (K) is added in every Ac folder and in `MEF Cmpd-R PROT`; the other PROT folders do not search it.

- **Fractionation method:** SCX (`PRIDE:0000561`) on 252 non-medium rows and HpH (`PRIDE:0000564`) on 436, set only when the file name contains `SCX` or `HpH`. The other 536 are `not available`.

## Judgement calls

1. **Map precedence.** The deposited MaxQuant experiment grouping (Index and `summary.txt`) is the authority for sample, condition and replicate. File-name `E#` tags are not used. They disagree with the deposited grouping for CBP112 (E1 holds files tagged E2) and for Kasumi-1.
2. **Ac and proteome sources.** Acetylome and proteome sources are kept separate, because the deposit does not say they share a lysate.
3. **Source granularity.** Each source is one MaxQuant experiment and one SILAC channel. Fractions are positional, because nominal fraction numbers (`F01–F10`, `a/b`, `SCX 1–5`, `HpH 1A–6A`) collide within an experiment.
4. **Cell lines.** The offline Cellosaurus lookup mislabelled two cell lines:
   - 293FT was fuzzy-matched to HEK293T. It was overridden with `CVCL_6911` (HEK293-FT, checked against the Cellosaurus API).
   - The Kasumi-1 disease came back as megakaryoblastic leukaemia. The paper says AML, so `acute myeloid leukemia` is used.
   - The engineered MEF clone has no Cellosaurus entry (`not available`).
5. **Mixed organism.** One organism template is allowed, so `vertebrates` plus `cell-lines` carries the 52 human RAW files too (Kasumi-1 and 293FT).
6. **Reserved words.** Where the paper is silent I used `not available`: strain, sex (except male for Kasumi-1), developmental stage, passage number, dissociation method, cell type (Kasumi-1 and 293FT), and MEF Cellosaurus identity. The precursor tolerance column was omitted rather than filled, because the converter rejects `not available` there.
7. **Label modifications.** They are listed as variable modifications, as `sdrf-tools search-params` reads them, and applied to all channels of a run.
8. **Control arms.** Inhibitor and KO experiments define the control arm only as `control`. The p300 in vitro "control" (heavy) arm is also undefined.
