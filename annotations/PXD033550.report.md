# PXD033550 annotation report

## Evidence used

- PRIDE metadata and protocol describe human hepatocyte spheroid proteomics, including label-free GW6471/vehicle comparison, a TMTpro 16-plex PISA experiment, and a six-channel TMT comparison acquired on a Q Exactive HF.
- Deposited `PridgeonCS_et_al_TMT-pro_Labelling.xlsx` explicitly maps every 16-plex channel to NXT629, MK886, DMSO, or GW6471 and replicate number.
- Deposited `20210827_HF2_CP_TMT6plex_Fractions.xlsx` maps the six TMT channels to DMSO or MK886; channel order within each group supplies replicate identifiers 1–3.
- The deposited fraction workbook identifies human protein results, supporting the organism assignment for the multiplexed acquisitions.

## Annotation scope

The SDRF represents all 61 deposited RAW files: 47 TMTpro fractions × 16 channels (752 assays), 12 TMT6 fractions × 6 channels (72 assays), and two label-free files, totalling 826 assay rows. The protocol describes 48 TMTpro fractions, but fraction 39 has no deposited RAW file and is not synthesized. The label-free raw filename contains `GW6451`, while the deposit’s protocol identifies the treatment as **GW6471**; the latter is used as the treatment factor and the filename itself is retained unchanged.
