# PXD014155 annotation report

**Status: ANNOTATED, split into two SDRFs (HeLa time course: `PXD014155.sdrf.tsv`; protein-mix runs: `PXD014155-proteinmix.sdrf.tsv`). Independent review: round 1 FAIL (3 important), fixed; round 2 on the fixed files, see "Review" at the end.**

## Study
"Evaluation of in-house stabilized trypsin" (PRIDE submitter Heissel; PI Hojrup, SDU). The PRIDE record has no reference; the matching paper is Heissel et al. 2019 PLoS One 14(6):e0218374 (PMC6597055), found by title/protocol/lab. Caveat: that paper cites PXD008193 and PXD013458 as its data IDs, not PXD014155, so the link rests on matching content (trypsin dimethylation, HeLa 58 C time course, Q Exactive HF, MaxQuant 1.5.3.30). Label-free DDA, Q Exactive HF.

## Files and templates
| File | Rows | Templates | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PXD014155.sdrf.tsv` | 30 x 40 | ms-proteomics v1.1.0, human v1.1.0, cell-lines v1.1.0 | `QHF1_*` HeLa lysate digests, 58 C |
| `PXD014155-proteinmix.sdrf.tsv` | 16 x 36 | ms-proteomics v1.1.0, vertebrates v1.1.0 | `QEHF*` simple protein mix (BSA, horse myoglobin, horse ADH, bovine CA) |

Why split (round-1 review finding): `cell-lines` makes `characteristics[cell line]` required with no reserved word allowed, so protein-mix runs cannot sit in the same file; `human` is the correct organism layer for HeLa. The protein mix has no human/cell sample, so ms-proteomics + vertebrates (organism `not applicable`) is the lightest fit; no organism-free organism template exists.

## Scope (deposited vs annotated)
Deposited: 46 RAW (30 QHF1, 16 QEHF) + 3 MaxQuant result zips (not runs). All 46 RAW annotated, none excluded. The deposit's design file lists `QHF1_07020`-`07029` (`DISCARD_*`) but those RAW files are not in PRIDE: no rows. Run list from PRIDE v3 `files/all` (all entries carry PXD014155).

## Key decisions
- HeLa mapping: `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt`/`summary.txt` in `Timecourse_Specific.zip` (07010-19 `BP`, 07030-39 `Met`, 07040-49 `Promega`; times 1/5/10/30/60 min; replicates 01/02 = biological replicate 1/2, paper: digestions in duplicate). Factors: `trypsin preparation`, `digestion time`.
- `Promega` = Promega sequencing grade trypsin (paper PSG). `BP` and `Met` kept as deposit labels, identity NOT asserted (review finding 3): the paper has crude, enhanced and PSG preparations but never says which label is which.
- Protein-mix runs: deposit only numbers them Experiment 1-16 and the paper gives no mapping to trypsin type/dose (2/4 %)/duration, so factor values are `not available`; each run is its own source (`Protein_mix_digest_01..16`), biological replicate 1 (the 16 may be conditions or replicates, unknown).
- Instrument Q Exactive HF (paper; RAW headers of one QHF1 and one QEHF file read 'Q Exactive HF'); PRIDE's `Q Exactive` overridden.
- Cell line: HeLa, CVCL_0030 via `sdrf-tools cellline lookup` (exact); age 30Y6M and sex female are Cellosaurus donor values, not stated in the paper; HeLa variant (parental vs S3) not stated.
- Modifications from deposited MaxQuant outputs. HeLa (v1.6.2.3): Carbamidomethyl (C) Fixed, Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term) Variable. parameters.txt lists no fixed mods and evidence.txt does not show fixed mods, but the round-1 reviewer measured +57.0215 Da per Cys on 3,001 Cys-containing peptides labelled Unmodified, proving carbamidomethyl was fixed (and the paper states IAA); my first draft wrongly omitted it. Protein mix (v1.5.3.30): Carbamidomethyl (C) Fixed; Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Carbamyl K, Carbamyl N-term (UNIMOD:5, `PP=Any N-term`) Variable. Variable lists are not in parameters.txt, so they come from evidence.txt (unobserved variable mods cannot be excluded).
- Cleavage agent Trypsin (MS:1001251); the "Unspecific" zip is a non-specific search for evaluating specificity only. Fragment tolerance 20 ppm; precursor tolerance and dissociation method `not available`. DTT/IAA from paper Methods.
- Reconcile was run against a scratch copy of the PRIDE record with the paper's digestion sentence appended (PRIDE text alone says trypsin was "dimethylated" and triggers a false `cleavage_agent_unsupported`): all values agree.

## `not available` / `not applicable`
- HeLa: organism part, cell type, disease, ancestry category, individual, developmental stage: not in paper (not available). (Cellosaurus disease/site were deliberately not imported.)
- Protein mix: organism, organism part, cell type, disease, developmental stage, strain, sex, material type: `not applicable` (protein standard). `sample type = standard`. Factor values, precursor tolerance, dissociation method: `not available`. (Review minor 4: `material type` changed from synthetic to not applicable.)

## Stated vs inferred
Stated: HeLa lysate, 58 C, time points, duplicates, DTT/IAA, instrument, DDA, protein-mix composition (also confirmed in evidence.txt proteins), Promega = PSG. Derived from data: Carbamidomethyl fixed (mass shift), variable mods (evidence.txt). From Cellosaurus: age, sex, accession. Not asserted: BP/Met identity.

## Validation
