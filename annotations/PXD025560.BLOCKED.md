# PXD025560 — BLOCKED

- **Evidence:** PRIDE identifies a human non-small-cell lung-cancer DIA validation cohort and deposits 100 RAW files. Each file encodes only an anonymized `sampleNNN` identifier; several are explicitly re-prepared or re-run acquisitions.
- **Why blocked:** The public deposit contains no sample sheet, clinical metadata table, or result file linking the anonymized sample identifiers to participant, tumour/control status, cohort subgroup, or original-versus-repeat acquisition. The study title alone cannot support the required source-to-assay mapping.
- **Unblocking requirement:** A deposited manifest mapping every `sampleNNN` identifier to its clinical sample and indicating which re-prepared/re-run files are technical repeats.
