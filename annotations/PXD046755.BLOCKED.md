# PXD046755 — annotation blocked

## Dataset

- **Title:** Insulin and Exercise-induced Phosphoproteomics of Human Skeletal Muscle Identify REPS1 as a New Regulator of Muscle Glucose Uptake
- **PRIDE record:** PXD046755
- **Publication:** Cell Rep Med 2025, doi 10.1016/j.xcrm.2025.102163

## Evidence recovered

Two TMT11 plexes (Plex1 = insulin clamp day, pre/post; Plex2 = exercise day, pre/post), 16 high-pH fractions each on an Orbitrap Eclipse (32 RAW); channel 131C = pooled reference. One timsTOF Pro 2 interactome archive (FLAG_CTR/FLAG_WT/IP_Basal/IgG_Basal x 6, per combined_protein.tsv).

## Why no SDRF was emitted

The subject and pre/post timepoint behind TMT channels 126-131N are not deposited: PhosphoSites.txt only has numbered reporter columns, and the protocol does not give the channel order. The interactome is deposited as a single .d.zip, while combined_protein.tsv lists 24 samples, so runs cannot be matched to samples without opening a 55 GB archive.

## Sources checked

PRIDE project record and protocols; PhosphoSites.txt header; combined_protein.tsv header; file list.

## Unblocking requirement

A channel-to-subject/timepoint table for both plexes (supplementary table of the paper or from the authors) and a list of the .d runs inside the interactome archive.
