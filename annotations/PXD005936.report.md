# PXD005936 annotation report

## Result

SDRF covering 24 deposited RAW files from two biological replicates of estrogen-receptor-beta antibody immunoprecipitation LC–MS/MS.

## Evidence

- PRIDE project: PXD005936, `Antibody validation in estrogen receptor beta research`.
- Submitter/PI: Margareta Ramström, Uppsala University, Sweden.
- Human HCT116erb and T47D mock cell lines; antibodies 1D5, PPZ0506, 14C8, and PPG5.
- The deposited `Samplesreplicate1.rtf` and `Samplesreplicate2.rtf` files provide the RAW-file-to-antibody, cell-line, molecular-weight-band, and replicate mapping.
- Affinity purification followed by in-gel tryptic digestion and data-dependent LC–MS/MS on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos Pro ETD.
- FTP archive: `https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2017/06/PXD005936/`.

## Conservative choices

No donor age, sex, or disease information was reported. Each file represents one gel-band acquisition; molecular-weight band, cell line, and antibody are retained as experimental factors.

Structural validation passes with `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf --sdrf_file annotations/PXD005936.sdrf.tsv --template ms-proteomics --template human --skip-ontology`.
