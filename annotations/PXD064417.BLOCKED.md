# PXD064417 — review blocked

The deposited `metadata.txt` maps all 22 RAW files to 21 woolly-rhinoceros enamel aliquots and one processing blank (`P0752`). The [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12804880/) confirms the decontamination design and no enzymatic digest. The deposited MaxQuant summary confirms unspecific cleavage and the variable deamidation (N,Q), oxidation (P), and phosphorylation (S,T,Y) searches.

The [22-row draft](../scratchpad/PXD064417/draft.sdrf.tsv) passes the official `ms-proteomics` plus `vertebrates` validator, structure check, and offline ontology check. It is not approved. The first independent review found missing modification values; those were added from the deposited MaxQuant output. The second independent review rejected `characteristics[biological replicate]=1` on the laboratory blank because it contains no biological specimen ([review report](../scratchpad/PXD064417/review2.json)). The current SDRF contract requires an integer in this column and does not permit `not applicable`, so the requested replacement cannot be written in a valid SDRF. The draft and [evidence manifest](../scratchpad/PXD064417/draft.evidence.json) remain in the accession scratchpad pending a schema-compatible blank convention or a documented decision on whether to exclude the blank from the contributed annotation.
