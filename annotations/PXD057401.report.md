# PXD057401 annotation report

Approved in two workflow-specific SDRFs covering all 59 PRIDE RAW files:

- `PXD057401-archaeological.sdrf.tsv`: 35 human tooth-enamel runs, 35 rows × 24 columns. The deposited Shiny output supplies one sex call per exact sample ID, including `21_3`; 20 male and 15 female calls. Templates: `ms-proteomics` and `human` v1.1.0. Official parser and independent hash-bound review PASS, SHA-256 `79b77068265dbd55e89515ee4e4b2ae7eb385759fd8567e562a44d9b40b3e0fe`.
- `PXD057401-standards.sdrf.tsv`: 24 synthetic peptide calibration runs, 24 rows × 19 columns, six levels A–F with four deposited RAW acquisitions each. Template: `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0. Official parser and independent hash-bound review PASS, SHA-256 `15a07f06efe4b64cdf68237a9503f3392537b5c7d7ce65d9b236028e1ca8ecec`.

The paper describes three repeats per standard, but PRIDE deposits four RAW runs per level. Skyline's processed output contains 23 of the 24 standard runs and omits `STD_F_1`; PRIDE lists that RAW run, so it remains in the standards SDRF. Skyline's analyte concentration values have no reported unit, so the SDRF records the level letters only. Per-individual age and archaeological context are not mapped in the deposited output. The generic reconcile helper flags `not applicable` cleavage despite direct acid extraction of enamel and pre-made standard peptides; no digest is indicated.

Sources: [PRIDE project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD057401), [paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12520-025-02205-7), deposited [Outputs.zip](https://ftp.pride.ebi.ac.uk/pride/data/archive/2025/04/PXD057401/Outputs.zip), and the adjacent evidence manifests.
