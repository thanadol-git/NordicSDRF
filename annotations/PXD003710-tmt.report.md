## Templates

`ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 + `vertebrates` v1.1.0. Mass-spectrometry proteomics of mouse (*Mus musculus*) brain tissue; DDA on an Orbitrap Elite. No cell line, no clinical or oncology metadata, not DIA.

The deposit was split into two SDRFs because the TMT and label-free arms differ in label, dissociation (HCD vs CID), MS2 analyzer, fragment tolerance and modifications, all of which are run-level constants in `build`.

## Design and file mapping

- 38 mice in six conditions: Peli1 KO or WT, sampled at 0 (before immunization), 10 and 20 days after MOG35-55 immunization (5/7/7 mice per genotype). These were pooled into 18 small pools (A/B/C per condition, 1-3 mice each), plus a reference pool of all 38 mice.
- One TMT10 kit was split across two 10-plexes: experiment 1 ("white") and experiment 2 ("grey"). Each holds 9 pools plus the reference in TMT131. Each plex was split into 60 mixed-mode HPLC fractions, with fractions 1-3 and 58-60 merged, giving 56 runs per plex.
- Rows: 2 plexes x 56 runs x 10 channels = 1120. All 112 TMT raw files are claimed, each with all 10 channels.
- Channel map, taken from Supplementary Methods Table 1 and cross-checked against the deposited workbook `ProteinexportandandstatisticsofTMTandLFdatasets_Peli1.xlsx` (the pool names above the `A6: <ch>/131` columns of sheets TMT_exp1_White and TMT_exp2_Grey). The two sources agree on all 20 channels (0 discrepancies):
  - Experiment 1 (`TMT10_Y*`): 126 KO 0 A, 127N KO 0 B, 127C KO 10 A, 128N KO 10 B, 128C KO 20 A, 129N KO 20 B, 129C WT 0 A, 130N WT 10 A, 130C WT 20 A, 131 reference.
  - Experiment 2 (`TMT10_B*`): 126 KO 0 C, 127N KO 10 C, 127C KO 20 C, 128N WT 0 B, 128C WT 10 B, 129N WT 20 B, 129C WT 0 C, 130N WT 10 C, 130C WT 20 C, 131 reference.
- Which file prefix belongs to which plex:
  - Every `TMT10_B*` raw header was acquired into folder `...TMT10-RRL-mousebrain-blue`, and every `TMT10_Y*` into `...mousebrain-yellow`.
  - In the workbook sheet "TMT Grey and White", the "TMT Blue experiment" block matches the grey sheet's statistics for 6262 proteins (white: 287). The "TMT Yellow experiment" block matches the white sheet for 6290 proteins (grey: 287).
  - So B = blue = grey = experiment 2, and Y = yellow = white = experiment 1.
- Factor values are `factor value[genotype]` (Peli1 knockout / wild type) and `factor value[sampling time]` (0 / 10 / 20 day). Both are `not applicable` on the reference channel.
- Source names are `TMT pool <KO|WT> <day> <A|B|C>`, with biological replicate 1/2/3 for A/B/C. The reference is `TMT reference pool of all 38 mice`, with biological replicate `pooled` and technical replicate 1 in experiment 1 and 2 in experiment 2.

## Limitations

- **Pool membership.** Which individual mice went into each pool is not deposited, only the count per pool. `characteristics[pooled sample]` is therefore `pooled` (2-3 mice) or `not pooled` (KO 0 C and WT 0 C contain one mouse each) rather than an `SN=` list, and TMT pools cannot be linked to the label-free individual mice.
- **B/Y-to-plex assignment.** Linking the raw folder names (blue/yellow) to the workbook's "TMT Blue/Yellow experiment" labels is an inference from the authors' own naming. It is numerically cross-checked (above), but not stated in a single document.
- **Fraction identifiers.** These are positional, 1-56. `TMT10_x1-3` is fraction identifier 1 (pooled HPLC fractions 1-3), and `TMT10_x58-60` is 56.
- **Sex, age, developmental stage.** The paper says only "age- and sex-matched"; sex and developmental stage are `not available`.
- **Organism part.** Encoded as `cerebral hemisphere`; the tissue was the frontal part of the right hemisphere.
- **Disease.** `normal` for the 0-day (non-immunized) pools and `experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis` (MONDO:0005134) at 10/20 dpi. The PRIDE entry "multiple sclerosis" was not used, because the mice carry the EAE model, not MS. The reference pool is `not applicable` (mixed).
- **Sampling time.** "0 day" denotes the pre-immunization time point of the design.
- **Search settings.** Modifications and tolerances come from the protocol text; no search parameter file was deposited. TMT6plex (N-term, K) and Carbamidomethyl (C) were confirmed in the deposited peptide table. Fragment tolerance 0.005 Da is written as stated, though it looks unusual and may be a typo for 0.05 Da.
- **Missing terms and columns.**
  - Alkylation reagent is not stated, so the column is omitted.
  - Fractionation is mixed-mode HPLC (Promix MP); PRIDE has no mixed-mode term, so the generic `High-performance liquid chromatography` (PRIDE:0000565) is used.
  - `characteristics[genotype]` is an extra column outside the two templates.
- **Isolation width.** Raw headers show 4 of 112 runs used an isolation-width 3 method, while the protocol states 2.5 Da. There is no column for this; it is noted here only.
