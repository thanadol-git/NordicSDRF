## Limitations and calls to attack in review

- The growth phase is inferred from the order of the sampling-point token in the file names; the paper gives 8/16/64 h (glucose) and 16/40/96 h (xylose). Glucose tokens 1/2/8 match 8-h sampling intervals, but the xylose lipid-phase token 10 would mean 80 h rather than 96 h, so no sampling time in hours is annotated.
- The same reactor letter 'A' is used for one glucose and one xylose cultivation; they are different bioreactor runs.
- Trypsin/P in the search is annotated as Trypsin. The lysis buffer contained DTT (paper Methods); the alkylation reagent is not stated, and no reagent columns are annotated.
