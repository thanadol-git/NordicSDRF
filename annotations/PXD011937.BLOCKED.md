# PXD011937 — review blocked

The 96-row draft maps all 32 deposited RAW files to three SILAC channels across four eight-fraction experiments. The [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6735892/) and deposited MaxQuant output support the channel and fraction assignments; inspected RAW headers distinguish Q Exactive HF pilot runs from Q Exactive HF-X main runs.

The draft passes the official `ms-proteomics`, `human`, and `cell-lines` validator with an offline ontology warning. It is not approved. The first independent review identified missing cell-line and disease specificity and requested direct evidence for the instrument mapping. Those changes were made in the source tables and the draft was rebuilt. The second independent review found that `characteristics[cell line]=HeLa S3` is absent from the permitted CLO/BTO/EFO ontology set, although the Cellosaurus subline is verified. It recommends using the supported broader `HeLa cell` value in that column while retaining the exact subline in the Cellosaurus fields. It also notes a minor disease-label normalization issue. See the [second review report](../scratchpad/PXD011937/review2.json).

After two failed independent reviews, annotation is paused under the review workflow. The [draft](../scratchpad/PXD011937/draft.sdrf.tsv) and [evidence manifest](../scratchpad/PXD011937/draft.evidence.json) remain in the accession scratchpad. No approval receipt exists.
