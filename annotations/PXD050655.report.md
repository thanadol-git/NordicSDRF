## Design choices and caveats

- `comment[data file]` is the `.d` folder name (not the zip).
- Sample-to-patient/time/dose mapping comes from the deposited `metadata.txt` (T1-T109). Day -6 = pre-treatment (run-in), Day 15 = post-treatment.
- Factor values: treatment (dose group) and treatment status (pre/post). Pre-treatment sections carry their patient's assigned dose group in `treatment`; the status column distinguishes them.
- T1-T8 (patient P15) have dose NA in metadata: treatment and status set to `not available`.
- T36-T38 have run1 and run2 re-measurements (technical replicate 1 and 2, our inference); T62 and T67 only have a run2 file (replicate 1). Samples T10, T68 and T108 are marked as not used for proteome scoring in metadata but have raw data, so they are annotated. Per-section biological replicate is 1 for all (sections of the same biopsy are separate sources; grouping is in `individual`).
- Age and sex not deposited: `not available`. Fragment tolerance and dissociation method not stated: `not available`. Instrument taken from protocol/PRIDE, not verified from analysis.tdf.
- Disease "celiac disease" applies to all (patients are treated CeD; no healthy control arm).
