## Study
Single-shot DIA proteomes of ex vivo AML cells from 30 patients treated 6 h with DMSO or 1 uM selinexor, plus 4 AML cell lines (selinexor-sensitive GDM-1, MV4-11; resistant NOMO-1, PL-21) (Cell Rep 2022, 10.1016/j.celrep.2022.111177).

## Templates
ms-proteomics + human v1.1.0 (cell-lines template not used: patient rows have no cell line)

## Scope: deposited vs annotated

## Sample map
Record: 'B' = before treatment (DMSO), 'A' = after 1 uM selinexor 6 h; file names give patient number and A/B, and cell line + replicate.

Spectronaut default mods assumed; TCEP/CAA from the record; trypsin.
