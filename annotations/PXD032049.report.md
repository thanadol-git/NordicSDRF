# PXD032049 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Andersen S, Nawrocki A, Johansen AE, Herrero-Fresno A, Menéndez VG, Møller-Jensen J, Olsen JE. "Proteomes of Uropathogenic Escherichia coli Growing in Human Urine and in J82 Urinary Bladder Cells". Proteomes 2022;10(2):15, doi:10.3390/proteomes10020015, PMID 35645373, PMC9149909 (Methods read). MS at SDU Odense (Larsen group). UPEC strain UTI89 grown in human urine or MOPS (T = 0–4 h), in MOPS ± glucose to exponential (OD600 0.5) / stationary (OD600 2.0) phase, and recovered from infected J82 bladder cells (trypsin- or scrape-detached pellets). Five TMT10plex sets, HILIC-fractionated (9–12 fractions), Q Exactive HF, Proteome Discoverer 1.4 / Mascot + Sequest.

ms-proteomics v1.1.0 only. The bacterial-strain/organism columns fall under the base template; no vertebrate/human donor columns apply.

## Key decisions
- Instrument Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523): RAW headers of all 52 files (methods `…HCD30/31_TMT…`). PRIDE says Q Exactive, overridden.
- DDA, HCD, NCE 31 (protocol; some methods are HCD30).
- Lys-C + trypsin; DTT in the lysis buffer (PRIDE:0000608); alkylating reagent not named → `not available` (Carbamidomethyl is still searched as static).
- Modifications from the PD workflow embedded in `Set_A.msf`: static Carbamidomethyl C, **static TMT6plex K and peptide N-term** (Sequest node `Static_2` TMT6plex (K), `Static_Terminal1` Any N-terminus); dynamic Oxidation M, Deamidated N/Q, Gln->pyro-Glu, Glu->pyro-Glu (N-term). The protocol prose reads as if TMT were variable; the deposited search file decides, per the brief. Tolerances 5 ppm / 0.02 Da (protocol).
- Fractionation HILIC (PRIDE:0000554). `comment[tmt set]` gives the set.
- Characteristics: organism Escherichia coli, strain UTI89; `growth condition` (medium + time/phase, or intracellular in J82) is the factor; `time` in hours for the time-course samples.
