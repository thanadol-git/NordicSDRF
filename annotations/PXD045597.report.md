# PXD045597 SDRF annotation report

## Study
Domain-specific O-mannose glycoproteomics in five human cell lines and a HEK293 O-Man glycosyltransferase KO panel (Mol Cell Proteomics 2024, PMID 38851451).

## Templates
ms-proteomics + cell-lines v1.1.0

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
Partial: 30/108 RAW (Datasets 5-9: one-plex light-diethyl O-Man glycoproteomes of BG1, Caco-2, HEK293 WT, HepG2, SH-SY5Y; BC2L-A elution fractions E1-E6). Not annotated: 78 runs of differential (heavy/light diethyl) KO-panel and purified-protein datasets, because the ZFN clone codes in file names (212-H8, 1063-A1, 1064-A4, 1065-C3, 1066-E8, WT-AC2, AH19-21) are not mapped to genotypes in the paper text or PRIDE; and the TMTC differential set (Dataset 3).

## Sample map
File names: dataset number, cell line, sample number, BC2L-A elution fraction (E1-E6). Paper: one-plex experiments used light diethyl labelling of crude membrane preparations.

## Key decisions

Lumos confirmed in RAW headers (including Dataset 9 E2-E5 without instrument in name). O-Man as Hex on S/T, C-Man as Hex on W. Fraction method not a CV term, so 'not available'.
